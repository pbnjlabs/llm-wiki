# Ki Mobility

US maker of manual wheelchairs, tilt-in-space chairs, pediatric chairs and
Axiom seating. Ki Mobility, 5201 Woodward Drive, Stevens Point, WI 54481;
**715-254-0991**; sales@kimobility.com; kimobility.com (dealer locator,
"Ki Parts" illustrated parts). EU representative and UK responsible person
are Etac (Sweden), Ki's parent company. Added 2026-10-26.

Ki writes two manuals per chair: a **User Instruction Manual and Warranty**
(English and Spanish in one PDF) and a dealer **Technical Manual** for a
qualified technician at an authorized Ki dealer.

## Common to every Ki chair manual

- **Default fastener torque 40 in-lb (4.51 Nm)** unless stated.
- **No slopes over 9°** (about 2 in per foot).
- Weekly / monthly / annual maintenance table, troubleshooting chart (pulls,
  looseness, caster float or flutter, squeaks), Teflon-based lube only, no
  WD-40 or petroleum solvents, sanitize with 70% alcohol.
- **Transit** only with the transit option (WC-4 §19 / ISO 7176-19),
  forward facing, pelvic and shoulder belts, back reclined 30° max, accessories
  removed; a chair in an accident shouldn't be used again.
- **Warranty:** frame and quick-release axles for the original purchaser's
  life (plus cross braces and hangers on folding chairs, tilt assembly and
  bearings on tilt chairs, polymers on the Ethos); other parts 1 year; wear
  items excluded; RA from Ki via the dealer. Seating: 6 months to 5 years by
  product.
