# Savaria

Canadian accessibility manufacturer (Savaria Corporation, TSX:SIS).
Stairlift documents come from **Savaria Concord Lifts, Inc.**, 2 Walker
Drive, Brampton, Ontario L6T 5E1; **Technical Support 800-791-7999**;
savaria.com and the Savaria Dealers website. Added 2026-10-26.

**Savaria Patient Care** is its beds, surfaces, ceiling lift and sling
division, built from **Span, Handicare and Silvalea**. Handicare makes the
Prism P440, C-450/C-625 and Castor 2 lift stand on file, so [[Prism]] equipment now sits
in the Savaria family too.
