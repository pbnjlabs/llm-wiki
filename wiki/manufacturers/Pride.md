# Pride (Pride Mobility / Quantum Rehab)

Pride Mobility Products makes power chairs and scooters under two
brands: **Quantum Rehab** (complex rehab chairs, "a division of Pride
Mobility Products") and **Pride** (Jazzy power chairs, Go Chair, Go Go
and other scooters). Phone numbers on the documents: Pride Technical
Service **1-877-800-1248** (Canada 1-888-570-1113); Quantum
**866-800-2002**; Pride main line 800-800-8586. Dealer downloads (firmware,
program files): prideservice.com.

## Things to know

- **Identify the controller before reading a code.** Q-Logic, Q-Logic 3
  and the PG/Dynamic controllers number their faults differently.
- **Brake resistance differs by chair:** about 80 Ω on the original Q6
  Edge, 60 Ω on the J6, 35 Ω on the Jazzy 614
  ([[Power-Chair-Troubleshooting]] §6a).
- **Torque values changed between generations** (motor screws 7 N·m on
  the 2017 guides, 15–20 N·m on the Stretto and Edge 4). Use the guide
  for that chair ([[Pride-Parts-Replacement]]).
- **Batteries:** AGM, gel or hybrid gel for the sealed lead-acid models;
  replace every battery as a matched set (house standard). **Never
  load-test lithium**; near-zero lithium may be in sleep mode.
- **Rated slopes:** 6° for the Jazzy 1450, J4, J6 and all scooters;
  7.5° for most Quantum chairs. PMS still builds ramps at 1:12
  ([[Ramp-Slope-and-Length]]).
- **VA:** Quantum chairs are on FSS contract V797D-70001 per Pride's
  chart ([[VA-FSS-Contracts]]). Pride is one of the brands on
  Scootaround VA jobs ([[Scootaround-VA-Repairs]]).
