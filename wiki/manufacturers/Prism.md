# Prism (Prism Medical)

Manufacturer/brand of patient/ceiling lifts. **All six documents on file
are fully ingested** (the C-450/C-625 technical manual, a generic
pre-install site-evaluation form, two P440 manual revisions, the
co-branded Castor 2 Post Lift Stand installation manual, and — filed
here but not Prism-authored — Vancare's ceiling-lift track installation
guide, see below).

## Model families

- [[C-450-C-625]] — overhead track-mounted patient ceiling lift, three
  drive/traverse configurations (Manual, Power/Manual-PT, Power X-Y/Power
  Turntable) sharing one combined PCB design. Two further "C-Series"
  siblings, **C-800 and C-1000**, are confirmed to exist (via
  [[Vancare]]'s cross-compatibility tables) but have no manual of their
  own on file yet.
- [[P440]] — **portable** ceiling lift (carried by hand between tracks,
  motorized up/down only, no powered traverse) — Prism's only other
  documented product family. A sibling model, **P-600**, is likewise
  confirmed to exist but undocumented.
- [[Prism-Castor-2-Post-Lift-Stand]] — a freestanding, portable two-post
  track accessory, sized in three part numbers that match the C-Series
  lifts' capacities exactly (450lb/625lb).

## Confirmed manufacturer relationship: Handicare

**"Prism" is not an independent manufacturer — it's a brand name
Handicare uses for at least the P440 and the Castor 2 Post Lift Stand.**
This was an open question for a while: [[C-450-C-625]]'s page has long
noted that its [[Ceiling Lift Evaluation Form]]'s example track diagram
uses a "Handicare" component legend, without explanation. It's now
resolved by two independent pieces of evidence found in a later ingest
pass:
- [[P440 Owner's Manual (2021, Rev 19-Oct)]] carries a "handicare P-440"
  nameplate throughout and gives Handicare USA (St. Louis, MO) and
  Handicare Canada contact addresses. Its Warranty section explicitly
  splits responsibility: **Handicare** guarantees new equipment (1yr
  install / 18mo manufacture, whichever shorter); **Prism** separately
  guarantees only *refurbished* equipment (3mo install / 6mo purchase) —
  meaning "Prism" appears to be Handicare's North American
  refurbished-equipment/dealer-facing brand, not a wholly separate
  manufacturing entity.
- [[Prism Castor 2 Post Lift Stand Installation Manual]] is co-branded
  "Prism" (product title) and "Handicare" (manual logo/authorization
  language).

The earlier-ingested [[C-450 C-625 Technical Manual]] and the 2017
[[P440 User Manual]] don't mention Handicare at all — either the
branding relationship postdates those documents, or they simply omit
it. Not confirmed whether C-450/C-625 itself is Handicare-manufactured;
only P440 and the Castor stand are confirmed so far.

## Cross-manufacturer note: Vancare

A 213-page ceiling-lift track installation guide found under
`MANUALS/Prism/` turned out to be authored by **[[Vancare]]**, a
separate track-system manufacturer — not a misfile like the Harmar/Bruno
or AutoSlide/Open Sesame cases elsewhere in this wiki, but genuine
supplier documentation: the guide's own parts tables confirm Prism's
C-Series and Portable lifts are designed to run on Vancare track
hardware (among others — Mikyway, Guldman, and ArjoTrack tracks are also
listed as compatible). See [[Vancare]] for the full writeup; kept as its
own manufacturer page rather than merged into this one.

## Cross-manufacturer note

Shares the [[Overspeed-Safety-Brake]] concept with Bruno's stairlifts — a
centrifugal mechanical governor, though Prism's version requires motor
replacement rather than Bruno's field reset.
