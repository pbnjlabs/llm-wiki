# Harmar

A different stairlift manufacturer from Bruno — makes the "CSL Series"
curved stairlift and its own staircase-measuring kit, "Helix PhotoMap"
(paper markers photographed and submitted to Harmar for a human-reviewed
rail design, vs. Bruno's on-site pass/fail software — see
[[Staircase-Measuring-System]] for the comparison).

## Product summary (from the one document on file)

- **CSL Series** — curved stairlift (product itself not otherwise
  documented here; only the measuring-kit manual is on file).
- **Helix PhotoMap Kit**: markers, calibration markers, camera, data/sync
  cable, charger, measurement/order form, tape measure.
- Process: place calibration markers exactly 2 ft apart on the lowest
  landing; place markers 16-24" apart along walls on all landings; place
  two markers per tread leading edge, working bottom-to-top; photograph
  each landing/step section from multiple angles (minimum 14 targets /
  7 markers visible per photo, ~50+ photos); submit via Dropbox (preferred,
  harmar.com/project-image-upload), email (Helix@harmar.com), or physical
  camera return.
- Contact: Harmar Technical Support, 800-833-0478.
