# Harmar

A stairlift, platform-lift, and vehicle-lift manufacturer, distinct from
Bruno. Its products were first discovered in this wiki as a single
misfiled document under Bruno's Curved SL folder; the human has since
created a dedicated `MANUALS/Harmar/` folder and populated it with the
manufacturer's real product-line manuals, giving Harmar four documented
product families:

- **[[Helix]]** (CSL Series) — curved-rail residential stairlift
- **[[Pinnacle-SL300]]** and **[[Pinnacle-SL600]]** — straight-rail
  stairlifts (SL300 residential-only, SL600 commercial-rated)
- **[[HighlanderII]]** — enclosed vertical platform lift (VPL400-1400)
- **[[AL-Series-Outside-Vehicle-Lifts]]** — hitch-mounted vehicle lifts
  for unoccupied mobility devices (~20+ model variants across 2 manuals)

Headquartered/warranty address: 1500 Independence Blvd., Suite 220,
Sarasota, FL 34234. Support: 800-833-0478 (general/LiftSquad), tech@harmar.com.

## Cross-cutting concepts

- [[Harmar-Warranty]] — every Harmar product line carries its own
  distinct warranty term (unlike Bruno's stairlift-vs-vehicle-lift split
  or Golden's scooter-vs-wheelchair split)
- [[Harmar-Stairlift-Beep-Codes]] — beeps-only major/minor fault scheme
  shared (with small variations) across Helix, SL300, and SL600
- [[Overspeed-Safety-Brake]] — Harmar's HighlanderII VPL has an
  Over-Speed Governor (OSG) switch on the ACME drive screw, a third
  manufacturer/mechanism alongside Bruno's and Prism's centrifugal
  governors
