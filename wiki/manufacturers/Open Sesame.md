# Open Sesame

**Open Sesame Door Systems, Inc.**, 462 Davis St., San Leandro, CA 94577.
Phone 1-800-673-6911 (9–5 Pacific, Mon–Fri), fax 510-638-2445,
info@opensesamedoor.com, opensesamedoor.com. They make **automatic
swing-door operators** for disability access, a different mechanism from
[[AutoSlide]]'s sliding-door drive. Like AutoSlide, this is a home
accessibility product, not mobility equipment.

## Product line

- **[[Open-Sesame-Model-133]]**: the residential operator. The door works
  freely by hand when the operator isn't running.
- **[[Open-Sesame-Model-233]]**: the public-access and heavier-duty
  operator. Its clutch is always engaged, so it also works as a door closer.
  It suits storm and screen doors and, with a rain shield, gates.
- Older units: Model 100 (sold before Sept 2012), and units with gray
  (390 MHz) or purple (315 MHz) antennas. Current units have a green
  433 MHz antenna.
- **Accessories:** remotes and keypads, wireless push pads, the Voice
  Interface (Alexa), electric strikes, and the proximity tag system, which
  uses [[AutoSlide]]'s RFID reader. See [[Automatic-Door-Operators]].

## Warranty

- **90-day satisfaction guarantee:** refund or exchange, in the original
  packing, with an RA number from Open Sesame. It doesn't cover shipping,
  installation or accessories. If the unit was bought from a dealer, the
  dealer's return policy applies. The serial number has to be on the unit.
- **3 years:** all factory parts repaired or replaced free.
- **Years 4–13 (10 year limited):** repair or replacement for a fee of
  **no more than $450**.
- **Not covered:** the operator's battery and remote batteries.
- **Clutch failure** (slipping, grinding or whining): call for an RA
  number and send the operator in.
