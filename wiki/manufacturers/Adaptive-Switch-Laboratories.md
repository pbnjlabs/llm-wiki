# Adaptive Switch Laboratories (ASL)

US maker of **alternative drive controls** for power wheelchairs: head
arrays, specialty joysticks, switch scanners, reset switches, mounts and
Bluetooth computer/phone access ("ATOM Technology"). Not to be confused
with Bruno's **ASL-250/275** vehicle lifts. Adaptive Switch Laboratories,
Inc., 125 Spur 191 Suite C, Spicewood, TX 78669; **800-626-8698**;
asl-inc.com (programming info via the QR codes on each sheet). Added
2026-10-27.

ASL controls plug into the chair's own electronics: **LiNX** (Input
Module), **Q-Logic** (Enhanced Display or SCIM), **R-net** (Omni / Omni2)
or **MK6i**. Programming is done in the chair's controller; see
[[Alternative-Drive-Controls]].
