# EZ-ACCESS

A division of Homecare Products, Inc. (Auburn, WA); US maker of aluminum accessibility ramps, threshold ramps and modular
platforms (ezaccess.com; customer service **1-800-451-1903**). The
`MANUALS/Ramps/` folder holds EZ-ACCESS material ("EZ Access" in its
filenames), so this page is its manufacturer page. EZ-ACCESS's own
manuals and spec sheets, added 2026-10-26, are sorted by product line
in `MANUALS/Ramps/EZ-ACCESS/<line>/`.

## Product lines

| Line | Page | Class |
|---|---|---|
| Line | Page | Class | Rating |
|---|---|---|---|
| TRANSITIONS | [[TRANSITIONS-Threshold-Ramps]] | Threshold plates, ramps and mats, up to ~6 in | 700 lb aluminum, 850 lb rubber |
| SUITCASE | [[SUITCASE-Ramps]] | Folding portable ramps, 2–10 ft | 800 lb (500 lb with a Top Lip Extension) |
| GATEWAY 3G | [[GATEWAY-3G]] | Solid one-piece ramp, 3–10 ft, optional handrails. Sold as portable; **PMS treats it as permanent** | 1,000 lb |
| PATHWAY 3G | [[PATHWAY-3G]] | Residential modular ramp system, plus matching stairs | 1,000 lb (stairs 850 lb) |
| PATHWAY HD | [[PATHWAY-HD]] | Commercial code-compliant modular ramps, platforms and stairs | 100 psf, 300 lb concentrated |

Warranty: **lifetime limited** on everything except the PATHWAY HD
(**3 years**).

EZ-ACCESS also sells loading ramps (pet, powersports, shed/equipment).
Those are out of scope for this wiki and not documented here.

The articles are marketing and consumer guidance, not manuals, so:

- They carry less weight than the manuals, which win wherever the two
  disagree. (PATHWAY capacity was the old example: 850 lb in the 2015
  manual vs. 1,000 lb in a 2021 article. The 2023 manual now says 1,000
  lb, so the article was right.)
- EZ-ACCESS's **own documents also allow slopes steeper than 1:12**
  (PATHWAY 3G 1:14 – 1:8, GATEWAY rise guides near 1:8, low-profile
  transitions down to 1:6). The 1:12 house standard overrides them too.
- They sometimes contradict each other, and some suggest slopes
  steeper than 1:12. **The wiki standard is 1:12 only (4.8°)**, which
  overrides the articles: see [[Ramp-Slope-and-Length]].
- Specs (sizes, capacities) come from articles as old as 2021 and may
  be outdated.

Sources are filed under `wiki/sources/EZ-ACCESS/`, with product-line
folders TRANSITIONS, Portable Ramps (SUITCASE and GATEWAY), PATHWAY 3G
and PATHWAY HD. Cross-cutting planning and ADA articles go in General.

Lifetime warranty on PATHWAY (register at ezaccess.com/warranty-satisfaction)
and on the TRANSITIONS Angled Entry Ramp and Angled Entry Mat (mat:
register at ezaccess.com/product-registration, per
[[TRANSITIONS Angled Entry Mat Instructions]]). **Full warranty terms**
([[EZ-ACCESS Lifetime Warranty (2017)]]): lifetime, parts and components only (not powder coat or applied
non-skid tape), parts and labor free, **original purchaser only, not
transferable**, proof of purchase, **report defects within 30 days**,
contact EZ-ACCESS first for an RA or site visit. Excludes installation,
relocation, improper loads, ground settling or regrading, and weather.
SUITCASE specs: [[SUITCASE Portable Folding Ramps Brochure]].

## Prices and pay scales

**PMS doesn't take work from EZ-ACCESS; EZ-ACCESS is only a
manufacturer.** Treat any EZ-ACCESS prices, rates or pay scales (the pay
scale sheets, the install times on [[Ramp-Install-Time-Estimates]], the
old invoices on [[EZ-ACCESS Invoice Examples (Historical)]]) as
**manufacturer suggestions**, not PMS rates.
