# Sunrise Medical

Maker of **Quickie** and **Zippie** manual wheelchairs (and other
mobility products). Sunrise Medical (US) LLC, 12002 Volunteer Blvd.,
Mount Juliet, TN 37122; customer service **800-333-4000**;
sunrisemedical.com (parts manuals, instruction sheets and videos).
Sunrise Medical Canada 1-800-263-3390.

**First manual-wheelchair manufacturer in the wiki** (added 2026-10-26).
PMS services VA-issued manual chairs through Scootaround
([[Scootaround-VA-Repairs]]).

## Model families

- [[Quickie-2-Series]]: Quickie 2, 2HD, 2 Lite, 2HP and 2HP HD folding
  chairs.
