# AutoSlide

Manufacturer of AutoSlide-branded automatic sliding-door retrofit kits
(Autoslide Pty Ltd; USA support centers in California and Florida; phone
(833) 337-5433, support@autoslide.com). **A different product category from
every other manufacturer in this wiki** — Bruno, Golden, Harmar, Pride, and
Prism all make mobility equipment (stairlifts, scooters, wheelchairs,
vehicle/platform lifts, patient lifts); AutoSlide makes a motorized drive
that automates an existing sliding door panel. Likely sold by Performance
Medical Supply as a touchless-entry / independent-living accessibility
product rather than mobility equipment proper — worth confirming with the
human whether this reflects a real new business line, since it sits outside
the scope described in `CLAUDE.md`'s project overview.

## Product line

- **[[AutoSlide-Drive-System]]** — the retrofit kit itself, in four
  variants (Standard, Standard iLock, Elite, Elite iLock) distinguished by
  motor power and whether a dynamic braking lock is built in. One shared
  installation manual (all variants, single sliding-panel-door
  installation) covers mounting, DIP-switch configuration, four operating
  modes (Auto/Stacker/Secure/Pet), width programming, rehanding, and
  troubleshooting.
- RFID pet-door sensor accessory — see
  [[RFID Pet Sensor Operation Instructions]], filed under a
  **mismatched raw-source folder name**
  (`MANUALS/Open Sesame/` — see that page's filing note). Confirms
  AutoSlide also sells/supports a second controller product name,
  "Multidrive," not otherwise documented in this wiki.

## Not yet ingested / not yet on file

Two accessories are named in the installation manual's FAQ but have no
manual on disk yet: a **Bluetooth module** (phone-app control, paired with
an "AutoPlus Hub") and a **home-automation relay/key-switch cable**.

## Warranty / return policy

2-year limited manufacturer's warranty on defects (excludes normal wear,
misuse, non-AutoSlide accessories, batteries, and commercial/institutional
use). Standard 30-day RMA-based return policy with a 20% restocking fee if
returned merchandise isn't in original condition/packaging — both documented
in [[AutoSlide Installation Manual (Standard, iLock, Elite, Elite iLock)]]
rather than a separate warranty document, unlike every other manufacturer in
this wiki (compare [[Harmar-Warranty]], [[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]],
[[Warranty]] for Bruno).
