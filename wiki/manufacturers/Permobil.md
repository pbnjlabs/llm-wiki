# Permobil

Swedish mobility maker of power chairs (with **R-net** electronics and **ICS**
seating), and owner of **TiLite** and **PDG Mobility** manual chairs and the
ROHO and Comfort seating lines. Added 2026-10-26.

- **TiLite:** TiSport, LLC, Pasco, WA; customer support **800-736-0925**
  (Pilot and Twist manuals give **800-545-2266**).
- **PDG Mobility** (Product Design Group): Richmond, BC; **800-736-0925**,
  sales.pdg@permobil.com; parts through the Permobil Store.
