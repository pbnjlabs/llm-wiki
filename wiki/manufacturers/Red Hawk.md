# Red Hawk

**"Red Hawk" is Performance Medical Supply's own folder name for a
cluster of folding power wheelchairs — it is not a brand printed on any
box, model, or manual.** All five manuals found in this folder are
raw-OEM or dealer-relabeled documents for products made by **two
unrelated Chinese manufacturers**, sold in turn under **four different
model names by two different regional distributors**. This is a more
tangled version of the cross-brand situations already seen elsewhere in
this wiki (compare [[Harmar]]'s misfiled Bruno document and
[[Vancare]]'s track-supplier relationship with Prism) — here, nothing is
simply misfiled; PMS appears to genuinely stock the same underlying
hardware under multiple retail names.

## OEM #1: Nanjing Jin Bai He Medical Apparatus Co., Ltd. (Jiangsu, China)
Manufactures a compact folding power wheelchair (steel frame, 2×250W
brushless motors, 2×6Ah lithium batteries, electromagnetic brake, 6km/h
max speed) sold under three names:
- **[[Air-Hawk-Eagle-HD]]** — identical product sold as "Air Hawk" by
  **Gilani Engineering PTY LTD** (Smithfield, NSW, Australia) and as
  "Eagle HD" by **Discover Your Mobility Inc.** (Hazel Park, MI, USA) —
  confirmed byte-for-byte identical technical parameters table (160kg
  capacity) across both manuals; only the model name, distributor
  contact page, and cover photo caption differ
- **[[Falcon]]** — same base architecture and manual template, but a
  **180kg** capacity (vs. 160kg) and an added reclining-backrest
  mechanism not present on Air Hawk/Eagle HD; distributed by Discover
  Your Mobility Inc.
- **[[Phoenix]]** — a visually similar folding chair but a **different
  manual template entirely** (no Nanjing Jin Bai He branding visible,
  120kg capacity, a stray reference to "TREK Mobility" battery-charger
  certification not otherwise explained); also distributed by Discover
  Your Mobility Inc. — likely the same manufacturer family, but not
  confirmed from the manual text alone the way Air Hawk/Eagle HD/Falcon
  are

## OEM #2: Anhui JBH Medical Apparatus Company Limited (Anhui, China)
A distinct, more modern-looking product line ("JBH" branded throughout,
carbon-fiber frame), sold under model number **DC02** — dealer-relabeled
as **[[Pegasus Plus (DC02)]]** in this folder despite no "Pegasus" name
appearing anywhere in the manual itself. Confirmed as a genuinely
different manufacturer from OEM #1: different company name, different
address (Anhui vs. Jiangsu), different frame material, different weight
capacity (140kg), different manual design entirely (modern JBH-branded
template vs. plain-text template).

## Naming note
Both OEMs' brand names abbreviate to "JBH" (Jin Bai He / JBH) despite
being confirmed as separate legal entities at separate addresses — a
coincidence or a trademark-family relationship this wiki cannot confirm
from the manuals alone. Don't assume shared parts/support between the
two lines.

## Known limitation
The [[Pegasus Plus (DC02)]] manual (`Pegasus_Plus_Heavy_Duty_Manual.pdf`)
appears **truncated**: its own table of contents lists Warranty (p.31),
Troubleshooting (p.32-36), Diagnostics (p.37), Wiring Diagram (p.38), and
EMC (p.39-43) sections, but the file itself is only 30 pages and the
content ends (with blank trailing pages) around page 27 — none of those
sections are actually present. Warranty terms for this model are
therefore **not documented** in this wiki; flag to the human if a
complete copy can be sourced.
