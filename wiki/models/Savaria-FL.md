# FL (Savaria fixed ceiling lift)

Savaria Patient Care's **fixed** (track-mounted) ceiling lift, the counterpart
of the [[C-450-C-625]]. Comes with manual traverse or motorized traverse. Only
the 2022 spec sheet is on file; **no owner's or service manual**. The portable
sibling is the [[Savaria-M-Series]] (PL), and most operating points in its
manual likely apply, but check.

## Specs

- SWL **272 kg (600 lb)**, **200 kg (440 lb)** or **130 kg (286 lb)**
- 7.5 kg manual traverse, 8.5 kg motorized traverse
- Lift speed 5.5 cm/s empty, 4 cm/s at 272 kg
- Li-ion 25.2 V 2500 mAh; about 80 lifts of 24 in at 200 lb, 30 at 600 lb
- Charger 100–240 V in, 29.4 V 1 A out; about 2 h
- Hand control up/down/left/right (IP67); lift IP21, 54 dBA
- **10-year life** with one battery replacement; ISO 10535:2021, IEC 60601-1
  and -1-11, CSA Z10535.1

## Safety mechanisms

On/off switch doubles as the emergency stop. Not for use with flammable
anesthetics.
