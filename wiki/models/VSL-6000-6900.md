# VSL-6000 / VSL-6900 (Bruno Curb-Sider)

Bruno's **boom (hoist) lift**: mounted on a base in a minivan, SUV or
**pickup bed**, it swings an arm out, lowers a strap with a **C-arm or
pickup claw** that hooks the **docking device** on the scooter or chair,
lifts it, and swings it in. The **VSL-6900** adds a **power-telescoping
head** (extends up to **8 in**) for narrow openings. Link to [[Bruno]].

Harmar equivalent: [[Hoist-Series-Inside-Vehicle-Lifts]].

## Capacity

| Model | Max load |
|---|---|
| VSL-6000 | **250, 400 or 450 lb** (450 only when configured at Bruno) |
| VSL-6900 | **Up to 400 lb** |

**Go by the capacity decal on the lift base.**

> [!warning] PMS field experience: 2024+ Golden GC440
> A Curb-Sider **couldn't lift a new-model (2024+) GC440**: the scooter was
> too close to the lift's upper weight limit (per the human). **PMS and
> the VA both missed that the upgraded GC440 is heavier.** The 2024 GC440
> is 222 lb with U1s and about **242 lb with the M50-12 batteries PMS
> installs**, so a **250 lb** VSL-6000 has almost no margin once the
> docking device is added. **Check the scooter's year and weight against
> the decal** before quoting a Curb-Sider. See [[Mobility-Device-Weights]].

## Installing (key points)

- Ships in 3 cartons: lift, base assembly, docking device. Base is
  **adjustable (movable legs)** or **custom (fixed legs)**.
- **Power:** 30 A twin lead. **Minivans: route inside, never under
  the vehicle. Pickups: wiring runs underneath**; route carefully. 12 V
  battery on hybrids/EVs. Disable a 3rd-row power-folding seat.
- **Base:** as far into the corner as possible; 3/8 in holes (check
  underneath first); support washers and 1/4 spacer plates; seal the
  bolts underneath. Cut bed liners or mats so the feet sit on the floor.
  **Aluminum truck beds need a special kit.** Base level, mast vertical.
- **Pickups: install the wheel chock plate** from the base kit, or drive
  base damage isn't covered by warranty.
- Lift base to sub-base: 6 bolts, **12 ft-lb max**. Breaker ON (LEDs
  flash); **remove and discard the shipping pin**; raise the arm and pin
  it with the 1/2 in bolt.
- **Vertical tube** height via the cross bolt and 3 set screws. **Arm
  angle** via the turnbuckle: tip as high as possible without touching
  the vehicle.
- **Limit switch cams** (IN = top, black/red; OUT = bottom, green/white),
  set per driver or passenger side. Don't over-tighten the set screw;
  the cam cracks.
- **Outer arm reach:** clearance = **half the device's width + 1 in**
  (e.g. 24 in scooter → 13 in), up to 9 in of arm extension. Test-swing
  the scooter in front end first.
- Pendant on a magnetic holder; in **open pickups keep it at least 12 in
  above the floor**, secure the harness with the P-clip, seal outside
  connections.
- Optional **fold-down quick-release pin kit** to fold the lift head.

## Operating

Scooter **alongside the vehicle, facing the opposite way, front wheel
even with the bumper**; about **4 ft clear** behind; **engine off**, in
park. UP a little, **OUT** over the device (hold the C-arm so it doesn't
swing into the car), **DOWN**, hook the docking device, **UP** — **stop
before the white stitching on the belt reaches the belt guide** — then
**IN** while guiding it by hand, **DOWN** onto the floor. VSL-6000: belt
stays **taut**; VSL-6900: belt a **little slack**. Set the device's
brake, stow the pendant where nothing presses its buttons.

**VSL-6900:** OUT until the arm is perpendicular to the bumper,
**EXTEND** so it clears, OUT the rest of the way; on the way in, IN to
the opening, **RETRACT**, then IN and DOWN.

**Emergency:** a **hand crank** under the base cover (4 thumb screws;
the 450 lb VSL-6000 has a rubber boot to remove) turns the motor shaft
**only to bring the lift home** so the vehicle can be driven. Store it in
the glove box.

## Maintenance

"Maintenance-free," but **dealer inspection yearly** (6 months with
heavy use): **replace the belt if worn to the black stitching**, check
electrics, adjust the docking device. **Chain tension:** loosen the 3
motor-mount nuts (and the encoder screws on the 6900), rotate the motor
counterclockwise, tighten the pivot nut first. **VSL-6900 actuator and
gas spring are under pressure**: follow the manual's order.

## Troubleshooting highlights

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Nothing works | Circuit breaker ON; fuse; breaker at the control box; connections; ground; battery and clamps |
| Only in/out or only up/down | Rocker switch, connection, **limit switch jumper on the board**, motor |
| Slow or stalls | Weak battery, corroded clamps, **chain too tight** |
| Backdrives with load at top | Overload, gearbox, wiring: call Bruno |
| Squealing outer arm | Winding drum or motor bearing: replace outer arm |
| Up and down reversed | Run down too far; belt wrapped backwards: run it down until it rewinds correctly |
| 6900 lifts or drops the load when extending | Run down too far; **J11 comp jumper OFF** (set ON); encoder or its coupling |
| 6900 extends only ~1 in | Actuator |
