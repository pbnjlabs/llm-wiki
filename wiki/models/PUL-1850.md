# PUL-1850 (Bruno "Stow-Away")

An **under-vehicle transfer seat**, mostly for **pickup trucks**. A
drawer slides out from a box under the cab door and a scissor lift
raises a seat to two transfer heights, so the user moves from a chair to
the seat to the vehicle seat. It carries a **person** (300 lb), unlike
Bruno's other vehicle lifts. Product code 018 ("under-vehicle seating",
[[Bruno-Serial-Numbers]]). The manuals were pulled from Brunopartner on
2026-10-22.

## Specs

- **300 lb user.**
- 12 VDC motor and **gear rack** for in and out; 12 VDC **linear
  actuator** to lift.
- Vehicle battery, **30 A ATO in-line fuse**.
- Manual backup tools are supplied (glove box).
- Ground clearance **12 in / 6 in** minimum. The box is about 20 × 14 ×
  6 in. **Mud flaps recommended.**
- **Runs only with the door open** (magnetic door switch), so it doesn't
  affect the vehicle's FMVSS certification.

## Install essentials (2018 manual)

- Vehicle-specific brackets (Ram 1500 2019+ kits TAS-K-18559 / 18560;
  drawer slides BRG-75012):
  [[ILS - Pickup Truck Bases and Stow-Away Mounting Kits]],
  [[ILS - Out-Rider (PUL-1100) and Stow-Away Service Sheets]].
- Rivet nuts ("Q" bit, ¼-20 mandrel). **Blue threadlocker on all
  hardware.**
- **Control box in the engine bay**, vertical or with the edges down. The
  harness runs on top of or inside the frame.
- **Polarity matters**: reversing it ruins the controller.
- **Door switch gap no more than ¼ in.** Blue to white reads closed with
  the door open. **If it runs with the door shut, move the switch.**

## Operation and emergency

- **Engine off, in park.**
- UP to the lower transfer height, transfer, insert the handle, UP to the
  upper height, transfer, then DN to stow. Exit is the reverse.
- **Electrical backup:**
  1. Unplug the pendant and check the fuse.
  2. Use the control-box board buttons: **OUT** (until LED A), **DOWN**
     (LED D), **IN** (LED F).
  3. **The buttons bypass all limits.**
- **Manual backup** (seat stuck high):
  1. Pull the fuse.
  2. Drop the gear rack out of the box and fit the safety pin.
  3. Unbolt the actuator, lower the pan onto the pin, and screw the
     actuator in until about 1 in of chrome shows.
  4. Continue per the manual.
