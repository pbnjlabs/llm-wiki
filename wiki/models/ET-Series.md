# ET Series (SC402E, SC442E, SC442EMS, SC443E, SC443EMS)

Pride travel scooters, current in 2026. **SC402E = 3-wheel**; the
**SC442E/SC443E** are 4-wheel, and the **EMS** versions have the 3.5 A
charger. The sheet doesn't spell out "ET"; Pride's earlier
[[Elite-Traveller]] used 40E/44E numbers. Spec sheet only
([[ET Series Specifications (INFINFB4539)]], Rev D, Apr 2026).

| | SC402E | SC442E / SC442EMS | SC443E / SC443EMS |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capacity | 300 lb | 300 lb | 300 lb |
| Max speed | 4.23 mph | 4.23 mph | 4.23 mph |
| Ground clearance | 1.29 in at transaxle | same | same |
| Turning radius | 31.1 in | 37 in | 39.2 in |
| Reversing width | 47.83 in | 57.36 in | 50.5 in |
| Length | 36.97–37.83 in | 39.37–40.79 in | 41.5 in |
| Width | 19.45 in | 19.45 in | 19.45 in |
| Front tire | 8 in solid | 7 in solid | 7 in solid |
| Range 12 Ah, at 300 lb | 5.5 mi | 6.6 mi | 7.1 mi (8.9 at 220 lb) |
| Range 18 Ah, at 300 / 220 lb | 9 / 11.3 mi | 10.4 / 13.8 mi | 11.3 / 14.1 mi |
| Weight without batteries (with seat) | 77.8 lb | 83 lb | 83.6 lb |
| Heaviest piece (front section) | 30 lb | 35 lb | 36.2 lb |
| Climb | 2.36 in | 1.97 in | 1.97 in |
| Charger | 2 A | 2 A (EMS: **3.5 A**) | 2 A (EMS: **3.5 A**) |

All: 8 in solid rear tires; molded seat with mesh back, 17 × 17 in, 23
lb; seat height 17.5 in; overall height 35.71–37.17 in; rear-wheel
drive, sealed transaxle, 24 V motor; regenerative + electromechanical
brakes; battery pack 12 Ah 19.5 lb or 18 Ah 27.4 lb; max slope 6°.
12 Ah doesn't meet AS 3695.2 / EN12184; Class A is with 18 Ah.
