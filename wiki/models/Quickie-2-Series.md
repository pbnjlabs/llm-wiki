# Quickie 2 Series (folding manual wheelchairs)

Sunrise Medical's **Quickie 2 family** of folding (X-frame) manual
wheelchairs. The **first manual wheelchairs in the wiki**: PMS sees them
on Scootaround VA repairs ([[Scootaround-VA-Repairs]]). Each owner's
manual has a dealer service section with torques, so it's the service
reference too.

## Key settings and checks

- **Rear axle position** is the main adjustment: forward = easier
  turning but tips backward more easily. Change in small steps with
  anti-tips on and a spotter, then **re-set the wheel locks**.
- **Axle play:** 0 to 0.010 in. Hold the bearing end with a 1/2 in
  wrench and turn the outer nut with a 3/4 in.
- **Wheel locks:** arms sink **at least 1/8 in** into a correctly
  inflated tire. Set tire pressure first.
- **Anti-tips:** 1-1/2 to 2 in off the ground, both the same height.
- **Footrests / footplates:** at least 2 in off the ground.
- **Casters:** housing square (90°) to the floor; re-check after any
  change to axle height, tire size, center of gravity or camber (2 Lite:
  re-square if more than 2° off). Raise the seat and casters together.
- **Seat height:** 3.375 in range on the Quickie 2 and 2HP (axle plate in
  frame holes); 1/2 in steps on the 2 Lite (axle sleeve holes, plates
  swapped).
- **Seat saddle:** reversible; "dynamic" is more rigid, "classic" folds
  more easily.
- **Tire pressure** (Quickie 2 and 2HP tables): pneumatic 20, 22, 26 in
  65 psi; 24 in 75 psi; high-pressure clincher 22 in 100 psi, 24–26 in
  110 psi; Schwalbe Marathon 85–145 psi. Check weekly.
- **Lubrication:** Teflon-based only (Tri-Flow). **Never 3-in-One or
  WD-40**, no grease.
- **Troubleshooting:** pulls to one side, looseness, sluggish turning,
  squeaks or caster flutter: check equal tire pressure, tighten
  hardware and spokes, lube frame joints, adjust caster forks.

## Maintenance schedule

User or caregiver weekly (tire pressure, wheel locks, loose hardware)
and monthly (armrests, axles and sleeves, quick-release axles, wheels and
spokes, casters, anti-tips, frame and crossbrace, upholstery). **Dealer
inspection and service at least once a year.** Inspect before use after
more than 3 months in storage. This fits the Joint Commission rule of
maintaining per the manufacturer ([[Joint-Commission-DMEPOS]]).

## Transit

- Riding in the chair in a vehicle needs the factory **WC-19 Transit
  Option** (four decaled securement points plus a wheelchair-anchored
  pelvic belt) and a SAE J2249 WTORS, forward facing, pelvic and torso
  belts. Without it, never ride in the chair in a vehicle. See
  [[Quickie and Zippie WC-19 Transit Securement Supplement (MK-100072 Rev I)]]
  and [[Transport-and-Tie-Downs]].
- The supplement lists the Quickie 2, 2 Lite and 2HD but **not the
  2HP**, although the 2HP manual mentions a transit option. Check the
  chair's decals.
- **A chair that's been in a collision must be replaced.**
- Retrofitting the transit kit to an existing chair starts with the
  [[Transit Retrofit Kit Pre-Installation Inspection (104975 Rev D)]].

## Warranty

Frame and crossbrace **for life** of the original owner (expected
service life 5 years); Sunrise-made parts 1 year; not tires and tubes,
upholstery, pads or grips. Void if the serial tag is removed. Dealer
gets prior approval; owner pays labor.
