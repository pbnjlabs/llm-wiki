# VPL-3153 / VPL-3175 (Bruno AC low-rise VPL)

Bruno's **AC-powered** short residential vertical platform lifts: the
same low-rise tower as the battery [[VPL-3100B]] (VPL-3153B/3175B), but
running from the wall with no batteries. No "B" in the model number.
Bruno's **VPL-3100 (AC) install manual (05-19-2025) and operator manual
(09-16-2024)** were pulled from Brunopartner on 2026-10-22
([[VPL-3100 (AC) Installation Manual (2025)]],
[[VPL-3100 (AC) Operator Manual (2024)]]). The tech tips, TechMate and
ILS sheets below cover the older generations. PMS has no AC unit on file
(see [[VPL-Troubleshooting]]).

## From the 2025 install manual

- **Dedicated GFI 120 V 15 A circuit** (120 VAC 8 A draw). Long wire
  runs hurt performance.
- **No battery backup.** The emergency is the **hand wheel**: unplug,
  top cover off, wheel in the brake shaft, counter-clockwise.
- Site: 4 in 3500 psi slab, 4 × ½ in anchors, 80 in headroom, ⅜–¾ in
  sill gap, fascia 125 lb per 4 sq in.
- **Limit bracket:** set from the factory mark on the channel. **Never
  adjust the red-marked factory nuts.**
- Cam and activator: start 1.5 in below the landing, set a 2 in gap.
- **Controller fault:** press the E-stop, wait 5 s, and pull it out.
  This also clears stuck or both-pressed buttons.
- Squeal: Mobil SHC 100 every 6 in along the screw.
- PCB-00143 LEDs E1–E18 and the E18 flash codes are printed in the
  manual (same as below). Wait 3 min after unplugging before touching
  the board.
- **Warranty:** 2 years on major components, 1 year on parts, 30 days
  labor.

Bruno's 2026 spec sheet (ILS-00834,
[[ILS - VPL Technical Specifications]]) confirms the travel:
**VPL-3153 11–53 in, VPL-3175 28–75 in**. It also gives a **½ hp 90 VDC
motor (8 A input), 9 ft/min, 15 A breaker**, and weights of **656 lb
(3153) and 713 lb (3175)**.

## Specs (from the tech tips)

- **Power:** 120 VAC cord, 14 AWG, **15 A max**; 15 A internal breaker
  and line filter. (DC 3153B/3175B: 18 AWG 10 A cord, 35 A battery
  breaker.)
- **Board PCB-00143 "90V VPL"** (2017 on): F1 20 A, F2 2.5 A slo-blo
  (24 VDC accessories), drives a **½ hp 90 VDC motor**. Schematic
  **SCH-00229**.
- 750 lb (340 kg) at about 9 ft/min (CSA certificate, "model VPL").
- **Remove AC power and wait 3 minutes** before touching the board.

## Troubleshooting

- **PCB-00143 board:** work down the 18 status LEDs (E1 HV bus, E2 3.3 V,
  E3 24 V F2, E4 top cover, E5 E-stop/nut safety, E6–E9 gates, E14/E16
  limits, E17 safety pan). **E18 red = fault: count the flashes** (1
  stall/overload … 7 motor failing, 8–9 AC line low). Most clear with the
  E-stop; otherwise AC off for 1 minute. Full tables on the PCB-00143
  source page.
- **Run conditions** (Bruno training table): to run **down**, E2, E9, E16
  and E17 lit and E15 lights on a down button, then E10 and E12 light; to
  run **up**, E2, E9 and E14 lit and E13 lights on an up button, then E10
  and E11 light. The first unlit LED along the safety chain is the open
  circuit; the board layout and colour ladder logic are on
  [[Training - VPL 90V Board PCB-00143 LED Functions and Ladder Logic (SCH-00229)]].
- **Relay-logic units:** the TB2 jumper map and the 115 VAC / 24 VAC
  steps are on the 2009–2010 guide page. Brake leads should see **105+
  VDC** from the bridge rectifier on a command.
- AC units: check the **120 VAC outlet** (GFI, breaker) first.

## Install notes

- **Jumpers:** yellow VPL-31924 on the terminal block, **25–29 install
  mode**, 25–27 with a platform gate; blue VPL-31910 removed for an upper
  gate/door ([[Tech Tip - VPL Jumper Sheets (2017)]]). On the PCB-00143
  strip: no gates **25–33**, upper gate/interlock 25–29, platform gate or
  lower interlock 27–29.
- **Cold weather package:** VPL-K-3122 (3153) / VPL-K-3129 (3175)
  ([[Tech Tip - VPL Cold Weather Solutions]]).
- Drive work is shared with the 3100B: [[VPL-Drive-Service]] (ACME
  screw/nut kits SRV-3153-006 / SRV-3175-006).
- **Drift or coasting:** Bruno's 2013 brake retrofit kits SRV-3100-032
  (S/N 12070300108 on) and SRV-3100-039 (PCB-00137 board; remove F2)
  ([[ILS - VPL Brake Retrofit Kits (AC VPL-3153-3175)]]).
- **Interlocks need 24 VAC** on the AC units (24 VDC on the 3100B);
  the wrong one can damage the board ([[ILS - VPL Electric Interlock Wiring Kits]]).
- **Both buttons pressed:** daughter-boards PCB-00131 (for PCB-00107)
  and PCB-00132 (for PCB-00129) lock out both directions if UP and DOWN
  are pressed together ([[ILS - VPL Electrical Retrofit Kits]]).
- Maintenance: [[VPL-Maintenance]].
