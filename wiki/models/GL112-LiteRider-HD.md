# GL112 LiteRider HD (Golden)

A **400 lb heavy-duty LiteRider** with a **one-piece frame**: unlike
the other LiteRiders ([[GL111-141-LiteRider]], [[GL110-140-LiteRider]])
it doesn't split into halves. Link to [[Golden]].

## Documents on file

- [[LiteRider HD Owners Manual (GL112)]] (Feb 2020)

## Specs

400 lb, 4 mph, 20.7 mi (U1) or 25 mi (40 Ah), **2-U1 standard or
2-40 Ah**, **90 A** controller, 44 × 24 in, 32 in wheelbase, 40 in
turning radius, 6 in clearance to the deck. **171 lb assembled**: frame
102, seat 27, batteries 21 lb each. 10 in solid tires, 3 in front and
2 in rear anti-tip wheels. Slide seat standard, 20 × 18 in. See
[[Mobility-Device-Weights]].

## Service notes

- **Beep codes 1–9** (one long beep, then the count):
  [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]].
- Freewheel: lever **up** = freewheel, **down** = drive ([[Freewheel-Mode]]).
- The 102 lb frame is the heaviest piece; plan a two-person lift.

## Warranty

[[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]] (lists "LiteRider HD: GL112"): lifetime
frame, 2 yr drive train and electronics, 1 yr batteries.
