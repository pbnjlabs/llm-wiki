# SRE-1550 (Bruno Electra-Ride II — legacy)

Bruno's older straight stairlift, the **Electra-Ride II** (1996–2009
documents), residential and commercial. Its sibling, the **SRE-1540**,
had a larger fixed-arm seat. It is no longer sold. Manuals were pulled
from Brunopartner on 2026-10-22.

## Specs (2009 manuals)

- **350 lb**, **0–38 ft/min** variable.
- 24 VDC 2-pole 1.02 hp, self-locking gearbox, **belt and pulley
  reduction** to rack and pinion.
- 45° maximum. The rail is a **steel channel** with an integral rack.
- Swivel 0°, 45° and 90° both ways, top and bottom.
- IR transmitters. End charging.

## Install and service essentials

From [[SRE-1550 Installation Manual (2009)]]:
- **Rail ½–1 in above the nosing.** Clamps at least **1½ in from the
  wall**.
- Draw the carriage onto the rail by **turning the motor pulley by
  hand**. Angle bracket 4 bolts at **70 ft-lb**.
- **Belts:** check them yearly (dry or worn). After a replacement, tension
  and torque the motor bolts per drawing **SRE-00648**.
- **Rail wear:** there should be no groove.
- **Grease:** clear the paint chips first, then grease the rack (and the
  rail too on steel-wheel carriages). The manual says white lithium;
  current Bruno guidance is PTFE ([[Stairlift-Maintenance]]).
- **Speed pot:** start fully CCW, then turn CW to the user's weight mark.
- **Commercial overspeed:**
  - Align the white marks on the cam and housing before fitting the
    carriage.
  - If it trips during service, rotate the cam back to the detent.
  - **A real overspeed trip means pulling the drive and returning it.**
  - The duct-tape manual trip test is on
    [[Tech Tip - Legacy SRE-1540, SRE-1550 and SRE-2000]].
- DIP switch, codes, charger LEDs and fuse are as on the [[SRE-2000]].
