# PDG Stellar and LT Series

PDG Mobility manual tilt-in-space chairs. Two of the four manuals were read by
OCR, so check numbers against the PDF.

| Model | Capacity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Stellar GL | 250 lb standard base, 300 lb upgraded | Secondary gas spring for 250–300 lb; seat depth 16–22 in; power tilt option |
| Stellar HD | 250 lb unless PDG specifies | 33.4 kg as tested |
| Stellar Impact | per Stellar | Addendum to the Stellar manual |
| Stellar GLT | 300 lb | **45° tilt**, tilt limit bumpers both sides, pre-tilt 5° or 0° |
| Bentley LT | 300 lb | **20° tilt**, no knee rise |
| Bentley LT-R | 300 lb | Rear drive wheel position |

## Service notes

- **Torques:** Stellar seat depth insert bolts **18 ft-lb**; sub-frame plates
  **25 ft-lb**; LT seat depth insert **104 in-lb**; seat pan screws snug, not
  over-tight.
- **Gas strut position** by user weight and seat depth: user feels **heavy**
  coming upright = move the strut attachment **down**; **too light** or the
  attendant has to push into tilt = move it **up**.
- Never run the GLT without its tilt limit bumpers.
- Tilt cable tension as on the [[PDG-Fuze]].
- Transit only with the TTS (4 white hook symbols).
