# Axiom Seating (cushions and backs)

Ki Mobility's **Axiom** wheelchair seating.

| Product | User weight | Warranty |
|---|---|---|
| Axiom Back (PB, PL, LB, DL; kids PXPL, PXAL, PXALC) | 300 lb with DEX or FXT hardware, **400 lb with BTX** | 24 months; covers 6 months |
| Axiom AS / ASP cushions (fluid pads) | 350 lb | 24 months; covers 6 months |
| Axiom-G, -S, -P foam cushions | 350 lb | 24 months; covers 6 months |
| Axiom SP Visco / SP Fluid | 350 lb | 24 months; covers 6 months |
| Axiom One cushion | 350 lb | covers 6 months |
| Axiom Custom Seating | per chair | hardware 5 years, seating 1 year, covers 6 months |

- **Cleaning:** wipe foam bases with a damp cloth, never spray or soak, air dry out of sunlight (can take days), make sure it's dry; covers per label (G/S/P covers: warm wash, tumble dry no heat).
- SP Fluid: check for bottoming out; align the fluid pad's hook and loop or you create a pressure point.
- Custom seating and backs have transit hardware (2-point and 4-point knob packages).
- Backs tech manual: sizing, laterals, headrests, DEX / BTX / FXT / pediatric hardware setup.
