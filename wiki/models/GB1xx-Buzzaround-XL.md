# Buzzaround XL / Buzzaround Lite (GB106, GB116, GB146, GB117, GB147)

Golden's mid-size folding travel scooter family. It comes in two
generations, plus the older Lite:

| Model | Generation | Wheels | Controller | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GB106 Buzzaround **Lite** | 1st | 3 | **PG S-Drive** | Discontinued |
| GB116 / GB146 Buzzaround XL | 1st | 3 / 4 | Dynamics R-Series (DR50-A01, 50 A) | Discontinued |
| **GB117 / GB147** Buzzaround XL | Current | 3 / 4 | Dynamics R-Series (IPB 2025: DR90-A01) | Current |
| GB107 Buzzaround LT | Current | 3 | Dynamics DR90-A01 | Current (shares the GB117/147 IPB) |

**GB147H** (4-wheel) is on Golden's 2026 VA lineup (per the human). Per
the [[Buzzaround XL Owners Manual (GB117, GB147)]], **H means the HD
model**: 325 lb capacity, two 22 Ah batteries, 14 mi range, 120 lb
assembled. The suffixes are **D** (standard), **H** (HD), and **S / Z**,
which use a different steering system and rear chassis (front fork with
shocks). The OM's "SHD" models suggest S means suspension; that is an
inference.

## Electrical and diagnostics

- **GB116/146, GB117/147:** Dynamics R-Series. Beep codes 1–9:
  [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]]. On the GB117/147, codes and serial number
  are printed under the battery box.
- **GB106:** **PG S-Drive**. Use [[S-Drive-Beep-Codes]] (codes 2/3/5–9),
  **not** the R-Series table.
- GB117/147: **30 A** breaker on the battery pack, **40 A** battery fuse,
  **5 A** charger fuse, 1 A throttle fuse. Motor 0.8–1.5 Ω, brake
  45–52 Ω. The GB116/146 guide uses looser limits: motor under 50 Ω,
  brake under 80 Ω.
- Charger plugs into the battery pack or the tiller. It reads about 27 V
  at rest and about 29.5 V with the charger on.
- GB106 batteries go in with the terminals facing **rearward**.

## What's confirmed

- **Every XL separates into front and rear halves.** GB116/146 use the
  frame-lock latch ([[Frame Lock Adjustment (GB116, GB146)]]). GB117/147
  use a **drivetrain release lever**: lift it, then lift the front frame
  off the drivetrain. The halves connect electrically through intermediate
  harness connectors.
- **GB147** uses a **tie-rod front-wheel steering linkage** with a
  toe-alignment spec (16 5/8 in front track width). See
  [[Field Alignment Procedure (GB147)]]. The frame-lock procedure is for
  GB116/146 only.
- **GB106** is branded "Buzzaround **Lite**". It shares the
  electromagnetic brake body with GB116/GB146; only the handle shape
  differs.
- The brake mechanism is **shared with the GL110/GL140 LiteRider**: same
  brake body, model-specific handle. See [[Brake-Replacement]].
- **Transaxle replacement** (GB106 before July 2012, GB116/GB146 old
  style before April 2012) requires swapping the controller at the same
  time. See [[Golden-Transaxle-Replacement]], which also covers
  [[GL110-140-LiteRider]].
- The GB117/147 transaxle is MBX-BZ7-TRANS-BA, the same for all suffix
  variants and the GB107.
