# TiLite CR1

Rigid TiLite chair with a **COG (centre of gravity) strut and clamp** axle
system and a tension-adjustable back and seat. Own 20-page service manual
(© 2024) and parts manual.

## Torques

| Fastener | Torque |
|---|---|
| Fork stem nut | 15 Nm (11 ft-lb) |
| COG frame clamp | 8 Nm |
| Camber clamp, anti-tip side / non-anti-tip | 8 Nm / 6 Nm |
| COG strut clamp | 4–5 Nm |
| Push handles, wheel lock bolts | 4 Nm |
| Back support pivot bolt | 5 Nm |
| Footrest clamp bolts (floating caster fix) | 10 Nm |

## Setup and troubleshooting

- Tire pressure **every 2–3 weeks**, to the sidewall maximum.
- Weight split about **80% rear / 20% casters**. Re-set **toe** after any COG
  change (end-plug flats perpendicular to the ground).
- **Floating caster** (one caster off the floor): check tire pressure, camber
  tube centring and toe; loosen the footrest clamp on the floating side three
  turns and tap the bolt up to free the wedge; loosen the two bottom camber clamp
  bolts on the opposite side; let the chair settle; level the footrest, clamp
  bolts 10 Nm; tighten the bottom clamps front to back in **2 Nm steps**.
  Repeat until all four wheels sit flat.
