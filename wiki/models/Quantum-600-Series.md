# Quantum 600 Series / Jazzy 600 (legacy)

The Quantum 600 series and the Jazzy 600 / 600 XL: older mid-wheel
chairs. Their parts guide has the same door, shroud and articulating-beam
layout as the [[Jazzy-600-ES]] and original [[Q6-Edge]] guides. Shipped with **VSI, Q-Logic, Remote Plus or VR2**
electronics (the VSI has no separate power module). No owner's manual
on file.

## Documents

- [[Quantum 600 Series Parts Replacement Guide (INFMANU4842)]] (Dec
  2016)
- Jazzy 600 warranty insert: lifetime frame, 2-year electronics,
  18-month motors by HCPCS code ([[Pride Warranty Update Inserts (2010)]])
- [[VR2 Controller Cable Replacement (INFINFB2005)]] (shown on a Jazzy
  600)

## Service notes

- **Star drive wheel: main nut 51 N·m (37.6 ft·lb); re-seat the axle
  key.** Silver/black rim wheel: lug nuts 25 N·m.
- Caster nut 30 N·m; motor to bracket 7 N·m; articulating beam 40 N·m.
- Rear electronics door hangs on a lanyard from the top shroud.
- Freewheel: pull **up** = drive, push **down** = freewheel.
- Codes depend on the electronics: VSI/Remote Plus/VR2 flash codes on
  [[Pride-Controller-Flash-Codes]] (VSI **eight bars** can be a
  motor-to-motor leak, see [[Eight Bar Flash Code for VSI Controllers]]);
  Q-Logic on [[Q-Logic-Fault-Codes]].
