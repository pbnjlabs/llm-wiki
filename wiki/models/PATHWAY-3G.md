# PATHWAY 3G Modular Access System

EZ-ACCESS's **residential** modular aluminum ramp system. Ramp sections,
platforms and handrails are combined into straight, L, U or switchback
layouts to reach high porches and entries. For the commercial,
code-compliant counterpart, see [[PATHWAY-HD]].

## Specs (from web articles)

- **1,000 lb** capacity, covering user, device and caregiver.
- Standard residential widths **36 in and 48 in**. The source article
  says this of EZ-ACCESS residential ramps generally; PATHWAY 3G is
  presumably included.
- Built-in slip-resistant surface, a choice of handrail styles, pickets
  optional.
- Aluminum construction: no rot or warp like wood, and it can be
  reassembled or reconfigured as needs change.
- Dealer install typically takes a few hours.

## Planning

Planning is covered on [[Ramp-Site-Planning]]: the 11 configuration
factors, landings no more than ~30 ft apart, a platform at the top for a
flush transition, headroom and obstacles.

## Storm tie-downs

From [[Securing PATHWAY 3G Before a Storm]]: in hurricane areas, anchor
the system with a **tie-down kit** (auger ground anchors, wire rope,
attachment hardware). Inspect the tie-downs regularly and replace any
that are frayed or loose. Re-tighten them periodically and after every
storm. For placement, follow the product manual.
