# FL / PL (Savaria ceiling lifts)

Savaria Patient Care's two ceiling lifts on one platform: the **FL**, fixed in a
track (manual or motorized traverse, like the [[C-450-C-625]]), and the **PL**,
a portable lift carried from track to track and pushed by hand (like the
[[P440]]). The 2026 owner's manual calls the PL the **M-Series Portable Ceiling
Lift**. Caregiver operated; install by an authorized Savaria dealer only.
**Only the PL has a manual on file;** the FL has just the spec sheet, so the
PL's operating and maintenance points probably apply to it, but check.

| | FL fixed | PL / M-Series portable |
|---|---|---|
| SWL | 272 kg (600 lb), 200 kg (440 lb) or 130 kg (286 lb) | 200 kg (440 lb) or 130 kg (286 lb) |
| Weight | 7.5 kg manual, 8.5 kg motorized traverse | 5 kg (11 lb) |
| Lift speed | 5.5 cm/s empty → 4 cm/s at 272 kg | 5.5 → 4.5 cm/s at 200 kg |
| Lifts per charge | ~80 of 24 in at 200 lb, ~30 at 600 lb | ~30 at 440 lb |
| Hand control | Up/down/left/right | Up/down; touchpads on the lift too |

**Both:** Li-ion 25.2 V 2500 mAh; charger 29.4 V 1 A (PL: **M920001 only**),
about 2 h; IP21 (hand control IP67); 54 dBA; duty max 10%, 1 min continuous;
**10-year life with one battery replacement**; 3-year warranty; ISO 10535:2021.

## How it works (PL)

- On/Off switch, battery LED, overheat LED, charger and hand control connectors
  (hand control held by a cable clamp and Phillips screw).
- Strap and carabiner to the sling bar; high, low and slack-strap limit
  switches; an **emergency brake** that stops a fall if the transmission or
  motor fails.
- **Won't lift while the charger is connected.** Charger LED: green solid =
  powered, full or disconnected; yellow, orange or red = charging; **green
  blinking = wrong plugging order** (mostly M920001-X): plug into the wall
  first, then the lift. Charge monthly in storage.
- Track: Savaria telescopic, rigid-arm and fixed track, Handicare aluminum
  portable track, Arjo track (accessories differ). Scale kit M921422.

## Safety mechanisms

- **Emergency stop:** the On/Off switch (both lifts).
- **Manual lowering:** power off, 4 mm Allen key (supplied), turn as labelled;
  slow, many turns; then call the dealer.
- **Emergency brake engaged:** **don't press Up, don't release the brake and
  don't lower manually.** Switch off and call the dealer.
- Low battery: beeps and blinks on a button press; may stop lifting but still
  lowers.

## Maintenance (PL manual)

- **Operator, before every use:** charge, strap, sling and stitching,
  carabiner, buttons, track and end stops, runs freely. **Every 4 months:**
  clean inside the track, test the emergency stop. **Yearly:** casing, hooks,
  track, trolley wheels.
- **Dealer, yearly:** transmission, strap-to-accessory point, **limit switches**,
  emergency brake turns freely, manual lowering, **load test at SWL**. **Every
  2 years: replace the strap** and lubricate the roller, spool shaft and spool
  gear (the C-450/C-625 strap goes every 5000 cycles or 3 years).
- Track end stops refitted at **20 Nm (15 ft-lb)**.
- Clean with a damp cloth or 70% isopropyl; **no phenol, chlorine, bleach,
  acid, ammonium or solvents**.

## Troubleshooting (PL manual §7)

Don't open the case; only an authorized Savaria dealer may.

| Problem | Action |
|---|---|
| Starts and stops repeatedly | Overload: over the SWL |
| Beeps, won't lift but lowers | Charge the battery |
| No charging light | Outlet has power (charger green)? The battery may not need charging |
| Doesn't move | Switch on; battery charged; hand control plugged in; **charger unplugged**; switch off a few seconds and on |
| Battery LED stays on after a reset | A command is stuck: switch off, unplug the hand control, switch on; then dealer |
