# PUL-1100 (Bruno Out-Rider)

Pickup-truck-bed-mounted arm lift that swings an *empty* wheelchair or
scooter from ground level up and over the truck bed wall into the bed for
storage — not a rider lift. Link to [[Bruno]].

## Documents on file

- [[PUL-1100 Installation Manual]] — the only manual on file (no separate
  operator/tech-support guide)

## Specs

- Load ratings by actuator: 100 lb (light-duty), 200 lb (standard), 350 lb
  (heavy-duty motor)
- Shipped in 4 cartons: lift, base assembly, head/arm, docking device
- Base bolts through the vehicle bed floor (with or without a bed liner —
  different washer/hardware stack for each)
- Drive: 12VDC linear actuator (LA36) for arm angle + separate lift/belt
  motor

## Key procedures

- **Base + arm assembly**: base bolted to bed floor → lift base slid onto
  base assembly (torque 12 lbf-ft) → offset arm inserted into drive base,
  height-adjusted, cross-bolted → outer arm mounted to offset arm.
- **Power wiring**: see [[Vehicle-Lift-Power-Wiring]] (shared pattern with
  [[ASL-275]]).
- **Limit switches**: 2 cams (top=OUT stop, bottom=IN stop for driver-side;
  reversed for passenger-side) — wired black/red = top switch,
  green/white = bottom switch. Cam rotation direction and IN/OUT
  correspondence depends on which side of the vehicle the lift is
  installed on; verify by operating the lift before final cam rotation.
- **Control board tuning**: ADJ/AXIS/SEL buttons set acceleration,
  deceleration, and speed independently per motor (hoist, in/out, ext/ret);
  holding all three for 5s resets to factory defaults.
- **Loading/unloading**: pendant sequence is ARM up → OUT → ARM down →
  BELT down (engage docking device) → BELT up to ~2" below motor head
  (stop at the white sight-line stitching on the belt) → ARM up → IN →
  ARM down into the truck bed.
- **Emergency operation**: hand crank on the motor shaft — for returning
  the lift into the vehicle only (not full load/unload), so the vehicle
  can be driven after a power failure.

## Troubleshooting highlights

- Most "won't operate" causes are electrical: breaker/fuse, loose or
  corroded battery cable clamps, weak vehicle battery, missing control-board
  jumper.
- Grinding noise → defective motor/worn gears → replace motor (no field
  rebuild documented).
- Belt runs backwards (up switch lowers, down switch raises) → belt has
  wrapped around the spool backwards from over-running the lift down → run
  the lift down further until the belt re-seats correctly on the spool.
- Load-dependent backdrive at top of lift → likely exceeded rated load,
  worn gearbox, or wiring fault → contact dealer.
