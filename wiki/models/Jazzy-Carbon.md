# Jazzy Carbon (and Carbon 27, 27X, HD)

Pride's folding carbon-fiber travel chairs, **made by Zhejiang Innuovo**
(China) and sold retail (K0899).

| | Carbon | Carbon 27 | Carbon 27X | Carbon HD |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Capacity | 300 lb | 300 lb | 275 lb | 400 lb |
| Max speed | 3.7 mph | 3.7 | 3.7 | 3.7 |
| Battery | 24 V 12 Ah Li-ion | 24 V 12 Ah | 24 V 10 Ah | 24 V 20 Ah |
| Charger | 2 A | 2 A | 3.5 A | 2 A |
| Weight | 39.6 lb | 27.78 lb | 27.8 lb | 32.2 lb |

Controller: 35 A brushless (2026 retail chart, [[Pride-Power-Chair-Lineup]]).
The Carbon owner's manual adds: 18 kg without battery, battery 1.8 kg,
2 × 24 V 250 W motors, folded 720 × 310 × 610 mm, range ≥15 km, climbs
≥6°, solid tires 178 / 216 mm.

## Using and servicing it

- **Unfold:** footrest forward, pull seat and back apart until the
  latch **clicks onto the locking pin**; check it before sitting.
  **Fold:** pull up the rear latch, lift the cushion.
- **Brake (freewheel) lever on each motor: A = drive, B = push.**
  Never in push mode on a slope.
- Anti-tippers: retract indoors, extend to the outer notch for slopes.
- Joystick: power, battery (all bars OK; under 2 bars, charge), 5-step
  speed, horn. **Turns itself off after 20 min idle.**
- Battery under the seat; charges on or off the chair (3-pin). Red =
  charging, green = almost full, 1–2 h more (6–8 h total).
- Provider checks the controller settings every 6–12 months.

## Fault codes

**Carbon: beep 2 + n**: 1 joystick cable, 2/3 right motor/brake, 4/5
left motor/brake, 6 overcurrent or locked wheels, 7 joystick not
centered, 8 controller (manual: joystick), 9 battery. **Carbon 27 /
HD:** display icons for controller, joystick, right/left motor, motor
Hall, brake and battery faults. Full table:
[[Pride-Controller-Flash-Codes]].

## Warranty

Owner's manual: lifetime frame; **1 year** on controller, motor/drive,
bearings and **battery**. Pride's 2026 chart: Carbon 27 and HD 5-year
frame, 1-year electronics, motors and batteries ([[Pride-Warranty]]).
