# Permobil F Corpus (F3, F5, F5 Corpus VS)

Permobil's current **front-wheel-drive** power chairs, same platform and
manual template as [[Permobil-M-Corpus]]. User manuals only
([[Permobil F Corpus User Manuals (F3, F5, F5 Corpus VS)]]).

| | F3 Corpus | F5 Corpus | F5 Corpus VS (standing) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Max user | 330 lb | 330 lb | **300 lb** |
| Weight with batteries | 379 lb | 417 lb | 454 lb |
| Speed | 6 mph | 7.5 mph | 5.8 or 7.5 mph |
| Batteries | 2 × group 34 or 24 SLA | group 24 | group 24 |
| Turning diameter | 62 in | 59 in | 59 in |

## Service notes

- Drive tires **35 psi**; breaker, freewheel, tool bag and torques as on
  [[Permobil-M-Corpus]].
- **F5 VS seat depth rule:** a user **over 240 lb (110 kg) needs at least
  an 18 in (445 mm) seat depth**. If the user's weight goes over that on a
  shallower seat, it needs a service call. Chest support depth screws
  **24 Nm**. Never remove the support wheels.
- Batteries: **replace both as a matched set** (house standard,
  [[Mobility-Batteries]]); no replacement procedure in these manuals.
- Diagnostics: [[R-net-Diagnostics]]; ICS seating: [[ICS Onboard Configuration Technical Manual (205247, Ed 8)]].
