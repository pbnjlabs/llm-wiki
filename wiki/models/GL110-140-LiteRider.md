# LiteRider — GL110, GL140 (older generation)

Golden's mid-size travel scooter, older generation. GL110 = 3-wheel,
GL140 = 4-wheel. Superseded by [[GL111-141-LiteRider]] — same drivetrain
(transaxle part RA11E103 is still labeled for GL110/GL140 even in the
newer IPB), same 2×22AH battery pack layout, same Dynamics R-Series
controller family — the generational change looks mostly electronic/
cosmetic rather than a mechanical redesign.

## Documents on file

- [[LiteRider Service Guide (GL110, GL140)]] — troubleshooting,
  replacement procedures, IPB
- [[Battery Replacement (GL110, GL140, GP160)]] — also covers GP160 (not
  yet ingested)
- [[Brake Replacement (GL110, GL140)]]

No dedicated operator manual or IPB is on file for GL110/GL140 alone
(only bundled into the Service Guide).

## Key procedures

- **Diagnostics**: 9-code beep scheme, identical in content to
  [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]] (confirmed on the Companion line) — battery
  low/very low, high voltage, current-limit timeout, brake fault, paddle/
  throttle-neutral fault, paddle/speed-pot fault, motor voltage fault,
  controller fault.
- **Brake**: shared brake body with Buzzaround XL/Lite (GB106/116/146) —
  see [[GB1xx-Buzzaround-XL]] — model-specific handle only. Replacement is
  a ~15 min procedure with the drivetrain halves separated.
- **Battery replacement**: same 2-battery-pack design as GP160 LiteRider
  PTC (not yet ingested).
- **Voltmeter/ohmmeter diagnostics**: the Service Guide's Appendix A/B
  give generic multimeter usage instructions — likely reusable across any
  future Golden ingest that needs voltage/continuity troubleshooting.
- **"Quarter test"**: sliding a coin into a harness connector's spring
  contacts to check they hold firmly — a recurring low-tech continuity
  check in the Service Guide's beep-code procedures.
- **Transaxle replacement** (pre-April-2012 old style): mandatory paired
  controller swap — see [[Golden-Transaxle-Replacement]], shared cross-
  model doc also covering [[GB1xx-Buzzaround-XL]] (GB106/116/146).
