# SRE-2000 (Bruno Electra-Ride Elite — legacy)

Bruno's straight-rail **Electra-Ride Elite** from the 2000s, residential.
It came before the [[SRE-2010]] Elite. It is no longer sold, but units
are still in the field. Manuals were pulled from Brunopartner on
2026-10-22.

## Specs (2007 manuals)

- **400 lb**, **0–28 ft/min** variable (speed potentiometer on the
  carriage, clockwise = faster).
- 24 VDC 2-pole 1.02 hp motor, self-locking worm gearbox, rack and
  pinion. **No electric brake**: the worm gear holds the load.
- **45° maximum.** The rail is a vertical aluminum plate with an integral
  rack.
- Seat swivel **0°, 30°, 60° and 90°**, both ways, **top and bottom**.
- IR call/send, 9 V transmitters. **End charging** (yellow LED on each
  charge bracket).
- Overspeed optional.

## Install and service essentials

From [[SRE-2000 Installation Manual (2007)]]:
- **Rail length:** add 5 in with an overspeed. Cut from the lower end.
  No section under 18 in. Never cut the joint end.
- **Height:** **1¼ in above every nose** (1½ in with the extended
  footrest). The rail is vertical or leans back, **never forward**.
- **Clearance:** **2⅝ in from the back of the rail** to the nearest
  obstruction.
- **Clamps:** on the top landing too, except with the standard
  extension. Every third tread at least. A B-lock clamp is through-bolted
  M6.
- **Joint:** tighten the **4 M6 Allen screws into the rack first**.
- **Carriage angle:** 2 × M14 (22 mm).
- **4-gang DIP:** #1/#2 modes, #3 right-hand, **#4 coast delay
  (600 → 900 ms for remote dropouts)**.
- **Codes:**
  - 4 short + 1 long = off the charge bumper.
  - 3 short + 2 long = voltage drop.
  - 2 short + 3 long = critical (under 16 V).
  - Chirp every 3 s = seat safety.
  - 1 short + 2 long = remote memory full.
- **Charger AC fuse:** Buss GSL 2 (5 × 20 mm).
- **Grease:** the manual says white lithium; current Bruno guidance is
  PTFE on the rack ([[Stairlift-Maintenance]]).
- **Yearly:** includes checking the **drive belts** and rail wear.
- **Overspeed reset (two levers):**
  1. Rotate the overspeed switch lever while pushing the wires in.
  2. Turn the cam lever just until it engages.

  The test fixture and the "rotate the cam and reposition the pin" reset
  are on [[Tech Tip - Legacy SRE-1540, SRE-1550 and SRE-2000]].
