# SRE-5000 (Bruno Elan, 2026)

Bruno's newest straight-rail **Elan** stairlift (variants SRE-5000,
SRE-5000CAN, SRE-5000G). PMS sells and services it. **No install,
operator or tech support manual is on file yet**: this page is built
from Bruno's 2026 instruction sheets (ILS) and TechMate. The sheets refer
to an "SRE-5000 Technical Support Guide" and SRE-5000 installation
manuals, which are still needed (index: Missing manuals).

Like the [[SRE-3050]], Bruno says the SRE-5000 is **not for** users who
scoot-pivot transfer with all their weight on one armrest, or whose knees
buckle on the way down to the seat: **use the Elite ([[SRE-2010]])**.

## Specs (ILS-01617, 04/30/26)

- **Private residence only.** ASME A18.1-2023 Sec 7, CSA B44.1/A17.5-2025;
  5000CAN to CSA B355:24; 5000G self-certified to EN 81-40:2020. ETL
  control 4004689.
- **300 lb (136 kg)**, one passenger.
- 2 x 12 V **3.2 Ah** sealed lead-acid; charger 100–240 VAC 1 A in,
  24 VDC 1 A out; on/off switch; 12–24 h on batteries with power off.
- 24 VDC 12 A geared motor, right-angle worm gearbox, **0.29 hp max**;
  8DP gear rack in an aluminium rail; PWM controller with ramping.
- Braking: dynamic, worm gear, electromechanical brake, **overspeed
  protection**.
- **21 ft/min** max; **22–52°**.
- **IR** call/send (line of sight); 3-position paddle switch at the end of
  the armrest.
- Rail **1.5 in above the step nose** (3 in with height-adjustable clamps
  for folding rails); **5 in from the wall** recommended (folding rail
  6 in); rails **15'6" and 19'8"**, cut to fit.
- Seat stops 0°, 67° and 90° at the top; no swivel at the bottom.
- Safety: seat swivel switch, carriage safety switches, belt (latch switch
  on G/CAN), 25 A breaker, 5 A control fuse, **slow-down ramps and terminal
  switches with a top final terminal stop state** (no separate final limit
  switch), diagnostic alarm and board LEDs, barrier armrest switches (G/CAN),
  emergency manual drive (G/CAN), E-stop and alarm (CAN only).
- 153 lb packaged (typical 15'9" install).
- Options: keyed call/send, power swivel (to 85°), power footrest
  (**magnetic break-away**), manual or power folding rail.

Folding rail (ILS-01614): minimum gap under the rail **3.0 in (3.5 in on
carpet)**, 75 in minimum rail length, adjustable folding-rail support.

## Serial numbers

Product code **036** (e.g. SN 26080361090 = August 2026). The motor
controller PCB kit changes at that serial: **SRV-5000-018 up to
26080361089, SRV-5000-001 from 26080361090** ([[Bruno-Serial-Numbers]]).

## Service (from the ILS sheets)

Sources: [[ILS - SRE-5000 Service Sheets]], [[ILS - SRE-5000 Specifications]].

- **Remotes:** DIP 1/2 on each remote (default 1 up, 2 down; all remotes
  the same). Hold LEARN/ERASE 10 s to erase (both LEDs flash 3 s); hold it
  and tap a remote button to learn (green LED solid 3 s when done).
- **Charger LED:** none = no AC or bad charger; red = AC on; **red
  flashing = bad batteries** (load test); yellow/green = charging/OK;
  **green flashing = reverse polarity**.
- **Paddle switch** to the other arm: "R B G" letters toward the seat
  bottom. Low-profile rocker: **SRE-50198**.
- **Power folding rail:** ships in **continuous mode** (folds and unfolds
  automatically while the paddle is held; slows at the mid-park to check
  the rail). Mid-park stop mode is set per the SRE-5000 tech support guide
  (not on file). Actuator/gas spring and fold switches are replaceable
  (manual rail one switch, power rail two).
- **Wheels:** four, ¼ in hex shoulder bolts **17 Nm**. **Gear motor:**
  M1 brown, M2 blue; screws **14 Nm**. **PCB:** kit by serial (above);
  ESD strap.
- **Final terminal test** (ILS-01446): crank the motor (¼ in socket) until
  the safety flap presses on the end cap; the arm switch must not move it.
- Kits: flood sensor SRE-K-5042L/R, USB SRE-K-5043WG, armrest footrest
  switch SRE-K-5025 (NA) / SRE-K-5026 (global/CAN), adjustable stand feet
  SRE-K-5015WG and SRE-K-5039WG.
- **Door at the top of the stairs:** adjustable mount on the step below
  the top landing; rail bottom **7.5 in above the upper landing**.
