# SRE-5000 (Bruno Elan, 2026)

Bruno's newest straight-rail **Elan** stairlift (variants SRE-5000,
SRE-5000CAN, SRE-5000G). PMS sells and services it. The full manual set was pulled
from Brunopartner on 2026-10-22, along with Bruno's 2026 instruction
sheets (ILS) and TechMate.

Like the [[SRE-3050]], Bruno says the SRE-5000 is **not for** users who
scoot-pivot transfer with all their weight on one armrest, or whose knees
buckle on the way down to the seat: **use the Elite ([[SRE-2010]])**.

## Specs (ILS-01617, 04/30/26)

- **Private residence only.** ASME A18.1-2023 Sec 7, CSA B44.1/A17.5-2025;
  5000CAN to CSA B355:24; 5000G self-certified to EN 81-40:2020. ETL
  control 4004689.
- **300 lb (136 kg)**, one passenger.
- 2 x 12 V **3.2 Ah** sealed lead-acid; charger 100–240 VAC 1 A in,
  24 VDC 1 A out; on/off switch; 12–24 h on batteries with power off.
- 24 VDC 12 A geared motor, right-angle worm gearbox, **0.29 hp max**;
  8DP gear rack in an aluminium rail; PWM controller with ramping.
- Braking: dynamic, worm gear, electromechanical brake, **overspeed
  protection**.
- **21 ft/min** max; **22–52°**.
- **IR** call/send (line of sight); 3-position paddle switch at the end of
  the armrest.
- Rail **1.5 in above the step nose** (3 in with height-adjustable clamps
  for folding rails); **5 in from the wall** recommended (folding rail
  6 in); rails **15'6" and 19'8"**, cut to fit.
- Seat stops 0°, 67° and 90° at the top; no swivel at the bottom.
- Safety: seat swivel switch, carriage safety switches, belt (latch switch
  on G/CAN), 25 A breaker, 5 A control fuse, **slow-down ramps and terminal
  switches with a top final terminal stop state** (no separate final limit
  switch), diagnostic alarm and board LEDs, barrier armrest switches (G/CAN),
  emergency manual drive (G/CAN), E-stop and alarm (CAN only).
- 153 lb packaged (typical 15'9" install).
- Options: keyed call/send, power swivel (to 85°), power footrest
  (**magnetic break-away**), manual or power folding rail.

Folding rail (ILS-01614): minimum gap under the rail **3.0 in (3.5 in on
carpet)**, 75 in minimum rail length, adjustable folding-rail support.

## Install essentials (5000-I)

- **Rail length** = stair length + 10 in (20 in seat at park); add 1.5 in
  per extra inch of seat. Ramped for a top door: A + B + 3 in (ILS-01666).
  Segments at least 24 in; the "16 ft" kit is 2 × 93 in and the "20 ft"
  kit 2 × 118 in.
- **Mounts:**
  - On the first step up and the first step down. No more than 4 steps
    without one, and at least 3 on a one-piece rail.
  - Extra mounts for an overrun over 12 in, or for hard transfers (a drop
    of more than 4 in into the seat).
  - **M8 25 ft-lb.**
- **Clearances:**
  - Step nose **1.5 in (2.0 in on carpet)**.
  - **2⅜ in to the nearest obstruction**, or 5 in from the front of the
    rail to the wall.
  - Footrest to nose **0.5–1.0 in**.
- **DIP switches** under the small top cover:
  - **1 ON = single-user** (silences the seat-reminder beep).
  - **2 ON = right-hand.**
  - Both OFF from the factory (LH, multi-user). There is no jumper,
    whatever the checklist says.
- **Footrest and post: hex 50 ft-lb, flat-head 40 ft-lb.** Seat height 3
  in (2 in with power options) in 1 in steps.
- PTFE grease on the rack. About 2 h for a full charge. **It charges
  anywhere on the rail.**

## Serial numbers and PCB matching

Product code **036** (e.g. SN 26080361090 = August 2026). The **gearbox
ratio changed on 19 Aug 2026 (ECN8485)** and the firmware changed with it,
so the board must match the serial ([[SRE-5000 Tech Support Guide]]):

| Serial | Board | Kit |
|---|---|---|
| **26080361089 or earlier** | PCB-00180 rev P3 or PCB-00194 rev 0 | SRV-5000-018 |
| **26080361090 or later** | PCB-00180 **rev 1** | SRV-5000-001 |

- **New board in an old carriage:** fast down, slow mode, **sudden stops
  going down**. Worst at 45° or more, riders over 250 lb, or full
  batteries.
- **Old board in a new carriage:** slow down, relay clicking at the top
  while charging, charger fault.

See [[Bruno-Serial-Numbers]].

## Service (from the ILS sheets)

Sources: [[ILS - SRE-5000 Service Sheets]], [[ILS - SRE-5000 Specifications]].

- **Remotes:** DIP 1/2 on each remote (default 1 up, 2 down; all remotes
  the same). Hold LEARN/ERASE 10 s to erase (both LEDs flash 3 s); hold it
  and tap a remote button to learn (green LED solid 3 s when done).
- **Charger LED:** none = no AC or bad charger; red = AC on; **red
  flashing = bad batteries** (load test); yellow/green = charging/OK;
  **green flashing = reverse polarity**.
- **Paddle switch** to the other arm: "R B G" letters toward the seat
  bottom. Low-profile rocker: **SRE-50198**.
- **Power folding rail:**
  - Ships in **continuous mode**: it folds and unfolds while the paddle
    is held, and slows at mid-park to check the rail.
  - **Mid-park stop mode** is set with the DIP switch on the fold circuit
    board (push-pin cover). **The remote always stops at mid-park.**
  - **Dead actuator:** pull the quick-connect pin, unplug the leads,
    lower the rail by hand and zip-tie it down.
  - **Obstruction sensitivity:** set screw under the lift-arm end cap,
    3 mm key, **clockwise = more sensitive**.
  - The actuator, gas spring and fold switches are replaceable (one
    switch on the manual rail, two on the power rail).
  - Install: fold section at least 75 in; rail **at least 3 in off the
    first nose and 6 in from the wall**; **set LH/RH before driving onto
    a fold rail** ([[SRE-5000 Folding Rail Install Guide]]).
- **Remotes dead, paddle fine:** the motor leads are too close to the IR
  sensor; re-route them.
- **Wheels:** four, ¼ in hex shoulder bolts **17 Nm**. **Gear motor:**
  M1 brown, M2 blue; screws **14 Nm**. **PCB:** kit by serial (above);
  ESD strap.
- **Final terminal test** (ILS-01446): crank the motor (¼ in socket) until
  the safety flap presses on the end cap; the arm switch must not move it.
- Kits: flood sensor SRE-K-5042L/R, USB SRE-K-5043WG, armrest footrest
  switch SRE-K-5025 (NA) / SRE-K-5026 (global/CAN), adjustable stand feet
  SRE-K-5015WG and SRE-K-5039WG.
- **Door at the top of the stairs:** adjustable mount on the step below
  the top landing; rail bottom **7.5 in above the upper landing**.

## Troubleshooting

- **Full code table:** [[SRE-5000 Tech Support Guide]] (diagnostics Rev 7,
  green and red LEDs plus tones).
- **Main faults:**
  - C-L: terminal switches.
  - C-C-L: both safeties.
  - C-C-C: over 55 °C.
  - C-C-C-C: over 16 A up or 4.25 A down.
  - **B-B-L-L: below 19.2 V.**
  - B-B-B-L-L: no charger.
  - C-C-C-L: final limit.
  - C-C: controller failure.
  - 5 × C: low-voltage stop.
- Battery voltage should be **16–28 VDC**.
- **Conflict:** the install manual gives "battery critical" as 2 short + 3
  long. The newer TSG table (B-B-L-L) wins.
- TechMate covers the symptoms: see [[Stairlift-Troubleshooting]]
  (sections 2, 4, 9 and 13).
