# Quantum Outback

Quantum's **four-motor outdoor** power chair. From the 2025 Quantum
chart ([[Pride-Power-Chair-Lineup]]): 300 lb with power positioning,
350 lb with a captain's seat; 5 mph (6 mph no-charge option);
**Group 27** batteries; **120 A Q-Logic 3**; HCPCS K0899; **not on the
VA FSS contract**. No spec sheet or owner's manual on file.

## Service notes

- Seats: TRU-Balance 3 REAC lift or TRU Balance Flex (seat to
  interface 35–44 N·m). The seat **props back** for access to the
  motors, terminal block and light module.
- **Terminal block** joins the four motors to the harness: **right
  motors to the middle section, left motors to the bottom section**.
  Photograph the ring terminals before taking it apart; it must go back
  exactly.
- Front end: sway bar and bushings, front frame and motor mount
  (**80–88 N·m**), **tie rod** (30–35 N·m). Rear: sway bar, control
  arms (18–22 N·m).
- Lights: front light pods (3–4 N·m, **hand tools only**), light
  harness and module. Backup camera and AM3/AM3L options.
- Lug nuts 25.8–28.5 N·m; battery fuse screw 3.7–4.1 N·m.
- The motor-mount nut torque is misprinted (19–22 N·m beside 11.8–16.2
  ft·lb); confirm with Quantum.
