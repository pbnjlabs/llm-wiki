# Permobil K450 (MX seat)

Pediatric power chair with the **MX seat**: seat lift, **seat-to-floor**,
optional tilt, ICS switchbox. **125 lb** max user, seat widths 10–16 in.
Source: [[Permobil K450 Owner's Manual (205230, 2020)]].

## Service notes

- Tires: front 210 × 65 at **36 psi (250 kPa)**, rear 13 × 5.00-6 at
  **17–29 psi (120–200 kPa)**. Safe slope **6°**.
- **Main fuse** (63 A, resettable, also the battery isolator) is
  **between the front wheels**, from the front.
- **Batteries** (group 34 gel, 60 Ah), one each side behind upper and
  lower side covers; fuse OFF first, ON after. **Replace both as a matched
  set** ([[Mobility-Batteries]]).
- **Seat stuck?** The supplied **emergency operation cable** goes into
  the charging socket and the seat module (behind the rear bumper and
  back cover). It bypasses every safety function; never drive with it on.
- No driving at floor level; the 16 in seat doesn't reach the floor.
  Crush strips stop and reverse the seat for 1.5 s.
- ICS: transport (WC19) mode, seat width/armrest and actuator calibration
  in [[ICS Onboard Configuration Technical Manual (205247, Ed 8)]].
  R-net: [[R-net-Diagnostics]].
