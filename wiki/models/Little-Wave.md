# Little Wave and Clik (pediatric rigid)

Ki Mobility's **pediatric rigid** chairs.

| Model | Capacity | Transit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Little Wave** | 165 lb (75 kg) | **125 lb** | 11 kg (24 lb); 5th wheel option |
| **Little Wave Clik** | 165 lb (75 kg) | 165 lb | **Grows with the user** (growing seat pan and sling); remove the canopy for occupied transit |

- Tech manuals: camber mount, COG, toe to zero, caster squaring and **caster flutter**, frame width (Little Wave), 5th wheel, growing seat. Retaining screws 80 in-lb (balanced tightening), set screws 40 in-lb.
