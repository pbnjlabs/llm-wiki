# Ultra X (S39 / S49 / SC493)

Pride travel scooter: **S39 = 3-wheel; S49 and SC493 = 4-wheel**.
Single motor. Spec sheet and the travel scooter owner's manual ([[Ultra X Specifications (INFINFB3702)]],
Rev J, Aug 2026).

| | S39 | S49 | SC493 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capacity | 300 lb | 300 lb | 300 lb |
| Max speed | 4.3 mph | 4.3 mph | 4.3 mph |
| Ground clearance | 1.25 in at motor | same | same |
| Turning radius | 36.5 in | 52.25 in | 48.75 in |
| Reversing width | 40.75 in | 42 in | 58 in |
| Length | 37 in | 40.25 in | 41.5 in |
| Width | 19.25 in | 19.25 in | 19.25 in |
| Tires (solid) | 8 in front and rear | 8 in front and rear | 7 in front, 8 in rear |
| Range (12 Ah, US standard) | 6.2 mi at 300 lb, 8 at 200 | 6.6 / 8.9 mi | 7.1 mi at 300 lb, 8.9 at 220 |
| Range (18 Ah, international) | — | 10.6 mi at 300 lb, 14.2 at 200 | — |
| Weight without batteries (with seat) | 76.25 lb | 81.5 lb | 83.4 lb |
| Heaviest piece (front section) | 29.5 lb | 34.5 lb | 36.2 lb |
| Overall height | 33.5 in | 34.75 in | 34.75 in |
| Climb | 2.25 in | 2.25 in | 1.97 in |

All: molded seat 17 × 17 in, 23 lb; seat height 14 in, seat-to-deck
16.5–18 in; rear-wheel drive, **single 24 V motor**; regenerative +
electromechanical brakes; two 12 V sealed batteries, **12 Ah standard
in the US** (20 lb pack each), 18 Ah standard internationally (29.5 lb
each); **2 A** off-board charger; max slope 6°. **Class A only with the
18 Ah batteries.**
