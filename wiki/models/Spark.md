# Spark (small folding)

Ki Mobility's small **folding** chair, **165 lb (75 kg)**, with pediatric options (pediatric T-arm and ELR, canopy, hemi wheel lock, one-arm drive). 13 kg (28.7 lb) as tested. Transit section present. Service in the [[Catalyst and Spark Technical Manual (DCN0485.16)]] (Spark frame width and depth tables).
