# GP304 Ally Pro (Golden)

The larger member of the folding **Ally** family: same electronics,
app, folding latch and fault codes as the [[GP303-Ally]], plus a
reclining back and adjustable arm width. Link to [[Golden]].

## Documents on file

- [[Ally Pro Owners Manual (GP304)]] (Rev. C, Jan 2025)
- [[Ally Pro Illustrated Parts Breakdown (GP304)]] (Rev. B, Nov 2024): own controller MBE-0068 and
  motors; joystick, battery and charger shared with the GP303

## Specs

**330 lb**, 5 mph, **13.1 mi**, 12 Ah lithium (5.4 lb), 40 A, 38.9 ×
25.2 in, 37.4 in turning radius. **75 lb assembled, heaviest piece
69.6 lb.** 10 in pneumatic drive wheels and **10 in omni-directional
casters**. Seat 21.1 × 16.9 in.

## Service notes

- Fault codes, voice prompts and the app: [[Ally-Fault-Codes]]. The
  manual's code-90 text says 265 lb; the spec is 330 lb.
- Recline: pull the two pins on the lower back; check they relock.
- Freewheel: two levers at the back, **down** = unlocked ([[Freewheel-Mode]]).
