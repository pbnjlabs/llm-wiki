# Pinnacle SL300

Harmar's entry-level straight-rail residential stairlift. 300 lb max
payload. **Residential only** — the warranty explicitly excludes both
outdoor and commercial installations (contrast with [[Pinnacle-SL600]],
which is commercial-rated).

## Options
- **Folding Rail** (model suffix SL300FR) — chassis-mounted obstruction
  sensors at top/bottom of the folding section; automatically activates
  near the hinge point during travel, returns to vertical/charging
  position when parked
- **Power Swivel** — swivel integrated into the primary armrest control
  instead of manual levers; a resettable fuse trips after 5s of continued
  input at a stop (1-min reset wait)
- **Power Footrest** — rocker switch under the armrest; self-resetting
  fuse on obstruction (30s reset wait)

Standard units use manual seat-swivel levers (85° max rotation, 45°
intermediate latch) and a manual folding footrest.

## Diagnostics
Chassis On/Off switch; beeps-only fault indication (no dedicated status
light distinct from operation). Major-fault beep table 1-8 (no "final
limit switch" code, unlike Helix/SL600's 1-9) — see
[[Harmar-Stairlift-Beep-Codes]]. Manual override: 1/4" **or** 7mm socket
at the motor shaft, "depending on your model" (not otherwise
disambiguated in this manual).
