# Pinnacle SL300

Harmar's entry-level straight-rail residential stairlift. 300 lb max
payload. **Residential only** — the warranty explicitly excludes both
outdoor and commercial installations (contrast with [[Pinnacle-SL600]],
which is commercial-rated).

## Options
- **Folding Rail** (model suffix SL300FR) — chassis-mounted obstruction
  sensors at top/bottom of the folding section; automatically activates
  near the hinge point during travel, returns to vertical/charging
  position when parked. Rail handing (left/right) is field-reversible —
  see [[Pinnacle SL300 Install Manual (2026, Rev L)]] for the full
  gas-strut/link-arm reversal procedure.
- **Power Swivel** (SKU SL300-PS) — swivel integrated into the primary
  armrest control instead of manual levers; ~5s full swivel time, a
  resettable fuse trips after 5s of continued input at a stop (2-min
  reset wait per the install supplement, though the owner's manual says
  1 min)
- **Power Footrest** (SKU SL300-PF; both together SL300-PSPF) — rocker
  switch under the armrest; self-resetting fuse on obstruction (30s reset
  wait). **Uses the SL600's adjustable footrest structure**, not SL300's
  own standard footrest, when this option is fitted. Control wiring must
  be manually re-routed to switch it from right- to left-hand armrest
  (SL600, by contrast, ships pre-wired to both) — see
  [[Pinnacle Power Swivel and Power Footrest Install Supplement]]

Standard units use manual seat-swivel levers (85° max rotation, 45°
intermediate latch) and a manual folding footrest. Standard/current rail
sections are 7'9" with a 7" recommended overrun (3'/5'/7'9" mid-sections
available) — set as the baseline by a 2018 bulletin, see
[[Bulletin - Pinnacle Rail Length Change]].

Two interchangeable motor suppliers are in circulation (grey/SEI, 7mm
manual-override hex; black/MDI, 6mm hex) — check which is fitted before
grabbing a manual-override socket. See
[[Bulletin - Pinnacle Seat Footrest Supply Chain Changes]].

## Diagnostics
Chassis On/Off switch; beeps-only fault indication (no dedicated status
light distinct from operation). Major-fault beep table 1-8 (no "final
limit switch" code, unlike Helix/SL600's 1-9) — see
[[Harmar-Stairlift-Beep-Codes]]. Manual override: 1/4" **or** 7mm socket
at the motor shaft, "depending on your model" (not otherwise
disambiguated in this manual).
