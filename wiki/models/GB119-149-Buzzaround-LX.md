# Buzzaround Luxury / LX (GB119, GB149)

The **Buzzaround Luxury**: the [[GB118-148-Buzzaround-EX]] platform with
a 375 lb rating and a high-back seat. **GB119 = 3-wheel, GB149 =
4-wheel.** Link to [[Golden]].

## Service notes

- **Flash/beep codes 1–9:** [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]].
- **Tiller fuse panel** (center of the tiller): **1 A** top = dash,
  lights and power; **2 A** middle = USB; **10 A** bottom = XLR charge
  port. Check these for a dead dash or a scooter that won't charge.
- Freewheel: lever **forward toward N** = freewheel ([[Freewheel-Mode]]).
