# Jazzy 600 ES

Pride Group 2 mid-wheel power chair. From the 2026
[[Pride Group 2 Power Chair End-to-End Chart (INFINFB4550, 2026)]]:
**300 lb**, 4 mph, **2 × NF-22** (37 lb each), **5 A** charger, **50 A
Dynamic** electronics, HCPCS K0822/K0823.

## Documents

- [[Jazzy 600 ES Parts Replacement Guide (INFMANU4844)]] (Rev A, 2017)
  — the only service document on file. No owner's manual or
  troubleshooting guide.

## Service notes (PRG)

Same base layout and torque values as the original [[Q6-Edge]]: front
battery door, rear electronics door, center panel, top shroud,
articulating beam, hub motors with an axle key and freewheel linkage.

- Seating: Contour seat, TB3 seat frame or Synergy seat (screws
  27.1 N·m / 35.3 N·m).
- **Run-flat drive wheel: don't take it apart**, replace the assembly.
  Lug nuts 25 N·m (18.4 ft·lb).
- Hub nut 51 N·m; motor to bracket 7 N·m; caster nut 27–34 N·m;
  articulating beam 40 N·m.
- Battery wiring diagram inside the front battery door; replace both
  batteries.

Controller codes: Dynamic service indicator 1–11
([[Pride-Controller-Flash-Codes]]). The [[Jazzy-614]] troubleshooting
guide is the closest Dynamic dealer guide on file.
