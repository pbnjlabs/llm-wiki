# Jazzy 600 ES

Pride Group 2 mid-wheel power chair. From the 2026
[[Pride Group 2 Power Chair End-to-End Chart (INFINFB4550, 2026)]]:
**300 lb**, 4 mph, **2 × NF-22** (37 lb each), **5 A** charger, **50 A
Dynamic** electronics, HCPCS K0822/K0823.

## Documents

- Retailer part numbers (Mobility Scooters Direct, 2026-10-27; confirm by serial with the manufacturer): [[Parts Catalog - Mobility Scooters Direct (Pride)#Jazzy 600 ES Parts|Jazzy 600 ES Parts]] (18)
- [[Jazzy 600 ES Parts Replacement Guide (INFMANU4844)]] (Rev A, 2017)
  — the only service document on file. No troubleshooting guide.
- [[Jazzy 600 ES Owners Manual (INFMANU4480)]] (Rev D, 2020):
  freewheel = push both levers **down**; seatback 90°, 102°, 105° or
  107°; care on [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]].

## Service notes (PRG)

Same base layout and torque values as the original [[Q6-Edge]]: front
battery door, rear electronics door, center panel, top shroud,
articulating beam, hub motors with an axle key and freewheel linkage.

- Seating: Contour seat, TB3 seat frame or Synergy seat (screws
  27.1 N·m / 35.3 N·m).
- **Run-flat drive wheel: don't take it apart**, replace the assembly.
  Lug nuts 25 N·m (18.4 ft·lb).
- Hub nut 51 N·m; motor to bracket 7 N·m; caster nut 27–34 N·m;
  articulating beam 40 N·m.
- Battery wiring diagram inside the front battery door; replace both
  batteries.

## Motor harness and codes (TechnoBulletin JAZZY-176, 2013)

- Power module **M1 → left motor, M2 → right motor**, through
  intermediate harnesses. Battery harness routing: JAZZY-175 (figure).
- The bulletin's Dynamic table numbers the motors and brakes **the
  other way round from the Jazzy 614 guides**: **3 = right motor,
  4 = left motor, 5 = right brake, 6 = left brake** (the 614 has 3/5
  left, 4/6 right). The rest match: 1 stall/user, 2 battery, 7 joystick
  module, 8 power module, 9 communications, 10 unknown, 11 incompatible.
  On a 600 ES, go by this table; if a side doesn't check out, test the
  other one too.
- Source: [[Pride TechnoBulletins (2006-2024)]]. Dynamic dealer steps
  per code: [[Jazzy-614]].

## Transit loops

Unoccupied-transit securement loops replace the trapeze-bar screws
(M10 × 30 front, M10 × 55 rear, **35 N·m**); fit the "not rated for
occupied transport" decals ([[Jazzy 600 ES Transit Loops (INFINFB3245)]]).
