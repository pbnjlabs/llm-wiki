# GP205 Alante Sport (Golden), with the GP206 / GP207 Alanté

A **front-wheel-drive** power chair in the Alanté family. It is named in
the [[Golden-Wheelchair-Warranty]] and uses the Pro-Flex "SMP" seat code
([[Golden-Seating-Systems]]). The 2012 service guide was filed under
"Discontinued Models", but Golden's **2021 owner's manual** still covers
it alongside the **GP206 and GP207 Alanté**, which have no page of their
own: this page covers all three. The later **GP208** "Alanté Sport" is a
different chair: [[GP208-Alante-Sport]]. Link to [[Golden]].

## Documents on file

- [[Alante Sport Service Guide (GP205)]] (2012)
- [[Alante Series Owners Manual (GP205, GP206, GP207)]] (Aug 2021)

## GP205 / GP206 / GP207 specs (2021 OM)

One spec sheet for all three, each with a CC (captain's) or SS (van/pan)
seat: 300 lb, 3.5 mph, 16.5 mi, 1.75 in clearance, 25 in turning radius,
2-U1 (22 lb each), **PG VR2 50 A**, 39.5 × 22.25 in. **157 lb (CC) /
154 lb (SS) assembled**: base 67, seat 46 / 43. 9 in drive wheels, 6 in
casters, 4 in front anti-tips. Medicare: GP205 K0823 / K0822, GP206 not
coded, GP207 K0816 / K0815. Freewheel: levers **forward** = freewheel,
**back** = drive (the GP208 is the reverse; [[Freewheel-Mode]]).
See [[Mobility-Device-Weights]].

## What's confirmed

- **PG VR2, 50 A** power module; swivel seat.
- **Two U1 12V 35 Ah** batteries.
- Flash codes 1–10 and the TruCharge gauge: [[TruCharge-Diagnostics]].
  Programmer codes: [[PG-Drives-Trip-Codes]].
- Field-replaceable parts: drive wheel, rear caster, motor/brake, power
  module, batteries. A full IPB is in the guide.
