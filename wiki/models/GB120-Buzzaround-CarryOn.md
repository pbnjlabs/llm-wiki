# Buzzaround Carry On (GB120, GB220)

Golden's smallest/lightest travel scooter, designed around airline travel
— quick-release lithium battery packs rather than a fixed or dock-charged
battery. A service guide, an owner's manual and an IPB are on file. The **GB220 Carry On HD** (400 lb) shares the owner's manual.
Not to be confused with the **GP130 Carry On power chair**
([[GP130-CarryOn-Power-Chair]]).

## Sell-sheet specs (Sep 2025)

From [[Golden Sell Sheets (2018-2026)]]: 300 lb, 3.6 mph, 12.5 Ah + 6.5 Ah spare lithium (14.4 + 7.5
mi), **65.5 lb assembled**, heaviest piece (main body) 47.5 lb, folds to
12 in high without the seat. See [[Mobility-Device-Weights]].

Both: 12.5 Ah and 6.5 Ah lithium packs, 28.7 in wheelbase, 48.75 in
turning radius, about 21.7 mi with both packs. Warranty lifetime frame,
**2 yr drive train, electronics and batteries**. The **fuse ships
loose**: fit it under the controller cover beneath the seat pedestal at
setup. Freewheel is a **switch** that works only with a battery in
([[Freewheel-Mode]]). Fault codes 1–9 flash on the battery meter with
horn beeps ([[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]]).

## Specs

- Two lithium-ion battery pack options (button-release, quick-swap):
  - 15AH / 360WH (PN MBE-BATT-15LITH) — **not** air-safe for carry-on
  - 6.5AH / 156WH (PN MBE-BATT-65LITH) — air-safe, subject to airline
    policy
  - Both: 24-29V, 10A working current, 80% capacity retained after 300
    charge cycles

## Electrical ([[Buzzaround Carry On Service Guide (GB120)]])

- **Dynamics R-Series** controller (under a cover). Beep/flash codes
  1–9: [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]].
- One 24 V lithium pack (15 Ah or 6.5 Ah; the Sep 2025 sell sheet says
  **12.5 Ah** for the large pack, so check the label when ordering) on two spring-loaded
  connectors in the centre of the deck. It reads about 29 V when full.
- **35 A** battery fuse and a **3 A** fuse. Motor 0.8–1.5 Ω.
- Field-replaceable parts include the transaxle, upper and lower main
  harness, steering assembly and **tiller shock**.

## Key procedures

- **Airline travel**: notify airline in advance, power off, remove
  battery pack(s), stow in protective carrying covers; max 2 packs
  carried on, each ≤160WH; pilot must be informed of battery location.
- **Kickstand install** (optional accessory): relocates the front basket
  mount bracket on the seat pedestal (see
  [[GB120 Kickstand Installation Guide]]) — changes how the unit is
  stabilized during later service work (stand on kickstand vs. flip
  upside down).
- **Tiller replacement** (MBB-120A-TA-COMPLETE, 17 steps): full stem swap
  disconnecting the main wiring harness under the tiller base. Reassembly
  orientation is critical — the lower steering stem's 4 cross-notches
  must align with the tiller while the steering tab points rearward and
  handlebar is centered, or steering will be misaligned. Tiller bolt gets
  medium-strength thread locker.
