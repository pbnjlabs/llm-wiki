# GP204 (Alante DX) and GP215 (Alante LT)

**Discontinued** power wheelchairs. The **GP215 Alante LT** is a sibling
with smaller batteries. The service guide's IPB calls the Alante DX the
**"Compass Jr"**, so expect that name on parts and older paperwork. Link
to [[Golden]].

## Electrical ([[Alante DX and LT Service Guide (GP204, GP215)]])

- **PG VR2** controller (joystick 3023-0021-01, power module
  3023-0020-07), 18–24 VDC.
- Batteries: **Alante DX two 22 Ah** (WP22-12NE); **Alante LT two 18 Ah**.
- **40 A** circuit breaker (P3026-0033-01).
- Flash codes 1–10 and the TruCharge gauge: [[TruCharge-Diagnostics]];
  programmer codes: [[PG-Drives-Trip-Codes]]. Motor under 5 Ω.

## Documents on file

- [[Alante DX and LT Service Guide (GP204, GP215)]] (2012): troubleshooting,
  charging, replacement steps, IPB
- [[Alante DX ELR Installation (GP204)]]: 1-page ELR install

## Notes

- The standard-seat ELR install uses a receiver-tube-and-hanger system
  (mount tube under seat, hang ELRs, swing inward to lock). For optional
  (non-standard) seat sizes, the document redirects to hardware shared
  with the **Alante GP201** and **Compass Sport GP605** — same legrest
  parts family across those three models. See
  [[Compass Sport ELR Installation (GP605)]] for the parallel GP605
  procedure.
- The [[Golden-Wheelchair-Warranty]] document names "**Alante GP207**"
  and "**Alante Sport GP205**" as covered models, not "GP204" — likely
  the same Alante family under different/newer part numbers, but not
  confirmed identical to GP204. Don't assume GP204 is covered by that
  warranty text without checking a GP204-specific source.
