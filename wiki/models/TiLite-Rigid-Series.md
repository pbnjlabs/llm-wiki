# TiLite Rigid Series

Permobil's **TiLite** rigid-frame (mostly titanium) manual wheelchairs, made by
TiSport LLC in Pasco, WA. One service manual covers the **Z, ZRA, ZR, Aero T,
TRA and TR**; the **Twist** has its own owner's manual. The [[TiLite-CR1]] is
covered on its own page.

## Transit ratings

| Model | Occupied transit |
|---|---|
| **Z** | Yes, with the factory Transit Tie-Down Option (ISO 7176-19:2022) |
| **ZRA** | Yes, with the option (ISO 7176-19:2008, WC19) |
| **Twist** | Yes, with the option (ISO 7176-19); 165 lb limit, **20 lb less in transit** |
| ZR, Aero T, TRA, TR | **No**: never occupied in a vehicle |

The **Transport** Tie-Down Option is for strapping down an **empty** chair only.
Check the stickers to tell the two options apart. See [[Transport-and-Tie-Downs]].

## Torques (service manual)

| Fastener | Torque |
|---|---|
| Footrest clamp Allen screws | 10 Nm |
| Backrest button heads | 10 Nm |
| Push handles (integrated or fold-down) | 5 Nm |
| Camber tube and camber mount clamps (Z) | 10 Nm |
| Camber set screw | 4 Nm |
| Axle sleeve | 26 Nm |
| Caster wedge Allen screw (6 mm socket) | 25 Nm |
| Twist outer screw | 7 Nm |

## Rules and checks

- **No water-based lubricants** near caster forks or rear axles.
- **Thread-patch fasteners: reuse at most twice**, then replace or use **Loctite
  242** on the last three threads.
- Footrests at least **5 cm (2 in)** off the ground, at least **4.5 cm** of
  extension tube inside the frame.
- Wheel stop **embeds at least 5 mm** into a tire at rated pressure.
- Casters identical both sides; reset **toe** after moving the camber tube.
- Serial label on the under-seat rigidizer tube.
- ZR3 and TR4 anti-tip removal / COG bumper supplements:
  [[TiLite Anti-Tip Removal and COG Bumper Supplements (ZR3, TR4)]].
