# VPL-3200B

Bruno's larger battery-operated vertical platform lift, model number
**VPL-3210B**. Built for multi-floor hoistway installs — max floor-to-floor
travel 123" (3.1m), vs. the smaller [[VPL-3100B]]'s 53"/75". Same basic
platform-lift architecture (Acme screw + drive nut drive, 750 lb capacity)
but scaled up: 1 HP motor (vs 1/2 HP), 2×34AH batteries standard (vs
2×17AH), heavier (1210 lb with batteries vs ~700-900 lb), and a
significantly more complex install.

## Spec summary

- Load capacity: 750 lbs (340 kg)
- Motor: 1 HP, 24VDC (2 HP peak); Acme screw + drive nut drive
- Batteries: 2×12VDC 34AH standard (main), plus a separate 12VDC 7.5AH
  battery (Bruno part BTR-12002) dedicated to the electrical emergency
  lowering kit
- Max floor-to-floor: 123" (3.1m) — a multi-story hoistway lift, not just
  a porch/deck lift
- Platform: 36"×48" standard, up to 36"×60" optional
- Emergency operation: **electrical emergency lowering kit (keyed switch,
  mounted outside the hoistway at the lower landing) *and* manual hand
  wheel** — the 3100B has only the hand wheel
- Regulatory: same as [[VPL-3100B]] (ASME A18.1 Sec.5, CSA B355, FDA Class
  II Patient Transporter)

## Key procedures

- **Tower assembly is the standout install difference from the 3100B**:
  the VPL-3200B tower ships as two half-towers that must be joined on site
  with a hoist. The two drive-screw halves are connected via a **coiled
  spring pin installation** — align red marks on each screw half, clean
  holes with isopropyl alcohol only, tap coiled pins in partway, apply
  retaining compound, press to controlled depth with a supplied insertion
  tool, verify pin depth with a gauge. This is a precision, safety-critical
  joint (drive screw = the load path) with its own dedicated kit
  (VPL-K-3218).
- **Top landing gate installation**: identical procedure to the
  [[VPL-3100B]] — see [[Top-Landing-Gate-Installation]].
- **Electrical emergency lowering**: open protective cover on main power
  switch, insert key, turn/hold to LOWER, release at desired stop, remove
  key at AUTO only (key auto-returns to OFF and stops the lift on
  release). Explicitly interchangeable with the manual hand wheel — either
  method safely moves a user to the lower-level exit during a failure.
- **PCB-00164 indicator lights**: a dedicated LED status board (BOT
  TIMER/TOP TIMER/DOWN/UP/DWN IN/UP IN/BOT LAND/TOP LAND/KEY PRIO/SAFETIES)
  used for on-the-spot diagnosis — e.g. "no lights" = E-stop, top cover,
  nut safety switch, pit switch, or a final limit switch open; "bottom 4
  LEDs on" = all safeties closed/OK. Not present on the simpler 3100B.
- **Battery charger diagnostics**: see [[Battery-Charger-LED-Diagnostics]]
  (identical table to 3100B).
- **Cold weather / drive-nut warning**: same as [[VPL-3100B]].

## Quirks / notes

- **Flood-zone unit**: per the operator manual, will not operate with more
  than 3" of standing water at the base — not mentioned in the 3100B docs.
- Two separate battery systems: main drive batteries (2×34AH) and a
  dedicated emergency-lowering battery (12VDC 7.5AH) — don't confuse them
  when troubleshooting a "won't lower" complaint; check both.
- Preventive maintenance and the troubleshooting table are otherwise
  near-identical to the 3100B's.
