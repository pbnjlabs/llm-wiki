# VPL-3200B

Bruno's larger battery-operated vertical platform lift, model number
**VPL-3210B**. Built for multi-floor hoistway installs — max floor-to-floor
travel 123" (3.1m), vs. the smaller [[VPL-3100B]]'s 53"/75". Same basic
platform-lift architecture (Acme screw + drive nut drive, 750 lb capacity)
but scaled up: 1 HP motor (vs 1/2 HP), 2×34AH batteries standard (vs
2×17AH), heavier (1210 lb with batteries vs ~700-900 lb; Bruno's 2026
spec sheet ILS-00986 says **1190 lb** with batteries), and a
significantly more complex install.

## Spec summary

- Load capacity: 750 lbs (340 kg)
- Motor: 1 HP, 24VDC (2 HP peak); Acme screw + drive nut drive
- Batteries: 2×12VDC 34AH standard (main), plus a separate 12VDC 7.5AH
  battery (Bruno part BTR-12002) dedicated to the electrical emergency
  lowering kit
- Max floor-to-floor: 123" (3.1m) — a multi-story hoistway lift, not just
  a porch/deck lift
- Platform: 36"×48" standard, up to 36"×60" optional
- Emergency operation: **electrical emergency lowering kit (keyed switch,
  mounted outside the hoistway at the lower landing) *and* manual hand
  wheel** — the 3100B has only the hand wheel
- Regulatory: same as [[VPL-3100B]] (ASME A18.1 Sec.5, CSA B355, FDA Class
  II Patient Transporter)

## Key procedures

- **Tower assembly is the standout install difference from the 3100B**:
  the VPL-3200B tower ships as two half-towers that must be joined on site
  with a hoist. The two drive-screw halves are connected via a **coiled
  spring pin installation** — align red marks on each screw half, clean
  holes with isopropyl alcohol only, tap coiled pins in partway, apply
  retaining compound, press to controlled depth with a supplied insertion
  tool, verify pin depth with a gauge. This is a precision, safety-critical
  joint (drive screw = the load path) with its own dedicated kit
  (VPL-K-3218).
- **Top landing gate installation**: identical procedure to the
  [[VPL-3100B]] — see [[Top-Landing-Gate-Installation]].
- **Electrical emergency lowering**: open protective cover on main power
  switch, insert key, turn/hold to LOWER, release at desired stop, remove
  key at AUTO only (key auto-returns to OFF and stops the lift on
  release). Explicitly interchangeable with the manual hand wheel — either
  method safely moves a user to the lower-level exit during a failure.
- **PCB-00164 indicator lights**: a dedicated LED status board (BOT
  TIMER/TOP TIMER/DOWN/UP/DWN IN/UP IN/BOT LAND/TOP LAND/KEY PRIO/SAFETIES)
  used for on-the-spot diagnosis — e.g. "no lights" = E-stop, top cover,
  nut safety switch, pit switch, or a final limit switch open; "bottom 4
  LEDs on" = all safeties closed/OK. Not present on the simpler 3100B.
- **Battery charger diagnostics**: see [[Battery-Charger-LED-Diagnostics]]
  (identical table to 3100B).
- **Cold weather / drive-nut warning**: same as [[VPL-3100B]].

## Troubleshooting

Start with [[VPL-Troubleshooting]]. VPL-3200B specifics from TechMate:
- Board ground is **screw terminal 67**. The chain reads 50/52
  (emergency lowering, pit switch at 51) → **58** (top cover, E-stop,
  nut safety) → **59** (upper gate) → **61** (mid stop; **2-stop lifts
  need a yellow jumper 59–61**) → **63** (platform gate/lower door).
- The **emergency lowering key switch must be on AUTO** for normal
  running. A switch left off AUTO looks like a dead lift.
- Power side: **60A double breaker** and, if fitted, an **aux contact
  disconnect** before the White Rodgers solenoid. Relay **ECR-24111**.
- No pit switch → its 3-pin needs the green–brown jumper.
- **Field notice (2011):** units with a platform gate, serials
  **11080300300–11100301707**, may have a jumper plug in the lower
  interlock harness that lets the lift go up with the platform gate open.
  Remove it ([[Tech Tip - VPL-3200B Lower Interlock Jumper Issue (2011)]]).
- **Older units** (2011 guide): 24 VDC at J1, fuse TB1-1–TB1-3; TB1-4
  covers top cap, **upper final limit and pit switch**; relays RLY1/RLY2
  with a copper jumper plate
  ([[Tech Tip - Tall VPL-3200 Troubleshooting Guide (2011)]]). Pre-2011
  SCH-00176 units whose interlock chatters have a wiring fix in
  [[Tech Tip - Tall VPL Installation Notes]].
- **Electric strike won't unlock at a landing:** ESI timer needs the
  landing limit **and** a call button; press the button 1 s
  ([[Tech Tip - VPL Delay Timers and ESI Shut-off Timers]]).
- TechMate supports the current board from serial 15070350265 (3210B) /
  15070350274 (3212B) / 15070350271 (3214B). Units back to
  12070300097/112/156 use the short-residential board ("troubleshoot it
  like a short"). Older → Bruno Technical Service.

## Quirks / notes

- **Warranty:** 1 yr all parts, 2 yr major parts (motor, acme screw,
  drive nut, brake, pulleys, belt, screw bearings), 30 days labor
  ([[Bruno Warranty Summary]], [[Warranty]]).

- **Flood-zone unit**: per the operator manual, will not operate with more
  than 3" of standing water at the base — not mentioned in the 3100B docs.
- **Travel by model** (2011 guide): VPL-3210B **123 in**, VPL-3212B
  **147 in**, VPL-3214B **171 in**; 28 in minimum for all. Panels: 3210B no
  C1, 3212B one, 3214B two.
- **Rigging** (tech tips): hoist rated **1000 lb** minimum; 2 people with
  a hoist, 3 without; forklift with 5 ft forks. Bruno's notes disagree on
  section weights (lower section about 550 lb vs about 900 lb)
  ([[Tech Tip - Tall VPL Installation Notes]]). **Bruno's shipping sheet
  ILS-00992 (2013) gives the tower bottom section as 570 lb** and the top
  section as 190/235/280 lb (10/12/14 ft); the whole tower ships at
  1420–1640 lb with pallet. A flood-zone tower moves 100 lb to the top
  section. Unload only with a dock or yard forklift
  ([[ILS - VPL Shipping, Handling and Brush Kits]]).
- **Mid landing (3-stop):** kit VPL-K-3233; the limit lever must point at
  the carriage; pull the yellow 59–61 jumper.
- **Shipping jumpers** (tall residential and FZ flood-zone units): yellow
  VPL-31924 59–61, blue VPL-31910 upper gate, red VPL-32252 emergency
  lowering, green VPL-32277 lower interlock; **60 A** battery disconnect
  ([[Tech Tip - VPL Jumper Sheets (2017)]]).
- Cold weather: package **VPL-K-3213**.
- Two separate battery systems: main drive batteries (2×34AH) and a
  dedicated emergency-lowering battery (12VDC 7.5AH) — don't confuse them
  when troubleshooting a "won't lower" complaint; check both.
- Preventive maintenance and the troubleshooting table are otherwise
  near-identical to the 3100B's.

## Field notes

- [[2026-10-01 Bruno-VPL door interlock spring]]: **door stuck locked**.
  The spring-loaded door interlock can partially fail. Test: lift at the
  top, pull the friction-fit post cap, push the lock rod down by hand
  (remove the call/send station's four screws if you can't reach it).
  Rod doesn't spring back, or is slow to: **replace the interlock
  assembly, VPL-31452L (left hand)**.
- [[2026-10-14 VPL-3100B squealing and jamming on rotted fascia]]: **squealing and getting
  stuck**. The platform was jamming on the deck's **plywood fascia**,
  which met the requirements at the 2020 install but was **delaminating
  6 years later**. **Not make-specific** (per the human). Fix: remove the
  fascia and **rebuild it with treated decking boards**. The unit was a
  VPL-3100B; the 3200B has the same fascia requirements.
