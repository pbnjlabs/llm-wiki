# CRE-2100 (Bruno Electra-Ride III — legacy)

Bruno's curved-rail stairlift before the [[CRE-2110]] Elite Curve.
Variants CRE-2100 and CRE-2100C (commercial). The only document on file
is a 2001 spec sheet with two dimension drawings (2001 and 2003). No
manual is on file; for service, start from the [[CRE-2110]] documents and
call Bruno Technical Service (1-800-656-0552).

## Specs (rev 09/06/2001)

- **Rated load 350 lb**, one passenger. (CRE-2110: 400 lb.)
- **Power:** 24 VDC from 2 × 12 V **7 Ah** sealed lead-acid batteries,
  on/off switch. Charger 110 VAC 1.5 A in, 24 VDC 3 A out, continuous
  monitoring.
- **Drive:** 24 VDC 2-pole motor, 1.02 hp intermittent duty, 3800 rpm no
  load; **20:1 self-locking worm gearbox**; 4:1 intermediate reduction by
  dual 3V poly-V belts; final drive **8DP rack welded inside the rail**
  with a spur gear on the gearbox shaft. PWM controller with ramping.
  (Belts are why Bruno's 2008 yearly sheet says to check them.)
- **Braking:** dynamic motor braking plus the self-locking worm gear.
- **Controls:** rocker under the armrest; **318 MHz** wireless call/send.
  Keyed controls optional.
- **Rail:** 5/16 in mild steel, custom lengths **to 50 ft**, 0–45°,
  normally **2 in above the step nose**, fits within 7–8 in of the wall.
  Extends 6 in past the top nose and 18–20 in past the bottom nose.
  21.63 in minimum turn diameter (2003 drawing). Rail 16 lb/ft.
- **Speed:** user adjustable, 28 ft/min max (25 loaded) on the spec
  sheet; the drawings say 35/28 (2001) and 32/25 (2003) ft/min.
- **Weights:** carriage about 100 lb, seat 25 lb.
- **Safety:** seat swivel switch, footrest safety switches, seat belt,
  amber charge lights at each end, 25 A manual-reset breaker, 5 A control
  fuse, end-stop bumpers, final limit switch, diagnostic board.
  **Overspeed brake optional**, standard in the **commercial package**
  (with keyed rocker and keyed call/send).
- 8 to 20 round trips per charge.
- Hand-written note on the spec sheet, **overspeed trip range:** carriage
  46–72 ft/min, motor 4892–8804 rpm, gearbox 61–110 rpm, overspeed shaft
  153–275 rpm.

## Overspeed

Reset (per the test-fixture tip): **rotate the cam and reposition the
activating pin**, same as CRE-2110 and SRE-2000. See
[[Overspeed-Safety-Brake]] and
