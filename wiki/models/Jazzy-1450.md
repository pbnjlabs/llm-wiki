# Jazzy 1450 / Quantum 1450 (Q1450)

Pride's 600 lb bariatric front-wheel-drive chair, sold as the **Jazzy
1450** (Pride, Group 2) and the **Quantum 1450 / Q1450** (Quantum,
Group 3 Very HD). Same base.

From [[Jazzy and Quantum 1450 Specifications (INFINFB3028)]] (Rev H,
Apr 2023):

| | |
|---|---|
| Models | Jazzy 1450 2VHD, 2SPVHD; Quantum 1450 3SVHD, 3SPVHD |
| Class | B (Group 3) |
| Capacity | **600 lb** |
| Max speed | 4.9 mph (4 mph UK); the 2025 Quantum chart says 4.5 mph for the Q1450 |
| Range | up to 16.5 mi |
| Ground clearance | 1.5 in at anti-tips |
| Turning radius | 26.5 in |
| Length | 45.25 in with / 38.25 in without front riggings |
| Width | 29.25 in |
| Wheels | 14 in solid drive; 9 in solid rear casters; 4 in front anti-tips |
| Suspension | swing arm |
| Weights | base 195 lb; batteries 52 lb each |
| Batteries | two 12 V Group 24, 70 Ah |
| Charger | 8 A off-board |
| Max slope | **Jazzy 6°, Quantum 7.5°** |
| Climb | Jazzy 2 in, Quantum 2.25 in |

## Electronics and codes

- **Jazzy 1450:** 90 A **PG VR2**; specialty controls available (2026
  Group 2 chart). Flash codes: [[Pride-Controller-Flash-Codes]].
- **Q1450:** 120 A **Q-Logic 3** or 90 A **VR2 6-key** (2025 Quantum
  chart). Codes: [[Q-Logic-Fault-Codes]] or the VR2 table.
- 4-pole motors.

## Billing and warranty (Pride charts)

- Jazzy 1450: HCPCS K0826/K0827 (Group 2 Very HD) or **K0839** (single
  power Very HD). Q1450: K0852/K0853, K0860 (Group 3 Very HD). FSS
  V797D-70001.
- Warranty: K0826/K0827 lifetime limited frame, 13 months electronics,
  motors and batteries; K0839 lifetime frame, **2 years electronics, 18
  months motors**, 13 months batteries.
- Seating: Jazzy 1450 high-back up to 26 W × 24 D in (Synergy the
  same). Q1450: Synergy and TRU-Balance 2 HD power tilt 20–32 W × 20–28
  D in, Q Captain 22–26 × 20–24 in.
