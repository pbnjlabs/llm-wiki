# PDG Eclipse

PDG Mobility's **bariatric** manual wheelchair. **600 lb (272 kg) or 1000 lb
(453 kg)** depending on configuration (see the order form). Not for users under
12. Quick-release axles only; the axle receiver and camber bar move together
(counter-hold in the camber bar hole), then re-set the wheel locks. Reclining
back up to 30°. **Pneumatic tire and tube replacement by a PDG dealer or
qualified technician.** Test chair 38 kg with 24 in heavy-duty urethane wheels.
