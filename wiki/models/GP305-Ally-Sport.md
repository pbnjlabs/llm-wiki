# GP305 Ally Sport (Golden)

The **power-folding** member of the Ally family: it folds and unfolds
itself on a button press. Shares the [[GP303-Ally]]'s controller,
app and fault codes. Link to [[Golden]].

## Documents on file

- [[Ally Sport Owners Manual (GP305)]] (Rev. B, Jan 2025)
- [[Ally Sport Illustrated Parts Breakdown (GP305)]] (Rev. A, Jan 2025): controller with harness
  MBE-0079, joystick MBE-0072, **folding actuator MBE-0069**, 10 Ah pack
  MBE-0075/0076, charger MBE-0078; nothing electrical shared with the GP303/304

## Specs

**265 lb**, 3.7 mph, 9.5 mi, **24 V 10 Ah lithium** (4.6 lb), 40 A,
42.3 × 24.7 in, 30 in turning radius. **92.1 lb assembled, heaviest
piece 87.5 lb** (the heaviest Ally). 10 in pneumatic drive wheels, 10 in
omni casters. Comparison table on [[GP304-Ally-Pro]].

## Service notes

- **Ships with the battery disconnected:** remove the dunnage under the
  pack and the connector cap, then refit and latch the pack.
- **Power fold / unfold:** stand beside it, power on, hold the two fold
  buttons for 3 s (voice prompt), tap the joystick. Moving the joystick
  stops it; start over. Nobody on it, children and pets clear.
- Novice mode is the **W** button. Joystick extends 2 in in 0.5 in steps.
- Fault codes: [[Ally-Fault-Codes]]. Freewheel levers **down** =
  unlocked ([[Freewheel-Mode]]).
