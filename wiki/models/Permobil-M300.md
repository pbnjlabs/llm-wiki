# Permobil M300

Legacy **mid-wheel-drive** chair with the **Corpus 3G** seat, sold with
**VR2** (PM90-A2) or **R-net** (PM120) electronics. Source:
[[Permobil M300 Owner's Manual (205255)]] (2014).

## Service notes

- Tires 3.00-8 drive and 200 × 50 casters, **29 psi**. Batteries **2 ×
  60 Ah group 34 gel** (max 10.2 × 6.6 × 7 in). 63 A breaker through a
  hole in the chassis cover.
- **Battery change:** front battery behind the front cover (battery locks,
  sticker inside the cover); rear battery needs the top and rear covers
  off and the **electronics pulled out by the release lips**. **Replace
  both as a matched set** ([[Mobility-Batteries]]).
- **Wheel locks:** lever on each drive wheel at the front; power off
  first; check the lever is fully in afterwards.
- Diagnostics: VR2 LED table ([[PG-Drives-Trip-Codes]]) or R-net
  ([[R-net-Diagnostics]]). The VR2 table's 2-LED row says "right" motor
  where it means left.
