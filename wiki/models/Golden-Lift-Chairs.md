# Golden AutoDrive Lift Chairs (PR series)

Golden's power lift chairs and recliners (4- and 5-zone) with the
**AutoDrive** hand control. PMS sells lift chairs; all lift chair sales
are final ([[PMS-Office-Procedures]]). Link to [[Golden]].

> **Missing manuals.** No lift chair owner's manual or service guide is
> on file for any PR model. Only this one reset sheet is. When working
> on one of these chairs, ask the tech to get a copy of the owner's
> manual from Golden lift chair tech support at **800-624-6374 x502**.

## Procedures

**1. Power Disruption Calibration.** Use it when the back won't come
fully upright or the footrest won't fully extend after a power outage.
Also do it after replacing any electrical part, or when a motor is out
of sync with the control box.
- **ZK3100-C5M:** hold **A + C + D** until the chair stops moving, then
  keep holding **5 more seconds**.
- **ZK3200-HC:** hold **A + C** until the chair stops, then keep holding
  **5 more seconds**.
- **ZKAD-5:** **can't be calibrated in the field.** If its lights flash
  in sets of 2, 3 or 4, call Golden lift chair tech service at
  800-624-6374 **x502**.

**2. Position Accuracy Calibration.** Use it when the preset buttons
(REJUV, SIT, SLEEP, TV, ZG) stop landing in the right place. The steps
are the same on all three controls: hold **C** until the footrest is
fully extended, release, then hold **A** until the back is fully
reclined. The chair goes to Trendelenburg and relearns its range.

**3. Factory Default Reset.** Use it to restore the factory presets
after a power disruption. The steps are the same on all three controls:
hold **M** until the lights flash, then within **5 seconds** press
**A**, then **B**. The other choice is to reprogram each preset, per
the owner's manual (not on file).

## Gotchas

- Chairs built **before 2021** are more sensitive to power fluctuations.
  In outage-prone homes, Golden suggests a battery backup (UPS) from an
  office supply store. This is separate from the chair's own backup
  batteries ([[Golden-Dealer-Tips]]).
- Power-disruption problems look like mechanical faults (back stuck
  partly reclined, footrest short). Calibrate before ordering parts.
