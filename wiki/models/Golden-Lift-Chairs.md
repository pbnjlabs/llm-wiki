# Golden Lift Chairs (power lift recliners)

Golden's power lift and recline chairs: 1-zone standard chairs,
MaxiComfort (zero gravity), Twilight (tilting cradle), bariatric and
private-label models. PMS sells lift chairs; all lift chair sales are
final ([[PMS-Office-Procedures]]). Golden lift chair tech support:
**800-624-6374 x502**. Link to [[Golden]].

Golden calls a lift chair a **medical device**. Model and serial number
are on the **hand control cord**, the **rear of the lift frame** and the
manual's cover; the **weight limit label** is next to the serial number
on the frame.

## Power and battery backup

- A **transformer** behind the chair (115–120 V in, **29 V DC** out on
  current chairs) feeds the control box, motors and hand control. **No
  extension cords.** Its power cord is zip-tied to the motor shaft or the
  back cross rail and must run under the wooden cross rail.
- Backup, by model: none (Golden suggests a UPS), **two 9 V alkalines
  in the transformer**, an **inline 9 V holder** zip-tied to the frame
  (wide dual-motor PR501/PR531), **four 9 V in two transformers**
  (PR502), or a **lithium pack** that slides onto the transformer and
  self-charges. **PR632: no backup.**
- A backup only brings the chair back to seated, once. **Replace 9 V
  batteries after one use and every year**; don't mix old and new or
  different chemistries. Leaking 9 V batteries can damage property.
- **SmartTek lights:** green LEDs on the transformer and on the
  motor-to-transformer cord, plus a lit hand control. Which light is out
  tells you where the power stops.

## Installation notes

- **Backs:** bolt-on (7/16 in flange nuts on the back's studs; run the
  footrest up to expose the brackets), bolt-on with a **back motor**
  (clevis pin and hairpin into the recline bar; PR119), or **KD
  brackets** that click down (lift the tabs to remove; PL830 needs a
  flat screwdriver on the black tab). Then Velcro the outside back to the
  arm cross rail.
- **Powered backs** plug in at the seat: **red/black** (PR446/448) or
  **red/yellow** (PR445) connectors, red to red; close the locks. Some
  zip the back to the seat fabric.
- **Hand control routing** (inside-arm models: PR446/448, PR535, PR632,
  PR735, PR766, PR515, PR761, PL830): unclip from the J-clip, unlock and
  separate the extension (this cuts power to all functions), route
  through the other side's J-clip, reconnect and lock. Others clip into a
  quick-release clamp above the magazine pocket.
- **Leg levelers** under the front: raise them so the footrest clears
  thick carpet, and to stop rocking.
- **Frame extensions** (some chairs): pull the detent clevis pins, swing
  the extensions out, refit the pins. For users uneasy when reclined.

**Universal backlit control** (2024): RISE, RECLINE, ZG or **ZG+**, TV,
HOME, SLEEP or **REJUV** (ZG+ and REJUV mean a **cradle** chair, which
also has tilt buttons), micro-adjust arrows, optional HEAD and LUMBAR,
and **M**. HOME can't be reprogrammed; headrest and lumbar aren't saved
in presets.

### M-button sequences (Universal manual, from its photos)

Hold **M** until the backlight flashes, then press, in order:

| Feature | Sequence | PR446/448 manual (S numbers) |
|---|---|---|
| Save a preset | the preset button | SP ~3 s, then S5/S6/S8/S10 within 5 s |
| **Factory reset** | backrest down, then footrest/seat down | SP ~4 s, S1, S4 |
| **Hand control disable** (all off) | backrest down, backrest up, SLEEP/REJUV | SP 4 s, S7 |
| **Function lock** (only RISE and RECLINE work) | backrest down, backrest up, footrest up, ZG | SP 4 s, S1, S2, S3, S6 |
| **Lights out** (backlight off after ~15 s) | backrest down, backrest up, HOME | SP, S1, S2, S5 |

Repeat the same sequence to turn disable, function lock or lights out
back off. **All three look like faults** on a service call: a dead
remote, half the buttons dead, or no backlight.

## Calibration and resets

**Setup / calibration** (owner's manuals; required before first use and
after outages, surges or an abrupt unplug):
- **ZK3200-HC and Universal control:** hold **backrest down + footrest
  up** (S1 + S3) until the chair stops, then **3 more seconds**.
- **ZK3100-C5M (Twilight):** hold **S1 + S3 + S11 (twilight in)**, then
  3 more seconds. The Universal manual: add the cradle button on cradle
  chairs.
- If it still misbehaves: wrong buttons, let go too early, or power was
  lost during setup. Do it again; the starting position doesn't matter.
- Chairs **without a control box can't be calibrated**.

**From the [[AutoDrive Hand Control Resets and Calibrations]] sheet**
(letters as on the sheet's diagram; comparing with the manuals, A ≈ S1
backrest down, B ≈ S4 footrest down, C ≈ S3 footrest up, D ≈ S11
twilight):

1. **Power Disruption Calibration.** Use it when the back won't come
   fully upright or the footrest won't fully extend after a power
   outage, after replacing any electrical part, or when a motor is out
   of sync. **ZK3100-C5M:** hold **A + C + D** until it stops, then
   **5 more seconds**. **ZK3200-HC:** hold **A + C**, then 5 more
   seconds. **ZKAD-5:** can't be done in the field; if its lights flash
   in sets of 2, 3 or 4, call x502.
2. **Position Accuracy Calibration.** Use it when the presets (REJUV,
   SIT, SLEEP, TV, ZG) stop landing in the right place: hold **C** until
   the footrest is fully out, release, then hold **A** until the back is
   fully reclined. The chair goes to Trendelenburg and relearns its range.
3. **Factory Default Reset:** hold **M** until the lights flash, then
   within 5 s press **A**, then **B**.

> [!note] 3 or 5 seconds?
> The owner's manuals say keep holding **3 seconds** after the chair
> stops; the 2021 AutoDrive sheet says **5**. Holding 5 covers both.

## Troubleshooting (symptom first)

From the Universal manual, the AutoDrive sheet and the 2004 manual.

| Symptom | Check, in order |
|---|---|
| **Nothing works** | Transformer plugged in and its LED green? Outlet on a wall switch? Breaker? Try another outlet; replace the transformer if its LED stays off. Cord from the chair to the transformer connected? **Hand control disable** turned on? Old chairs: motor **thermal protector** tripped after 5–6 continuous cycles; wait 10–15 min |
| **Some buttons work, some don't** | **Function lock** on? Hand control damaged? Motor and control-box cables (and the back's red/black connectors) connected? Out of calibration: recalibrate. Then hand control or control box |
| **Back won't come fully up / footrest short after an outage** | Power disruption calibration before ordering parts |
| **Presets in the wrong place** | Position accuracy calibration or factory reset, then re-save the presets |
| **No backlight** | **Lights-out** feature on? Damaged or failed hand control |
| **Stops while lifting** | Power outage or surge; out of calibration; a wire pulled loose during the lift (route cords away from the mechanism); backup battery flat |
| **Heat or massage dead** | Wiring from main power to the **junction box** and from the junction box on; the heat/massage remote |
| **Rocks** | Leg levelers; something under the chair; frame damage |
| **Motor runs and won't stop** (old chairs) | Defective switch or motor: unplug, call the dealer |
| **PL340 does nothing** | Press **PWR** (20-minute timer); if it glows red it's locked: hold PWR 4 s |

Chairs built **before 2021** are more sensitive to power fluctuations;
in outage-prone homes Golden suggests a UPS as well as the chair's own
backup batteries ([[Golden-Dealer-Tips]]).

## Warranty

**Golden's standard terms** ("Consumer Limited Lifetime and Seven Year
Warranty", most Golden-branded and some private-label chairs):
- **Lifetime** (original owner): lift mechanism, recline mechanism and
  chair frame. **Labor subsidized 3 years.**
- **3 years:** electrical parts. **Labor subsidized 1 year.**
- **Years 4–7:** electrical parts at a discount off suggested retail:
  **35% (year 4), 25%, 15%, 5% (year 7)**.
- Golden doesn't pay for examination, freight or packaging, or for
  service calls just to teach the user or find a bad power connection.
  **Over the weight limit voids it.** Fabric shading isn't guaranteed.
  Claims must be approved by Golden first. Valid in all 50 states,
  Canada and Puerto Rico. Opening an electrical component, or placing the
  chair too close to the wall, voids it.

**Exceptions:**

| Chair | Terms |
|---|---|
| Capri, Monarch | 6 months labor; Monarch 2 yr, Capri 1 yr electrical; no years 4–7 |
| PR118, PR202 | 6 months labor. PR118 electrical: the 2023 text says both 2 years and 1 year: **check with Golden** |
| **PR502** (700 lb) | **2 years** mechanism and frame (labor 2 yr), **1 year** electrical (labor 1 yr) |
| PR117, PR119, 115, 165 | **1 year** on everything; 115/165 **labor not covered** |
| 116, 166 | Lifetime mechanisms; 1 yr wood frame and electrical; labor not covered |
| PL340 | Lifetime mechanism and frame; 1 yr electrical; labor 1 yr; **no in-home service** |
| Healthy Back PR127, AmeriGlide PR458 | Lifetime / 3-year, no years 4–7; claims through the seller |
| Relax The Back PR805 Bordeaux | Lifetime metal frame, scissor and springs; 3 yr motor, electrical and seat cushion; 1 yr wood frame; 1 yr labor |
| Relax The Back (all) | Plus a **12-month protection plan**: Golden in-home service, parts, labor and freight, or a replacement chair; 15% restocking fee if the return isn't a factory fault |

## Massage, heat and audio

- **Massage and heat remote:** zones lumbar and seat; SELECT, WAVE,
  PULSE; massage stops after 15 min; heat LO (amber) / HI (red), 30-min
  timer.
- **Nirvana** air massage (10 air chambers) with **HeatWave**
  far-infrared heat; massage in 15-min cycles, heat 30–60 min.
- **PL340:** MSG and Heat buttons, 20-min shut-off, pause while moving.
- **PR128:** hand-pump air lumbar (bulb to firm, button to deflate).
- **Perfect TV Chair:** built-in speakers, TV audio transmitter within
  32 ft.
- Features on newer chairs: 1 A or 2 A USB, wireless charger, storage
  compartments, cup holders, device mount, power grommet.
