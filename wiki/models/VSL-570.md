# VSL-570 / VSL-570E (Bruno "Big-Lifter")

Bruno's **in-vehicle boom hoist** for wheelchairs, scooters and power
chairs **up to 400 lb**. It mounts in the rear corner (or behind a van
side door) of full-size vans, SUVs and minivans. It is the predecessor
generation to the [[VSL-6000-6900]] Curb-Sider (its service sheets list
the VSL-570/600/670/690 together). Product code 011 (rear hoist,
[[Bruno-Serial-Numbers]]). The manuals were pulled from Brunopartner on
2026-10-22.

## Install essentials (2023 manual)

- **Rating:** the decal on the base is the vehicle-specific maximum.
  Using a fender washer instead of the 4-hole support washer **lowers
  the rating**.
- **Base:**
  - **Adjustable** (movable legs, can move to another vehicle) or
    **custom**.
  - Cut liners or mats so the feet sit flat. Seal underneath.
  - **Level base, vertical mast.**
  - **6 base bolts, criss-cross, 12 ft-lb (150 in-lb) maximum.**
- **Positions:** right rear (US standard), left rear, van side door.
- **Power:** a 30 A fused lead from the battery **through the interior**.
  Harness black to black, red to red. **Disable any third-row power
  seat.**
- **Adjust:**
  - Vertical tube height (½ in cross bolt: **don't over-tighten**).
  - Arm angle (turnbuckle).
  - Outer arm length (set screw), so the device swings in **front end
    first**.
- **Magnetic pendant holder** VSL-K-4441H.
- **Docking device** U-bolted low on the seat post at the balance point.
  C-arms span 10–14 in ([[Docking-Devices]]).

## Service

- Inspect **yearly**, or **every 6 months** with heavy use: belt, swivel
  tube (Magnalube PTFE grease), electrics, docking device.
- **Chain tension:**
  1. Unloaded, motor cover off.
  2. Loosen the 3 motor-plate nuts and rotate the motor
     **counter-clockwise**.
  3. Tighten the **pivot (lower) nut first**.
- Troubleshooting: breaker or fuse, connections, ground, the vehicle
  battery.
- Lift belt kits and head parts are shared with the Curb-Sider family
  ([[ILS - Curb-Sider (VSL-6000-6900) Service Sheets]]).
