# GP303 (Ally)

Foldable power wheelchair — despite sharing the "GP" prefix with the
Compass line (GP600/605/615/620), this is a **distinct, unrelated model**
("Ally"), not a Compass variant. The source document was originally
clustered with Compass by number; corrected here after reading the actual
content. Link to [[Golden]].

## Sell-sheet specs (Aug 2024)

From [[Golden Sell Sheets (2018-2026)]]: 330 lb, 5 mph, 9 mi, 12 Ah lithium, **56 lb assembled**,
heaviest piece 49.5 lb, 41 × 24.5 in, folding. Compare the lighter
[[GP302-Cricket]] (39.3 lb). See [[Mobility-Device-Weights]].

## Notes

- The Ally's frame collapses into a "bow" shape for transport — a
  fold-flat mechanism not present on any other Golden model ingested so
  far.
- Main wiring harness routes through the armrests and a hinged frame
  spacer bar; replacement requires folding the frame into transport
  position to access a hinged spacer bar, then fishing the new harness
  through with a bent steel wire.
- Harness connects to the control module via three connectors (positive,
  negative, EXPN) — no motor-harness disconnection needed for this
  procedure, only the main harness.
