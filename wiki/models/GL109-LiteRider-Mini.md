# GL109 LiteRider Mini (Golden)

A **discontinued** small travel scooter in the LiteRider family. It
still appears on the covered-models list of the scooter warranty (see
[[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]]). Link to [[Golden]].

## Documents on file

- Retailer part numbers (Mobility Scooters Direct, 2026-10-27; confirm by serial with the manufacturer): [[Parts Catalog - Mobility Scooters Direct (Golden)#Golden LiteRider Mini Mobility Scooter 3-Wheel GL109D|Golden LiteRider Mini Mobility Scooter 3-Wheel GL109D]] (3)
- [[LiteRider Mini Service Guide (GL109)]] (2016)

## What's confirmed

- **Dynamics R-Series** controller, 18–24 VDC, **two 12 Ah** batteries
  in a removable pack.
- Beep codes 1–9: [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]].
- Rear frame with a transaxle/motor/brake drivetrain, plastic decks,
  front and deck baskets, anti-tip wheels.
- Field-replaceable parts: drive wheel, drive train, throttle pot,
  battery, circuit breaker, battery-harness fuse, controller,
  front-to-rear harness, control panel, motor/brake.
