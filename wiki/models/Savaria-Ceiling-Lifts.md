# Savaria Ceiling Lifts (FL, PL / M-Series)

Savaria Patient Care's ceiling lifts: the **FL** fixed lift and the **PL**
portable lift. The portable lift's owner's manual calls it the **M-Series
Portable Ceiling Lift** (same 5 kg, 200/130 kg platform as the PL). Install by an
authorized Savaria dealer only. Savaria now also owns Handicare, maker of the
[[Prism]] lifts ([[C-450-C-625]], [[P440]]).

## Specs

| | FL fixed | PL / M-Series portable |
|---|---|---|
| Weight | 7.5 kg manual, 8.5 kg motorized traverse | 5 kg (11 lb) |
| SWL | 272 kg (600 lb), 200 kg (440 lb) or 130 kg (286 lb) | 200 kg (440 lb) or 130 kg (286 lb) |
| Lift speed | 5.5 cm/s empty → 4 cm/s at 272 kg | 5.5 → 4.5 cm/s at 200 kg |
| Battery | Li-ion 25.2 V 2500 mAh; ~80 lifts of 24 in at 200 lb, ~30 at 600 lb | same; ~30 lifts at 440 lb |
| Charger | 100–240 V in, 29.4 V 1 A out, ~2 h | **M920001 only**, ~2 h |
| Hand control | Up/down/left/right (IP67) | Up/down (IP67); touchpads on the lift too |

Both: IP21, 54 dBA, duty **max 10%, 1 min continuous**, **10-year life with one
battery replacement**, 3-year warranty, ISO 10535:2021. The **On/Off switch is
the emergency stop**.

Portable lift fits Savaria telescopic and rigid-arm track, Handicare aluminum
portable track and Arjo track (accessories differ). Scale kit M921422.

## Charging

- Charger LED: **green solid** = powered, full or disconnected; **yellow, orange
  or red** = charging; **green blinking/fading** = wrong plugging order (mostly
  M920001-X): plug the charger into the wall first, then the lift.
- **It won't lift while the charger is connected.** Can stay plugged in when
  idle; charge monthly in storage.
- Low battery: beeping and blinking battery LED on a button press; it may stop
  lifting but still lowers.

## Emergency

- **Stop:** turn the On/Off switch off.
- **Manual lowering:** power off, 4 mm Allen key (supplied), turn as labelled;
  slow, many turns; then call the dealer.
- **Emergency brake** stops a fall if the transmission or motor fails. If it has
  engaged, **don't press Up, don't release the brake and don't lower manually**:
  switch off and call the dealer (or Savaria).

## Troubleshooting (owner's manual §7)

Don't open the case; only an authorized Savaria dealer may.

| Problem | Action |
|---|---|
| Starts and stops repeatedly | Overload: the load is over the SWL |
| Beeps during use, won't lift but lowers | Charge the battery |
| No charging light with charger connected | Outlet has power (charger green LED on)? The battery may not need charging; the charger decides |
| Doesn't move on touchpad or hand control | Power switch on; battery charged; hand control plugged in; **charger unplugged**; reset by switching off a few seconds and on |
| Battery LED stays on after a reset | A command is stuck on: switch off, unplug the hand control, switch on. Still stuck or dead = dealer |

## Maintenance

**Operator, before every use:** charge, strap, sling and stitching, carabiner,
lift and hand control buttons, track and hardware, end stops in place, runs
freely. **Every 4 months:** clean inside the track (damp cloth slid end to end);
test the emergency stop (switch off, hand control dead). **Yearly:** casing,
support hooks and carabiner, track, hardware and trolley wheels.

**Dealer, yearly:** casing, transmission, strap-to-accessory connection point,
**limit switches (high, low, slack)**, emergency brake turns freely, all
emergency devices, manual lowering, **load test of lift and track at SWL**.
**Every 2 years:** **replace the strap**, lubricate the roller and spool shaft
and the transmission spool gear. Savaria parts only.

- **Track end stops:** always refit (clevis pins and locking rings) after
  service, **20 Nm (15 ft-lb)**.
- Clean with a damp cloth and cleaner or 70% isopropyl wipes; **no phenol,
  chlorine, bleach, acid, ammonium or solvents**; never soak it.
- Slings: wash per label, cool water, no bleach, no iron, no tumble dry.

Compare: the Prism/Handicare [[C-450-C-625]] replaces its strap every 5000 cycles
or 3 years. See [[Ceiling-Lift-Troubleshooting]].
