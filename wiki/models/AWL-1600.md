# AWL-1600 (Bruno "Back-Saver")

Bruno's light **hitch-mounted lift for a folded manual wheelchair**. A
platform rises up a slide tube and a foam **Hold-Tite** clamps the chair
arms for travel. Product code 016 ("light exterior",
[[Bruno-Serial-Numbers]]). The install manual was pulled from Brunopartner
on 2026-10-22.

## Install essentials (2020 manual)

- **Hitch:**
  - Class I/II 1¼ in or Class III 2 in receiver, **7–25 in off the
    ground**, fitted by a hitch installer after an underbody inspection.
  - No rusty vehicles.
- **Adapter by receiver height** (round up):
  - 6–11 in: 3 in drop-raise, up.
  - 12–15 in: standard, up.
  - 16–18 in: standard, down.
  - 19–24 in: 3 in drop-raise, down.
  - Part numbers: ASL-16608 / ASL-16605 (3 in) and ASL-16607 / ASL-16604
    (standard), Class II / III ([[Hitch-Adapters]]).
- Magnalube on the adapter, anti-seize on the stainless.
- **Swing-Away** option: never swing it loaded. Latch pin adjusts with
  the swing arm screw.
- **Power:**
  - From the battery **through the interior**.
  - Optional plate holder AWL-01673 on the 4-pin flat trailer plug.
  - **Lighting resistor kit AWL-K-1601** for hyper-flash and
    trailer-detect alerts.
  - **Dielectric grease on the ground**; road salt kills it
    ([[Vehicle-Lift-Power-Wiring]]).
- **Hold-Tite:**
  - Raise the chair until about 2 in of travel remains, then lower the
    foam U onto the armrests until it deforms around them.
  - Armpads deflect: move it up. Chair loose: move it down.
- **Vehicle effects:**
  - Rear sag (helper springs or air shocks), and **re-aim the
    headlights**.
  - Tire load ratings.
  - Extra length when reversing.
  - Touchless car washes only, with the chair off.

## Operation and troubleshooting

- Keyed switch under a watertight cover.
- Holding DOWN after landing unwinds the belt. **Holding UP at the top
  stalls the motor.**
- Dead or slow: battery wires not reconnected after vehicle service,
  corroded ground, connections, bulbs.
