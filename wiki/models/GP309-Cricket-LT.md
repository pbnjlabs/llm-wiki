# GP309 Cricket LT (Golden)

A **25.8 lb carbon-fiber folding power chair**, the lightest Golden
chair on file. A lighter sibling of the [[GP302-Cricket]] (39.3 lb).
Link to [[Golden]].

## Documents on file

- [[Cricket LT Owners Manual (GP309)]] (Rev. A, Feb 2026)

## Specs

300 lb, 3.7 mph, 9.5 mi, 24 V 10 Ah lithium (**2 lb**), **35 A**,
36 × 24 in, 29.5 in turning radius, **1.25 in ground clearance**. 11 in
drive wheels, 6.5 in casters. Seat 18 × 18 in. Warranty 5 yr frame,
13 months drive train and electronics, 1 yr battery.

## Service notes

- **Battery ships disconnected:** hook the two lower tabs inside the
  frame and rotate the pack up until it clicks. To check charge off the
  chair, press the pack gauge's button twice.
- Charger LED **blue** charging, green full (like the GP302 and GP301).
- Folds by pulling the two strap handles under the seat pad; unfold by
  pressing down on the seat edges. Never put a hand between seat and
  frame.
- Freewheel: two **red** levers at the back, push **backward** to
  unlock ([[Freewheel-Mode]]).

## Beep codes

The horn beeps twice, then the count. The controller maker isn't named.
Close to the Micon table on [[GP302-Cricket]] (and the
[[GP301-Stride]]), but **1 and 8 differ**.

| Beeps | Fault | Check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Communication failure | Joystick-to-controller connection |
| 2 | Right motor | Right motor connection |
| 3 | Right brake | Right brake connection; freewheel lever in drive |
| 4 | Left motor | Left motor connection |
| 5 | Left brake | Left brake connection; freewheel lever in drive |
| 6 | Overcurrent | A locked-up motor; controller connections |
| 7 | Joystick not centered | Let go of the joystick; restart |
| 8 | **Joystick failure** | Joystick connections; technician |
| 9 | Battery low | Charge; the pack may need replacing |

The manual's remedies for 4 and 5 say "right" where they mean left.
