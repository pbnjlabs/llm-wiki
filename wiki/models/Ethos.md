# Ethos (rigid, polymer suspension)

Ki Mobility's **rigid** chair with the seat frame on **ISO Tech polymer** suspension elements. **275 lb (125 kg)**, transit rated to 275 lb. 15 kg (33 lb) as tested.

- **Polymers** set the ride by user weight and center of gravity (standard chart: 100–120 lb blue/red, 121–165 lb black/blue, 166–209 lb black, 210–275 lb green/black; a firm chart too). Spares ship with the chair; keep the tower tape in place.
- **Toe to zero** with 2–8° camber adapters (not with 0° plugs): 5 mm camber clamp bolts, rotate the camber tube, flats perpendicular to the ground.
- Tech manual: seat and base frame replacement, polymer replacement, highlight kit, COG and caster position, camber tube, seat height, transit install.
- Warranty includes the polymers for life.
