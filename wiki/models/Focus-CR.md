# Focus CR (tilt-in-space)

Ki Mobility's **tilt-in-space** chair with optional recline. Standard **300 lb (136 kg)**, HD **400 lb (181 kg)**, transit 300 lb. 25 kg (55 lb) as tested.

- **Tilt:** trigger cable to rotary-frame cog locks. Adjust the cable (jam nut, 10 mm and 3/8 in) so the cogs seat fully, lock at every position and never drag. Never leave a user parked with the triggers pinned open.
- **Power tilt kit:** REAC RCB10 control box, 2 Ah battery pack, external charger, pendant and adaptive-switch ports ([[Focus Power Tilt System User Instruction Manual (DCN1130.2)]]). Install, motor replacement and control box in the tech manual.
- Tech manual: tilt mechanism installation, **rotary frame replacement**, seat height and frame setup charts, COG, vent and battery tray. Pivot joint screws **240 in-lb**, three screws 144 in-lb, 120 in-lb.
- Lifetime warranty includes the tilt assembly and bearings.
