# CR45 (tilt-in-space)

Ki Mobility **tilt-in-space** chair, **300 lb (136 kg)**, transit section present. 33 kg (73 lb) as tested; folds to 1156 × 648 × 819 mm.

- Hand (trigger) or foot tilt; cable adjustment as on the Focus.
- Options: reclining and dynamic backs, attendant foot lock, canopy, IV and O2 holders, pediatric ELR, growing seat pan.
- Tech manual: caster seat-height matrix, hand and foot tilt installation, COG, bearing construction.
