# M-Series (Savaria portable ceiling lift, PL)

Savaria Patient Care's **portable** ceiling lift. Savaria's 2022 spec sheet
calls it the **PL**; the 2026 owner's manual calls it the **M-Series
Portable Ceiling Lift** (same 5 kg, 200/130 kg lift). Like the [[P440]], it's
carried from track to track and pushed along by hand; only up/down is
motorized. Operated by a caregiver, never by the person being lifted. Install
by an authorized Savaria dealer only. Its fixed sibling is the [[Savaria-FL]].

## How it works

- **Controls:** UP/DOWN touchpads on the lift, plus an optional wired hand
  control (plugs into the front connector, held by a cable clamp and Phillips
  screw). On/Off power switch, battery LED, overheat LED, charger connector.
- **Electrical:** Li-ion 25.2 V 2500 mAh battery, charger **M920001 only**,
  about 2 h to charge. **It won't lift while the charger is connected.**
- **Mechanical:** strap with carabiner to the sling bar; trolley on the track;
  high, low and slack-strap limit switches; an **emergency brake** that stops
  a fall if the transmission or motor fails.

## Specs

- SWL **200 kg (440 lb)** or **130 kg (286 lb)**
- 5 kg (11 lb)
- Lift speed 5.5 cm/s empty, 4.5 cm/s at 200 kg; soft start and stop
- About 30 lifts per charge at 440 lb
- IP21 (hand control IP67), 54 dBA
- Duty **max 10%, 1 min continuous**
- **10-year life** with one battery replacement; ISO 10535:2021

## Safety mechanisms

- **Emergency stop:** the On/Off switch.
- **Manual lowering:** power off, 4 mm Allen key (supplied), turn as labelled;
  slow, many turns; then call the dealer.
- **Emergency brake:** if it has engaged, **don't press Up, don't release the
  brake and don't lower manually**. Switch off and call the dealer.
- Low battery: beeps and blinks the battery LED on a button press; it may stop
  lifting but still lowers.

## Charging

- Charger LED **green solid** = powered, full or disconnected; **yellow, orange
  or red** = charging; **green blinking/fading** = wrong plugging order (mostly
  the M920001-X): plug the charger into the wall first, then into the lift.
- Can stay plugged in when idle; unplug before use; charge monthly in storage.

## Track compatibility

Savaria telescopic and rigid-arm portable tracks, Handicare aluminum portable
track, Savaria and Arjo track (accessories differ). Portable scale kit M921422,
frame M921423. Savaria slings recommended.

## Maintenance

**Operator, before every use:** charge, strap, sling and stitching, carabiner,
lift and hand control buttons, track and hardware, end stops in place, runs
freely. **Every 4 months:** clean inside the track (damp cloth slid end to end);
test the emergency stop. **Yearly:** casing, hooks and carabiner, track,
hardware and trolley wheels.

**Dealer, yearly:** casing, transmission, strap-to-accessory point, **limit
switches (high, low, slack)**, emergency brake turns freely, emergency devices,
manual lowering, **load test of lift and track at SWL**. **Every 2 years:**
**replace the strap**; lubricate the roller, spool shaft and transmission spool
gear. Savaria parts only. Refit track end stops (clevis pins, locking rings) at
**20 Nm (15 ft-lb)**.

Clean with a damp cloth and cleaner or 70% isopropyl wipes; **no phenol,
chlorine, bleach, acid, ammonium or solvents**; never soak it. Slings: wash per
label, cool water, no bleach, iron or tumble dry.

## Troubleshooting

Don't open the case; only an authorized Savaria dealer may.

| Problem | Action |
|---|---|
| Starts and stops repeatedly | Overload: the load is over the SWL |
| Beeps during use, won't lift but lowers | Charge the battery |
| No charging light with the charger connected | Outlet has power (charger green)? The battery may not need charging |
| Doesn't move on touchpad or hand control | Switch on; battery charged; hand control plugged in; **charger unplugged**; switch off a few seconds and on |
| Battery LED stays on after a reset | A command is stuck: switch off, unplug the hand control, switch on. Still stuck = dealer |

See also [[Ceiling-Lift-Troubleshooting]].
