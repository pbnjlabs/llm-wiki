# ASL-275 (Bruno Out-Sider / Out-Sider with Swing-Away)

Hitch-mounted platform lift for scooters and powerchairs (optionally with a
Swing-Away arm to clear the vehicle's tailgate/hatch). Max lifting capacity
350 lb (159 kg). Not compatible with RVs. Link to [[Bruno]].

Successor to [[ASL-250]] (2019 manual on file) — same "Out-Sider"
branding and core architecture, but ASL-250's ramped **HTP** platform
variant (FWD/RWD/MWD chock configurations) is not documented anywhere in
this model's manual; see [[ASL-250]] for the full generational
comparison.

## Specs

- Platform styles: standard scooter, standard powerchair, standard
  lightweight powerchair, large scooter — each with its own useable
  dimensions
- Hitch compatibility: Class II (1.25") or Class III (2")
- Height adjustment: 6"-24" hitch interface, via 3 hitch adapter options
  (Standard, 3" offset, 6" offset) selected from a height lookup table
- Installed weight: 110-125 lb (with Swing-Away), 128 lb with large
  platform

## Key procedures

- **Hitch setup**: measure hitch receiver height → look up adapter
  (Standard/3"/6") and mast-hole position from the table → apply Magnalube
  to adapter → mount adapter, leveling the mast to 90° vertical before
  final tightening.
- **Swing-Away install** (if equipped): bolt to hitch adapter, attach
  lift/mast tube, connect harnesses (dielectric grease on connections),
  adjust swing-arm height via the swing-arm adjustment screw (clockwise =
  raise, counterclockwise = lower).
- **Power wiring**: see [[Vehicle-Lift-Power-Wiring]] (shared pattern with
  [[PUL-1100]]); optional 7-pin trailer-connector kits for SUVs/trucks
  (INS-K-2002 no brake light / INS-K-2003 with third brake light). Bruno's
  2020 sheet ILS-01450 names the adapters **ASL-16730 (no third brake
  light) / ASL-16716 (with)**; the manual's INS-K numbers may be the kits
  that contain them (not confirmed). With the ignition off the twin lead
  must read battery voltage: 30 A trailer fuse, bypass a trailer tow
  relay (Ford), connect the loose red/black wire (GM).
- **Platform/wheel-chock adjustment**: scooter and powerchair platforms
  each have their own wheel-chock spacing table by wheel diameter (e.g.
  scooter 12"-14" wheels → 5.625" between chocks). Hold-Tite foot or arm
  is tensioned by running the lift up until the actuator ratchets, then
  backing off slightly.
- **Completion checklist**: verify lift/mobility device doesn't block
  tail/brake/turn signal lights or the license plate (extension bracket
  procedure included if needed); optional third brake light kit.
- **Emergency operation**: hand crank on the motor shaft (or 3/8" electric
  drill) — clockwise raises the platform, counterclockwise lowers it.

## Troubleshooting highlights

- Electrical fault isolation uses the circuit board's KEY/UP/DOWN
  terminals: jumper KEY-to-UP and KEY-to-DOWN in turn to bypass the
  keyswitch/rocker switch and isolate whether the fault is the switch,
  harness, or circuit board.
- Lift operates backwards → reversed polarity at the battery leads →
  check (+) red / (−) black at the electrical enclosure.
- Weak vehicle battery is a common cause of slow/underpowered operation —
  test by starting the engine and re-checking lift performance.
