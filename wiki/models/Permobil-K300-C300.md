# Permobil K300 / C300

Legacy **front-wheel-drive** chair: **K300** with the pediatric **PS
Junior** seat, **C300** with a Corpus seat. VR2 or R-net. Sources:
[[Permobil K-C300 Owner's Manual (205220)]] (2014) and
[[PS Junior Seat User Manual (205228)]].

## Service notes

- **Main fuse is a 50 A fuse that must be replaced if it blows** (not a
  resettable breaker like the other Permobil chairs). It's under the
  front battery cover, on top of the front battery.
- Tires: front (drive) 3.00 × 8, rear 210 × 65, **29 psi**. Safe slope
  only **6°**. **Support wheels must always be fitted.**
- **Batteries** (group 34 gel, 60 Ah): front under the front battery
  cover, rear under the rear cover (guide lips both sides). Leave the
  strap on the new battery. **Replace both as a matched set**
  ([[Mobility-Batteries]]).
- **PS Junior max user weight depends on seat depth:** 17 in = 165 lb,
  down to 10 in = 66 lb (full table on the source page). Arm rest screws
  **10 Nm**.
- Diagnostics: [[R-net-Diagnostics]], VR2 on [[PG-Drives-Trip-Codes]].
