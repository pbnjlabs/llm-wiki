# Little Wave Arc (pediatric folding tilt)

Ki Mobility's **pediatric folding tilt-in-space** chair, **165 lb (75 kg)**, transit 165 lb. 20 kg (44 lb) as tested.

- **Hand or foot tilt.** Foot pedal cables: the mech-lock actuators must be fully released with the pedal up and slide freely with it pressed. Lanyard pin locks the triggers.
- Options: contracture and multi-angle footrests, reclining and dynamic backs, attendant foot lock, hemi wheel lock, canopy, IV pole, vent and battery tray.
- Tech manual: **two seat depth procedures (builds before and after 08/12/2026)**, adjusting the upper roller, rigidizer bar. Pivot joint screws 240 in-lb, 144 in-lb elsewhere.
