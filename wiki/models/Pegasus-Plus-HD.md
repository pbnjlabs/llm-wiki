# Pegasus Plus HD

Heavy-duty **carbon-fiber folding power wheelchair**, **440 lb (200 kg)**,
sold by **Discover Your Mobility Inc.** (Warren, MI; **866-868-9694**).
Same JBH manual template as the [[Pegasus Plus (DC02)]], but a bigger
chair: 250 W motors (DC02: 180 W), 490 mm seat (400 mm), 23 kg (19 kg),
and its manual's sample frame number reads **DC06**. Source:
[[Pegasus Plus HD User Manual (HD-06-02-2024)]] (complete, 56 pp.).

## Specs

440 lb; 2 × 250 W brushless rear drive; **2 × 24 V 6 Ah lithium**
(29.4 V 2 A charger); 3.7 mph; ≥ 12.4 mi; **max 8°**; solid PU wheels
175 × 50 front, 325 × 55 rear; seat 490 × 450 mm; folds to 600 × 340 ×
800 mm; IPX4.

## Service notes

- **Fault codes** show on the controller screen (full table on the
  source page). Most useful: **01** joystick off neutral at power-up;
  **02 / 46** brake not engaged or left drive abnormal, i.e. **a freewheel
  lever left in manual**; **22** battery plug; **26 / 31** charger;
  **30** flat; **43 / 54, 53 / 64** a motor not plugged in; **48 / 59,
  51–52, 62–63, 72–99** replace the motor driver.
- **Freewheel levers:** the manual's text says push down, its photo says
  **up = freewheel, down = drive**. Check the label on the motor.
- **Charging:** power off, charger to the chair first; **max 6 h** with
  both batteries in, **3 h** for one battery (or direct to a battery with
  the pigtail). Orange = charging, green = full.
- **Batteries:** replace **both** together (house standard,
  [[Mobility-Batteries]]). Store at about 80%, top up every 2 months.
  Airline: ≤ 25% charge.
- **Weekly brake test:** nudge the joystick until the brakes click, in
  four directions. Monthly: anti-tip wheels, tire wear, fork play.
- Controller connector pins bend: straighten carefully.
- Hold a screen button **5 s** for the equipment page (**device ID,
  firmware**) to quote to the distributor.
- **Warranty:** frame 3 yr, motors and controller 1 yr, battery 6 months,
  wear parts 3 months; parts only. Parts from Discover Your Mobility.
- Not a vehicle seat; never ride in it in a vehicle.
