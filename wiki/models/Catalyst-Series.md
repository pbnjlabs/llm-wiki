# Catalyst Series (folding)

Ki Mobility's **folding** chairs.

| Model | Standard | HD | Transit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Catalyst 4** | 250 lb (113 kg) | 350 lb (159 kg) | **not rated** | 4E/E variants: attendant locks, width/depth change kits |
| **Catalyst 5** | 300 lb (136 kg) | 350 lb (159 kg) | 300 lb | Reclining, dynamic and half-folding backs, one-arm drive, amputee axle plate |
| **Catalyst 5Vx** | 300 lb (136 kg) | 350 lb (159 kg) | 300 lb | Adjustable height back, transit wheels, narrower (730 mm) |

All three are about 16 kg (35 lb) as tested; Catalyst 4 and 5 fold to 760 × 330 × 730 mm.

- **Never ride in a Catalyst 5 with an oxygen tank holder fitted.** Transit chairs: remove the seat pan's four pins before lifting it.
- Lifetime warranty on the frame, cross braces, hangers and quick-release axles.
- Tech manual ([[Catalyst and Spark Technical Manual (DCN0485.16)]]): axle plates (universal, amputee, vertical), caster squaring, **chair tracking/pulling correction**, HD configuration, **replacing cross tubes**, one-arm drive, drum brake, hemi wheel lock. Values in the text: 55 ft-lb bottom nut, 35–41 ft-lb outside nut, 8 ft-lb, 144 in-lb, 50 in-lb wheel lock bars.
