# GR596 Eagle All Terrain (Golden)

The successor to the [[GR595-Eagle]]: bigger, faster and much heavier,
with a 200 A controller, turn signals and NFC key fobs. **The heaviest
Golden device on file (377–407 lb).** Link to [[Golden]].

## Documents on file

- [[GR596 Eagle All Terrain Owners Manual]] (Rev. B, Oct 2025)

No IPB or service guide yet.

## Specs

| Capacity | 500 lb | 500 lb |

13 × 6.5 in pneumatic tires (**35 psi**), 76 in turning radius, 6° max
incline. Warranty **5 yr frame, 13 months drive train and electronics**,
1 yr batteries (the Eagle had 2 yr).

> [!warning] Vehicle lifts
> At 377–407 lb, 63 in long and nearly 32 in wide, it's over the
> capacity of every outside scooter lift on [[Mobility-Device-Weights]]
> (AL160 and Bruno ASL-275: 350 lb; AL100HD: 400 lb). Check with Harmar
> or Bruno before quoting any lift.

## Service notes

- **E-codes** on the display, with beeps: **E1 = sleep mode** (15 min
  idle with the key on; key off and on), E2–E9 = the S-Drive codes
  ([[S-Drive-Beep-Codes]]). E3 can appear from heavy regenerative
  braking downhill; E9 can come from low batteries.
- Power on with the key **or an NFC fob** held to the reader above the
  display.
- **Freewheel switch** at the rear near the right drive wheel; only
  works with the batteries in ([[Freewheel-Mode]]).
- Assembly: rear shroud off (unplug its wiring) to drop the batteries
  in; the seat post goes in one way only; the seat drops on at 45° and
  turns forward to lock.
- Charger port on the left side of the tiller.
