# Go Go Sport (73 / 74)

Pride's sturdiest Go Go travel scooter, **325 lb**: **73 = 3-wheel, 74
= 4-wheel**. Spec sheet and the travel scooter owner's manual ([[Go Go Sport Specifications (INFINFB3254)]],
Rev E, Apr 2025).

| | 3-wheel (73) | 4-wheel (74) |
|---|---|---|
| Class / capacity | A / 325 lb | A / 325 lb |
| Max speed | 4.7 mph US/Canada (4 mph elsewhere) | same |
| Ground clearance | 3 in mid-frame, 1.5 in at motor | same |
| Turning radius | 36 in | 50.5 in; **41.5 in with EZ Turn** |
| Length / width | 42 / 21.25 in | 43 / 21.25 in |
| Tires (solid, 3 in wide) | 8 in front, 9 in rear | 9 in front and rear |
| Range | 12.75 mi | 12 mi |
| Weight without batteries (with seat) | 92.75 lb | 100 lb |
| Heaviest piece (front section) | 35.75 lb | 44 lb |

Both: folding seat 17 × 17 in, 23 lb; seat height 14 in, seat-to-deck
17–18 in, overall height 35–36 in; rear-wheel drive, sealed transaxle,
24 V motor; regenerative + electromechanical brakes; two 12 V **18 Ah**
batteries in a **27.5 lb pack**; **2 A** off-board charger; max slope
6°; climbs 2 in.
