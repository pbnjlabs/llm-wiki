# Hybrid Vehicle Lifts (AL600 / AL690 / AL6000 / AL6000HD)

A **third distinct Harmar vehicle-lift architecture**, alongside
[[AL-Series-Outside-Vehicle-Lifts]] (hitch-mounted, platform folds down
behind the vehicle) and [[Hoist-Series-Inside-Vehicle-Lifts]]
(cargo-mounted, boom arm swings outside to dock). "Hybrid" lifts mount
**inside** the cargo area on a structural rear leg (+ front support/2nd
leg) and use a powered tower to swing an unoccupied mobility device's
platform straight out the back or side door — no hitch, no boom, just a
telescoping/rotating tower. Indications of use across every Hybrid
manual: lift, secure, and transport an **unoccupied** mobility device for
mobility-impaired persons — **not for human transport**, identical
restriction to the other two architectures.

This resolves a previously-open item: [[Harmar Parts Diagrams]] had
flagged an "AL600 Hybrid" tower/base/platform assembly known only from an
exploded-view drawing, filed under the AL-Series page as a thin,
unconfirmed variant. Full owner's and install manuals (found in a later
`MANUALS/Harmar/` drop) confirm AL600 is **not** an AL-Series
hitch-mounted variant at all — it's the flagship model of this separate
inside-mounted family. [[AL-Series-Outside-Vehicle-Lifts]] has been
corrected accordingly.

## Two hardware generations

Both generations share the same core mechanism (powered tower: Lifting
Motor for up/down, Rotational Motor for in/out; wired 2-button hand
control or optional wireless key-fob; magnetic-back hand control; 2-min
auto-off; 1/4" hex manual-override ports on both motors for power
failure) and the same 3-year limited parts warranty (see
[[Harmar-Warranty]]), but differ enough in generation/accessory depth to
document separately:

- **AL6000 / AL6000HD** (older, 2021-2022: [[AL6000 Series Owner's Manual (2021, Rev A)]],
  [[AL6000 Series Installation and Service Manual (2022, Rev C)]]) — the
  richer accessory set: optional Q'Straint auto-retracting straps,
  optional hold-down arm (presses the seat cushion directly, vertically
  and horizontally adjustable), optional puddle lights, adjustable
  L-shaped mounting brackets, a BA25 folding-3rd-row kit, and platform
  tilt adjustment. Rail lubrication maintenance is documented in fine,
  rail-by-rail detail (Loctite LB 8700/Moly-50 grease).
- **AL600 / AL690** (current, 2025: [[Hybrid Vehicle Lifts Owner's Manual (AL600-AL690, 2025)]],
  [[Hybrid Vehicle Lifts Installation and Service Manual (AL600-AL690, 2025)]])
  — a simplified successor: cambuckle strap-hook securement only (no
  retractors/hold-down-arm/puddle-lights options documented), a BA-02 kit
  for folding 3rd rows instead of BA25, and a single blanket rail-grease
  instruction (Jet Lube CB-2) rather than the AL6000's detailed diagrams.
  Splits into two named mounting configurations instead of a HD/non-HD
  capacity split:
  - **AL600** — rear/3rd-row mount, platform swings out the back
  - **AL690** — 2nd-row/side-door mount, front leg + rear leg instead of
    a single rear leg, platform swings out the side

## Shared accessories
- **AL617 Extension Plate** — lets a scooter's lighter front wheels rest
  ahead of the main platform so the heaviest portion (drive wheels/
  motors/batteries) still lands closest to the tower; referenced by both
  generations' owner's manuals, confirming it's a cross-generation
  accessory
- **Anchor Plate** (center-seat-post chairs/scooters) or **U-Bolts, kit
  ALA580** (tubular-frame devices without a center post) for attaching
  strap hooks to the mobility device — identical hardware/procedure
  across all four models

## Installation (common pattern across both generations)
Two-person job (single-person disassembly workaround documented in both
install manuals — detach tower from base, place base first, reattach
tower); wiring harness run first for power during install (~19',
SAE J1128, black-to-negative-first/red-to-positive-last, 20A self-
resetting breaker — same convention as [[Vehicle-Lift-Power-Wiring]]);
rear/front leg(s) structurally mounted via existing seat attachment
points ("dog bones") where available, or two drilled 3/8" holes
(avoiding gas tank/fuel/brake/electrical lines) where not; leg spacing is
measured in inches from the relevant closed door's internal surface and
varies by model/mount location (see individual source pages for exact
figures).

## Troubleshooting
Both generations use the same core diagnostic: bad electrical
connection(s)/circuit breaker is the stated #1 cause of a slow,
intermittent, or non-operating lift, isolated by progressively applying
known-good power closer to the motor (shop battery direct → lift plug →
through vehicle harness). Front/back tipping during lift = mobility
device positioned unevenly on the platform, not a mechanical fault.
Rattling while driving = platform not fully docked/locked (press "IN"
until seated).
