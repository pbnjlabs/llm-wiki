# VPL-3300B (Bruno commercial VPL)

Bruno's **commercial** battery vertical platform lifts. These are
"short commercial" **VPL-3353B / VPL-3375B** and "tall commercial"
**VPL-3310B / 3312B / 3314B** in TechMate
([[TechMate VPL Troubleshooter]]). The install and operator manuals, the
3-gate supplement and the five enclosure supplements were pulled from
Brunopartner on 2026-10-22. The ILS spec sheet, drawings and kit sheets
are below.

## From the manuals

- **Unenclosed short commercial (3353B/3375B)**
  ([[VPL-3300B Installation Manual (2022)]], [[VPL-3300B Operator Manual (2023)]]):
  - 793 / 835 lb (17 / 34 Ah), ½ hp, **53 in travel**. A18.1 Section 2
    limits unenclosed travel to **60 in**.
  - **Top and bottom final limits** standard. Options include a pit
    switch, a telephone kit and door/gate operators.
  - **AC is hard-wired to a dealer-supplied disconnect.**
  - **Junction boxes on both sides:** upper = AC in, lower = landing
    controls (6-wire gate, 3-wire call/send).
  - **Call/send box at least 3 ft from the platform**: white up, black
    down, red key.
  - **PCB-00164 timer:** 1–10 s for the electric strike or door
    operator (JP8/VR2 top, JP9/VR3 bottom).
  - **Test:** with the gate wheel held unlocked it must stop within 2 in.
  - Batteries BTR-12003 (17 Ah) / BTR-12001 (34 Ah). Don't bump the
    platform with the hold bar off.
  - Troubleshooting adds "motor if the relays click".
  - **ESI gate:** hold UP a few seconds at the top to release it. The
    bypass key is for emergencies only.
- **Tall commercial (3310B/3312B/3314B)**
  ([[VPL-3310B Tall Commercial Installation Manual (2020)]], [[VPL-3310B Tall Commercial Operator Manual (2019)]]):
  - 1210 / 1304 / 1400 lb, **1 hp**, travel 123 / 147 / 171 in.
  - Usually in a **hoistway**. **A non-flush door needs a 60° deflector
    shield.** Walls within ⅛ in of plumb.
  - **AC is hard-wired to the tower-side disconnect and is not fused.**
  - Two-piece tower hoisted in (crate plywood protects the wall, ½ in gap
    behind the tower). Line up the **red marks on the screw halves**, then
    the coiled spring pin.
  - **Emergency lowering key switch:** mounted **outside the hoistway**,
    turn and hold to LOWER. It **bypasses the safeties, so authorized
    personnel only**.
  - **Lock out three sources: DC breaker, AC and the emergency lowering
    switch.**
- **3-gate system (3353B)** ([[VPL-3353B 3-Gate Installation Manual (2019)]]):
  - Outer gate, wing and side walls. Actuator bracket **6.5 in** from the
    side wall edge.
  - **Runs with the gates open = the yellow jumper is still in the
    controller.**
  - Stops 2 in above the landing = adjust the gate rod or actuator.
- **Enclosures** ([[VPL Commercial Enclosure Installation Manuals (2019)]]):
  - Straight-through, 90° adjacent and same-side styles.
  - **Pull the tower breaker before plugging in the door harness**, or
    the 24 V fuse blows.
  - The door closer comes off for an automatic operator.
  - Leveling bolts in the corners.
  - Enclosure checks: yearly controls, and every 2 months the platform
    must not move with a door open.

## Spec summary (VPL-3353B, ILS-01100 Rev 14, 2021)

- 750 lb, one passenger with a mobility device; **commercial**, indoor or
  outdoor.
- 24 VDC battery drive, 24 VDC relay control, **35 A breaker and
  disconnect**, 10 ft/min, precision landing control (no separate
  brake).
- 1 in ACME screw, bronze drive and safety nuts, 3.94:1 dual poly-V belts.
- Travel **11–53 in**, 2 stops (tall 3310B-series: 3-stop kit VPL-K-3349).
- **Upper and lower final limits.** **Lighted controls** and an
  **illuminated E-stop with alarm** as standard. **Platform gate
  standard** (Bruno EMI interlock).
- Options: 34 Ah batteries, pit switch, landing and platform gate
  operators, flood sensor, manual lowering tool.
- Site prep as for the residential VPLs (GFI 15 A, 4 in 3500 psi slab,
  four anchors, 80 in headroom, 3/8–3/4 in sill gap, 125 lb fascia); the
  unenclosed 3353B needs **no wall attachment**, but the housing must stay
  intact. See [[ILS - VPL Technical Specifications]].

## Notes

- **Commercial differences** that matter in the field: the weekly
  operational checklist is required; the **door operator must be
  hardwired** (signal and AC in separate conduits); the 18-conductor
  travelling cable carries **phone power (BLK16/17)**
  ([[ILS - VPL Traveling Cable Kits]]); flush-mount doors use the
  12-conductor **VPL-33287** harness.
- **Commercial VPLs have a relay** that times the interlocks, so a delay
  timer is only recommended there for electric strikes
  ([[Tech Tip - VPL Delay Timers and ESI Shut-off Timers]]).
