# Pegasus Plus (DC02)

A folding power wheelchair manufactured by **Anhui JBH Medical Apparatus
Company Limited** (No. 116, Qicang Road, Mingguang, Anhui, 239400,
China; www.jbhmedical.com) under model number **DC02** — "Pegasus Plus
Heavy Duty" is Performance Medical Supply's own filename/dealer label;
no "Pegasus" branding appears anywhere in the manual itself, which is
entirely JBH-branded. **Confirmed as a different manufacturer** from the
other three [[Red Hawk]] models (Nanjing Jin Bai He, a different company
at a different address) despite the superficially similar "JBH"
abbreviation — see [[Red Hawk]] for the full naming-coincidence note.

## Specs
- Frame: **carbon fiber** (the other three Red Hawk models use steel/
  aluminum frames per their construction diagrams)
- Max weight capacity: **140 kg**
- Motor: 180W×2 brushless, rear-drive
- Battery: 24V 6Ah×2 lithium
- Max speed 6 km/h (3.7mph); max grade 8°; driving range 25km
  (15.5 miles) — longest range of the four Red Hawk models
- Seat: 400mm width × 400mm depth; armrest spacing 490mm
- Net weight (finished product) 19kg; gross weight with export packing
  24.5kg
- Rated for a 140kg simulated human-model load test per the manual's own
  safety guidance

## Indications for use
Motor-driven indoor/outdoor transportation for a disabled or elderly
person limited to a seated position — same general indication as the
other Red Hawk models, but this manual is notably more clinically
detailed (contraindications section: not for users with visual weakness,
intellectual impairment, neck disease, paraplegia below the chest,
osteoporosis, or hypochondriasis, unless operated by others).

## Assembly
Ships disassembled further than the other Red Hawk models: joystick,
chair, tools, charger, lithium battery (×2), safety belt, seat cushion,
and user's manual are packed separately. Expand by grasping the push
bar and pulling up while pushing the seat down until it clicks, then
attach seat cushion, backrest cushion (Velcro, JBH logo to the rear),
and joystick controller (slot in, tighten knob, connect wire harness
matching white-arrow-to-white-arrow polarity).
