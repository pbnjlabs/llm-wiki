# Permobil Koala MiniFlex

Small **front-wheel-drive** power chair for young children: **35 kg
(77 lb)** max user, 6.5 km/h, R-net PM120, **2 × 40 Ah gel**. Source:
[[Permobil Koala MiniFlex Owner's Manual (2014)]].

## Service notes

- Tires 2.50 × 8 drive and 200 × 50 pivot, **200 kPa (29 psi)** (the
  maintenance text allows 200–250). Drive rim and wheel screws **24 Nm**.
- **Battery replacement:** seat fully up, **disconnect the crush
  protection cable** with the battery cover, remove the spacer plate,
  note positions; **reconnect the crush protection** afterwards.
  **Replace both as a matched set** ([[Mobility-Batteries]]).
- 63 A breaker lever at the back of the chassis; safe slope **6°**.
- Diagnostics: [[R-net-Diagnostics]].
