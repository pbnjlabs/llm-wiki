# GR595 Eagle (Golden)

Golden's **off-road, heavy-duty 4-wheel scooter**, on Golden's 2026 VA
lineup. It followed the [[GR575-Patriot]]; its successor is the
[[GR596-Eagle-All-Terrain]] (377–407 lb, 200 A, 11.2 mph). Link to
[[Golden]].

No service guide is on file.

## Electrical and diagnostics

- **PG S-Drive** controller (140 A, MBC-595-CNT). Fault codes flash and
  show as an E-code on the LCD: [[S-Drive-Beep-Codes]] (2/3/5/6/7/8/9).
  Codes and serial number are printed **under the floor mat**.
- Batteries: **22NF** (MBE-BATT22-BW) or **Group 24 75 Ah**
  (MBE-BATT24-BW). Charger: **8 A** high-power (MBE-8ACHG-HP), XLR port.
- Circuit breaker with harness (MBE-595-BREAKER); Bluetooth harness;
  turn signals.
- Tires: 13 in pneumatic, **35 psi**.

## Specs ([[Golden Sell Sheets (2018-2026)]])

- **500 lb** capacity; **8 mph**; range **23 mi** (2-NF22) or **32 mi**
  (optional 2-75 Ah).
- **56.5 × 27 in**, wheelbase **40 in**, 5 in ground clearance, 69 in
  turning radius.
- **Assembled weight 302.5 lb** with NF22 batteries (37 lb each); the
  75 Ah batteries are 52 lb each, so it's heavier with those. Base 179
  lb, seat 49.5 lb.
- The **2022 owner's manual** gives **300 lb assembled with a 239 lb
  base**. The base weights don't agree, so weigh the unit before
  choosing a lift.
- **140 A controller**, front and rear suspension, 13 in pneumatic tires,
  full LED lights, high-back captain's seat.
- Warranty: 5 yr frame, 2 yr drive train and electronics, 1 yr
  batteries.

**Vehicle lifts:** the heaviest and longest Golden scooter on file. See
[[Mobility-Device-Weights]] before choosing a lift.
