# MedMizer AC GOV Hospital Bed (Basic and Advanced)

MedMizer's **AC GOV** hospital bed, in **Basic** and **Advanced**
versions, handled by **Performance Medical Solutions**. Accessories
covered by MedMizer's videos: **static and dynamic mattresses**, **half
and full rails**, and a **trapeze**. The only document on file is the
PMS/MedMizer training packet; there's no MedMizer manual in the wiki yet
(the manuals are behind the AC GOV Bed Support QR code).

## Key rules

- **Never lift or move the bed with one technician.** Two techs load and
  unload each piece; strap it in the van.
- Split the bed for transport by unplugging **control box ports 3 and
  4**, then removing the **black bolt** (bottom, one side) and the **top
  bolts, nuts and spacers** (keep them in the hardware bag). Reassemble
  with the **bolt head outside**, and plug ports 3 and 4 back in.
- Steel-toed footwear; floor blanket in the van.
