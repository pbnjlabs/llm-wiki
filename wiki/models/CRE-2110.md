# CRE-2110 (Bruno Elite Curve)

Bruno's custom curved-rail residential/commercial stairlift. Variants:
CRE-2110, CRE-2110C, CRE-2110CAN, CRE-2110G, CRE-2110OS. Sibling in spirit
to the straight-rail [[SRE-2010]]/[[SRE-3050]] Elite/Elan lines, but rail is
custom-fabricated per staircase (see [[Staircase-Measuring-System]]) rather
than off-the-shelf.

## Spec summary

- Rated load: 400 lb (181 kg); CRE-2110G: 420 lb (190 kg)
- Power: 24VDC, 2×7AH batteries; 24VDC direct-drive gear-motor, .68 HP,
  58:1 self-locking worm gearbox
- Speed: max 25 ft/min (0.13 m/s)
- Track: custom lengths to 60 ft (18m), 5/16" mild steel welded rail with
  welded gear rack, joined with welded side clamp blocks
- Braking: dynamic motor braking + self-locking worm gearbox +
  electro-mechanical motor brake; **overspeed brake is optional** on
  CRE-2110C/G/OS/CAN, not standard on base CRE-2110 (contrast the
  straight-rail SRE line, where overspeed is standard) — see
  [[Overspeed-Safety-Brake]]
- Call/send: 2.4GHz RF (C/G/CAN variants) or 900MHz-intermediary IR
  (base CRE-2110/OS)

## Key procedures

- **Installation**: custom rail arrives in numbered sections (clamps sized
  per section since a curve's clamp radius differs from straight sections);
  slope angle is checked every 2 ft of rail with an angle finder against
  job-specific elevation-view drawings. Horizontal zero-degree-slope
  adjustment screw is factory-set — do not touch without contacting Bruno.
- **Overspeed reset (commercial units only, CRE-2110C/G/OS/CAN)** — see
  [[Overspeed-Safety-Brake]]. **Differs mechanically from the straight-rail
  SRE reset**: instead of a motor-shaft socket tool, the CRE-2110 uses two
  levers (overspeed lever + cam lever) — rotate the overspeed switch lever
  while pushing in on the wires to engage the switch, then rotate the cam
  lever until you *just feel* it engage. Rotating the lever too far will
  prevent reset.
- **Diagnostic codes**: shares the same code table as [[SRE-2010]] and
  [[SRE-3000]] — see [[Diagnostic-Codes]].
- **Remote learning/programming**: same LEARN/ERASE pattern as the SRE
  line. Optional IR/RF repeater kit (CRE-K-2147) available for whole-home
  multi-unit or non-line-of-sight installs.
- **Circuit breaker**: two most frequent trip causes — foreign object
  jamming rail/gear rack, or exceeding rated load capacity.

## Troubleshooting

Start with [[Stairlift-Troubleshooting]] (symptom-first, from Bruno's
[[TechMate Stairlift Troubleshooter]], which has a CRE-specific
single-beep tree). CRE specifics:
- **Charges only at the ends (and mid-park)**: C3 when stopped between
  stations is expected.
- **L2 at the bottom or L3 at the top = wrong hand setting**: DIP switch
  3 on the diagnostic board under the seat, ON = right hand.
- TechMate: the overspeed is tripped when the hole isn't centered in the
  notch. Its fix is to remove the black cap and turn the manual crank
  counter-clockwise (5/16" / 8 mm) until the hole centers. That's not the
  install manual's two-lever reset (Key procedures above). Use the manual
  if they disagree.
- Grinding: first check the carriage isn't rubbing the rail (gear on the
  rack flange → ride-out adjustment; guides/rollers out of place).
- **Ride-out** (gear climbing the rack on inside turns): with seat and
  footrest on, press lightly on the footrest while it runs; it goes away
  → fine. Check for paint in the rack, the nylon guide on the downside
  axle, and the business-card gap between rack and flange (card fits →
  call Bruno). Fix: loosen the four gear-motor screws and turn the
  tension screw out **½ turn**, then ¼ more if needed; never
  over-tighten it ([[Tech Tip - CRE Ride-Out]]).
- **Dead at the top, no lights, no B+/B−:** the **final limit** is up on
  its ramp. Slide the end bracket / charge contacts back so the end limit
  trips first ([[Tech Tip - CRE-2110 Final Limit and End Limit Adjustment]]).
- **Rail doesn't fit at install:** fix the bottom section's slope before
  anything else; rail at least **2½ in from the step nose**; then check
  the "C" and landing dimensions against the print
  ([[Tech Tip - CRE Fit Issues Service Presentation]]).
- **IR repeater ("puck") system** (receivers in wall outlets, emitter on
  the upper battery): channels on every receiver and the emitter must
  match. Full steps on [[Stairlift-Troubleshooting]]. Bruno's install
  training is the Brainshark video "Installation of IR Receivers for CRE".
- RF double remote failure: newer remote **CRE-01065**, board
  **SRV-2110-001**, antenna parts SRE-20778 + ANT-00109 (from an
  internal-only TechMate branch).
