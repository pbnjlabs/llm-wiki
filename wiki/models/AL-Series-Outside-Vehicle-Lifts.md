# AL-Series Outside Vehicle Lifts

Harmar's hitch-mounted "Outside Vehicle Lift" family — transports an
**unoccupied** mobility device (scooter, power chair, or manual
wheelchair) on a fold-down platform behind the vehicle. Both manuals on
file are generic across the whole line despite being filed under
`AL100-OutsideLifts-*` filenames; confirmed models named across the two
documents: AL003, AL010, AL015, AL030, AL050, AL100, AL100-DE, AL100HD,
AL105, AL105L, AL160, AL300, AL300-FULL, AL300HD, AL301XL, AL301XL-HD,
AL500, AL500HD, AL560, AL560XL, AL580, AL580XL, AL580-HDX.

This is Harmar's counterpart to Bruno's [[PUL-1100]]/[[ASL-275]] (arm-lift
and hitch-platform vehicle lifts, respectively). Worth a future
side-by-side with [[Vehicle-Lift-Power-Wiring]] if a second
manufacturer's wiring concept page is ever justified — not written yet
since only Bruno's version exists as a concept page today.

## Model differentiation (from the hitch-weight/adapter tables — no
individual per-model manual exists)
- **AL003 / AL030** — lightest duty, ball-hitch compatible (25-40 lb lift
  weight); AL030 "Power Tote" has its own ball-mount installation section
- **AL100 / AL100-DE / AL100HD / AL160 / AL300 / AL300HD** — mid-weight,
  Class 2/3/5, wheel-cradle adjustment procedure shared across
  AL100/160/300
- **AL105 / AL105L** — swing-away accessory/variant (opens for trunk
  access); AL105L uses a push-down-and-away latch release instead of a
  pull pin
- **AL300-FULL / AL560 / AL560XL / AL580 / AL580XL** — power-chair-
  capable, hold-down-arm securement instead of straps; AL560/AL560XL use
  a dedicated platform-gap adjustment
- **AL580-HDX** — heaviest-duty, purpose-built for mid-wheel power chairs
  (wheel cradles preset for Quantum Edge 2.0/3.0 power bases), adjustable
  platform gaps in 1/2" increments, requires the actual end-user power
  chair present during install to validate frame/battery-tray clearance

## Installation
- Hitch classes 1, 2, 3, 5; per-model tongue-weight table plus a
  ~40-part-number hitch-adapter sizing chart (by HV/D1/D2 hitch geometry)
- 23' vehicle wiring harness (SAE J1128), black wire to battery negative
  first / red to positive last, 20A self-resetting circuit breaker; a
  stand-alone battery pack (BAT141386) is offered for hybrid/EV vehicles
  without a usable 12V accessory battery
- Anchor plate (center-seat-post mobility devices) or U-bolt kit
  (tubular-frame devices) for strap-type securement models

## Operation notes
- **NOT for human transport** — occupied use voids the warranty
- Always walk the mobility device on/off manually (never drive it on)
- A ratcheting sound at full up/down travel is normal (actuator clutch
  overload protection) **only in the UP direction** — a ratchet while
  lowering means something is wrong (do not continue)
- Manual override: hex-shaft crank under a coin/key-removable motor-cover
  cap, after unplugging the lift/vehicle harness connectors
