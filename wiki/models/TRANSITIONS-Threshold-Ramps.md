# TRANSITIONS Threshold Ramps

EZ-ACCESS's threshold line for small rises at doorways, sliding-door
tracks and single low steps. Five products. **Every product now has
EZ-ACCESS's own instructions and spec sheet** (added 2026-10-26), and
those override the **2021 web article** ([[Different Types of Threshold Ramps]])
and the 2018 parts sheet [[EZ Access Parts and Pieces]] (origin
unknown) wherever they differ:

- [[TRANSITIONS Angled Entry Plate Instructions and Specs]] (2021, 2017)
- [[TRANSITIONS Angled Entry Ramp Instructions and Specs]] (2021, 2017)
- [[TRANSITIONS Modular Entry Ramp Instructions and Specs (2022)]]
- [[TRANSITIONS Angled Entry Mat Instructions]] (2025) and
  [[TRANSITIONS Angled Entry Mat Technical Specifications (2025)]]
- [[TRANSITIONS Modular Entry Mat Instructions and Specs]] (2021, 2020)

**Common to all five:** wheelchairs and scooters, **not commercial
use**; must sit **flush** against the landing; **fasten or anchor before
use**; check for damage and tighten fasteners regularly; clean with
non-alkaline detergent; lifetime limited warranty.

## Sizing tables

**Angled Entry Plate (TAEP)**: one-piece aluminum, 0.695 in thick,
**700 lb (350 lb per wheel)**, applied grip surface, ships assembled.

Fasten through the two top holes: 1/4 × 1 in self-drilling stainless
screws into wood (supplied), or 1/4 × 1-1/2 in pin drive anchors into

**Angled Entry Ramp (TAER)**: aluminum, self-supporting, screw-in
swivel feet, **700 lb (350 lb per wheel)**, two holes in the bottom for

instructions:

| Model | Size (L × W) | Weight | No feet | With feet |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TAER 12 | 12 × 36.25 in | 5.15 lb | 1-3/8 in | 1-15/16 – 2-3/4 in (1 in feet) |
| TAER 24 | 24 × 36.25 in | 10.4 lb | 1-11/16 in | 2-1/8 – 3-3/8 in (1-1/2 in feet); 3-3/16 – 4-3/8 in (2-1/2 in feet) |
| TAER 36 | 36 × 36.25 in | 15.15 lb | 3-5/16 in | 3-5/8 – 5-7/8 in (2-1/2 in feet) |

**Resolved 2026-10-26:** the manufacturer's table matches the 2021
article and Harmar's 2025 sell sheet (1⅜ – 5⅞ in overall), so **size by
it**. The 2018 parts sheet's narrower ranges (1.5–2, 2.25–3.5, 3.5–4.5
in) don't match any manufacturer document and are no longer used.
Without feet each ramp fits only its own height, so there are small
gaps between the no-feet height and the feet range (e.g. TAER 12 at
1-1/2 – 1-7/8 in). The TAER 24 ships with both 1-1/2 and 2-1/2 in feet.
The parts sheet also shows a **6 in transition** at the low end of each
ramp. The field note below about removing the leveling feet applies to
this product.

Assemble from the 1 in foot up: join each extension at about 45°,
engage the bulb and rotate down, then drive a #10-24 × 1/2 in screw
into the bulb **on both sides of every joint**. The screws only keep it
aligned: lift it by opposite corners or take it apart to move it.
Anchor the foot and the flap: 1/8 in pilot holes and the supplied 1/4 ×
1 in screws into wood or aluminum, or installer-supplied **1/2 in**
anchors set flush into concrete.

**Angled Entry Mat (TAEM)**: one-piece recycled rubber, **850 lb**,
black or grey, Blank or Welcome (Welcome from 1 in up). Usable width
36-1/16 in on every size.

| Rise | Usable length | Overall width | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1/2 in | 3 in | 36-7/8 in | 2 lb |
| 3/4 in | 5-3/4 in | 37-5/8 in | 4 lb |
| 1 in | 8-1/2 in | 38-3/8 in | 7 lb |
| 1-1/4 in | 11-1/4 in | 39-1/8 in | 11 lb |
| 1-1/2 in | 14 in | 39-7/8 in | 18 lb |
| 1-3/4 in | 16-3/4 in | 40-5/8 in | 21 lb |
| 2 in | 19-1/2 in | 41-3/8 in | 29 lb |
| 2-1/4 in | 22-1/4 in | 42-1/8 in | 38 lb |
| 2-1/2 in | 25 in | 42-7/8 in | 49 lb |

- **Fit:** the mat's top edge must be within **1/4 in** of the landing
  height and **flush against it**. If the landing is lower, **trim the
  height down**; it can also be notched for the door jamb. **Never trim
  the width.**
- **Anchor** with 2 fasteners, about 1 in in from each lower corner:
  the supplied #10 × 3-1/2 in screws into wood, or 1/4 × 1-1/4 in drive
  pin anchors into concrete (1/4 in masonry bit, 2 in deep).
- The parts sheet's "rubber threshes" (RT 1 – RT 2.5) are these mats.
  The 2021 article listed only the 1-1/2 and 2-1/2 in sizes (TAEM
  1.5-1, TAEM 2.5-1).

**Modular Entry Mat (TMEM)**: recycled rubber wedge, **850 lb**.

| Part | Size (H × L × W) | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| TMEM 2.5-1 mat | 2.5 × 24 × 48 in | 40 lb |
| TMEMR 2.25 riser (pair) | 2.25 × 24 × 24 in each | 23.5 lb each |

- Up to 2-1/2 in alone; trim the height, depth **or width** (unlike the
  Angled Entry Mat) with a reciprocating saw, razor knife or hand saw.
- **Up to 4-3/4 in** with two mats and one pair of risers: the front
  mat stays full height (trim width only), the rear mat sits on the
  risers and is screwed to them. The parts sheet's RT 3 – RT 5 builds
  match this (TMEM 2.5-2 × 2 plus TMEMR 2.25 risers).
- Fasten with 2 supplied screws each into wood (heads 1/4 in below the
  surface), or supplied 1/4 in mushroom-head pin anchors in 2 in deep
  holes, 1 in from the corners at the taper, into concrete.
- Also sold as a **cargo wedge** for a vehicle's 1–3 in drop to the
  cargo floor (cut to a cardboard template).

Install time: 40 min for 3–5 in, 20 min for ½–2½ in rubber thresholds
(see [[Ramp-Install-Time-Estimates]]).

## Gotchas

- **The Modular Entry Ramp's sizes leave gaps** above 2.25 in:
  thresholds of 2.25–2.75, 3.25–3.75, 4.25–4.75 and 5.25–5.75 in match
  no size. EZ-ACCESS's own 2022 table confirms it. Use an Angled Entry
  Ramp in those ranges (its feet ranges run from 1-15/16 to 5-7/8 in
  with small gaps of their own).
- For the Modular Entry Ramp, the number in the model name is the
  **nominal top height**. Match the measured threshold to the range
  column, not the name.
- **1/4 in rule:** the Modular Entry Ramp and both mats must sit
  within 1/4 in of the landing height. Padding underneath indoors can
  push it over.
- **Angled Entry Ramp: leveling feet and flush fit (field note from
  PMS)**: at an entry door, the Angled Entry Ramp's leveling feet can
  sometimes be removed to get the right height. Some entryways still won't sit flush with the
  transition, so check this on site. See [[Ramp-Site-Planning]].
- **Mat vs. Angled Entry Ramp:** PMS has **no house rule** for which
  to use. Choose by the measured threshold height and each product's
  range. Mats carry 850 lb, the aluminum products 700 lb.
- Threshold install rule (from [[How to Measure and Install a Ramp]]):
  seat the ramp firmly against the threshold with no gap.

## Harmar's threshold ramps (compared)

**PMS mostly uses EZ-ACCESS TRANSITIONS** (per the human). Harmar sells
two kinds:

- **Aluminum = the same TRANSITIONS products.** Harmar's 2025 sell sheet
  is co-branded with EZ-ACCESS: Modular Entry Ramp, Angled Entry Ramp
  and Angled Entry Plate, all **700 lb** ([[TRANSITIONS Aluminum Threshold Ramps Sell Sheet (Harmar, 2025)]]). Harmar's older 2022 guide
  said 600 lb; go by 700.
- **Rubber = Harmar's own design, and it works differently** ([[Harmar Rubber Threshold Ramps Spec Sheet (2020)]]):

| | EZ-ACCESS Angled Entry Mat | EZ-ACCESS Modular Entry Mat | **Harmar rubber threshold ramp** (RAMPRBKIT) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Build | **One piece** | Wedge mat + 2¼ in riser squares + a second mat | **Sections that dovetail together front to back**, each adding ½ in |
| Heights | ½ – 2½ in (¼ in steps) | Up to 2.5 in alone; ~4.75–5 in with risers | **1 – 4 in** in ½ in steps |
| How you fit it | **Trim the height down** to within ¼ in of the landing; never trim width | Trim; stack risers | Pick the number of sections; can be trimmed, cut or notched |
| Width | 36 in usable (40–43 in overall) | 48 in | 36 in usable (39–46 in overall) |
| Capacity | 850 lb | — | 850 lb |
| Slope | — | — | Max **4° (7%)**, inside the 1:12 house standard |
| Anchoring | 2 fasteners at the lower corners | — | Not stated |

A 4 in Harmar rubber ramp is roughly **53 in deep and 130 lb** if built
from all seven sections (wiki arithmetic; check the kit).
