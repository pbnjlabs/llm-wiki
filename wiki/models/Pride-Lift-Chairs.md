## Before troubleshooting

1. Transformer in a live, **non-switched** outlet; test the outlet.
2. Every connection plugged in and locked; reconnect everything, power
   included, while testing.
3. Cables free and not pinched; power cable from control box to
   transformer not crimped, cut or cracked.
4. **Dip switch** in the standard control box set right, or the hand
   control runs the wrong things.
5. **Hand control flash codes** (newer controls): buttons blinking
   **every second** = hand control or the USB device/cable (unplug the
   USB device to tell which); **every 5–7 seconds** = the chair **lost
   power** (low-power mode): check the outlet. 5-LED controls: LED 3 =
   actuator; 3 + 4 = zone 1A/1B; 3 + 5 = zone 2A/2B; 3 + 1 = back heat;
   3 + 2 = base heat.
6. **Locked hand control?** Lock/unlock button lit: hold about **10 s**
   to unlock (hold 5 s to lock).
7. Push-button hand controls are on standard chairs with serial numbers
   ending **F40 or F50** (PQ-365 FAQ).

## Symptom → check

- **Stopped / clicks but won't move:** transformer, motor, hand control,
  motor relay box, control box.
- **Drops, or drops suddenly and quits:** broken **motor clevis or
  stroke tube**, bent or broken scissors or lift frame, cracked
  lift/recline bracket, broken side bracket pin, T-bracket catching.
  Keep people out of the chair until it's found.
- **Won't lift or recline fully, sits crooked, one side lands first:**
  **dual motors out of timing** (Pride's fix is TechnoBulletin LC-029,
  not on file), bent scissors, twisted wood frame, leveling leg (tighten
  to lower, loosen to raise), uneven floor, loose bolts, worn foam.
- **Footrest won't close:** gap between the lift/recline bracket and the
  frame should be **3/16–1/4 in**; move the footrest slightly up or down
  on its holes; side brackets, scissors, stroke tube.
- **Squeaks / pops:** scissors, loose or overtight bolts, lift frame
  rubbing the wood frame (frame spacers missing), T-bracket.
- **Upholstery:** foam, fabric, chaise pad position, spring clips,
  buttons (tufting needle, about 1.5 in knot).

## Service basics

- **Setup (KD chairs):** locking clip vertical, slide the back on until
  **each side clicks**, pull up to check, fasten the fabric flaps; plug
  heat and massage quick-releases together first.
- **Most repairs:** chair seated, **unplugged**, KD back off, chair
  upside down on a table. Motors hang on clevis and cotter pins: fit the
  bottom pin first, then **recline the chair fully** to fit the top one
  (infinite-position chairs: one motor to the kidney bracket, the other
  to the lift/recline bracket). Run a full cycle after any wiring work
  to check nothing is pinched.
- **Motor types:** DRVMOTR1171 standard 24 V, DRVMOTR1139 Megamat Plus,
  DRVMOTR1308 Okin Delta Drive (top pin to the T-bar/side bracket).
- **Lift frame:** adhesive spacers on, front about 1 in from the front
  corners, T-bracket level. KD sleeve screws **6.8 N·m (5 ft·lb)**.
- **Two-button (Limoss) hand control:** replaced together with the
  motor control box.
- **Lithium battery backup** clips onto the external transformer; take
  it off for long storage (the chair drains it).
- **Measuring** for a replacement or quote: seat to floor, depth, width
  (4 in from the back), back height, overall width, arm height; don't
  compress the foam.

## Warranty (2021 insert)

Lifetime lift/recline mechanism and frame (not models 102, 105, 106,
107, 250, 358XXL; **over the weight limit voids it**); Heritage,
Infinity and Oasis: motor, hand control, control box, heat and massage
4 years full then pro-rated 25/15/5% in years 5–7; RF pendants 90 days;
lithium pack 1 year; fabric and labor not covered.
[[Pride Lift Chair Warranty (INFINFB2685)]].

## Joint Commission (house standard)

Lift chairs are delivered and set up like other equipment: check the
home setup and train the user on the hand control (including the lock
and the "don't get in or out without the hand control" rule)
([[Joint-Commission-DMEPOS]]).
