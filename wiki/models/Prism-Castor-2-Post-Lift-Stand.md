# Prism Castor 2 Post Lift Stand System

A portable, freestanding two-post aluminum track stand — an accessory
that lets a Prism/Handicare ceiling lift ([[C-450-C-625]], [[P440]])
operate anywhere without a permanent ceiling mount. Co-branded "Prism"
(product name) and "Handicare" (manual logo), confirming the same
manufacturer relationship documented on
[[P440 Owner's Manual (2021, Rev 19-Oct)]] — see [[Prism]].

## Part numbers (capacity-named to match the C-Series lifts)
| Part # | Description | Length | Load |
|---|---|---|---|
| 343100 | "Regular Track" | 82in (2083cm) | 204kg (450lb) |
| 343101 | "Track Plus" | 197in (5004cm) | 204kg (450lb) |
| 343102 | "Track Plus" | 156in (3962cm) | 283.5kg (625lb) |

The 450lb/625lb load ratings correspond exactly to the "C-450"/"C-625"
naming of [[C-450-C-625]] — this stand is clearly sized as that lift
family's portable/freestanding companion, not a generic accessory.

## Specs
- Construction: extruded aluminum (post bases, vertical supports,
  track)
- Max height 244cm (99in), floor to highest point
- Max total length 500cm (197in), depending on track length purchased
- Unit weight up to 50kg, no single piece over 15.5kg
- Adjustable dimensions: height 204-250cm, lifting-range height
  192-227cm, length 237-530cm (depending on track), distance between
  bases 206-498cm
- Mounted on castors for repositioning between rooms

## Installation
Two-person assembly with basic tools (17mm wrenches, 2.5mm Allen key,
torque wrench, level). Post assemblies bolted together first (25-35 N·m
torque), then track brackets slide onto the track and are set-screwed
(4-5.5 N·m), then track is bolted to the post assemblies (25-35 N·m).
An installation checklist covers defect inspection, torque
verification, level-floor confirmation, and scheduling annual
maintenance — but the manual references a "Required load test" without
detailing the procedure itself.

## Restrictions
- No user-serviceable parts — opening the unit voids the warranty
- Only Prism Medical ceiling lifts suitable for the stand's rated load
  may be hung from it
- Install/service restricted to persons authorized by Prism Medical
  Ltd.
