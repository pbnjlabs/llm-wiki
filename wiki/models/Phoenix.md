# Phoenix

A folding power wheelchair distributed by **Discover Your Mobility
Inc.** (66 E Ten Mile Road, Hazel Park, MI 48030; (866) 868-9694 — same
distributor as [[Falcon]] and the "Eagle HD" branding of
[[Air-Hawk-Eagle-HD]]), but documented in a **completely different
manual template** with no Nanjing Jin Bai He branding anywhere —
manufacturer identity is **not confirmed** from this manual alone,
unlike the other three Red Hawk models. See [[Red Hawk]] for the
cluster-wide picture.

## Notable quirk
The battery-care section instructs "Do not use any battery chargers
that have not been certified by **TREK Mobility**" — a third company
name that appears nowhere else in this manual (no address, no other
mention) and doesn't match either confirmed manufacturer in the
[[Red Hawk]] cluster. Likely leftover boilerplate from a template
originally written for a different distributor's rebrand; not resolved.

## Specs
- Max weight capacity: **120 kg** — lowest of the four Red Hawk models
- Motor: DC brushless, 180W×2 (lower rated power than the 250W×2 motors
  in Air Hawk/Eagle HD/Falcon), DC 24V
- Battery: lithium polymer, 6Ah×2, DC 24V
- Front wheels 7"×1.75" PU solid; rear wheels 8"×1.95" PU solid
- Max speed ≤6.0 km/h; max grade 8°; max range 20km; charging 6-8 hours
- Unfold size 940×630×950mm; fold size 640×320×750mm; net weight 21kg
  without battery

## Controller
Same functional layout as [[Air-Hawk-Eagle-HD]]/[[Falcon]] (Power On/
Off, Speed +/−, Horn, 360° joystick, 5-LED indicator) but a **different
diagnostic scheme**: a numbered red-light-flash system (1 = low battery,
2 = no joystick signal, 3 = charging, 4 = joystick moved during power-on/
check motor connections, 5 = brake solenoid disconnected/damaged) rather
than the other three models' 9-pattern slow/quick flicker chart.

## Free Wheel Mode
Two red levers, one at each rear wheel, pushed in the direction shown to
disengage the drive for manual pushing — functionally the same concept
as Air Hawk/Eagle HD/Falcon's electromagnetic brake lock/unlock lever,
described with different terminology.
