# Go Go LX (50LX / 54LX)

Pride travel scooter that comes apart for transport: **50LX = 3-wheel,
54LX = 4-wheel**. Spec sheet only on file
([[Go Go LX Specifications (INFINFB3186)]], Rev F, Nov 2019).

| | 3-wheel (50LX) | 4-wheel (54LX) |
|---|---|---|
| Class / capacity | A / 300 lb | A / 300 lb |
| Max speed | 4.4 mph | 4.4 mph |
| Ground clearance | 1.5 in at motor | 1.5 in |
| Turning radius | 33.5 in | 44.75 in (48 in UK) |
| Reversing width | 43 in | 57 in |
| Length / width | 39.75 / 20.5 in | 41.75 / 20.5 in |
| Tires (solid) | 8 in front, 8 in rear | 7 in front (8 in UK), 8 in rear |
| Range 12 Ah / 12 Ah ext. / 18 Ah / 18 Ah ext. | 6 / 8 / 9.5 / 11.7 mi | 5.5 / 8.6 / 10.5 / 12.6 mi |
| Weight without batteries (with seat) | 85 lb | 90 lb |
| Heaviest piece (front section) | 33 lb | 39 lb |

Both: folding molded seat 17 × 17 in, 23 lb; seat height 15 in,
seat-to-deck 16.25–17.75 in, overall height 33.5–35 in; rear-wheel
drive, sealed transaxle, 24 V motor; regenerative + electromechanical
brakes; two 12 V sealed batteries **12 Ah or 18 Ah**, in a **pack**: 12
Ah 19 lb, 12 Ah extended 23.5 lb, 18 Ah 30 lb, 18 Ah extended 31.75 lb;
**2 A** off-board charger; max slope 6°; climbs 2 in. With 12 Ah
batteries it doesn't meet AS/NZS 3695.2 / EN12184.
