# Jazzy EVO 613 / 613 Li

Pride Group 2 power chair (K0822/23) with a **lead-acid or lithium**
battery option.

| | EVO 613 | EVO 613 Li |
|---|---|---|
| Capacity | 300 lb | 300 lb |
| Max speed | 5.1 mph | 5.1 mph |
| Batteries | 2 × U-1 (23.5 lb) | **1 × 25.6 V 32.4 Ah LiFePO4** (18.5 lb) |
| Charger | 3.5 A | 3.5 A |
| Controller | 40 A PG **GC3** | same |

(2026 Group 2 chart, [[Pride-Power-Chair-Lineup]].) Warranty: EVO 613
Li battery **3 years** ([[Pride-Warranty]]).

## Documents

- [[Jazzy EVO Series Owners Manual (INFMANU5239)]] (Rev A, 2021).

## Notes

- Lead-acid version: the charger won't start **below 18 V**.
- Lithium version: one sealed battery box; don't open it. Charge at
  **32–104 °F**. Don't load-test lithium ([[Mobility-Batteries]]).
- **Charge reminder** after 8 h of driving without a charge.
- GC3 codes: [[Pride-Controller-Flash-Codes]]. Care:
  [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]].
