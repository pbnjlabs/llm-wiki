# K2 Plus (Savaria straight-rail stairlift)

The **K2 Plus** is the K2 for **public and Canadian installs** as well as
homes: ASME A18.1 2003–2017 §4 and §7, **CAN/CSA B355** (public) and B613
(private); residential and commercial seat kits. Install, settings, LEDs,
DIP switches and troubleshooting follow the [[K2]].

## Differences from the K2

- Four shipping boxes; **flip-up rail rated 350 lb (159 kg)** with 1 in of
  diagonal rail offset from the bottom landing.
- **Parvalux 90CB-5000** motor and gear (C2190427); its own rollers
  (vertical C6400011, side C6400012) and brake dog (C6400001); battery listed
  as **107039** (12 V 9 Ah).
- Hall calls on the **LUT440** receiver; 915 MHz remotes (keyed public
  C6190124-915, 2-button C6190134-915).
- **B355 and B613 units have no three carriage diagnostic LEDs** (the
  install guide prints "B316", most likely a misprint for B613; the owner's
  manual says B613).
- **Capacity (owner's manual):** **400 lb residential, 350 lb commercial**,
  flip-up rail 350 lb. Duty: normal 10, heavy 25, excessive 40 cycles a
  day; **max 5 starts an hour**; −10 to 40 °C.
- **Sleep:** idle over 1 h, press the remote twice; over 24 h, run it from
  the arm toggle for a second first.
- **E-stop** standard on CSA B613 and all commercial units.
- **Maintenance interval (owner's manual):** commercial every 6 months;
  residential yearly for normal use, every 6 months for heavy or excessive
  use. The MCP below (every 6 months) still governs public or
  code-inspected installs.
- **Maintenance Control Program** (ASME A18.1): every item every 6 months,
  incl. charger output to the rail, overspeed test with **125% rated load**
  (bulletin 001277), seat post bolts 40 ft-lb, flip-up rail gas spring,
  motor temperature (−10 to 40 °C normal), 9 V remote batteries.
