# GP130 Carry On Power Chair (Golden)

A small **center-wheel-drive travel power chair** with a lithium pack
and a folding seat post. Not the same product as the
[[GB120-Buzzaround-CarryOn]] scooter, though both are sold as "Carry
On". Link to [[Golden]].

## Documents on file

- [[Carry On Power Chair Owners Manual (GP130)]] (Rev. B, 2024)

## Specs

300 lb, 3.85 mph, 12.5 mi, 12.5 Ah lithium pack (6 lb), 2 A charger,
40 A controller, 38 × 24 in, 21.25 in turning radius. **74 lb
assembled** (base 46, seat 22, battery 6). See [[Mobility-Device-Weights]].

## Service notes

- **No power out of the box:** the fuse ships loose. It goes in the
  holder under the controller cover, beneath the basket (pack out first).
- **The seat carries an electrical connection** through the seat post.
  A seat that isn't fully clicked down can stop it driving.
- Freewheel levers are in the **center** of the chair; push them back
  to unlock ([[Freewheel-Mode]]).

## Fault codes

Joystick lights flash the count; some faults also sound the horn until
cleared. The controller's maker isn't named. The layout resembles the
PG VR2 table ([[TruCharge-Diagnostics]]) but has no code 6 and puts the
brake at 9.

| Flashes | Fault | Check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Low battery | Battery connections; charge |
| 2 | Left motor | Left motor connections |
| 3 | Left motor | Left motor connections; **stall** (something stopping the wheel) |
| 4 | Right motor | Right motor connections |
| 5 | Right motor | Right motor connections; stall |
| 7 | Joystick | Not centered at power-up; restart hands-off; connections |
| 8 | Controller | Controller connections |
| 9 | Motor brake | Probably in freewheel: check the levers |
| 10 | Excess battery voltage | Overcharged or overheated; battery connections; charger |
