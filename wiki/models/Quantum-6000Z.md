# Quantum 6000Z (legacy)

Older Quantum mid-wheel chair with a **rear swing-arm**, built with
**Hammer** or **H2** motors (H2 optionally with **Accu-Trac**). No
owner's manual on file.

## Documents

- [[Quantum 6000Z Parts Replacement Guide (INFMANU4847)]] (Jan 2017)

## Service notes

| Fastener | Torque |
|---|---|
| Lug nuts | 25 N·m |
| 4/5-spoke main hub nut | 51 N·m |
| Caster nut | 30 N·m |
| Strut shoulder bolts (caster arm, frame, swing-arm) | 10 N·m |
| Caster arm to frame | 27.6 N·m |
| Motor to bracket (Hammer, H2) | **12 N·m** |
| Swing-arm to frame | 21.5 N·m |
| Articulating beam | 59 N·m |

H2 motors with Accu-Trac: unplug the Accu-Trac lead before removing the
motor. Freewheel (Hammer/H2): pull **up** = drive, push **down** =
freewheel.
