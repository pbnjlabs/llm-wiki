# SRE-2750 (Bruno Electra-Ride LT — legacy)

Bruno's lighter straight stairlift from around 2009–2011, the
**Electra-Ride LT**. It came before the Elan ([[SRE-3000]]). It is no
longer sold. Manuals were pulled from Brunopartner on 2026-10-22.

## Specs (2011 install manual)

- **275 lb**, **0–32 ft/min**.
- 24 VDC 2-pole **½ hp**, self-locking gearbox, rack and pinion, worm
  gear brake.
- 45° maximum. The rail is an **aluminum channel** with an integral rack.
- Swivel 0°, 67° and optional 90°, top and bottom. Stairs at least 32 in
  wide.
- IR transmitters. **End charging** through **4 charge strip blocks**.
- 24 VDC 2 A charger, fuse **Bussmann GMD-2A** slow-blow.

## Install and service essentials

From [[SRE-2750 Installation Manual (2011)]]:
- **Rail 2 in off the noses** (set 2.5 in on thick carpet).
- **Back edge of the rail 2.5 in from the wall, 3.5 in with the 90°
  swivel.**
- **Clamp bolts only 10 ft-lb**: more deforms the rail.
- **Wedge locks** on the first and last clamps if the rail doesn't reach
  the floor.
- Shim dips over 6 mm. Joint plate clamp **FIX-00525**.
- **Adjustable clamp set SRE-27291** if A is under 3 or B is over 12.
- **Custom one-piece rail:** cut the joint end. The rack is 3–4 in
  shorter than the rail, re-fixed with the saved M6 × 25.
- **Carriage angle:** 3 × M10 at **80 ft-lb**. RH uses the M8 safety
  lock screw through the angle retainer plate.
- First power-up can take up to 10 s to respond.
- **DIP and codes:** as the [[SRE-2000]], except switch-active codes are
  2 beeps, pause, 5 beeps.
