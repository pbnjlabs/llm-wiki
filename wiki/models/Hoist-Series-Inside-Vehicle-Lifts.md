# Hoist-Series Inside Vehicle Lifts

A **second, distinct Harmar vehicle-lift architecture** alongside
[[AL-Series-Outside-Vehicle-Lifts]] — discovered via the
[[Hoist Vehicle Lift Brochure]] and confirmed by individual model parts
diagrams. Where the AL-series (Outside) lifts mount on a trailer hitch
and fold a platform down behind the vehicle, Hoist lifts mount **inside**
the vehicle's cargo area and swing a boom arm **outside** to pick up and
load the mobility device into the vehicle — keeping it out of the weather
and off the road, and preserving use of a rear backup camera. Also
internally referred to as "Boom Lifts" in at least one bulletin.

Models confirmed via the brochure and parts diagrams: **AL055, AL065,
AL215, AL225, AL425, AL425HD, AL435, AL435T, AL815CC, AL825, AL825CC,
AL835**.

## Model differentiation
- **Van/SUV/minivan hoist lifts** — AL055, AL065 (lightweight,
  disassemble two-piece, manual rotation), AL215 (low-profile, weather-
  resistant remote), AL225 (power rotation), AL425/AL425HD (2-axis, sleek
  profile, only 30" headroom required — AL425HD adds capacity for
  heavier scooters/power chairs), AL435 (3-axis, boom extension, largest
  capacity in this group)
- **Truck hoist lifts** — AL435T (Tailgater, folding arm reaches 43" to
  curbside or over a 24" tailgate, passenger-side install only),
  AL815CC/AL825/AL825CC (crew-cab-focused, "CC" suffix = crew cab
  mounting), AL835 (heaviest-duty truck-bed lift, up to 350 lb)
- All models require a docking device attached to the mobility device
  (not included) except where a universal base adapter is bundled

## Specs (representative, per-model in the brochure)
- AL215, AL055 and AL065 rotate by hand; every other model has power
  rotation, and the AL435/AL435T add power extension
- Pendant remote controls (4-button on smaller models, 6-button on
  AL435/AL435T) with backlit buttons, built-in flashlight, magnetic
  back for stowage
- 3-year transferable warranty (parts only), same term as
  [[AL-Series-Outside-Vehicle-Lifts]]. Per the inside-lift manual it
  starts at install (**no later than 180 days from manufacture**) and is
  **void if the vehicle/chair application isn't approved by Harmar's
  Compatibility Calculator**. See [[Harmar-Warranty]]

## User capability requirements (per the brochure)
Walking front-to-rear of the vehicle (AL435T requires this even after the
device is stowed), standing up to 3 minutes during the lift cycle,
holding continuous pressure on the handheld control, attaching the
docking device (may require bending/kneeling/hand dexterity).

## Operating (owner)
Per [[Inside Lifts Installation and Owner's Manual (2017, Rev F)]] and [[Truck Lifts Installation and Owner's Manual (AL800 Series, 2017)]]:
- Chair parallel to the vehicle, parking brake on, seat back folded.
- **Strap must be taut and point straight down** before lifting, or the
  chair swings. Hands and feet clear.
- AL435 series: **EXTEND** for wide chairs, **RETRACT** to swing in
  through narrow openings.
- **Inside lifts: set the chair firmly on the floor** with a little
  strap tension; never transport it hanging. **Truck lifts: stop with
  about ¾ of the weight on the bed**, straps taut.
- **AL215 has no rotation lock:** hook the strap to the side-wall gusset
  and snug it when not in use.
- Keep the hand control inside (truck lifts: unplug it and keep it in
  the cab; never leave it in rain or snow). Outside exposure: dielectric
  grease on the connector.
- Check the strap every use; **dealer inspection yearly**.

## Installation (key points)
Full steps on the two manual pages.
- **Wiring:** ~23 ft harness, 20 A self-resetting breaker, black first,
  **red last**, never a secondary power source, don't cut it. See
  [[Vehicle-Lift-Power-Wiring]].
- **Trial fit first:** run the lift through its full range with the
  chair's folded size before drilling. At least **three 3/8 in holes**.
- **Bases:** BA00 flat floor, BA01R1 Stow 'n Go seat well (2 floor + 3
  wall bolts), BA22 heavy duty, truck-bed base (≥1 in clearance, ≥4 in
  of leg extension inside the base). **Never bolt to the floor skin
  alone; heavier chairs require bolting through the frame or
  cross-members.** 28–30 ft-lb.
- **Mechanical stop bolt** (power rotation): 2.50 in for passenger side,
  3.00 in for driver side, never both. Feed the lifting wire through the
  base adapter before mounting.
- **Driver side or wrong rotation:** swap the rotation-motor wires and
  flip the limit switches. Stop bolt: touch, back off ¼ turn, lock at 25
  ft-lb.
- **Truck lifts:** about 180° of rotation; post tube just clears the bed
  wall; arm bolts **≥100 ft-lb**, bolt in from the top; in-cab control
  box with power switch.

## Troubleshooting
- **Won't run, slow or intermittent:** connections first (#1 cause),
  then the harness for chafe, then the breaker ~6 in from the battery.
  Test with a **known-good 12 V battery**: a meter can show 12 V through
  one strand that can't run the motor (draws up to 30 A).
- **Strap winds backwards:** hold the button that extends the strap
  until it rewinds; strap goes **over the top of the spool**.
- **Chair tips:** docking device off center or wrong DD.
- **Arm stiff:** clean and grease the base bushings (white lithium).

## Docking devices
Every hoist and truck lift **needs a docking device** on the mobility
device (center seat post or four-post seat frame). Harmar's types, per
[[Harmar Product Insights (2021-2023)]]: **C-arms** (13, scooters and power chairs), **spreader bars** (9,
power chairs; 19/24/30 in; DD45 is the 4-point carabiner system),
**straight bar** DD17 (seat-off), **cable harness** DD39 (small folding
scooters), **manual chair lifting bars** DD22/23/34. The Compatibility
Calculator picks the exact DD. Full list on the source page.

Lift selection (same sheets): hoists keep the device out of the weather
and fit in a garage, but are the hardest to operate ("moderate+") and
suit highly capable users.
