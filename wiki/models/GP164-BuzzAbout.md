# GP164 BuzzAbout (Golden)

The **BUZZ™ABOUT**: a rear-wheel-drive take-apart power chair, heavier
than the [[GP162-LiteRider-Envy]], with a captain's seat and lights.
Link to [[Golden]].

## Documents on file

- [[BuzzAbout Owners Manual (GP164, LiNX)]] (Nov 2021)

## Specs

300 lb, 4 mph, 11 mi, **2-22 Ah** (28.75 lb pack), **40 A Dynamic LiNX**,
36 × 23 in, 28.5 in turning radius. **155.35 lb assembled**: front half
29.2, rear half 33.85, seat with arms 34.8, battery pack 28.75. 9 in
drive wheels, 6 in casters. Captain's seat 17 × 16 in, three heights.
See [[Mobility-Device-Weights]].

## Service notes

- **Diagnostics:** [[LiNX-Diagnostics]] (status light 1–7, OONAPU,
  drive inhibit).
- Headlight/taillight **bypass switch** and a circuit breaker **reset
  button** on the base.
- Disassembly needs no tools: joystick cable, seat, pack, then the
  locking handle lifts the frame off the drive train.
- Battery pack: **AGM or gel only**.
- Freewheel: levers **down** = freewheel, **up** = drive ([[Freewheel-Mode]]).
