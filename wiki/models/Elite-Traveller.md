# Elite Traveller (40E / 44E)

Pride's lightest disassembling travel scooter on file: **40E = 3-wheel,
44E = 4-wheel**. Spec sheet, dealer guide and the travel scooter owner's manual
([[Elite Traveller Specifications (INFINFB3208)]], Rev C, Nov 2019).

| | 3-wheel (40E) | 4-wheel (44E) |
|---|---|---|
| Class / capacity | A / 300 lb | A / 300 lb |
| Max speed | 4 mph | 4 mph |
| Ground clearance | 1.38 in at motor | 1.38 in |
| Turning radius | 33 in | 44 in |
| Reversing width | 42 in | 42 in |
| Length / width | 37 / 19.5 in | 39.5 / 19.5 in |
| Tires (solid) | 8 in front and rear | 7 in front, 8 in rear |
| Range 12 Ah / 12 Ah ext. / 18 Ah / 18 Ah ext. | 7.25 / 8.5 / 10.5 / 12.4 mi | 6 / 8.2 / 9.75 / 12 mi |
| Weight without batteries (with seat) | 75.5 lb | 79.5 lb |
| Heaviest piece (front section) | 29.5 lb | 34 lb |
| Seat-to-deck | 16.25–17.75 in | 15.75–17.25 in |

Both: folding seat 17 × 17 in, 22.5 lb; seat height 14.25 in; overall
height 33.5–35.5 in; rear-wheel drive, sealed transaxle, 24 V motor;
regenerative + electromechanical brakes; battery pack **12 Ah standard
(19.5 lb)**, optional 12 Ah extended (23.5 lb), 18 Ah (29.5 lb) or 18 Ah
extended (31.75 lb); **2 A** off-board charger; max slope 6°; climbs 2
in. 12 Ah doesn't meet AS/NZS 3695.2 / EN12184.

## Troubleshooting and beep codes

From [[Elite Traveller Basic Troubleshooting (INFINFB2039)]] (Rev B,
2008) and the dealer [[Elite Traveller Technical Troubleshooting Guide (INFMANU3206)]]
(2006). The Go-Go Elite Traveller **SC40E/44E** runs a **PG S-Drive
45 A** controller (ELEASMB4893), so the beeps are the S-Drive set
([[S-Drive-Beep-Codes]]): **ten fast beeps, then the count**, played
once (reinsert the key to repeat). Confirm on a current 40E/44E against
its own manual (not on file).

| Beeps | Condition | Do |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Battery voltage too low | Charge fully |
| 3 | Battery voltage too high | Provider |
| 5 | Solenoid brake trip (freewheel lever in freewheel?) | Key out, lever to drive, restart |
| 6 | Throttle pressed while the key went in | Release throttle, reinsert key |
| 7 | Throttle or speed pot fault | Provider |
| 8 | Motor disconnected | Provider |
| 9 | Controller trip (pack installed with the key in, or overheated) | Wait several minutes, reinsert key |

- **Won't power up:** key fully in, battery pack seated tight,
  quick-disconnect joined, red to +, black to −, push the breaker reset.
- **Powers up, won't move:** the **power-down timer** cut it after a long
  idle (remove and reinsert the key), or the **charger is plugged into
  the scooter** (inhibits driving even if not in the wall).
- **Range:** batteries over a year old, charging habits (daily 8–14 h;
  weekly 12–14 h when idle), wall-switched outlet, no extension cords.
  Watch the meter rise while charging; if it doesn't, check the charger
  fuse and the outlet.

## Dealer tests (TTG)

| Beep / symptom | Test |
|---|---|
| No power | Breaker; battery box connector over 18 V; **fuse 5c**; console; harnesses |
| 3 | Over 32 V; charger output over **30 V** → charger |
| 5 | Drive mode; brake **30–65 Ω** |
| 7 | Throttle pot **5 kΩ** end to end, **12.5 kΩ** each side; speed pot 0 Ω at hare, **25 kΩ** at tortoise |
| 8 | Motor 0.5–1.5 Ω |
| 9 | Key in while fitting the battery box, or hot (rest 10–15 min); drags in freewheel → brake, motor or transaxle |

## Repairs on file

[[Elite Traveller Repair Instructions]]: throttle pot (ELEASMB4829),
speed pot (fit the knob at **full rabbit**), motor (DRVMOTR1269; brake
goes on with its **handle up, freewheel position**; keep the connector
spring), drive wheel nut **28 N·m (20 ft·lb)**, S-Drive controller,
and the **Elite Traveller Plus (SC53/54)** throttle pot and lower
console (pull the harness through the tiller with a string; route it
under the handlebar).
