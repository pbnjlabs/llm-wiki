# VPL-3100B

Bruno's smaller battery-operated vertical platform lift (wheelchair porch/
deck lift). Variants: **VPL-3153B** and **VPL-3175B** — same platform,
differ only by max floor-to-floor travel (53"/1.3m vs 75"/1.9m). Sibling
model to the larger [[VPL-3200B]] — see that page for how Bruno
differentiates the two.

## Spec summary

- Load capacity: 750 lbs (340 kg)
- Motor: 1/2 HP, 24VDC (2 HP peak); drive is an **Acme screw + drive nut**
  mechanism (not the rack/pinion + overspeed governor used by Bruno's
  [[SRE-2010]]/[[SRE-3000]]/[[SRE-3050]] stairlifts)
- Batteries: 2×12VDC 17AH standard, 2×34AH optional
- Platform: 36"×48" standard, up to 36"×60" optional
- Emergency operation: manual hand wheel only (no electrical emergency
  lowering kit — contrast [[VPL-3200B]])
- Regulatory: ASME A18.1 Sec.5, CSA B355, FDA Class II Patient Transporter

## Key procedures

- **Top landing gate installation**: shared procedure across both VPL
  models — see [[Top-Landing-Gate-Installation]].
- **Top cover / service switch**: a push-pull safety interlock. Cover in
  place → switch pushed down → unit operates normally. Cover removed →
  switch pulls up "for service" → unit disabled unless manually pulled up
  during install/service work. This is the VPL's equivalent safety gate to
  the stairlifts' [[Overspeed-Safety-Brake]], though mechanically
  unrelated (interlock switch, not a governor).
- **Drive nut retention hardware is factory-set — do not adjust.** Failure
  heeded warning: could cause the platform to fall, serious/fatal injury.
  This is the VPL's single most safety-critical fastener, analogous in
  criticality to the stairlifts' overspeed brake but with no field reset —
  it's simply never to be touched.
- **Emergency lowering (hand wheel only)**: disconnect batteries/breaker,
  remove top cover, insert hand wheel on shaft, turn **counterclockwise**
  to lower.
- **Cold weather (<0°F/-18°C)**: raise platform to mid-travel when idle;
  before use, run lift down then fully up then to desired level — warms up
  the Acme screw/nut mechanism.
- **Battery charger diagnostics**: see [[Battery-Charger-LED-Diagnostics]].

## Quirks / notes

- **Warranty:** 1 yr all parts, 2 yr major parts (motor, acme screw,
  drive nut, brake, pulleys, belt, screw bearings), 30 days labor
  ([[Bruno Warranty Summary]], [[Warranty]]).

- Ships with the ramp/ramp-activator/vertical-bar on one side; moving them
  to the opposite side is a documented but involved field procedure
  (remove/reinsert pins, washers, brackets — orientation-sensitive, wrong
  orientation is explicitly shown as "INCORRECT" in the manual).
- No electrical emergency-lowering kit — only the 3200B has that.

## Field notes

- [[2026-10-01 Bruno-VPL door interlock spring]]: **door stuck locked**.
  The spring-loaded door interlock can partially fail. Test: lift at the
  top, pull the friction-fit post cap, push the lock rod down by hand
  (remove the call/send station's four screws if you can't reach it).
  Rod doesn't spring back, or is slow to: **replace the interlock
  assembly, VPL-31452L (left hand)**.
