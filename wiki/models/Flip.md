# Little Wave Flip (pediatric tilt)

Ki Mobility's **pediatric tilt-in-space** chair, **165 lb (75 kg)**, transit 165 lb. 25 kg (55 lb) as tested. Also built as **Flip for x:panda** (R82 x:panda seat on an adaptor plate) and Flip for Leckey.

- Trigger or foot tilt; lanyard pin holds the triggers locked, or unlocked while parked in a stable tilt.
- Tech manual: 63 pages of seat-height charts, hand and foot tilt, attendant foot lock rod guide, adaptor plates for Leckey and x:panda. Pivot joint screws 240 in-lb, three screws 144 in-lb.
