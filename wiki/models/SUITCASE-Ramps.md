# SUITCASE Folding Ramps

EZ-ACCESS's aluminum folding portable ramps, for travel, vehicles and
short or temporary rises. Lengths run **2–10 ft**. They are
**temporary-use only**, for example loading a scooter into a van.

All three styles are rated **800 lb** (per EZ-ACCESS's brochure,
[[SUITCASE Portable Folding Ramps Brochure]]).

| Variant | Lengths | Width | Build |
|---|---|---|---|
| SUITCASE Singlefold | 2–8 ft | 30 in | One piece, full-length hinge, **extruded** slip-resistant surface; built for high traffic |
| SUITCASE Singlefold AS | 2–6 ft | 29.5 in | Folds in half, **splits into two halves**, **applied** slip-resistant surface |
| SUITCASE TRIFOLD AS | 5–10 ft | 29.5 in | Folds lengthwise **and** across the width at mid-length, so it stores shorter; splits into two halves; applied surface |

**"AS" = separates into two sections, with an applied (taped-on) grip
surface.** EZ-ACCESS's warranty doesn't cover the applied non-skid tape
([[EZ-ACCESS Lifetime Warranty (2017)]]).

**Singlefold (one piece) weights:** 2' 11 lb, 3' 17 lb, 4' 23 lb, 5' 29
lb, 6' 34 lb, 7' 41 lb, 8' 47 lb. **Uses per the brochure:** 2–3 ft
curbs and 1 step; 4–5 ft 1–2 steps (5 ft some minivans); 6–7 ft 1–3
steps, some minivans and SUVs; 8 ft 2–3 steps; 10 ft 2–4 steps. Full
folded sizes on the brochure page.

- **Width 29.5 in** (Singlefold AS and Trifold AS). Weights, from
  [[EZ Access Parts and Pieces]]:

  | Singlefold AS | Trifold AS |
  |---|---|
  | 2' – 12 lb | 5' – 32 lb |
  | 3' – 16 lb | 6' – 38 lb |
  | 4' – 20 lb | 7' – 44 lb |
  | 5' – 26 lb | 8' – 50 lb |
  | 6' – 30 lb | 10' – 60 lb |

  Singlefold AS runs 2–6 ft; Trifold AS runs 5–10 ft. The brochure gives
  the same totals as per-section weights (e.g. 6 ft Singlefold AS = 15 lb
  × 2).
- Install time: 15 min each ([[Ramp-Install-Time-Estimates]]).
- Slip-resistant surface. Self-adjusting ground transition plates.
- **Top Lip Extension** ("TLE") accessory (fits all SUITCASE ramps)
  extends the top lip **from 3 in to 9 in** to clear a vehicle bumper.
- Use SUITCASE, not [[GATEWAY-3G]], when the ramp moves often. PMS treats
  GATEWAY as permanent, and it is held to 1:12. GATEWAY
  suits a home where the ramp is repositioned only occasionally.

**Exempt from the 1:12 house standard.** SUITCASE ramps are only for
temporary use, such as loading a scooter into the back of a van, so the
1:12 rule doesn't apply. Check the mobility device's rated max incline
and owner's guide instead (see [[Ramp-Slope-and-Length]]). If one is
being used as a home's everyday access ramp, 1:12 applies again, and
[[GATEWAY-3G]] or [[PATHWAY-3G]] is usually the right product. See [[Ramp-Slope-and-Length]].

**Step rise (PMS).** Steps leading up to a porch only matter for a
SUITCASE ramp laid over them. Measure and size it by **the
manufacturer's guidelines**, not the PMS porch-height method used for
PATHWAY (see [[Ramp-Site-Planning]]).
