# ASL-700 (Bruno Chariot)

A **wheeled exterior platform lift**: it plugs into a **Class I or II
(1.25 in) hitch** and rides on **its own two swivel wheels and tires**
behind the vehicle, with its own brake and turn lights. Scooters and
power chairs up to **350 lb**. Because it's a wheeled carrier, it has a
**VIN** and comes with a **Manufacturer's Certificate of Origin**; review
the state's **title requirements** with the customer. Link to [[Bruno]].

Compare [[ASL-275]] (hitch-mounted Out-Sider, no wheels, class II/III)
and Harmar's [[AL-Series-Outside-Vehicle-Lifts]].

## Specs

- **350 lb** max; three-piece adjustable aluminum platform.
- Scooters: up to **27.25 in wide**, wheelbase **29–38.5 in**. Power
  chairs: up to 27.25 in wide, wheelbase up to 38.5 in.
- Platform assembly 33.8 in wide, 39.4–47.4 in long.
- **Hitch: Class I or II, 1.25 in receiver only**; fits receiver heights
  **8–16 in** via a **4-bar linkage** hitch adapter. **Frame-rail hitch
  recommended; no trunk-pan/floor-style hitches.**
- Tires **4.80/4.00-8, load range C, 6-ply**, 8 in rims, 5-on-4½ bolt
  pattern. **35 psi.**
- Securement: **3 auto-ratcheting retractable belts** to a "dog bone"
  plate under the seat cushion; wheel chocks for scooters.
- **Max speed 65 mph.** Flat-bed tow only. Touchless car wash only.

## Installing (key points)

- **Hitch first**, by a hitch tech, with a **flat-4 trailer light
  harness**. Have the underbody inspected; rusted vehicles aren't
  suitable. The hitch installer can run the power cable too (it ships
  separately).
- **Power:** black to negative, orange to positive, 30 A butt connector;
  12 V battery on hybrids/EVs. Leave slack in the harness and flat-4 for
  vertical travel.
- **Hitch height setting** (receiver lip to ground): 8–9¾, 10–11¾,
  12–13½, or 13¾–16 in. Linkage bars as **parallel to the ground** as
  possible. Jam nuts **15 ft-lb**, then center lock nuts **30 ft-lb**;
  grease all 8 fittings.
- **At least 6.5 in between the bumper and the back of the lift.**
- **Hitch clamp:** a specific tightening order; the four 3/8 × 3.5 in
  bolts to **29 ft-lb**. Attach the **2 safety cables**.
- Customer brings the mobility device: adjust chocks and belts on it.
  Train them, review title paperwork, leave the manual and **emergency
  tool in the glove box**.

## Operating

Key switch ON → hold **DOWN** to the ground → chair on (scooter wheels in
the chocks; turn the chair off) → hold **UP** to the top → hook the **3
belts** → key OFF. **Operate from the side, never seated on the chair.**
Empty: fold the platform up and strap it (folding down is OK for highway
fuel economy). Drive slower, watch crosswinds, back up carefully (adds
length); remove it before vehicle service.

**Emergency:** key OFF, then a drill on the emergency tool: **clockwise
raises, counterclockwise lowers**.

## Maintenance (warranty depends on it)

- **Wheel nuts 75–88 ft-lb** at the first 10, 25 and 50 miles and after
  any wheel removal; **tire pressure weekly**.
- 3,000 mi / 3 mo: wheel nuts, tires. 6,000 / 6 mo: suspension.
- **9,000 mi / 12 mo:** re-torque everything (swivel plates and chassis
  35–40 ft-lb), grease the 8 linkage fittings and the swivel assembly,
  check lights (2 people), dielectric grease on connections, lube the
  draw bar, belts, structure.
- **Swivel assembly:** clean with soap and water (no solvents on the
  polyurethane bearings), Teflon grease, **castle nut 17 ft-lb**.
- **Sealed hub: never open it.**
- **Dealer service at least every 12 months; skipping the schedule
  voids the warranty.**

## Troubleshooting highlights

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Won't run | Key ON; fuse; connections; ground first |
| Slow or stalls | Weak battery: charge or fast idle |
| Lights test "hot" with power off | Vehicle multiplex system switches ground: add a "low side switching" adapter (10 A or 4 A) |
| Wheels flutter / won't swivel | Castle nut 17 ft-lb; re-grease swivel |
| Rough ride | Tires over-inflated: 35 psi |
| Platform sags (outer edge doesn't touch first) | Adjust hitch height so the unit is level |
| Folded platform, actuator clicks both ways | Platform pulled in too far: keep it flat to the bumpers; back the motor off with the emergency tool |
