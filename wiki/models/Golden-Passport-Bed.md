# Golden Passport Bed (Hi Lo Tilt HS adjustable base)

Golden's **home adjustable bed base** with hi-lo, tilt, head and foot
articulation, massage and a wireless remote. Manual ID LIT-BD100A-OM.
**Not a hospital bed**: Golden says it's for in-home use only and not
approved for hospital use. PMS's hospital beds are a different product
([[MedMizer]]). Link to [[Golden]].

## Documents on file

- [[Passport Hi-Lo Adjustable Base Owners Manual]] (Rev. B, 2023)

## Specs

- **880 lb** total (mattress plus people, spread evenly); Twin, Twin XL,
  Full, Queen; two bases sync for a King XL.
- Height 14.5–23.5 in (legs in 5, 2.5 and 2 in sections); backrest
  0–65°, legrest 0–35°.
- Power supply 100–240 V in, **29 V DC 1.8 A** out. **10% duty cycle**:
  2 min running, 18 min rest.

## Service notes

- **Remote dead or base won't respond:** unplug the base 30 s; check the
  outlet and breaker; replace the remote's AAAs; **re-pair** (press the
  control box pairing button twice, then hold the remote's pairing
  buttons within 60 s and 2 m until it beeps).
- **Stuck raised:** hold the **emergency lowering button** on the
  control box. In a power cut, two 9 V batteries in the power supply
  will flatten it **without a load** only.
- **Won't go flat:** obstructions, or no clearance to the wall or
  headboard.
- Head and foot motor cables pair by colored tabs (purple, yellow);
  route all wires clear of moving parts.
- Warranty: 1 yr parts and labor, years 2–3 parts only, lifetime on
  the steel frame; residential use only, through the retailer.
