# SRE-3000 (Bruno Elan — legacy)

Predecessor to the [[SRE-3050]]. Same "Elan" branding and core architecture
(rack-and-pinion drive, folding rail option, overspeed governor,
electromechanical brake). Its last install and operator manuals (2019,
trilingual) were pulled from Brunopartner on 2026-10-22, along with the
older 2015 install manual. Treat it as the prior hardware generation
still in the field, not a currently-sold model.

## Specs

- 300 lb (136 kg) capacity, 18 ft/min max speed
- 24VDC 2-pole motor, 50Nm torque, 16A max, rack-and-pinion drive
- 1Nm electromechanical motor brake + overspeed governor
- Max incline 45° (52° special order); seat swivel 0°/67°/90°
- Rail lengths (2015 "new" length scheme): 8'/10' sections; 16' (std or
  folding), 18' (folding-only), 20' (std-only) full lengths — up to 32'
  (31'-6" actual) optional track length

## Key procedures

Structurally near-identical to the [[SRE-3050]] install manual — same
section order, same figures reused (rail prep, joint, folding rail,
carriage LH/RH conversion, seat, finishing). Notable specifics:
- **Standfoot orientation** is safety-critical and shared with SRE-2010:
  standfeet must have the long nose facing the center of the staircase
  with no gap between rail and standfoot at the front — wrong orientation
  can drop the carriage.
- **Circuit board diagnostics**: same multi-user/single-user audio
  diagnostic system later reused in SRE-2010 and SRE-3050 documentation.
- **Overspeed**: reset and regulatory test procedures are the same
  content later reused verbatim in [[SRE-2010]] and [[SRE-3050]] docs —
  see [[Overspeed-Safety-Brake]].
- **Diagnostic codes**: shares the same code table as [[SRE-2010]] and
  [[CRE-2110]] (SRE-3000 has its own PCB column, PCB-00124140) — see
  [[Diagnostic-Codes]].
- **Folding rail**: same install/troubleshooting content family as later
  models — see [[Folding-Rail]].
- The 2015 manual has no "Rail Cross Pin Installation" step. **The 2019
  manual adds it**, matching the later SRE-3050.
- **2019 manual specifics** ([[SRE-3000 Installation Manual (2019)]]):
  - Rail = stair length **+ 12 in**. A "16 ft" two-piece rail is 15 ft 9 in.
    Never cut a folding rail assembly under 75 in.
  - No more than 4 steps without a bracket. Adjustable set SRE-30150 if
    the rail ends under 3 in past the top nose.
  - **2 in off the noses (2.5 in on carpet). 3 in from the back of the top
    of the rail to the nearest obstruction.** Bolts 25 ft-lb.
  - **Single-user = jumper on the middle and right pins** (under the top
    cover).
  - Single-LED charger: flashing red = bad batteries, flashing green =
    reverse polarity.
  - **Drive adjustment:** vibration going down only = too loose,
    vibration both ways = too tight. Loosen the 3 drive bolts, turn the
    jack screw ⅛ turn, and let it self-center.
  - **Folding rail joint reset:** bridge the joint with spare rack while
    tightening the fold jack screw. Never remove the main rail screw.
  - **Overspeed reset:** right-hand safety panel off, rotate the governor
    to center; if it's stuck, ¼ in socket or SRV-3000-002, CW for LH and
    CCW for RH.
  - It charges anywhere on the rail. The power folding rail has a
    disable switch and a flashing yellow LED.

## From Bruno's tech tips

- **Brake:** kit **SRV-3000-016** (ILS-01324) replaces the motor brake,
  hex drive nut and roll pin. Centre the brake disc by releasing it
  electrically with one motor lead unplugged
  ([[Tech Tip - SRE-3000 Elan Brake Replacement (ILS-01324)]]). Named for
  the SRE-3000; not confirmed for the SRE-3050.
- **Board swap:** ESD strap; four white standoffs; re-learn remotes with
  the key switch on ([[Tech Tip - SRE-3000 Elan PCB Replacement]]).
- **Loose carriage / vibration:** loosen the three motor bolts, set screw
  ¼ turn left, run 6 in each way, torque to **20 ft-lb (27 Nm)**
  ([[Tech Tip - SRE-3000 Drive Adjustment]]).
- **Overspeed reset on the rail:** safety flap off, push the overspeed
  fully right then left, then centre the switch roller in the valley with
  the power on; listen for the click and power-up beep
  ([[Tech Tip - SRE-3000 Overspeed Reset on the Rail]]).
- **Gear rack:** new **SRE-30177** and old **SRE-30066** have different
  ends and **can't be mixed on one rail**
  ([[Tech Tip - SRE-3000 Gear Rack Change (ILS-01079)]]).
- **Clamps:** standard **SRE-30001** (5.22 in) or adjustable
  **SRE-30150** (6.19–9.19 in)
  ([[Tech Tip - SRE-3000 Clamp Comparison (Adjustable Stand Foot)]]).
- **Folding rail:** loose mid-park contact strip, a bump at the joint
  (latch adjustment), a carriage jammed at the fold (gear rack timing
  with FIX-00525), and binding under a heavy rider (**lower end cap not
  on the floor**). See [[Tech Tip - SRE-3000 Folding Rail Fixes]].
- **Battery:** BTR-12008 = **MK ES3-12** (12 V 3 Ah), 10–15 trips per
  charge, about 3–5 years ([[Stairlift-Maintenance]]).
- **Temperature:** above 35 °F only, and Bruno counts an ordinary
  **garage as exterior** ([[Tech Tip - SRE-3000 Temperature Range]]).
- **Serial number:** on the carriage (battery housing, motor, board
  panel); the loose decal on the breaker goes in the data-plate window
  under the seat ([[Bruno-Serial-Numbers]]).

## Troubleshooting

Start with [[Stairlift-Troubleshooting]] (symptom-first, from Bruno's
[[TechMate Stairlift Troubleshooter]], which still covers the SRE-3000).
SRE-3000 specifics:
- Has a working **key switch**. Off = beeps once on command both ways;
  turn it 90° before anything else.
- Seat won't stay folded up → loose fold hardware (thread locker and
  tighten).
- Elan rules apply: beeps only, continuous charge, motor (not brushes)
  when it clicks with voltage at the motor leads.
- Bruno's Elan guide: at least **23 V up / 21 V down** to run, cut-out
  about 16 V under load; motor leads (blue/brown) about **23–24 V up,
  12–13 V down** ([[Tech Tip - SRE-3000 Elan Troubleshooting Guide]]).
- One IR call station flaky? **Unplug the opposite-end IR sensor.** Works
  → reflection interference, not a bad part
  ([[Tech Tip - SRE-3000 IR Call-Send Interference]]).
