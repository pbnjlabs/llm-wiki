# GA541 Avenger

Golden's largest 4-wheel scooter — captain's seat, rear-wheel drive,
750W motor. Link to [[Golden]].

## Specs

- Weight capacity: 500 lb
- Max speed: 7 mph; operating range 29.6 mi (at 200 lb rider)
- Batteries: 2× NF22 (37 lb each), AGM or GEL-cell SLA only
- Drive: rear-wheel, 750W motor with electro-mechanical brake, PG S-Drive
  electronic controller (PG S 140 Amp), off-board charger
- Turning radius 67", length 57.5", width 24.5"
- Tires: pneumatic, 13" front and rear, split-rim wheels
- Frame: lifetime warranty; splits into front/rear halves via a ball
  detent pin for transport (won't fit a compact car trunk)

## Key procedures

- **Controls**: TruCharge volt meter/diagnostics gauge, speed dial,
  self-centering thumb-lever "wig-wag" (right thumb = forward, left thumb
  = reverse) — reverse speed is capped at 50% of the dial setting.
- **Disassembly for transport**: unplug battery connectors → remove
  Velcro straps + batteries → unplug main cable, charger connector, rear
  lights connector → remove ball detent pin retaining clip → pull pin →
  separate front/rear halves. Reassemble in reverse order; always verify
  the ball detent pin is fully seated and retained before sitting on the
  scooter again.
- **Charging**: new batteries must be charged 12 hours before first use
  (even if the gauge already reads full); routine charging is 6-8 hours,
  ideally overnight after every use.
- **Circuit breaker**: 50A manual-reset breaker under the battery cover,
  next to the freewheel release — wait 5 minutes after a trip (to let the
  motor/electronics cool) before resetting.
- **Diagnostics**: TruCharge display 10-code fault table — see
  [[TruCharge-Diagnostics]] (confirmed for GA541 only so far; verify
  against other Golden models as they're ingested).
