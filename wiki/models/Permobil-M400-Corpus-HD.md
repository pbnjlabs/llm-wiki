# Permobil M400 Corpus HD

Heavy-duty **mid-wheel-drive** chair, **450 lb** max user, R-net PM120,
**2 × 74 Ah gel** batteries, 63 A main breaker. Source:
[[Permobil M400 Corpus HD User Manual (337723)]] (2020).

## Service notes

- Tires drive 300 × 8 and caster 200 × 50, **29–36 psi**. Drive wheel
  rim and wheel screws **24 Nm (18 lb-ft)**.
- **Battery replacement:** seat lift up, breaker OFF, three chassis covers
  off (four knobs), **pull the locking handles out to release the
  electronics**, rear seat support (2 screws), terminals per the sticker
  inside the front cover, strap out. **Replace both batteries as a
  matched set** (house standard, [[Mobility-Batteries]]).
- Charger max **10 A average / 12 A RMS**.
- A tripped main breaker usually means a real electrical fault: find it
  before resetting.
- Safe slope only **7.5°**; obstacle 2.4 in.
- R-net LCD/LED diagnostics: [[R-net-Diagnostics]].
