# Jazzy Ultra Light

Pride's lightest folding Jazzy, retail (K0899): **300 lb**, 3.7 mph,
**25.2 V 10 or 20 Ah lithium-ion**, 3.5 A charger, 40 A PG **nVR2**,
**32.6 lb** in one piece (2026 retail chart,
[[Pride-Power-Chair-Lineup]]). Warranty: 5-year frame, 13 months on the
rest ([[Pride-Warranty]]).

## Documents

- Retailer part numbers (Mobility Scooters Direct, 2026-10-27; confirm by serial with the manufacturer): [[Parts Catalog - Mobility Scooters Direct (Pride)#Jazzy Ultra Light Parts|Jazzy Ultra Light Parts]] (15)
- [[Jazzy Ultra Light Owners Manual (INFMANU5449)]] (Rev B, 2024).

## Notes

- One lithium battery in a removable box. The **battery power switch
  on the back of the box is also the main circuit breaker**: chair dead
  → check that switch first.
- Charges through the joystick socket or the battery box port. Pride
  charger: amber = charging, green = done; not done in 24 h → call.
- VR2 flash codes: [[Pride-Controller-Flash-Codes]]. Care:
  [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]].
