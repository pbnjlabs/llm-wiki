# PDG Fuze

PDG Mobility's **manual tilt-in-space** chairs (centre-of-gravity tilt on a gas
strut with a mech-lock), now Permobil.

| Model | Capacity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fuze T50 / T50N | 300 lb (136 kg) | Power tilt option ([[PDG Power Tilt Accessory Owner's Manual (PD24244 Rev 6)]]) |
| Fuze M50 | 400 lb | Spec table misprints "136 kg (400 lbs)"; rear caster arm bolts **18 ft-lb** |
| Fuze JR | 250 lb | Spec table says 300 lb; go by the text and the label |

## Service notes

- **Tilt limiter** on the upper frame: 20°, 30°, 40° or 50°.
- **Tilt cable:** won't tilt with the trigger pulled = **too loose**: turn the
  sheath adjustment nut clockwise (more thread showing), lock the jam nut. Tilts
  without the trigger = **too tight**: reverse. Check the cable ball sits in the
  lever slot.
- **QR axle nut:** hold the detent end with 7/16 in, turn the nut with 3/4 in in
  half turns (counter-clockwise if it won't lock, clockwise to remove play).
- **Oil QR axles monthly with 3-in-1** per PDG; TiLite, Ki and Sunrise say not to
  use 3-in-One ([[Manual-Wheelchair-Service]]).
- **Anti-tippers are mandatory.** Cracked frame or weld = back to the service
  agent.
- Casters: 1/4 in Allen on the journal shoulder bolt, 3/4 in socket on the
  1/2-20 stem nut; keep the lower frame level. Seat-to-floor 13–20 in.
- **Transit:** only with PDG's Transit Tiedown System (**4 white hook symbols**),
  forward facing, WC-4 §18 tie-downs. See [[Transport-and-Tie-Downs]].
