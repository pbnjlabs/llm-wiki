# GP161 LiteRider Envy Lite (Golden)

A lighter, lower-capacity version of the [[GP162-LiteRider-Envy]]:
rear-wheel drive, splits into front and rear halves. Link to [[Golden]].

## Documents on file

- Retailer part numbers (Mobility Scooters Direct, 2026-10-27; confirm by serial with the manufacturer): [[Parts Catalog - Mobility Scooters Direct (Golden)#Golden LiteRider Envy LT Parts|Golden LiteRider Envy LT Parts]] (6)
- [[LiteRider Envy Lite Owners Manual (GP161)]] (Oct 2021)
- [[LiteRider Envy Lite Illustrated Parts Breakdown (GP161)]] (Dec 2023): **PG nVR2 40 A** joystick
  and power module (the owner's manual says 50 A; go by the label),
  12 V 18 Ah batteries, 35 A breaker, 3.5 A charger; brake handle and
  battery box shared with the GP160 and GP162B

## Service notes

- **Diagnostics:** 10-LED flash codes, the dual-motor VR2 table on
  [[TruCharge-Diagnostics]]. Lock/unlock by key presses and joystick
  moves.
- **Batteries** live in a pack; replacing them means opening the pack.
  **AGM or gel only.**
- Main circuit breaker reset is **under the battery box handle**; wait
  about a minute before resetting.
- Freewheel: levers **down** = freewheel, **up** = drive ([[Freewheel-Mode]]).
