# GATEWAY 3G Solid Surface Portable Ramp

EZ-ACCESS's one-piece (non-folding) aluminum portable ramp. Lengths up
to **10 ft**, with or without handrails. EZ-ACCESS classes it as
"semi-permanent": it stays at a home long-term but can be moved to
another entrance or house.

> **PMS classification: permanent** (confirmed by the human twice,
> 2026-09-24). It is **not** a temporary-use ramp like the SUITCASE.
> PMS and this wiki treat the GATEWAY 3G as a long-term, permanent
> ramp. It can technically be
> moved, but that doesn't change the classification. The **1:12 (4.8°)
> slope standard applies** (see [[Ramp-Slope-and-Length]]), unlike the
> temporary-use [[SUITCASE-Ramps]].

## Specs ([[GATEWAY 3G Instructions and Technical Specifications]])

EZ-ACCESS's own documents (instructions 2023, spec sheet 2024) override
the clippings below.

- **1,000 lb**, 6000-series aluminum, **residential use only**, made in
  the USA. Lifetime limited warranty (powder coat excluded).
- **3–10 ft** long, **36 in usable width** (37 in overall; 41.75 in with
  rails). 2 in curbs on 3–8 ft, **4 in curbs on 9 and 10 ft**.
  Self-adjusting lower plate, fixed top lip plate with two holes.
- **Three styles:** ramp only (24–72 lb), Two-Line handrails (TL, 44–106
  lb) or Vertical Pickets (VP, 46–128 lb). Rails 36 in overall height,
  34.5 in usable. **Handrails are required if anyone walks on it.**
- **Rise, PMS:** 1:12, so **1 in of rise per foot**: 3 ft for 3 in up to
  10 ft for 10 in. EZ-ACCESS's guide is steeper (3 ft up to 4 in, 6 ft
  up to 9 in, 10 ft up to 15 in) and **doesn't apply to PMS installs**.

| Length | Ramp only | TL | VP | PMS max rise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 ft | 24 lb | 44 lb | 46 lb | 3 in |
| 4 ft | 30 | 52 | 58 | 4 in |
| 5 ft | 36 | 60 | 68 | 5 in |
| 6 ft | 42 | 68 | 78 | 6 in |
| 7 ft | 50 | 74 | 90 | 7 in |
| 8 ft | 54 | 82 | 98 | 8 in |
| 9 ft | 68 | 96 | 118 | 9 in |
| 10 ft | 72 | 106 | 128 | 10 in |

- **Install:** handrail end brackets bolt into the side rails with the
  ramp face down (5/16-18 × 1-1/2 in, 1/2 in socket); posts on the
  bracket studs; **upper end loops are larger** than lower and not
  interchangeable. Top lip plate fully on a level landing; **anchor it
  before each use** with the clevis pins (1/4 in holes) or flush-set
  hardware. Glue the 4 corner caps on with construction adhesive.
- **Care:** tighten fasteners before each use; 180-grit touch-up on
  architectural rails only; magnesium chloride or CMA for ice (salt and
  calcium chloride damage concrete and metal).

## From the clippings

- Suited to multiple steps, high curbs and raised entries within its
  length. Handrails are recommended for walking users, not only device
  users.
- EZ-ACCESS markets it for temporary access after surgery and for homes
  that want no permanent modifications. PMS still treats it as
  permanent, so size it at 1:12 regardless.
- Doesn't fold. For frequent transport, use [[SUITCASE-Ramps]] instead.
- Slip-resistant aluminum surface, self-adjusting ground transition plate.
