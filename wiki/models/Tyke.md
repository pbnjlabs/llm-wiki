# Tyke (pediatric mobility base)

Ki Mobility's folding **mobility base** (stroller-style) for an R82 x:panda child seat. **User 74 lb (33.5 kg)**; storage bag 15 lb. Suspension, seat angle adjustment on the frame, rear tie-downs.

- **Interface plate screws 80 in-lb** (6 mm). Plate position 1 for an x:panda seat depth under 9.75 in, position 2 over; rear-facing only in position 3, infant sizes only, no footplate.
- **Reversing the seat changes transit labelling** (kits #120802 to rear-facing, #120801 back): rear-facing has no transit rating.
- Ventilator mount (Pulmonetics LTV) and O2 holder available.
