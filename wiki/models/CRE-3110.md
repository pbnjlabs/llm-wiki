# CRE-3110 (Bruno curved rail, 2026)

A newer Bruno **curved-rail** stairlift that appears in Bruno's 2026
instruction sheets. The only document on file is its spec and dimension
drawing, [[ILS-01620 CRE-3110 Technical Specifications]] (Rev P1,
05/28/26). No manuals yet. The low-profile rocker it shares with the
SRE-3050 (SRE-30544) suggests Elan-style controls.

## Specs

- **300 lb (136 kg)**, one passenger. (CRE-2110: 400 lb.)
- 2 x 12 V **7 Ah**; charger 110 VAC 1.5 A, 24 VDC 2 A.
- 24 VDC direct-drive gear motor, **1/3 hp**, **25 ft/min** max; 8DP rack.
- Braking: dynamic plus electromechanical motor brake.
- Rocker on the armrest; wall call/sends top and bottom.
- **0–65°** (CRE-2110: 0–50°).
- Standard: final limit switch, **overspeed**, keyed control on the
  carriage.
- Options: power swivel, power footrest, reduced extension at the lower
  landing, **Smart Swivel for narrow staircases down to 26 in (660 mm)**,
  short footrest.
- Rail **7 in from the wall** typical; component weights: seat 32 lb,
  footrest 6 lb, seat mount 6 lb, carriage 70 lb, rail 6 lb/ft.
