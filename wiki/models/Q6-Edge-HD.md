# Q6 Edge HD

Quantum's heavy-duty mid-wheel-drive Edge. Spec sheet only on file.

From [[Q6 Edge HD Specifications]] (Rev C, Feb 2020):

| | |
|---|---|
| Class | B (Group 3) |
| Capacity | **450 lb**; 450 lb with TRU-Balance 3 HD power tilt; **400 lb with the TRU-Balance 10 in power-adjustable HD lift** (and with iLevel, per the 2025 chart); 500 lb with TRU-Balance Bariatric Tilt/Synergy (UK only) |
| Max speed | 4.7 mph (UK 4 mph); 3.5 mph with HD iLevel (2025 chart) |
| Range | up to 17.5 mi; 15.66 mi with knobby tires |
| Ground clearance | 3.5 in at motor, 3 in at base frame, 2.5 in at caster pivot |
| Turning radius | 19.75 in |
| Length | 49.1 in with front rigging, 35.6 in without |
| Width | 26.25 in |
| Drive wheels | 14 in solid (std), pneumatic optional; 6 in solid casters |
| Suspension | ATX |
| Weights | base 143.5 lb; Group 24 batteries 52 lb each; total under 436.8 lb (as tested with factory transit system) |
| Batteries | two 12 V **Group 24** |
| Charger | 8 A off-board |
| Max slope / climb | 7.5° (US) / 3.5 in |
| Max seat | 28 × 28 in TRU-Balance Bariatric Tilt; with iLevel 20 × 20 to 24 × 24 in |

Electronics (2025 chart): **120 A Q-Logic 3, or 75 A Q-Logic NE / NE+**;
4-pole motors. The chart lists the drive wheel as 14 in run-flat.
