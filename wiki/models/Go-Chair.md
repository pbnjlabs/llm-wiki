# Go Chair (travel power chair)

Pride's take-apart travel power chair: **300 lb**, **2 × 20 Ah
nano-silica** batteries (16 lb), 3.5 A charger, 40 A PG **GC3**. The
**Go Chair Med** is DME-coded K0821 (portable), 3.7 mph; the retail Go
Chair (K0899) is 3.8 mph and **36 lb** heaviest piece (2026 charts,
[[Pride-Power-Chair-Lineup]]). Warranty: 5-year frame, 13 months on the
rest ([[Pride-Warranty]]).

## Documents

- [[Go Chair Owners Manual (INFMANU4750)]] (Rev E, 2022).

## Notes

- **Freewheel:** pull both motor levers toward the rear. Don't charge in
  freewheel.
- **Main circuit breaker on the front of the battery box.** The box has
  its own charger port; it charges on or off the chair.
- Seat height: two positions, 1 in apart.
- Infrequent use: weekly charge of 12–14 h.
- GC3 codes: [[Pride-Controller-Flash-Codes]]. Care:
  [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]].
