## Notes from the manuals

- **Freewheel:** Elite ES pull both levers rearward toward the seat
  post; Elite HD/14 rotate both **outward**. **The HD/14 gives no
  warning or code if only one lever is engaged**, and it will still
  drive, unpredictably. Check both by eye at delivery and service.
  Flip the foot plates down to reach them.
- **HD seat depth by weight:** 270–400 lb, 18–24 in deep seats;
  401–450 lb, 20 in or deeper only.
- ELRs 12–19 in; heavy-duty drop-in and elevating leg rests on the HD.
- Codes: GC3 or Dynamic, [[Pride-Controller-Flash-Codes]]. Care:
  [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]].
