# TiLite Folding Series

TiLite's folding manual chairs, the **X** and **2GX**, covered by one Permobil
service manual (PD16521 Rev 6). General rules match the rigid chairs
([[TiLite-Rigid-Series]]): no water-based lube near bearings, thread-patch
fasteners reused twice at most, Loctite 242.

## Transit

The **X** with the Transit Tie-Down Option is crash-tested (ISO 7176-19:2022) as
a forward-facing seat. **The 2GX is not** and must not be occupied in a vehicle.
See [[Transport-and-Tie-Downs]].

## Torques

| Fastener | Torque |
|---|---|
| Axle sleeve | 22 Nm (26 Nm in one procedure: check the step) |
| M8 Allen screw | 15 Nm |
| Footplate bolts | 15 Nm max |
| Hex bolts (tighten alternately) | 40 Nm |
| Outer screw | 7 Nm |
| Flip-back hinge and back cane bolts | 5 Nm |

Back angle: the **locking-block grooves must line up with the pivot bracket**.
Wheel stop embeds at least 5 mm into the tire.
