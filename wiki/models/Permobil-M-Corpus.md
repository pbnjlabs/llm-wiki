# Permobil M Corpus (M1, M3, M5, M Corpus VS)

Permobil's current **mid-wheel-drive** power chairs on R-net, with Corpus
power seating on ICS. User manuals only on file
([[Permobil M Corpus User Manuals (M1, M3, M5, M Corpus VS)]]); **no
service manual or parts manual**. The front-wheel-drive siblings are
[[Permobil-F-Corpus]].

| | M1 | M3 Corpus | M5 Corpus | M Corpus VS (standing) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Max user | 300 lb | 330 lb | 330 lb | **265 lb** |
| Speed | 5 mph | 6 mph | 7.5 mph | 6 mph |
| Batteries | 2 × group 34 SLA | group 34 or 24 | group 24 | group 24 |
| Max slope | 10° | **7.5°** | 10° | 10° |
| Obstacle | 3 in | 3 in | **4 in** | 2.5 in |

**Model version 2 vs 3** (on the serial label, lower left of the chassis
and on the seat tilt): same chair; v3 has the **Power Platform** radio
(flight mode), v2 ConnectMe. App setup: [[MyPermobil Activation Guide (DOC0095-03)]].

## Service notes

- **Drive tires 35 psi (250 kPa).** Casters are solid.
- **Main circuit breaker** lever at the back: the user may reset it once;
  a second trip needs a tech. Charging breaker self-resets in 5 min.
- **Freewheel:** two levers out = free. If it still drives in freewheel,
  it's a fault.
- Tool bag: 11/13 mm wrenches, 2–6 mm Allen, 15 mm socket. Torques:
  panel holder **9.8 Nm**; M1 adjustments mostly 9.8 Nm, swing-away
  footplate angle 24 Nm, height 7.4 Nm, panel angle 2.9 Nm.
- **Batteries:** the user manuals don't cover replacement. Per PMS house
  standard, **always replace both batteries as a matched set**
  ([[Mobility-Batteries]]).
- **Diagnostics:** R-net trip text and code on the joystick screen, or LED
  flashes: [[R-net-Diagnostics]]. Seat (ICS) faults and actuator
  calibration: [[ICS Onboard Configuration Technical Manual (205247, Ed 8)]].
- **VS standing:** level ground only; chest and knee supports always on to
  stand. Contraindications: low bone density, poor circulation.
- Transport: four tie-downs or a Dahl MK II / VarioDock (136 kg limit).
