# PATHWAY HD (Code Compliant Modular Access System & Platforms)

EZ-ACCESS's **commercial** aluminum modular family: ramps, platforms,
decks and stairs built to the IBC and ADA. The residential counterpart
is [[PATHWAY-3G]]. PMS's ramp work is mostly residential PATHWAY 3G;
the HD matters for commercial jobs and for a **VPL upper landing at 30
in or higher**, where code needs picketed guards (see
[[Aluminum Platforms for VPLs (2026)]]).

## Key specs

| | PATHWAY HD | For comparison, PATHWAY 3G |
|---|---|---|
| Rating | **100 psf live, 300 lb concentrated** (2021 IBC) | 1,000 lb; 40 psf (IRC) |
| Slope | **1:12 required** | 1:12 ideal, 1:14 – 1:8 allowed |
| Ramp widths | **48, 54, 60 in** clear | 36, 48 in |
| Ramp lengths | 2–6 ft, plus a **6 ft starter ramp** (tapered end, must sit fully on the ground) | 2–8 ft |
| Curb | **4 in** (or a barrier a 4 in sphere can't pass) | 2 in |
| Handrail | **36 in** (34–38 allowed), child rail 25 in, clearance 1-1/2 in | 34.5 in, lower rail 24 in |
| Guards | Frame top rail **42 in**; pickets 3-3/4 in typical, **3-15/16 in max** | Pickets 4 in max gap |
| Platforms | 5 × 5, 5 × 6, 6 × 6, 5 × 7 ft; 1 × 4.5 and 1 × 5 ft extenders; 5.5 in minimum height | 4 × 4 to 8 × 5 |
| Legs | PHDSLxxPR 10–112 in, 1.5 in square, **feet through-bolted** | PSLxxPR 10–100 in |
| Installer | **EZ-ACCESS approved technician** | Qualified installer |
| Warranty | **3 years** | Lifetime |

- **Guards from 30 in of rise** (EZ-ACCESS worksheet). Two-line rails
  are for lower rises; the two-line closure to a stair landing is rated
  only up to 30 in.
- **Bracing:** cross braces under platforms and ramps **over 36 in**
  (TXB36 for 5–6 ft sides and ramps, TXB736 for 7 ft sides), sometimes
  lower depending on the site; more at 72 in.
- **Torques:** leg bracket locknuts 20 ft-lb, leg set screws 15 ft-lb
  (same as the 3G); stair riser set screws 317 in-lb (26 ft-lb) minimum.
- **Starter ramp rails** must have their lower post supports anchored (4
  concrete anchors, 1/4 or 3/8 × 2-1/2 in min, each) for a
  code-compliant install.
- **Inspection:** visual before each use; **all fasteners annually**,
  more often in harsh environments.
- Options: handrail **return to ground** (1 ft level min, straight or
  90°), platform extenders so end loops don't block a landing, extended
  or California / Massachusetts compliant loops, stamped seismic and
  wind calculations.

## Stairs

| System | Treads | Rise | Compliant rise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adjustable Stair | 1–8 | 12–67 in in 7 in bands (12–18, 19–25 ... 61–67) | 14, 21 ... 63 in |
| Multi-Story Stair | 14–16 | 103–123 in, 138–144 in | 105, 112, 119, 140 in |

7 in risers, 12-1/4 in treads, 56 in wide (50-3/16 in between rails),
36 in handrails, 42-1/4 in guards. **Risers may vary by no more than 3/8
in** through the stairway (ADA/IBC), so set the system at its compliant
height. Risers level or sloped away from the landing 1:48 – 1:50 max for
drainage. The spec sheet also lists 6 in riser steps from 18 to 60 in.
