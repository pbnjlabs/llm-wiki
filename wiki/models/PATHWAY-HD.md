# PATHWAY HD (Code Compliant Modular Access System & Platforms)

EZ-ACCESS's **commercial/industrial** aluminum modular family. The
residential counterpart is [[PATHWAY-3G]]. It comes in three platform
forms, and all of them integrate with PATHWAY HD ramps and stairs.

| Product | Use |
|---|---|
| Code Compliant Modular Access System / Platform Walkway | 48 in usable width, two-way traffic, independently adjustable legs, quick-install handrails. Designed to ADA and most local codes. |
| Platform Decking | Custom-size wide decks (stages, patios, viewing platforms). Handrails optional. |
| Work Platforms | Adjustable elevation over cables and pipes. Can be built multiple stories high (mezzanines, equipment access). |

- Extra reinforcement for heavy loads can be engineered through
  EZ-ACCESS R&D.
- Made in the USA.
