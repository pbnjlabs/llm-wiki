# GR575 Patriot (Golden)

A heavy-duty scooter that came before the [[GR595-Eagle]]. It is
covered by the [[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]] with a **5-year frame**
(not lifetime). Seat code SMD ([[Golden-Seating-Systems]]). Link to
[[Golden]].

## Documents on file

- [[GR575 Patriot Service Guide]] (2014). It covers troubleshooting,
  replacement steps and a full IPB.

## What's confirmed

- **PG S-Drive, 140 A** controller, 18–24 VDC.
- **Two 12V 75 Ah** batteries. The battery fuse is **125 A**; there are
  also 15 A and 3 A fuses and a main circuit breaker.
- Beep codes 2/3/5/6/7/8/9: [[S-Drive-Beep-Codes]].
- It has a mechanical **hand brake** (the IPB has a dashboard and hand
  brake drawing) and a high/low speed switch.
- Field-replaceable parts: controller, drive wheel, dash, key switch,
  high/low switch, **motor brushes**, motor/brake, transaxle, batteries,
  shrouds.
