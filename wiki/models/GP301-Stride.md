# GP301 Stride (Golden)

A **folding rear-wheel-drive power chair** with a 10 Ah lithium pack.
Heavier than the [[GP302-Cricket]], lighter than the [[GP303-Ally]].
Link to [[Golden]].

## Documents on file

- Retailer part numbers (Mobility Scooters Direct, 2026-10-27; confirm by serial with the manufacturer): [[Parts Catalog - Mobility Scooters Direct (Golden)#Golden Stride Folding Power Wheelchair (GP301) Parts|Golden Stride Folding Power Wheelchair (GP301) Parts]] (5)
- [[Stride Owners Manual (GP301)]] (Apr 2024)
- [[Stride Illustrated Parts Breakdown (GP301)]] (Jul 2024): **two versions like the Cricket:
  GP301A = Micon joystick (MBE-0001), GP301B = LiNX joystick (MBE-0052)**,
  different 10 Ah batteries (MBE-0002 / MBE-0053), charger MBE-0014 (shared
  with the GP302A). The GP301B's codes would be [[LiNX-Diagnostics]].

## Specs

3.7 mph, 9.3 mi, 24 V 10 Ah Li-ion (3.8 lb), **50 A** controller, two
200 W motors, 2 A charger. 42.5 × 24.4 in; folds to 15.7 in long.
**58 lb assembled** (the assembly page says about 55). Flat-free 12 in
drive and 8 in casters. Sling seat 17 × 18 in.

> [!warning] Capacity: 300 or 265 lb?
> The spec table says 300 lb; the safety page and beep-code table say
> **265 lb**. Use 265 lb until Golden confirms.

## Service notes

- **Won't power up** until the battery harness behind the pack is
  connected (ships unplugged).
- To stand folded, both **anti-tip brackets must be extended and
  locked**. Fold with the freewheel levers in **drive (forward)**.
- Charger LED **blue** while charging, green when full; driving is
  inhibited while charging.
- **Beep codes** (horn twice, then the count): the same 1–9 table as the
  Micon-controlled [[GP302-Cricket]], so probably the same controller
  family (the manual doesn't name it).
