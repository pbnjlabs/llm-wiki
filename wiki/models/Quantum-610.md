# Quantum 610 (legacy)

Older Quantum mid-wheel chair. No owner's manual on file.

## Documents

- [[Quantum 610 Parts Replacement Guide (INFMANU4846)]] (Jan 2017)

## Service notes

- Drive wheel main nut **51 N·m**; re-seat the axle key.
- Caster nut 30 N·m; foot platform 25 N·m; **articulating beam screw
  58 N·m (43 ft·lb)**.
- Freewheel: push the levers **inward** for drive, pull **outward** for
  freewheel ([[Freewheel-Mode]]).
- Original warranty insert INFINFB2606 (Q610) is in the PQ-365 library
  but wasn't pulled.
