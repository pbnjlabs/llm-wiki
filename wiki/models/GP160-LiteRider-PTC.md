# GP160 LiteRider PTC (Golden)

A **discontinued** compact power chair. It came before the
[[GP162-LiteRider-Envy]] and shares its PG VR2 controller and code
table. Link to [[Golden]].

## Documents on file

- [[LiteRider PTC Service Guide (GP160)]] (2012). It covers
  troubleshooting, charging, replacement steps and an IPB.
- [[Battery Replacement (GL110, GL140, GP160)]]. The battery pack design
  is shared with the GL110/GL140 LiteRider scooter.

## What's confirmed

- **PG VR2** controller (joystick control unit, power module, bus
  cable), 18–24 VDC.
- **Two 12V 18 Ah** batteries.
- Flash codes 1–10 and the TruCharge gauge: [[TruCharge-Diagnostics]].
  Programmer trip codes: [[PG-Drives-Trip-Codes]].
- Motor resistance under 5 Ω, not shorted.
