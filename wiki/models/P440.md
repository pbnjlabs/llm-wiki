# P440 (Handicare Portable Ceiling Lift, sold under the Prism brand)

A **portable** ceiling lift — Prism's second documented product family
alongside the permanently track-mounted [[C-450-C-625]]. Where C-450/
C-625 stay fixed in a track and traverse under their own power, the
P-440 is designed to be **carried by hand** from track to track (bedroom,
bathroom, living room) and manually pushed along whichever track it's
hung on; only the lifting (up/down) function is motorized. Two SKUs:
**303070** (standard hooks) and **303073** (extended hook).

**Manufacturer confirmed: Handicare.** The 2021 revision of this
manual ([[P440 Owner's Manual (2021, Rev 19-Oct)]]) shows a "handicare
P-440" nameplate throughout and gives Handicare USA/Canada contact
addresses — resolving the "Handicare" component-brand mystery
previously only hinted at in [[C-450-C-625]]'s evaluation-form note. See
[[Prism]] for the full Handicare/Prism relationship.

## How it works
Same three-subsystem shape as C-450/C-625 (pneumatic control /
electrical / mechanical) but simpler:
- **Pneumatic**: a piston-displacement airline hand control drives
  up/down only — no traverse function, since the lift itself is moved by
  hand
- **Electrical**: 24VDC motor, 2×12VDC 2.3Ah batteries, ~2 min auto-off,
  15A internal fuse, low-battery alarm (orange → red + fast beep as
  charge drops, UP disabled first, DOWN/emergency-down still functional)
- **Mechanical**: carry bar hooks (sling loop must sit on the hook base,
  never resting on the locking-clip latch — a mis-seated loop can slide
  off under load), lift tape/strap with a slack-tape detector, trolley
  eyelet connection (direct, reacher arm, lanyard, or lanyard+reacher)

## Specs
- Max load: 440 lbs (199.5 kg)
- Lifting range: up to 1.96m; lift weight 10.5 lbs (4.8 kg)
- Service life: 7 years or ~22,500 cycles, whichever comes first; lifting
  strap recommended replacement every 3 years/5,000 cycles (sooner under
  heavy use)
- Multiple international charger/plug variants documented (US, UK, AU)

## Safety mechanisms
- Emergency lowering: dedicated red button, ~2 sec delay + audible alarm,
  **lowering only** — no emergency raise; failure of this must be
  reported to the dealer immediately
- Emergency Stop switch: cuts all motor power instantly; once tripped in
  a real emergency, requires inspection by an authorized dealer before
  being returned to ON (also required for charging — lift won't charge
  with Emergency Stop OFF)
- Slack-tape detector: same fail-safe concept as [[C-450-C-625]]'s
  slack-strap/thick-strap limit switches, though implemented more simply
  here (a single sensitivity point rather than a tape-switch roller
  assembly)

## Track compatibility
Per [[Ceiling Lift Installation Guide]] (Vancare, Inc.'s track manual —
found filed under `MANUALS/Prism/` but not a Prism-authored document,
see [[Vancare]]): the P-440 (and a sibling **P-600**, 600 lb capacity,
not otherwise documented in this wiki) requires a trolley, and is sold
with trolley adapters for **All Vancare Track and Accessories**, **Mikyway
Track**, **Guldman Track**, and **ArjoTrack** — confirming Prism's
portable lifts are designed to run on multiple competing track systems,
not just a Prism/Vancare-exclusive rail.

## Accessories (per the 2021 manual)
Track (fixed and cut-to-length TrackPlus), 45°/90° curves, Transgate
system, Multi-Port and Quick Fit turntable systems, track end stops,
"H" system trolley set, wall-mount/3"/6" track brackets, a reacher
pole, and custom slings — "only slings authorized by Handicare are to
be used with this lift." See also the co-branded
[[Prism-Castor-2-Post-Lift-Stand]], a freestanding track accessory sized
to match the C-Series lifts' capacities rather than the P440 directly.

## Known quirk: carabiner type varies by manufacturing year
The 2021 manual shows three different carabiner styles as all valid
depending on when the unit was manufactured, with correct/incorrect
orientation photos: a sling strap loop must sit at the base of the
carabiner, never resting on the latch — a mis-seated loop can slide off
under load (the manual calls this a serious injury/death risk).

## Warranty
Per the 2021 manual's definitive split: **Handicare** guarantees new
equipment for 1 year from install date or 18 months from manufacture
date, whichever is shorter; **Prism** separately guarantees refurbished
equipment for 3 months from install or 6 months from purchase,
whichever is shorter. Void if serviced by anyone other than Handicare
or an authorized agent. **Different terms and documents** from
[[C-450-C-625]], which has no warranty text documented on file.
