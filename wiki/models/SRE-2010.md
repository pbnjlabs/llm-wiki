# SRE-2010 (Bruno Elite)

Bruno's "Elite" straight-rail residential stairlift. Variants: SRE-2010,
SRE-2010C, SRE-2010OS, SRE-2010CAN, SRE-2010G, SRE-2010E/EOS. Sibling
product line to the [[SRE-3050]] Elan — see that page for how Bruno
differentiates the two.

## When to use Elite over Elan

Bruno explicitly recommends the **Elite (SRE-2010)** instead of the Elan
(SRE-3050) for:
- Users who scoot-pivot transfer from a wheelchair without bearing weight
  through their legs (full body weight lands on a single stairlift
  armrest)
- Users with leg weakness whose knees buckle with uncontrolled lowering
  during stand-to-sit transfer

(Source: this recommendation appears in the SRE-3050 documentation, not an
SRE-2010 doc — see [[SRE-3050 Operator Manual]].)

## Key procedures

- **Opposite-hand conversion**: full ~35-step field procedure (flip seat
  swivel plate, carriage, footrest, rewire) — documented in the tech
  support guide, not summarized here in detail; see source PDF.
- **Overspeed reset**: see [[Overspeed-Safety-Brake]] — identical mechanism
  to SRE-3050/SRE-3000.
- **Folding rail** (manual or power): see [[Folding-Rail]]. The Elite
  carriage takes its own Folding Rail Carriage Safety Bracket part/procedure
  (distinct from the Elan's), installed over the carriage's downside cover.
- **Rail mounting/anchoring**: see [[Rail-Mounting-Guidance]] — Bruno's
  single cross-model reference for anchoring into different staircase
  construction types (wood, hardwood, engineered wood, marble/tile, steel,
  poured concrete, precast, cinder block/landscape block, railroad ties,
  glass).
- **Remote reprogramming**: IR remote DIP switch (SW3, 4 positions) +
  circuit board LEARN/ERASE button — identical mechanism to SRE-3050.
- **Diagnostic modes**: multi-user (default, full audio diagnostics) vs.
  single-user (silences the seat-swiveled-at-top beep) — jumper-selectable
  on the circuit board.
- **Diagnostic codes**: shares the same code table as [[SRE-3000]] and
  [[CRE-2110]] — see [[Diagnostic-Codes]].

## Quirks / notes

- Two hardware generations for seat arm extension: pre- and post-October
  2022 — check install date before ordering replacement parts.
- The tech support guide bundles the "SRE-2010 Model Rail Mounting
  Guidance" document, but Bruno also embeds the *identical* document in
  the SRE-3050 tech support guide — despite the title naming only the
  2010, it's a cross-model reference in practice.
