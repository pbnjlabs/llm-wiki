# SRE-2010 (Bruno Elite)

Bruno's "Elite" straight-rail residential stairlift. Variants: SRE-2010,
SRE-2010C, SRE-2010OS, SRE-2010CAN, SRE-2010G, SRE-2010E/EOS. Sibling
product line to the [[SRE-3050]] Elan — see that page for how Bruno
differentiates the two.

## When to use Elite over Elan

Bruno explicitly recommends the **Elite (SRE-2010)** instead of the Elan
(SRE-3050) for:
- Users who scoot-pivot transfer from a wheelchair without bearing weight
  through their legs (full body weight lands on a single stairlift
  armrest)
- Users with leg weakness whose knees buckle with uncontrolled lowering
  during stand-to-sit transfer

(Source: this recommendation appears in the SRE-3050 documentation, not an
SRE-2010 doc — see [[SRE-3050 Operator Manual]].)

## From Bruno's ILS sheets

- **Specs (ILS-00826, 2026):** 400 lb (2010G 420 lb); 0–50° (C/CAN/G/OS
  30–50°); 25 ft/min; IR call/send (outdoor 2010E: **2.4 GHz RF**, keyed,
  IPX3, charger indoors, 0–125 °F); rail **6 in from the wall** (XL seat
  9⅛ in), 2 in above the step nose; 30 A breaker.
- **Gear motor changed supplier in Feb 2025:** kits SRV-2010-046L/R; new
  drive adjustment lever and drive lock spacer; **34 Nm**; leads now
  **blue/brown**: runs the wrong way → swap brown and blue on the PCB.
- **Brake kit SRV-2010-045** (hex hub 0.059–0.079 in) or **SRV-2110-032**
  for 2025-and-later carriages. Brushes: ILS-01426.
- **Seat belts:** BLT-00005 (before Oct 2020) → kit SRV-2010-035L/R
  (BLT-00035); new seat from **Sept 27, 2022** uses BLT-00037 (kits
  SRV-2010-040 indoor / -041 outdoor). 4-point harness doesn't replace the
  belt.
- **Elite footrest handle** defect (tight joint cracks the lift link):
  replace handle and link (ILS-01533).
- Quiet **Mode 3** = DIP 1 on, 2 off. The ILS wording "single-user DIP 1
  and 2" is ambiguous. The 2026 install manual's table is clear:
  **single-user = #2 ON only**, both ON = battery warnings only.

## Install essentials (2026 manuals)

From [[SRE-2010 Install Manual]] and [[SRE-2010E Install Manual]]:
- **Rail length** = stair length + 9 in (20 in seat), **plus 4 in with an
  overspeed brake** (C/OS/G/EC/EOS). Add 1.5 in per extra inch of seat. A
  "16 ft" rail is 186 in and a "20 ft" rail 236 in. No piece under 24 in.
- **Mounts:**
  - None on the top landing as standard. Use the first two steps down
    from the top, the bottom landing and the first step up.
  - No more than 3 steps without one. An adjustable set (SRE-30150WG)
    goes on the landing for a long overrun.
  - Bolts 25 ft-lb.
- **Clearances:**
  - Rail 2 in off the noses (2.5 in on carpet).
  - **Back of the top of the rail to the nearest obstruction: 4.25 in
    (5.25 in with an outdoor canopy, 7.25 in with the wide seat).**
  - Footrest to nose ½–¾ in, seat to wall ½ in.
- **RH jumpers:** 3-, 6- and **12-pin** (LH 3- and 6-pin).
- **Carriage angle bolts:** 32–50° A+B, 25–35° A+C, under 25° any two top
  holes, 50 ft-lb. Footrest 50 ft-lb.
- **Jack screw:** M6 × 25 if the rack ends ½ in or less from the end,
  M6 × 40 if ½–1¼ in; no more than one turn past finger tight. **Rail
  cross pin** with kit SRV-3000-023. Top end plate 22 ft-lb.
- **DIP (4-gang, under the seat hinge plate cover):**
  - Multi-user = 1 OFF, 2 OFF. Single-user = 2 ON. Quiet = 1 ON.
    Battery warnings only = both ON.
  - **#3 ON = right-hand.**
- **Indoor** units charge anywhere on the rail. **Outdoor (E/EC/EOS) units
  charge only parked against an end contact.** An internal charge lead
  runs in the rail hollow; the un-notched contact edge is +, and every
  rail connection gets dielectric grease. C3 = not parked.
- **Outdoor:**
  - Keyed **2.4 GHz RF** remotes (9 V), mounted 35–48 in up.
  - Seat cover and bag CRE-00995/CRE-00999.
  - Charger indoors.
  - 0–125 °F, not within 20 min of lightning.
  - Re-grease the rack after storage.
  - **Warranty 5 years on major components** (indoor: lifetime).
- **Power swivel (Elite):** at the top, **release and press again** to
  swivel. Seat to swivel 4 × M6, 150 in-lb. Shipping jumpers are kept on
  the harness.

## Key procedures

- **Opposite-hand conversion**: full field procedure (flip seat swivel
  plate, carriage, footrest, rewire). Bruno's standalone copy is
  ILS-01409 ([[Tech Tip - SRE-2010 Opposite Hand Conversion (ILS-01409)]]).
  Key numbers: no power tools; seat jam nut Loctite 242 at **70 ft-lb**;
  **DIP switch 3 ON = right hand**; carriage angle bolts by stair angle
  (32–50° holes A+B, 25–35° A+C) at **50 ft-lb** with safety washers;
  batteries back in the **same orientation**.
- **Overspeed test, SRE-2010C:** Bruno's kit **SRV-2010-018** is a drop
  test (stop within 1.5 in), which pulls and re-shrinks the spur gear with
  an induction heater ([[Tech Tip - SRE-2010C Overspeed Test (SRV-2010-018)]]).
- **Overspeed reset**: see [[Overspeed-Safety-Brake]] — identical mechanism
  to SRE-3050/SRE-3000.
- **Folding rail** (manual or power): see [[Folding-Rail]]. Power folding
  rails built after Bruno's 2026 update have board **PCB-00193** and a
  notch around the down limit switch. The Elite
  carriage takes its own Folding Rail Carriage Safety Bracket part/procedure
  (distinct from the Elan's), installed over the carriage's downside cover.
- **Rail mounting/anchoring**: see [[Rail-Mounting-Guidance]] — Bruno's
  single cross-model reference for anchoring into different staircase
  construction types (wood, hardwood, engineered wood, marble/tile, steel,
  poured concrete, precast, cinder block/landscape block, railroad ties,
  glass).
- **Remote reprogramming**: IR remote DIP switch (SW3, 4 positions) +
  circuit board LEARN/ERASE button — identical mechanism to SRE-3050.
- **Diagnostic modes**: multi-user (default, full audio diagnostics),
  single-user (silences the seat-swivelled-at-top beep), quiet, or
  battery warnings only. Set on the 4-gang DIP switch under the seat
  (table above). Older docs say "jumper".
- **Diagnostic codes**: shares the same code table as [[SRE-3000]] and
  [[CRE-2110]] — see [[Diagnostic-Codes]].

## Troubleshooting

Start with [[Stairlift-Troubleshooting]] (symptom-first, from Bruno's
[[TechMate Stairlift Troubleshooter]]). SRE-2010 specifics:
- Read the **code display under the seat** first: L1 seat safety, L2/L3
  limits, d4/d5/d7 over-current/over-temperature/low voltage, d8
  unlearned remote, P0 communication, F = board ([[Diagnostic-Codes]]).
- **Indoor SRE-2010 is continuous charge. Outdoor (OS/EOS) units charge
  only at the ends**, so C3/C4 matter there.
- Elite charger has separate red/yellow/green/blue LEDs (meanings on
  [[Stairlift-Troubleshooting]]).
- Owner-level codes (2026 owner's manual): A2 seat up, A3 seat out of
  position, **A7 seat or footrest jammed** (power options), C1–C6
  charging and battery, L1–L3, L6 mid-park, L8 soft stop.
- **Power folding rail (Elite):** it stops at mid-park and the rider
  presses again. The rail panel has a **disable switch**; in an emergency
  switch it OFF and move the rail **slowly** by hand (a fast lift damages
  the fold gearbox).
- Seat won't stay folded up → **gas spring**.
- Clicks but won't run with voltage at the motor leads → **brushes**.
- Seat safety jump: the 2-pin (brown, white) at the board; installation
  jumper is the 6-pin.
- **Motor lead check** (blue/orange, older non-power-option guide): at
  25 V+ resting, about **21 V up and 12 V or more down**. Low or none →
  board ([[Tech Tip - SRE-2010 Troubleshooting Guide (No Power Options)]]).
- Old-style **power folding rail bounces back**: lower actuator plate
  adjustment, not the linkage bar
  ([[Tech Tip - SRE-2010 Power Folding Rail Bounce-Back (Old Style)]]).
- RF remotes (SRE-2010E): see the antenna and double-remote-failure notes
  on [[Stairlift-Troubleshooting]] (board SRV-2010-007).

## Quirks / notes

- Two hardware generations for seat arm extension: pre- and post-October
  2022 — check install date before ordering replacement parts.
- **Older SRE-2010E (outdoor) clamps are die-cast aluminium** and can be
  eaten away by acid step-washes or galvanic action. Bruno: replace them
  right away; current clamps are composite
  ([[Tech Tip - SRE-2010E Clamp Deterioration]]).
- **Old-style rail** units (before the 4/23/2013 rail change): custom-cut
  sections from 9/20/2011 have numbered joint decals; rail length is floor
  to top nose + 9 in; rail 4.5 in from the wall
  ([[Tech Tip - SRE-2010 Quick Install Guide (Old Style Rail)]]).
- Footrest lift link: the old **plastic link** is replaced by a
  **spring-steel** one, left one turn loose to flex
  ([[Tech Tip - Footrest Lift Linkage Replacement]]).
- Serial numbers: model digit **2** = Elite ([[Bruno-Serial-Numbers]]).
- Yearly service: [[Stairlift-Maintenance]].
- The tech support guide bundles the "SRE-2010 Model Rail Mounting
  Guidance" document, but Bruno also embeds the *identical* document in
  the SRE-3050 tech support guide — despite the title naming only the
  2010, it's a cross-model reference in practice.
