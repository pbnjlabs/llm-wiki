# Liberty (tilt-in-space, folding or rigid)

Ki Mobility **tilt-in-space** chair on **gas springs**, built folding or non-folding (convertible). **250 lb (113 kg)**, transit 250 lb. 23 kg (51 lb) as tested.

- **Tilt cable:** take out slack so the trigger has **1/8 in of play** before it pulls; check both sides lock at every position.
- Folding: separate the sides and pull the center strap up at a slight angle. A low side guard can stop the last few degrees of tilt.
- Tech manual: **adjusting and replacing gas springs** and the trigger assembly, replacing tilt cables, converting to folding, wheel pull correction, fold lever, transit install. Values: 80 ft-lb, 55 ft-lb bottom nut, 144 in-lb.
