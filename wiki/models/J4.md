# J4 / J4E / J4EHD

Quantum's Group 2 mid-wheel-drive chairs. **J4** = base; **J4E** = with
power seat elevate; **J4EHD** = heavy-duty J4E (400 lb). Spec sheets on
file for the J4 and J4E; the J4EHD is only on the 2025 chart.

| | J4 ([[J4 Specifications]], 2019) | J4E ([[J4E Specifications (INFINFB4645)]], 2024) | J4EHD (2025 chart) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Class | — | Group 2 | Group 2 |
| Capacity | 300 lb | 300 lb | 400 lb |
| Max speed | 5 mph | 5 mph; **1.25 mph with elevate** | 5 mph; 1.25 elevated |
| Range | 17.6 mi | 16.1 mi | — |
| Ground clearance | 2.9 in battery tray, 2.5 in caster pivot | 3.13 in battery tray | 3.13 in |
| Turning radius | 21 in | 20.5 in | 23 in |
| Length | stowage 32.3 in | 40.5 in (stowage 32.2) without front riggings | 35.5 in |
| Width | 24.5 in | 24.4 in | 24.4 in |
| Drive wheels | 14 in flat-free; 6 in solid casters | 14 in (solid available); 6 in casters | 14 in flat-free |
| Base weight | 131.6 lb | 138.6 lb; total under 377.2 lb with TRU-Balance 3 | 138.6 lb |
| Batteries | 2 × NF-22, 38 lb (AGM or gel) | 2 × NF-22, 38 lb (AGM, gel or hybrid gel) | 2 × NF-22, 38 lb |
| Charger | 8 A off-board | 8 A off-board | — |
| Max slope / climb | 6° / 2 in | 6° / 2 in | — |
| Electronics (2025 chart) | 75 A QLNES | 75 A QLNES or 120 A **Q-Logic 3e** | 75 A QLNES or 120 A Q-Logic 3e |
| Motors | 2-pole | 2-pole | 4-pole |
| HCPCS | K0822/23, K0835 | K0835 | K0837 |

All: ATX suspension, two-motor Mid-Wheel 6, "Intelligent Braking"
regenerative + disc park brake, FSS contract V797D-70001.

([[Q-Logic-3-Bluetooth-and-Remote-Programming]]). Codes:
