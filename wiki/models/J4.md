# J4 / J4E / J4EHD

Quantum's Group 2 mid-wheel-drive chairs. **J4** = base; **J4E** = with
power seat elevate; **J4EHD** = heavy-duty J4E (400 lb). Spec sheets on
file for the J4 and J4E, and a 2021 **J4 HD** sheet
([[J4 HD Specifications (INFINFB4295)]]): 400 lb, 5 mph, 21 in turning
radius, 15.7 mi, base 131.6 lb, NF-22, 8 A charger, **6° max slope**,
2 in climb, 14 in flat-free drive wheels, 6 in solid casters. The J4EHD
is on the 2025 chart.

## Owner's manuals

- [[J4 and J4 HD Owners Manual (INFMANU5068)]]: freewheel lever on each
  motor (one in freewheel = drives in a circle); **seat dump −5° to
  +21° in 2.5° steps**; seat width **16–20 in**; armrest height 3 in in
  1-in steps; manual recline back on the **J4 only**.
- [[J4E and J4E HD Owners Manual (INFMANU5454)]]: power elevating seat
  from the **Q-Logic 3e** ([[Q-Logic 3e Basic Operation Instructions (INFMANU5154)]]:
  lock = hold Mode); AFP elevates to 13°, single actuator **300 lb**,
  dual actuator **450 lb**, 40–105 °F.
- Shared care and charging: [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]].
