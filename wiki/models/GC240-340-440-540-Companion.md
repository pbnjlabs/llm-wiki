# Companion — GC240, GC340, GC440 (and GC540 "Companion HD")

Golden's mid-size scooter line. GC240/GC340/GC440 share one platform and
one operator manual/service guide; **GC540 "Companion HD" is a distinct
heavier-duty variant**, not a color option — see below. Predecessor
generation: [[GC221-321-421-Companion]].

## GC440 model change (2024)

### GC440 batteries (PMS practice)

Per the human, the batteries PMS installs:

| GC440 | Battery (MK Power) | Size | Weight each | Pair |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pre-2024** | **MU-1 SLD M** (U1, AGM) | 35 Ah, 7.72 × 5.12 × 7.09 in | 23.1 lb | 46.2 lb |
| **2024+** | **M50-12 SLD M** (AGM) | 50 Ah, 7.83 × 6.54 × 6.73 in | 33.2 lb | 66.4 lb |

- Golden's 2013 service guide confirms the older Companion took two
  35 Ah U1s. Golden's 2024 sell sheet lists U1 as standard on the new
  GC440 with "up to 50 AH" optional; PMS installs the 50 Ah.
- Swapping MU-1 SLD M for M50-12 SLD M adds 10.1 lb per battery, 20.2 lb
  per pair. Prices and specs:
  [[Battery Price Sheets (MK, Interstate)]].
- **House standard: replace every battery in the device**, as a matched
  pair ([[Mobility-Batteries]]).
- **Weight:** Golden's 222 lb assembled figure is with its standard U1s.
  With M50-12s the 2024 GC440 is about **242 lb** (wiki arithmetic: +2 ×
  ~10 lb). Use that for lift and hitch checks ([[Mobility-Device-Weights]]).


> [!important] 2024-and-newer GC440 is heavier
> Per the human: **the GC440 changed in 2024.** The biggest difference
> for PMS is that the **new model (2024–present) is heavier and needs an
> appropriate Harmar vehicle lift**. Don't reuse a lift choice made for a
> pre-2024 GC440 (the 2021 reference order on
> [[Harmar Orders and Installs Workflow]] paired a GC440 with an AL100).
> **This has already bitten PMS:** a Curb-Sider couldn't lift a 2024+
> GC440 because it was too close to the lift's weight limit; PMS and the
> VA both missed the weight change ([[VSL-6000-6900]]).
> **Get the scooter's year** (serial number under the carpet, right rear)
> and run it through Harmar's Compatibility Calculator.

**The 2024 GC440 weighs 222 lb assembled, with batteries** (Golden sell
sheet, Feb 2024). With an 83 lb AL100 that's 305 lb on the hitch, 345 lb
with an AL105 swing-away; see [[Mobility-Device-Weights]]. The pre-2024 GC440's weight isn't on
file.
The service sheets here are dated 2012–2017 (pre-change); the parts
breakdown (Nov 2024) and the VA owner's manual (2025) date from after it.

## Specs (GC240 / GC340 / GC440)

- Weight capacity: 350 lb (GC240) / 400 lb (GC340, GC440)
- Drive: rear-wheel, 90A Dynamics controller (DR90-A01)
- 3-wheel: GC240, GC340. 4-wheel: GC440
- Speed: ~5-5.4 mph; range 15-18.4 miles
- Batteries (GC440 by generation, per the human; see "GC440 batteries" below):
  pre-2024 = 2 × U1 (MK **MU-1 SLD M**); 2024+ = 2 × 50 Ah (MK **M50-12
  SLD M**). General spec: 12V/U1 ×2 (GC240) or 12V/50AH ×2 (GC340/GC440), air-travel
  safe
- Suspension: GC340/GC440 only (not GC240)
- Warranty: lifetime frame / 2yr drivetrain+electronics / 1yr
  batteries+wear parts — see [[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]] (the VA contract
  packaging of this manual carries the same terms under a "Department of
  Veterans Affairs Warranty" heading, plus an added VA-specific 2-year
  service plan)

## GC540 "Companion HD" — how it differs

Per the [[Companion and Companion HD Illustrated Parts Breakdown]], GC540
shares only the tiller/handle assembly with GC340/GC440. Everything else
is its own part family:
- Separate front chassis, rear chassis, chassis moldings, electrical
  system drawings
- Battery: one 22NF pair (vs. U1/50AH on 240/340/440)
- Controller: Dynamics Rhino 2 (DS120, 140A) vs. Dynamics 90A DR90-A01
- Seat: SN2 swivel-with-lock (vs. the standard MBH-MMSSM slide mechanism
  shared by GC221/321/421/240/340/440)
- 2" anti-tip wheels (vs. 3" on GC240)

The [[Companion HD Owners Manual (GC540)]] (Rev. A, 2023) fills in the
rest: 500 lb, 5.7 mph, 26.8 mi, 2-NF22 (37 lb each), **8 A charger**,
**120 A** controller (the IPB's Rhino 2 says 140 A: check the label),
225.5 lb assembled (base 101.5, seat 50), 22 × 20 in captain's chair,
4 seat heights, 10.75 in solid tires, no hand brake, Medicare K0808. It
uses the **same R-Series flash/beep codes** ([[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]])
and the scooter warranty with a **5-year frame** (the text says
"GR540", a typo). Its freewheel lever goes **back toward N** for
freewheel. No GC540 service guide is on file.

## GC340R "ScooterBug" Companion

A 3-wheel Companion variant with its own owner's manual
([[ScooterBug Companion Owners Manual (GC340R)]], 2019). The manual
doesn't explain the ScooterBug name.

- **Slower: 3.1 mph** (GC340: 5.1). 400 lb, 90 A, 2-U1 or 2-40 Ah,
  45.5 × 23.5 in, 34.5 in wheelbase, 41 in turning radius.
- **176 lb (U1) / 192 lb (40 Ah)** assembled; base with batteries
  104 lb, seat 31.5.
- **Front and rear 3 in anti-tippers** and a wrap-around bumper as
  standard.
- Its warranty names **the GC340R only** (lifetime frame, 2 yr drive
  train and electronics, 1 yr batteries). Same flash/beep codes.

## Key procedures

- **Seat removal/installation**: pinchless hinges standard; lift seat
  rotate lever, lift seat straight up/out. See
  [[Seat Lock Mechanism Operating Instructions (GC240, GC340, GC440)]].
- **Full disassembly** (for transport): remove seat → remove rear shroud
  → remove batteries (cut shipping zip ties first if new) → pull T-handle
  next to seat post to separate front/rear frame sections. Reassembly is
  the reverse; frame sections "snap" together via a frame hook when
  properly aligned.
- **Freewheel mode**: brake handle lever toggles engaged (braked, normal
  drive) vs. disengaged (freewheel, push by hand) — pushing too fast in
  freewheel makes the motor act as a generator and become hard to push.
- **Main circuit breaker**: 50A, resettable, under the seat on the rear
  shroud panel. Trips from low tire pressure (pneumatic tires), long
  climbs, curbs, run-down batteries, or overweight load.
- **Diagnostics**: see [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]] — a 9-code flash/beep
  scheme, distinct from the GA541 Avenger's [[TruCharge-Diagnostics]]
  10-bar display.
- **Charging**: off-board charger via a port on the tiller; charge new
  batteries 12 hours even if the gauge shows full, to condition them.
