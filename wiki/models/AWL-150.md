# AWL-150 (Bruno "Lifter")

Bruno's small **in-vehicle hoist for manual wheelchairs**, up to
**200 lb**. It lifts the chair (or a light scooter) by a belt and docking
device and stows it **upright** in the trunk or cargo area. It is the
current version of Bruno's old trunk-lift family (AWL-100/125/150/160,
VSL-175: [[Tech Tip - Trunk Lift Motor MDC-12009 Replacement]]). The
manuals were pulled from Brunopartner on 2026-10-22.

## Specs and install (2023 manual)

- 12 VDC motor on a 9–14 VDC input, direct switch, worm gearbox, belt
  lift.
- **Mounting:**
  - **Post-style trunk base:** a 15 in base plate (taller on request),
    stabilized by **two threaded rods to the trunk drip rails at
    70–80°**.
  - **Custom or adjustable sub-base** (left or right rear): 5/16 in
    grade 5 bolts with 4-hole support washers.
- **The post must be vertical**: a parked lift that swings on its own
  isn't plumb. Seal every hole.
- **Power:**
  - Twin lead through the firewall and the cab, **fuse at the battery +**.
  - A separate **10 AWG chassis ground**.
  - The lift also grounds through its mounting bolts.
  - Hybrids and EVs use the 12 V battery ([[Vehicle-Lift-Power-Wiring]]).

## Operation and service

- Chair **2 in behind the bumper**. Fold it, and don't roll it folded
  (the dock is in the spokes).
- Arm latched at 90°. **Stop before the white stitching reaches the belt
  guide.**
- **Never use it with the belt frayed more than ⅛ in.**
- Nothing resting on the switch box (it burns the motor and drains the
  battery).
- **Troubleshooting:**
  - **Switch reversed** = the belt wound backwards on the spool
    (over-extended).
  - Stalls = twisted belt, jammed worm gear, or a loose motor-shaft clip.
  - One direction only = the switch.
- **Warranty:** 3 years (vehicle lift Gold).
