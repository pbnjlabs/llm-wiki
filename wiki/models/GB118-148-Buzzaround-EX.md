# Buzzaround Extreme / EX (GB118, GB148)

Golden's larger travel scooter, marketed as "Buzzaround Extreme" ("EX" in
model-number shorthand). **GB118 is the 3-wheel variant, GB148 is the
4-wheel variant of the same platform** — confirmed by the rental bumper
kit differences (GB148's kit requires removing a front wheel and nose
piece that the GB118 kit doesn't touch). A service guide, an owner's manual and an IPB are
on file. The 375 lb, high-back-seat version is the
[[GB119-149-Buzzaround-LX]].

## Sell-sheet specs (Apr 2025)

From [[Golden Sell Sheets (2018-2026)]]: 350 lb, 5 mph, 18 mi, 2-U1 (24 lb pack). **GB118 154 lb**
assembled (45 × 22 in, 34.7 in wheelbase); **GB148 161 lb** (44 × 22 in,
33.8 in). Pieces: front half 46 (3W) / 53 lb (4W), rear half 36, seat
23. See [[Mobility-Device-Weights]].

## What's confirmed

- **Dynamics R-Series** controller. Beep/flash codes 1–9:
  [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]].
- Protection: **40 A** circuit breaker, a **100 A** fuse in the
  left-side battery pack, a **10 A** charger fuse, a 1 A throttle fuse.
  Motor 0.8–1.5 Ω, brake 45–52 Ω.

- Uses **two removable battery packs**, charged simultaneously on a
  dedicated dual-footplate charging dock (indoor use only). Charging dock
  fuse: 10A blade-style, must be replaced with exact type/rating.
- Anti-tip wheel assemblies double as tie-down mounting points via an
  add-on kit (tie-downs must angle inward toward the scooter to avoid
  damage).
- Distinct from the GB120 Carry On's single quick-release battery pack
  design — EX uses the charging-dock model instead.
