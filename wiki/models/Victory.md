# Pride Victory 9 / Victory 10 scooters

Pride's full-size Victory scooters. No owner's manual or spec sheet on
file; the documents are service ones. The older Victory (before 2002),
Rally and Shuttle used a different charger setup (see
[[Technical Troubleshooting Guide - Battery Guide (2004)]]).

## Documents

- [[Victory 9-10 Technical Troubleshooting Guide (INFMANU3986)]] (2010)
- [[Victory, Rally and Revo Repair Instructions]]: tiller fuse kit,
  Rally/Shuttle/Victory motor and transaxle
- Charger: Battery Maximizer EA24030-9C / EA1148B for the Victory 10
  ([[Pride Battery Charger Manual (2022)]])

## Service notes

- **PG S-Drive** controller: beep codes 3 high voltage, 5 brake,
  6/7 throttle and speed pot, 8 motor, 9 controller trip
  ([[S-Drive-Beep-Codes]]). Brake **30–65 Ω**; motor 0.5–1.5 Ω;
  throttle pot about 2.5 kΩ; speed pot 100 kΩ.
- **No power: check the key fuse first.** The fuse block is on the
  **back of the tiller**: 10 A (red), 5 A (tan), four 1 A (black), ATO
  type, match ratings exactly.
- **Freewheel lever:** pull **back** for drive, push **forward** for
  freewheel.
- Motor/transaxle: keep the planetary gear's lower rim flush with or
  below the motor face plate when refitting, O-ring in place.
