# VSL-4400 (Bruno Joey)

An **inside platform lift** for full-size vans, minivans and SUVs. A
platform on an **in/out rail and up/down tower** carries the scooter or
power chair out the rear opening, down to the ground, and back in. **350
lb.** The "VSL-4400G" operator manual covers the same lift. Link to
[[Bruno]].

Closest Harmar equivalent: the inside-platform hybrids
([[Hybrid-Vehicle-Lifts]], AL600/AL690).

## Specs

- **350 lb**; level ground; works down to **-25°F** (start the vehicle
  first in extreme heat or cold). Meets F/CMVSS 302.
- Ships in 4 cartons: up/down assembly, in/out assembly,
  platform/barrier, **vehicle-specific mounting kit**.
- **Built-in wheel chocks** for scooters and mid-wheel-drive chairs.
  **Front- and rear-wheel-drive chairs need extra chocks** (VSL-K-4444,
  required for FWD per the price list).
- Optional **barrier belts** (VSL-K-4446, required for full-size vans
  per the price list).
- Replacement hardware: **Grade 8 only**.

## Installing (key points)

- **Power cable:** battery → existing firewall grommet → **inside the
  vehicle** under trim to the floor pocket ~42–48 in from the rear.
  **Never under the vehicle.** In-line fuse at the battery.
- **Door safety switch is mandatory** (normally closed; magnet on the
  hatch, 1/4–3/8 in gap). The remote only works with the rear door
  open. **Without it, the lift drains the vehicle battery.** Test with a
  multimeter: continuity with the door open, none closed.
- Disable a **third-row power-folding seat** (disconnect its switch).
- Mount with the vehicle's kit; remove and save the shipping bracket.
  Up/down assembly bolts **23 ft-lb** with threadlocker; platform nuts
  **30 ft-lb**; **barrier must always be installed** (23 ft-lb).
- Platform angle is factory-set: adjust only if the in/out motor stalls
  under a load under 350 lb.
- **Set the platform chocks** for the customer's device (move the two
  groups of 5 screws); keep the scooter's battery weight centered.
- **Completion checklist:** mast light goes off when fully in; unloaded
  platform lifts no more than **0.5 in** when stowed; remote light goes
  out with the door closed; platform pin seats in the rail hole.

## Operating

Two buttons. **OUT**: travels out, then down; **release when the
platform is flat on the ground**, or the belt keeps unwinding and
reverses up/down. Chair on, power off (belts across it if fitted).
**IN**: up, then in; it stops by itself and the **mast light goes off**.
Keep the remote cord clear of the hatch. The bumper may dip slightly
with a load; that's normal. Engine may run (in park).

**Emergency:** backup crank from the operator manual bag. Remove the
black caps; crank **motor #1 clockwise = up**, then **motor #2 clockwise
= in**. Repair before further use.

## Troubleshooting highlights

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Won't run | Loose +/− power; check remote backlight and mast light |
| Slow or stalls | Weak/dead battery, overload, uneven ground, extreme temperature (start the engine) |
| Moves the wrong direction, or stops at end of travel | **Reset the board: pass the back of the remote over the door safety switch** (its magnet resets it) |
| Mast light stays on after going in | Platform not fully in: check alignment and the pin |
| Stowed platform lifts more than 0.5 in | Pin alignment |
| Goes out but not down; or out, down 2–3 in, stops | OUT limit switch; jammed UP limit switch |

**Maintenance:** dealer inspection yearly, every 6 months with heavy
use.
