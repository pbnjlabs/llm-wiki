# K2 (Savaria straight-rail stairlift)

Savaria Concord Lifts' **K2** battery-powered **straight-rail** stairlift
(aluminum rail with a steel gear rack). **300 lb (136 kg).** ASME A18.1
public (§4) and private (§7). The commercial/Canadian version is the
[[K2-Plus]]. Savaria Technical Support **800-791-7999**; troubleshooting
guide on the Savaria dealer website.

## Key specs and settings

- **Rail:** cut length = nose-to-floor + top extension (chart by stair angle
  and seat height); **minimum 30 in** (50 in with flip-up rail); gear rack
  always on the **left** viewed from the bottom; brackets **≤ 40 in apart**,
  six sets per 16 ft, both sides of a splice; rail **1/2–3/4 in off the stair
  noses**; **1 in minimum** from the wall to the chair back; wall or barrier
  behind any exposed rail.
- **Torques:** splice screws **40 in-lb**; rail bracket cap nuts **10
  ft-lb**; rack bolts **50 in-lb**; seat post levelling bolts **40 ft-lb
  (55 Nm)**, don't overtighten.
- **Power:** two **12 V 9 Ah** batteries in series (24 V; C2190459); Delta
  4 A charger; charge at the landings by strips (bottom 80 mm, top 60 mm from
  the limits) or along the rail by **full bus line**. Red (positive) always
  on the rack side. **+33 V DC** on the rail; 1–1.2 A charging falling to
  0.4 A when full.
- **Main board LUT125 DIP switches:** 1 reverse remote buttons, 2 brake
  installed, 3 beep when running, **4 soft start/stop at the charge cams (OFF
  for full bus line)**, 5 no charger warning/sleep, 6 silent. Cycle power
  after changing.
- **LEDs (carriage front):** all on = ready; middle + right = LH obstruction;
  middle + left = RH obstruction; middle only = both; **all off = safety
  circuit fault**. Board: green charging, yellow sleep, red safety circuit
  open. All safety LEDs flashing at 11.5 V = sleep.
- **Remotes:** hold the board's program button, press the remote; up to 4
  units in one area (change remote DIP switches). Outdoor units: LUT420 V2
  hall call receiver.
- **Overspeed:** brake dog engages the rack. Check it isn't tripped before
  fitting the carriage. Trip test: flip the dog up, run down, crank back,
  press it down. Manual lowering with the handwheel on the motor shaft (LH:
  clockwise = up).
- **Fuses:** 20 A AGC by the main PCB, 30 A mini blade in the battery
  ground.

## Troubleshooting (K2 guide)

| Symptom | Check |
|---|---|
| Won't run, all carriage LEDs off | Safety circuit: power, **overspeed tripped**, final limits, seat swivel switch; jumper kit C6290223 to isolate |
| Won't run one way | Obstruction on that side (footrest, underpan/safety pan, obstruction switch); normal at that landing; seat switch wire (down terminal 15, up 17) |
| No power, no board LEDs | Main switch on top of the carriage; batteries in series, 12 V each off the unit; battery leads |
| Remotes reversed | DIP 1 |
| Doesn't slow at landings | DIP 4 ON |
| Slow on bus line/strips | DIP 4 OFF |
| Slow, not charging | Low batteries |
| Seat play | Support shims (washers) on the swivel bolt |
| Swivel switch stuck on | Plastic shim on the switch pin (bulletin 001153) |
| Carriage rocks front to back | Shim the vertical rollers (kit C6290268) |

## Owner's manual notes

From [[K2 Stairlift Owner's Manual (001022)]] (2016):
- **Beeps about 20 s while parked = not charging** (cord, or not sitting
  on a charge contact; continuous beeping off a contact). **Runs to an
  end, stops, won't restart = low battery.**
- Don't switch the main power off after each ride; it stops charging.
- Owner manual lowering: power off, pry the access cover off the lower
  side, handwheel on the motor shaft under the battery tray (LH:
  clockwise = up, RH: clockwise = down), leave the power off, call for
  service.
- Its spec table adds **"350 lb up to 45°"**; the newer install guide
  says **300 lb** only. Go by the 300 lb label.
- It sets owner checks every **3 months** (monthly in adverse
  environments) and all technician items every 6 months, stricter than
  the install guide below.

## Maintenance

Owner every 6 months: swivel switch, sensors, e-stop, belt, hinges.
Technician every 6 months (limits) and yearly (overspeed, roller lube with
**Super Lube or wet graphite grease**, stops, brackets, rack, footrest,
wiring, noise, motor temperature); every 6 months for heavy or outdoor use.
**Replace the angle rollers every 3 years.** Seat swivel: wheel bearing
grease. **Outdoor units:** clean and lubricate every 6 months (3 in extreme
environments) to keep the warranty: Nu Finish wax on paint, Fluid Film on
hardware and switches, dielectric grease on the charging strips. See
[[Stairlift-Maintenance]].

## Options

Manual and power folding footrest, footrest extension (500265), L-seat
(reversible) and V-seat, seat height change (seat post swap), 5-point
harness (107326 / 107326-B), flip-up and power folding rail, full bus line
(16 ft C6290187, 24 ft C6290201), Wifi board (not on power units), Savaria
Link, hand change (bulletin 001109).
