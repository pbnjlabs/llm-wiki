# J6

Mid-wheel-drive power wheelchair from Pride Mobility (Quantum Rehab
division). Two contract variants share identical base hardware but are
governed differently:

| Variant | Max speed | Range |
|---|---|---|
| J6 (standard), 2015 sheet | 4 mph | 15 mi |
| J6 (standard), **2020 sheet (newest)** | 4 mph | **13.4 mi** |
| J6 VA, 2015 sheet | 4.7 mph | 10 mi |

Both: 300 lb weight capacity (**250 lb with TRU-Balance 3**, per the
2020 sheet and the 2025 Quantum chart), 22 in turning radius, two-motor
mid-wheel 6-drive, "Intelligent Braking" electronic regenerative + disc
park brake, 10 in drive wheels (pneumatic or solid), 5 in front / 6 in
rear solid casters, Active-Trac Extreme (ATX) suspension, two 12V U-1
batteries (AGM, Gel-Cell or Hybrid Gel) with 5-amp off-board charger,
max incline 6°, max obstacle climb 2.4 in. Class of use A.

Component weights (2020 sheet): base **100.2 lb** without seat and
batteries, seat 55.4 lb, batteries 23.4 lb each.

The 2025 [[Quantum End-to-End Chart (INFINFB4546, 2025)]] still lists
the J6: Group 2 (K0822/K0823, K0835), FSS V797D-70001, 2-pole motors.

## Controller

The 2025 Quantum chart lists the J6 with **70 A Q-Logic NE or NE+**
electronics (icon-based fault display; no NE/NE+ manual is on file, only
the [[NE+ Firmware Compatibility List (2009)]]). Older builds may have a
Q-Logic or Q-Logic 3 hand control, so check the chair. See
[[Joystick-Controllers]] for operation and programming,
[[Q-Logic-Fault-Codes]] for numbered codes, and
[[Q6 Edge Basic Troubleshooting]] for a symptom-based troubleshooting
checklist that applies broadly across Q-Logic-equipped Pride mid-wheel
chairs (written for the Q6 Edge family but not chair-specific in its
content).

## Seating

Standard seat is fixed (manual recline, seat height via trapeze bars);
compatible with the optional [[Tru-Balance-3-Power-Positioning|TRU-Balance 3]] tilt/recline/elevate seating system for power positioning — the J6 OM
explicitly defers joystick and TRU-Balance-3 operation to their own
manuals rather than duplicating it.

## Known quirks/gotchas

- The J6/J6 VA speed-vs-range split is a **software/governing** difference
  between contract variants, not a hardware difference — don't assume a
  faster J6 has different motors or batteries.
- The J6 OM (Rev G/2018) predates the more detailed disinfectant-product
  cleaning guidance found in the newer
  [[Q6 Edge 3 Stretto Operator Manual|Q6 Edge 3 Stretto OM]] (Rev C/2022) —
  if a tech needs a specific disinfectant validated for a J6, the newer
  Q6 Edge 3 Stretto manual's list is the more current Pride guidance even
  though it's a different model's manual.
