# Falcon

Same manufacturer, manual template, and core architecture as
[[Air-Hawk-Eagle-HD]] (Nanjing Jin Bai He Medical Apparatus Co., Ltd.),
distributed by Discover Your Mobility Inc. (USA) — but a genuinely
different variant, not just a rename:

## Differences from Air Hawk / Eagle HD
- **180 kg max weight capacity** (vs. 160 kg) — the actual "heavy duty"
  differentiator in this lineup, despite Eagle HD (not Falcon) carrying
  the "HD" suffix
- **Reclining backrest** — a spring-handle mechanism (pull up the spring
  handle, push down the backrest tube under force, release at the
  desired angle) not present in the Air Hawk/Eagle HD table of contents
  or procedures at all
- Folded/unfolded dimensions and all other technical parameters
  (motor, battery, wheels, speed, range) are otherwise **identical** to
  Air Hawk/Eagle HD — same 250W×2 motors, same 6Ah×2 lithium batteries,
  same 6km/h max speed — suggesting a shared base chassis with a
  reinforced/re-rated frame and an added backrest option
- Folding/unfolding, battery, and troubleshooting sections are otherwise
  word-for-word the same content family as Air Hawk/Eagle HD

## Warranty
Identical table and terms to [[Air-Hawk-Eagle-HD]] (main frame 3yr;
motor wheel reducer/PU tires 1yr, other parts 2yr; battery 6mo;
controller 1yr; joystick 1yr).
