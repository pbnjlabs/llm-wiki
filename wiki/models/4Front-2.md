# 4Front 2 / 4Front 2 HD

Quantum front-wheel-drive chairs with rear casters and front anti-tips.
Spec sheets on file for both.

| | 4Front 2 ([[Quantum 4Front 2 Specifications (INFINFB4329)]]) | 4Front 2 HD ([[Quantum 4Front 2 HD Specifications (INFINFB4335)]]) |
|---|---|---|
| Rev | A, Apr 2021 | A, Aug 2021 |
| Class | B (Group 3) | B (Group 3) |
| Capacity | 300 lb | **450 lb**; 400 lb with 10 in lift |
| Max speed | 6.25 mph; 3.5 mph with 10 in lift; 4 mph UK | 5 mph; **2.7 mph with lift**; 4 mph UK |
| Range | 16.34 mi (Group 24) / 17.4 mi (Group 34) | 15.9 mi (Group 24) |
| Ground clearance (occupied) | 1.9 in anti-tips, 2.6 in motor pivot, 3.14 in battery tray | 1.9 / 2.6 / 3.15 in |
| Turning radius | 25.9 in | 24.5 in (2025 chart: 25.9 in) |
| Length | 45.2 in with / 39.9 in without front riggings | 45.9 / 39.7 in |
| Width | 24.2 in | 24.3 in |
| Wheels | 14 in flat-free drive, 9 in flat-free casters, 4 in flat-free anti-tips | same |
| Suspension | SRS | SRS |
| Base weight | 130 lb | 140 lb (2025 chart: 130 lb) |
| Batteries | Group 24 73 Ah, 51.5 lb (std) or Group 34 60 Ah, 44.8 lb | Group 24 73 Ah, 51.5 lb |
| Charger | 8 A off-board | 8 A off-board |
| Max slope / climb | 7.5° / 3.34 in | 7.5° / 2.95 in |
| Electronics (2025 chart) | 75 A QLNES or 120 A Q-Logic 3 | 120 A Q-Logic 3 |
| HCPCS | Group 3 std/single/multiple power | Group 3 HD (K0850, K0858, K0862) |

Both: two-motor front-wheel drive, "Intelligent Braking" regenerative +
disc park brake, 4-pole motors, FSS V797D-70001. Seat-to-floor heights
are about 0.25 in lower loaded because of the suspension.

**Chart vs sheet:** the 2025 chart gives the HD a 25.9 in turning radius
and 130 lb base; its own 2021 sheet says 24.5 in and 140 lb. The sheet
is the model-specific document.
