# Jazzy Air / Jazzy Air 2 (elevating seat)

Pride's Jazzy with a **power elevating seat** that can be driven
raised. The **Jazzy Air Med** is DME-coded (K0830/31, seat elevator);
the **Jazzy Air 2** is retail (K0899) and adds under-seat storage.

| | Jazzy Air Med (2026 Group 2 chart) | Jazzy Air 2 (2026 retail chart) |
|---|---|---|
| Capacity | 300 lb | 300 lb |
| Max speed | 3.7 mph (same elevated) | 4 mph (same elevated) |
| Batteries | 2 × U-1 (22.2 lb each) | 2 × U-1, 40 Ah optional |
| Charger | 5 A | 5 A |
| Controller | 40 A PG **VR2** | 40 A PG VR2 |
| Weight | | 202 lb with seat |

(From [[Pride-Power-Chair-Lineup]].)

## Notes from the manual

- Lift actuator by **REAC**; duty cycle **6 min on / 54 min off**.
  Don't run it continuously when testing.
- Speed is reduced while elevated (the spec insert gives the figure).
