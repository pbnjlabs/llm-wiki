# Rogue Series (rigid ultralight)

Ki Mobility's **rigid** ultralight chairs.

| Model | Capacity | Transit | Weight (as tested) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Rogue** | 275 lb (125 kg) | yes, 275 lb | 12 kg (26 lb) | Original; 2023 manual |
| **Rogue 2** | 275 lb (125 kg) | yes, 275 lb | 13.6 kg (30 lb) | Index towers and camber tube |
| **Rogue XP** | 200 lb (91 kg) | yes, 200 lb | | Growable youth version: 5th wheel, pediatric arms, step tube, grows in width |
| **Rogue DT** | 300 lb (136 kg) | yes, 300 lb | 13.6 kg (30 lb) | Newest (2026); hub locks, Frog Legs caster option |
| **Rogue ALX** | 250 lb (113 kg); HD 350 lb | **125 lb only** | 11 kg (24 lb) | Aluminum; camber mount assembly |

## Service highlights (tech manuals)

- **Tower clamp bolts 70 in-lb, tightened in sequence 1-2-3-4** (Rogue 2/XP); lower nut and four bolts 50 in-lb; set screws 40 in-lb; Rogue DT values 30–100 in-lb and one nut 35 ft-lb; ALX retaining screws 80 in-lb, tightened in a balanced way.
- Procedures: caster angle and squaring, camber tube and towers, center of gravity, seat height, **toe to zero**, wheelbase width, reversing wheel locks, one-arm drive, 5th wheel (XP), transit install.
- Default torque (user manuals): 40 in-lb.
