# VS Seat System Owner's Manual (205216)

44-page US owner's manual (Ed 7, 2014-12, order no. 205216-US-0) for
Permobil's older **VS** standing seat system (Junior and Adult), on ICS
electronics. Covers design, operation, adjustments (arm rest, chest
support, head rest, trunk and lumbar support, footplate, knee support,
**angle gauge**, positioning belt, control panel), care and transport.
Its successor on current chairs is the Corpus VS
([[Permobil-F-Corpus]], [[Permobil-M-Corpus]]).

| | Junior | Adult |
|---|---|---|
| Seat height (power lift) | 21–31 in | 21–31 in |
| Seat width | 17 / 19 in | 17 / 19 in |
| Seat depth | 16–20 in | 18–24 in (18–20 without the extension) |
| Back and leg rest angle | 90–180° | 90–180° |
| Max user weight | **170 lb** | **220 lb, or 265 lb with seating limitations** |

- Permobil requires standing chairs to be **delivered and fitted only with
  trained healthcare professionals present** (blood pressure swings and
  other risks).
- Support wheels: on standing seats they sit either side of the leg
  rest; on non-standing seats, on the rear edge of the footplate.
- The self-adhesive **angle gauge** on the seat frame tracks a user's
  progress toward full standing.
- ICS calibration lengths for the VS actuators:
  [[ICS Onboard Configuration Technical Manual (205247, Ed 8)]].
