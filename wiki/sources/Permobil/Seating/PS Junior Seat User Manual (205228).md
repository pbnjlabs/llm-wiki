# PS Junior Seat User Manual (205228)

44-page US user manual (edition 7, updated 2014, order no. 205228-US-0)
for the **PS Junior** pediatric seat on the **Permobil K300**. Covers seat
functions, the control panel, adjustments (back angle, seat width and
depth, arm rests, leg rests, footplate, seat angle, positioning belt)
and care. Feeds [[Permobil-K300-C300]].

- **Max user weight on the K300: 165 lb**, but it **depends on seat
  depth**: 10 in 66 lb, 11 in 77, 12 in 88, 13 in 99, 14 in 121, 15 in
  143, 16 in 154, **17 in 165 lb**. Depth is set by three nuts each side.
- Arm rest pad and arm rest angle screws **10 Nm**; check the nuts sit
  in the UniTrack rail.
- Size (17 in depth, 16 in width, 90° back, 70° leg rest): 39 in long,
  22 in wide, 17–23 in tall (seat pan to push handles), max leg rest
  length 14 in; seat about 58 lb.
