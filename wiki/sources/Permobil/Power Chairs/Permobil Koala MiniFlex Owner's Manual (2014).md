# Permobil Koala MiniFlex Owner's Manual (2014)

150-page US owner's manual (Ed 2, 2014-08, order no. 205339-US-0) for the
**Koala MiniFlex**, a small **front-wheel-drive** power chair for
children. Feeds [[Permobil-Koala-MiniFlex]].

## Specs

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Max user weight | **35 kg (77 lb)** |
| Weight with batteries | 94 kg |
| Size L × W × H | 965 × 585 × 750 mm; transport 740 × 585 × 680 mm |
| Seat depth / width | 250–350 mm each; seat height 340–590 mm; back 80–145° |
| Tires | drive 2.50 × 8 in, pivot 200 × 50 mm; **200 kPa (2 bar)** (drive wheels 200–250 kPa in the maintenance text) |
| Range / speed | 25–30 km / 6.5 km/h; braking distance 1.1 m |
| Slopes | climb and safe **6°**; static 16° down, 19° up and side |
| Obstacle / clearance | 60 mm / 65 mm; turning radius 570 mm |
| Electronics | R-net CJSM-sw / PM120 |
| Batteries | **2 × 40 Ah gel**, 12 V, 8 h; 14.4 kg each |
| Main fuse | **63 A** automatic breaker, lever at the back of the chassis (no need to remove the cover) |

## Service content

- **Tool bag:** Allen keys, 11 and 13 mm spanners, seat lift crank,
  security key.
- **Drive wheel tube:** wheel off (4 screws), split the rim (6 screws),
  rim and wheel screws **24 Nm**, 200 kPa.
- **Battery replacement:** seat up to max, panel off, loosen the battery
  cover and **disconnect the crush protection cable**, loosen the
  terminals, remove the **spacer plate**, note battery positions, lift
  out; reverse, and **reconnect the crush protection** before closing.
- Charging socket on the right of the chassis; batteries under the centre
  cover.
- Breaker, brake release and R-net diagnostics as on the other Permobil
  chairs ([[R-net-Diagnostics]]).
