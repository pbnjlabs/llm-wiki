# Permobil M400 Corpus HD User Manual (337723)

206-page user manual (Ed 6, 2020-10-23, order no. 337723 eng-CAN) for the
**M400 Corpus HD**, Permobil's heavy-duty **mid-wheel-drive** chair. The
older, longer template: it includes **battery replacement**, inner tubes,
casters, the breaker and **R-net diagnostics**. Feeds
[[Permobil-M400-Corpus-HD]].

## Battery replacement (5.4)

Gloves and goggles. Level floor, leg rest out, **seat lift up**, control
panel off, **main breaker OFF**. Remove the four knobs, then the front,
upper and rear chassis covers. **Pull the locking handles outward to
release the electronics** (tabs both sides). Remove the rear seat support
(2 screws). Loosen the terminals (diagram on the sticker inside the
front cover), lift the batteries out by their straps. Reverse to fit.

## Wheels

- **Drive wheel inner tube:** wheel off (4 screws), deflate, split the
  rim (6 screws), new tube, rim screws **24 Nm (18 lb-ft)**, inflate to
  200 kPa, wheel screws **24 Nm**.
- **Caster tube:** rim halves held by 3 Allen screws; don't pinch the
  tube.

## Breaker, brakes, storage

- A tripped main breaker "often indicates a major electrical fault":
  find the cause before resetting it to ON. Turn the control panel off
  first.
- Brake release: check monthly; chair must not drive when released.
- Storage: at least 5 °C; long-term, charge fully, breaker off, **charge
  every 6 months** (the newer Corpus manuals say monthly).

## Troubleshooting (6)

Six-row symptom table (won't start, won't drive, switches off after
1–30 min, stops, slow, won't charge: charging breaker resets in 5 min).
**R-net LCD:** module (PM/JSM), trip text, 4-digit trip code. **R-net
LED:** the 1–10 LED table matches [[R-net LED Error Codes]], except
**6 LEDs = "battery charger connected"** (PGDT's sheet: drive inhibited by
an external signal, which a charger is). **7 + 5 LEDs** = communication
error; actuator indicator = actuator error. Apart from approved spare
parts there are **no serviceable parts in R-net modules**; send them to
Permobil. Detail on [[R-net-Diagnostics]].
