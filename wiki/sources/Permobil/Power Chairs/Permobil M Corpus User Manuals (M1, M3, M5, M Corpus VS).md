**Model version 2 vs 3:** the specs and procedures are identical. Version
2 calls its connectivity **ConnectMe**; version 3 has a **Power Platform**
(radio transmitter, with a flight mode). The rest is copy editing. The
model version is printed on the serial number label (lower left of the
chassis, and on the seat tilt).

## What's in them

User-level only: overview and specs, tool bag, safety warnings, driving,
seat functions (lift, tilt, anterior tilt, backrest tilt, leg rest tilt,
footplate lift; the M1 has only tilt and leg rest), charging, freewheel,
vehicle and air transport, main circuit breaker, storage, manual
adjustments, a maintenance schedule, cleaning, tire pressure and a
six-row troubleshooting table. **No service procedures, battery
replacement or wiring.** Control panel details are in separate manuals at
documentation.permobil.com/manuals/control_panels/.

## Shared facts

- **Tool bag:** 11 and 13 mm wrenches, 2–6 mm Allen keys, 15 mm socket.
- **Drive tires 250 kPa (35 psi)**, check weekly. Casters are solid
  polyurethane.
- **Main circuit breaker:** lever at the back. **Reset it once yourself;
  if it trips again, call service.** Turn off the control panel first.
  The charging circuit breaker resets itself after 5 minutes.
- **Freewheel:** two levers, out = freewheel, in = drive. Must not be
  drivable in freewheel; if it is, it needs service.
- **Charging:** charger socket on the front of the joystick; plug into
  the chair first, then the wall. No driving while charging.
- **Energy saving:** switches off after 20–30 minutes idle.
- **Transport:** four tie-downs (yellow labels), ISO 10542-1 straps, or a
  **Dahl MK II / VarioDock** dock (max user 136 kg); lock plate install:
  refs.permobil.com/IFU/332849. Forward-facing only, vehicle 3-point belt.
- **Storage:** at least 5 °C (41 °F); for long storage charge fully,
  turn off the main breaker, charge monthly.
- **Torques:** parallel panel holder **9.8 Nm (7.2 lb-ft)**, with at
  least 10 mm (0.4 in) gap to the armrest. VS chest support depth **24 Nm
  (17.7 lb-ft)**.
- **Service life five years.** Driving range about 25 km (16 mi) at
  25 °C; batteries are harmed below −25 °C.

## Model-only content

- **M1:** swing-away and power leg rests, fixed panel holder, more
  torques: backrest tilt, armrest height and angle, armrest pad, leg rest
  angle, footplate height all **9.8 Nm**; swing-away footplate angle
  **24 Nm (17.7 lb-ft)**, its height **7.4 Nm (5.5 lb-ft)**; power leg
  rest web belt gap **2–10 mm**, belt bracket screws **2.9 Nm**; control
  panel angle screws **2.9 Nm (2.1 lb-ft)**. Also control panel cleaning
  and corrosion protection.
- **M Corpus VS:** **standing function** (level ground only, speed cut
  when standing), chest support and knee support **always** used to
  stand, contraindications (low bone density, poor circulation).
