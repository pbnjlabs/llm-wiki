# Permobil K/C300 Owner's Manual (205220)

103-page US owner's manual (version 8, 2014-05, art. no. 205220-US-0)
for the legacy **K300 / C300** front-wheel-drive chair (K300 with the
pediatric **PS Junior** seat, see [[PS Junior Seat User Manual (205228)]];
C300 with a Corpus seat). VR2 or R-net electronics. Feeds
[[Permobil-K300-C300]].

## Specs (K/C300 chassis with CS seat)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Size L × W × H | 42.5 × 24.25 × 42.5 in; transport 34.5 × 24.25 × 30.5 in |
| Weight with batteries and seat | 260 lb |
| Tires | front (drive) 3.00 × 8, rear 210 × 65; **29 psi (200 kPa)** |
| Range / speed | 16 mi / 5 mph (about 2.5 mph reverse) |
| Slopes | climb 10° up and down, **safe 6°**, side 6° |
| Obstacle / turning radius | 2.4 in / 25 in |
| Electronics | **VR2:** JSM/JSM-L, PM70/PM70-A2. **R-net:** JSM-L-SV, PM90 |
| Batteries | group 34 gel, 60 Ah, 8 h charge |
| Main fuse | **50 A blade fuse, replaced if blown** (not a resettable breaker) |

## Service content

- **Main fuse location:** under the front battery cover, on top of the
  front battery.
- **Support wheels** must always be fitted when driving (tip risk).
- **Brake release:** check monthly; it must not drive when released.
- **Front tire tube:** chock, deflate, tire off the rim, new tube.
- **Front battery:** panel off, two knobs off the chassis cover, lift it
  a little at the front, lift the front battery cover up and forward,
  disconnect (sticker inside the cover), strap out, **leave the strap on
  the new battery**. **Rear battery:** lift the chassis cover at the
  rear, pull the rear battery cover's guide lips straight out on both
  sides, lift it up and back, disconnect, strap out.
- **Cleaning:** never hose the chair; don't rinse the control panel.
- **Diagnostics:** VR2 and R-net LED/LCD tables (see
  [[R-net-Diagnostics]], [[PG-Drives-Trip-Codes]]).
