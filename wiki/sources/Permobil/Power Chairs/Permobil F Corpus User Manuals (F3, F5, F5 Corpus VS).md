## F5 Corpus VS only

- **Standing function:** level ground only, moving or not; max speed cut
  automatically when standing (turtle on the display).
- **Support wheels:** label says never remove them.
- **Chest support** must be used to stand: height 3 mm Allen (keep the
  fixing visible at the lower edge of the sleeve), depth 4 mm Allen,
  **24 Nm (17.7 lb-ft)**. **Knee support** must be used to stand, below
  the kneecap; adjusting it is a technician job.
- **Contraindications:** low bone density, poor circulation (standing
  only under medical supervision).
- **Seat depth rule:** users **over 110 kg (240 lb) need a seat depth of
  445 mm (18 in) or more**; if a user's weight goes over that on a
  shallower seat, it needs a service call.
- FCC and Industry Canada radio statements.
