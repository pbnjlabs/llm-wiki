# Permobil M300 Owner's Manual (205255)

111-page US owner's manual (Ed 4, 2014-04, item 205255-US-0) for the
legacy **M300** mid-wheel-drive chair with the **Corpus 3G** seat, sold
with either **VR2** or **R-net** electronics. Feeds [[Permobil-M300]].

## Specs (M300 chassis with Corpus 3G)

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Size L × W × H | 41 × 24 × 46 in; transport 31 × 24 × 32 in |
| Weight with batteries and seat | 345 lb |
| Max battery size | 10.2 × 6.6 × 7 in (group 34) |
| Tires | drive 3.00-8, casters 200 × 50, **29 psi (200 kPa)** |
| Range (R-net) | 13 mi |
| Speed | 6 mph |
| Slopes | climb 9°, safe 8°, side 9°; static 16° down, 10.5° up, 14.5° side |

The max user weight is in the safety text by reference only; it isn't in
the spec table on file.

## Service content

- **Tool kit:** Allen keys, **10-11 and 12-13 mm spanners**, seat lift
  crank, 2 screwdrivers.
- **Battery change** (front, then rear): level floor, leg support out,
  seat lift up, panel off, **breaker OFF (through a hole in the chassis
  cover)**, front cover off; front battery: loosen the battery locks and
  terminals (sticker inside the front cover), strap out. Rear battery:
  top cover (2 knobs), rear cover back and up, **pull the release lips
  both sides to remove the electronics**, battery locks, terminals
  (sticker inside the rear cover), strap out.
- **Magnetic wheel locks:** a release lever on each drive wheel at the
  front; out = free, in = locked. Power off first; check the lever goes
  fully in.
- **Breaker:** automatic, reset by switching ON; find the cause first.
- **Diagnostics:** VR2 LED flash table (1 LED low battery … 6 LED
  charger connected …) and R-net LCD/LED, same meanings as
  [[R-net-Diagnostics]]. The VR2 table says "check the connection to the
  **right** drive motor" for the **left**-motor open circuit (2 LEDs), a
  misprint.
