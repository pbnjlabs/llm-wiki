# Permobil K450 Owner's Manual (205230, 2020)

114-page US owner's manual (06-2020, order no. 205230-US-0) for the
**K450** pediatric power chair with the **MX seat** (seat lift,
seat-to-floor, optional tilt, ICS switchbox). Prescription-only. Feeds
[[Permobil-K450]].

## Seat functions

- **Seat-to-floor:** a support wheel at the back of the footplate lets
  the leg rest glide forward as the seat lowers. **No driving at floor
  level.** The widest (16 in / 400 mm) seat won't go all the way down.
- **Crush protection** strips along the seat sides: stop, reverse 1.5 s,
  stop.
- Max speed drops whenever the seat lift is above its lowest position.

## Service content

- **Battery replacement** (one battery each side): panel off, **main fuse
  OFF**, upper side cover (knob), lower side cover (knob), strap the
  battery out, connect the new one (sticker inside the lower cover),
  slide it in, covers on, **fuse ON**, repeat on the other side.
- **Emergency operation cable** (supplied): moves a seat function when
  it fails for any reason other than flat batteries or a bad adjustment
  device. Plug one end into the **charging socket**; remove the rear
  bumper (2 Allen) and the back cover (2 Allen) to reach the **seat
  control modules** (2 or 3, depending on tilt); unplug the function's
  cable from its module and plug it into the emergency cable; use the
  lever. **All safety functions are bypassed:** crush risk, never drive
  with it connected.
- R-net diagnostics: [[R-net-Diagnostics]]. ICS settings (transport mode,
  seat width, actuator calibration): [[ICS Onboard Configuration Technical Manual (205247, Ed 8)]].
