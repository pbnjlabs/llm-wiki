# TiLite Parts Manual (2023, Sections 1-12)

The TiLite parts manual as **12 separate section PDFs** (PD17090–PD18158,
revisions 2024–2025), covering the ZR, TR, ZRA, TRA, Aero Z, Aero T, Twist,
2GX, Aero X and TX. **The Pilot and CR1 have their own parts manuals.** The
download held up to five identical copies of each section in "Files (2)"–"(6)"
folders; one of each was kept.

## Covers

1. **Policies and procedures:** order by part number through Customer Experience
   (**800-736-0925 option 2**); custom frames need TiLite's original specs; returns
   need an **RMA number** on the box; FOB factory shipping.
2. **Frames and footrests** (77 pp.): ZR and TR series 2–3 frames, ZRA and TRA,
   Aero Z, Aero T, Twist, depth-adjustable frames.
3. **Backrests:** hinge assemblies with and without armrests for each frame.
4. **Armrests and side guards.**
5. **Upholstery:** tension-adjustable seat rails and slings, solid seat pans,
   back upholstery.
6. **Axles and axle plates:** aluminum and titanium adjustable and zero-play
   camber tubes, Twist camber tube, axle assemblies per model, amputee and
   depth-adjustable axles, frog leg suspension.
7. **Wheel locks:** standard, offset and extended clamps; MBL composite scissor;
   compact push and pull to lock; Newton grade aid; MBL Lock-A.
8. **Caster wheels and forks:** standard, Precision Lock, SlipStream and Glide
   forks; **adhesive and thread locker key**.
9. **Rear wheels and handrims:** TiLite Shadow, Spinergy SPOX, LX, SLX and LXC,
   X-Core, spokes, quick-release and quad axles.
10. **Accessories:** tip assist, impact guards, luggage carriers, crutch holders,
    tray, bags, spoke guards, seat belts (Bodypoint).
11. **Anti-tips** per frame, incl. amputee axle plate and frog leg.
12. **Transit and transport tie-downs:** transport and transit brackets (with
    seatbelt bracket) per frame and armrest type.
