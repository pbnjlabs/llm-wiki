# TiLite Folding Wheelchair Service Manual (PD16521 Rev 6)

48-page Permobil service manual for the TiLite folding chairs **X and 2GX**
(PD16521 Rev 6). A duplicate " (1)" copy was skipped. Same general rules as the
rigid manual (no water-based lube near bearings, thread-patch fasteners reused
twice at most, Loctite 242).

## Covers

- Footrests, back supports (fold down: lift both latches, push the canes
  forward; when changing the angle **the locking-block grooves must line up with
  the pivot bracket**), armrests, side guards, upholstery.
- **Axle plates and sleeves:** centre of gravity, rear seat height, wheel
  spacing, camber. Axle sleeve **22 Nm** (and 26 Nm in one procedure);
  M8 Allen screw **15 Nm**; footplate bolts **15 Nm** (don't exceed 15 Nm on
  that assembly); hex bolts **40 Nm**, tightened alternately; outer screw
  **7 Nm**; flip-back hinge and back cane bolts **5 Nm**.
- Axles, wheel locks (5 mm embed rule), casters and forks, rear wheels,
  anti-tips.
- **Transportation:** the **X** with the Transit Tie-Down Option is crash-tested
  (ISO 7176-19:2022) as a forward-facing seat. **The 2GX is not**, and must not
  be occupied in transit. The Transport option is for an unoccupied chair only.
