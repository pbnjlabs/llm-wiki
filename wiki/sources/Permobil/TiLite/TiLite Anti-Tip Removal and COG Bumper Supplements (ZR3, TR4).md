# TiLite Anti-Tip Removal and COG Bumper Supplements (ZR3, TR4)

Two one-page Permobil supplements (PD27509 ZR3, PD27510 TR4; Rev 1, 2026-06)
for **removing the anti-tip assembly and fitting COG bumpers**. Dealer or
qualified technician only: removing anti-tips reduces stability.

## Covers

- Kits **905016** (ZR3) and **905014** (TR4). Tools: 4 and 5 mm Allen (TR4 also
  a 10 mm wrench), ruler.
- Wheels off, frame level; loosen the anti-tip receiver clamp bolts; remove the
  COG mount bolts to free the anti-tip adaptor; slide the receivers off along the
  camber tube; fit the kit's **shorter COG mount bolts**; check both COG clamps
  are the same distance from the frame end (TR4); swap a camber clamp bolt for
  the COG bumper each side; slide the camber tube out to remove the anti-tip parts
  and back with **equal overhang both sides**; **camber plug flats perpendicular
  to the ground**; tighten; wheels on; **re-check toe-in/toe-out**.
