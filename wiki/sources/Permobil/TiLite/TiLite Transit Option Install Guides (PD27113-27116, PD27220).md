# TiLite Transit Option Install Guides (PD27113-27116, PD27220)

Five one- and two-page Permobil sheets (Rev 1, 2026-05-05) for fitting the
TiLite **transit tie-down brackets**. Only an authorized Permobil dealer or
qualified technician may reconfigure a chair for transit.

## Covers

- **Rigid** (PD27116 swing-away armrests, PD27115 no swing-away, PD27114 and
  PD27113 depth-adjustable frames): front transit bracket halves clamp around
  the caster arms, bolts **12 Nm**, clearance from bracket to frame **8–14 mm**;
  rear transit bracket on the inboard side, loop down, through the backrest
  hinge, frame, seatbelt bracket and armrest receiver, **18.2 Nm**; transit hook
  decals front and rear, notification decals by the backrest hinge, warning decal
  under the rigidizer.
- **Folding** (PD27220, transit or transport): split front brackets **4 Nm**;
  60° tie-down anchor mounts on the top two rear frame holes **7 Nm** (different
  screws with flip-back armrests); spacer bolt through the top axle plate hole,
  snug only (it should still spin); ISO 7176-19 and transit decals.
- Parts lists with item, part number and quantity for each kit.
