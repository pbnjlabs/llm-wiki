# TiLite CR1 Service Manual

20-page Permobil service manual for the **TiLite CR1** rigid chair (© 2024).

## Covers

- **Fitting:** centre of gravity, seat to footrest, wheel locks, anti-tips.
- **Replacing parts:** casters, forks (stem nut **15 Nm / 11 ft-lb**),
  footrest, push handles (**4 Nm**), side guards, tension-adjustable back and
  seat, back support (pivot bolt **5 Nm**), anti-tips, **COG camber clamps**
  (anti-tip camber clamp **8 Nm**, non-anti-tip **6 Nm**), camber tube, COG strut
  and clamp (strut clamp **4–5 Nm**), COG frame clamp (**8 Nm**), wheel lock
  adjustment bolt and track bolt **4 Nm**.
- **Performance:** check tire pressure **every 2–3 weeks**, filled to the
  sidewall maximum. Re-set **rear wheel toe** (rotate the camber tube in the
  strut; end-plug flats perpendicular to the ground) after any COG change.
  Optimal weight split about **80% rear / 20% casters**.
- **Troubleshooting a floating caster:** level surface, casters trailing; check
  tire pressure, camber tube centred and straight, toe; loosen the footrest clamp
  on the floating side three turns and tap the bolt up with a mallet to free the
  wedge; loosen the two bottom camber clamp bolts on the opposite side; let the
  chair settle; level the footrest; clamp bolts **10 Nm**; then tighten the
  bottom clamps front to back in **2 Nm steps** to spec (up to 2 Nm over to even
  the clamp gaps). Repeat until all four wheels sit flat.
- Toe-in/toe-out, stainless and titanium quick-release axles, **debris in caster
  axles**.
