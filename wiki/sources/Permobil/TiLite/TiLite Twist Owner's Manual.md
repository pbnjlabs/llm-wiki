# TiLite Twist Owner's Manual

116-page TiLite owner's manual for the **Twist** (OM0006 Rev B). Two identical
copies; one kept.

## Covers

- **Weight limit 165 lb (74 kg)**; 20 lb less with the transit option.
- Warnings, inspections and maintenance, camber mounts (rear seat height by the
  strut notches, 5 mm Allen), floating caster checks (tire pressure, camber tube
  centred, toe), axle sleeve **26 Nm**, outer screw 7 Nm.
- **Transit:** factory Transit Tie-Down Option crash-tested to ISO 7176-19 and
  approved for occupied transit; the Transport Option is unoccupied only.
