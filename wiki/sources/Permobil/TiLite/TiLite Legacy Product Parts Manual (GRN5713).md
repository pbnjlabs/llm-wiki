# TiLite Legacy Product Parts Manual (GRN5713)

**921-page** TiLite parts manual for older and discontinued models (table of
contents dated 2015-11-24). The wiki took only its contents; look parts up in
the PDF. A duplicate copy was skipped.

## Covers

- Policies and procedures, adhesive and thread locker key.
- **Frames:** TR, TRC (series 1–3, fixed back), Aero T, TRA series 2, ZRA (and
  depth adjustable), ZR series 2, Aero Z, Twist, Z10, Aero R, YG and YGS (disc.),
  BB, Evo, **2GX and Aero X** (series 1–2, swing-away, fixed front, depth
  adjustable, hemi height, heavy duty, transit and transport frames), X, SX,
  reinforced SX, TX, XC; crosstubes (standard, reinforced, easy fold, heavy duty,
  triple brace), hangers, footrests and footplates.
- Then backrests, armrests, upholstery, axles, wheel locks, casters, wheels and
  accessories for the same models.
