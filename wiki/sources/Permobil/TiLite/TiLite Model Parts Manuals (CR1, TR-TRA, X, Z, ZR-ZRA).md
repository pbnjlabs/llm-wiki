# TiLite Model Parts Manuals (CR1, TR-TRA, X, Z, ZR-ZRA)

Five current Permobil per-model parts manuals (© 2026): **CR1** (Rev 11, 20 pp.),
**TR / TRA** (Rev 4, 56 pp.), **X** (PD16565 Rev 7, 45 pp.), **Z** (Rev 8, 45 pp.)
and **ZR / ZRA** (Rev 3, 55 pp.). Exploded views with assembly numbers.

## Covers

- Sections in each: backrests and push handles, armrests and side guards,
  footrests, centre of gravity, frames, wheel locks, anti-tips, casters and
  forks, upholstery, rear wheels and handrims, accessories. TR/TRA and ZR/ZRA
  add **anodize kits**; ZR/ZRA adds **transport kits**; X adds hangers, cross
  brace and ELR.
- Examples: G4 locking backrest hinge assemblies (902000-series for 1.25 in ZR
  frames, 902018-series for 1 in TR frames); Z locking hinge 805020-01/-02; CR1
  back supports 804711/804762/804744/804771.
- Order parts at store.permobil.com, parts.tilite@permobil.com or (800) 736-0925.
