# TiLite Pilot Owner's Manual

59-page TiLite owner's manual for the **Pilot** (OM0010 Rev A). Two identical
copies in different download folders; one kept.

## Covers

- **Weight limit 120 lb (54 kg).** With the transit option, the rider must weigh
  20 lb less than the table maximum.
- Warnings, safety inspections and maintenance (Chapter 2), adjustments
  (axle sleeve **26 Nm**), tires (Schrader and Presta valves; solid tires need
  special equipment), transit.
- **Transit:** factory-only Transit Tie-Down Option, crash-tested to WC19 and ISO
  7176-19; the Wheelchair Transport Option is unoccupied only. Check the stickers
  to tell which is fitted. Warranty modification for transit chairs.
- Customer support (800) 545-2266.
