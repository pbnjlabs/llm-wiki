# TiLite Rigid Wheelchair Service Manual (Rev 7)

49-page Permobil service manual for the TiLite rigid chairs **Z, ZRA, ZR,
Aero T, TRA and TR** (© 2023, 2026; Rev 7). Three identical " (1)/(2)/(3)"
copies in the download were skipped. TiSport, LLC (Pasco, WA), TiLite
customer support **800-736-0925**.

## Covers

- **General rules:** keep left-right symmetry; **no water-based lubricants**
  near caster forks or rear axles (they damage bearings); **thread-patch
  fasteners may be reused at most twice**, then replace or put **Loctite 242**
  on the last three threads; Permobil parts only. Serial label on the under-seat
  rigidizer tube. SmartDrive: follow its own documentation.
- **Footrests:** at least **5 cm (2 in) ground clearance**; at least **4.5 cm**
  of extension tube inside the frame tube; clamp Allen screws **10 Nm**; set the
  footplates with the user wearing shoes.
- **Backrests and push handles:** backrest button heads **10 Nm**; integrated or
  fold-down push handles **5 Nm**.
- **Armrests, side guards, upholstery** (check stitching and webbing for wear).
- **Axles and camber (Z):** vertical struts come short, medium and long; camber
  tube clamps to the struts and camber mount clamps to the frame **10 Nm**, same
  holes and same distance both sides; set screw **4 Nm**. Moving the camber tube
  for seat height or centre of gravity may need a **toe-in/toe-out** reset. Work
  upside down with the wheels off on a padded surface.
- **Axles:** axle sleeve **26 Nm**.
- **Wheel locks:** the wheel stop must **embed at least 5 mm** in a tire at its
  rated pressure; test by pushing the locked chair. Compact push- and
  pull-to-lock (5 mm Allen), extension handles.
- **Casters and forks:** always identical sizes both sides; LiteSpeed casters
  have the wider spacer on the recessed side, facing out in the trailing
  position; caster wedge Allen screw **25 Nm** (6 mm socket); 17 mm nylock on
  the fork stem.
- **Rear wheels:** quick-release and quad quick-release axles; pull the wheel to
  check the locking balls hold.
- **Anti-tips** and COG anti-tip adaptors.
- **Transportation:** **only the Z (ISO 7176-19:2022) and ZRA (ISO 7176-19:2008,
  WC19) with the factory Transit Tie-Down Option are crash-tested** as forward-
  facing seats. **ZR, Aero T, TRA and TR must not be occupied in transit.** The
  **Transport** Tie-Down Option is for an unoccupied chair only. Tested with the
  Q'Straint standard lap belt and a three-point belt; after any collision have
  the chair evaluated by the manufacturer.
