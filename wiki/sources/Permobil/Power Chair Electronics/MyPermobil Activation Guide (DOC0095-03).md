# MyPermobil Activation Guide (DOC0095-03)

Two-page quick guide for the **MyPermobil** phone app. **Permobil
Connect** is an order option on new **M- and F-series Corpus and Corpus
VS** chairs; it has to be ordered with the chair. Wi-Fi is needed for
setup.

Steps: confirm the chair was ordered with Permobil Connect, download
MyPermobil (Android or Apple), "activate a new wheelchair", accept the
policy, create an account and verify the e-mail, allow Bluetooth and turn
on the chair. **If it won't connect, turn the chair off and on three
times** to reboot its Bluetooth.
