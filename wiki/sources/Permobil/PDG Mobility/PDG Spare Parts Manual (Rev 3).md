# PDG Spare Parts Manual (Rev 3)

93-page Permobil parts manual for PDG Mobility chairs. Sections: frames,
backrests and push handles, armrests and side guards, footrests, casters and
forks, anti-tips, rear wheels and handrims, seat pan, **tilt** (pp. 65–74), wheel
locks, upholstery, hardware, accessories, **obsolete models** (pp. 88–91).
Glossary of abbreviations (TTDS transit tie-down system, HSTF/LSTF high/low seat
to floor, T-A take-apart, UF/LF upper/lower frame and others). Order through the
Permobil Store, sales.pdg@permobil.com or (800) 736-0925.
