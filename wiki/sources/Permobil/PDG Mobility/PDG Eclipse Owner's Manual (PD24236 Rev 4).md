# PDG Eclipse Owner's Manual (PD24236 Rev 4)

44-page PDG owner's and maintenance manual for the **Eclipse** bariatric manual
wheelchair (Rev 4, 2025-11-24).

## Covers

- **Capacity 600 lb (272 kg) or 1000 lb (453 kg)** depending on configuration
  (shown on the order form). **Not for users under 12.**
- Quick-release axles only; axle receiver and camber bar move together for
  positioning (use the hole in the camber bar for counter-torque); re-set the
  wheel locks after; check stability with the user seated.
- Reclining backrest up to 30°, 1 in narrower than the seat; not with the back
  extension.
- **Pneumatic tire or tube replacement must be done by a PDG dealer or qualified
  technician.**
- Tilting by an assistant on non-removable parts; maintenance, cleaning,
  troubleshooting and transit (TTS only) as the other PDG manuals.
- Test configuration: 605 mm seat width, 560 mm depth, 38 kg (84 lb), 24 in heavy
  duty urethane wheels, 5 in casters; overall width 696–1391 mm by configuration.
