# PDG Fuze Owner's Manuals (T50, M50, JR)

Three PDG Mobility (now Permobil) owner's and maintenance manuals, each 52
pages and dated **2025-11-24**: **Fuze T50 / T50N** (PD24242 Rev 7), **Fuze
M50** (PD24240 Rev 3) and **Fuze JR** (PD24238 Rev 7). Manual **tilt-in-space**
chairs with a centre-of-gravity tilt on a gas strut and mech-lock. PDG Product
Design Group, Richmond BC; **(800) 736-0925**, sales.pdg@permobil.com. Same
layout in all three; differences noted.

## Covers

- **Capacity:** T50/T50N **300 lb (136 kg)**; **M50 400 lb** (its spec table
  prints "136 kg (400 lbs)": 400 lb is 181 kg); **JR 250 lb** in the text but
  "136 kg (300 lbs)" in its spec table. Go by the text and the chair's label.
- **Maintenance (§11):** check nuts and bolts before use; keep quick-release axles
  clean and **oil them monthly with 3-in-1 oil** (PDG's instruction, unlike
  TiLite, Ki and Sunrise); keep wheel locks clean; sidewall tire pressure, never
  over-inflate; table: rolls straight, wheel locks, clothing guards, upholstery,
  rear wheels (no side play, QR axles lock), casters spin to a gradual stop,
  tires, clean and wax.
- **Troubleshooting (§12):** veers left/right, sluggish, 3 of 4 wheels touching,
  caster flutter, squeaks and rattles, looseness, QR won't lock or has play: equal
  tire pressure, caster stem nuts and axle bolts, caster angle, both casters
  touching together, axle nut, bearing seating. **Cracked frame or weld = service
  agent.** M50 adds gas spring won't lock / won't unlock.
- **Seat-to-floor (§14.3):** 13–20 in by axle plate position and caster journal
  (small, medium, tall) and fork hole, from set-up charts by wheel and caster size.
- **QR axle nut (§14.10.2):** hold the detent end with 7/16 in, turn the nut with
  3/4 in in half turns: counter-clockwise if it won't lock, clockwise to remove
  play.
- **Anti-tippers are mandatory** (safety and warranty); hole positions by rear
  wheel size.
- **Casters:** 1/4 in Allen on the journal shoulder bolt, 3/4 in socket on the
  1/2-20 stem nut; keep the lower frame level. M50 rear caster arm bolts
  **18 ft-lb**.
- **Tilt (§14.14):** squeeze the push-bar or armrest trigger to release the
  mech-lock; attendant holds the push bar. **Tilt limiter** on the upper frame at
  20°, 30°, 40° or 50° (location depends on plug-in vs swing-away front rigging).
- **Tilt cable tension (§14.15):** cables stretch and sheaths compress. **Won't
  tilt with the trigger pulled = too loose**: turn the sheath adjustment nut
  clockwise (more thread showing), then lock the jam nut. **Tilts without the
  trigger = too tight**: reverse. Check the cable ball is in the lever slot.
  Videos on PDG Mobility's YouTube channel.
- Options: take-apart frame, headrest, belts, O2 holder, IV pole, ELRs, reclining
  or fold-down back.
- **Transit (§14.21):** only with PDG's **Transit Tiedown System (TTS)**, shown by
  **4 white hook symbols** at the brackets; tested to WC-4 §19 and ISO 7176-19
  (M50: 2022 edition), forward facing, WC-4 §18 tie-downs (e.g. Q'Straint
  S5-6329-11). Not every configuration is transit compatible; aftermarket seating
  changes this.
