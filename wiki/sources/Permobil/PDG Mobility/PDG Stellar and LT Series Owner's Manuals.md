# PDG Stellar and LT Series Owner's Manuals

Four PDG Mobility manual tilt wheelchair manuals, all **2025-11-24**:
**Stellar Series** (Stellar GL; PD24248 Rev 5, 47 pp.), **Stellar HD** (PD24246
V2, 14 pp.), **Stellar Impact** (PD24232 V2, 8-page addendum) and **LT Series**
(Stellar GLT, Bentley LT, Bentley LT-R; PD24367 Rev 4, 56 pp.). The Stellar
Series and LT PDFs have **unreadable text layers and were read by OCR**; check
numbers against the PDF.

## Covers

- **Capacities:** Stellar GL **250 lb** standard base, **300 lb** upgraded base
  and seat frame (with a **secondary gas spring for 250–300 lb** users); Stellar
  HD **250 lb** "unless otherwise specified by PDG"; Stellar GLT, Bentley LT and
  LT-R **300 lb**.
- **LT Series:** Stellar GLT **45° tilt**; Bentley LT **20° tilt with no knee
  rise**; Bentley LT-R (rear drive wheel position). Tell them apart by the pivot
  location. GLT tilt limit bumpers both sides (never run without them; 1/4-20
  bolt), and **anterior pre-tilt 5° or 0°**.
- **Stellar GL:** seat depth **16–22 in** (rear insert bolts **18 ft-lb**);
  sub-frame plates **25 ft-lb**, check the seat pan is level with the strut fully
  extended; frame ID (standard base has a rectangular rear crossbar; not for
  agitated users); some options need 20 in composite or 24 in heavy-duty wheels,
  fixed axles, and aren't TTS compatible.
- **Gas strut uplift:** strut position by occupant weight and seat depth (chart).
  User feels **heavy** coming back upright: move the strut attachment **down**;
  feels **too light** or the attendant must push into tilt: move it **up**. Cable
  tension as on the Fuze.
- LT seat depth insert **104 in-lb**; seat pan screws snug to no free play, don't
  overtighten; sub-frame **25 ft-lb**.
- **Stellar HD:** procedures for front riggings, seat-to-floor heights, seat tilt,
  gas strut and cable, **tilt limiter**, motor vehicle use; 720 mm wide, 33.4 kg as
  tested, 1235 mm turnaround.
- **Stellar Impact addendum:** intended use, storage and disposal, seat-to-floor
  height, tilt limiter and other procedures that differ from the Stellar manual.
- **Transit:** only with the TTS (4 white hook symbols); WC-4 §18 / ISO 10542
  WTORS; rider plus items within the rated capacity.
