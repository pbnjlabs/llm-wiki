# PDG Power Tilt Accessory Owner's Manual (PD24244 Rev 6)

36-page PDG manual (Rev 6, 2026-04-16) for the **Power Tilt** accessory that
tilts a PDG manual tilt chair from a switch. Install and service by a competent
technician (assembly drawings in the appendix).

## Covers

- **Parts:** electric linear actuator, battery assembly, controller box, tilt
  limiting assembly, control switches. The actuator stops itself at maximum and
  minimum tilt.
- **Switches:** toggle, 2-button or egg switch (egg = toggle mode, reverses each
  press); up to two at once, plugged into the controller jacks (undo the strap,
  pull the controller out); mono (egg) switches need PDG's mono-to-stereo adapter.
  A part-seated plug causes odd behaviour.
- **Tilt limit slider** (Stellar GL and Fuze T50; not Stellar LEAP or Bentley),
  set relative to the lower frame; the chair always returns to level unless
  there's a fault.
- **Batteries:** **two modular 12 V lead-acid packs**, one on the chair and one on
  the charger; swap often (ideally daily); check monthly; unplug the controller if
  unused for days (it drains the pack). Off-board charger, 120 V, 3-pin XLR. Don't
  carry the charger on the chair.
- **Troubleshooting:** actuator whines but doesn't move = low battery, failed
  actuator or obstructed frame; controller clicks and LED on but no movement =
  actuator cable unplugged or actuator/cable failed; no sound at all = switch or
  power cord unplugged, tilt limit switch misadjusted or failed, dead battery,
  failed switch or controller; no charger LEDs = outlet, connection, **charger on
  240 V**, failed charger. Procedures to disable the tilt limit switch and check
  3-position and egg switches with a meter; flowcharts.
