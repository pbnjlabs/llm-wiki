# Quickie 2HP Owner's Manual (128226 Rev C)

76-page Sunrise Medical owner's manual for the **Quickie 2HP** and
**2HP HD** folding manual wheelchairs, with or without the transit
option (128226 Rev. C, © 2025). English pp. 1–25, then Spanish and
French. **The PDF's text layer is garbled**, so the wiki read it by OCR
of the English pages; check numbers against the PDF. Same layout as the
[[Quickie 2 and 2HD Owner's Manual (MK-100070 Rev G)]]; this page lists
what differs.

## Differences from the Quickie 2 / 2HD

- **Capacity: 2HP 250 lb (113 kg), 2HP HD 350 lb (159 kg).**
- **Camber preset at 5°** at the factory (camber washers).
- Seat height 3.375 in by moving the axle plate in the frame holes;
  axle bolts 144 in-lb.
- **Two wheel-lock types** (high-mount push/pull and scissor). The
  section header says "use a torque setting of 144 in-lb when setting up
  wheel locks" but the steps say **115 in-lb**; the Quickie 2 manual
  uses 115 with 144 as the maximum, so torque to 115.
- **Fixed front footrests** (extension tube bolted through the frame),
  aluminum angle-adjustable footplate, and a platform flip-up footplate
  on the **fixed front frame**. No Z-Finity section.
- Armrest receiver uses 3 hex bolts; fit adjusted with 4 hex bolts (7/16
  in wrench). Swing-away armrests height by quick-release pin.
- Tire pressures, caster (75 in-lb), anti-tip receiver (115 in-lb),
  backrest (144 in-lb), axle play and warranty as the Quickie 2.
