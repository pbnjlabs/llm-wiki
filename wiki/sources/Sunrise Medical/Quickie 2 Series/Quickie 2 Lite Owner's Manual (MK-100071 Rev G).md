# Quickie 2 Lite Owner's Manual (MK-100071 Rev G)

32-page Sunrise Medical owner's manual for the **Quickie 2 Lite** folding
manual wheelchair (MK-100071 Rev. G, © 2025). Same layout and warnings
as the [[Quickie 2 and 2HD Owner's Manual (MK-100070 Rev G)]]; this page
lists what differs.

## Differences from the Quickie 2 / 2HD

- **Capacity 265 lb (120 kg).** Z-Finity footrest 165 lb.
- **Transit Option:** two front and two rear hooks, plus a
  **non-removable fastener (C)** that must stay on the frame for transit
  safety.
- **Folding push handles** (press the unlock button to fold).
- **Axle plate:** center of gravity is moved by removing the **four
  axle-plate bolts**, nuts and camber spacers and sliding the plate;
  plate and camber must match on both sides. Camber by spacers between
  the lower axle plate attachment and the frame.
- **Seat height:** move the axle sleeve between holes in the axle plate;
  swap the plates left to right and upside down for 1/2 in steps.
- **Wheelbase with anti-tip hardware:** the axle sleeve threads in or
  out with the anti-tip receiver; leave the sleeve's flats parallel to
  the back of the receiver. After adding camber, **square the anti-tips**
  to the chair's centerline and torque the receiver to **100 in-lb**
  (11.3 Nm). Anti-tip receivers otherwise 115 in-lb.
- **Axle nut:** same 0 to 0.010 in play; also check the quick-release
  button sits **flush with the wheel**.
- **Caster squareness:** check whenever axle height, tire size, center
  of gravity or camber tube changes: casters turned rearward, a square
  against the back of the fork; **more than 2° off vertical needs
  re-squaring**. An out-of-square caster lifts off the floor as it
  swivels.
- **Fixed-front frame option:** caster housing moves between preset holes
  (cotter clip, clevis pin, two bolts with a 7/16 in wrench); check for
  footrest and wheel interference and squareness, then re-check
  stability with a spotter.
- **Platform and auto-fold footrests** (height by set screw, 2 in floor
  clearance).
- Backrest height: telescoping tube, holes 1 in apart, 144 in-lb.
- Torques otherwise as the Quickie 2: M5 44, M6 62, M8 88, others 60
  in-lb; wheel locks 115, casters 75, footrest set screw 144, Z-Finity 120
  max.
- Warranty as the Quickie 2 (frame and crossbrace for life).
