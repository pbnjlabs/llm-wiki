# Quickie 2 and 2HD Owner's Manual (MK-100070 Rev G)

32-page Sunrise Medical owner's manual for the **Quickie 2** and **Quickie
2HD** folding manual wheelchairs, with or without the transit option
(MK-100070 Rev. G, © 2025; English only, Spanish and French online).
The **first manual wheelchair manual in the wiki**. It covers rider
warnings, user maintenance and a **dealer service section with torque
values**, so it doubles as the service reference.

## Covers

- **Capacity** (rider plus anything carried): **Quickie 2 300 lb
  (136 kg), Quickie 2HD 350 lb (159 kg).** Option limits: Topolino rear
  wheels 265 lb, Xtender power assist 265 lb, **Z-Finity footrest 165
  lb**.
- **Parts** (p. 4): push handle/backcane, backrest, pneumatic tire,
  aluminum handrim and rim, quick-release axle, anti-tip, caster wheel,
  fork and housing, footrest, swing-away latch, **X-hinge** (folding
  frame), wheel lock, seat sling, armrest.
- **Transit** (p. 6): the **Transit Option** is a WC-19 tie-down and
  occupant restraint system, identified by **four securement points with
  decals, two front and two rear**. Without it, nobody may sit in the
  chair in a moving vehicle. Never use a chair that has been in a
  vehicle accident. See [[Quickie and Zippie WC-19 Transit Securement Supplement (MK-100072 Rev I)]].
- **Use limits:** firm, even surfaces only; casters of at least 5 in for
  regular 1/2 in obstacles; **not for street use**; **slopes, ramps and
  side-hills under 6°**, straight up and down, never turn on a slope; a
  3/4 in drop-off can stop a caster; curbs over 4 in only with help;
  stairs need **two attendants** (backward up, forward down). Never use
  an escalator.
- **Component warnings** (§VII): **anti-tips 1-1/2 to 2 in off the
  ground**; **footrests at least 2 in off the ground**; armrests and
  footrests detach and must never be used to lift the chair; caster pin
  locks only stop the casters swiveling; sling seats and stock foam
  aren't pressure relief, and the sling needs a cushion on it; use only
  Sunrise-grade high-strength fasteners and **don't reuse fasteners**;
  check tire pressure **weekly**; quick-release axles are locked only
  when the button pops fully out; **wheel lock arms must sink at least
  1/8 in into the tire**; positioning belts aren't seat belts or
  restraints; laundering reduces the upholstery's flame retardancy.
- **Tire pressures** (p. 13): pneumatic 20, 22 and 26 in **65 psi**
  (448 kPa), 24 in **75 psi** (517 kPa); high-pressure clincher 22 in
  100 psi, 24–26 in 110 psi; Schwalbe Marathon 24 and 25 in 85–145 psi.
- **Maintenance chart** (p. 15), user and caregiver: weekly tire
  pressure, wheel locks and a look for loose hardware; monthly armrests,
  axles and sleeves, quick-release axles, wheels and spokes, casters,
  anti-tips, frame and crossbrace, upholstery; **annual dealer service**.
  Axles and sleeves every 6 months. Clean paint monthly, wax every 3
  months; wipe axles weekly; hand-wash and drip-dry upholstery. **No
  grease or oil**: Teflon-based lube only (Tri-Flow), never 3-in-One or
  WD-40.
- **Troubleshooting chart** (p. 15): pulls left or right, looseness,
  sluggish turning, squeaks and rattles, caster flutter. Fixes: equal
  correct tire pressure, tighten nuts and bolts, tighten spokes, Tri-Flow
  between frame joints, adjust the caster fork.
- **Use** (§VIII): mounting rear wheels, wheel locks, anti-tips (three
  wheel heights, both equal), flip-back and swing-away armrests,
  swing-in/out footrests (60°, 70°, 80°) and ALR/ELR legrests, Z-Finity
  footrest, backrests and stroller handles, seat sling tension (hook and
  loop under the seat), side guards, **folding** (remove wheels, flip up
  swing-away footrests, lift the sling sharply at the center; unfold by
  pressing the seat rail down until it sits in the saddles), storage
  (inspect after 3 months unused).
- **Dealer service** (§IX), see the torque table on [[Quickie-2-Series]]:
  annual inspection; default torques M5 44, M6 62, M8 88, others 60 in-lb;
  center of gravity (axle sleeve on the indexed plate, 1/2 in marks);
  **camber 0°, 3° or 6°** set by camber washers, axle plate bolts 144
  in-lb; wheelbase width (axle sleeve nuts **30 ft-lb**); seat height
  (axle plate in frame holes, **3.375 in range**, adjust casters to
  match); **axle play 0 to 0.010 in** (3/4 in wrench on the nut, 1/2 in
  to hold the bearing end); four wheel-lock types (115 in-lb, never over
  144); caster forks and casters 75 in-lb; caster housing **square to the
  floor** using a right triangle on a flat table (eccentric bolts);
  reversible caster housings for footrest clearance; anti-tip receivers
  115 in-lb; armrest receivers (5/64 in set screws); footrest height 144
  in-lb; Z-Finity clamps 120 in-lb max; backrest height and angle 144
  in-lb; back-depth plate for aftermarket backs (not for transit);
  tension-adjustable back (at least 2 in of hook-and-loop engagement);
  reversible **seat saddle** ("dynamic" is more rigid, "classic" folds
  more easily); side guards; limb support.
- **Warranty** (p. 31): **frame and crossbrace for life** of the
  original owner (expected service life 5 years); Sunrise-made parts 1
  year; tension-adjustable back 12 months. Not tires and tubes,
  upholstery, pads or grips. Void if the serial tag is removed. US and
  Canada; not transferable; the dealer gets prior approval and the owner
  pays labor.
