# Quickie One-Arm Drive Option (930331 Rev G)

8-page Sunrise Medical instructions for the **One-Arm Drive** option on
the **Quickie 2, Quickie IRIS, Zippie 2 and Zippie GS** (P/N 930331
Rev. G, © 2026). Two handrims on one side drive both wheels through a
removable drive shaft. Safety, maintenance, tools and warranty are in
the chair's own manual.

## Covers

- **Parts:** inner and outer handrim, drive shaft assembly, drive shaft
  storage brackets, axle tabs, crossbraces.
- **Axles:** axle plates on both sides with bushings. **No camber with
  one-arm drive.** Only **20, 22 and 24 in wheels**.
- **Hubs:** thread the screw partly through the threaded hub, align the
  keyways with the alignment tool or keyed axle, draw both hubs into the
  wheel with the washer and nut, fit the hub screws in sequence (1/8 in
  Allen), remove the tool.
- **Drive side:** handrim hub on the dual-handrim side; washer, axle
  through the axle-plate bearings, **four washers (Quickie and Zippie)**,
  spacer, wheel, washer, key, handrim assembly, washer and nut.
  **Opposite side:** washer, axle, spacer, wheel, key, washer, nut.
- **Drive shaft:** compress, twist and lock it, fit one end to an axle
  tab, unlock and fit the other. Store it in the brackets on the lower
  front and rear frame or on the crossbrace (Zippie GS: lower frame
  only).
- **Adjustments:** center of balance by the second bearing position or
  by inverting the axle plate; **seat height range 2-1/2 in on the
  Quickie 2** (axle plate in frame holes; 1-1/4 in on the Zippie GS); a
  high seat can "out-adjust" the caster forks and tip the seat forward,
  so fit longer forks. Re-set the wheel locks (1/8 in into the tire)
  after any axle change. Can be moved to either side.
- **Driving:** both handrims together go straight; with the outer rim
  on the right, the outer rim alone turns right and the inner rim alone
  turns left. Keep fingers out of the spokes and hub.
