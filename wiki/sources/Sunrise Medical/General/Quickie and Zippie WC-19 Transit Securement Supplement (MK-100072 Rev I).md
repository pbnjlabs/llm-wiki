# Quickie and Zippie WC-19 Transit Securement Supplement (MK-100072 Rev I)

8-page Sunrise Medical supplement for chairs built with the **WC-19
Transit Option** (MK-100072 Rev. I, © 2025). Image-only PDF, read by
OCR. It ships with each transit chair; the owner's manuals point to it.

## Covers

- **Chairs and transit capacities:** Quickie 2 300 lb, Quickie 2 Lite
  265, Quickie 2HD 350, Quickie IRIS 300, IRIS HD 400, Zippie IRIS 225,
  Q7, QRi, 5R and 7R 265, Nitrum 275 (open frame) / 300 (hybrid), Zippie
  Sphynx 250, Zippie X'CAPE 165 lb. (The **Quickie 2HP isn't listed.**)
- **Sunrise's position:** where possible the rider transfers to the
  vehicle seat and uses its belts. No tie-down system is DOT-approved
  for riding in a wheelchair. Use only a WTORS meeting **SAE J2249**.
- **Rules:** occupant must weigh **over 51 lb**; chair **forward facing**;
  use **both pelvic and upper-torso belts**; backrest tilt **30° max**
  (seat angle set to 10° on adjustable chairs); remove trays, IV poles,
  backpacks and other mounted items; postural belts and supports aren't
  restraints; keep the buckle clear of anything that could press its
  release; factory seating system only; **inspect WTORS gear monthly**.
- **After a crash:** a chair in a sudden stop must be inspected; **a
  chair in a collision must be replaced**, and its warranty is void.
- **Securement points:** four factory transport brackets (two front, two
  rear) marked with the WC-19 symbol, plus a **wheelchair-anchored pelvic
  belt**. Pelvic belt to its anchor with a 5 mm Allen, **144 in-lb (16.3
  Nm)**. Use only a pelvic belt meeting ANSI/RESNA WC-4 §19 and SAE J2249.
- **Clear zones:** frontal **26 in** from the head with pelvic and
  torso belts, **37 in** with a pelvic belt only; rear **16 in**; seated
  head height about 47–61 in.
- **Belt fit:** pelvic belt **45–75°** to horizontal (30–45° optional),
  low on the pelvis, not held off the body by armrests or wheels, not
  twisted, snug; torso belt over the shoulder, anchored above and behind.
- **Ratings** (RESNA WC-4:2017): lateral stability "Good" for all except
  the IRIS HD ("Acceptable"); belt positioning "Excellent" or "Good" for
  most, **IRIS HD "Poor"**.
- **Tested configurations** (p. 7): e.g. Quickie 2 Lite and Quickie 2 at
  16 × 18 in, 36 lb; Quickie 2HD 18 × 20 in, 46 lb, each with turning
  radius and turnaround width (Quickie 2: 27 in radius, 50 in width).
