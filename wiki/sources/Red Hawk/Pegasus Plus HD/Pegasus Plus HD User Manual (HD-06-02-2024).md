# Pegasus Plus HD User Manual (HD-06-02-2024)

**Complete** 56-page user's manual for the **Pegasus Plus HD** folding
power wheelchair, titled "HD-06-02-2024 Pegasus Plus Heavy Duty Folding
Electric Wheelchair User Manual" (PDF made 2025-08-29). It's distributed
by **Discover Your Mobility Inc.**, 11055 E 9 Mile Rd, Warren, MI 48089,
**(866) 868-9694**, discovermymobility.com. The same company sells the
[[Air-Hawk-Eagle-HD]], [[Falcon]] and [[Phoenix]], from an older Hazel
Park address. The layout and wording are the same template as the JBH
[[Pegasus Plus Heavy Duty Manual]] (DC02), but this is **a heavier
chair**, and its sample frame number reads **DC06**-2018-05-001 (DC02 in
the other manual). Raw file saved from `~/Downloads` under a readable name
(the download was a hash-named `_optim.pdf`). Feeds [[Pegasus-Plus-HD]].

## Setup, folding, batteries

- Arrives packed: joystick, chair, tools, charger, 2 lithium batteries,
  safety belt, seat cushion, manual. Unfold by pulling the push bar up and
  pressing the seat down until it locks.
- **Fold:** power off, cushion off, press the **red button** and push the
  top of the backrest forward; the seat cross bar is the carry handle.
- **Battery:** slide into the battery shell on each side by its handle;
  remove by pressing the soft plastic button and pulling. Power off first.

## Controller

- Power button: press for standby, **hold 3 s to switch off**. **[+]/[−]**
  set speed sections **1–5**. USB port and charging port on the panel.
- Screen icons: Bluetooth, lock state, temperature (overheat / too cold),
  speed limit, real-time speed, battery, odometer. **Low battery:** the bar
  turns yellow and the speed-limit icon shows; no power: speed-limit icon
  lit; charging: green icon flashes.
- Hold the left or right screen button **5 s** to change page: **main,
  QR code, equipment number (device ID and firmware version)**. Faults pop
  up as a **code** (table below).

## Charging

- **Through the controller port** (both batteries): power **off**, plug the
  charger into the chair first. **Max 6 hours with two batteries, 3 hours
  with one.** Charger light **orange = charging, green = full**.
- **Direct to a battery:** power off, flip open the port cover on the
  battery, use the pigtail adapter; **max 3 hours per battery**.
- About **500 cycles**, life 2–5 years. **Replace both batteries together**
  (same type and capacity; don't mix old and new). Matches the PMS house
  standard ([[Mobility-Batteries]]).
- Storage: charge every **2 months to about 80%**. Air travel: **≤ 25%
  charge**; other transport ≤ 50%. Store batteries −5 to 35 °C.
- Over-discharge protection cuts top speed; over-current protection shuts
  the motors off.
- The manual says "do not charge indoors in case of fire". Treat that as
  "charge in a supervised, fire-safe place", as its own warning puts it.

## Freewheel

Power off, then the two red levers on the motors. **Conflict:** the text
says "push down on the two red levers", but the photo's labels say
**UP = free wheel / manual, DOWN = lock / power**. Go by the label on the
chair. Code **46 "left drive abnormal"** means check the left lever is in
position.

## Transport

**Not compatible with tie-down systems**, and never ride in it in a moving
vehicle. Fold it, power off, and don't put it
loose on the front seat.

## Warranty (p. 37)

From the factory date, **parts only**, shipped to the user; labour is
the user's cost. **Frame 3 years; motors 1 year; controller and CPU 1
year; battery 6 months; wear parts (tires, seat and back, armrests,
straps) 3 months.** Non-transferable. The usual exclusions (negligence,
abuse, improper storage, handling or operation, unapproved modification).

## Troubleshooting (p. 38–42)

| Question | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| No power | Battery or controller/CPU/joystick connection, or battery low | Connect the battery, tighten all connections, charge |
| Noisy or vibrating when turning | Speed set too low, or motor damaged | Raise speed; replace the motor |
| Won't charge | Charger light off / always green / stops early | Replace charger; battery not connected or worn; capacity drops with age (normal) |
| Controller won't connect | Bent male pins on the controller | Straighten them with a small tool |

**Maintenance:** **daily** check the joystick lever (not bent, springs
back, rubber base OK); **weekly** unplug the controller and charger
connectors from the battery box and check for corrosion, check the
controller is tight (don't overtighten), and do the **brake test** (on
level ground: power on, nudge the joystick until you hear the brakes
release, let go, repeat back, left and right); **monthly** check the
anti-tip wheels, front and drive wheel wear, and the front forks for
wear or play (bearing). Monthly deep discharge recommended; pull the
batteries for long storage. Wipe dry after it gets wet; keep it out of
the sun. Storage −40 to 65 °C.

## Diagnostics: fault codes (p. 43–45)

|---|---|---|
| 01 | Joystick out of neutral | Release the joystick |
| 02 | Electromagnetic brake not engaged | Engage the brake (freewheel levers) |
| 12 | Joystick communication error | Check all harnesses |
| 14 | Joystick faulty | Replace the joystick |
| 22 | Battery communication error | Battery quick-release plug |
| 23 / 24 | Low-temperature charging / discharging fault | Warm it up, power on again |
| 25 | Charging temperature too high | Recharge after the battery cools |
| 26 | Charging current fault | Replace the charger |
| 27 | Discharge temperature too high | Off, wait, on |
| 28 | Discharge current fault | Unplug and replace the battery |
| 29 | Over-voltage | Leave it on a while, then off and on |
| 30 | Under-voltage | Charge |
| 31 | Charging failure | Replace the charger, then the battery |
| 42 | Controller communication abnormal | Signal interface connection |
| 43 / 54 | Left / right drive not connected | Motor-to-controller connector |
| 44 / 55 | Left / right drive over-temperature | Off a few minutes; if it persists, replace the drive |
| 45 / 56 | Left drive overload / right drive over-current | Off and on; then replace the drive |
| 46 | Left drive abnormal | **Left freewheel lever position**; connecting wire |
| 47 / 58 | Left / right motor fault | Driver-to-motor connection |
| 48 / 59 | Left / right motor MOS failure | Replace the motor driver |
| 49 / 60 | Left / right motor phase fault | Driver-to-motor connection |
| 50 / 61 | Left / right motor locked | Release the joystick (left: then replace the motor; right: check the connection) |
| 51–52 / 62–63 | Phase current offset or main wire current fault | Replace the motor driver |
| 53 / 64 | Left / right motor not connected | Driver and motor connectors |
| 57 | Right brake fault | Driver-to-motor connection |
| 65 | Controller power cut | Off and on |
| 66 | Handrail (armrest) position fault | Set the armrest position |
| 72–99 | Drive failure | Replace the driver |

The left and right lists aren't symmetric (no left brake code; 45 says
overload, 56 over-current); that's as printed.
