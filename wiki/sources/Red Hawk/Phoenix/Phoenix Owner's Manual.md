# Phoenix Owner's Manual

25 pages, "Phoenix Power Wheelchair — Operating Manual." Distributed by
**Discover Your Mobility Inc.** (66 E Ten Mile Road, Hazel Park, MI
48030; (866) 868-9694), confirmed via the Guarantee Card on the final
page — same distributor as [[Falcon]] and the "Eagle HD" branding of
[[Air-Hawk-Eagle-HD]]. Unlike those three, this manual uses a
**completely different template** with no Nanjing Jin Bai He branding
visible anywhere, and mentions an unexplained third company, "TREK
Mobility," in the battery-care section — manufacturer identity for this
specific model is not confirmed. See [[Phoenix]] for full specs and the
"TREK Mobility" quirk.
