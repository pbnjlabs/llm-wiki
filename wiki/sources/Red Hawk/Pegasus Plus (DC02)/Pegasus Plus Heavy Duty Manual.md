# Pegasus Plus Heavy Duty Manual

30 pages on disk (PDF metadata confirms this is the true page count),
"User's Manual — DC02," branded throughout as **JBH** — manufactured by
**Anhui JBH Medical Apparatus Company Limited** (No. 116, Qicang Road,
Mingguang, Anhui, 239400, China). "Pegasus Plus Heavy Duty" appears
nowhere in the manual text; it's Performance Medical Supply's own
filename/dealer label for the DC02. A different manufacturer from the
Nanjing Jin Bai He products in this folder ([[Air-Hawk-Eagle-HD]],
[[Falcon]], possibly [[Phoenix]]) — see [[Red Hawk]] for the naming
coincidence this raises.

## Known limitation: truncated file
The manual's own table of contents lists content through page 44
(Warranty p.31, Trouble Shooting and Maintenance p.32-36, Diagnostics
p.37, Electrical Wiring Diagram p.38, EMC p.39-43, Warranty Card p.44),
but the file's actual content ends around page 27 (Charging Batteries),
followed by blank trailing pages to 30. **Warranty terms and
troubleshooting content for this model are not available from this
file.** See [[Pegasus Plus (DC02)]] for what could be extracted (specs,
assembly, indications for use, contraindications) and flag to the human
if a complete copy can be sourced.
