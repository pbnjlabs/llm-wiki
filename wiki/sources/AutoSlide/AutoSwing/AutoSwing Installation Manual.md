# AutoSwing Installation Manual

AutoSlide's installation manual for **AutoSwing**, its swing-door operator
(catalogue no. ASW8-1). 24 pages, web version, undated (the PDF is from
January 2023). Details are on [[AutoSwing]].

## Covers

- Box contents. The Pro version adds a backup battery and a 4-button remote.
- Specs, components, and choosing the arm (pull arm for in-swing, push arm
  for out-swing; the operator always goes on the inside).
- Mounting the base and arm, then power-up and direction learning with
  DIP switch #1.
- Electric lock wiring (maglock, electric strike, smart lock).
- Controller buttons, app programming, DIP switches, fire alarm setup,
  sensors, and setting up the AutoPlus Gateway.
- Safety: required door signs, when safety sensors are needed, and power
  cord rules.

## Rough edges in this copy

- The tools list still has the writer's notes in it ("I believe there are
  two different sizes…"), and step 11 says "See section ___".
- Door weight is listed as **220.5 lb (100 kg)** under features but
  **198.4 lb (90 kg)** in the spec table. Use 198 lb.
