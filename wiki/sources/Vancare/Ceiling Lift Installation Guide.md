# Ceiling Lift Installation Guide

213 pages, "03 December 2021." **Vancare, Inc.-authored**, found filed
under `MANUALS/Prism/` (raw sources are immutable, not moved) —
see [[Vancare]] for why this isn't a Prism document despite its
location, and why it's kept as its own manufacturer's source rather than
folded into Prism's. For trained/certified installers only per its own
scope statement.

## Structure (high-level — not transcribed page-by-page; this is a
parts-catalog-heavy reference, same treatment as Harmar's consolidated
engineering documents)

- **Planning** (p.4-7): 4-stage workflow — client consultation, site
  survey (pick-up points, obstructions, structure type), track layout
  design, order submission via distributor email
- **Track Layout and Installation** (p.8-65): Basic (single-path) track;
  Full Room Covering / traverse (X/Y) systems — two parallel fixed
  tracks spanned by a third traversing track; Transition Gate System
  (TGS) for connecting separate track runs
- **Turntable Systems** (p.66-80)
- **Attaching to Structure** (p.81-164): split by setting — Home Care
  (wood framing: Top-Down [most common, requires attic access],
  Bottom-Up [no attic access], Wood Blocking [new construction/TJI
  joists], Wall Mount) vs. Institutional (Concrete Slab, Structural
  Beam/Open Web Joist)
- **Power Supply Installation** (p.165-168): two charging architectures
  — **End Point** (docking station at one track terminus) and
  **Omni/Constant** (a cut-to-length "TransStrip" continuous charging
  rail spanning the whole track, eliminating the need to dock)
- **Ceiling Lift Installation** (p.169) and **Testing and Inspection**
  (p.170-173): Final Inspection (FI, before first use) and Preventative
  Maintenance (PM, annual or after overload/modification) share one
  inspection-procedure checklist; load testing at 150% SWL, function and
  deflection testing at 100% SWL (max allowable deflection = span/200)
- **Parts and Accessories** (p.174-202): hand control part-number tables
  (digital: 2/4/6-way; pneumatic: 2/4/6-way), **Prism/Vancare
  cross-compatibility tables for C-Series and Portable lifts** (see
  below), then track profiles (Regular/Flush Mount/Super Track/
  TrackPlus, each with dimensions and cut-length part numbers),
  brackets, turntables, TGS hardware, slings
- **Appendix A: Span and Cantilever Details** (p.203): max distance
  between supports and max cantilever, tabled by lift capacity (200-1000
  lb) × track type (Track/Super Track/TrackPlus/90°/45° arcs)
- **Appendix B: Ceiling Lift Power Requirements** (p.204): charger specs
  (100-240VAC input, 28.8V fast-charge/27V float output, UL/CSA/CE
  certified) — **explicitly "compatible with all C-450, C-625, C-800 and
  C-1000 Ceiling Lifts"**
- **Appendix C: Forms** (p.205+): Homecare Project Assessment Form,
  Site Assessment Form, Final Checklist/Inspection forms (FC/CL),
  Preventative Maintenance forms (PM/TPM) — referenced throughout the
  Testing and Inspection section

## Cross-manufacturer confirmation (the key finding for this wiki)

Page 174's hand-control/parts tables are the source of two facts not
documented anywhere else in this wiki:
- **C-800 and C-1000** are real Prism "C-Series" model numbers (800 lb
  and 1000 lb capacity respectively) alongside the already-documented
  [[C-450-C-625]] — no manual of their own is on file, only this parts
  cross-reference and the shared charger's compatibility list
- **P-600** (600 lb capacity) is a sibling model to [[P440]]'s P-440 —
  same "Portable Ceiling Lifts" table, same trolley-compatibility
  options, no manual of its own on file
- Prism's portable lifts (P-440/P-600) are compatible with **four**
  track brands via different trolley part numbers: Vancare, Mikyway,
  Guldman, and ArjoTrack — confirming Prism doesn't lock its portable
  lift line to one track manufacturer
