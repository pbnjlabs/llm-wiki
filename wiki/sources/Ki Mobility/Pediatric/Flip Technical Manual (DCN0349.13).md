# Flip Technical Manual (DCN0349.13)

Ki Mobility **dealer technical manual** for the Flip (134 pages), written for a qualified technician at an authorized Ki dealer (an ATP or trained clinician). It supplements the user manual. Procedures are mostly step photos; the wiki summarizes what's covered and the values in the text.

## Covers

- **Seat height configuration charts (pp. 4–66)**, axle, caster arm and fork references, caster construction and adjustment, chair width, seat depth, quick-release axle, **hand tilt and foot tilt**, transit, anti-tip, back height and angle, reclining and dynamic backs, seat pan, wheel locks, **attendant foot lock and rod guide replacement**, drum brake, handrims, footplates, 4-way latch, Pro ELR, T-arm, pediatric flip-up armrest, canopy, footrest length, contracture and multi-angle footrests, pediatric Pro ELR, IV holder, **Flip for Leckey** and **Flip for x:panda** adaptors, tipping lever, ergo grip.
- Torques found: pivot joint screws (E) and (F) **240 in-lb**; three screws (G) **144 in-lb**.
- Frame: seat frame 1 in, connecting tube 13/16 in.
- Spoke tension: one-arm-drive, Maxx and low-cost spokes 60–100 kgf; drum-brake wheels 90–120 kgf radial side, 60–90 kgf crossed side; Superlight 92–112 kgf.
