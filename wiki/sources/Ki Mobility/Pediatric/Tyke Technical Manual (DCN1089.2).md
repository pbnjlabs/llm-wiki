# Tyke Technical Manual (DCN1089.2)

Ki Mobility **dealer technical manual** for the Tyke (14 pages), written for a qualified technician at an authorized Ki dealer (an ATP or trained clinician). It supplements the user manual. Procedures are mostly step photos; the wiki summarizes what's covered and the values in the text.

## Covers

- **Adapter plate direction and center of gravity:** the x:panda interface plate screws **80 in-lb** (6 mm Allen). Position 1 if the x:panda seat depth is under 9.75 in, position 2 if over; **reversed (rear-facing) only in position 3**, infant x:panda or x:panda Shape only, and no footplate.
- **Changing seating direction changes transit labelling:** to rear-facing, remove the transit loop and WC-19 stickers, fit a "No Transit" sticker and transit covers (parts kit **#120802**); back to forward-facing, refit the stickers (kit **#120801**).
- Replacing rear and front wheels, caster fork, swivel lock, tilt knob assembly and tilt mechanism; adjusting the rear suspension; wheel lock; storage tray; ventilator mount (Pulmonetics LTV); O2 holder.
