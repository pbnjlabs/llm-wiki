# Arc Technical Manual (DCN0666.10)

Ki Mobility **dealer technical manual** for the Arc (69 pages), written for a qualified technician at an authorized Ki dealer (an ATP or trained clinician). It supplements the user manual. Procedures are mostly step photos; the wiki summarizes what's covered and the values in the text.

## Covers

- Seat depth setup, seat height charts and references, caster construction and adjustment, quick-release axle, chair width, **seat depth (post and pre 08/12/2026 builds are different)**, **hand tilt and foot tilt**, transit, anti-tip, back height and angle, folding reclining backrest, dynamic back, removable seat pan, wheel locks, attendant foot lock, drum brake, handrims, footplates, 4-way latch, Pro ELR, T-arm, pediatric flip-up armrest, canopy, footrest length, contracture and multi-angle footrests, pediatric Pro ELR, high mount extension, folding IV pole, IV and O2 holders, vent and battery tray, rigidizer handle and bar, hemi wheel lock, **adjusting upper roller**, tipping lever, ergo grip.
- Torques found: **144 in-lb** (several); pivot joint screws (E) and (F) **240 in-lb**; three screws (G) 144 in-lb.
- Spoke tension: one-arm-drive, Maxx and low-cost spokes 60–100 kgf; drum-brake wheels 90–120 kgf radial side, 60–90 kgf crossed side; Superlight 92–112 kgf.
