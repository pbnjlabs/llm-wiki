# Clik Technical Manual (DCN0374.8)

Ki Mobility **dealer technical manual** for the Clik (65 pages), written for a qualified technician at an authorized Ki dealer (an ATP or trained clinician). It supplements the user manual. Procedures are mostly step photos; the wiki summarizes what's covered and the values in the text.

## Covers

- Chair measurements (the **Little Wave Clik grows with the user**), caster mount, forks and stems, **camber mount assembly**, camber tube and adapters, quick-release axle, backrest, relaxed back, back posts, stroller and push handles, armrests, canopy, cane holder, luggage, handrims, **growing seat pan and growing seat sling**, side guards, transit, footrests, cross tube, anti-tips, **5th wheel**, wheel locks, one-arm drive, **center of gravity**, **wheelbase width**, **squaring caster**, **toe to zero**, drum brake, tipping lever, EZ grip.
- Torques found: 3 mm screws **40 in-lb**; two 6 mm retaining screws **80 in-lb**; balanced tightening (one to **50 in-lb**, then **80 in-lb**); grip collar 50 in-lb minimum.
- Frame tube 1-1/8 in.
- Spoke tension: one-arm-drive, Maxx and low-cost spokes 60–100 kgf; drum-brake wheels 90–120 kgf radial side, 60–90 kgf crossed side; Superlight 92–112 kgf.
