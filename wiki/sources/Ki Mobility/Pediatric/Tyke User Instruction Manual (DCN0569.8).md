# Tyke User Instruction Manual (DCN0569.8)

Ki Mobility user instruction manual and warranty for the **Tyke** (DCN0569.8, 29 pages, English then Spanish). The wiki read the English half.

## Covers

- **Capacity** (user plus everything carried): **74 lb (33.5 kg)** user; storage bag 15 lb (7 kg).
- **Transit:** Transit section present (rear tie-downs on the base).
- **Mobility base** (stroller-style, folding) for a child seat, normally the **R82 x:panda**. The manual goes to the caregiver.
- Parts include a folding mechanism, separate seat angle adjustment on the frame, suspension and rear tie-downs.
- Unfolding: unfold the push bar, pull the base open until the release handles click, then check the locking pin is engaged. Rear wheels click onto the axle.
- The x:panda must be installed correctly or it can detach from the base.
