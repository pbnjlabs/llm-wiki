# Ethos User Instruction Manual (DCN0578.5)

Ki Mobility user instruction manual and warranty for the **Ethos** (DCN0578.5, 51 pages, English then Spanish). The wiki read the English half.

## Covers

- **Capacity** (user plus everything carried): Standard and transit **275 lb (125 kg)**.
- **Transit:** Transit option.
- Rigid chair with a **suspended seat frame on polymer elements** (ISO Tech polymers in the towers). Spare polymers of other densities ship with the chair.
- **Polymer chart (standard setup)** by user weight and center of gravity: 100–120 lb blue or red; 121–165 lb black or blue; 166–209 lb black; 210–275 lb green or black. A firm setup chart is also given. Keep the tape inside the tower in place when changing them.
- **Setting toe to zero** (only with 2°, 4°, 6°, 8° camber adapters; 0° plugs can't toe): loosen the two camber clamp bolts (5 mm), remove wheels, rotate the camber tube (forward = toe in, rearward = toe out), flats perpendicular to the ground.
- Warranty adds the **polymers** to the lifetime items.
- Spec sheet: 15 kg (33 lb), 680 mm wide, folded 910 × 600 × 570 mm.
- **Default torque:** fasteners not otherwise specified **40 in-lb (4.51 Nm)**. Don't overtighten frame hardware.
- **Ramps and slopes:** avoid anything over **9°** (about 2 in per foot), up, down or across.
- **Maintenance table:** weekly tire pressure and wheel locks; monthly axle sleeve nuts, quick-release axles, casters and wheels spin freely (clean hair from caster housings), tires, handrims, grips, armrest receivers, anti-tips level; frame inspection for cracks or bends, upholstery, cushions, spokes, all fasteners, rolls straight; **annual** check by a qualified technician at an authorized supplier.
- **Cleaning:** no petroleum solvents, degreasers or WD-40; sanitize with 70% alcohol, 15 min contact. Lubricate frame joints with Teflon-based lube only.
- **Troubleshooting chart:** pulls to one side, looseness, hard turning, caster float, caster flutter, squeaks or clicks, wobble. Fixes: equal correct tire pressure, tighten fasteners and spokes, check casters touch the ground, optimize center of gravity, check rear wheel and caster interference, Teflon lube, clear caster and bearing debris.
- **Warranty:** frame, polymers and quick-release axles for the life of the original purchaser; other Ki-made parts **1 year**; wear items (upholstery, tires, arm pads, tubes, armrests, grips) not covered; North America; void if the serial tag is removed or the chair is modified. Expected frame life 5 years. Dealer gets an RA from Ki Mobility (715-254-0991).
