# Rogue ALX and Little Wave Technical Manual (DCN0577.7)

Ki Mobility **dealer technical manual** for the Rogue ALX, Little Wave (69 pages), written for a qualified technician at an authorized Ki dealer (an ATP or trained clinician). It supplements the user manual. Procedures are mostly step photos; the wiki summarizes what's covered and the values in the text.

## Covers

- Chair measurements, caster mount, forks and stems, **caster angle and squaring**, **camber mount assembly** (Rogue ALX and Little Wave separately), camber tube and adapters, **center of gravity**, **toe to zero**, quick-release axle, backrest, back posts, stroller and push handles, armrests, cane holder, luggage, handrims, seat pan and upholstery, side guards, transit, footrests (incl. high mount for 10–14 in and 15–19 in widths), **Little Wave frame width**, anti-tips, **Little Wave 5th wheel**, wheel locks, drum brake, **caster flutter**, tipping lever, EZ grip, transit wheel.
- Torques found: two 5 mm retaining screws **80 in-lb**; screws tightened in a balanced way, one to **50 in-lb** then the last to **80 in-lb**; set screws **40 in-lb**; grip collar 50 in-lb minimum.
- Spoke tension: one-arm-drive, Maxx and low-cost spokes 60–100 kgf; drum-brake wheels 90–120 kgf radial side, 60–90 kgf crossed side; Superlight 92–112 kgf.
