# Ethos Technical Manual (DCN0407.12)

Ki Mobility **dealer technical manual** for the Ethos (69 pages), written for a qualified technician at an authorized Ki dealer (an ATP or trained clinician). It supplements the user manual. Procedures are mostly step photos; the wiki summarizes what's covered and the values in the text.

## Covers

- Calf strap mount, footrests (angle adjustable, flip-under, hybrid), footrest length, T-arm and swing-away arm, **seat frame replacement**, **base frame replacement**, **ISO Tech polymer replacement**, highlight kit, **center of gravity and caster position**, seat pan and sling, anti-tips, **camber and camber tube**, quick-release axle, **toe to zero**, caster adjustment, forks and stems, backrest, back posts, stroller and push handles, side guard, handrims, **front and rear seat height**, installing transit, wheel locks, tipping lever, EZ grip.
- Frame: rear base frame 1-1/4 in, front base frame 1 in, seat frame 1-1/8 in, camber tube 1-1/8 in.
- Torques found: set screws **40 in-lb**; grip collar 50 in-lb minimum.
- Spoke tension: one-arm-drive, Maxx and low-cost spokes 60–100 kgf; drum-brake wheels 90–120 kgf radial side, 60–90 kgf crossed side; Superlight 92–112 kgf.
