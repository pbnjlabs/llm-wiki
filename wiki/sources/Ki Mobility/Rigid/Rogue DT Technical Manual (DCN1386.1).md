# Rogue DT Technical Manual (DCN1386.1)

Ki Mobility **dealer technical manual** for the Rogue DT (53 pages), written for a qualified technician at an authorized Ki dealer (an ATP or trained clinician). It supplements the user manual. Procedures are mostly step photos; the wiki summarizes what's covered and the values in the text.

## Covers

- Backrest assembly (angle, relax position, height adjustable rigidizer bar), back post height, push handles (EZ grips, bolt-on, rigid stroller), **tower and camber tube** (camber tube clamp), anti-tips, **center of gravity**, **front and rear seat height**, **toe to zero**, wheelbase, rear wheels (spoke tension, handrim construction and tightening), wheel lock clamp and locks, **hub locks**, quick-release axle, casters (forks, stems, Frog Legs Phase II, single-sided fork), **squaring casters**, footrests (footplates, length, angle, wings, risers), armrests and side guards (direct and clamp-on receivers, composite, aluminum, carbon fender), seat pan and upholstery.
- Torques found: **30, 50, 70 and 100 in-lb** on various screws; a nut **35 ft-lb**; check the figure for each fastener.
- Tools include 11 and 16 mm wrenches, tape measure, level or T-square.
- Spoke tension: one-arm-drive, Maxx and low-cost spokes 60–100 kgf; drum-brake wheels 90–120 kgf radial side, 60–90 kgf crossed side; Superlight 92–112 kgf.
