# Rogue 2 and Rogue XP Technical Manual (DCN1032.7)

Ki Mobility **dealer technical manual** for the Rogue 2, Rogue XP (73 pages), written for a qualified technician at an authorized Ki dealer (an ATP or trained clinician). It supplements the user manual. Procedures are mostly step photos; the wiki summarizes what's covered and the values in the text.

## Covers

- Chair measurement references (Rogue 2 and XP), backrest height, caster mount, **caster forks and stems**, **caster angle and squaring**, **index towers and camber tube**, quick-release axle, backrest assemblies, back posts, stroller and push handles (XP), armrests, cane holder, luggage, handrims, seat pan and upholstery, side guards, transit, footrests (hybrid, angle adjustable, flip-under, high mount), XP frame width, anti-tips, **XP 5th wheel**, wheel locks and **reversing wheel lock**, **center of gravity**, **seat height**, **setting toe to zero**, **wheelbase width**, one-arm drive, **growing the Rogue XP in width**, tipping lever, drum brake (XP), EZ grip.
- Torques found: lower nut (B) **50 in-lb**; four bolts **50 in-lb**; tower clamp bolts (D) **70 in-lb**, tightened **in sequence 1-2-3-4**; set screws **40 in-lb**; **80 in-lb**; grip collar bolt (4 mm) 50 in-lb minimum.
- **Two revisions on file:** this page is the combined Rogue 2 / Rogue XP manual (DCN1032 rev 7, 2025-09-05, 73 pp.). `Rogue2_Tech_Manual.pdf` is the **older rev 5** (2024-11-19, 57 pp., Rogue 2 only) with the same procedures and torques; **use rev 7**.
- Frame tube 1-3/8 in, camber tube 1-1/8 in.
- Spoke tension: one-arm-drive, Maxx and low-cost spokes 60–100 kgf; drum-brake wheels 90–120 kgf radial side, 60–90 kgf crossed side; Superlight 92–112 kgf.
