# Focus Technical Manual (DCN0340.12)

Ki Mobility **dealer technical manual** for the Focus CR (145 pages), written for a qualified technician at an authorized Ki dealer (an ATP or trained clinician). It supplements the user manual. Procedures are mostly step photos; the wiki summarizes what's covered and the values in the text.

## Covers

- Seat height configuration charts (pp. 4–26), frame setup by depth, frame depth for backrest offset, axle, caster arm and fork references, quick-release axle, caster construction and caster/footplate compatibility, chair width, seat depth, **tilt mechanism installation** (p. 44), **rotary frame replacement** (p. 55), transit, anti-tips, back height and angle, reclining and dynamic backs, **center of gravity**, caster arm, wheel lock configurations, attendant foot lock, one-arm drive, drum brake, handrims, 4-way latch, armrests, bearing construction, IV holder, vent and battery tray, **power tilt installation** (p. 111), **power tilt motor replacement** (p. 119), **power tilt interface control box** (p. 122), contracture and multi-angle footrests, ELRs, canopy, ergo grip.
- Torques found: **120 in-lb**; pivot joint screws (E) and (F) **240 in-lb**; three screws (G) **144 in-lb**.
- Frame tubes: base frame 1-1/2 in, caster arm 1-3/8 in, seat frame 1 in. Tools include a 30 mm wrench.
- Spoke tension: one-arm-drive, Maxx and low-cost spokes 60–100 kgf; drum-brake wheels 90–120 kgf radial side, 60–90 kgf crossed side; Superlight 92–112 kgf.
