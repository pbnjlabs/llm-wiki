# Liberty Technical Manual (DCN0311.18)

Ki Mobility **dealer technical manual** for the Liberty (73 pages), written for a qualified technician at an authorized Ki dealer (an ATP or trained clinician). It supplements the user manual. Procedures are mostly step photos; the wiki summarizes what's covered and the values in the text.

## Covers

- Seat height (rear wheels, casters, caster position in fork, axle position), seat depth (backrest, axle plate), back height, seat width, **rigid, non-folding and converting to folding frame**, axle plate, quick-release axle, casters, fork and stem replacement, **wheel pull correction**, **adjusting and replacing gas springs**, gas spring trigger assembly, frame cover, fold lever replacement, depth, seat sling and seat rail, short back, **replacing tilt cables**, strap, installing transit, wheel locks, handrims, footrests and ELRs, 4-way latch, backrest angle, seat upholstery and pan, armrests, anti-tips, hemi wheel lock, drum brake, transit wheels, cane holder, tipping lever, ergo grip.
- Torques found: **80 ft-lb**; bottom nut (A) **55 ft-lb**; three screws (G) **144 in-lb**.
- The Liberty tilts on **gas springs** (unlike the Focus/CR45 rotary cog locks).
- Spoke tension: one-arm-drive, Maxx and low-cost spokes 60–100 kgf; drum-brake wheels 90–120 kgf radial side, 60–90 kgf crossed side; Superlight 92–112 kgf.
