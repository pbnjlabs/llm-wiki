# Focus CR User Instruction Manual (DCN0255.7)

Ki Mobility user instruction manual and warranty for the **Focus CR** (DCN0255.7, 62 pages, English then Spanish). The wiki read the English half.

## Covers

- **Capacity** (user plus everything carried): Standard **300 lb (136 kg)**, HD **400 lb (181 kg)**, Transit **300 lb**.
- **Transit:** Transit option.
- **Tilt-in-space** chair with reclining backrest option, vent and battery tray, oxygen holder, canopy, one-arm drive, IV holder, drum brake.
- **Trigger tilt:** squeeze, rotate the seat, release to lock. **Cable adjustment:** loosen the jam nut (10 mm and 3/8 in wrenches), thread the adjuster until the slack is gone but the cogs still seat fully in the rotary-frame teeth; it must lock at every position with no cog drag. Tighten the jam nut only.
- Never leave an occupied chair with the triggers pinned unlocked.
- Spec sheet: 25 kg (55 lb), 640 mm wide, turning radius 765 mm, static stability >12°.
- A power tilt kit is covered by [[Focus Power Tilt System User Instruction Manual (DCN1130.2)]].
- **Default torque:** fasteners not otherwise specified **40 in-lb (4.51 Nm)**. Don't overtighten frame hardware.
- **Ramps and slopes:** avoid anything over **9°** (about 2 in per foot), up, down or across.
- **Maintenance table:** weekly tire pressure and wheel locks; monthly axle sleeve nuts, quick-release axles, casters and wheels spin freely (clean hair from caster housings), tires, handrims, grips, armrest receivers, anti-tips level; frame inspection for cracks or bends, upholstery, cushions, spokes, all fasteners, rolls straight; **annual** check by a qualified technician at an authorized supplier.
- **Cleaning:** no petroleum solvents, degreasers or WD-40; sanitize with 70% alcohol, 15 min contact. Lubricate frame joints with Teflon-based lube only.
- **Troubleshooting chart:** pulls to one side, looseness, hard turning, caster float, caster flutter, squeaks or clicks, wobble. Fixes: equal correct tire pressure, tighten fasteners and spokes, check casters touch the ground, optimize center of gravity, check rear wheel and caster interference, Teflon lube, clear caster and bearing debris.
- **Warranty:** frame, hangers, tilt assembly (including bearings) and quick-release axles for the life of the original purchaser; other Ki-made parts **1 year**; wear items (upholstery, tires, arm pads, tubes, armrests, grips) not covered; North America; void if the serial tag is removed or the chair is modified. Expected frame life 5 years. Dealer gets an RA from Ki Mobility (715-254-0991).
