# Catalyst 4 User Instruction Manual (DCN0259.7)

Ki Mobility user instruction manual and warranty for the **Catalyst 4** (DCN0259.7, 49 pages, English then Spanish). The wiki read the English half.

## Covers

- **Capacity** (user plus everything carried): Standard **250 lb (113 kg)**, HD **350 lb (159 kg)**.
- **Transit:** **Not transit rated**: the manual has no transit section.
- Folding (cross-brace) chair; frame, cross braces, hangers and quick-release axles have the lifetime warranty.
- Sections: height adjustable T-arm, swing-away and flip-back armrests, swing-away hangers (incl. 4-way latch), extension tubes, ELR and Pro ELR, residual limb support, rear wheels, wheel locks, anti-tips, upholstery, oxygen tank holder, folding, cane and crutch holder, folding handle, removable seat pan.
- Spec sheet (as tested): 16 kg (35 lb), 810 mm overall width, folded 760 × 330 × 730 mm, turning radius 1460 mm.
- **Default torque:** fasteners not otherwise specified **40 in-lb (4.51 Nm)**. Don't overtighten frame hardware.
- **Ramps and slopes:** avoid anything over **9°** (about 2 in per foot), up, down or across.
- **Maintenance table:** weekly tire pressure and wheel locks; monthly axle sleeve nuts, quick-release axles, casters and wheels spin freely (clean hair from caster housings), tires, handrims, grips, armrest receivers, anti-tips level; frame inspection for cracks or bends, upholstery, cushions, spokes, all fasteners, rolls straight; **annual** check by a qualified technician at an authorized supplier.
- **Cleaning:** no petroleum solvents, degreasers or WD-40; sanitize with 70% alcohol, 15 min contact. Lubricate frame joints with Teflon-based lube only.
- **Troubleshooting chart:** pulls to one side, looseness, hard turning, caster float, caster flutter, squeaks or clicks, wobble. Fixes: equal correct tire pressure, tighten fasteners and spokes, check casters touch the ground, optimize center of gravity, check rear wheel and caster interference, Teflon lube, clear caster and bearing debris.
- **Warranty:** frame, cross braces, hangers and quick-release axles for the life of the original purchaser; other Ki-made parts **1 year**; wear items (upholstery, tires, arm pads, tubes, armrests, grips) not covered; North America; void if the serial tag is removed or the chair is modified. Expected frame life 5 years. Dealer gets an RA from Ki Mobility (715-254-0991).
