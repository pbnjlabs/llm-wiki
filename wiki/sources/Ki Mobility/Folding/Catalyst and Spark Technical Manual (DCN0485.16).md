# Catalyst and Spark Technical Manual (DCN0485.16)

Ki Mobility **dealer technical manual** for the Catalyst 4, 5, 5Vx, Spark (123 pages), written for a qualified technician at an authorized Ki dealer (an ATP or trained clinician). It supplements the user manual. Procedures are mostly step photos; the wiki summarizes what's covered and the values in the text.

## Covers

- Tools, frame tube sizes, spoke tension, front seat height, Spark and Catalyst 5 depth and width tables, Spark frame width, depth adjustment, fork and stem, **caster squaring**, 4-way latch, armrests (T-arm, flip-back, swing-away, tubular flip-up on Spark), canopy, multi-angle footrest, pediatric Pro ELR, ELR and Pro ELR, hangers, footrest length, **transit and caster-fork transit covers**, anti-tips, transit wheels (5Vx), side guards, half-folding, angle adjustable, reclining (Catalyst 5), depth adjustable and dynamic backs, stroller handles, EZ grip, **universal, amputee (Catalyst 5) and vertical axle plates**, quick-release axle adjustment, cane holder, IV, luggage, O2, handrims, footplates, seat upholstery and pan, **one-arm drive**, **chair tracking/pulling correction**, wheel locks and attendant locks (4E/E), drum brake, **HD configuration**, back height, Catalyst 4E/E width and depth change, folding handle, hemi wheel lock, tipping lever, **replacing cross tubes**.
- Torques found in the text: bottom nut **55 ft-lb**; three screws (G) **144 in-lb**; a bolt **8 ft-lb**; an outside nut **35–41 ft-lb**; master/follower lock assemblies to wheel lock bars **50 in-lb**. The procedures are mostly photos, so check the figure for which fastener each value belongs to.
- Tools: 2.5–6 mm Allen, 8–24 mm wrenches, utility blade, Phillips, rubber mallet, **calibrated torque wrench**.
- Spoke tension: one-arm-drive, Maxx and low-cost spokes 60–100 kgf; drum-brake wheels 90–120 kgf radial side, 60–90 kgf crossed side; Superlight 92–112 kgf.
