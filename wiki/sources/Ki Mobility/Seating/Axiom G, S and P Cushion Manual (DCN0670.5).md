# Axiom G, S and P Cushion Manual (DCN0670.5)

Ki Mobility user instruction manual and warranty for the **Axiom-G, Axiom-S, Axiom-P** (DCN0670.5, 17 pages, English then Spanish). The wiki read the English half.

## Covers

- **Capacity** (user plus everything carried): **350 lb (158 kg)**.
- **Transit:** —.
- Axiom-G, -S and -P **foam** cushions.
- Outer cover: machine wash warm, tumble dry no heat, don't iron. Foam base: damp cloth only, air dry.
- Warranty 24 months (cushion), 6 months (covers).
