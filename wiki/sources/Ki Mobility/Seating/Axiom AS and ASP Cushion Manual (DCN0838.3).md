# Axiom AS and ASP Cushion Manual (DCN0838.3)

Ki Mobility user instruction manual and warranty for the **Axiom AS, Axiom ASP** (DCN0838.3, 16 pages, English then Spanish). The wiki read the English half.

## Covers

- **Capacity** (user plus everything carried): **350 lb (158 kg)**.
- **Transit:** —.
- Axiom AS and ASP **cushions** (foam base with fluid pads). For postural support, pressure distribution and sitting tolerance.
- Cleaning: covers per label; wipe the foam base with a damp cloth (don't spray or soak), air dry out of sunlight (24 h to days), confirm dry before use; sanitize without immersing.
- Warranty: base and fluid pads **24 months**; covers 6 months; not punctures, tears or burns.
