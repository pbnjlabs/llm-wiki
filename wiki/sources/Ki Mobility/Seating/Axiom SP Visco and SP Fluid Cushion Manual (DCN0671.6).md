# Axiom SP Visco and SP Fluid Cushion Manual (DCN0671.6)

Ki Mobility user instruction manual and warranty for the **Axiom SP Visco, SP Fluid** (DCN0671.6, 20 pages, English then Spanish). The wiki read the English half.

## Covers

- **Capacity** (user plus everything carried): **350 lb (158 kg)**.
- **Transit:** —.
- Axiom SP Visco and SP Fluid cushions. Includes a **bottoming-out** check for the SP Fluid.
- Align the fluid pad's hook-and-loop with the inner cover; a misaligned edge makes a pressure point.
- Warranty: SP base, fluid pad and Visco base **24 months**; covers 6 months.
