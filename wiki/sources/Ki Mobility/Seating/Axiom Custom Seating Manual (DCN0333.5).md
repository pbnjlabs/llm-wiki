# Axiom Custom Seating Manual (DCN0333.5)

Ki Mobility user instruction manual and warranty for the **Axiom Custom Seating** (DCN0333.5, 22 pages, English then Spanish). The wiki read the English half.

## Covers

- **Capacity** (user plus everything carried): per the wheelchair.
- **Transit:** Transit use section; transit 2-point and 4-point knob hardware packages.
- Axiom **custom** seating systems: hardware and installation, removing and reinstalling seating from the frame, lateral trunk and thigh supports.
- Tighten all fasteners after any adjustment before use.
- Warranty: hardware **5 years**, custom seating 1 year, upholstery covers 6 months.
