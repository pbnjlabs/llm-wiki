# Axiom Back User Instruction Manual (DCN0672.8)

Ki Mobility user instruction manual and warranty for the **Axiom Back** (DCN0672.8, 22 pages, English then Spanish). The wiki read the English half.

## Covers

- **Capacity** (user plus everything carried): **300 lb (136 kg)** with DEX or FXT hardware; **400 lb (181 kg)** with BTX hardware.
- **Transit:** Axiom Back transit safety section.
- Ki's **Axiom** backrests: posterior (PB), posterior lateral (PL), lateral (LB) and deep lateral (DL) shells, low, mid and tall; cover over foam on a base.
- Sections: pushing or lifting (not by the back), transit safety, maintenance, cleaning, sanitizing, reassembly after cleaning.
- Warranty **24 months** (back) and **6 months** (covers).
