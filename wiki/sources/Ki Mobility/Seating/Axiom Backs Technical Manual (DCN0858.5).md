# Axiom Backs Technical Manual (DCN0858.5)

Ki Mobility **dealer technical manual** for the Axiom Back (43 pages), written for a qualified technician at an authorized Ki dealer (an ATP or trained clinician). It supplements the user manual. Procedures are mostly step photos; the wiki summarizes what's covered and the values in the text.

## Covers

- Axiom back product weights and sizing: posterior (AXPB), posterior lateral and lateral (AXPL, AXLB), deep lateral (AXDL), and Axiom for Kids (PXPL, PXAL, PXALC).
- Lateral supports (Omnilink swing-away; custom back PL and PB laterals flush, 1 in and 2 in offset, fixed or swing-away), contour lateral wings, pediatric trunk and pelvic laterals, shoulder strap guides, **headrests** (standard straight, multi-axis, POSAlinc), universal mounting plate.
- **Backrest hardware:** DEX, BTX and FXT hardware and pediatric hardware, each with general setup and fitting adjustments. (BTX is the 400 lb hardware.)
