# MedMizer AC GOV Hospital Bed Training

**Performance Medical Solutions'** training packet for handling
**MedMizer AC GOV Basic and Advanced hospital beds**. Eight scanned
pages: 4 pages of PMS training and sign-off, then 4 pages of MedMizer's
own "AC GOV Bed: Separating for Transport" sheets. House practice plus
manufacturer instructions. MedMizer's phone number is left out.

## Rules (pages 1–2)

- **Everyone who unloads, disassembles, loads or reassembles** these
  beds must **read all the manuals and watch all the videos** first.
- **A Basic or Advanced bed must never be lifted or moved by one
  technician.**
- **Two technicians load and unload each piece.** **Strap the pieces**
  for transport so they don't damage the bed or the van.
- Use sound judgment on whether a location is safe to install in; **if
  it's unsafe, call your supervisor before going ahead**.
- Wear **steel-toed boots or shoes** and company-approved attire.
- Each van carries a **transport (furniture) blanket** to protect floors;
  ask the office manager for one.

## Separating the bed for transport (MedMizer, pages 5–8)

Main parts: **two frame pivot bolts**, the **control box**, and a
**black bolt**.

**To split the bed:**

1. Find the **control box** on the inside of the stationary seat section
   and head section frame. From the top, push a **small screwdriver**
   into the **two open slots** to release the cover.
2. **Unplug the cables from ports 4 and 3.** Refit the cover and let the
   unplugged cables hang free.
3. Place the bed **1–2 in from a wall** and **lock the casters**.
4. Remove the **black bolt** joining the two frames, on the **bottom,
   one side only**.
5. Remove the **bolt, nut and spacer on the top side of each frame**.
   **Put them in the hardware bag**; they're needed for reassembly.
6. After the second bolt comes out, **lean the two frames back toward the
   wall** and make sure they're secure.

**In-home setup of the split frame:**

1. Lean the sections against the wall and **reinstall the bolt, plastic
   washer and nut**: **bolt head on the outside** of the frame, thread and
   nut on the inside.
2. Remove the control box cover, **plug the cables back into ports 3 and
   4**, and refit the cover.
3. **Lay the bed on its side**, protecting the floor, with the casters
   locked.
4. Continue with the **installation video from step 12**.
