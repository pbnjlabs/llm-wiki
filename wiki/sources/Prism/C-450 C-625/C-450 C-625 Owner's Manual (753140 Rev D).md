# C-450 C-625 Owner's Manual (753140 Rev D)

22-page **Handicare** user manual for the **C-450 (450 lb / 204 kg) and C-625
(625 lb / 284 kg)** ceiling lifts (753140 Rev D, 2022-08-15, 2nd edition). It
**confirms Handicare as the maker** of the C-Series. Complements the
[[C-450 C-625 Technical Manual]].

## Covers

- Install and service only by Handicare-authorized and trained people; charger
  outside the patient vicinity; decommission after **10 years**.
- **Charging:** 5 h before first use; leave on charge; end-point or **constant
  charging** strips (not with Return-To-Charge). Orange = charging, green = done.
  **Low battery** (<25%): orange, beep every 10 s on a button press, "Low Batt!",
  usable after 1 h charge. **Depleted:** red, "UP: INHIBITED!" (down, emergency
  down and traverse still work). Supplied charger only. Not for tracks with lip
  separation under 13.5 mm.
- **Hand control** (pneumatic): align the ribbed pins and grey rib in the
  grommet; test; older carry bar 360760 needs hook 635603.
- Turn on by any button; auto-off after ~5 s.
- Transfers, X-Y traversing beam operation (black/white buttons move the beam,
  blue/yellow move the lift).
- **LCD:** battery % or lift count; **PM alert after 1000 lifts or 5 working hours**
  (as printed) beeps every 5 s and shows "Maintenance". **Reset:** lift off, hold
  Up + Down until "Tech-Prog", release, press Up until "Done".
- **Emergency:** pull the red cord once = stop (push the white tab back to reset);
  **pull and hold = powered emergency lowering**; manual lowering with the
  **4 mm hex key** in the motor shaft behind the side clip, clockwise. Inspect
  after any emergency lowering.
- Clean the case and carry bar with **isopropyl alcohol** only; strap with Virox
  Accel TB RTU; **no chlorine**.
- Quick-release hook (red latch), **Return To Charge** (hold blue + yellow 3–5 s;
  stops on 15–30 lb load change, 35–50 lb overload), carry bars (QRS or direct,
  bull horn or spring latch; 360931, 360935, 360980–360982).
- **Inspection:** each use (strap, stitching, sling, cord, buttons, carry bar,
  noises, end stops); monthly (runs freely); **semi-annually** in high use (>1500
  lifts/yr) or heavy users (e-stop and lowering, strap fully out, carry bar pin,
  upper and lower limits, charging, LEDs, case); **annually** by a Handicare-
  authorized technician.
- **Specs:** 24 V motors; charger Soneil 2403SRM20 (alternates 2403SRM30 or Mascot
  9940 only; **not the A450/A625 charger**); **2 × 12 V 5 Ah SLA**; IP22; lift range
  96 in; 21–23.5 lb; duty 1 min on / 9 off; **service life 22,500 cycles or 10
  years; replace the strap every 5000 cycles or 3 years**; 30–40 lifts at 625 lb,
  50–60 at 450 lb per charge after break-in.
- Warranty: new equipment 1 year from install or 18 months from manufacture,
  refurbished 3 months.
