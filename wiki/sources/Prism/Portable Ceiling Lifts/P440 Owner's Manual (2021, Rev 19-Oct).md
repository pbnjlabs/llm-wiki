# P440 Owner's Manual (2021, Rev 19 Oct)

P/N 753001, Rev: 19 Oct 2021. A newer, more complete revision of
[[P440 User Manual]] (2017) — same product (SKUs 303070 standard-hook,
303073 extended-hook; 440lb capacity; pneumatic piston-displacement hand
control), but **explicitly and repeatedly branded "Handicare"**
throughout (product photos show a "handicare P-440" nameplate on the
lift itself) rather than staying silent on manufacturer identity the
way the 2017 manual did.

## Resolves an old open question
[[C-450-C-625]]'s page has long flagged that its
[[Ceiling Lift Evaluation Form]] uses a "Handicare" brand component
legend in its example track diagram, without explanation. This manual
confirms why: **Handicare is the actual manufacturer**, and its own
Warranty section (below) spells out the Handicare/Prism relationship
explicitly for the first time in this wiki. See [[Prism]] for the full
write-up.

## New content not in the 2017 manual
- **Lift Accessories** catalog: track (fixed lengths and TrackPlus),
  45°/90° curves, Transgate system, Multi-Port and Quick Fit turntable
  systems, track end stops, "H" system trolley set, wall mount/3"/6"
  track brackets, a reacher pole, and custom slings — "only slings
  authorized by Handicare are to be used with this lift"
- **Carabiner type varies by manufacturing year** — three different
  carabiner styles shown as all being valid depending on when the unit
  was made; correct vs. incorrect orientation photos included (a
  strap loop resting on the latch, rather than seated at the hook base,
  can slide off under load — serious injury/death risk per the manual's
  own caution)
- **Hooks can be turned upward** for transport/storage (both standard
  and extended-hook variants shown)
- **Service Record History** — blank purchase-info and repeated
  service-log forms for the dealer/technician to fill in
- Four documented ways to attach the lift hook to the track: direct to
  trolley, via a reacher, via a lanyard, or lanyard + reacher combined

## Warranty (definitive Handicare/Prism split)
1. **Handicare** guarantees all new equipment (ceiling lifts, floor
   lifts, slings, service parts, track/accessories) for **1 year from
   installation or 18 months from manufacture, whichever is shorter**
2. **Prism** guarantees all *refurbished* equipment for **3 months from
   installation or 6 months from purchase, whichever is shorter**
3. Void if serviced by anyone other than Handicare or an authorized
   agent
4. Contact: Handicare USA, Inc. (10888 Metro Court, St. Louis, MO 63043;
   314-692-9135) or Handicare Canada (81 Romina Drive, Concord, ON
   L4K 4Z9; 877-304-5438)
