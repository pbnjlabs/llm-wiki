# P440 User Manual

Rev: 14 MAR 2017. Owner's manual for the **P-440**, a **portable**
ceiling lift — Prism's first documented product distinct from the
permanently track-mounted [[C-450-C-625]] family. Unlike C-450/C-625
(fixed position, electro-mechanical powered traverse), the P-440 is
carried by hand and manually pushed along the track; only the up/down
lifting function is motorized.

## Key content
- Four components make up the system: lift (P-440), track, trolley,
  sling — track/trolley/sling ship with the lift at initial purchase
- Pneumatic (piston-displacement airline) hand control, same control
  technology family as C-450/C-625's air-switch PCB, but here it only
  drives up/down — no powered traverse
- Two model SKUs: **303070** (standard hooks) and **303073** (extended
  hook) — hooks can also be manually flipped upward for storage/transport
- Max load 440 lbs, 24VDC motor, 2×12VDC 2.3Ah batteries, auto-off after
  ~2 min idle, internal 15A fuse, duty cycle 1 min ON / 9 min OFF
- Emergency lowering via a dedicated red button (2-second delay, audible
  alarm, no raising function) if both the hand-control DOWN and the
  lift's own DOWN button fail; separate red Emergency Stop switch cuts
  all motor power and must be inspected by an authorized dealer before
  being reset to ON
- Mounts to the trolley eyelet four ways: direct connection, via a
  reacher arm, via a lanyard, or lanyard+reacher combined — accommodates
  different ceiling/track heights without a height-adjustable lift body
- Cleaning: isopropyl alcohol only, never submerge/expose to liquids
  (not drip-proof)
- **Warranty differs from C-450/C-625** (no warranty terms documented on
  that page): 1 year from install date or 18 months from manufacture
  date, whichever is shorter (3 months/6 months for refurbished units);
  void if serviced by anyone other than Prism Medical or an authorized
  agent; liability limited to parts replacement, no consequential-damage
  coverage
