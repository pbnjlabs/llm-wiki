# Ceiling Lift Evaluation Form

4-page blank pre-install site-survey form (Performance Medical Supply's own
form, not a Prism-authored document) used to assess a home before a ceiling
lift install. Not model-specific — generic to ceiling-lift installs, not
tied to the C-450/C-625 specifically.

- **Patient section**: name, weight/height, amputee status (above/below
  knee, one leg/both) — affects lift capacity/sling selection, not detailed
  further on the form itself.
- **Lift purpose**: bed-to-chair only vs. bed-to-chair-and-bathtub vs.
  other — determines track routing/room coverage needed.
- **Site-built home path**: checks for attic space directly above the
  install room(s) and whether another room sits above (relevant for
  ceiling-track mounting into joists from above vs. below).
- **Mobile home path**: different wall/ceiling-height requirements —
  transportable motors with wall-mounted tracks instead of standard
  ceiling track, since mobile-home ceiling joists can't always take the
  same mounting hardware. Requires locating and confirming attic access.
- **Room sketch + example diagrams**: hand-drawn joist-direction and room
  dimension examples (bathroom layout, bedroom layout), plus a
  professionally-drafted example track layout drawing with a **Handicare**
  brand component legend (3"/6" brackets, wall mount bracket, standard/
  super track, docking station, quickport/multiport turntables, transition
  gate) — notable: the legend is Handicare-branded, not Prism, suggesting
  Performance Medical Supply's ceiling-lift site-survey process/track
  hardware isn't exclusively tied to the Prism C-450/C-625 product line.
  Worth keeping in mind if/when a second ceiling-lift brand is ingested.
- Ends with a homeowner signature/date line — this is a customer-facing
  document used during a sales/install visit, not an internal repair
  reference.
