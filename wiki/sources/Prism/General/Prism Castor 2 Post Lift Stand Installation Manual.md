# Prism Castor 2 Post Lift Stand Installation Manual

10 pages, Rev 03/13/2018. Co-branded **"Prism"** (title) and
**"Handicare"** (logo) — a portable, freestanding two-post track stand
that lets a Prism/Handicare ceiling lift ([[C-450-C-625]], [[P440]])
operate without permanent ceiling mounting. Confirms the Handicare/
Prism brand relationship independently of
[[P440 Owner's Manual (2021, Rev 19-Oct)]] — see [[Prism]] for the full
picture.

## Key content
- Three part numbers, matching [[C-450-C-625]]'s capacity naming
  directly: **343100** "Regular Track" (82in, 204kg/450lb load — matches
  "C-450"), **343101** "Track Plus" (197in, 204kg/450lb), **343102**
  "Track Plus" (156in, 283.5kg/625lb — matches "C-625")
- Aluminum construction (post bases, vertical supports, track), max
  height 244cm (99in) floor to highest point, max unit weight 50kg with
  no single piece over 15.5kg
- Adjustable system dimensions (height 204-250cm, length up to 530cm
  depending on track length) via telescoping vertical supports and
  castors for repositioning
- No user-serviceable parts — opening the unit voids the warranty;
  install/assembly restricted to trained operators per Prism Medical
  Ltd. authorization
- Torque-specified assembly (25-35 N·m on vertical-support-to-bracket
  bolts, 4-5.5 N·m on track-bracket set screws) with a bracket-to-track
  slide-on connection
- Installation checklist covers defect-free inspection, torque
  verification, level-floor confirmation, and scheduling annual
  maintenance checks — but does not itself specify the required load
  test procedure (references "the Required load test" without detailing
  it in this document)
