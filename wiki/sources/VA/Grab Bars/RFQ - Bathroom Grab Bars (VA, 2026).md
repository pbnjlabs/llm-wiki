# RFQ: Supply & Installation of ADA-Compliant Bathroom Grab Bars (VA)

A **VA request for quote**, not a manufacturer manual. Issued **26 May
2026**, version 2.0, **quote deadline 9 June 2026**. One page, scanned
upside down, marked "Page 2 of 2" although it starts with the title, so
it may be the whole RFQ. Filed under the issuing body (like the
Standards documents, e.g. [[ADA Technical Guide - Ramps and Curb Ramps]]);
there's no manufacturer page.

**Left out on purpose:** the VA requester's name, email and phone
number. Look them up on the raw PDF.

## 1. Scope of work

- **Install** ADA-compliant bathroom grab bars **provided by PSAS**
  (VA Prosthetic and Sensory Aids Service). The vendor installs; VA
  supplies the bars.
- Sizes: **12, 18, 24 and 32 in**, provided by VA Prosthetics.
- Locations: **shower and toilet**.
- Substrates: **ceramic tile and fiberglass**.
- Load rating: price for a **max weight of 350 lb**.
- Finish: rust-proof material.
- **ADA compliance required.**
- Warranty: **at least 2 years on labor**, from the vendor.

## 2. Site visit

- Not required before quoting.
- PSAS provides a **"1090"** (VA form) for each job. The vendor may
  visit to check conditions, substrates and mounting locations.
- The address goes only to shortlisted vendors.
- **Before and after photos** recommended.

## 3. Deliverables

- Proof of insurance (COI)
- References from similar projects (initial application)
- **Detailed methods for blocking, ceramic tile and fiberglass
  substrates**
- Warranty terms (minimum 2 years)
- Project timeline from notice to proceed to completion
- Payment terms: payment once the invoice is received and the project
  is complete

## 4. Questions the vendor must answer

1. Warranty on materials and installation
2. Service area
3. Surface finishing provided
4. **Mounting method in ceramic tile and fiberglass**
5. **How proper blocking or reinforcement is ensured**
6. Lead time for materials and installation
7. How the veteran contacts the vendor if there are issues

## 5. Quote submission

To the VA requester by the deadline (contact details omitted).

## 6. Acceptance and award

Scored on completeness, pricing, product quality, installation approach,
experience, references, warranty, and ability to meet the timeline.
Awarded to the vendor giving **the best overall value to the veterans**.
