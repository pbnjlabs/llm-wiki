# VA Form 10-0103: HISA Application

**"Veterans Application for Assistance in Acquiring Home Improvements
and Structural Alterations"** (HISA). VA Form 10-0103, **March 2025**
edition, OMB 2900-0188 (expires 30 June 2027). One page, blank. The raw
file is named `HISSA 100103.pdf`. Filed under the issuing body.

HISA is the VA benefit for **home improvements and structural
alterations**. **The veteran fills in and signs this
form** and submits it to the VA health care facility where they get
care. It isn't sent by PMS.

## What's on the form

- Previous HISA application? (date if yes)
- Veteran name, SSN, VA file number, address, phone (customer details;
  never copied into the wiki)
- **Item 6: the person or firm the veteran got a satisfactory bid
  from**, with a **signed copy of the bid and the plans and
  specifications attached**. This is where a PMS quote comes in.
- **Advance payment:** the veteran can ask for **50% of the authorized
  benefit up front** (38 CFR 17.3130(a)).
- Signature and date.

## Certification points relevant to PMS

- HISA is a **lifetime benefit with a cost limit**, across all
  projects combined. When it runs out, it's gone.
- If a project costs more than the veteran's **remaining benefit**, the
  **veteran pays the difference** or the benefit isn't authorized.
- The **VA takes no responsibility** for maintenance, repair or
  replacement, and gives **no warranty** on installed equipment.
  Warranty and service come from the installer and manufacturer.
- US states, territories, DC and Puerto Rico only.

## At the end of the job

The veteran signs a **HISA statement of satisfaction and request for
final payment**, with the final invoice and color photos attached, and
the installer signs a compliance statement. See
[[PMS Porch Lift Install Instructions and HISA Final Payment]].
