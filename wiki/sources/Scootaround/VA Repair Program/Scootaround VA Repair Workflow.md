# Scootaround VA Repair Workflow

How PMS handles **mobility device repairs for Veterans Affairs** that
come through **Scootaround**. Four scanned pages: Scootaround's repair
process and sample quote, plus PMS's handwritten notes and a sample PMS
invoice. Not a manual. The sample quote is dated 30 Jan 2024; the PMS
invoice sample is dated 18 Feb 2025.

**Left out on purpose:** phone numbers, and the sample invoice's
placeholder veteran name and address.

## Repair process (p. 1, Scootaround)

1. **Call from Scootaround** to discuss the scope of work and repairs.
2. **Approval work order.** If PMS accepts the job, a work order arrives
   by email within 30 minutes of the call.
   PMS note: **print it twice and pin the email.**
3. **Work authorization and initial assessment.** The work order
   authorizes PMS to book an appointment with the veteran, assess the
   equipment and decide what repairs are needed.
4. **Quote generation.** Send the quote to Scootaround **by replying to
   the initial authorization email.**
5. **Quote approval.** Scootaround emails a **purchase order**, usually
   within 30 minutes.
6. **Repair.** The purchase order authorizes the work. Do only what's
   on the PO. Extra parts or labor go to step 7.
7. **Additional repairs (optional).** Send an updated quote as in
   step 4; an amended PO follows.
8. **Completion.** **Reply to the purchase order email** when the
   repair is done; Scootaround then pays per the PO.
   PMS note: include **"repair complete", the tech's name and the repair
   date.**

## Quote rules (p. 2, Scootaround sample quote)

- Reference the **Scootaround work order number**.
- Give the equipment **make, model and serial number** as assessed.
- List each part with the **manufacturer's part number**.
- Bill **assessment, one-way travel and repair labor as separate
  lines**.
- Always bill at the **contracted shop rate, in 15-minute increments**.
  (The sample uses $87/hr as an example rate.)
- **No tax:** VA work is tax exempt. See the contract-supplied **E595-E**
  form.
- Always reply to the original Scootaround email for the case, so the
  quote is tied to it.

## Parts pricing (p. 3, PMS handwritten notes)

- **Part price billed = MSRP × 0.75.** PMS makes **75% of MSRP**;
  Scootaround gets **25% off MSRP**.
- **Golden Technologies parts: 2 × what PMS is charged.**
- The handwritten "70% of MSRP" at the top of the page is a slip;
  **75% is correct** (confirmed by the human, 2026-09-29).

## Sample PMS invoice (p. 4)

Billed to Scootaround Inc., with these fields: Veteran ID, **P.O. number
= the work order #**, technician initials, serial number, equipment.

| Line | How it's billed |
|---|---|
| Equipment | Manufacturer, model and serial (sample: Pride Jazzy Sport) |
| Assessment labor | **$80/hr, 15-min increments of $20** |
| Travel labor | **$80/hr, 30-min increments ($40)** |
| Repair labor | **$80/hr, 15-min increments** |
| Parts | Part # and **MSRP × .75** (sample: $780 MSRP → $585) |
| Sales tax | 0% |

**Travel is billed in 30-minute increments** (confirmed by the human),
even though Scootaround's sample quote shows all labor in 15-minute
increments. Assessment and repair labor stay at 15 minutes.
