# Scootaround Work Order and Invoice Examples (2025)

Two one-page examples marked up by PMS. **Left out on purpose:** PMS
phone numbers. The invoice's "veteran" name and address are placeholders.

## Work order email (`scootaround_wo_ex.pdf`, 19 Feb 2025)

Marked "Ex: Work Order Email".

- Scootaround (VA Services, sfvaservices@scootaround.com) sends
  **"&lt;WO #&gt; – Initial Work Approved – Proceed with Assessment"** to
  PMS's Columbia inbox, with the work order attached.
- Its next steps: review the work order and contact the veteran to book
  the assessment; assess and prepare a quote; **reply to that email with
  the quote attached**. Repair photos are optional.
- A PO follows once the quote is approved. Changes go in as an updated
  quote.
- **PMS's reply in the example:** the work order had **no serial number**,
  so PMS replied to the same email asking for it. Check every work order
  for the serial number.

## Invoice (`scootaround_invoice_ex.pdf`, 18 Feb 2025)

Marked "Example". It's the same sample invoice as page 4 of
[[Scootaround VA Repair Workflow]]: billed to Scootaround Inc.; P.O.
number = work order #; tech initials; serial number. The lines are
assessment at $80/hr in 15-minute steps ($20), travel at $80/hr in
30-minute steps ($40), repair at $80/hr in 15-minute steps, and parts at
MSRP × .75 ($780 → $585). Sales tax 0%.
