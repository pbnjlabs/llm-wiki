## Contract update (25 Apr 2025)

- **The VA requires claims closed within 20 days.** Contact the veteran
  **within 24 hours** of getting the work order, and finish the
  evaluation **within 5–7 business days**.
- Report delays (parts, can't reach the veteran) promptly. **A claim can
  be taken back and given to another vendor.**
- Send quotes fast. Slow quotes can also get the claim reassigned.
- Veterans get customer-service surveys, so quality still counts.

## Vendor guide (pages 3–6)

- **Scope:** repairs to VA-issued **power chairs, manual chairs and
  scooters**. Not covered: other VA equipment (chair lifts, ramps,
  rollators), equipment the VA didn't issue or approve, **new add-on
  accessories**, damage that doesn't affect safe use, and smart drives
  or power assists.
- **The screening call** checks that the vendor has capacity, **an
  account to buy parts from the manufacturer**, can service that kind of
  device, and that the veteran is **within 2 hours**.
- **Timeline:** finish within **20 business days** of the work order.
  Contact the veteran within 24 h, **assess within 3 business days**,
  quote by **day 7**, and finish the repair by **day 20**.
- **Assessment:** in person and thorough (mechanical, electrical,
  seating), including watching the veteran use the device. **No
  tele-assessments or photo-only assessments.** **Never question the
  VA's prescribed device or settings.**
- **Quote lines:** work order number and veteran's name; make, model and
  serial; **one line per one-way trip**; assessment time; labor time; and
  each part with its **OEM part number and name**, priced per the pricing
  agreement.
- **Not billable:** round-trip travel, shipping, taxes (Scootaround is
  tax exempt), hazmat fees, shop supplies, flat "service call" rates,
  and aftermarket or generic parts Scootaround hasn't approved.
- **Parts:** **OEM only, never from third-party wholesalers.** The one
  exception is batteries: **MK, Interstate and Electro Battery** are
  approved.
- **Warranty:** always check the serial number with the manufacturer.
  Warranty parts go on the quote at **$0.00** with the OEM number, marked
  as warranty. **Labor and travel are still billable.**
- **Quote not approved** (the VA may replace the device instead): send a
  revised quote for **assessment and one-way travel only**. It's then
  closed and paid.
- **Cancelled after parts were bought:** the VA pays the **restocking
  fee**. Quote the assessment, one-way travel and restocking fee, and
  attach the manufacturer's invoice showing the fee.
- **Payment:** within **30 days** of the completion email. Don't resend
  anything unless asked.

## Conflicts

- **Assessment deadline:** 5–7 business days in the April 2025 email,
  3 business days in the vendor guide. Both put the quote at day 7 and
  the finish at day 20.
- **Tax-exempt form:** the guide says **E-959E**; Scootaround's sample
  quote says **E595-E**. Use whatever form came with the contract.
