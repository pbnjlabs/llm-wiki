# Open Sesame Mounting Instruction Sheets

Three stand-alone sheets (2020), each one the same as pages of the
[[Open Sesame Installation Guide]]:

| Sheet | Door | Key measurements |
|---|---|---|
| Door mount (2 pp) | Inward-opening | Operator on the door **3 in from the hinges (M133) or 4 in (M233)**. Arm shoe on the jamb **13 in from the hinges**. Arm at 90° with the door closed. Remove molding so it sits flush. |
| Lintel mount (1 p) | Outward-opening | Operator under the lintel, 3 in (M133) or 4 in (M233) from the hinges. Arm 13 in from the hinges, shoe **1⅜ in down** from the top of the door. |
| Outswing parallel (1 p) | Outward, wider opening; storm/screen doors | Operator on the door 4–6 in from the hinge side, arm parallel to the jamb (2.25 in). **Direction switch away from the hinges.** Commercial outward doors need the parallel-mount L bracket. |

**Don't mount the arm shoe too high above the operator.** The joints
bind and the screws work loose.
