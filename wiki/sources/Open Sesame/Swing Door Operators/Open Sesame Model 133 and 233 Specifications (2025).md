# Open Sesame Model 133 and 233 Specifications (2025)

Two one-page spec sheets (rev. 2025), written in architect's-spec style.
Specs are collected on [[Open-Sesame-Model-133]] and [[Open-Sesame-Model-233]].

## Where they differ

- **133:** the clutch disengages whenever the operator isn't running, so
  the door works by hand as if there were no operator. It has an optional
  auto-close mode.
- **233:** a constant-torque slipping-disc clutch (15 in-lb) is always
  engaged, so the operator works as a door closer with ADA-compatible
  closing force. It has an optional no-park mode and can run a gate if a
  rain shield is fitted.

## Where they're the same

- 24 V AC from a 20 VA plug-in transformer (115 V AC in). The operator
  takes 16–24 V AC/DC.
- 12 V 1.2 Ah sealed lead-acid backup battery.
- Strike output 12 V DC, 1 A. Opens up to 120°.
- 2⅝ × 4 × 14⅛ in, 10 lb, powder-coated steel.
- Door or lintel mount, left or right hand. Door mount needs at least
  1¼ in between the door and the ceiling.
- **Hold-open adjustable 5–50 s.** The install guide, owner's manual and
  M210 board sheet say 5–35 s, so go by the knob on the unit.
- 3 yr warranty / 10 yr limited warranty.
