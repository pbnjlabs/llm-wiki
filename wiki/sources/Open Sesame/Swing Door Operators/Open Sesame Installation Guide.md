# Open Sesame Installation Guide

Installer's guide for both swing-door operators, [[Open-Sesame-Model-133]]
and [[Open-Sesame-Model-233]]. 24 pages, undated (the PDF is from April 2020).

## Covers

- Tools and box contents (operator, wiring kit, wall transformer, remotes,
  strike and strike tester, screw pack, 4 mm Allen wrench, M233 clutch tool).
- **Choosing the mount** (door viewed from the inside): door mount for
  inward-opening doors, lintel mount for outward-opening, parallel mount for
  outward doors that need a wider opening, or storm/screen doors.
- **Left-hand conversion**: units ship set up for right-side hinges.
- Door, lintel and parallel mount steps (the same pages are also sold as
  separate sheets, see [[Open Sesame Mounting Instruction Sheets]]).
- Electric strike wiring (Adams Rite 7440, fail-secure).
- Wiring diagrams: residential; French door with one operator; double door
  with two operators and two strikes.
- Adjustment knobs, the M233 clutch, remote programming, a troubleshooting
  table, and Model 133 and 233 board diagrams.
