# Open Sesame M210 Circuit Board Sheet (2022)

One-page layout of the **new Model 210 circuit board** used in the
[[Open-Sesame-Model-233]] (May 2022). **Disconnect the battery before
removing or fitting the board.**

- Align the red paint mark with the magnet on the black disc.
- Direction switch: slide it toward the hinges, except on a parallel mount.
- 6-wire door cord. For a left-hand conversion, run the cord behind the
  board and out through the notch.
- **Blue pot:** opening angle (clockwise opens wider).
- **Yellow pot:** close delay, 5–35 s.
- **Blue jumper, speed:** off = slowest, top = slower, middle = slow,
  bottom = normal.
- **Red jumper, park:** both pins = park feature; one pin only = no park.
- **Black jumper:** both pins = normal operation. One pin = single
  operation (press once to open, it stays open until pressed again).
- Radio receiver trigger terminals BLU and BLK. Fuse F1.
