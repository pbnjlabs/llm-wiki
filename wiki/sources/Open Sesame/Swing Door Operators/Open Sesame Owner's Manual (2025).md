# Open Sesame Owner's Manual (2025)

Owner's manual for the swing-door operators. 20 pages, 2025. Leave it
with the customer next to the door.

## Covers

- **Keep the unit plugged in at all times** so the battery stays charged.
- The serial number is on the bottom of the operator and inside the cover.
- Removing the cover (two screws; open the door to reach the top one).
  When putting it back, seat the phone cable in its notch.
- Adjustment knobs and the auto-close jumper (Model 133 only).
- **Battery:** 12 V sealed lead-acid, MK ES1.2-12 or equivalent. It runs
  about **2 days** without power and lasts **3–5 years**. Test with the
  operator off: it should read **13–14 V DC**. Orange wire goes to +,
  black to −. **Reversing them damages the charging circuit.** Write the
  date on a new battery.
- Remote batteries (12V 23A, 2× CR2016, CR123, 9V) last about a year.
  Remotes have a range of up to 100 ft.
- Programming remotes, push pads and the touch pad, changing the keypad
  code, and erasing all remotes.
- **Guarantee and warranty:** 90-day satisfaction guarantee, plus 3 yr
  full / 10 yr limited (see [[Open Sesame]]).
- The same troubleshooting table as the install guide. Some fixes are
  marked "models prior to 2021".
