# Open Sesame Electric Strike Dimension Sheets

Four one-page dimension drawings (2020) for the electric strikes Open
Sesame sells. They're drawings only; for install steps, follow the
instructions packed with the strike.

| Strike | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Adams Rite 7440** | Standard strike in the Residential bundle. Supplied **fail-secure**, which is required. One black strike wire to green, the other to yellow. |
| **HES 1006** | Faceplate about 4⅞ × 1¼ in, body depth about 1-11/16 in. |
| **Trine 3234** | French door bundle. Wood or metal version (3234/3234W). |
| **Trine 3334** | Dimension sheet only; not listed on the survey forms. |

The strike runs on the operator's 12 V DC strike output (green −,
yellow +, up to 1 A). Test it with the strike tester plugged into the
RJ-11 jack, which should make the strike buzz.
