# Open Sesame Remote Control Programming (2025)

3-page programming sheet. **Check the antenna color first.** It's the
coiled wire behind the red and black buttons on top of the operator.

- **Green antenna (433 MHz, current units), page 1:**
  - Remotes: press the red program button for 1 s, then within 15 s hold
    the remote button for 3 s (or touch the touchpad).
  - **Keypad:** default code **1234**. To change it: `##` + old code, the
    new code, `1##`, wait, then `**`. To reset to 1234, disconnect the
    battery, hold any key while reconnecting, and keep holding for 5 s.
  - **Erase all remotes:** press the red button 5 times within 3 s.
  - Receiver light: 5 flashes = programmed remote, rapid blinking = remote
    not programmed, no flash = suspect the remote's battery.
- **Gray (390 MHz) or purple (315 MHz) antenna, older units, pages 2–3:**
  LiftMaster remotes (890MAX, 893MAX, 892LT, 371L, 81LM, 62LM) and keypads
  (377LMM, 387LM, 877MAX). Press their buttons 2× for purple or 4× for
  gray. To clear old programming, hold the red button for 10 s. Model 100
  operators sold before Sept 2012 need that erase first.
