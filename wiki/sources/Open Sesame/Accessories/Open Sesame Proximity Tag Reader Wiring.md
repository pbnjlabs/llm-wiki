# Open Sesame Proximity Tag Reader Wiring

One-page wiring diagram (2020) for Open Sesame's **Proximity Sensor
System**. It uses **AutoSlide's RFID reader**, the one in
[[RFID Pet Sensor Operation Instructions]], so the AutoSlide RFID manual
in `MANUALS/Open Sesame/` is there on purpose. The Residential "Autoslide"
bundle on the [[Open Sesame Field Survey Forms]] includes it, with 2 tags.

1. If the operator has a 4-wire door cord, replace it with the **6-wire
   cable**, which plugs into the RJ-12 jack. If you're also using Open
   Sesame remotes, leave the wires on the bottom 3-position terminal block.
2. The reader has two jacks: one on the back, and one under the cover
   (cut out the marked area to use it). With the back jack, the reader sits
   on the cable unless the cable runs inside the wall.
3. Note which end of the AutoSlide cable fits the reader. Cut the plug off
   the other end and connect the red, black and yellow wires to the RJ-12
   jack colors shown. **A cut long cable can't be used as an extension
   anymore.**
