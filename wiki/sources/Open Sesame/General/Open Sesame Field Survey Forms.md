# Open Sesame Field Survey Forms

Two 2-page blank forms (2023), **Residential Access** and **Public
Access**. Page 1 and the door questions are the same on both; only the
bundle choices differ. The checklist is on
[[Automatic-Door-Operators]].

## Door questions (both forms)

Hinge side and swing direction (looking from the inside), mount format,
application (interior, exterior, French, storm, screen), door location,
door and frame material, height and width, distance to the ceiling,
lintel clearance, door-to-wall distance at 90°, reveal, hinge type,
whether the closer can be reversed, strike needed, 115 V AC outlet
within 30 ft, French doors (one or both), the **pinky test**, weather
stripping, and dragging.

## Bundles

- **Residential:**
  - Residential bundle: M133, A-R 7440 strike, one remote, transformer and wiring kit.
  - "Autoslide" bundle: M133, A-R 7440, 6-wire kit, proximity sensor system with 2 tags.
  - French door bundle: M133, Trine 3234, one remote.
  - Screen/storm door: M233, long screw spacer, drop plate.
  - Model 133 operator only.
- **Public:**
  - Public bundle: M233 with battery backup and radio receiver, two 5 in wireless wall push pads, transformer with 30 ft cord and RJ-11 jack.
  - Public bundle special: two 36 in vertical push pads.
  - Screen/storm door, or Model 233 operator only.
- **Colors:** antique white, dark bronze, silver.
- **Accessories:** remotes (B) ECU, (C) keychain, (D) 1-button, (H) 2-button, (I) 4-button, (E) keyless entry pad; push pads (FL) large, (FN) narrow, (FT) touch pad, (FW) 36 in vertical; Tash Switch Mini-cup; Voice Interface; strikes; proximity sensor system.

Customer fields are blank on these copies.
