# U.S. Access Board Technical Guide: Ramps and Curb Ramps

19-page guide from the U.S. Access Board (July 2015; "Chapter 4:
Accessible Routes") explaining the **2010 ADA Standards** for ramps
(§405), handrails (§505) and curb ramps (§406), with section numbers
and dimensioned drawings. Filed in `MANUALS/Ramps/` but not an EZ-ACCESS
document. This is the **authoritative ADA reference** in the wiki. It
replaces the EZ-ACCESS blog summary
([[Understanding ADA Ramp Requirements]]) wherever the two differ.

## Covers

- pp. 1–2: when ramps are required (changes in level over ½ in; running
  slopes over 5 %), plus a summary drawing of every §405 requirement;
  slope and cross slope, clear width, rise per run.
- pp. 3–4: landings, turning landings, doors at landings.
- pp. 5–6: handrails (height, extensions, continuity, cross-section,
  clearance). Most of the dimensions are in the drawings only.
- p. 7: aisle ramps in assembly areas.
- p. 8: edge protection, wet conditions.
- pp. 9–16: curb ramps (running slope, flares, top landings, built-up,
  parallel, diagonal, islands, detectable warnings).
- pp. 17–19: common questions (no limit on the number of runs, curved
  ramps, **portable ramps**, handrail spacing, extensions).
