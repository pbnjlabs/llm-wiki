# Joint Commission DMEPOS Standards (effective 2026-09-01)

The Joint Commission's (formerly JCAHO) accreditation standards for
**Durable Medical Equipment, Prosthetics, Orthotics and Supplies
(DMEPOS)** organizations. Standards are effective **September 1, 2026**.
**PMS adheres to these standards** (per the human, 2026-10-12). See
[[Joint-Commission-DMEPOS]] for what they mean in the field.

**Raw copy.** The source is Joint Commission's public standards web
tool, not a PDF. On 2026-10-12 the full text was extracted from the
page's embedded data into a Markdown file:
`MANUALS/Standards/Joint Commission/Joint Commission DMEPOS Standards (eff 2026-09-01).md`.
The page HTML is saved next to it as a snapshot. The extract has
**139 standards and 487 elements of performance (EPs)**, verbatim, with
each EP's attributes (almost all are "Critical Change EP"; many also
need "Documentation"). The website shows only standard titles 10 at a
time, so use the extract. Re-extract when Joint Commission publishes a
new edition.

**Chapters** (number of standards): Accreditation Participation
Requirements APR (11), Leadership LD (24), Rights and Responsibilities
RI (10), Provision of Care PC (16), Record of Care RC (7), **Equipment
Management EQ (6)**, **Environment of Care EC (11)**, Emergency
Management EM (15), Human Resources HR (8), Infection Prevention IC (2),
Information Management IM (7), Medication Management MM (14), National
Patient Safety Goals NPSG (4), Performance Improvement PI (4).

The program also covers respiratory, infusion, oxygen, medication and
orthotics/prosthetics services. Many EPs apply only to those services or
only "for DMEPOS suppliers serving Medicare beneficiaries". The
[[Joint-Commission-DMEPOS]] page picks out the ones that touch PMS's
work (delivery, setup, repair, cleaning, storage, records, training).
