# K2 Plus Maintenance Control Program (001365)

12-page Savaria **Maintenance Control Program** for the K2 Plus (P/N 001365
Rev 000, 2024-09-25), per ASME A18.1, for certified elevating-device
maintenance personnel. Each unit's MCP should be adjusted to its site and use
and kept with the controller. Routine inspections **at least every 6
months**; periodic tests within 1, 3 or 5 years.

## Covers (all every 6 months)

- **Rail:** damage, cleaning, roller grooves, rail and bracket fixings,
  extension brackets, gear rack tight and pitched, top and bottom final
  limits, mechanical end stops, **charger output to the rail**, rubber end
  caps, loose wires and ground, copper bus strip and end connections, splice
  bars; flip-up rail hinge, its bracket feet and **gas spring** (rail must
  stay up when folded).
- **Carriage:** frame, battery support, rollers and bearings (lube angled
  and straight rollers with Super Lube or wet graphite), **overspeed brake
  test per bulletin 001277**, charging electrode pin contact over the whole
  travel, carriage bumper sensors, **seat post bolts 40 ft-lb (55 Nm)**,
  swivel plate and its screws (Loctite), seat cable, the three diagnostic
  LEDs, PCB and connections, motor temperature (normal operating range
  −10 to 40 °C), noise, footrest level and sensors, flip-up rail pushrod
  mechanism (clear synthetic grease).
- **Seat:** damage, armrest and seat fold hinges hold position, toggle
  switch, seat belt, e-stop, **swivel (wheel bearing grease)**, swivel pin
  locks, handles, seat level.
- **Remotes and hall calls:** function (light on when pressed), key switch,
  **9 V battery**, wall brackets.
