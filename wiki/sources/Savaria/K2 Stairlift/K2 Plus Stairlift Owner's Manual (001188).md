# K2 Plus Stairlift Owner's Manual (001188)

28-page **owner's manual** (part no. 001188, 30-m01-2024) for the
Savaria [[K2-Plus]]. Built to **ASME A18.1** (US), **CSA B355/B613**
(Canada) and **EN 81-40** / 2006/42/EC (Europe). Description, specs,
operation, flip-up rail, manual lowering, troubleshooting, LEDs and a
**full maintenance schedule** by commercial vs residential and duty.

## Specs

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Use | Residential indoor straight run; commercial where permitted |
| Capacity | **Residential 400 lb (182 kg); commercial 350 lb (159 kg); flip-up rail max 350 lb** |
| Travel | Max 32 ft (9.75 m), 15 ft/min, incline 30–55° |

| Charging | 110 V AC; full travel on the batteries in a power cut |
| Options | Savaria Link remote monitoring |
| Warranty | 36 months |

## Operation notes

- Key switch on the **bottom of the toggle-switch arm**; main power
  switch **on top of the carriage**. Don't switch it off after each
  trip.
- **Sleep mode:** idle **over an hour** = press the remote **twice** to
  wake it; idle **over 24 hours** = run it from the **arm toggle** for a
  second, then the remotes work.
- **Flip-up rail:** lower it by the top end until the rubber pad touches
  the floor, keeping the hinge clear, and **check the flip-up rack meshes**
  before running. A **push rod mechanism with a safety switch** stops
  downward travel while the flip-up is folded.
- **E-stop** on the left arm: standard on every **CSA B613** and
  **commercial** unit; optional on some residential.
- Not for carrying items, firefighting or fire evacuation; no unsupervised
  children under 16.

## Manual lowering

Main power **OFF** → remove the **manual lowering hole plug** on the
bottom of the carriage → handwheel into the hole onto the motor shaft →
**LH: clockwise = up; RH: clockwise = down** → call the dealer, leave the
power off.

## Troubleshooting and LEDs

Same owner table as the [[K2 Stairlift Owner's Manual (001022)]]: no
lights (charger, key/power, house breaker); all lights on (seat locked,
obstruction); **beeps ~20 s parked = not charging**; **runs to the end
and won't restart = low battery**. LEDs as on the [[K2]]. **B355 and B613
units have no diagnostic LEDs.** (The install guide says "B355 and
B316", most likely a misprint for B613.)

## Maintenance schedule

**Commercial: every item every 6 months.** **Residential: every year for
normal use, every 6 months for heavy or excessive use** (duty bands
above). Outdoor or harsh sites: more often. Warranty is void if a
non-Savaria-approved technician works on it.

- **Rail:** damage, cleaning, roller grooves, rail and bracket fixings, bracket extensions, gear rack tight and pitched, top and bottom final limits, mechanical end stops (T-nuts), **charger output to the rail**, rubber end caps, loose and ground wires, **bus line strip** (dents, dirt, insulated end connections), splice bars; flip-up hinge, brackets either side of the flip-up, **gas spring** (rail stays up when folded).
- **Carriage:** frame, battery support, rollers and bearings (lube angled and straight rollers with **Super Lube or wet graphite grease**), **overspeed brake per bulletin 001277**, charging electrode pin contacting the bus line along the whole run, side bumper sensors (adjust sensitivity), **seat post bolts 40 ft-lb (55 Nm)**, seat swivel plate and its screws (Loctite), seat connection wire, the 3 LEDs, PCB connections, motor temperature (−10 to 40 °C normal), noise, footrest level and its sensors and wire; flip-up push rod switch, bracket (Loctite), bending, and retraction (**clear synthetic grease**).
- **Seat:** seat, armrests and hinge, folding seat hinge, toggle switch, seat belt bolts, **e-stop**, swivel (**wheel bearing grease** on the seat post and swivel), swivel pin locks, swivel handles, seat level.
- **Remotes and hall calls:** light on press, keyed hall call switch, **9 V battery**, wall brackets.

**Compare:** the [[K2 Plus Maintenance Control Program (001365)]] (2024)
puts every item at 6 months (the ASME A18.1 MCP). The yearly residential
interval in this owner's manual is the minimum for normal home use; the
MCP still governs a public or code-inspected install.
