# K2 Stairlift Owner's Manual (001022)

16-page **owner's manual** (part no. 001022, 19-m02-2016) for the Savaria
[[K2]] straight-rail stairlift: the one the
[[K2 Stairlift Installation Guide (001021 Rev 047)]] says to hand the
customer. Owner-level only: description, specs, features, operation,
manual lowering, owner troubleshooting, LEDs, a maintenance schedule and
a maintenance record page.

## Specs

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Use | Residential, indoor, straight run |
| Capacity | 300 lb (136 kg); **"350 lb (159 kg) up to a maximum stair angle of 45°"** |
| Travel | Max 32 ft (9.75 m), 15 ft/min (0.08 m/s), incline 30–55° |
| Drive | Rack and pinion; 24 V DC, 1/3 HP, 2500 RPM motor |
| Width | 10¾ in folded, 20½ in unfolded |
| Charging | 110 V AC; charge stations at the top and bottom of the rail |
| Power failure | Full up and down travel on the batteries |
| Warranty | 36 months |

**Capacity conflict:** the newer install guide (2025) gives **300 lb**,
also with extension brackets or a flip-up rail, and doesn't mention 350
lb. Go by the **300 lb** in the install guide and on the unit's label.

## Operation and safety

- **Key switch** (¼ turn clockwise), then the **ON/OFF switch**. **Don't
  turn the power switch off after each trip**: that stops charging and
  drains the batteries.
- Seat swivels **one way only**, at the top; lift and hold the handle,
  release to lock. Never swivel while moving. Constant-pressure toggle;
  IR remotes must be pointed at the lift.
- Safety edges on the footrest and carriage and an **underpan sensor**:
  on an obstruction, run the other way, clear it, carry on. Upper and
  lower limits, mechanical stops, **seat swivel switch**, optional e-stop
  on the left arm, overspeed brake.
- **Test the keys and e-stop every month.**

## Manual lowering (owner's version)

Main power **OFF** → pry off the access cover on the lower side of the
unit (flat screwdriver) → fit the handwheel on the **motor output shaft,
below the battery tray** → **LH unit: clockwise = up**; **RH unit:
clockwise = down** → call the dealer and **don't turn the power back on**.

## Owner troubleshooting

| Symptom | Check |
|---|---|
| Won't run, no lights | Charger plugged in; key and power ON; house breaker or fuse |
| Won't run, all lights on | Seat locked in the riding position; obstruction at the footrest or carriage |
| Beeps about 20 s while parked | **Not charging**: cord in the outlet; lift fully at the top or bottom charge contact (continuous beeping off a contact) |
| Runs to the end, stops, won't restart | **Low battery**: charge it |

LEDs: same table as [[K2]] (all on = ready; middle + right = LH
obstruction; middle + left = RH; middle only = both; **all off = safety
circuit fault**).

## Maintenance (this manual)

- **Owner every 3 months:** seat swivel switch (won't travel unless
  locked), footrest and carriage sensors, e-stop, seat belt, seat and arm
  hinges. **Monthly** for adverse environments.
- **Technician every 6 months:** limits, overspeed brake, lube the angle
  and straight rollers (**Super Lube or wet graphite grease**), top
  mechanical limits, rail brackets, gear rack tight and pitched, footrest
  level, covers, wiring, noise, motor temperature.
- **Every 36 months:** replace the angle rollers, inspect the vertical
  rollers. Seat swivel: wheel bearing grease as needed.

**Schedule conflict:** the 2025 install guide puts the owner checks at
**every 6 months** and some technician items yearly. This 2016 manual is
stricter (owner every 3 months, all technician items every 6 months).
Telling the customer the stricter schedule is the safe choice.
