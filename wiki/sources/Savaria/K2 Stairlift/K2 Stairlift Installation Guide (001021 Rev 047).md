# K2 Stairlift Installation Guide (001021 Rev 047)

50-page Savaria Concord Lifts installation guide for the **K2** straight-rail,
battery-powered stairlift (P/N 001021 Rev 047, 2025-10-24; first released
2015). Covers install, setup, diagnostics, DIP switches and maintenance. A
duplicate " (1)" copy in the download was skipped. Savaria Technical Support
**800-791-7999** (2 Walker Drive, Brampton, Ontario).

## Covers

- **Codes:** ASME A18.1 (2003–2014) Section 4 public and Section 7 private.
- **Capacity 300 lb (136 kg)**, also with extension brackets or the flip-up
  rail. Needs a standard 110 V outlet at the top or bottom for the charger.
- **Shipping weights:** drive carriage 79 lb, rail boxes 75 lb, extra rail
  36 lb, flip rail 80 lb, V-seat 56 lb, L-seat 54 lb. Three boxes.
- **Handing:** viewed from the bottom, the **steel gear rack is always on
  the left** of the aluminum rail whatever the handing.
- **Rail length = nose-to-floor + top rail extension.** Angle chart from
  riser and tread (e.g. 8.5 in riser, 10 in tread = 40°); extension chart by
  angle and seat height (knee height of the user; 30° and 18 in = 8 in).
  **Minimum rail 30 in** (50 in with a flip-up rail). Cut the aluminum
  separately from the steel rack, from the top, with a non-ferrous
  reverse-rake mitre blade; mark the rack 3/4 in below the top for the end
  cap; leave the top rack piece out until the carriage is on.
- **Splices:** splice bars front and back, screws **40 in-lb (4.5 Nm)**; a
  1 m rack length centred on the joint.
- **Charging:** strips at the bottom **80 mm** from the bottom limit and at
  the top **60 mm** from the top limit. **Red (positive) always goes under
  the rack.** Optional **full bus line** instead of strips: casing 2 in
  shorter than the track, copper strip 1/2 in shorter than the casing,
  stoppers at the far end, and **DIP switch 4 OFF** for continuous charging.
- **Rail brackets:** six sets per 16 ft, **never more than 40 in apart**,
  one each side of a splice, one at the bottom and top steps; domed cap nuts
  with washers, **10 ft-lb (13.5 Nm)**. Keep the rail **1/2 to 3/4 in off
  the stair noses**. Extension brackets 310294/310295 (kit 310293). Anchor
  only once the carriage is on and the rail is in its final position;
  supplied anchors may not suit the steps. At least **1 in from the wall to
  the chair back**.
- **Carriage:** check the **overspeed brake dog** wasn't tripped in transit
  (reset it), remove the red tag, adjust the **charging pins** with the
  2 mm spacer washers (too much spring pressure wears the pin), keep the
  seat jumper plugged in until the seat is fitted. Rack bolts **50 in-lb
  (5.7 Nm)**.
- **Remotes:** hold the remote program button on the board (amber LED on),
  press any remote button (LED off). Up to **4 units in one area**: change
  the remote DIP switches.
- **Seat post levelling:** three M10 × 16 bolts front and back, **40 ft-lb
  (55 Nm)**. Seat swivel bolt, with 0.438 × 1.000 in washers as support
  shims if the seat moves.
- **Seat mounting dimensions** for the L-seat and V-seat at narrow, middle
  and wide arm settings (rail-to-wall and seat-back-to-wall).
- **Limit cams** at the top and bottom; **mechanical stops 1 in above the
  carriage rollers** at the top; mount the white charger; lightly lube the
  rack teeth; wipe the charging strips; outdoor units get LUT420 V2 remote
  hall calls.
- **Tests:** controls, folding parts, swivel lock, seat belt, footrest,
  underpan and carriage edge sensors, limits, mechanical stops, swivel
  switch, plus an ASME-style checklist. **Manual operation:** power off,
  handwheel on the motor shaft below the battery tray (LH unit: clockwise
  goes up). **Overspeed trip test:** flip the brake dog up with a
  screwdriver, run down until it catches the rack, crank back up, press it
  down.
- **Work by others:** a **wall or barrier behind any exposed rail**.
- **Diagnostics** (Appendix A): carriage LEDs (all on = ready; middle +
  right = LH obstruction; middle + left = RH obstruction; middle only = both;
  all off = safety circuit fault). Board LEDs (green = charging, yellow =
  sleep, red = safety circuit open). **DIP switches on the LUT125:** 1
  reverses the remote buttons, 2 brake installed, 3 beeps when running, 4
  soft start/stop (charge detected at cam), 5 no charger warning or sleep
  mode, 6 silent. **Cycle the power after any DIP change.** All safety LEDs
  flashing at 11.5 V = sleep mode. Wiring diagrams (standard and outdoor)
  and safety circuit.
- **Maintenance** (Appendix B): owner every 6 months (swivel switch,
  sensors, e-stop, seat belt, hinges); technician limits every 6 months,
  overspeed, roller lube, stops, brackets, rack, footrest, covers, wiring,
  noise and motor temperature yearly (every 6 months for excessive use or
  outdoors). **Lube rollers with synthetic grease (Super Lube) or wet
  graphite; seat swivel with wheel bearing grease. Replace angle rollers
  every 3 years.**
- Appendix: Savaria Link option (guide 001180); tech bulletin to **swap the
  toggle switch to the other arm**.
