# K2 Plus Stairlift Parts Catalogue (001189 Rev 012)

52-page Savaria parts catalogue for the K2 Plus (P/N 001189 Rev 012,
2025-05-23). Carriage and mainframe, side chassis (RH/LH, residential and
COM), seat posts, L-seat conversion kit, limit switches, charging electrode,
**OSG (overspeed governor) assembly** and switches, manual footrest, Plus
seat (residential C6490002, commercial C6490016), L-seat, rails (bottom and
extension 8 ft, flip-up), fitting kits, remotes and receivers, miscellaneous.
