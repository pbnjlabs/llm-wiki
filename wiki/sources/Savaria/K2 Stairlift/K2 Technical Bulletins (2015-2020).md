# K2 Technical Bulletins (2015-2020)

Twelve short Savaria technical bulletins for the K2 (one for the K2 Plus),
2015–2020. They rank with the manuals; where the install guide is newer, it
wins.

## Covers

- **K2 Plus manual trip test (001277, 2020):** put **125% of rated load** on
  the seat, run to the top, open the side and top covers, run down until the
  brake dog shows, flip it up with a screwdriver, run down until it catches
  the rack, crank up with the handwheel, press the dog down, test.
- **Seat post levelling bolts (001064, 2016):** three bolts front and back,
  **40 ft-lb (55 Nm)**, don't overtighten.
- **Seat support shims (001151, 2017):** flat washers 0.438 × 1.000 in on the
  3/8 in seat swivel bolt to take out side-to-side or fore-aft play; the seat
  must still swivel.
- **Seat swivel switch pin shim (001153, 2017):** a white plastic shim
  between the spring and washer on the switch pin stops it **sticking up and
  holding the swivel switch on**. Pin cutout aligned with the switch arm;
  brackets aligned so it doesn't bind.
- **Roller and shim kit (001098, 2017; kit C6290268):** vertical rollers
  C6240002 and side rollers C6240003 (four each), 1 mm shim washers
  C6000437. Shim the vertical rollers (remove the C-clip) until there's **no
  front-to-back play** of the carriage on the rail.
- **Seat height change (001139, 2017):** swap the carriage seat post
  assembly: seat and cable off, top cover, bushing, swivel locking plate (4
  screws), half-moon covers, the 6 levelling bolts; level the new post and
  torque the bolts to 40 ft-lb.
- **Footrest extension (001216, 2019; kit 500265):** new mounting plate on
  two M10 × 40 screws; keep the **bolt jig** on the same side as before.
- **5-point seat harness (001246, 2019):** P/N 107326 (children), 107326-B
  (adults): shoulder straps to the seat back bracket, lap straps to the arms,
  front strap to the seat plate.
- **Wifi board (001173, 2018):** mounts in the cover on standoffs, toggle
  switch and antenna tied in; **not for K2 power units**.
- **Full bus line (001041, 2015):** as in the install guide (DIP 4 OFF,
  positive on the rack side, no power to the charger while connecting).
- **Outdoor and extreme environments (001100, 2017):** to keep the warranty,
  clean and lubricate critical points **every 6 months** (every 3 months in
  extreme environments). Ground the unit. Stainless: ZEP or Weiman's cleaner,
  no abrasives. Painted surfaces: automotive wax (**Nu Finish**). Switches,
  battery terminals, hardware and zinc, aluminum and copper: spray lube
  (**Fluid Film**), kept off rubber and off anything the rider touches.
  **Dielectric grease** on the charging strips or bus lines.
- **Hand change (001109, 2017):** reversing the L-seat (swivel lever, seat
  post bracket, swivel lock, switch flipped 180°) and the carriage (move the
  3-LED board, footrest to the other side; its R/L connectors swap; flip the
  seat post).
