# K2 Plus Stairlift Installation Guide (001187)

44-page Savaria installation guide for the **K2 Plus** (P/N 001187,
2024-09-25; first released 2019). Same procedure as the
[[K2 Stairlift Installation Guide (001021 Rev 047)]]: rail cut, splices,
brackets, carriage, charging strips or full bus lines, seat levelling, limits,
stops, remotes, tests, manual operation and overspeed trip test.

## Differences from the K2

- **Codes add public and Canadian use:** ASME A18.1 2003–2017 Section 4
  (public) and 7 (private), **CAN/CSA B355-09/-15/-19 (public)** and CSA B613
  (private). Residential and commercial seat kits.
- **Four boxes** (rail in two).
- **Flip-up rail rated 350 lb (159 kg)**; keep 1 in of diagonal rail offset
  from the bottom landing.
- Hall calls use the **LUT440** receiver board on the LUT125.
- **B355 and B316 units have no three diagnostic LEDs** on the carriage.
- PC-125 (public Canada) wiring diagram.
- Maintenance appendix in the MCP format (rail, carriage, seat, remotes);
  see [[K2 Plus Maintenance Control Program (001365)]]. Seat post bolts 40
  ft-lb.
