## Covers

- Homecare, assisted living or hospital; caregiver operated only (never by the
  person being lifted); install by an authorized Savaria dealer; **10-year life**.
- **Specs:** 5 kg (11 lb); **SWL 200 kg (440 lb) or 130 kg (286 lb)**; 5.5 cm/s
  unloaded to 4.5 cm/s at 200 kg; soft start/stop; IP21; 54 dBA; **duty max 10%,
  1 min continuous**. Same platform as the PL ([[Savaria-M-Series]]) in
  [[Savaria FL and PL Ceiling Lift Technical Specifications (2022)]].
- **Compatibility:** Savaria telescopic and rigid-arm and Handicare aluminum
  portable track systems; Savaria and Arjo track (accessories differ); portable
  scale kit M921422 / frame M921423; Savaria slings recommended.
- **Use:** check end stops and clevis pins; hand control optional (touchpads by the
  hooks); the **On/Off switch is the emergency stop**.
- **Manual lowering:** power off, 4 mm Allen key (supplied), turn as labelled;
  slow, many turns; then call the dealer.
- **Emergency brake** stops a fall if the transmission or motor fails. If it has
  activated, **don't press Up, don't release the brake and don't use manual
  lowering**; switch off and call the dealer.
- **Charging:** low battery = beeping and blinking LED on a button press. Charger
  **M920001 only**; green on the wall, yellow/orange/red while charging, green
  when full; about 2 h; can stay plugged in; unplug before use; charge monthly in
  storage.
- **Operator maintenance:** before every use (charge, strap, sling and stitching,
  carabiner, buttons, track, end stops, free travel); clean the track and test the
  emergency stop every 4 months; dealer schedule and troubleshooting in sections
  6–7.
