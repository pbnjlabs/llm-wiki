# Savaria FL and PL Ceiling Lift Technical Specifications (2022)

Two-page Savaria spec sheet (2022; ceiling-lift.com).

| | FL fixed | PL portable |
|---|---|---|
| Weight | 7.5 kg manual, 8.5 kg motorized | 5 kg (11 lb) |
| SWL | 272 kg (600 lb), 200 kg (440 lb) or 130 kg (286 lb) | 200 kg (440 lb) or 130 kg (286 lb) |
| Lift speed | 5.5 cm/s empty → 4 cm/s at 272 kg | 5.5 → 4.5 cm/s at 200 kg |
| Battery | Li-ion 25.2 V 2500 mAh; ~80 lifts of 24 in at 200 lb, ~30 at 600 lb | same; ~30 lifts at 440 lb |
| Charging | ~2 h; charger 100–240 V in, 29.4 V 1 A out | same |
| Hand control | Up/down/left/right (IP67) | Up/down (IP67) |

Both: IP21 lift, 54 dBA, **10-year life with one battery replacement**, 3-year
warranty, ISO 10535:2021, IEC 60601-1 and -1-11, CSA Z10535.1. On/off switch
doubles as the emergency stop. Not for use with flammable anesthetics.
