# Savaria Patient Care Slings Catalogue (V18D)

49-page sling catalogue from **Savaria Patient Care** (legacy **Handicare**
and Silvalea designs; drawings still carry Handicare title blocks). Undated
(version V18D). Slings are compatible with Savaria ceiling and floor lifts,
and Savaria evaluates compatibility with other makers' lifts.

## Covers

- **Fabrics:** polyester, mesh, disposable non-woven (single patient,
  short term), PolySlip, quilted mesh, mesh/poly, **spacer** (multi-stretch,
  considered among the safest to leave in place), Superfine Plus (bathing),
  Silva-Spacer with COOLMAX (bariatric).
- **Sling families by task:** seated (pp. 6–31), hygiene, sit-to-stand,
  walking, repositioning (e.g. Tri-Turner spacer, in-situ repositioning
  sheets, SWL up to 625 lb), clip options (clip-style lifts; SWL to 600 lb),
  disposable, bariatric, specialty, pediatric. Application chart: up from the
  floor, up in bed, bed to chair, to tub or commode, toileting, standing and
  gait training, turning.
- **Sizing:** standard sizing by weight and height for most models;
  individualized sizing charts (pp. 77–95) for some.
- **Care:** many slings wash at up to 80 °C (176 °F), tumble dry lowest heat.
  Check the label for safe working load and care.
