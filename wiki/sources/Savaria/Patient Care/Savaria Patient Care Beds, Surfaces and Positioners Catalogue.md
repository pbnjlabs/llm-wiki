# Savaria Patient Care Beds, Surfaces and Positioners Catalogue

37-page product catalogue from **Savaria Patient Care** (the Savaria
division built from **Span, Handicare and Silvalea**; savariapatientcare.com).
Undated. **Marketing material**: product features and specs, not service
instructions.

## Covers

- **Beds:** Encore (premium: Smart Stop low-position auto-pause at 12.5 in,
  low 7-7/8 in to high 31 in, GlideAlign retractable deck, ReadyWide
  expanders to 39 and 42 in, foot-end controller with staff lockout, optional
  backup battery), ReadyWide, Advantage, Weigh Scale bed, dolly, Risk
  Manager, profiles and finishes.
- **PressureGuard support surfaces:** powered air (bariatric APM, APM-i,
  APM-2, APM2 Safety, Supreme APM), non-powered and convertibles (Custom Care
  Convertible LAL, Custom Care Convertible, Custom Care, Span-Care), microclimate
  management (Easy Air XL, Easy Air LR, Easy Air Protocol, Topper).
- **Geo-Mattress therapeutic foam:** UltraMax (and for stretchers), Max,
  Pro, Plus, Wings, Atlas HC, Gel-80.
- Specialty surfaces (bariatric, extra-wide and consumer sizes, recessed
  deck, in-home styles), overlays and seating, positioners, seating and
  surface selection guides.
