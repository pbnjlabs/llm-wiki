# SRE-2010E Install Manual (02-25-2026)

Install manual for the **Elite outdoor straight rail**: SRE-2010E,
2010EC (commercial) and 2010EOS (overspeed) (34 pages). Specs ILS-00797,
drawing ILS-00799. It follows the indoor [[SRE-2010 Install Manual]]
step for step (same rail length rules, mounts, carriage angle table,
jack screw, cross pin and DIP table). Only the differences are listed
here.

- **Operating range 0–125 °F.** Don't use it within **20 minutes of
  lightning**. No pressure washers.
- **Charging (end charge, not continuous):**
  - A **charge lead runs inside the rail's center hollow** to a proximity
    **charge contact at each end**, fixed with a key/M10 nut.
  - Charger at the bottom: spades out of the top and the white plug out
    of the bottom. Charger at the top: the reverse.
  - Fish the wire through the joint without pinching it.
  - Trim the lead **4 in past the rail end (8 in on EC/EOS)**: smooth =
    male, red ribbed = female.
  - **The un-notched edge of the contact is always positive, + on top.**
  - **Dielectric grease on every rail connection.**
- **EC/EOS:** final limit actuator (slide it down **5 in** with an OSG)
  and the overspeed end stop SRE-21254T on nut bar SRE-30490, 2 in from
  the end. Remove the end stop before fitting the carriage.
- **Overspeed reset (EC/EOS):**
  1. Remove the motor-side cap.
  2. Turn the gear motor **clockwise with a 5/16 in socket** to move up;
     the switch resets itself.
  3. If it doesn't, remove the panel fastener and slide off the cover,
     then center the switch roller in the cam valley.
  4. Inspect the drive, and return the carriage to the factory if it
     can't be fixed.
- **Wall clearance** (back of the top of the rail): **4.25 in, 5.25 in
  with a canopy, 7.25 in with the wide seat**.
- **Seat:** a **fabric control enclosure cover** and the **antenna** go
  on after the carriage covers. Harnesses: **12-pin** (swivel to board),
  **2-pin** (keyswitch, white heat shrink), **3-pin** (footrest). Fit the
  canopy bracket before the seat if ordered.
- **Bag cover:** CRE-00995 top seat cover and CRE-00999 bag (rod and
  push nuts under the front two seat bolts).
- **Remotes:** keyed **2.4 GHz RF** call/send, one at the top and one at
  the bottom, with the **key switch 35–48 in above the floor** (check
  local code). **One 9 V battery.** Close the switch cover against
  moisture. RF repeater kits **CRE-K-2142WG** (NA) / CRE-K-2144WG
  (UK/EU).
- **Charger indoors (32–104 °F).** A 10 ft output extension is included.
  Longer cords slow charging.
- **Parts:**
  - Tan seats SRE-21330 (standard) / SRE-21331 (XL).
  - Exterior rails SRE-21314 (16 ft) / SRE-21194 (20 ft).
  - Carriages SRE-21383 (E) / SRE-21384 (EC/EOS).
  - Rail kits SRE-K-2185/2186 (E) and SRE-K-2197/2198 (EC/EOS).
- **Warranty:** outdoor CRE-SRE, **5 years on major components, 2 years
  on parts**. Cosmetic damage is excluded.
