# Tech Tip - SRE-3000 IR Call-Send Interference

Two copies of one 1-page tip (Bruno article 103, modified 4/23/2014; portal tech-tips 172 and 44). A top call station worked intermittently. Swapping the transmitters and replacing the top IR sensor didn't help. **Unplugging the lower IR sensor fixed it:** a reflected signal reached the lower sensor out of phase and interfered with the upper one.

**Tip:** when one transmitter misbehaves, **unplug the IR sensor at the opposite end**. If that fixes it, it's interference, not a bad part.
