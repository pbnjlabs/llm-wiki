# SRE-2010 Owner's Manual (2010-O, 02-27-2026)

Owner's manual for the **Elite indoor** stairlift (14 pages).

**Specs:**
- **400 lb.**
- Constant pressure (armrest plus 2 IR remotes).
- **45° maximum (specials to 50°).**
- Swivel stops **0°, 60° and 90° at the top**, with an optional **45° at
  the bottom**.
- 24 VDC charger on 120 V.

**Operation:**
- Soft start. **Down is slightly slower than up.**
- **Keyed units:** the key OFF disables both the arm switch and the
  remotes.
- Remotes are for an empty seat only.
- **Power swivel:** at the top, **release and press the paddle again**
  (same direction) to swivel. This differs from the Elan SRE-5000, which
  keeps going.
- **Power footrest** follows the seat; never fold it by hand.

**Folding rail:**
- **Manual rail:** fold it only from mid-travel or above. If the carriage
  passes mid-park and the **carriage safety bracket** hits the folded
  rail, call the dealer.
- **Power rail:**
  - It **stops at mid-park**. Press again to fold or unfold, and the
    carriage waits until the rail is fully down or up.
  - It beeps continuously while moving, and the **yellow LED flashes**.
  - On the remote it also stops at mid-park.
  - The control panel on the rail has a **disable switch**: OFF stops
    folding (to leave the rail down long-term).
  - **Emergency:** switch OFF, then **lift or lower the rail slowly** by
    hand at the marked point. A fast lift can damage the fold gearbox.

**Codes under the seat:**

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A2 | Seat up |
| A3 | Seat out of the ride position |
| A7 | Seat or footrest jammed |
| C1 | Charging |
| C2 | Off charge |
| C4 | Parked, no charger |
| C5 | Battery critical |
| C6 | Battery low |
| L1 | Seat safety |
| L2 / L3 | Up / down limit |
| L6 | Mid-rail park |
| L8 | Soft stop |

**Charging:** **it charges anywhere on the rail.** The red power light
should always be on; yellow means charging, green means float.

**Charger LEDs** (red/yellow/green/blue, 2.2 A):

| LEDs | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Red + yellow | Charging |
| Red + green | Full |
| Yellow and green blinking alternately | Low voltage |
| Red and yellow blinking alternately | Defective or sulfated batteries |
| Green fast blink | Bad contact or sulfation |
| Blue | Connector reversed |

**Owner care:**
- Remotes take **2 AA, changed yearly**.
- **Yearly dealer service.**
- Storage: breaker off and charger unplugged. Afterwards, charge to
  green and Pledge the rail.
- Troubleshooting: multi-user beeping, a slow lift (batteries), refitting
  the paddle, charger lights, the on/off switch, the display under the
  seat (blank = no power), the outlet or a switched outlet, the key, and
  the footrest sensors.
- **Warranty:** Gold, lifetime on major components, 2 years on parts.
