# Tech Tip - SRE-3000 Folding Rail Fixes

Five short SRE-3000 Elan folding-rail tips (Bruno articles 111 and 114 from 2014, the rest posted 2016-01-13; portal tech-tips 166, 176, 184, 52, 159).

- **Mid-park contact strip came loose** (article 114): contact cement or green Loctite in the channel, smear bonding agent, slide the bronze strip back, wipe off the excess and let it dry. Make sure the strip's end is **burr-free**.
- **"Bump" at the folding joint** (RMA #35265, two copies): a gap where the rails meet with the latch closed. Loosen the latch's two bolts, push the rails together, reposition the latch and tighten. Then redo the standard folding-rail gear rack adjustment.
- **Carriage jammed at the fold on a new Elan:** remove the access cap and turn the motor shaft extension with a ¼ in socket or the **SRV-3000-002** back-up tool to drive it up (**CW for LH, CCW for RH**). Run it about 3 ft above the fold. Loosen the gear rack tension bolt (bottom end cap) and the shear bolt on the folding section, clamp the rack segments in line with **FIX-00525** (or a piece of new segmented rack and a clamp), tighten the shear bolt, then the tension bolt, remove the fixture, check the ride and the fold.
- **Binds at the seam only with a heavy rider** (about 250 lb, or a tech standing on the footrest): the rail twisted at the fold because **the lower end cap wasn't resting on the floor** with the rail down. Bruno's checklist: folding-rail support clamp fitted and adjusted (both back screws in the tread), a standard clamp on the next tread up, **lower end cap on the floor** when down and locked, rail lock engaging.
