# Parts Drawings - SRE-2010 Elite

Bruno's assembly drawings (exploded views with parts tables), pulled 2026-10-22 from the Brunopartner parts lookup (Service → Parts Lookup by Serial #). That lookup only takes an exact serial number, so the set was rebuilt from each model's top-level drawing, following assemblies three levels down ([[Bruno-Parts-Lookup]]). **These are Bruno's current revisions**: for an older unit, run its serial on the portal for the parts list as built, or check the manual's exploded view. Dealer prices on the portal are manufacturer suggestions and are not recorded here.

No SRE-2010 model drawing exists; the set starts from the carriages in the 2026 manuals (indoor SRE-21385L/RWG, overspeed SRE-21386LWG, outdoor SRE-21383LT/21384LT), seats SRE-21324LWG/21326LWG and the rail kits.

- **Gear motors MDC-24031 (RH) / MDC-24032 (LH)** with brake (pre-2025 style; the Feb 2025 replacement kits are on [[ILS - Stairlift Gear Motor and Brush Replacement]]). Carriage PCB **PCB-00136** (IR motor control/receiver, 2010/G modular), user interface PCB **SRE-27391** under the seat.
- **Batteries BTR-12002** (12 V 7 Ah), **30 A breaker CBK-30004** with aux switch harness CRE-01050, drive safety switch CRE-00961, overspeed switch harness SRE-21167 (LH).
- Charger **BCR-24024** (24 VDC 2 A, NEMA 5-15P; UK BCR-24025), gear rack segment SRE-30177, final limit actuator SRE-30029WG, gas springs SRE-20769 (30 lb) / SRE-20135 (60 lb, XL seat), remotes TMR-00004.
