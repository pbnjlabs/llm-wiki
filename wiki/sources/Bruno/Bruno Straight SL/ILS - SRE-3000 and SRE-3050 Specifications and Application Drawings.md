# ILS - SRE-3000 and SRE-3050 Specifications and Application Drawings

Elan spec sheets and application drawings.
- **ILS-01024 Rev 8 (2-6-2019), SRE-3000 / 3000G** and **ILS-01462 (04/16/26), SRE-3050 / 3050CAN / 3050G** (private residence only; A18.1 Sec 7, to the 2023 edition for the 3050): **300 lb**; 2 x 3 Ah; charger 105–230 VAC, 24 VDC **1 A**; 16 A geared motor, 442 lb-in, 0.18 hp; **IR** call/send; rail 2 in above the step nose; **22–45° (specials to 52°)**; **18 ft/min**; rails 15'9" / 19'8" (3000), 15'6" / 19'8" (3050); seat stops 0°, 67°, 90° at the top, **no swivel at the bottom**; **25 A breaker, 5 A fuse**; 12–24 h on batteries with power off; 185 lb (3000) / 180 lb (3050) unit; gearbox tested at 2100 lb. 3050 options: keyed call/send, power swivel (85°), power footrest, manual or power folding rail (**30–45°**). The 3050 sheet repeats the **not-for-scoot-pivot / buckling-knee users → use the Elite** advice.
- **Application drawings:** rail **5 in from the wall** standard (top back of rail 3 in), **folding rail 6 in** (top back 4 in), with folding-rail minimum gaps **2.0 in / 2.5 in with carpet** (3050); 2010G/3000G metric versions; seat swivelled at 67° and 90°; power-option drawings ILS-01219 / ILS-01461.
