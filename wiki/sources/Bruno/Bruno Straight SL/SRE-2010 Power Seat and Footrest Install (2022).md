# Elite (SRE-2010) Power Seat and Power Footrest Install (2010G-NA POW OPT, 02-01-2022)

A 42-page supplement to the Elite install manual for the **power swivel
seat**, the **power footrest**, or both. It is for North American and
global units, and includes NA and global wiring schematics plus power
swivel parts lists. Kits: **SRE-K-2078WG** (power swivel seat install)
and **SRE-K-2086WG** (power footrest seat install). Start with the
carriage on the rail.

**Power footrest:**
- Discard the 2 front M10 hex bolts. Pull the actuator top pin (keep the
  2 white spacers).
- Mount with the **kit's M10 flat-heads, as low as possible, 50 ft-lb**.
- Re-pin the actuator (nylon/steel washer order per the figure). The
  actuator and bracket should be vertical.
- **Harnesses:**
  - Feed the orange and white harness to the carriage.
  - **Keep the 3-, 6- and 12-pin shipping jumpers**: slide them onto the
    3-pin carriage harness for later service.
  - Orange connector to **"PWR FOOTREST AUX"** on the board.
- Set the carriage angle (4 bolts, 50 ft-lb).

**Power swivel:**
- Rear seat bracket SRE-20562WG.
- Detach the display PCB from its hook-and-loop pads. Feed the board and
  3-pin rocker harnesses bottom-to-top; the orange swivel harness goes
  top-to-bottom.
- Join the two red connectors.
- **6-pin and 12-pin carriage to swivel**, orange to **"PWR SWV AUX"**.
- Spacer SRE-20826 with an M8 × 20: **behind the hole with a power
  footrest, in front with a standard footrest**.
- **Seat to swivel: 4 × M6, 150 in-lb.**
- With a standard footrest, the swivel hangs on an **"L" bracket, tab
  forward**.

**Test:**
- Lift the seat: the footrest folds. Lower it: the footrest drops.
- At the top, **release UP and press it again** to swivel (it beeps at
  the stop). DOWN swivels it back.

**Seat heights (floor to seat / footrest to seat, inches):**
- **Power footrest:** factory 21.8/18.3. With the seat and footrest
  raised it goes up to 25.8/20.3.
- **Power seat only:** factory 24.3/21.5, up to 29.6/24.5.
- Positions marked # need bracket **SRE-20710WG**.
- To change: move the 4 M8 flat-heads before the footrest goes on.
