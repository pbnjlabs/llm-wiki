# ILS - SRE-2010 Carriage Retrofit Kits

Four short 2008–2010 sheets for adding switches and harnesses to older SRE-2010 carriages: **SRV-2010-001 mid-park charge kit** (extra PBS-00101 switch, harnesses SRE-20609/SRE-20621, mid-park charge assembly on the rail, check continuity, show the user); **SRV-2010-010 Elite folding rail carriage kit** (PBS-00101, harnesses SRE-20894/SRE-20621); **SRV-2010-009L/R power carriage kit** (PBS-00101 and PBS-00102 switches); **SRV-2010-011 manual folding rail carriage upgrade** (drawing only).
