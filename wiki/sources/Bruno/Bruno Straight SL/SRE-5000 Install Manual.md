# SRE-5000 Install Manual (5000-I, 08-10-2026)

Bruno's install manual for the 2026 **Elan SRE-5000** straight rail
(P/N SRE-5000-I, 21 pages). It doesn't cover the folding rail, power
footrest or power swivel. Those are in
[[SRE-5000 Folding Rail Install Guide]] and
[[SRE-5000 Power Options Install Guide]]. Specs are cross-referenced to
ILS-01614 and ILS-01617 ([[ILS - SRE-5000 Specifications]]). **Indoor
only, heated, above 35 °F.** Not for scoot-pivot or knee-buckling
transfers: those users need the [[SRE-2010]] Elite.

**Tools and torque:** 10 mm magnetic socket on a 12 in or longer
extension, a metal-cutting saw, and a 25–50 ft-lb torque wrench. Use
grade 10.9 metric hardware only: **M8 25 ft-lb, M10 47, M12 83, M14 133**.

**Rail length:**
- No obstruction at the top: **A (top nose to lower floor) + 10 in**,
  which gives a 20 in seat at park. Add **1.5 in of rail per extra inch of
  seat height** (no more than 18 in extra).
- Ramped rail for a door at the top: **A + B + 3 in**, per ILS-01666.
- No segment shorter than **24 in**. Keep the factory ends at the joints.
- Kit names aren't real lengths: the **16 ft kit is 2 × 93 in**, the
  **20 ft kit 2 × 118 in**, and the "32" kit 3 × 118 in.

**Rail prep:**
- **Joints:** two joint brackets, each held by #10-16 × ⅝ in
  self-drillers 2 in from the joint, then two more 1 in from it. That
  makes **8 screws per joint**, 4 front and 4 back, all inside the scribe
  lines.
- **Slow-down ramps** go **1.5 in from each rail end**.
- **End caps:** 5/16-18 swage-forming screws, holes greased. **No impact
  driver.**
- **Charge strips:** charge block first, then the strip holders. Cut the
  last holder **1¾ in short** of the rail end. Bevel the strips and
  scour them with Scotch-Brite.
- **Gear rack:** must end within **1¼ in** of the top end. If it runs
  past, cut ½ in short at a tooth valley, with the **cut end facing
  away** from the other segments. No gaps. Don't let segments free-fall
  down the rail. One retaining screw goes between the teeth about ¾ in
  from the bottom end.

**Rail mounts:**
- One on the **first step up** and one on the **first step down from the
  top**.
- At least 2 per segment (3 on a one-piece rail), and **never more than 4
  steps without one**.
- **Upper overrun more than 12 in:** add an adjustable-height mount on
  the landing.
- **Hard transfers** (user drops more than 4 in into the seat): mounts on
  the two top steps, and at the lower landing plus the first step.
- Nut bars at each mount and joint: **M8 × 25 (mounts) and M8 × 15
  (joints), 25 ft-lb**. No gap between the rail and the mount; mounts
  sit flat.
- **1/8 in spacer** under the lower end cap.

**Clearances:**
- Step nose: **at least 1.5 in, 2.0 in on carpet**.
- Wall: **at least 2⅜ in (60 mm) from the nearest obstruction**, or
  **5 in from the front of the rail** to the wall.
- Fasten the top mount first (3 bolts), check running clearance, then
  fasten the rest: the wall-side screws first, then the front one.
- Footrest to step nose: **0.5–1.0 in**.
- Anchoring for other stair types is in
  [[ILS-01512 SRE Rail Mounting Guidance (2026)]].

**Carriage DIP switches** (top cover, power off, ESD strap):

| Switch | OFF (factory) | ON |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Multi-user (beeps every 3 s if the seat isn't in the ride position) | Single-user, only at the customer's request |
| 2 | Left-hand | **Right-hand** |

Also:
- Move the **M10 × 12 button-head screws** to the upstairs side.
- The harness exits on the **downstairs** side; plug the unused hole.

**Carriage on:**
- Pull the last 2 rack segments and slide the carriage on.
- On right-hand installs the **overspeed gear meshes first**. If it trips,
  rotate the triangular overspeed housing back to center, or turn the
  motor shaft with a ¼ in socket or SRV-3000-002: **CW for LH, CCW for
  RH**.
- Put the serial decal in the seat data-plate window.
- If several lifts are **within 100 ft**, reprogram the remotes
  (ILS-01644).
- Refit the rack and charge block. The **jack screw** compresses the
  rack: use the shorter set screw that fully compresses it.

**Footrest, seat post and seat:**
- Footrest and safety plate on M10 × 12 flat-heads. The post offset
  faces upstairs: M10 × 35 top, M10 × 50 bottom. The top holes give the
  lowest seat. The seat rises in 1 in steps to 3 in.
- Level the footrest, then torque: **hex 50 ft-lb, flat-head 40 ft-lb**.
  Fit the safety screw.
- **Seat:** grease the 2 nylon washers. With the seat at 45° upstairs,
  drive the swivel pin through the upstairs hole and fit the retaining
  ring. **Remove the seat and footrest before lifting the carriage off**
  (never lift it by the chair).
- Remove the **shipping jumper**. Connect the 8-pin seat and 3-pin
  footrest harnesses, then snap in the paddle.

**Finish:**
- The charger (6 ft cords) goes to the top or bottom charge block, on an
  **unswitched outlet**.
- A full charge takes **about 2 h**. A low battery may run down but not
  up; wait about 10 min.
- Thin coat of **PTFE grease on the gear rack**.
- Down speed self-adjusts to rider weight and slope, and **up is
  noticeably faster than down**.

**Audio notices:**
- Chirp: power-up, or a safety activated.
- Chirp every 3 s: seat safety open.
- 3 short + 2 long every 4 min: parked, no charger, below 24.5 V.
- **2 short + 3 long: battery critical, below 19.2 V.**
- 5 short: low-voltage stop. 4 short: over-current. 3 short:
  over-temperature.
- 2 beeps, a pause, then 5 (start-up) or 3 (running): multiple buttons
  held.
- **Conflict:** the newer tech support guide (diagnostics Rev 7,
  9/4/2026) gives battery critical as **B-B-L-L (2 short, 2 long)**. The
  TSG wins ([[SRE-5000 Tech Support Guide]]).

**Warranty:** the 2023 indoor SRE text: **lifetime on major components,
2 years on parts, 30 days labor**. Install within 14 months of shipping.

**Checklist quirk:** the checklist still says "move jumper within
carriage, RH install only". On the SRE-5000 that is DIP switch 2. Hand
over ILS-01446 and the owner's manual, train the user, and wipe the rail
with Pledge.
