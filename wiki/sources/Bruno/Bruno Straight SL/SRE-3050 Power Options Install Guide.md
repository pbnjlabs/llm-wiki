# SRE-3050 Power Options Install Guide (3050-POW OPT, 04-16-2024)

A 28-page supplement to the [[SRE-3050 Install Manual]] for the Elan
**power swivel seat** and **power footrest**. Drawing ILS-01461;
schematic SCH-00233, wiring table and exploded views at the back.

**Carriage:**
- Remove the 2 front shroud screws and unplug the **left IR eye
  harness**, then swing the shroud aside and pop the center section.
- Route the **footrest/aux harness to the main PCB** and out the front.
  Refit the shroud with the screws moved to the **upstairs side**.
- Carriages ship **left-hand**: for RH, re-route the harness and move
  the **LH/RH jumper**. The power footrest also ships LH (see the RH
  wiring table).

**Power footrest:**
- Upper hole: M10 socket head plus safety washer. Safety plate: 2 × M10
  flat-heads.
- Level it, then torque to **55 Nm (40 ft-lb)** with a power swivel. The
  footrest-only steps say **50 ft-lb**.
- Fit the **M6 (or M8) safety shear bolt**. Quick-release pin with 2
  nylon spacers in the actuator clevis.

**Power swivel (check LH/RH):**
- Remove pivot lock plate SRE-30536WG.
- Seat swivel harness down through hole **c1 (LH) or c2 (RH)**. Mount
  with M6s from kit SRE-K-3094.
- **AUX harness (3-pin orange) to the large header** on the footrest PCB,
  **swivel harness (2-pin orange) to the small header**.
- **Grease both thrust washers.**
- **Seat prep:**
  - Remove the release lever, seat safety switch, spring and black plate.
  - **Drill out the inner countersunk hole** with the supplied bit (cuts
    into the lock plate to take out lateral play), then fix the lock
    plate with an M4 flat-head and nylock.
  - Fit switch **PBS-00148** in the seat base.
- Lock plate screws **6.7 ft-lb (9.15 Nm)**.

**Footrest only:**
- Offset assembly on the footrest bracket, **pointing at the upper
  landing**. There are 2 hole sets, LH and RH.
- Swivel harness SRE-30145 through hole H1 (LH) or H2 (RH). Save the
  jumper on the harness.
- **Grease the nylon washers.** Seat pin through the upstairs hole with
  the seat at 45° upstairs, then the spiral retaining ring.

**Torque note:** the guide gives both 40 ft-lb and 50 ft-lb for the
footrest bolts.
