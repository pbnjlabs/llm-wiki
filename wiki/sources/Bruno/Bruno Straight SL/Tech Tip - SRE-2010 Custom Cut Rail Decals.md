# Tech Tip - SRE-2010 Custom Cut Rail Decals

2-page note (portal tech-tip 45): from **9/20/2011** (to the 4/23/2013 rail change), SRE-2010 custom-cut rail sections carry a **numbered weatherproof decal at each joint**. Match the numbers to assemble the rail in order. The upper slot joint bar covers the decals, so they can stay on.
