# SRE-2000 Operator Manual (Electra-Ride Elite, 03-14-2007)

Owner's manual for the legacy **SRE-2000** (22 pages).
- **Specs:** 400 lb, 45°, swivel 0°, 30°, 60° and 90° in both
  directions **at the top and bottom**. The maximum speed is
  dealer-adjustable.
- **Charging:** at the **ends only**. It must touch the upper or lower
  charge bracket, or the batteries drain. A **yellow LED on the
  bracket** shows contact. If a door blocks the top, send it to the
  other end with the transmitter.
- Charger LEDs, transmitter (9 V) battery, storage and troubleshooting
  match the [[SRE-2000 Installation Manual (2007)]].
