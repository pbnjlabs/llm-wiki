# Tech Tip - SRE-2010 Power Folding Rail Bounce-Back (Old Style)

5-page checklist for an **old-style SRE-2010 power folding rail** that "bounces back" instead of seating on the floor (portal tech-tip 55).

Check in order: unit on; bumper gently touching the floor; short end hitting the larger U-channel (metal to metal, usually the actuator side); charge contacts moved; gear rack misaligned (the pivot bar is probably bent too; you can grind a chamfer on top of the rack); **lower actuator mounting plate** out of adjustment; scuff marks on the alignment blocks ("biscuits").

Lower actuator plate reset: loosen its two M6 Allen bolts, lower the rail with the rocker plus gentle hand pressure, push it to seat, jog the carriage (it moves only when the rail is fully seated), tighten the bolts, cycle the rail.

**Don't adjust the linkage bar for bounce-back.** It only works the limit switches, won't fix bounce-back, and can disable a set of limits.
