# Tech Tip - SRE-2010 Standfoot Orientation

3-page safety notice (portal tech-tip 46): standfeet on SRE-2010 rails **must** be fitted in the orientation shown (photos with check marks). The wrong orientation "may lead to serious personal injury and/or property damage". Refers to the install manual for the full procedure. The [[SRE-3000 Installation Manual]] gives the rule in words: long nose toward the centre of the staircase, no gap at the front between rail and standfoot.
