# Parts Drawings - SRE-3050 Elan

Bruno's assembly drawings (exploded views with parts tables), pulled 2026-10-22 from the Brunopartner parts lookup (Service → Parts Lookup by Serial #). That lookup only takes an exact serial number, so the set was rebuilt from each model's top-level drawing, following assemblies three levels down ([[Bruno-Parts-Lookup]]). **These are Bruno's current revisions**: for an older unit, run its serial on the portal for the parts list as built, or check the manual's exploded view. Dealer prices on the portal are manufacturer suggestions and are not recorded here.

Starts from the **SRE-3050** model drawing (which also lists the SRE-3000 remote).

- **Gear motor MDC-24027** (24 V with brake, gear and key, "Tornado") in carriage SRE-30508; **motor controller SRE-30317**; battery housing SRE-30507 with **BTR-12008** (12 V 3 Ah) batteries and breaker sub-assembly SRE-30293; final limit switch SRE-30534; overspeed gear shaft SRE-30619; wheels SRE-30613.
- Seat: paddle switch SRE-30496, seat belt BLT-00035. Remotes TMR-00004 / keyed SRE-00963AWG.
- Options: power footrest actuator SRE-30021 and aux PCB **SRE-30211**; power swivel actuator **SRE-30608** with aux PCB **PCB-00127**.
