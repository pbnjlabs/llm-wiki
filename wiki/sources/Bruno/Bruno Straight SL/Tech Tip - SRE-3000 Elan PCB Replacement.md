# Tech Tip - SRE-3000 Elan PCB Replacement

2-page procedure for changing the SRE-3000 Elan motor control board (portal tech-tip 156). **Wear an ESD wrist strap.**

1. Carriage 6–8 in down from the top, breaker off (on a LH unit this eases the safety flap off).
2. Remove the battery housing (13 mm) and unplug the red/black battery connector. Remove the top rounded cover and the safety flap (two screws).
3. Pull the board off its **four white standoffs**, snap the new one on, and move the wires one at a time (note their places).
4. Reconnect the battery, refit the housing loosely.
5. If the remotes were changed from factory defaults, **learn them to the new board** with the **key switch on** (off gives a single beep).
6. Refit the safety panel and cover, tighten the housing, and test every function and safety.
