# Tech Tip - SRE-2010 Quick Install Guide (Old Style Rail)

34-page photo walkthrough of an SRE-2010 install on the **old-style rail** (portal tech-tip 38; the date is the portal posting date, the content is older). It has no revision date and some joking captions. Use the current SRE-2010 manuals for anything they cover; this is for old-rail units still in the field.

- **Box contents:** carriage/manuals/call-sends; seat, footrest, charger, shrouds; install kit SRE-K-2050, rail joint kit SRE-K-2061, hardware kit SRE-K-2048, charge lead kit SRE-K-2056, Lubriplate, stand feet SRE-20502 (8).
- **Rail length:** floor to top step nose **+ 9 in** for normal seat height (74 in → 83 in). Cut with a 14 in metal-cutting dry-cut saw. Never cut the joint end; the gear rack stops short of the rail end.
- **Placement:** clamps two at the top, two at the bottom, one each side of the joint, no more than **two blank treads**, flat side toward the wall. Rail **4.5 in from the wall** (to its front) and at least **2.5 in out from the step noses**.
- **Carriage:** lock bolts torqued to **50 ft-lb (67.5 Nm)** after leveling on the footrest. Under 32° stair angle → carriage adjustment section of the install manual. Remotes act as left hand until the seat is on.
- **Charging check:** charger red/yellow light on and green LED on the carriage, at both ends.
- **Seat swivel:** ships 90° at the top, 0° at the bottom (bottom can be changed to 45°).
- **Gear rack grease:** Lubriplate, a bead **6 in on, 6 in off**.
- **DIP switch 2 ON = single-user mode** (no beep when the seat is swivelled).
- Armrest extension: three positions, three bracket screws.
