# Tech Tip - SRE-3000 Elan Brake Replacement (ILS-01324)

ILS-01324 Rev 0 (11-10-2016), 6 pages: fitting the **SRV-3000-016 Elan (SRE-3000) motor brake repair kit** (portal tech-tip 42). Tools: 13 mm socket, needle-nose pliers, wire cutters; the kit has the 3 mm and 1.5 mm hex keys, roll pin, hex drive nut, set screw, blue thread locker and ties.

1. Loosen the two 13 mm battery-cover bolts enough to remove the upper access cover.
2. Cut the ties, unplug the brake leads from the board and the circuit-breaker harness, remove the **three 3 mm screws** and lift the brake off.
3. Loosen the 1.5 mm set screw and slide the old brake drive hex nut off the shaft. Replace the roll pin.
4. Fit the new hex nut **groove side toward the motor end bell**, blue thread locker on the new set screw, and tighten while pressing the nut down.
5. **Centre the brake disc:** if its hex opening isn't centred the mounting holes won't line up. Reconnect the brake leads, disconnect **one motor lead** (blue or brown), breaker on, seat the brake flange-down on the shaft, and hold the rocker (down if at the top limit, up if at the bottom). The motor won't turn but the brake releases, so you can rotate it until the holes line up.
6. Breaker off, reconnect, new ties, blue thread locker on the brake screws, test, refit covers.

The kit is named for the **SRE-3000**. The wiki has no confirmation that it fits the SRE-3050.
