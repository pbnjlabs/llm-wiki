# SRE-5000 Power Options Install Guide (5000-POWOPT-I, 04-16-2026)

An 8-page supplement to the [[SRE-5000 Install Manual]] for the **power
footrest** and **power swivel seat**. Drawing: ILS-01616 (with power
options the seat adjusts up **2 in**, not 3).

**Power footrest:**
- **Carriage:**
  - Lift off the top shroud (2 screws).
  - Route the footrest aux harness behind the frame to **J1 "PWR FOOTREST
    AUX"** on the main PCB.
  - Move the lower front-cover button-head screw to the other side.
- **Mounting:**
  - Seat post on the footrest: 35 mm hex top, 50 mm hex bottom.
  - Lift the assembly on and fix it with the 50 mm hex.
  - Pull the actuator off the **magnetic connection block** and fit the
    lock plate with 2 × 25 mm flat-heads.
  - Level it, fit the safety screw, then torque: **flat-heads 40 ft-lb,
    hex 50 ft-lb**.
- **Wiring:**
  - Replace the 3-pin carriage jumper with the footrest limit switch
    harness.
  - Aux harness (orange 3-pin) to **J10** on the footrest PCB.
- **Footrest only (no power swivel):**
  - Replace the seat swivel harness with the kit harness (brown/white to
    the seat safety switch).
  - Fit the orange **footrest fold switch** in the seat bottom.
  - Kit harness 2-pin red to **J8 "F-FOLD"** on the footrest PCB.

**Power swivel (check LH/RH):**
- **Mounting:**
  - Remove the pivot lock plate, the 2 M6 screws and the thrust bearings.
  - Remove the shroud (3 Phillips).
  - Mount the unit on the 4 tapped holes with M6 socket heads.
- **Wiring:**
  - 8-pin carriage jumper out, seat swivel harness in.
  - Fold-switch 2-pin red to the footrest PCB, if a power footrest is
    fitted.
  - Refit the shroud.
- **Thrust bearings:** **grease both faces** (stops noise) and put them
  back.
- **Seat prep:**
  - Remove the swivel lever, spring and **seat safety switch** (the power
    swivel replaces them).
  - Feed the harness through the pear-shaped hole.
  - Turn the seat 90° and connect the 3-pin.
  - Back to the ride position. Lock plate with 2 M6 plus a T25, and cap
    the unused pivot hole.
