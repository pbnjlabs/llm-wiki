# SRE-3000 Installation Manual (2019, trilingual)

The last Elan **SRE-3000** install manual (140 pages: English pp. 1–45,
Spanish from p. 46, French from p. 91; schematics and exploded view at
the back). It **supersedes the 2015 manual** ([[SRE-3000 Installation Manual]]).
Listings: A18.1-2017, CAN/CSA-B613, A17.5/B44.1-2014.

**Specs:**
- **300 lb**, **18 ft/min**.
- 24 VDC 2-pole motor, 50 Nm, 16 A. **1 Nm electromechanical brake.**
- **22–45°, specials to 52°.**
- Swivel 0°, 67° and 90° at the top.

**Tools and torque:** an 18 in extension on the 10 mm magnetic socket,
a T-25 bit, a heavy-duty snap ring tool, a brass hammer, and the
**FIX-00525 rail clamp** (or a bar clamp with a piece of gear rack) for
joints. Grade 10.9: M8 25, M10 47, M12 83, M14 133 ft-lb.

**Rail:**
- **Length = stair length + 12 in** (20 in seat). Add 1.5 in per extra
  inch of seat. Door at the top: + nose-to-door + 3 in.
- **"16 ft" two-piece = 15 ft 9 in.** No piece under 24 in. **Never cut
  a folding rail assembly under 75 in.**
- **Mounts:**
  - At least 2 per segment and 3 on a one-piece rail. On a folding rail,
    2 per mid-rail section, not counting the fixed bracket at the fold.
  - **No more than 4 steps without one.**
  - Adjustable set **SRE-30150** if the rail ends **less than 3 in
    horizontally** or more than 12 in diagonally past the top nose.
- Bottom of the rail **1/8 in off the floor**. Mount bolts **25 ft-lb**.
  **2 in off the noses (2.5 in on carpet).**
- **Back of the top of the rail to the nearest obstruction: 3 in.**
- Jack screw and **rail cross pin** as on the later manuals.

**Folding rail:** keep the **shipping brace** on until the rail is in.
Fit-and-finish checks for a power rail:
- With the **disable switch off** the rail can't fold.
- A continuous beep while it moves.
- The **control box light flashes yellow**.

**Single-user mode:** a **jumper**, not a DIP switch. Move it from the
left+middle pins (multi-user) to the **middle+right pins** (single-user),
under the top carriage cover.

**Charger LED** (single LED):
- Red = AC on.
- **Flashing red** = bad batteries.
- Yellow = charging.
- Green = full.
- **Flashing green** = reverse polarity.

**Drive adjustment (carriage vibration):**
- Vibrates **down only** = too loose: seat the gear further into the
  rack.
- Vibrates **both ways** = too tight.
- Fix: loosen the 3 drive bolts 1–2 turns (5 and 6 mm Allen), turn the
  **jack screw ⅛ turn**, and run about 3 ft each way to self-center.
  Repeat if needed, then retighten.

**Reset the folding rail joint:**
1. Check the factory rack gap at the fold.
2. With the top end-plate jack screw already tight, loosen the fold
   rail's jack screw and its M5 button-head.
3. Bridge the joint with a **piece of spare gear rack** and tighten the
   fold jack screw until the loose piece drops away.
4. Re-tighten the M5.
5. **Never remove the main rail screw**: the carriage and rack would
   fall.

**Overspeed reset:**
1. Slide off the **right-hand safety panel** (2 Phillips) and remove the
   motor-side cap.
2. Rotate the governor until the wheel sits centered in the notch.
3. If it's stuck, turn the motor shaft with a **¼ in socket or
   SRV-3000-002: CW for LH, CCW for RH**.
4. Inspect the drive.

This is the same as the [[SRE-5000]].

**Troubleshooting:**
- Battery voltage **16–28 VDC**.
- Remotes backwards (slow up, fast down) = **RH not set**, or the seat
  not fitted (the circuit board).
- Check the keyswitch.

**Warranty:** Gold, lifetime on major components, 2 years on parts,
30 days labor.
