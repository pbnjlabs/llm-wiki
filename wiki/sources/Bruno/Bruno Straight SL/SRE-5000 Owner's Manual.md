# SRE-5000 Owner's Manual (5000-O, 08-06-2026)

Owner's manual for the Elan **SRE-5000** (14 pages). The warranty page
footer says 03-11-2026.

**Specs:**
- 300 lb.
- Constant pressure: an armrest paddle and **two IR remotes**.
- **45° maximum, specials to 52°.** The spec sheet says 22–52°.
- Swivel stops 0°, 67° and 90°, **at the top only**.
- Charger 100–240 VAC 1 A in, 24 VDC 1 A out.

**Operation:**
- Soft start. Goes **down slightly slower than up**.
- Footrest obstruction sensors stop it; run the other way, then clear
  the object.
- The **paddle breaks away** and snaps back in. The dealer can move it
  to the other arm.
- **Remotes are for an empty seat only.**
- Power outage longer than 1 h: turn the carriage breaker off and unplug
  the charger to save the batteries.
- **Breaker with on/off switch** on the back of the carriage. It trips
  on a jam or overload.

**Options:**
- **Power swivel:** keep holding the paddle at the top and the seat
  turns. The remote returns it.
- **Power footrest:** follows the seat (raise the seat and the footrest
  folds). **Never fold it by hand.** If it jams it returns to its last
  position.
- **Manual folding rail:** fold it only with the carriage **at or above
  mid-park**.
- **Power folding rail:**
  - Ships in **continuous mode**. Mid-park stop mode is set by the
    dealer.
  - It beeps continuously while moving. If it hits an obstruction it
    stops and briefly reverses.
  - With the remote, the carriage stops at mid-park and the rail folds
    or unfolds while the button is held.
  - If it won't lower, get off at the top and call the dealer.

**Charging:** it **charges anywhere it's parked on the rail**
(continuous charge). This answers TechMate's open question.

**Charger LED:**

| LED | Meaning |
|---|---|
| None | No AC or a failed charger |
| Red | AC on |
| Flashing red | Bad batteries |
| Yellow | Charging |
| Flashing green | Reverse polarity |

**Remotes:**
- **2 AA, changed yearly.**
- IR receivers on both sides of the carriage.
- Keep sunlight off the receivers.
- Clean the lenses with a non-wax cleaner.
- Don't use a remote while riding.

**Owner care:**
- **Pledge on the rail monthly** (not the gear rack).
- Charger always plugged in.
- **Yearly dealer service.**
- Long storage: breaker off, charger unplugged, then recharge before
  use.

**Owner troubleshooting:**
- Beeping at the top with the seat turned = multi-user mode. The dealer
  can set single-user.
- **Clean the charge strips with isopropyl wipes.** Red only (not
  yellow) means the carriage isn't on the strips, they're dirty, or a
  wire is off.
- Check the on/off switch, the outlet, the house breaker, and for a
  switched outlet.

**Warranty:** lifetime on major components, 2 years on parts, 30 days
labor.
