# ILS - Folding Rail Install and Service Sheets

Folding-rail sheets for the SRE-2010 and SRE-3000/3050.
- **Installing (ILS-01383 tech tips, 2018; ILS-01497, 2022):** don't cut the pivot-tube strap or remove the joint shipping bracket until the rail is on the stairs; fish-tape the charge harness through the rail. **Special folding-rail bracket** on the first step: **2 in from the wall, 2½ in from the step nose** (rail about 4 in from the wall). Gear rack segments 7/8 in from the short-rail end; tighten the **jacking bolt only until the rack stops rattling (no impact driver)**. Remaining mounts: **2 in (no carpet) / 2½ in (carpet) minimum** off every step nose. Use the stacked end plate and wire-relief spacer, then a **⅛ in spacer** at the base: **bottom of the rail ⅛ in or less off the floor** (landing pad SRE-30492WG for thick carpet). Primary bracket bolt **5 ft-lb**, special bracket **50 ft-lb**; floor support bar screws B to just touch the floor, then A.
- **Comms (ILS-01383):** the main board talks to the folding-rail board through the **third finger and the purple wire**. Rail stops abruptly, works intermittently or needs several presses, or no slow-down at the soft stops → check the carriage contacts on the charge and mid-park strips; on the strip the charger should show **red + yellow or red + green** (red only = bad contact). Disable switch: **"I" on, "O" off**.
- **Bounce-back:** bend the limit switch roller up slightly (ILS-01383); 2011 old-style version ILS-01004 (= the tech tip); on the 2022 rail, back off the floor support screws B (ILS-01497).
- **Retaining plate kit (ILS-01394, 2018):** riveted to the primary bracket to stop the internal gear shafts pushing out; standard on new rails.
- **Replacing folding-rail gear rack (ILS-01550, 2022)** without removing the carriage: carriage on the short rail for the upper rack (end plate, midrail segment T25; don't let rack slam; jacking bolt finger tight then **no more than one turn**), then carriage above the mid-park, rail folded, for the short-rail rack (kits SRV-3050-022L/R or -023L/R); set the joint with a piece of rack across it.
- **Carriage safety bracket (ILS-01577):** kit **SRE-K-3089L/R** (Elite and Elan brackets); see [[Folding-Rail]]. Check it by pressing the rail's lower switch and driving gently into the folded rail: it must stop; adjust the rail limit switch if not.
- **Pivoting end plate (ILS-01502, 02/05/2021, kit SRE-K-3111):** folding rail up; remove the old end plate with the rail clamp tool on to stop the gear rack falling out; bolt base end plate and pivot plate (pivot finger-tight); clamp tool at the joint, lower the rail; jacking screw just snug against the gear rack; fit the cross pin (SRE-K-3055) on the short rail; set the gap under the end-cap bumpers so the floor is **no more than ⅛ in** off the hard surface (mounting pad for thick carpet); clamp the pivot plate, tighten the stand-foot bolts, raise the rail and drill 8 mm for the shoulder bolt; check the gap along the bumpers.
- **Shipping (ILS-01073):** cut the actuator zip tie, bolt the actuator snug but free to pivot. **ILS-00913:** short linkage bar SRE-20918 orientation and roll pin flush.
