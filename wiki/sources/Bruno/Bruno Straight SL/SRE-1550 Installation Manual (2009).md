# SRE-1550 Installation Manual (Electra-Ride II, 02-09-2009)

Dealer install manual for the legacy **SRE-1550 Electra-Ride II**
straight stairlift, residential and commercial (44 pages; © 1996–2009).

**Specs:**
- **350 lb**, **variable 0–38 ft/min**.
- 24 VDC 2-pole 1.02 hp. Self-locking gearbox plus a **belt reduction**
  (motor pulley) to rack and pinion.
- Worm-gear brake. 45° maximum.
- Rail: a **steel channel with an integral rack**.
- Swivel 0°, 45° and 90° in both directions, top and bottom.

**Install:**
- **Rail ½–1 in above the nosing.** Run a plumb line along the rail and
  shim any bow.
- **Clamps:**
  - At least **1½ in from the wall** (more if the seat swivels toward
    the wall), with the nut toward the wall.
  - Same placement pattern as the SRE-2000.
  - One screw at the wall side of the top and bottom clamps first, to
    allow adjustment.
- **Carriage:** breaker off, remove the left motor cover, slide it in,
  and **turn the motor pulley by hand** to draw the carriage onto the
  rail.
- **Angle bracket: 4 bolts at 70 ft-lb.**
- **Final limit switch and ramp:** adjust the limit switches at both
  ends. The motor-end switch is reached by sliding the charge contact
  out. Failure to adjust them is called out as a hazard.

**Commercial overspeed:**
- **Before installing the carriage**, line up the **white mark on the
  cam** with the white mark on the housing.
- A trip during service: **rotate the cam back to its detent** (bypass
  the switch and drive up).
- **A real overspeed trip means removing the whole drive** and returning
  it for diagnosis.

**Electrical:** 4-gang DIP (modes, #3 hand, #4 coast delay), learn
remotes, and charger LEDs and fuse, all as on the SRE-2000.

**Lubrication:** first run it 5–6 times and **vacuum out paint chips**,
then white lithium on the rack teeth only. Carriages with **steel
wheels** also grease the rail.

**Speed pot:** start fully CCW, then turn CW to the **user's weight
range** mark, test with the rider, and lock the collet.

**Yearly:**
- **Belts** (dry or worn).
- **Rail wear: there should be no groove.**
- Contacts, a battery load test, the safeties, the charger output, the
  speed, the belt and the hardware.

**Belt replacement:** tension the new belt and torque the motor bolts per
drawing **SRE-00648**.

**Troubleshooting:** 16–28 VDC. Check the charger is on. Backwards =
wrong hand.
