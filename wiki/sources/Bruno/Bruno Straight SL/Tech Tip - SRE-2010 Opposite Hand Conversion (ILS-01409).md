# Tech Tip - SRE-2010 Opposite Hand Conversion (ILS-01409)

ILS-01409 Rev 0 (07-30-2018), 36 pages: English (pp. 1–12), then Spanish and French translations of the same 12 pages. Residential SRE-2010 units only. A Bruno dealer-portal tech tip (portal tech-tip 215). It's the same conversion the [[SRE-2010 Tech Support Guide]] carries, as a standalone sheet.

- **No power tools**: they strip vital screws.
- **Seat swivel plate:** unplug the board harness and limit switch, remove the 1-1/2" seat jam nut, move the cam to the other face of the actuator ring and flip it, slide the limit switch to the other side of its spacer plate (don't flip it), then move every swivel-plate part to the opposite side one at a time. The limit-switch roller must sit in the cam notch, and the switch must trip only past 0°. **Loctite 242 on the jam nut, 70 ft-lb (95 Nm).**
- **Swivel angles** (optional, done at the same time): bolt 1 sets 0° (ship) or 45°, bolt 2 sets 90° (ship) or 60°.
- **DIP switch 3 ON = right hand.** Until the seat is on and the DIP set, the unit runs down faster than up.
- **Carriage:** pull both batteries (**same orientation going back in**, lower battery first), re-pivot the carriage to the opposite side, and use the angle-bolt table: 32°–50° bolt holes A+B, 25°–35° A+C, under 25° any two top holes as far apart as possible. **Four adjustment bolts at 50 ft-lb, safety washer on each.**
- **Rocker switch** (optional): move the harness to the other arm. Green to the "fat" side, black middle, red to the "slim" side.
- **Footrest:** flip the gas spring to the other side of the lift-link tab and mirror the lever (Loctite 242 on its button-head screw).
