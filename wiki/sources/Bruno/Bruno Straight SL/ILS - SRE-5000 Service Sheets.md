# ILS - SRE-5000 Service Sheets

Eighteen 2026 SRE-5000 service and install sheets (April–August 2026). Summarized by task on [[SRE-5000]].
- **Remotes (ILS-01644):** remote DIP 1/2 (default 1 up, 2 down); breaker off, top cover off; breaker on, wait for the beep; **hold LEARN/ERASE 10 s** to erase (both board LEDs flash 3 s); hold LEARN/ERASE and tap a remote button until the remote's LED goes out (green board LED flashes, then solid 3 s).
- **Paddle switch to the other arm (ILS-01645):** armrest covers, arm side caps, barrier switch covers (T25); move the paddle SRE-50071WG and foot-fold UI cover across; **letters "R B G" toward the seat bottom**. **Paddle replacement (ILS-01657):** pry the armrest cover up, unplug, swap.
- **Charger LEDs (ILS-01653):** none = no AC or charger failed; **red** = AC on (normal); **red flashing = defective batteries** (load test); **yellow or green** = charging/OK; **green flashing = reverse polarity** (check the rail strip wiring).
- **Folding rail:** actuator/gas spring replacement (ILS-01654: side panel pins, quick-connect pin and gas spring clip with the rail up, lower gently, swap, extend the new actuator with a **12 V battery** to refit the pin); **switch assembly** (ILS-01659: 2 mm hex; manual rail uses one switch, power rail two; note wire colours); **adjustable stand feet** SRE-K-5015WG 6.47–7.97 in (folding rail) and SRE-K-5039WG 8.72–10.22 in (ILS-01630).
- **Carriage:** wheels (ILS-01655: carriage off, safety flaps off, four wheels on ¼ in hex shoulder bolts **17 Nm**); charge contact (ILS-01656: back cover, two M5 screws); **gear motor** (ILS-01672: front/top covers, IR sensors, key switch (5000G) or manual override disable switch (5000CAN), hanger plate; leads **M1 brown, M2 blue**; one brake lead to the PCB, one to the breaker harness; three screws **14 Nm**).
- **Motor controller PCB, by serial:** **SRV-5000-018 for units up to SN 26080361089**; **SRV-5000-001 for SN 26080361090 and later** (ILS-01669, ILS-01668). ESD strap; note every harness; battery housing loose until the covers line up.
- **Options:** flood sensor **SRE-K-5042L/R** (ILS-01628; sensor under the front cover in series with the 3-pin safety connector); USB kit **SRE-K-5043WG** (ILS-01648); armrest power-footrest fold switch **SRE-K-5025 (NA)** / **SRE-K-5026 (global/CAN)** (ILS-01612/01613: start with the switch down so the **break-away footrest** reconnects at power-up).
- **Door at the top (ILS-01666):** standard mount on the first step, **adjustable mount on the step below the top landing**; raise the rail until its bottom is **7.5 in above the upper landing**; standard mount at least 1.5 in (2 in carpeted) from the step nose.
- **Australian addendum (ILS-01664):** max **45°**, rail earthing kit SRE-K-5045. (It says "ILS-01617 notes that the SRE-3050G can be installed at angles up to 52°": a copy-paste slip for SRE-5000G.)
