# Tech Tip - SRE-3000 Battery Life

1-page dealer Q&A (portal tech-tip 182). Bruno's answers:
- **Trips per charge:** same as the older SRE-2750, **10–15 trips**. Bruno's test (full batteries, full rider weight, 16 ft run) gave **10–11**.
- **Battery life:** about the same as other Bruno lifts (the dealer's rule of thumb was 3–5 years). It depends on **depth of discharge**: heavy users or many trips discharge deeper and shorten it.
