# SRE-5000 Manual and Power Folding Rail Install Guide (5000-FLDRL-I, 03-27-2026)

A 9-page supplement to the [[SRE-5000 Install Manual]] for the **manual
and power folding rail**. Kit SRE-K-5008WG, adjustable stand foot
SRE-K-5015WG. Grade 10.9 torques are the same as the main manual.

**End plate:**
- Floor support bar on the lower holes (2 × M6 × 16), bolts at the top of
  the slots.
- Jack screw bar on top, with 2 × M6 × 35 screwed down to the support
  bar.

**Rail:**
- A one-piece fold section can't be cut **shorter than 75 in**. Join any
  extra section the standard way.
- **Charge harness** through the slot in the back of the rail and out the
  upper end (fish tape helps).

**On the steps:**
- 1/8 in floor spacer and **carpet plate** at the bottom. The cardboard
  insert across two noses near the top acts as a temporary support.
- Adjustable mounts on the first step down from the top, and **2 steps
  above the actuator shroud**.
- **Rail bottom at least 3 in off the first step nose.** Rail front edge
  **at least 6 in from the nearest obstruction**.
- Set the support tube as vertical as possible, then remove the spacers.
  **Keep the 1/8 in gap** under the end plate, parallel to the floor
  (adjusting screw on the support bar). The gap holds until the carriage
  crosses the joint.

**Gear rack:** don't let it free-fall (it can knock the factory joint out
of line). Cut the last piece ½ in short.

**Carriage:** **set the LH/RH DIP switch before driving onto a folding
rail**, or the rail can be damaged.

**Charge harness:**
- Trim it if more than 6 in sticks out, and refit the connectors.
- Y-harness: blue to the rail harness, large and small female to the + and
  − contacts.
- Charger at the top: the other Y leads go to the charger. Breaker OFF,
  slide the contact into the rail, then the spacer and end plate. **Jack
  screw 5 ft-lb.**
- Charger at the bottom: through the rear circuit board cover and the
  shroud slot.

**Test:**
- Paddle down from the top: the rail unfolds as it goes and **slows at
  mid-park** to confirm.
- Going up: it folds once past mid-park.
- **On the remote it stops at mid-park and then folds.**
