# Tech Tip - SRE-3000 Battery (MK ES3-12, BTR-12008)

Two 1-page copies of the MK Battery **ES3-12** data sheet (one titled "SRE-3000 BTR-12008 Battery Specifications (11-2013)"; portal tech-tips 154/40 and 64). So the SRE-3000's **BTR-12008** battery is an **MK ES3-12**: 12 V, **3 Ah** (20 h rate), sealed lead-acid, F1 (faston) terminals, about 3 lb.

Key numbers: charge 14.4–15.0 V cycle use (max 0.9 A), float 13.5–13.8 V; charge at **0–40 °C (32–104 °F)**; cycle life 200 cycles at 100% depth of discharge, 225 at 80%, 500 at 50%; standby life 3–5 years; shelf charge retention 92% at 1 month, 80% at 6 months.

Matches the SRE-3050's spec of 2 × 3 Ah 12 V batteries ([[SRE-3050 Tech Support Guide]]); the wiki hasn't confirmed the part number for the 3050.
