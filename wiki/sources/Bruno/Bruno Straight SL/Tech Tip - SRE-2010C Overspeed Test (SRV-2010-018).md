# Tech Tip - SRE-2010C Overspeed Test (SRV-2010-018)

19-page procedure for the **SRV-2010-018** overspeed test kit on the **SRE-2010C** (commercial) carriage (portal tech-tip 39). It's a **drop test**, not the trip-key test in the install manuals.

- **Safety:** don't run the kit's induction heater near anyone with a pacemaker or hearing aid, or near sensitive electronics. Read the heater's own manual first.
- **Torque table (metric grade 10.9):** M8 25 lb-ft, M10 47, M12 83, M14 133.
- **Prep:** carriage off the rail, gear guard off, pull the **spur gear** with the supplied puller (discard the retaining ring), remove the 6 mm key, degrease the gear. Clamp the rail with **FIX-00525** about 24 in from the top, refit the carriage, fit the test end cap and quick-release belt, and hang the carriage on the belt. Safety brackets stay off during the test.
- **Test:** power on, press and hold "Press to Release". The overspeed must stop the free-falling carriage **within 1.5 in (38 mm)**. Write the test date on the spur gear with the paint pen.
- **Restore:** heat the spur gear to **360 °F** on the induction heater and slip it back on the output shaft, fit a new E-clip, **check the 6 mm key is in place**, then refit gear guard, switch brackets and safety brackets (don't over-tighten), covers, charge contact and end cap.
