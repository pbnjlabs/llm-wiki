# SRE-2750 Installation Manual (Electra-Ride LT, MAN-2750-1, 05-17-2011)

Dealer install manual for the legacy **SRE-2750 Electra-Ride LT**, a
lighter straight stairlift that came before the Elan
([[SRE-3000]]) (42 pages). It is an FDA Class II patient transporter, and
the dealer must report malfunctions.

**Specs:**
- **275 lb**, **0–32 ft/min**.
- 24 VDC 2-pole **½ hp**, self-locking gearbox, rack and pinion.
- Worm-gear brake. **45° maximum.**
- Rail: an **aluminum channel with an integral rack**.
- Swivel 0°, 67° and (optional) 90°, top and bottom.
- 24 VDC 2 A switch-mode charger.
- Stairs at least 32 in wide.

**Tools:** the **FIX-00525 joint plate clamp** ("highly recommended"),
snap ring pliers, and ⅛, 5/16 and ½ in bits.

**Rail:**
- **Length** = stair length + measurement B. The seat sits 22 in at its
  lowest; reduce B by 1.5 in per inch of extra seat. The chair extends
  about 2 in past the rail at the top.
- **Adjustable clamp set SRE-27291** if A is under 3 or B is over 12.
- No section under 18 in.
- **Two-piece rails:** cut the plain end (2 holes) and drill the end cap
  holes through the cap (5/16 in).
- **Custom one-piece rail:**
  1. Remove the rack and keep the M6 × 25.
  2. Cut the joint end, then cut the rack's unthreaded end so it is **3–4
     in shorter than the rail**.
  3. Drill a 0.281 in ("I") hole and re-fix the rack with the M6 × 25
     fully threaded.
- **Charge strip holes:**
  - Template SRE-27204 with kit SRE-K-2723, 10 holes at 5/16 in.
  - Enlarge holes 3, 4, 7 and 8 to **½ in**.
- **Joint plate:** the rack faces the middle of the stairs with the
  teeth toward the wall, and the chamfers point up. 2 screws from below
  and 10 from above, flush.
- **Clamps:**
  - Bottom landing, first tread, top landing, first tread down, above
    and below the joints, then at least every third tread.
  - If a landing can't be drilled, add a clamp on the second-to-last
    step and at the top.
- **Wall clearance (back edge of the rail): 2.5 in, or 3.5 in with the
  90° swivel.**
- **Clamp bolt only 10 ft-lb** (1–1½ turns). **Over-tightening deforms
  the rail.**
- **Height off the noses:** **2 in (5 cm)** on hardwood. On thick carpet
  set it 2.5 in so it ends up at least 2 in.
- A 16 mm shim goes under the bottom while setting. **Shim dips over
  6 mm.** On a carpet runner, shim the wood side only.
- **Wedge locks:** only when the rail bottom doesn't reach the floor,
  on the **first and last clamps**, teeth up, swinging in from the top
  side. Tap them home.

**Carriage:**
- Slide it on by hand until the gear meets the rack (keep the guide pads
  clean). At first power-up the remote may take **up to 10 s** to
  respond.
- **Angle:** level the footrest, then **3 × M10 gold-zinc 80 ft-lb**.
- **RH:** remove the M8 **safety lock screw**, pivot the seat post
  across, and refit the lock screw through the angle retainer plate.
- **Seat:** keep the snap ring. Route the harnesses through the pivot
  tube. Remove the carriage jumper and **keep it on the harness**.
- **Final limit ramp:** a snap-on ramp set just before the switch roller
  touches it. 2 × M5.

**Electrical:**
- **4 charge strip blocks:** red jumpers to blocks 1 and 3, black to 2
  and 4.
- Charger at the top or bottom. In the cable channel, ribbed = red,
  smooth = black. A plug lock is suggested.
- Charger LEDs (2.2 A). **Fuse Bussmann GMD-2A slow-blow, 5 × 20 mm.**
- IR transmitters on **Velcro** (bracket kit SRE-K-1580 optional).
- **Codes:** same 4-mode DIP as the SRE-2000, but switch-active-at-power-up
  is **2 beeps, pause, 5 beeps**, with long beeps marking the active
  switches.

**Adjustments:** seat height, seat cover gap, seat swivel. Lubrication,
long-term storage, troubleshooting, the yearly list and the schematic
are as on the [[SRE-2000 Installation Manual (2007)]].
