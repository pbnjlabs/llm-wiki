# Tech Tip - SRE-3000 Overspeed Reset on the Rail

Two copies of one 2-page tip (Bruno article 104, modified 4/23/2014; portal tech-tips 174 and 43): resetting an SRE-3000 overspeed **without taking the carriage off the rail**.

1. Remove the safety flap cover (two screws, on the key side). The overspeed is then visible.
2. Push its lower corner fully right, then fully left.
3. With the unit **on**, bring it back to centre so the switch roller sits in the **centre of the valley**. You should hear a click and the board's power-up beep. Voltage back at board B+/B− confirms it.
