# ILS - SRE-3050 Seat Reinforcement and Seat Bottom (Service Bulletin 81)

The two fixes behind **Bruno Service Bulletin 81** for the SRE-3050 seat (ILS-01500 Rev 6-15-2021, a UK copy ILS-01500-G, and ILS-01501, 4/8/2021).
- **Steel plate addition (ILS-01500):** kit plate **SRE-30551** with hardware **SRV-K-3052** (with drill bit and hex bit) or **SRV-K-3053**. Remove the five MTFS-04003 screws (discard) and the upholstery; **inspect the seat bottom**: a crack **all the way through** (hinge, rib) = replace it (kit **SRV-3050-020**: give Bruno the serial and end-user details); shallow or partial crazing = reuse. Fit the plate either way: eight M6 flat-head screws started with the drill on its **lowest speed/torque** (fast melts the plastic), finished by hand; drill two #20 holes and fit two rivets with adhesive washers; refit the upholstery with the thicker **MTFS-04501** screws, snug only.
- **Seat bottom replacement (ILS-01501):** pry off the hinge cover SRE-30477 (discard) and pull the damper DPR-00101; unscrew the switch bracket SRE-30513; at 45° lever the seat bottom out of its pivot with a screwdriver (snap tabs may break); move the upholstery to the new SRE-30554; snap it in at 45°; damper (**dot up**) in the new cover; **copy the serial number onto the new seat bottom's decal** and cover with clear overlay DEC-00667.
