# SRE-2010E Operator Manual (03-05-2026)

Owner's manual for the **Elite outdoor straight** stairlift, residential
and commercial (12 pages).

**Specs:**
- 400 lb. **45° maximum.**
- Swivel stops 0°, 60° and 90° at the top (optional at the bottom).
- **0–125 °F.** The page calls the seat the "Elite Curve" and says it
  swivels 135°, an apparent copy from the CRE manual.

**Weather:**
- Clear **ice and snow** from the cover and the footrest safeties.
- No pressure washer. Not within **20 minutes of lightning**.
- Have the dealer inspect it **after a harsh winter**.
- **Use the seat cover:** take it off to ride (drawstring) and store it
  in the pouch under the seat.
- **Never remove the bottom carriage covers** except for service.

**Key switch:** OFF disables the unit and both remotes.

**Charging:**
- It charges **only when parked tightly against the bracket at either
  end**; elsewhere the batteries slowly drain. Always park at an end.
- **C3 = off charge and not parked.** The other codes match the indoor
  [[SRE-2010 Owner's Manual]] (C1, C2, C4–C6, L1–L3, L6, L8).
- **Charger LEDs:** same Elite 4-LED table. The charger lives indoors;
  if it gets wet, don't use it until it's inspected.

**Remotes:** wall-mounted, keyed, **9 V battery, yearly**.

**Paint:** touch up scratches on receipt, after install and yearly.
Rust from untreated scratches can void the warranty.

**Storage:**
- Breaker off. Remove the keys and the **batteries** (store them per
  the battery maker). Charger indoors, cover on.
- **Return to service:**
  1. Reinstall the batteries.
  2. Clean and dry the seat and carriage.
  3. **Re-grease the gear rack.**
  4. Breaker on, charger in, then test-run.

**Warranty:** outdoor, 5 years on major components, 2 years on parts.
