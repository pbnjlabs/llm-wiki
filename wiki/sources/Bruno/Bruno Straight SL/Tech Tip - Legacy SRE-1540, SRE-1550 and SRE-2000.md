# Tech Tip - Legacy SRE-1540, SRE-1550 and SRE-2000

Three tips for Bruno's **older straight stairlifts** (portal tech-tips 56, 57, 37).

**SRE-1540 vs SRE-1550 seat** (drawing, 2009): SRE-1540 large seat SRE-00059 with **fixed arms**, 15.79 in deep, 18.88 in between the arms. SRE-1550 seat SRE-00528: 13.92 in deep, 16.44 in between the arms closed, about 20 in open.

**SRE-1550 manual overspeed test** (tools: 13 mm wrenches, 50/50 isopropyl alcohol and water, paper towel, duct tape): end cap off; jog the carriage to the end; clean the overspeed (LH: wipe left while turning it right; reverse for RH); put a ½ x 12 in strip of duct tape on the overspeed up to the alignment mark; run down ¾–1½ in and **yank the tape tail**. That trips the overspeed and cuts all power. Bruno suggests practising before the official test. Then **breaker off**, move the carriage ¾–1½ in toward the top **by the belts**, realign the marks, remove all tape residue, breaker on, check operation.

**Overspeed test fixture** (FIX-00381 to FIX-00385; **SRE-1550/2000 only**): it drives the motor at **over 96 VDC** to overspeed the carriage downhill. The lift must be **unoccupied**, the stairs blocked, nobody below. Follow state/local load and witness rules (rated load on the data plate). Strap test weights down (not the seat belt), **20% on the footrest and 80% on the seat**. Carriage several feet below the top; swap the heavy orange, blue, red and black board wires onto the fixture leads; press and hold the test switch. It should run down, trip and stop abruptly (if it runs up, the motor wiring is reversed). Reconnect per your notes, then reset: **CRE-2100, CRE-2110 and SRE-2000** by rotating the cam and repositioning the pin; **SRE-1540 and SRE-1550** by moving the carriage up with the large gearbox pulley and resetting the cam under the carriage. Run it several times to confirm.
