# SRE Folding Rail Install Manual (P/N 2010-FLDRL-I)

13-page installation supplement (dated 2026-04-10, folding rail carriage
safety bracket section dated 2022-05-09) explicitly written to cover the
folding-rail option for **both** the SRE-2010 Elite and SRE-3050 Elan —
title page just says "SRE Folding Rail." Covers:
- Pre-installation rules: never cut a folding-rail assembly shorter than
  75" (1905mm); minimum 2 rail mounts per mid-rail section
- Folding rail assembly steps: end-plate orientation, floor support bars,
  gear rack segments, torque specs (22 ft-lb end plate bolts, 5 ft-lb
  primary bracket, 50 ft-lb special folding rail bracket), landing pad
  (SRE-30492WG) for thick carpet, charge strip/gear rack installation
- Charger harness routing for both "charger at top of rail" and "charger at
  rail joint" configurations (SRE-3050 folding rail wiring specifically
  illustrated)
- **Folding Rail Carriage Safety Bracket** — a safety retrofit (separate
  part numbers/procedure per model: Elite SRE-2010 vs. Elan SRE-3050
  carriage) that stops the carriage if it bypasses mid-park and would
  otherwise impact the folded short rail. See [[Folding-Rail]].
- Customer education: manual vs. power folding rail behavior, pinch-point
  warning, disable switch use
