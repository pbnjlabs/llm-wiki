# SRE-1550 Operator Manual (Electra-Ride II, 01-14-2009)

Owner's manual for the legacy **SRE-1550** (22 pages).
- **Specs:** 350 lb, 45°, swivel 0°, 45° and 90° in both directions at
  the top and bottom. The speed is dealer-adjustable.
- **End charging:** a yellow LED on the upper and lower charge brackets
  shows contact.
- Charger LEDs, transmitter battery, storage and troubleshooting follow
  the [[SRE-2000 Operator Manual (2007)]].
