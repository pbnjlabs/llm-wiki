# SRE-3000 Operator Manual (2019, trilingual)

Owner's manual for the Elan **SRE-3000**: English pp. 1–18, then
Spanish and French (58 pages).

- **Specs:**
  - 300 lb, 45° (specials to 52°).
  - Swivel 0°, 67° and 90° **at the top only**.
  - **Charger 120 VAC 0.35 A in, 24 VDC 1 A out.**
- **Operation:** key switch (OFF disables the chair switch and the
  remotes). Rocker on the armrest. The seat locks at 0°, 67° and 90°.
  Power swivel option with jam sensing. Remotes are for an empty seat.
- **Power folding rail:**
  - Stops at the mid-park, beeps continuously while moving, and the
    **yellow LED** on the control panel flashes.
  - The **disable switch** stops folding.
  - In an emergency, switch it OFF and move the rail **gently** by hand.
- **Charging:** it **charges anywhere it's parked on the rail**.
  Single-LED charger as in the
  [[SRE-3000 Installation Manual (2019)]].
- Owner care, storage (breaker off, charger unplugged) and
  troubleshooting are as on the later Elan manuals.
