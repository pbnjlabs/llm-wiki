# Tech Tip - SRE-3000 Gear Rack Change (ILS-01079)

Bruno article 115 (ILS-01079, modified 4/23/2014; portal tech-tips 168 and 53, the second a 1-page image-only copy). The SRE-3000 gear rack changed part numbers:
- **SRE-30177 = new style**, **SRE-30066 = old style**. The ends are shaped differently.
- **The two can't be mixed on one rail.** Match the rack already on the rail when ordering.
