# ILS - SRE-2010 Specifications and Application Drawings

SRE-2010 Elite spec sheets and application drawings (current specs dated 07/22/26).
- **ILS-00826, SRE-2010 / 2010C / 2010OS (and CAN, G):** **400 lb** (2010G **420 lb**); A18.1 Sec 4 or 7 (2010C/OS listed to the 2017–2023 editions); 2 x 7 Ah; charger 105–230 VAC, 24 VDC 2 A; 0.68 hp, 58:1 worm gearbox; **IR** call/send; rail **2 in above the step nose**; angle **0–50° (SRE-2010)**, **30–50° (2010C, CAN, G, OS)**; **25 ft/min**; rails 15'6" and 19'8"; seat stops 45° (bottom), 0°, 60°, 90° (top); armrests 4 in width adjustment; **30 A breaker, 5 A fuse**, key switch on the carriage; 28–36 h on batteries with power off; 300 lb unit; gearbox tested at 3200 lb. Options include a commercial package with overspeed, power swivel (85°), power footrest, manual or power folding rail (30–45°), larger seat and footrest.
- **ILS-00797, SRE-2010E/EC/EOS (outdoor):** same drive; powder-coated steel over e-coat, stainless hardware, weatherized electronics, **IPX3** enclosure; **charger must be inside** (all outside wiring 24 V); charges at **either end** on watertight sliding contacts; **2.4 GHz RF** call/send, keyed controls standard; marine vinyl, bag cover; rails to 60 ft; **0 °F to 125 °F**; overspeed on EC/EOS.
- **ILS-00828:** older SRE-2010G spec (UK distributor, A18.1-2005 era).
- **Application drawings** (ILS-00827/01343 indoor, 00829/01344 2010G, 00799 outdoor, 01471 **XL seat**, 00853/00879 power options): rail **6 in from the wall** standard (top back of rail 4 in), outdoor **6⅛ in**, **XL seat 9⅛ in**; seat-swivelled distances at 60° and 90°. ILS-01549 is a standard-assemblies parts table for SRE-2010/C/OS.
