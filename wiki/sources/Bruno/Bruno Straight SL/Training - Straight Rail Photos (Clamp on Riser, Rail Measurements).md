# Training - Straight Rail Photos (Clamp on Riser, Rail Measurements)

Two photos from Bruno's dealer **training** library (Installation & Troubleshooting docs). No text comes with them, so this page only says what they show.

- **Adj Clamp on riser.jpg:** the bottom of a straight rail with an **adjustable clamp bolted to the face of the bottom riser** (slotted plates, two bolts each side), not standing on a tread. A rail foot stands on the tread above it.
- **ramping the rail measurements.jpg:** a straight rail on adjustable stand feet on a training staircase, its top end resting against the top landing. Yellow tags on the door above the landing read **"SRE 3050 / 7.5″ / ELAN"**, **"ELITE / SRE 2010 / 6″"** and **"SRE 2010 C / 8″"**. The photo doesn't say what the three measurements are.

**Open:** what the three figures measure (and when Bruno wants the riser clamp) still needs confirming with the human or Bruno before any page states it.
