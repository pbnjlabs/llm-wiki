# SRE-2000 Installation Manual (Electra-Ride Elite, MAN-2000-1, 11-14-2007)

Dealer install manual for the legacy **SRE-2000 Electra-Ride Elite**
residential straight stairlift (56 pages). This was the predecessor of
the [[SRE-2010]] Elite. CSA-listed. Indoor, heated, above 35 °F.

**Specs:**
- **400 lb**, **variable speed 0–28 ft/min**.
- 24 VDC 2-pole **1.02 hp** motor, self-locking gearbox, rack and pinion.
- **Brake: self-locking worm gear only.**
- **45° maximum.**
- Rail: a **vertical aluminum plate with an integral gear rack**.
- Swivel 0°, 30°, 60° and 90° **at the top and bottom**.
- IR call/send (9 V transmitters).

**Rail:**
- **Length** = stair length + measurement B (from the application
  guide), **plus 5 in with an overspeed brake**. Cut excess off the
  **lower** end, and cut the cable channel back about 3 in first.
- No section under **18 in**. **Never cut the joint end** (the M6 gear
  rack bolts stay).
- Drill the end holes with the metal template (⅛ in pilot, then 5/16 in).
- **Clamps** (longer foot outside):
  - Bottom landing, first tread up, top landing, and first tread down.
    **No clamp on the top landing with the standard extension.**
  - The tread above and below each joint, then at least **every third
    tread**.
  - At least one clamp under the **B lock clamp**. Drill through it with
    a ¼ in bit and fit M6 with the nut on the wall side.
- **Joint:**
  - Loosen the 5 joint-plate screws.
  - Fit 2 × M6 × 45 Allen and 3 × M8 × 16. If the rack won't line up,
    loosen the M5 set screws two turns.
  - Anti-seize helps.
  - **Tighten the 4 M6 Allen screws into the rack first**, then the M6
    hex.
- **Rail height:** **1¼ in above every step nose** (**1½ in** with the
  extended footrest). The rail must be vertical or lean slightly back,
  **never forward**.
- **Clearance:** **2⅝ in from the back of the rail to the nearest
  obstruction**. Seat to wall **½–1 in**.
- On carpet, seat the clamps with a dead-blow hammer first. Wood-tread
  fasteners only; pilot holes in hardwood.

**Carriage:**
- Lift it onto the recessed rack, then feed it on with the remote.
- **Angle:** loosen the 2 **M14** bolts (22 mm) and level the carriage at
  the top.
- **Footrest:** the longer **M14 × 25** bolts are on the LH or RH side
  (marked). The upper hole puts the footrest about 3½ in up, the lower
  hole about 5 in.
- **Seat post:** 2 × M10 × 20 (upper) plus the front cover mount
  (lower). The seat height uses the same 4 M10 × 20.
- **Final limit ramp kit** on the top section: 2 × M6 × 30 for the block,
  then the slotted bar.
- **Charge brackets:** SRE-20286 and SRE-20287 swap ends by hand. In the
  rail channel, ribbed lead (female) = red +, smooth (male) = black −.

**Electrical:**
- 25 A breaker with the on/off switch.
- **4-gang DIP:**
  - #1/#2 diagnostic mode: multi-user OFF/OFF, single-user OFF/ON, quiet
    ON/OFF, battery warnings only ON/ON.
  - **#3 = right-hand.**
  - **#4 = coast delay**, 600 ms normally; set it ON for **900 ms** if
    the remote signal drops out.
- **Learn remotes:**
  1. Set SW3 on the transmitter (4 channels).
  2. Hold LEARN/ERASE about **12 s** to clear.
  3. Hold it again and press a transmitter button until the LED goes out.
- **Charger:** red/yellow/green LEDs. **AC fuse: Buss GSL 2, 5 × 20 mm.**
  Turn the charger's own power switch on.
- **Audio codes:**
  - Chirp = power-up or a safety.
  - **4 short + 1 long every 3 min = stopped off the charge bumper.**
  - 3 short + 2 long = battery voltage drop.
  - **2 short + 3 long = critical** (under 16 V).
  - Chirp every 3 s = seat safety.
  - 1 short + 2 long = transmitter memory full.

**Adjustments:**
- **Speed potentiometer** on the carriage: loosen the collet nut,
  **clockwise = faster**.
- **Right-hand conversion:**
  1. DIP 3 ON.
  2. Pivot the carriage on its M14 bolts.
  3. Swap the long and short front M14 bolts.
  4. Move the footrest handle to the upstairs side.
- The rocker can be moved to the left arm.

**Lubrication:** **white lithium grease on the gear rack only.** Bruno
now says PTFE for all CRE/SRE ([[ILS-01446 Maintenance Control Plan for CRE-SRE Chair Lifts]]).

**Yearly:**
- Clean and regrease.
- **Check the belts.**
- Check rail wear.
- Scotch-Brite the charge contacts.
- Load test the batteries.
- Check the safety switches and the charger output (moving up, on and
  off the contacts).
- Check the speed, belt, wiring, swivel and hardware.

**Overspeed (optional) reset, two levers:**
1. Rotate the overspeed switch lever while pushing the wires in to
   engage the switch. Too far and it won't reset.
2. Rotate the cam lever **just until** the switch engages.

**Troubleshooting:**
- Battery voltage **16–28 VDC**.
- Backwards, slow up and fast down = wrong hand.
- Remote dropouts: coast delay to 900 ms.

The drive assembly notes call for **Loctite 242 on the set screws (50
in-lb)** and **Loctite 680 in the pulley bores**.
