# Tech Tip - SRE-3000 Serial Numbers and Carriage Decals

Two copies of Bruno article 113 (modified 4/23/2014; portal tech-tips 178 and 54) plus the SRE-3000 carriage decal sheet (portal tech-tip 185).

Where the SRE-3000 serial number is:
- On the **carriage**: back of the battery housing, on the motor, and on the board mounting panel. The **carriage box** is the only box with one.
- A loose **serial/date-code decal** is zip-tied to the carriage **circuit breaker** with the key and a card. The installer sticks it in the window of the **data plate under the seat**. Seats and footrests aren't tied to one carriage, so they carry only month/year date codes.
- The **product registration sheet** with the serial is in the carriage carton.
- Decal sheet: barcode serial labels for the two final-inspection sheets, paperwork and back of carriage; serial on motor, box and back of electrical panel; "O/S TESTED" on the overspeed cover; "Disconnect switch STOP" by the breaker. The card says **don't lose the carriage key** and **don't turn the breaker on until the carriage is on the rail**.
