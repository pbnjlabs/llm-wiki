# Tech Tip - SRE-2010E Clamp Deterioration

1-page Bruno note (June 29, 2012, clamp kit SRE-K-2131T; portal tech-tip 47). **Older-generation SRE-2010E (outdoor) clamps are die-cast aluminium** and can be eaten away. Bruno's guess was an **acid wash** used to clean the steps, or a galvanic reaction. Current clamps are a thermoformed composite that resists both.

**Corroded die-cast clamps must be replaced right away.** Bruno's wording is emphatic.
