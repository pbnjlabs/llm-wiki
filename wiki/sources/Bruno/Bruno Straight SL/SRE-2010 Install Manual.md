# SRE-2010 Install Manual (2010-I, 02-25-2026)

Install manual for the **Elite indoor straight rail**: SRE-2010, plus
the 2010C and 2010OS overspeed versions (28 pages). The specs are on
ILS-00826 and the drawings on ILS-01343 and ILS-01471
([[ILS - SRE-2010 Specifications and Application Drawings]]). Units with
the overspeed gear (OSG) go in under ASME A18.1-2023, units without it
under A18.1-2014. **Indoor, heated, above 35 °F.**

**Tools and torque:** an **18 in** extension on the 10 mm magnetic
socket, a 1½ in adjustable wrench (for a hand change), a 50–80 ft-lb
torque wrench, and threadlocker. Grade 10.9 only: **M6 7 ft-lb, M8 25,
M10 47**.

**Rail length:**
- **Stair length (top nose to floor) + 9 in** gives a 20 in seat. Add
  1.5 in per extra inch of seat.
- **Add 4 in for an overspeed brake** (2010C/OS/G).
- Door at the top: stair length + nose-to-door + 2 in, plus 4 in with an
  OSG.
- The armrest sticks out 5–8 in past the rail at the top.
- Seat height adjusts in 1 in steps, up to 3 in.
- **"16 ft" rail = 186 in, "20 ft" rail = 236 in.**
- No piece under **24 in**; if one would be, take at least 12 in off two
  rails. Never cut a factory joint end.

**Rail prep:**
- **Bottom:** charge strip carrier and charge contact at the bottom.
  Bend the slow-down strip tab down a little (not touching the rail).
  End plate on 5/16-18 self-threading screws, **no impact driver**.
- **Joint:** pin plus upper and lower joint brackets halfway in.
  Screws no more than 2¾ in from the joint.
- **Top:** charge contact, slow-down ramp and **final limit bracket**
  (upper edge flush with the charge contact).
- **2010C/OS top:** **overspeed end stop** SRE-21254WG on nut bar
  SRE-30490, 2 in from the end, plus a 4 in carrier filler.
- **Carrier:** trim it **2.5 in shorter than the rail** (6½ in on
  2010C/OS), or it goes wavy and noisy.

**Rail mounts:**
- At least 2 per segment and 3 on a one-piece rail. **No more than 3
  steps without one.**
- **No mount on the top landing** as standard. Use one on the **first
  two steps down from the top**, one on the **bottom landing**, and one
  on the first step up.
- **Overrun:** more than 6 in horizontal or 12 in diagonal past the top
  nose needs an adjustable mount set SRE-30150WG on the landing.
- **Ramped rail:** rail height above the top landing is **6 in, or 8 in
  with an OSG**.
- No drilling at the bottom (tile or concrete): put an adjustable mount
  on the riser.
- **Long nose of the mount toward the middle of the stairs**, with no
  gap. Rail mount bolts **25 ft-lb**, tapped down in the slot first.
- Nut bars: one per mount plus one over each joint.

**Clearances:**
- Bottom of the rail **1/8 in off the floor**.
- Off the step nose: **2 in, 2.5 in on carpet**.
- **Back of the top of the rail to the nearest obstruction: 4.25 in
  (7.25 in with the wide seat).** Measure at the top, middle and bottom.
  The ILS spec sheet gives "6 in from the wall", measured differently.
- Fix the top mount with 2 screws, wall side first. Check clearances,
  then fix the rest, back screws first.
- Running clearances: **footrest to nose ½–¾ in, seat to wall ½ in.**

**Gear rack:** slide it in carefully (a free-fall knocks the joint out).
The top piece ends within ½–1¼ in of the rail end; cut it ½ in short.

**Carriage:**
- Under 25°, adjust the carriage angle off the rail.
- Breaker off. Remove the upper charge contact and the top two rack
  pieces, then slide the carriage on (never lift it by the switch
  housing). Serial decal on the seat.
- **Jumpers:** LH = 3-pin and 6-pin. **RH = 3-pin, 6-pin and 12-pin.**
- Drive about 6 in on with the remote.
- Overspeed tripped (C/OS): rotate the cover, or turn the shaft with a
  **5/16 in** socket or **SRE-21182**.
- Several lifts: reprogram the remotes (ILS-01552).

**Footrest:** 2 hex bolts **50 ft-lb**, gas spring pin. Level the pan,
then set the **carriage angle**:

| Stair angle | Hole A | Hole B | Hole C |
|---|---|---|---|
| 32–50° | Bolt 1 | Bolt 2 | none |
| 25–35° | Bolt 1 | none | Bolt 2 |
| Under 25° | any two of the five top holes, as far apart as possible | | |

Angle bolts **50 ft-lb**.

**Seat:**
- Remove the shipping jumpers (keep them).
- Rear seat support bracket (M10 and safety washer).
- Seat in the notches. **6-pin** (swivel to paddle) and **12-pin**
  (swivel to diagnostic board).
- Allen screw, then the M10 support bolt pulled forward, then the front
  bolts. Footrest covers.
- **The paddle points down** (it breaks away less). If it is flipped up,
  show the user how to refit it.
- Footrest height in **⅝ in** steps, seat in 1 in steps.

**Finish:**
- Charge contact, top end plate **22 ft-lb**.
- **Jack screw:** M6 × 25 if the rack ends ½ in or less from the end, M6
  × 40 if ½–1¼ in. Finger tight, then **no more than one turn**.
- **Rail cross pin** (kit SRV-3000-023): drill template after the first
  rack tooth at the bottom, ⅛ in bit (DRB-13001) through 2 walls only,
  pin flush.
- Charger adapter at the top or bottom. **PTFE grease on the rack.**
- Low battery: runs down but not up. Charger LEDs: ILS-01545.

**DIP switch** (4-gang, under the seat hinge plate cover, breaker off):

| Mode | #1 | #2 | #3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Multi-user (factory: beeps every 3 s with the seat turned) | OFF | OFF | |
| **Single-user** | OFF | **ON** | |
| **Quiet** | **ON** | OFF | |
| **Battery warnings only** | ON | ON | |
| Left-hand / right-hand | | | OFF / **ON** |

**Warranty:** Gold, lifetime on major components, 2 years on parts, 30
days labor. The checklist covers the seat swivel stop (ILS-01548), a
rocker switch (ILS-01542), DIP 3 (ILS-01546), and **up should be
noticeably faster than down**.
