# Tech Tip - SRE-3000 Clamp Comparison (Adjustable Stand Foot)

1-page drawing (Rev 1, 10/25/12; portal tech-tip 186) comparing SRE rail mounts:
- **SRE-30150 adjustable clamp** (adjustable stand foot): **6.19 in** fully collapsed to **9.19 in** fully extended.
- **SRE-30001 standard clamp:** **5.22 in**.

Use the adjustable one where the rail must sit higher off a tread than the standard clamp allows.
