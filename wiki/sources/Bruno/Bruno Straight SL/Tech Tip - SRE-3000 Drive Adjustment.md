# Tech Tip - SRE-3000 Drive Adjustment

5-page procedure for a **loose SRE-3000 carriage** (vibration) (portal tech-tip 41): remove seat, footrest, top and front covers and the left safety panel; loosen the **three motor mounting bolts**; turn the set screw **¼ turn left**; run 6 in up and 6 in down; snug the bolts; run down the rail and feel for vibration. None → torque the motor bolts to **27 Nm (20 ft-lb)**. Still vibrating → another ¼ turn. Refit everything.
