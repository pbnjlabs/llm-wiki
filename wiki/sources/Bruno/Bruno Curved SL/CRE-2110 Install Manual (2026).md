# CRE-2110 Install Manual (2110-I, 04-15-2026)

Install manual for the **Elite Curve indoor**: CRE-2110, CRE-2110OS and
CRE-2110C (27 pages). It **supersedes the 2018 manual**
([[CRE-2110 Install Manual]]). Specs are on ILS-00905, the drawing is
ILS-00904, and the parts list is ILS-01578. The A18.1-2023 version
applies with an overspeed, 2014 without.

**Kit and tools:**
- It ships in 4 cartons plus the rail sections. The **antenna kit** and
  **RF remotes CRE-01065 (2.4 GHz XBee, 2 AA)** are standard, and the
  charger is **BCR-24024** (24 VDC 2 A).
- Tools include the **SRV-2110-012 rail (joint) clamp**, a digital level,
  a staple gun for the charger cord, and fish tape.
- Grade 10.9: **M6 7 ft-lb, M8 25, M10 47**.

**Rail:**
- Sections are **numbered on the underside from #1 at the bottom**. File
  paint off the joint ends.
- Fit the lower end plate and charge bracket before the rail goes on the
  stairs.
- **Lower final limit bracket:** only when the rail is **zero-degree at
  the bottom**. The conical washer's convex side faces the screw head.
- **Mounts:** set them per the application drawing, with the **larger
  foot outside** (chair side).
- **Slope** is checked against the elevation view: **within ½° every
  2 ft**, on the rack flange or the rail top (rail top only on a 180°
  curve). Nose-to-rail and rail-to-stringer figures are starting points.
- **Joints:**
  - 2 × **M10 × 70** bolts.
  - **SRV-2110-012 clamps** at the top and bottom of the joint.
  - **50 ft-lb**, then remove the clamps.
  - Joints must be flush.
- Shim under a clamp if the rail leans.
- **Flat landing adjustable mount** (CRE-K-2145):
  - Mounts **3 in and 12 in from the rail end**, and the next one no more
    than 24 in further.
  - M12 hardware **67 ft-lb**, then lag screws.
- **Zero-degree slope screw:** factory set; don't touch it without
  calling Bruno.

**Carriage and seat:**
- Drive on with the remote until all the wheels are on, keeping it level.
- Footrest on its **middle hole**, 50 ft-lb.
- Harnesses: **3-pin footrest, 6-pin swivel to paddle, 12-pin swivel to
  diagnostic board**.
- **Seat Allen screw 25 ft-lb, then the front bolts 50 ft-lb.** Raise
  the seat by swapping the 4 hex bolts (50 ft-lb).
- Footrest height in **⅝ in** steps: the middle holes give the lowest
  footrest, the lower holes the highest. The **top hole may hit the step
  nose.**
- **Rail mount screws: back screws first, no air gap, 225 in-lb
  (25 Nm).**

**Charge and limits:**
- The charge cable runs in the adhesive channel on the back of the rail.
- Upper end cap, upper **final limit**, then the upper charge contact.
  Mount the final limit **so its cam doesn't touch the carriage final
  limit** at the top. Wires go black to black, red to red.
- The charge contact moves horizontally (4 bolts) and vertically (2
  Phillips).
- **Proximity charge tip:** for the highest top seat and lowest bottom
  seat, swap the mounting bar and the oval nut plate. Add 2 M6 screws and
  jam nuts at the bottom for stability. **The wheels must not touch the
  end cap, and the final limit must not chatter.**
- **Charging is at the ends (or mid-park) only.** If a door stops it
  parking, send it to the other end. A **plug lock** is suggested.
  Charger LEDs: ILS-01545.

**Overspeed reset (OS/C) after a trip test:**
1. Breaker off.
2. Pry the end cap off to reach the **gear motor hex shaft**.
3. With an **8 mm ratchet, turn counter-clockwise** to back off the
   locking tension.
4. **Rotate the OS trip plate by hand** until its groove lines up with
   the hole.

This replaces the 2018 manual's two-lever reset and agrees with TechMate.

**Remotes:** keyed call/send **no higher than 48 in and no lower than
15 in** per A18.1 4.10.1. The manual prints "15 in (220 mm)", a typo.
One remote at the top and one at the bottom.

**Test:**
- Up is **noticeably faster than down**, with a soft start.
- **Key OFF disables the unit and the remotes.**
- Pledge the rail, and **re-grease the rack (PTFE) about once a year**.

**DIP switch:** same 4-gang table as the Elite
([[SRE-2010 Install Manual]]): single-user = #2 ON, quiet = #1 ON,
battery warnings only = both ON, **#3 ON = right-hand**.

**Warranty:** indoor CRE Gold, lifetime on major components, 2 years on
parts, 30 days labor; cosmetic damage excluded. The checklist also says
**mark the model on the data plate** under the seat.
