# ILS - Picture Perfect 3.0 Instructions and Camera Settings

Seven Picture Perfect sheets: **ILS-01397** StairsChecker 3.0 instructions (dated 01/01/24, 14 pages; newer than [[Picture Perfect Instructions (2022 Rev 5)]]), **ILS-01403** target placement examples (01-20-2020, newer than [[Picture Perfect Staircase Target Placement]]), camera settings for **Canon Rebel T5/T6/T7** (ILS-01410), **Canon T3** (ILS-01463), **Olympus E420/450/520/620** (ILS-01464), **Pentax K110D** (ILS-01465), and **external flash** set-up (ILS-01527, 05/17/26).

- **Computer:** Windows 7–11, i3 or better, 4 GB RAM; **no Apple, Android/Chromebook, Kindle or Ryzen machines**; some Surface Pros work. Windows 7: turn off "rotate pictures" on import.
- **Targets:** step-nose pairs 1–30 L/R on matching risers (#1 on the first riser), **tight to the full stair width** (over 48 in wide: keep pair spacing ≤ 48 in); all 3 landing levels **level** (shim with cards or coins) with the scale bar by the lower level; flat targets 2 in apart, each code used once; mark wall lines, corners, posts, carpet edges, park spots.
- **Shooting:** format the card, check settings, flash on, work **top-down**, every code in **at least 10 photos**, rotate the camera often, about **50 photos** minimum. Outdoors: early or late (no direct sun), no rain or wind, cover shiny surfaces, external flash recommended; over 30 risers → call Bruno.
- **StairsChecker:** keyboard F1 job info, F2 landing levels, F3 unsolved codes, F4 messages, F6 riser widths. Passed jobs: upload photos via File Share on the Curved Stairlifts homepage and submit the electronic order form. Help: 1-866-882-4990.
- **Camera settings (Canon):** lens fixed at **18 mm**, manual focus, stabilizer off, auto-rotate off, **f/22, 1/200 s, ISO 400, image size L**, mode M, flash on. Olympus: 14 mm hard stop, f/22, **1/180**, ISO 400, flash ¼, Large. The cameras are modified and won't take normal pictures. Bruno gives no warranty on kit parts.
- **External flash:** Flashpoint Zoom Li-on R2 at **M, 1/8**; Godox V860III-C at **M, 1/16, zoom A (auto)**; recharge daily.
