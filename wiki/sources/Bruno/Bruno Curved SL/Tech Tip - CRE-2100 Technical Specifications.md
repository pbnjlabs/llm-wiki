# Tech Tip - CRE-2100 Technical Specifications

6-page scan (portal tech-tip 33) of the **CRE-2100 / CRE-2100C Electra-Ride III** spec sheet (rev 09/06/2001, ISO 404.58) and two dimension drawings (2001 and 2003). The CRE-2100 is the curved-rail model before the [[CRE-2110]]. Full numbers on [[CRE-2100]].

Highlights: 350 lb, 2 × 7 Ah 12 V batteries, 24 V motor with 20:1 worm gearbox and a 4:1 poly-V belt reduction, 8DP rack welded inside the rail, 318 MHz call/send, 0–45°, rails to 50 ft, overspeed brake optional (standard in the commercial package). A hand-written note on page 3 gives **overspeed trip speeds** (carriage 46–72 ft/min).
