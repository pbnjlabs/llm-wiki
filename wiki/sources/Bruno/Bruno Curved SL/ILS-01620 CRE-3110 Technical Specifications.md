# ILS-01620 CRE-3110 Technical Specifications

ILS-01620 Rev P1 (05/28/26), 2 pages: spec and dimension drawing for the **CRE-3110**, a newer Bruno curved-rail stairlift. See [[CRE-3110]] for the numbers. Highlights: **300 lb**, 2 x 7 Ah, 1/3 hp, **25 ft/min**, **0–65°**, rail **7 in from the wall**, final limit, overspeed and keyed carriage control standard; options power swivel, power footrest, reduced lower-landing extension, **Smart Swivel for staircases down to 26 in (660 mm)**, short footrest.
