# Tech Tip - CRE-2110 Final Limit and End Limit Adjustment

2-page tip (portal tech-tip 35). Unit at the top, **no power at board B+/B−, no lights**: check where the **final limit switch** is sitting. If it's up on the ramp, move the **end bracket / charge contacts** away from the end of the rail so the **end limit trips before the final limit**. The end-limit bracket has slots for moving it toward or away from the end plate.
