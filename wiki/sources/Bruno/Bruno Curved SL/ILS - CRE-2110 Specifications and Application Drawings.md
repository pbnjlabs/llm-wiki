# ILS - CRE-2110 Specifications and Application Drawings

- **ILS-00905, CRE-2110 / C / OS / CAN / G:** **400 lb** (G 420 lb); A18.1 Sec 4 or 7; 2 x 7 Ah; 0.68 hp, 58:1 gearbox; **2.4 GHz RF** call/send; rail **2½ in above the step nose**, within **7–8 in of the wall**; extends about 6.5 in past the top nose and 18–20 in past the bottom; **0–50°**; **25 ft/min**; custom to **60 ft**; seat stops 45°, 0°, 60°, 90°; green charge light on the carriage, amber at each rail end; 30 A breaker, 5 A fuse; carriage 100 lb, seat/footrest 50 lb, rail 17.5 lb/ft; options include mid-park and charge, power swivel to 90°, larger seat and footrest, overspeed (C, G, OS, CAN).
- **ILS-01123, CRE-2110E / EC / EOS (outdoor):** charger inside, all outside wiring 24 V; keyed controls standard; weather cover; **0 °F to 125 °F**; rail 16 lb/ft; overspeed on EC/EOS.
- **Application drawings:** indoor (ILS-00904), outdoor (ILS-01137), 2110G global (ILS-01584), power options (ILS-00937). **ILS-01578** is the CRE-2110/C/OS basic-unit parts list.
