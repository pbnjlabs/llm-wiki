# CRE-2110E Install Manual (2110E-I, 05-06-2026)

Install manual for the **Elite Outdoor Curve**: CRE-2110E, EOS and EC
(34 pages). Specs ILS-01123, drawing ILS-01137. It follows the indoor
[[CRE-2110 Install Manual (2026)]] step for step: numbered rail sections,
½° slope check every 2 ft, M10 × 70 joints at 50 ft-lb with SRV-2110-012
clamps, the flat-landing mount rules, mounts at 225 in-lb, final limit
and proximity charge contacts, the 8 mm CCW overspeed reset, and the DIP
table. Only the differences are listed here.

- **Weather:**
  - **0–125 °F.** Don't use it within 20 minutes of lightning. Slippery
    when wet.
  - **Touch up paint scratches** on receipt, after install and yearly.
    Rust from untreated scratches can void the warranty.
- **Kit:** an **IR repeater kit** in the install box. 2.4 GHz RF repeater
  kits CRE-K-2142WG (NA) / CRE-K-2144WG (UK/EU).
- **Cloth covers** (after the footrest):
  - **Large cover** over the control panel: 7½ in Velcro strip, a flap
    over the **antenna jack**, then screw the antenna on through the
    second hole.
  - **Small cover** over the motor: 5½ in strip, seat bottom bracket and
    seat harness through the 2 back-seam holes.
  - Then the seat, as indoors.
- **Charger** indoors (32–104 °F). A 10 ft output extension (SRE-20254)
  is included. Longer cords slow charging.
- **Keyed remote:** close the switch cover against moisture.
- **Parts:**
  - Tan rail clamps A–M (CRE-007xxT, -008xxT, -010xxT), mid-park charge
    assembly CRE-00875T, tall feet CRE-00956T / CRE-01036T.
  - Proximity charge kits **CRE-K-2134T std, -2137T special, -2139T
    extreme**.
  - Carriages **CRE-01208LT/RT**, covers CRE-00996/00997, exterior seat
    cover and bag CRE-00995/CRE-01189.
  - Chargers BCR-24024 (UK BCR-24025).
