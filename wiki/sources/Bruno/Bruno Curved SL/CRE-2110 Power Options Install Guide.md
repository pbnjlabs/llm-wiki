# CRE-2110 Power Options Install Guide (2110-POW OPT, 08-24-2022)

A 21-page supplement for the **power swivel seat** and **power footrest**
on the CRE-2110, OS, C and CAN. Drawing ILS-00937, schematic SCH-00178.

**Swivel angle (set it before mounting the seat):**
- The **large cam** works the seat home switch (factory set).
- The **small cam** works the extended switch. It is factory set to
  **90°** and **adjusts in its slot from 30° to 90°**.

**Power footrest:**
- Jumper harness connected. Drive the carriage just onto the rail, then
  breaker off.
- To raise or lower the seat post, move the 4 M8 flat-heads and jam nuts.
- Footrest on the **kit's M10 flat-heads, 40 ft-lb (54 Nm)**. The middle
  hole is the lowest; the higher holes bring it closer to the floor and
  the nose.
- **Never lift the footrest or seat by the actuator.**
- **Actuator:** shoulder bolt with 2 nylon washers (one each side of the
  footrest plate) and a nylock. Tighten fully (⅛ in Allen plus ⅜ in
  wrench), and don't force the footrest up afterwards.
- **Harnesses:**
  - Remove the factory jumpers.
  - The 2-pin actuator harness goes up to the carriage plug **"FOOTREST"**.
  - The 3-pin grey safety harness goes down into the footrest.

**Power swivel:**
- Rear seat bracket. Remove the swivel cover (4 × M6) and the shipping
  twist ties. Hang the base on the factory M8 bolts.
- **Screw position:** in front of the hole with a standard footrest,
  behind it with a power footrest.
- Bundle the harnesses under the seat, **clear of the IR/RF eyes**.
- **Seat to base: 4 × M6, 150 in-lb.**
- Display PCB harness: wires point down.
- **File a rounded notch** in the footrest cover for the screw heads.

**Test:**
- Seat up → footrest folds; seat down → footrest drops.
- At the top, **release UP and press again** to swivel to the stop
  (beep). DOWN returns it.
