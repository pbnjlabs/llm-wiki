# CRE-2110E Operator Manual (2110E-O, 06-22-2026)

Owner's manual for the **Elite Outdoor Curve**, residential and
commercial (11 pages). It is almost word for word the
[[SRE-2010E Operator Manual]]:
- 0–125 °F; no lightning within 20 min; ice and snow removal; no
  pressure washer; dealer check after a harsh winter.
- Seat cover with drawstring and pouch. Never remove the bottom covers.
- Wall-mounted keyed remotes on a **9 V battery, changed yearly**.
- Paint touch-ups.
- Storage: remove the batteries and keys; on return, re-grease the rack.
- **End charging:** a **yellow LED on the charge bracket** shows correct
  parking. C3 = not parked.
- Outdoor warranty: 5 years on major components, 2 years on parts.

**Curve-specific:**
- **Mid-park option:** it always stops and beeps at mid-park. Turn the
  key ON (armrest or remote box) and press again to go on. It also stops
  and beeps at each end.
- The seat swivels 135° in total, with stops at 60° and 90° at the top
  (optional 45° at the bottom).
