# ILS - CRE-2110 Drive and Overspeed Sheets

- **ILS-01196 (2014), drive adjustment for ride quality in turns and angle changes:** loosen the four gear-motor screws **¾–1 turn**, turn the tension screw out **½ turn**, run a few feet, tighten to **34 Nm**, ride the whole rail; another ¼ turn if needed. Turns won't feel as smooth as straights, but no transition should be uncomfortable. Over-tightening the adjustment screw damages the gearbox. (Same procedure as [[Tech Tip - CRE Ride-Out]], with the torque.)
- **ILS-01573 (02/23/23), retrofit the CRE-01126L/R OSG overspeed** to a carriage with the old **knurled-cam** design: grinder, drill fixture FIX-03623L/R, tap M5, screw adjustment block CRE-01122, new lower carriage assembly, **pinion heated with a propane torch** onto the shaft, overspeed assembly and harness CRE-01106; run up and down to check it doesn't trip early. Kit SRV-2110-014L/RWG.
- **ILS-01576 (03/29/23), replace a CRE-01126 or CRE-01100 overspeed assembly** with **SRV-2110-022L/R**: unplug, three screws, slide it off the spur gear, fit the new one. The kit includes a factory **Overspeed Certificate CRE-01176**: **keep it with the unit's documents for inspectors.**
- **ILS-01451 (09/27/22), CRE-2110E exterior bag cover:** top seat cover CRE-00995 and bag CRE-00999 on a bracket under the seat front.
