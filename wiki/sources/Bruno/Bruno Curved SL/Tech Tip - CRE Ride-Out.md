# Tech Tip - CRE Ride-Out

Two CRE tips (portal tech-tips 31 and 34). **Ride-out** = the carriage's gear climbing out of the rack, usually on inside turns.

**Is it ride-out?** Does the damage show going up only, or down only? With the bare carriage, or with seat and footrest on? If it kicks out with seat and footrest on, press lightly on the footrest while it runs: still kicks out → it may have to go back to Bruno; goes away with weight → it's fine, no return. Then check for **excess paint in the gear rack** (clean the rack and the carriage spur gear), the **nylon guide on the downside axle** (missing → send one), and the **business-card check**: if a card slides between the gear rack and the flange, call Bruno (possible return).

**Drive adjustment for ride-out (CRE-2110):** seat, footrest and drive covers off; slightly loosen the **four gear-motor mounting screws**; turn the **gear-motor tension screw out ½ turn** (counter-clockwise), which eases gear pressure on inside turns but makes the straights feel rougher; run a few feet up and down to seat the drive; tighten; reassemble and run the inside turn. Still riding out → one more **¼ turn**. Going further doesn't help (the screw holes limit travel), and over-tightening the adjustment screw damages the gearbox.
