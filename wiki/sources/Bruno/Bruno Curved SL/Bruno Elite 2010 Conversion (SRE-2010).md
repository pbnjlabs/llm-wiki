# Bruno Elite 2010 Conversion (Opposite Hand) — misfiled under Curved SL

8-page excerpt (pages 43-50) from an **SRE-2010 straight-rail** install
manual, footer dated "SRE-2010 INSTALLATION 05-02-2016" — **not** CRE-2110
curved-rail content, despite living in the `Bruno Curved SL/` folder.

Covers converting an SRE-2010 to the opposite hand at install time: seat
swivel plate and wiring (unplug harness, remove/flip cam and actuator
mounting ring, move seat mounting bracket/swivel bearing housing/rotation
stop bolt to the opposite side, Loctite the seat jam nut to 70 ft-lb),
carriage (disconnect/reorient batteries — orientation matters, pivot
carriage to opposite angle), optional rocker arm switch rewiring
(green="fat" side, black=middle, red="slim" side), and footrest (flip gas
spring to opposite side of the lift-link tab).

This is a different document from the ~35-step field conversion procedure
in [[SRE-2010 Tech Support Guide]] — that one is post-installation field
service; this one is part of initial installation.
