# Parts Drawings - CRE-2110 Elite Curve

Bruno's assembly drawings (exploded views with parts tables), pulled 2026-10-22 from the Brunopartner parts lookup (Service → Parts Lookup by Serial #). That lookup only takes an exact serial number, so the set was rebuilt from each model's top-level drawing, following assemblies three levels down ([[Bruno-Parts-Lookup]]). **These are Bruno's current revisions**: for an older unit, run its serial on the portal for the parts list as built, or check the manual's exploded view. Dealer prices on the portal are manufacturer suggestions and are not recorded here.

Starts from the **CRE-2110** and **CRE-2110E** model drawings and the outdoor carriage CRE-01208LT. Many of the 44 drawings are rail clamps, spiral stands and adjustable feet (A–M, P, T) and seat/footrest assemblies.

- **Gear motor MDC-24033** (24 V with brake, RH) in drive CRE-01203LT; batteries **BTR-12002**; **30 A breaker CBK-30004**; carriage wheels **CRE-10001** (with bearing and Magnalube-G grease); tapered roller bearings BRG-75007/75008 in the leveling assembly CRE-00949.
- Rail charge harness **CRE-00761** (pigtails CRE-00763/00764), mid-park charge assembly CRE-00875WG, IR repeater receivers **CRE-01163** (BlastIR 2-pack).
- Seat shares the Elite's user interface PCB SRE-27391 and gas spring SRE-20769; gas spring tool CRE-00905 (kit CRE-K-2112).
