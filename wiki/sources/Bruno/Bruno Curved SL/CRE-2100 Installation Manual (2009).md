# CRE-2100 Installation Manual (Electra-Ride III, MAN-2100-1, 2009)

Dealer install manual for the legacy **CRE-2100 Electra-Ride III**
curved stairlift (44 pages). The cover says 02-02-2009 and the footers
say 02-09-2009. It is the first CRE-2100 manual on file (before this only
a 2001 spec sheet).

**Rail and clamps:**
- **Rails are numbered on the underside**, #1 at the bottom. The bolts
  holding the protective ends are the **joint bolts: keep them**.
- Fit the lower end plate and charge bracket first. The upper ones go on
  after the carriage.
- **Clamps:** set per the application drawing.
  - The **longer foot faces outside** (chair side).
  - **Curve clamps:** the foot faces inward, with the **rounded head on
    the outside** of the rail.
  - The spacer ridge goes down, the bolt thread points away from the
    stairs, and the nut is nearest the wall.
- Start with the **landing section**. Joints are **M10 × 70**.
- **Slope within ½° every 2 ft**, measured with an angle finder **on the
  guide strip on the front of the rail** against the job's elevation
  view. Shim if the rail leans.
- **Zero-degree horizontal adjustment screw** on the lower carriage:
  factory set, don't touch it.

**Carriage and seat:**
- Seat swivel plate assembly with adjusting bolts.
- Seats: **CRE-00415 ("1550"-style)** or **CRE-00523 (23 in wide)**.
- Charger **BCR-24018** (24 VDC 2 A); rail charge harness CRE-00574.

**Overspeed reset, two levers** (same as the SRE-2000):
1. Rotate the overspeed switch lever while pushing the wires in.
2. Turn the cam lever just until it engages.

**Charging and electrical:**
- **End charging:** park at the charge brackets.
- Charger LEDs and AC fuse as on the SRE-2000.
- **Learn up to 4 transmitters:** hold LEARN/ERASE and press a
  transmitter button until the LED goes out.
- 4-gang DIP: diagnostic modes, #3 hand, **#4 coast delay 600/900 ms**.
- Beeps: 4 short + 1 long = off the charge bumper; 3 short + 2 long =
  voltage drop; 2 short + 3 long = critical (under 16 V).

**Speed and grease:**
- **Speed potentiometer** (clockwise = faster); set it with the rider on.
- **White lithium** on the rack, regreased yearly. Bruno now specifies
  PTFE ([[ILS-01446 Maintenance Control Plan for CRE-SRE Chair Lifts]]).
