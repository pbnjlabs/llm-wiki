# Tech Tip - CRE Fit Issues Service Presentation

11-page Bruno service slide deck on a **curved rail that doesn't fit** (portal tech-tip 32).

- Start at the **bottom section**: check its angle/slope (must be as exact as possible), bottom-of-step to rail, and wall to the rail's outer edge.
- The rail must be **at least 2½ in from the step nose**, or the carriage/footrest can hit it.
- **Don't go further if the slope is off.** If the rail creeps toward the wall, re-check and reposition the preceding sections' slope.
- Only once the slope is right, check the staircase measurements: the **step-nose to step-nose "C" dimension** on straight flights and the **landing dimensions**, against the print and Bruno's original order.
