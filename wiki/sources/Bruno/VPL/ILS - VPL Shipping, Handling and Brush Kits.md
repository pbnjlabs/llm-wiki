# ILS - VPL Shipping, Handling and Brush Kits

- **ILS-00992** (09-24-2013), **VPL-3200B/3300B shipping and handling:**
  - **Extended forklift forks (5 ft minimum)**, a loading dock or yard forklift, and **at least two people** with PPE. **Don't unload it without a dock or yard forklift.**
  - Tower bottom section **570 lb** (52.5/76.5/100.5 in for 10/12/14 ft); top section 190/235/280 lb. A **flood-zone tower moves 100 lb** from the bottom section to the top.
  - Tower shipping weight 1420/1530/1640 lb.
  - Platform 360/380/400 lb (48/54/60 in); add 87 lb for a platform gate, 28 lb for a ramp, 111/116 lb for a 36/42 in top gate.
- **ILS-01425** (08/20/2018), **unpacking a 4 ft VPL and fitting the platform**:
  - Slide the tower onto a 2×4 and plywood support (fix the wood together), tip it onto its legs.
  - Platform on a furniture dolly.
  - Run the carriage with the **setup switch harness VPL-32249** (supplied with tall VPLs) to line up the holes.
- **ILS-01412** (05/14/18), **tower brush kits VPL-K-3160, 3161, 3242, 3243, 3244**: punch the brush into the extrusion (4+ per section); #20 drill, holes 1 in from the ends and every 9 in; the **brush laps 1/4–3/8 in past the gap**; blind rivets; crimp cut ends.
