# ILS - VPL ACME Screw, Nut and Bearing Service

Bruno's drive service sheets.

**Short VPL (VPL-3100 family):**
- **ILS-00743** (08-27-07), kit **SRV-3100-004**: ACME nut replacement.
- **ILS-00772** (12-12-07), **SRV-3100-005/006** stabilizer kit: **nuts ½ in apart**; screw-block bolts **12 in-lb**; safety bolt **200 in-lb**; the nut switch must trip within **7/16 in**; break-in cycles after fitting.
- **ILS-01129 Rev 3** (06/11/23, 14 pages), **SRV-3153-006 / SRV-3175-006**: short VPL screw and nut replacement. It replaces the 2016 Rev 2 on [[Tech Tip - Short VPL ACME Screw and Nut Replacement (ILS-01129)]] with the same steps, and adds factory torques: **motor adjustment nuts 150 in-lb** (final torque after belts are set), **pulley locking hub bolts 95 in-lb**. It ends by sending you to the **maintenance control plan ILS-01444** for the final inspection.
- **ILS-01029** (09-30-11): **top bearing support fix**. Shim **VPL-32194** under the screw at the bottom bearing, drop the top bearing, and check the support plate hole. **7/8 in = correct**. **About 1 7/16 in (near the bearing OD) = fit service parts VPL-31933**: new support plate plus stacks of three spacers per bolt. Tighten in a cross-corner pattern, set screws, check pulley alignment, remove the shim.

**Tall VPL (VPL-3210B/3212B/3214B, VPL-3310B/3312B/3314B):**
- **ILS-01185 Rev 3** (10-25-2018), **SRV-3200-001** (Service Bulletin 60): **screw joint pin inspection**. Run it at about 10 ft/min and listen at the joint cover. Look for **brass grit**: **if brass filings are present, lock the unit out and order nut kit SRV-3200-002**. The pins must not stand **past the thread root** (feel with a small screwdriver). Replace **one pin at a time** with coiled spring pins: clean the grease first, then heat with a propane torch (Loctited) with a **fire extinguisher at hand**, and drive out with a 3/16 in punch. Regrease the whole screw.
- **ILS-00975 Rev 4** (10-25-2018), **VPL-K-3218 coiled spring pins:** align the **red marks** on the two screws; clean the holes with **isopropyl alcohol only**; tap in with a soft hammer; **retaining compound required**; press to depth with the insertion tool (black screw flush). Wipe the threads, wire-brush burrs, and **gauge both ends**: the gauge must turn smoothly over the joint. Remove the black shipping spacer at the bottom bearing.
- **ILS-01186 Rev 1** (3-24-16): tall VPL nut replacement (superseded by ILS-01538).
- **ILS-01538** (6/7/22, 16 pages): **tall VPL screw and/or nut replacement**. Platform off on a dolly, carriage on a 12–16 in block (not on the stabilizer base). Note the belt tension and the screw-top-to-pulley height; the **joint pins carry the lower screw's weight**, so clamp it before the last pin. Reassembly:
  - **½ in gap** between the safety nut and the drive nut. **Tool VPL-32422 must fit between them.**
  - Safety plate to the back.
  - Bearing plate **35 ft-lb**, lower bearing **18 ft-lb**, about **3/16 in** from the bearing top to the threads.
  - Nyloc nuts on the drive-nut bolts **not tight**: **4–6 threads showing**, bolts lift about **½ in**.
  - Nut-switch bracket **10 ft-lb**.
  - White stabilizer block screws spin by hand; **about 1/8 in** from the bronze blocks to the brackets.
  - Grease: **Mobil Aviation Grease SHC 100**.
  - Test with the setup harness **VPL-32249** (safeties bypassed, so take care), test the nut switch with the tool, then run the **ILS-01444** checks.
- **ILS-01544** (5/10/22), **nut safety switch testing tool, all VPLs:** block the carriage. Put the tool between the switch activation plate and the switch. **If it won't fit, the drive nut has dropped onto the safety nut: replace both nuts.** With the tool in, the carriage **must not run either way**. **Never bypass the nut safety switch.**
