# Tech Tip - VPL Certifications (ETL and CSA)

Two certificates (portal tech-tips 118 and 150), useful when an inspector asks.

- **Intertek ETL Authorization to Mark** (issued 21-Jun-2014, control 4004689) for **commercial** VPLs **VPL-3353B, 3375B, 3310B, 3312B, 3314B**: ASME A18.1-2011, ASME A17.5-2011 / CSA B44.1-11, CSA B355-09.
- **CSA Certificate of Compliance** 1832895 (May 10, 2011) for the AC VPL, the **VPL-3100B** and the **VPL-3200B**: CSA B44.1 / ASME A17.5 elevating devices for the handicapped, Canada and US. All cord connected, 120 VAC 60 Hz, **340 kg (750 lb)**: the AC "model VPL" rated 15 A at 0.046 m/s (9 ft/min); the **VPL-3100B** and **VPL-3200B** each rated **3 A at 0.051 m/s (10 ft/min)**.
