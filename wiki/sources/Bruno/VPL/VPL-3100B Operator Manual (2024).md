# VPL-3100B Operator Manual (09-16-2024)

The current owner's manual for the battery **VPL-3153B / VPL-3175B**
(9 pages). It supersedes the 2019 trilingual edition
([[VPL-3100B Operator Manual]]).

**Specs:**
- 750 lb. Unit weight **705 / 745 lb (3153B, 17 / 34 Ah)** and **758 /
  798 lb (3175B)**.
- **10 ft/min (7–11 typical).**
- Charger 120 VAC 3 A. **2 × 12 V 17 Ah standard** (34 Ah U1 optional).
- ½ hp 24 VDC motor (2 hp peak).
- Travel 53 in or 75 in. Platforms 34 × 48, 34 × 54 or 34 × 60.
- FDA Class II, 510(k)-exempt, product code PCE.

**Operation:** a keyed constant-pressure rocker; releasing it stops the
lift. **Park it mid-way when not in use** (discourages use by others, and
keeps the underpan out of ice). Below 0 °F, clear ice and snow, run it
down, up, then board. **Anti-corrosive de-icer or sand, never salt.**

**Power outage:** it keeps running on batteries. If it won't go up, it
should still go down.

**Hand wheel:**
1. Top cover off.
2. **Disconnect the batteries or switch the breaker off.**
3. Wheel in the brake shaft opening, counter-clockwise to lower.

**Charger LEDs (power, charge, float, fault):**

| Power | Charge | Float | Fault | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| on | on | on | on | Power-up (2 s) |
| on | — | — | — | No battery |
| on | — | — | on | **Shorted output or reversed battery** |
| on | flash | — | — | Soft start |
| on | on | — | — | Bulk charge |
| on | on | flash | — | Nearly full |
| **flash** | — | — | — | **AC disconnected** |
| on | — | on | — | Full |
| on | — | — | **flash** | **Bad or sulfated batteries, or over-temperature** |

**Owner troubleshooting:**
- Gate open, E-stop pressed, key off.
- Batteries flat, breaker tripped.
- A long charge time = batteries.
- Slow = no AC, or not charged.

Yearly dealer inspection.
