# ILS-01444 Maintenance Control Plan for VPLs

ILS-01444 (08/01/22), 29 pages: Bruno's **maintenance control plan for vertical platform lifts**, written for units under **ASME A18.1-2017 or newer**. Bruno recommends it for **every VPL**, whatever code it went in under. **Keep it with the installation and operator manuals** so inspectors and technicians can see it. A18.1 Section 2 = commercial, Section 5 = residential.

**Weekly operational checklist**: required for Section 2 (commercial) lifts, optional for Section 5 (residential). Authorized personnel run it, and the pass/fail log is **kept 5 years**. 16 items, including:
- Platform, call/send and gate controls work and stop on release; the lift **stops within ½ in** of each landing.
- **E-stop** stops it; the **alarm** sounds (where fitted).
- **Charger LEDs:** **solid red = AC power on**, **flashing red = no AC power**, **yellow = charging**, **green = fully charged**.
- **Gates, doors and interlocks:** won't open unless the platform is at that landing; the lift won't run with one open; they **lock within 2 in** of the platform leaving the landing.
- **Safety pans:** press each side in **3 spots**; the lift must stop going down.
- **Emergency lowering** works. It **bypasses the safeties**, so the path must be clear.
- Covers on, no odd noises, area clean, lighting working.

**Evacuation:** at the lower landing, **push the gate rod up** (or use the bypass key) to release the gate. At the upper landing, **push the actuator wheel up**.

**Inspection schedule (A18.1-2017 §10):**
- **Routine (10.2): every 6 months commercial, every year residential.**
- **Periodic (10.3.1): yearly**: safeties and terminal stopping devices.
- **Acceptance and 5-year (10.3.3):** bottom normal stop with **125% of rated load**; top stop with no load; up and down speed.
- **Coastal or salt-air sites: inspect and lubricate every 4 months.**

**Site checks:** lighting at least **50 lx** at the landings; **sill gap 3/8–3/4 in**; **headroom 79 in** (80 in for ADA); overhead beams at least 79 in; a **door operator holds the door open at least 20 s**.

**Drive (service items 17–21):**
- **Brake:** **commercial and newer residential VPLs have no separate brake** (the ACME screw holds the load). Older residential units have one on top of the tower; check it releases and holds.
- **ACME screw:** degrease it (Super Clean or similar), then regrease with **Mobil Aviation Grease SHC 100 (Bruno LUB-00114)**. Check for brass filings and for **abrasion between the drive nut and the screw**.
- **Nuts:** the drive (top) nut and safety (bottom) nut should sit **about ½ in apart**. If the drive nut wears enough to drop onto the safety nut, the **nut safety switch** stops the lift; **replace both nuts**. The **four drive-nut bolts must have at least ¼ in of play** up and down. The safety-nut plate bolt is tight. **Never remove or bypass the nut safety switch**; test it with tool **VPL-32422** ([[ILS - VPL ACME Screw, Nut and Bearing Service]], ILS-01544).
- **Tall VPLs:** the screw joint about **8 ft up** is pinned; the pins must sit **below the thread root**. If they stand proud, replace them (**ILS-01185**, kit SRV-3200-001) and the nuts (**ILS-01186**, SRV-3200-002). Short VPL nuts: **ILS-00743**, SRV-3100-004.
- **Bearings:** **Mobil Epic EP 102** (LUB-00114 listed beside it in the text) through the zerk, **1–2 squirts**.
- **Belts: about ½ in deflection**, pressing one belt at a time halfway between the pulleys near the back of the tower. Short VPLs: loosen the four motor nuts and pry the motor. **Tall VPLs: loosen the two idler bolts, put a torque wrench in the idler's 3/8 in hole at 60 in-lb, retighten.** Belt dressing if dry.
- **Pulleys** run true; the key on the screw end (used with the manual lowering wheel) is flush.

**Switches (22–25):** lower, middle and upper limits; **final limit** (lift stops and won't run up when tripped); gate **enable switches**; **top cover switch** (three positions: run, service, and the manual-lowering cut-out).

**Guide rails are not lubricated.**

**Batteries (DC units):** AGM, **17 Ah or 34 Ah**. Load test them (e.g. RC-300); **replace below 70%**, and **replace both**. Dielectric grease on the connections.

**Finish:** treat rust early. **Touch-up paint is free under warranty**: champagne PNT-00107 / spray SPC-00105, graphite PPN-00104 / SPC-00104, tan PPN-00107 / SPC-00107. Wax painted surfaces; no salt or de-icer on the lift.

**Maintenance and repair log** (5 years): date, malfunction, any accident, work done, who did it.
