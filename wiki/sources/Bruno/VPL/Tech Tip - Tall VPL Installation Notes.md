# Tech Tip - Tall VPL Installation Notes

Five tall-VPL (VPL-3200B family) install notes (portal tech-tips 129, 128, 126, 127, 121).

**Training notes (Feb 2011):**
- **2 people with a hoist, 3 minimum without.**
- Lower section (legs off) about **550 lb**; install it first, on an appliance dolly or skate skids, with a rough opening and no door yet. Tallest section about **900 lb** total; upper section on the 10 ft lift about 180 lb. Free-standing units can be assembled on their back and stood up.
- Control box comes turned 90° (rotate it on its bracket); call/sends are plug and play; the **E-stop / kill switch has to be wired in**.
- Screw joint: clean the pin holes free of grease (cotton swab), cylindrical Loctite, brass hammer, set the pin with the tool without pushing it out the far side, spin the shaft and re-tap: it **must be smooth or the nut will catch**. Tighten jam nuts alternately side to side.
- Top cap switches on the two outside pins; start the limit switches directly across from the landing.
- L brackets are best at the top; side tie-ins can't be lower than the landing. **Tie the tower feet to the floor last.**
- Remove the yellow spacer under the screw before lifting the carriage. Platform walls tighten from underneath.
- **Older units with SCH-00176** (before the Feb 2011 print and PCB-00125 board) whose **interlock chatters**: move the white wire from TB5-2 to the red wire and jumper TB4-5 to TB4-6.
- **Cut the J1 jumper** when a lower gate is fitted. If it was cut by mistake, run a jumper from BLK12 to TB5-2. **Not cut → the door can be opened wherever the platform is: a big safety issue.**
- The pit switch (emergency stop) can be overridden with the lower emergency switch; use lockout/tagout instead.

**Shipping and handling:** two pallets (tower; platform). Forklift with **extended forks, 5 ft (1.5 m) minimum**; at least 2 people, PPE; pick up at the heavy end, close to the mast, carried low. A loading dock is preferred; a lift-gate truck works with more people or equipment.

**Panels:** VPL-3210B has **no C1 panel**, 3212B one, 3214B two; the angled tips point inward. **Mobile cart:** Bruno drawing (8/10/11) for a welded 2½ in square-tube cart with casters to move the tower. **Side anchors:** photos of a tall VPL in a home tied in at the side.

**Conflict:** the training notes put the lower section at about 550 lb and the tallest section at 900 lb; the 2011 troubleshooting guide's tips say the lower section is about 900 lb and the heaviest top section about 230 lb. The [[VPL-3200B Install Manual]] gives only the total (1210 lb with batteries). Plan rigging for the heavier figure.
