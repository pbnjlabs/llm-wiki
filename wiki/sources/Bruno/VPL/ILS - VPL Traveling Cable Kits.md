# ILS - VPL Traveling Cable Kits

- **ILS-00790** (05-07-2008), **SRV-3100-009 travelling cable fix kit**.
- **ILS-00990** (01/7/11), **cable carrier SRV-3153-004 / SRV-3175-004** (VPL-3153(B)/3175(B)). Mount it on the centre tube (3153B: beside the white cable clamp; **3175B: about 12 in below the electrical enclosure**). The **cable weight is tied up out of use**; snap the cable into the carrier. The cable must not bind or pull tight anywhere in travel.
- **ILS-01220** (06-26-14), **travelling cable replacement and wire cross-reference** for kits SRV-3153-007, SRV-3175-007, SRV-3153B-001, SRV-3175B-001, SRV-3200B-001, SRV-3353B-001, SRV-3375B-001.
  - **Note the routing before removing it**; routing it differently can damage the new cable.
  - Yellow connector to the board; grounds at the enclosure stud and under the carriage cross-member; red and black to the **nut safety switch**.
  - The chart matches [[Tech Tip - VPL Traveling Cable Assignments]] and fills in its cut-off column. **Low-rise residential = 12 conductors; tall residential and commercial = 18 conductors.** BLK12 red/green mid-landing, BLK13 red/yellow key status, BLK14/15 phone line, **BLK16/17 phone power (+24 V / GND) on commercial only**; YEL/GRN chassis ground.
