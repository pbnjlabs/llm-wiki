# Tech Tip - VPL Field Tensioning of Belts

2-page procedure (portal tech-tip 143) for VPL drive belts on **serial 12020300831 and later**.

1. Pry point: the small gap between the brake plate's right front standoff and the motor ground strap's lower leg.
2. Loosen the three motor stud nuts (no gap between the isolator bushings) and the ground-strap stud; remove the strap.
3. Change belts as usual (brake plate off), rotating the motor in to shorten the centre distance. Rotate it out to start the tension.
4. Torque the ground-strap stud to **150 in-lb**; refit the strap loosely enough to slide; pry it toward the back of the unit until the belt feels taut mid-span; tighten the strap fastener (9/16).
5. Torque the other three motor studs to **150 in-lb**.
6. **Target: ⅛ in deflection mid-span with 2½–3½ lb of force** (use a belt tension gauge; Bruno suggests getting one). Without a gauge: tight, with resistance when pressed, never so loose that it slips with a load on the platform. Don't over-tighten.

⚠️ **Conflict:** Bruno's maintenance control plan ILS-01444 (2022) says **about ½ in deflection** (tall VPLs: idler at 60 in-lb), with no force given. It's newer and ranks with the manuals, so use ½ in ([[ILS-01444 Maintenance Control Plan for VPLs]], [[VPL-Maintenance]]).
