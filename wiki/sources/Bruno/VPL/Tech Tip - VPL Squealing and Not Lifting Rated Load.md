# Tech Tip - VPL Squealing and Not Lifting Rated Load

Two short Bruno tips about a carriage that isn't square to the screw (portal tech-tips 146 and 134).

**Squealing** (bring a square, blue Loctite and parts from Bruno): **fit the ball plungers first**. Still squealing → loosen the bolt at the front of the stabilizer and slide a square in so it rests on the screw shaft and the horizontal bar. Where the screw meets the square, **under 90° (leaning toward the platform) → shim the back; over 90° → shim the front.**

**Won't lift its rated load:** often the carriage isn't square, so the drive nut loads unevenly and binds. Top and back covers off; measure the gap between carriage and nut with **feeler gauges** (front or rear). Shim to match: **.001–.005 → .005 shim, .006–.010 → .010, .011–.015 → .015**, and so on. Jack the carriage about ¼ in (hydraulic or car jack), slip the shim in, lower, test, refit covers.
