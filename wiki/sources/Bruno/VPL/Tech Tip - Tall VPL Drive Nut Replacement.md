# Tech Tip - Tall VPL Drive Nut Replacement

1-page outline (portal tech-tip 123) for changing the **drive nuts on a tall VPL**: raise the platform for access to the lower screw bearing and carriage nuts; **all AC and battery power off**; block the platform; turn the screw by hand to take the load off; remove the lower bearing; remove the nut hardware at the carriage flange (note where each piece goes and how far the retaining nuts are run on); spin the nuts down off the bottom; clean the old grease off the screw and re-grease with Bruno's grease; fit new nuts with about **½ in between the top nut's bottom edge and the bottom flange**; refit hardware; line the screw up with the lower bearing bore; with the bearing bolts loose run fully down, then up, then tighten.

Bruno's full procedure is ILS-01538 (2022), which replaces ILS-01186 (2016): [[ILS - VPL ACME Screw, Nut and Bearing Service]].
