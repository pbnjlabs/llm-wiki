# Parts Drawings - VPL Residential Short (3153-3175)

Bruno's assembly drawings (exploded views with parts tables), pulled 2026-10-22 from the Brunopartner parts lookup (Service → Parts Lookup by Serial #). That lookup only takes an exact serial number, so the set was rebuilt from each model's top-level drawing, following assemblies three levels down ([[Bruno-Parts-Lookup]]). **These are Bruno's current revisions**: for an older unit, run its serial on the portal for the parts list as built, or check the manual's exploded view. Dealer prices on the portal are manufacturer suggestions and are not recorded here.

Covers the battery **VPL-3153B / 3175B** (4 ft tower VPL-31976CP, 6 ft VPL-31977CP) and the AC **VPL-3153 / 3175** (towers VPL-31974CP / 31975CP). Top-level drawings: handwheel VPL-31119, tower, install kit VPL-K-3103CP; platform, controls, gates and decals are options listed on the model drawing.

- **Battery towers:** charger **BCR-24020** (24 VDC 5 A), **½ hp 24 VDC motor VPL-33375**, brake mounting plate VPL-35093.
- **AC towers:** **90 V DC brake with manual release BRK-90001**, cogged belt **VBL-35001** (AX 35 in), motor listed as **VPL-31953 "Elektrim 1 hp AC motor assy"**. The 2025 AC manual and spec sheet say ½ hp 90 VDC; check the unit's motor tag before ordering.
- Carriage and screw: ACME screw nut with flange **VPL-31535**, safety nut switch bracket VPL-32156; limit switches VPL-31964/31965 (lower) and VPL-31971 (upper); wheel assembly VPL-31874, clevises VPL-31879L/R.
