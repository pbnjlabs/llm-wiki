# ILS - VPL Platform Conversions and Canopies

**Platform conversions:** **ILS-00729/00730** (04-30-2007) convert a **54 in** platform between RH 90° and straight-through. **ILS-00947/00948** (04-04-18, left-hand unit shown) do 90° ↔ straight-through on the current platform: the hand rail (four 5/16-8 button-head screws), the 90° front wall, back panel and side panel swap; save the **ramp bracket nylon washers**; a heat gun lifts the anti-slip strip. Going to 90°: entry angle bracket **VPL-31774L**, new anti-slip strip flush with the bracket end, back panel to side panel with SHCS-25007, side tube and caps, and the **ramp goes into the tower-side bracket first**. Tighten everything at the end.

**Canopy (ILS-00846 drawing; ILS-00847, 2008; ILS-00944 36 in canopy, 2010):** U-tubes onto the horizontal rails (holes facing in), top alignment plate, caps; clamp each rail centred and flush inside the platform wall; drill **#1 pilot holes in the top tube only**, ¼-20 × ½ in self-tapping screws with **AnchorLube**; hook-and-loop canopy. **No canopy on a 90° platform** (ILS-00846).
