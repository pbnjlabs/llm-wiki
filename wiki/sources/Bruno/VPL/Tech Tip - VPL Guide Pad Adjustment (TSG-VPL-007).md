# Tech Tip - VPL Guide Pad Adjustment (TSG-VPL-007)

TSG-VPL-007 Rev 0 (10-06-2021), 8 pages, for guide pads that **grab and make the platform shudder** (portal tech-tip 216). The serial range on the cover was left as "XXXXXXXXXXX".

**Section A, pads:** remove the four hex bolts and the alignment weldments; **buff or round off the top and bottom edges** of the guide pads (sandpaper or file). Run the full length to confirm it was the inner pads; if the **outside pads** are noisy, loosen that side's corner bolts ¼ turn at a time, running the full length each time, until quiet. Refit (bolts until the lock washers bottom out). Then **loosen the four lower bearing bolts 1–2 turns** (don't remove them), run the platform empty up and down 4–8 times to align the screw, stop it within 24 in of the bottom and tighten the bolts flush.

**Section B, ball plungers** (if it still shudders or drops): at about 6 in up, mark the platform wall's top on tape on the tower while standing on one side, then the other; **0.5–0.75 in of deflection is normal**. Repeat every foot to the top. At the mark with the **least** deflection, heat the ball plungers and weldments to free the old Loctite, clean them, apply fresh blue Loctite, turn them in until they bottom out, then **back out ¼ turn**. Still binding on one side → that side another ¼ turn; both → both. A dry lubricant on the tower's inside columns may still be needed.
