# Tech Tip - Tall VPL-3200 Troubleshooting Guide (2011)

13-page guide for the **tall VPL-3200B** family, "1st to current" as of March 2011 (portal tech-tip 201). TechMate only supports tall units from about 2012 (and fully from 2015), so this covers early ones. Same method as the low-rise DC guide ([[Tech Tip - Low-Rise DC VPL Troubleshooting Guides (2010-2011)]]) with tall-unit terminals:

- 24 VDC at J1; fuse TB1-1 to TB1-3; TB1-4 = top cap, **upper final limit and optional pit switch**; TB2-5 = E-stop then nut safety; TB3-5/TB3-4 upper gate closed/locked.
- Jumps: TB2-1–TB1-7 up, TB2-1–TB5-6 down. Motor: unplug the **green/blue Anderson connector** in the harness channel and apply 12–24 VDC (+ orange up).
- Relays RLY1 (up) and RLY2 (down) have a **copper jumper plate**; contacts upper open, lower closed.
- Lower limit TB5-3–4, safety pan TB5-5–6 (the order is swapped from the low-rise guide).
- Diagrams cover the emergency lowering system, options and the optional phone connection.

**Tips page:** max floor-to-floor **VPL-3210B 123 in, 3212B 147 in, 3214B 171 in**; minimum **28 in** for all. A hoist must lift **1000 lb** minimum; the charger draws **2 A**; the top tie-in sees about **200 lb** of pull; the **lower section weighs about 900 lb**, the heaviest top section about 230 lb. Offset doors with an interlock need a **toe guard** round the interlock to prevent pinch points; Bruno recommends **flush doors**.
