# Tech Tip - VPL Lower Bearing Replacement

2-page general procedure (portal tech-tip 137) for the **lower screw bearing**.

- **The screw hangs from the top thrust bearing** (on the brake mounting plate). It must **never rest on the lower bearing**; there should be about a **¼ in gap** between the screw end and the top of the lower bearing journal.
- Block the platform to unload the screw; front cover and top cap off; power off. Check the two top **pulleys are aligned** with a straight edge (if not, the screw has dropped).
- Remove the brake (heat the splined hub to break its Loctite; re-Loctite the key, hub and set screw on assembly), the brake mounting plate (emery paper if the keyway edges drag), and the belts. Loosen the top bearing collar's two set screws and four bolts (three spacer plates each side).
- Turn the screw to raise it with the top bearing, slide the bottom bearing out (four bolts), check the journal, fit the new bearing hand-tight and align it.
- Turn the screw back down into the bearing, set the ¼ in gap, refit the top spacers and bolts (tap the bearing if the set screws galled it) and the set screws. Pulleys should line up again.
- Belts and tension ([[Tech Tip - VPL Field Tensioning of Belts]]), brake plate (its bearing **flush** with the plate top), brake. Cycle fully down at least once, then tighten the lower bearing bolts; check nothing touches above the screw pulley.
