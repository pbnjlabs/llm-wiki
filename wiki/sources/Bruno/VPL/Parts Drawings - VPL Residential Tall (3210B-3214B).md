# Parts Drawings - VPL Residential Tall (3210B-3214B)

Bruno's assembly drawings (exploded views with parts tables), pulled 2026-10-22 from the Brunopartner parts lookup (Service → Parts Lookup by Serial #). That lookup only takes an exact serial number, so the set was rebuilt from each model's top-level drawing, following assemblies three levels down ([[Bruno-Parts-Lookup]]). **These are Bruno's current revisions**: for an older unit, run its serial on the portal for the parts list as built, or check the manual's exploded view. Dealer prices on the portal are manufacturer suggestions and are not recorded here.

Covers the tall residential battery **VPL-3210B / 3212B / 3214B** (towers VPL-32240CP / 32241CP / 32242CP).

- **1 hp 24 VDC motor VPL-32376** with harness VPL-32420; charger **BCR-24020**; **2 × BTR-12001 12 V 33 Ah U1** batteries on support bracket VPL-32164.
- **Matched ACME screws** VPL-32002 (12 ft) / VPL-32003 (14 ft), flange nut **VPL-32043**, screw pin VPL-32008 and **test nut VPL-34620** in kit VPL-K-3218 (coiled spring pin, ILS-00975).
- Idler pulley VPL-31508, brake mounting plate VPL-31501, cable weight VPL-32204, 3-stop actuator bracket VPL-32287, dealer setup switch harness VPL-32249 and **lower interlock jumper VPL-32277** (kit VPL-K-3230CP).
