# Tech Tip - VPL Door Interlock Wiring (Waupaca, HES 5200, Upper Gate Switches)

Three wiring tips (portal tech-tips 142, 145, 130).

**Waupaca interlocks:** still used, but the old wiring kits are for units **up to these serials**: VPL-3153 12070300108, 3175 12070300111, 3153B 12070300110, 3175B 12070300109, 3210B 12070300097, 3212B 12070300112, 3214B 12110300156. Old kits: upper door VPL-K-3121 (ILS-00863) with Waupaca VPL-31335 (left AC) / VPL-31617 (left DC); lower door VPL-K-3131 (ILS-00993) with VPL-31336 / VPL-31618. **Newer units use VPL-K-3138 (upper door, ILS-01048) and VPL-K-3139 (lower door, ILS-01049).** Toe guard supplied by others.

**HES 5200 electric strike (double switch), lower interlock**, strike wire → Bruno harness: solenoid red → red, black → black; latch-bolt-monitor (door locked) yellow → green, brown → brown (blue not used); latch-bolt monitoring N.O. orange → white (green not used).

**Upper landing gate switches** (after replacing the harness or a switch): **door closed** switch PBS-00118: brown to the middle terminal, blue to the bottom. **Door lock** switch PBS-00116 (roller lever): the roller must sit on the **beveled edge** or the switch is damaged; green to the middle terminal, brown to the back; the outer terminal (nearest the lever) stays empty. Test: the actuator wheel unlocks the gate, and the lift **doesn't move at all with the door open**.
