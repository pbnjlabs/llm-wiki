# Tech Tip - GAL Interlock Wood Posts (Left and Right)

Two 1-page Bruno drawings (portal tech-tips 135 and 136): how to machine a **4 x 4 wood post** to take Bruno's **GAL interlock** kit (VPL-K-3102L for left-hand swing; the right-hand sheet is its mirror). The drawing gives the pocket and the ¾ in and ⅜ in cable holes, about 7 11/16 in and 23/32 in from the references shown. Use the PDF for the dimensions.
