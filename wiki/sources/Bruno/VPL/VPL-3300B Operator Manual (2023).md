# VPL-3300B Operator Manual (unenclosed commercial, 03-07-2023)

Owner's manual for the unenclosed commercial **VPL-3353B / 3375B**
(10 pages). It follows the residential battery manuals (keyed rocker,
cold weather, no salt, hand wheel, 4-LED charger chart, yearly dealer
inspection). Commercial additions:
- **Electric strike interlock (ESI) gate:** at the top landing, hold the
  call/send UP for a few seconds to release the strike.
- If the ESI won't unlock, use the **bypass key, in an emergency only**.
