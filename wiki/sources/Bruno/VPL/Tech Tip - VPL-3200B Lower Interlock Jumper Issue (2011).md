# Tech Tip - VPL-3200B Lower Interlock Jumper Issue (2011)

2-page Bruno field notice (portal tech-tip 148). **Affected:** VPL-3210B, 3212B and 3214B with the **platform gate option**, built **August 1 to October 19, 2011, serials 11080300300 to 11100301707**. A **jumper plug left in the lower interlock harness** bypasses the platform gate's electrical interlock, so the lift can start **up from the lower landing with the platform gate open**. (The gate still can't be opened anywhere else, because of its mechanical lock and the actuator ramp.)

**Action:** remove the jumper plug: a **6-position connector with one blue wire**, near the bottom of the tower on the centre tube inside the gray wire duct, on the harness labelled lower interlock. Then check the platform ramp and every safety.
