# Tech Tip - VPL Jumper Sheets (2017)

Seven one-page Bruno drawings (Rev 0, Sept–Dec 2017; portal tech-tips 203–209) showing the **shipping jumpers** on each VPL family, plus an older commercial jumper article (tech-tip 120). Jumpers stand in for gate, door and option switches that aren't fitted yet; **remove each one only when its switch harness is plugged in**. All refer to **NFPA 70 Article 620** for install wiring. Left/right-hand units are mirror images.

| Sheet | Models | Power | Jumpers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low rise AC | VPL-3153, 3175 | 15 A breaker; 120 VAC cord, 14 AWG, 15 A max | **Yellow VPL-31924** terminal block: **25–29 install mode**, 25–27 platform gate fitted. **Blue VPL-31910** gate harness: remove for an upper gate/door |
| Low rise DC | VPL-3153B, 3175B | **35 A breaker** (lockable); cord 18 AWG, 10 A max | Yellow VPL-31924 (25–29 install, 25–27 platform gate); blue VPL-31910 (upper gate/door) |
| Low rise commercial | VPL-3353B, 3375B | 35 A breaker; 120 VAC line input, battery lead to a 35 A min disconnect | Yellow VPL-31924 (24–28 install, 26–28 platform gate); white VPL-33067 3-pos interlock jumper on the travelling cable (platform gate); brown VPL-33315 6-pos (lower gate/door); blue VPL-31910 (upper gate/door); **red VPL-33068 pit switch** |
| Tall residential (and FZ) | VPL-3210B, 3212B, 3214B (FZ = flood zone) | **60 A disconnect** (batteries) + AC disconnect; cord 18 AWG 10 A | Yellow VPL-31924 **59–61 normal mode** (remove for a mid landing); blue VPL-31910 upper gate; **red VPL-32252 emergency lowering** (remove to fit the kit); green VPL-32277 lower interlock (lower door or platform gate); platform gate and safety pan jumpers |
| Tall commercial (and FZ) | VPL-3310B, 3312B, 3314B | 60 A disconnect + AC disconnect; 120 VAC 3 A max | As tall residential, plus **red VPL-33068 pit switch** |

On commercial units every jumper except the yellow one sits in the junction boxes **outside** the tower; the yellow one is on the terminal strip inside the electrical enclosure.
