# VPL-3310B Tall Commercial Installation Manual (3310B-I, 11-17-2020)

Install manual, with schematics, for the **tall commercial battery VPL**
(VPL-3310B, 3312B and 3314B), usually in a **hoistway** or enclosure
(67 pages).

**Specs:**
- 750 lb. **1210 / 1304 / 1400 lb** with 2 × 34 Ah batteries. The
  3310B's "379 kg" is a misprint.
- 10 ft/min. **1 hp 24 VDC** (2 hp peak).
- **Travel 123 / 147 / 171 in** (10, 12 and 14 ft towers).
- An **emergency lowering kit** as standard, plus a hand-wheel provision.
- Options: 90° and same-side platforms up to 42 × 60, GAL and ESI kits.

**Site:**
- **Hoistway without a flush-mounted door: fit a deflector shield at 60°
  from horizontal on the door.**
- Walls and floor no more than **⅛ in out of plumb**.
- **AC is hard-wired to the disconnect on the side of the tower and is
  not fused**: supply protection per code.
- The hoist and its support must carry the tower weight.
- Drawings ILS-01176 to 01179 (hoistway, pit and no pit).

**Tower:**
- **Bottom half:**
  - Save the **4 × 9 ft crate plywood** to protect the wall.
  - Skate dollies plus 3 hooks; turn it 90° while it hangs.
  - Keep **½ in between the tower and the wall** for the covers.
  - Fit the legs in the hoistway. Discard the shipping plate and the
    yellow bars.
- Fit the angle panel supports and the back and side covers (the short
  cover on 12 and 14 ft units). Seams must be flush.
- **Top half:**
  1. Screw eyes, with 2 washers on top and 2 below.
  2. Hoist and lower it slowly, lining up the **red marks on both drive
     screw halves** and the side tubes.
  3. Finger-tighten the 4 top nuts with a ¾ in flex gear wrench, then
     the jam nuts.
- **Coiled spring pin** through the screw joint (VPL-K-3218, ILS-00975).

**Electrical:**
- Junction boxes and harnesses as on the [[VPL-3300B Installation Manual (2022)]].
- PCB-00164 indicator lights (the layout also shows PCB-00159).
- An electrical enclosure layout label (LBL-00212).

**Emergency lowering (key switch, commercial):**
- **The switch assembly must be mounted outside the hoistway.**
- Open the cover on the main power switch, then **turn and hold the key
  to LOWER**. It returns to OFF when released; remove the key at AUTO.
- **It bypasses the safety circuits, so authorized personnel only.**
  The hand wheel (breaker off, counter-clockwise) is the alternative.

**Lockout:** lock out **the DC breaker or disconnect, the AC input and the
emergency lowering switch** before service.

**Batteries:**
- Unplug AC, disconnect off, hold bar off (don't bump the platform).
- White Molex leads. **BTR-12001 (34 Ah)**.
- Reconnect, and confirm the charger LEDs show charging.

Cold weather, charger chart, maintenance and troubleshooting are as on
the other Bruno VPLs.
