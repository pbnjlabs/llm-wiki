# Tech Tip - Tall VPL Flush Mount Doors

1-page sell sheet (10-11; portal tech-tip 149): Bruno **flush-mount doors** for VPL-3200B hoistways, steel with window or oak, with a steel frame and **an included interlock**. The door face is flush with the finished hoistway wall, so there's nothing to snag and no pinch points. Bruno's 2011 tips recommend flush doors over offset ones, which need a toe guard round the interlock. The flush doors use a **Von Duprin** electric strike, which should have a shut-off timer ([[Tech Tip - VPL Delay Timers and ESI Shut-off Timers]]).
