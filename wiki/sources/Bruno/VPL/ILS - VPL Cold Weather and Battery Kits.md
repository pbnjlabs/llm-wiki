# ILS - VPL Cold Weather and Battery Kits

- **ILS-00864** (4/23/09): **34 Ah battery package upgrade**: support **VPL-31656**; afterwards the **charger LED flashes once a second** while charging.
- **ILS-00924** (01/12/10): **DC VPL cold-weather nut heater** (**VPL-K-3126 / 3130**). The plate and resistor get warm or hot, which is normal. **Below 15 °F (-10 °C)** the thermostat closes and the nut heater gets hot (plate cool); **above 32 °F (0 °C)** it opens (plate warm). Units **before 11/2009 need a ground hole drilled**. Put the updated schematic in the pouch, dated.
- **ILS-01010** (06-10-11): DC cold-weather packages **VPL-K-3126 (3153B, 17 Ah), K-3130 (3175B, 17 Ah), K-3132 (3153B, 34 Ah), K-3133 (3175B, 34 Ah)**: junction box on the charger bracket, **heater pads under the batteries print side up with the 1 in tab folded up**, one red and one white wire per pad, plus a travelling cable carrier.
- **ILS-01011** (06-10-11): **heater pad retrofit SRV-3100-024 (17 Ah) / SRV-3100-025 (34 Ah)**; drill and tap two #8-32 holes if the charger bracket has none.
- **Battery pads turn on below +20 °F (-5 °C) and off at +32 °F (0 °C)** (ILS-01010, 01011, 01107).
- **ILS-01107** (06-10-13): **commercial** cold-weather package **VPL-K-3304 (17 Ah) / VPL-K-3305 (34 Ah)**, same pads.
- **ILS-00994** (01-30-11): **tall VPL VPL-K-3213** (10, 12, 14 ft): a **thermostatic duplex outlet (on at 40 °F/4 °C, off at 60 °F/15 °C)** for **two 60 W battery blankets only**, since anything else is a fire risk and voids the warranty. Older units need two 5/16 in holes; wrap each battery with its wire tie; the charger cord runs through the outlet box. Verify the charger works and the blankets warm.
