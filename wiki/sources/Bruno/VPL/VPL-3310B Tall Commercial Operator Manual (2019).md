# VPL-3310B Tall Commercial Operator Manual (08-15-2019)

Owner's manual for the **enclosed tall commercial VPL-3310B / 3312B /
3314B** (40 pages: English pp. 1–12, Spanish 13–24, French 25–36). It
follows the other battery VPL operator manuals (keyed rocker, mid-way
parking, cold weather, charger LEDs, owner troubleshooting, yearly
inspection). Differences:
- **Emergency operation, electrical:** turn and hold the key switch to
  LOWER (it bypasses the safeties, authorized people only).
- **Emergency operation, manual:** the hand wheel.
