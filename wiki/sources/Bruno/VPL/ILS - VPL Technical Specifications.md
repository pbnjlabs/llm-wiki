# ILS - VPL Technical Specifications

Bruno's spec sheets: **ILS-00834 VPL-3100 residential** (04/02/26), **ILS-00986 VPL-3210B residential tall** (04/02/26) and **ILS-01100 VPL-3353B commercial** (Rev 14, 9/28/2021). All are rated **750 lb (340 kg), one passenger with a mobility device**, FDA Class II 510(k)-exempt (890.3930), ETL listed, with a **16 in self-lowering ramp**, a 3.94:1 dual poly-V belt reduction and an ACME screw with a **bronze drive nut and bronze safety back-up nut**.

| | VPL-3100 (AC) | VPL-3100B (DC) | VPL-3210B | VPL-3353B |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Use | Residential | Residential | Residential | **Commercial** |
| Motor | ½ hp **90 VDC**, 8 A input | ½ hp **24 VDC** | **1 hp** 24 VDC, 20 A | 24 VDC |
| Speed | **9 ft/min** | 10 ft/min | 10 ft/min | 10 ft/min |
| Breaker | 15 A | **35 A** | **60 A** + disconnect | 35 A + disconnect |
| Charger | — | 5 A, 24 VDC | 5 A, 24 VDC (3 A input) | 3 A input |
| ACME screw | 1 in | 1 in | **1¼ in** | 1 in |
| Travel | 3153: 11–53 in; 3175: 28–75 in | same | **up to 123 in**, 2 stops | 11–53 in, 2 stops |
| Final limits | upper | upper | upper | **upper and lower** |
| Weight | 3153 656 lb, 3175 713 lb | 3153B 665 lb, 3175B 718 lb (+40–80 lb batteries) | **1190 lb** with batteries | — |

- **Emergency lowering:** the **VPL-3210B** has an outside keyed switch that lowers on a **separate battery**; the VPL-3100 sheet lists only an **optional manual hand crank** (through the safety-interlocked top cap). The VPL-3353B has **lighted controls** and an **illuminated E-stop with alarm** as standard, and a **platform gate as standard**.
- **Gate weights:** platform gate 68 lb; top landing gate 70 lb (36 in high), 93 lb (36 in wide), **99 lb (42 in)**, 108 lb (42 in wide).
- **Options:** 34 Ah batteries, **cold-weather package below 20 °F (-7 °C)**, landing and platform gate operators, timers, hardwire kit, pit switch, **flood-zone tower** (3210B: electronics and batteries up top), flood sensor, handrail, manual lowering tool.
- **Site prep (all three):** 120 V 15 A **GFI** circuit (required outdoors and in wet areas by the NEC); clear pathway (no conduit, no liquid, steam or gas discharge); **80 in headroom** throughout travel; **4 in, 3500 psi reinforced slab** (insulation sheet under it where it freezes); **four ½ in (3/8 in bolt) × 2½ in min concrete anchors**; tower fastened to a wall near the upper landing with the 5/16-18 holes (**200 lb wall load**; VPL-3353B unenclosed needs none but may use them); **sill gap 3/8–3/4 in**; fascia smooth and non-perforated, full length and width, taking **125 lb on any 4 in square**.
