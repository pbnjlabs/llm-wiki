# Tech Tip - VPL-31496 Motor Replacement

1-page note (portal tech-tip 147), serial **08100300135 and up**: the old face-mount motor **VPL-31496** (5 wires) is replaced by **VPL-31953** (4 wires). Make a **14 AWG jumper from the ORANGE tab to the BROWN tab**; the other wires go to their labelled tabs.
