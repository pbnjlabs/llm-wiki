# ILS - VPL Landing Gates, Platform Gates and Flush-Mount Doors

Bruno's gate and door drawings and install sheets.

**Gate framing (ILS-00804, 1/04/19, 42 in high; ILS-01562, 11/03/22, residential 36 in):** framing (by others) must take a **200 lb concentrated load in any direction**. Rough opening **44¼ in (36 in gate) / 50¼ in (42 in gate)**, doubled 2×4 framing, sill flange flush with the fascia board (3/8 in minimum fascia), 3/16 in pocket.

**Top landing gate detail (ILS-00938 Rev 5, 4/8/13, 42 in; ILS-01561, 11/03/22, residential 36 in):** 1½ in square 12 ga frame, 3 × 1.5 in uprights, call/send in the frame, **cam locking actuator** on the platform. Alignment for straight-through, 90°/adjacent and same-side platforms: the inside of the platform perimeter tube is **flush with the inside of the gate upright on the handle side**; **2 in** from the ramp face to the gate post; **sill gap 3/8 in min, 3/4 in max**; **1¾ in** from the platform side to the framing on 90° units. **Left-hand gate with a right-hand unit**, and vice versa. Adjust the brackets until the cam actuator works. "Do not drill" zone marked on the platform. The residential version adds an **offset cam actuator** when needed. **ILS-01570** (01/16/23) shows the gate activator assembly: the **roller wheel can be flipped inside or swapped for the offset wheel** in the field (access cover on the gate face) to get better contact with the ramp bracket. Spacers go where the harness needs clearance.

**Gate handle retrofit (ILS-00861, 2009)** and **platform gate install (ILS-00945, 04-04-18):** remove the ramp brackets (**save the nylon shoulder washers**, rim facing in); extended ramp bracket on the tower side only; gate wires through the ramp bracket hole; lock washer then flat washer on the top screws; vertical spacer on the handle side; dome caps; reinstall the ramp tower side first; **rivet the safety pan extension** under the platform, straight edge out; plug the gate harness into the tower wiring.

**Mechanical gate interlock for non-Bruno gates (ILS-01071, 07-25-12):** required by the residential elevator code; Bruno gates already have one. It's released by the platform's motion. Mortise it flush in the jamb with the **actuator wheel 6–7 in above the landing**; beak bevel faces out. Wires: **brown common**, **blue door-closed (upper switch)**, **green door-locked (lower switch)**, plus black/red/white call/send. Adjust the actuator and wheel arm until it opens the lock.

**Flush-mount doors (ILS-01027, 10/21/20, oak or steel 36/46 in, LH/RH; ILS-01194 Rev 3, 10/21/20, aluminum):** oak doors come unfinished, steel in gray primer; the steel door with window is **90-minute fire rated** (oak with steel frame: no rating). Delay-action hydraulic closer. The tack-plate sill is knocked off at install. With a fixed ramp, raise the frame by the ramp height. **Rough opening = frame + ½ in.** **3/8 in** clearance, **2–3 in running clearance**. Tables give the offset from the tower ('C'–'F') for each door size, swing, landing, hand and platform length.
