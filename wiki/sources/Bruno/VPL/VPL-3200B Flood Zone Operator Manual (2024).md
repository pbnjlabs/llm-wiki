# VPL-3200B Flood Zone Operator Manual (3200B-OFZ, 09-19-2024)

Owner's manual for the **VPL-3210B-OFZ flood zone** tall residential VPL
(10 pages). It is the same as the standard
[[VPL-3200B Operator Manual]], except:
- **"It will not operate if there is more than 3 in of standing water at
  the base."**
- **1210 lb with 2 × 34 Ah batteries** (34 Ah standard). The manual
  prints "379 kg", which is wrong: 1210 lb is about 549 kg.
- 10 ft/min (7–11 typical). Charger 120 VAC 3 A.
- Hand wheel: breaker off first.
- Same 4-LED charger chart as the [[VPL-3100B Operator Manual (2024)]].
