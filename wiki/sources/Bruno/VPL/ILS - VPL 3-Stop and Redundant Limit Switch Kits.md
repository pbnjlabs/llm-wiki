# ILS - VPL 3-Stop and Redundant Limit Switch Kits

- **ILS-01072 VPL-K-3233** (07/12/12): **tall residential VPL-3200B 3-stop assembly**. Same content as [[Tech Tip - Tall VPL 3-Stop Assembly (VPL-K-3233)]]: the bracket slides into the right-side limit rail and is set at the mid landing; **the lever must point toward the carriage**, or the switches get damaged; the ESI harness goes to "Mid-Landing Interlock"; test that call stations call the right way.
- **ILS-01247 VPL-K-3349** (Rev 3, 10/10/18), **commercial VPL-3300B tall 3-stop**:
  - Switch wires TB10: black 58, white 60, green 61, brown 64, blue 65, orange 66.
  - Enclosure harness: white 59, brown 60, red 63, black 64.
  - The **MUR1 timer** is set to **"Ac" mode**, delay about 2 s on the orange knob. Its LED **blinks all the time** and stays on for the set time at the mid landing.
  - **Hold the mid-landing button until the door opens.** If it opens while passing the mid landing, raise the delay slightly.
- **ILS-01454** (6/11/19), **redundant landing limit switch kit**: a second switch stacked on each middle (landing) limit, with blue wires to the middle tabs, orange to the tops and black to the bottom of the second switch; the three blues crimped together (with the brown at the bottom assembly). **Test: the lift must stop with either middle switch, or both, pressed**, at the top and at the bottom.
