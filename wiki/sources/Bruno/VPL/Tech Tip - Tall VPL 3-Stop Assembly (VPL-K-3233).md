# Tech Tip - Tall VPL 3-Stop Assembly (VPL-K-3233)

5-page instructions (portal tech-tip 122) for adding the **VPL-K-3233 3-stop limit switch assembly** to a VPL-3200B. Tools: ⅛ in Allen, Phillips, 9/16 wrench.

1. Main disconnect off; middle panel(s), electrical enclosure cover and splash shield off.
2. Slide the backing plate into the limit switch rail on the right of the centre tube; bracket flush with the rail, bolts hand-tight until it's set at the mid landing.
3. Route the cable through the travelling-cable slot and the channel into the enclosure; tie off the excess away from moving parts.
4. **The limit switch lever must point toward the carriage/platform.** Running it wrong damages the switches, lever or activation bracket.
5. Wire the interlock harness per the drawing; route it in the raceways. Unplug the jumper and plug the electric strike control harness into the one labelled **"Mid-Landing Interlock"**.
6. Power on; test the landing interlocks and that the call station calls the platform the right way.

On 3-stop lifts the 59–61 yellow jumper comes out for the mid landing ([[Tech Tip - VPL Jumper Sheets (2017)]]).
