# VPL-3353B 3-Gate Installation Manual (3353B3G-I, 08-24-2019)

Supplement for the **VPL-3353B 3-gate system**: an outer (lower landing)
gate, wing walls and side walls that box in an unenclosed commercial
lift (34 pages). The lift itself goes in per the
[[VPL-3300B Installation Manual (2022)]]. Drawings ILS-01154 (pit) and
ILS-01155 (no pit).

**Before you start:**
- The tower and platform must be **level, plumb and anchored**, with the
  top landing gate and call/send boxes wired.
- **Wiring access is poor once the gate walls are up.**

**Fixing:**
- Anchor the walls and gate to the slab (½ in anchors).
- The wall brackets fasten to the **fascia wall**.

**Assembly:**
- Wing wall on the landing gate side (VPL-33182 brackets).
- **Outer gate:** fit the lock "fork" and 6 brackets (VPL-33181,
  VPL-33203 from kit VPL-K-3315, VPL-33182).
- Wider wing wall on the gate side, outer gate actuator bracket, then the
  side wall (flush side toward the platform).
- **Actuator bracket 6.5 in from the outer edge of the side wall.** Adjust
  it if the outer gate won't open.
- **Wiring:** through the outer gate bottom tube, leaving 6 in spare.
  Join the 6 wires by color.
- Access panels, then the **ramp**: its notches fit over the gate's
  bottom tube.

**Troubleshooting:**
- **Stops 2 in above the landing:** adjust the platform gate rod, or the
  lower gate actuator wheel or bracket.
- **Won't run either way:** a gate isn't fully closed.
- **Runs with the gates open:** the **yellow jumper is still in the
  controller**. Remove it.

The platform gate uses a **19 in gas spring closer** (SPR-00008).
