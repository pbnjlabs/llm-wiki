# Tech Tip - Short VPL ACME Screw and Nut Replacement (ILS-01129)

ILS-01129 Rev 2 (12-1-2016), 13 pages: replacing the **ACME screw and/or drive nuts** on a short VPL with kits **SRV-3153-006 / SRV-3175-006** (portal tech-tip 188). For nuts only, skip steps 20 and 27–31. Tools: wood blocks 12–16 in, wrenches, vise grips, Allen keys, sockets, tape and 6 in scale, pry bars, dead-blow hammer.

1. Platform off the tower if fitted (rear side wall off, four bolts; a furniture skate under it helps). Top cap, front and rear covers off.
2. **Top cap switch up (service position).** Run the carriage up about 2.5 ft, slide a **12–16 in wood block** under it and lower onto it. Unplug the main power cord.
3. Brake (three Allen screws, wires can stay on), retaining clip, brake pad hub and splined hub, brake mounting plate (four bolts, four spacers).
4. **Note the belt tension**, loosen the four motor bolts, slide the motor, remove both belts. Pop the pulley's locking hub with its two bolts in the threaded holes.
5. **Measure the upper bearing's position** (screw threads to bearing bottom) so the new screw goes in the same place. Remove the top bearing bolts (loosen its two set screws) and the bottom bearing bolts (no set screws).
6. Mark and remove the lower limit bracket (screw out the back), the nut safety switch bracket and the safety nut plate. Remove the nut bolts (the **short bolt is left rear**), lift the screw out of the bottom bearing and run the nuts off.
7. Reassemble in reverse: bearing's rotating face **up**; set the bearing to the step-5 measurement with the large nut.
8. **Gap between the two ACME nuts = ½ in (12.7 mm). Never let the bolts pull the two nuts together: risk of serious injury or death.** The three long bolts and the short one end up with **4–6 threads past the nut** and **free to move up and down**, by design.
9. Belts back to the original tension (two people helps), motor locking plate secured, **grease the screw with the Bruno grease in the kit**.
10. Run up and down a few times and down to within 1 ft of the bottom bearing, then tighten its four bolts.
