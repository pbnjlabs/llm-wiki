# VPL Commercial Enclosure Installation Manuals (2019)

Five supplements to the main VPL install manuals, one per **commercial
enclosure** style (30–58 pages each, mostly assembly drawings):

| Manual | Lifts | Style |
|---|---|---|
| 3300BENCL-I (08-22-2019) | VPL-3353B, VPL-3375B | Straight-through |
| 3353BENCL-I 90ADJ (01-07-2019) | VPL-3353B, VPL-3375B | 90° adjacent |
| 3310BENCL-I (01-07-2019) | VPL-3310B, 3312B, 3314B | Straight-through (tall) |
| 3310BENCL-I 90ADJ (01-07-2019) | VPL-3310B, 3312B, 3314B | 90° adjacent (tall) |
| 3310BENCL-ISS (01-07-2019) | VPL-3310B, 3312B, 3314B | Same-side on/off (tall) |

**Common steps:**
1. Build the enclosure **from the base up** per the drawings: outer
   walls, door, fascia channels and panels.
2. Route the wiring to the top landing gate, then the top section and
   the remaining fascia, the top landing gate, and the landing gate
   wiring.
3. **Tower knife brackets** (one each side, self-drilling screws), the
   tower center panel, wing wall covers and cap plugs.
4. Anchor the enclosure and VPL to the slab (4 anchors, not supplied):
   **4 in 3500 psi slab**. Drawings ILS-01277 and ILS-01278 for the tall
   straight-through.

**Details:**
- **Door:** two people slide it in, mallet it flush, 3 bolts a side. Fit
  the supplied **door closer**, unless an **automatic door operator** is
  going on (no holes are provided for it; follow the operator's
  instructions).
- **Door wiring:**
  1. From the junction box, cut the cable 6–12 in past the interlock,
     strip 4 in of jacket and ⅜ in of each wire, and match colors.
  2. **Pull the tower breaker before plugging in the door connector**, or
     the **24 V fuse blows**.
  3. Test the call/send and the interlock.
- **Not level?** Use the 8 leveling bolts (HCS-38604), 2 in each corner.
- **Fixed ramp:** centered in the door opening, anchored through its
  tabs.

**Enclosure maintenance:**
- **Every 12 months:** call/send controls and cleanliness.
- **Every 2 months:** the platform must not move with any door open or
  unlocked.
- The tower maintenance is in the main manual.
