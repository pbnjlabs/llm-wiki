# VPL-3200B Flood Zone Tower Installation Manual (3200B-IFZ, 09-30-2025)

Install manual for the **flood zone** version of the tall residential
battery VPL (**VPL-3210B-OFZ**, 10 ft tower) (75 pages). The procedure
**matches the standard [[VPL-3200B Install Manual]]** (09-23-2025) step
for step:
- Two-piece tower hoisted on a plywood-protected wall.
- Red marks on the drive screw halves, coiled spring pin (VPL-K-3218,
  ILS-00975).
- Gates, cam and activator, upper limit.
- Emergency lowering kit VPL-K-3227 and the multiple power-source
  lockout.
- Battery change, charger LEDs, maintenance and troubleshooting.

What differs is the hardware in the flood-zone parts list:
- **Electrical enclosure assembly "RES FZ" (VPL-32359)** and a flood-zone
  wiring diagram label (LBL-00228).
- **34 Ah battery package "FZ" (VPL-32394)**, with charger BCR-24020 on
  its own mounting bracket (VPL-33961).
- **2-pole NEMA 3R disconnect box with a 60 A breaker (EBX-00011)**, with
  ¾ in NPT nipple and locknuts.
- A **power cord with a C13 connector** (VPL-32325), a molded top cover
  with a window (VPL-32389), and a 1 hp 24 VDC motor.

The operator manual says it **won't run with more than 3 in of standing
water at the base** ([[VPL-3200B Flood Zone Operator Manual (2024)]]). The
cover page also carries a stray "SRE-2010G INSTALLATION 06-07-2021"
footer.
