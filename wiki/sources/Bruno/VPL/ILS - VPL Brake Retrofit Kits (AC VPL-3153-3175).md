# ILS - VPL Brake Retrofit Kits (AC VPL-3153-3175)

Two 2013 sheets for fitting an **electro-mechanical brake** to **AC VPL-3153/3175** lifts. Bruno calls these "**special cases of excess drifting or coasting**".
- **ILS-01121** (08/16/13), **SRV-3100-032**: units **S/N 12070300108 and up**. Brake plus a **VPL-31989 brake diode module** piggybacked on the white (NEU) and yellow wires at the board.
- **ILS-01165** (09/25/13), **SRV-3100-039**: units with the **PCB-00137** AC board. The brake plugs into **BRK+ (white) / BRK- (black)** on the board, and **fuse F2 comes out** because that circuit isn't needed with the electro-mechanical brake.

Mechanical steps (both):
1. Discard the blue capacitor.
2. Handle into the lever with a split washer.
3. Tapered mounting plate onto the screw shaft, taper toward the brake plate.
4. **Green Loctite 609** in the keyway, 3/16 × 1 5/8 in key held with a T-handle.
5. Splined hub on, set screws with **blue Loctite 242** (wipe the excess).
6. Pad hub (hub side toward you), then the brake with its harness to the right.
7. **Three brake bolts at 67 in-lb**, rubber boot back on.

Test: the brake must **release as the motor starts** and not drag.
