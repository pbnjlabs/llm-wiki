# Bruno VA FSS Price List (2021–2026)

Bruno's own **Authorized Federal Supply Schedule Price List** for the
VA: Medical Equipment and Supplies, **FSC Group 65, Part II, Section A
(65 II A)**, FSC Class 6515, SIN **A-76** (patient lifts for hospital
and home use). **Contract 36F79721D0099**, period **3/1/2021 –
2/28/2026**. Bruno Independent Living Aids, Oconomowoc, WI; business
size: small. Pages 10–24 of PMS's [[PMS FSS Contracts Binder]].

**How PMS uses it (per the human):** a manufacturer's FSS price is the
**ceiling for what PMS can charge the VA** for that item, and the
reference for **building VA bids**. Not needed for daily work; it's on
file for when it's asked for. These are Bruno's prices, not PMS rates.
**PMS's Bruno dealer (Solutions) has no 65 II A contract and doesn't
handle any 65 II A work** (per the human). See [[VA-FSS-Contracts]].

> [!warning] Older contract period
> The contract has been **renewed** (5-year terms, per the human). This
> copy shows the 2021–2026 period, so prices may have changed. Check the
> current list (GSA eLibrary or the VA MedSurg catalog search) before
> quoting a number.

Bruno VA contact: va.solutions@bruno.com, 877-245-2838. Dealer service
line (returns, damage, warranty): **1-800-656-0552**.

## Curved rail (CRE) ordering rules (p. 19)

CRE units are custom-built to the staircase, so they have their own
rules:

1. **Bruno won't accept a CRE order until an authorized Bruno dealer has
   done a site evaluation** (see [[Stairlift-Evaluation]],
   [[Staircase-Measuring-System]]).
2. **Cancellation after the order is received but before it ships:**
   - Site drawings done: **$350** fee for the engineering layout.
   - Drawings accepted and the unit in production: **50%** fee for
     materials, fabrication and assembly.
3. Delivery 42 days ARO, expedited 21 days. **All rail components are
   custom and can't be returned or exchanged for credit.**

## Bruno Gold Government Warranty (pp. 20–22)

Three government versions. All periods start on the **date of
installation by a Bruno Authorized Dealer**, and labor is covered for
the first **30 days** only.

| Product | Major components | All other parts |
|---|---|---|
| Indoor stairlift | **Lifetime**, while the original recipient owns the lift (motor, gearbox drivetrain, stationary rail; not optional equipment) | 2 years |
| Outdoor and custom rail stairlift | 5 years (motor/gearbox drive train, rail system) | 2 years |
| Vertical platform lift | 2 years (motor, Acme screw and drive nut, brake, pulleys, belt, screw bearings) | 1 year |

- **Indoor stairlift only:** the install date used for the warranty can
  be **no later than 14 months after the lift shipped from the
  factory**.
- **Not covered:** misuse, abuse, accidents, neglect, or modifications
  not made by a Bruno Authorized Dealer; consumables (e.g. remote
  batteries); labor after 30 days; reasonable wear and tear.
- All service and warranty issues go to **Bruno and the ordering
  facility**. All maintenance and repair beyond the owner's-manual
  basics must be done by a Bruno Authorized Dealer to keep coverage.
- Bruno, with the ordering facility, can repair, replace, or (if
  neither is practical) refund the purchase price. No consequential
  damages.

These match the dealer terms on [[Warranty]], with the two extra
conditions above.

## Returned goods policy (p. 23)

- **Every request needs the serial number.** The RA number must be
  written clearly on the outside of every box.
- UPS returns: Bruno sends a call tag with the RA number. LTL returns:
  call Bruno's shipping coordinator (ext. 5349) or Bruno Service at
  1-800-656-0552.
- Unauthorized returns are refused and sent back at the ordering
  facility's expense.
- **Bruno must receive the return within 30 days of the RA issue date.**
- Warranty parts are inspected. **No problem found: returned, or charged
  a 15% (minimum) restocking fee.** Find the actual problem before
  requesting parts.
- Anything returned that wasn't shipped in error and isn't a warranty
  item: **minimum 15% restocking fee**.
- **Installed product: refused, or minimum 25% restocking fee**, at
  Bruno's discretion.
- Shipped in error or a warranty item: no restocking fee, Bruno pays
  return freight.
- Must be packed with care and returned in good condition.
- **Custom and special orders can't be returned for credit.** Nothing
  older than **60 days** is accepted as a general return (warranty
  only).

## Damaged goods claims (p. 24)

- **Visible damage:** don't sign the delivery receipt until all damage
  or loss is noted with the carrier's driver.
- **Concealed damage:** tell the delivering carrier immediately.
- Either way, **request an inspector**, and don't move or throw out the
  product or packing until the inspector's damage report is done. The
  carrier is responsible; Bruno files the claim once it has the report.
- Don't send damaged product back to Bruno without prior approval and an
  RA. It will be refused.
