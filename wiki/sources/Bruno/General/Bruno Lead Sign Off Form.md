# Bruno Lead Sign Off Form

A one-page, undated letter to the veteran to get **the property
owner's permission** before the VA installs equipment. Blank form; it
doesn't say who wrote it. The file name ties it to **Bruno lead** jobs;
the form itself doesn't mention Bruno.

## Two sections

1. **The veteran owns the property:** certifies they own the address and
   authorizes the VA to install the described equipment on it for the
   named veteran. Veteran signs and dates.
2. **The veteran rents or lives with a relative or friend:** the
   **property owner** fills in their name, address and phone and signs
   and dates.

Get this signed before a VA install on a property the veteran may not
own, e.g. a stairlift or vehicle lift job.
