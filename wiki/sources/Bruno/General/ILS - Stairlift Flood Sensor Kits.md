# ILS - Stairlift Flood Sensor Kits

Flood (water) sensor kits: **CRE-K-2156L/R (CRE-2110, ILS-01589)**, **SRE-K-2206L/R (SRE-2010, ILS-01590)**, **SRE-K-3095L/R (SRE-3050, ILS-01591)**; the SRE-5000 kit is on [[ILS - SRE-5000 Service Sheets]]. ESD strap (ILS-00679, SRV-000-001).

- New positive and negative battery harnesses (fused power for the sensor), the **sensor mounted at the lowest point of the carriage, about level with the floor** (metal tabs down, cable out the top; CRE on the drive cover through a 5/8 in hole, SRE-2010 on the rear panel, SRE-3050 under the front cover), and the sensor wired **in series with the 3-pin safety/limits connector (J15)** (cut the old plug's locking tabs if needed).
- **Test:** run down and hold a **damp cloth** under the sensor: it stops at once; it can still drive up, away from the water. Allow about **½ hour to dry** before running down again (water-based cleaners trip it too).
- **Tell the user about the flood stop.**
