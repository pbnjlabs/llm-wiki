# ILS - Stairlift Armrest and Paddle Switch Sheets

Armrest and switch sheets.
- **ILS-01541, Elan: move the paddle switch to the other arm:** footrest cover off, unplug the 8-pin; seat off (swivel pin); pry the armrest covers; swap the armrest harness and switch assembly SRE-30513 and cover plate SRE-30480 across; **loop the harness round the backbone cover's cylinder** (lets the arm fold 90°).
- **ILS-01563, Elite: seat switch changeover** (video QR): seat-back upholstery (five T30), arm screws, reroute the harness through the tube keeping the service loop; narrow end of the switch opening in the upper cover; seat belt screws **30 in-lb**.
- **ILS-01542 / ILS-01490 / ILS-01646, low-profile rocker:** pull the paddle actuator CRE-31026 straight off, snap the rocker on. **SRE-30544 for SRE-3050 and CRE-3110; SRE-50198 for SRE-5000, SRE-2010 and CRE-2110** (ILS-01646, 2026).
- **ILS-01494, Elan armrest bearing:** drive out the arm pin; new bearing SRE-30465L/R; **green retaining compound** on the pin's knurl, ¼ in proud then flush; seat the harness in the wiring support (slides in easiest at 45°); a knocked-off bumper won't stay on, so don't reuse it.
- **ILS-01547, Elite armrest extension adjustment:** image only.
