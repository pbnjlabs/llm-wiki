# ILS - Stairlift USB Charging Retrofit Kits

USB charging port retrofits for the global ("G") models: **CRE-K-2157L/R (CRE-2110G)**, **SRE-K-2207L/R (SRE-2010G)**, **SRE-K-3092 (SRE-3050G)**; the SRE-5000 kit SRE-K-5043WG is on [[ILS - SRE-5000 Service Sheets]]. New battery harnesses with USB pigtails, port **PMR-00201** on a new cover, decal DEC-00687. **Blue LEDs** on the port show power; rotate the cover for USB-A and USB-C; test with a phone. ESD strap required.

Feeds: [[CRE-2110]], [[SRE-2010]], [[SRE-3050]]
