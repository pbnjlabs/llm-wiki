# Tech Tip - Bruno Return Goods Policy

3-page Bruno dealer policy (portal tech-tip 24) on **returns and shipping damage**.

**Returns (RMA):**
- **No RMA number on the outside of every box → refused**, sent back at the dealer's cost.
- Must arrive **within 30 days** of the RMA date; **serial numbers must match** what was given to Technical Service.
- Warranty parts: Bruno sends a **UPS call tag** with the replacement part. No replacement needed → request one with the RMA Pick-Up Request Form (return.request@bruno.com). LTL returns are arranged through Bruno's traffic coordinator.
- **No defect found** (or not a manufacturer's defect) → **15% minimum restock fee**, plus damaged or missing parts.
- **Full units** not shipped in error or under warranty: **15% minimum**; installed or not brand new: **25% minimum** or refused. **Over 60 days old: no returns** except warranty. **Custom and special orders: no returns.**

**Shipping damage:** Bruno isn't responsible for carrier damage or loss. **Don't sign the delivery receipt until visible damage is noted with the driver.** Report concealed damage to the carrier **immediately**, ask for an inspector, and keep the product and packing until the report is done; then file the claim with the carrier. Don't send damaged product back to Bruno without approval and an RMA.

Bruno Technical Service 1-800-656-0552 (press 33 for returns).
