# ILS - Stairlift Australian Addenda

Four 07/27/26 addenda for the **Australian market** (SRE-3050G, SRE-2010G, SRE-2010E, CRE-2110G; the SRE-5000G one is on [[ILS - SRE-5000 Service Sheets]]). Not US requirements; useful as examples of extra code steps: AS1735.7 and AS/NZS 3000 compliance; **straight models limited to 45°** (CRE average ≤ 45°); access-along-stairway, end-person (300 x 600 mm) and **1700 mm head clearance** checks; stairway flush and lit to **75 lx**; **rail earthing** (kits SRE-K-2217, CRE-K-2162: ground wire from the charger's power ground to the rail, never to the moving folding section); call/send mounted in line of sight but out of the user's way.
