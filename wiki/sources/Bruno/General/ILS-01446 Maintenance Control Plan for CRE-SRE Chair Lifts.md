# ILS-01446 Maintenance Control Plan for CRE-SRE Chair Lifts

ILS-01446 Rev 4 (04/20/26), 22 pages: Bruno's **maintenance control plan for all CRE and SRE stairlifts** installed where **ASME A18.1-2017, -2020 or -2023** applies (section numbers are A18.1-2017's). It **must be kept with the installation and operator manuals** and shown to inspectors or authorized technicians on request. A18.1 Section 4 = commercial (not a private residence), Section 7 = residential.

**Weekly operational checklist** (optional for Section 7 residential lifts), conducted by authorized personnel, with a pass/fail log **kept 5 years minimum**:
1–3. Arm controls and call/sends work and stop on release; stops at each end and at the folding-rail mid-park (SRE-5000 in continuous mode runs through it).
4. Audible/visual diagnostics: park on the charge strip, swivel the seat, press the arm switch: it should sound. **CRE-2110 and SRE-2010: if silent, check it isn't in Mode 3 (quiet: DIP 1 on, DIP 2 off)**; set both off to test, then back. The display under the seat should show **C4** with the seat swivelled (as written).
5. Seat belt latches and releases. 6. Charger LEDs show AC and charging/charged on the strip.
7. Seat safety switch stops it when swivelled. 8–9. **Carriage and footrest safety panels**: pressing one stops the lift, and holding it in still lets it drive the other way.
10. **Wheels spin freely**; a cyclic clunk or squeak about **every 5 in** = a flat or damaged wheel (dealer removes the carriage). Nothing else touches the rail (CRE: check through the turns).
11. Key switch off disables the arm switch (SRE-3000/2010 beep both ways). 12. Breaker cuts power. 13. No odd noise or vibration on a full seated ride.
14. **Rail clean (Pledge).** 15. **Soft stops, rail charge strips and carriage contacts clean (rubbing alcohol); charge contacts every 6 months.**
16. **Yearly:** rail mount hardware tight; **regrease gear rack teeth only, with PTFE grease**; heavy shavings under the rack = excessive wear.
17. Power footrest folds with the seat and reverses on resistance (SRE-5000: magnetic break-away, resets next power cycle; **never force a power footrest by hand**); rests on its stop tabs. 18. Power swivel at the top reverses on resistance. 19. **Power folding rail** (never fold by hand): fold/unfold at mid-park, **disable switch** test (off = rail stays down and carriage passes mid-park), beep and yellow LED while moving. SRE-5000 defaults to **continuous mode** (folds automatically while you hold the paddle; slows at mid-park); mid-park stop mode is set per its tech support guide.

**Inspection and testing checklist** (inspection company):
- **Routine (A18.1 10.2): every 6 months for Section 4 (commercial), every year for Section 7 (residential)**: controls, seat switch, **code data plate** (under the seat: A18.1 edition, rated load, speed), ride, key switch, covers, **drive machine brake** (click on release; squeal or slow travel = brake not releasing; look through the cap plug behind the motor; hot = dragging), gear rack, wheels, governor, safety panels, normal stops (mid-park, soft stops), final stops, rail (wear marks, cracks, flush joints).
- **Periodic (10.3.1): every year**: safety panels, governor/overspeed, normal and final terminal stopping devices.
- **Five-year (10.3.3)**: governor/overspeed test plus a **metal tag on the safety carrier** with test date and tester's name; **brake test with 125% of rated load** (strap the weight on and run down to the lowest landing).

**Overspeed field tests:**
- **SRE-2010C/EC/EOS/OS, SRE-3000, SRE-3050, SRE-5000:** breaker off; seat, footrest, drive cover and safety panels off; refit seat and footrest; run down on the remote and **insert the overspeed trip key in the flange cover slot** (replacement key **SRE-20770**). Reset, reassemble, retest. (Brainshark: "SRE Overspeed Governor Field Test Fixture Instructions".)
- **CRE-2110C/OS, wheel-driven overspeed:** while running down, press the reset arm down with a ruler.
- **CRE-2110C/EC/EOS/OS, gear-driven overspeed:** on a straight section, stand above the carriage, put a **magnet retriever** (at least 1.5 lb pull, head 5/16 in max, e.g. McMaster 5838A14) in the manual trip pocket and run down: it trips within a few inches. Breaker off, cap off the motor hex shaft, back off with an **8 mm** ratchet counter-clockwise, rotate the OS trip plate to neutral (groove aligned with the hole).
- **Final limit:** SRE-5000: crank the motor (¼ in socket) until the carriage safety flap presses on the end cap; the arm switch must not run it. Others: hold the final limit switch under the carriage; it must not run; it reboots on release.

**Maintenance and repair log** (5 years minimum): date and time, malfunction, any accident and its nature, completion date, who, what was done.
