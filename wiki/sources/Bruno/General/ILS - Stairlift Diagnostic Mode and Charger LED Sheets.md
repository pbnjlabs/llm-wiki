# ILS - Stairlift Diagnostic Mode and Charger LED Sheets

Elan and Elite one-pagers (2022).
- **ILS-01539 Elan / ILS-01546 Elite, multi-user to single-user:** units ship in **multi-user** mode (audible diagnostics). **Single-user** silences them, e.g. no beep every 3 s with the seat swivelled at the top. **Elan:** top carriage cover off, move the jumper to the **two right-hand pins**. **Elite:** seat up, hinge plate cover off, DIP switch positions **1 and 2** on the 4-gang switch.
- **Multi-user beeps (both):** power-up chirp; safety device chirp; seat safety chirp every 3 s; low voltage stop 5 short; over-current 4 short; over-temperature 3 short; parked, no charger 3 short 2 long (every 4 min); battery critical 2 short 3 long (every minute until over 16 V); multiple buttons at power-up 2 / pause / 5 (every 5 s); multiple buttons 2 / pause / 3 (every 30 s); transmitter memory full 1 short 2 long.
- **ILS-01543 / ILS-01545, charger LED information** (Elan and Elite): image-only tables, not transcribed. See the charger LED list on [[Stairlift-Troubleshooting]].
