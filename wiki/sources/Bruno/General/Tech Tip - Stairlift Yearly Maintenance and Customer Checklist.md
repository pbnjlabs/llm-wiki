# Tech Tip - Stairlift Yearly Maintenance and Customer Checklist

2-page scan, ©2008 (portal tech-tip 59, "Stairlift Yearly Operating Maintenance"; image only, read visually).

**Page 1, yearly maintenance:** clean rails, racks and wheels and regrease; dry or worn belts (lubricate); rail wear (no groove); clean charging contacts on carriage and rail ends with Scotch-Brite; battery load test; safety switches (footrest, carriage, seat); armrest switch and key switch; charger output; load test on the remotes (voltage travelling up, against and away from the contacts, contacts and lights); speed; seat belt; exposed wiring; seat swivel moves and locks; hardware tight.

**Page 2, customer inspection and checklist** (Electra-Ride era): the customer signs that the seat, back, rail and carriage paint are undamaged and the charger's red light is on, and that they were shown the warranty, lap belt, rocker switch, footrest safety switch, final limit switch, rail and charger lights, breaker, swivel latch and transmitters. A tear-off strip gives the dealer, installer, model and serial number.

The current model manuals carry their own, longer yearly lists. See [[Stairlift-Maintenance]].
