# ILS - Stairlift Overspeed Test Sheets

Three overspeed test sheets.
- **ILS-00543** (2010): the **96 VDC overspeed test fixture** (FIX-00381–385). Same as the tech tip on legacy lifts, plus a **lead chart**: orange/blue connections by model and hand for SRE-1540, SRE-1550 (not after SN 03060300543), SRE-2000, **CRE-2100 and CRE-2110**. Unoccupied lift, stairs blocked, weights strapped 20/80 footrest/seat.
- **ILS-00791** (05-20-08): **SRE-2010C flange cover replacement and trip-key overspeed test** for regulatory inspection (A18.1-2003). Fits the new **slotted flange cover** (Loctite 242 on the three M6 shoulder bolts; keep the three nylon washers under the stop flange). Test: weight on, run down on the remote, push the **trip key** into the slot (flip 180° for LH). **Reset:** power off, move the switch wire from **N/O to N/C**, power on and run **up only 3–4 in**, power off, seat the plunger in the stop-flange notch, wire back to N/O, test both ways.
- **ILS-01025** (10-11-11): the **SRV-2010-018 drop test** kit, same content as [[Tech Tip - SRE-2010C Overspeed Test (SRV-2010-018)]].

Current method for all SRE models is the trip key in ILS-01446 ([[ILS-01446 Maintenance Control Plan for CRE-SRE Chair Lifts]]).
