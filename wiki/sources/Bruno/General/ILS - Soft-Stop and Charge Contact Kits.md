# ILS - Soft-Stop and Charge Contact Kits

- **Soft-stop kits SRE-K-3033 (standard rail) and SRE-K-3041 (folding rail) (ILS-01074, ILS-01080, 2013, SRE-2010 and SRE-3000):** the **slow-down strip must connect to the negative rail charge strip**. Black-end lead to the strip at the **charger end**, red-end lead to the other end (folding rail: the charger **must** be at the top). Commercial units with overspeed: top strip **4 in in from the end cap**. Disconnect the top lead while fitting the carriage.
- **SRV-3000-013 soft-stop retrofit (ILS-01214, 2021):** SRE-3000 built **after 7/31/2012 (SN 12070330924)**; older units need a new main PCB; **all SRE-3050s**. New lower charge contact + slow-down assembly slid to the lower end cap; cut the charge strip carrier 1¼ in short; upper assembly fitted after the carriage is on. **Don't remove the lower end cap.**
- **Self-adjusting charge contact (ILS-01265, 2018):** SRE-2010 (rear skirt off, swap with existing hardware) and SRE-3000 (battery housing off; check the bracket height with the **SRV-3000-015 Elan position tool** and file the posts 4–6 strokes at a time if it's out; Loctite 242, **3.5 Nm**). **Don't pre-load the finger springs with the wires.** Worn rail strips: **flip them to the unused side** or replace.
