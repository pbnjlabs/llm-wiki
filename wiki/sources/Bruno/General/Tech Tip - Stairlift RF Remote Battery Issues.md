# Tech Tip - Stairlift RF Remote Battery Issues

2-page Bruno tip (portal tech-tip 63) on RF remotes that keep killing batteries.

- **X-Bee Pro (high-power) remotes aren't a fix** for battery problems. They draw more current, so batteries drop faster.
- The 9 V battery works down to **4 V**. Use a good alkaline (Energizer, Rayovac, Duracell).
- Battery reads fine and swapping it in and out brings the remote back → a **contact problem** between the board and the battery.
- Remotes **time out after 3 minutes** of a held button (e.g. in a drawer with something on it).
- Designed for **2–3 runs a day on a 24 ft run**. Bruno suggests **replacing remote batteries yearly**.
- Before calling Bruno, know: run length, uses per day, whether the remotes send someone up/down or just park/charge, number of users, residential or commercial, standard or keyed, and where and how they're mounted.
