# ILS - Final Limit Compression System

Three 2014 sheets for jurisdictions that require **testing the final limit with the end cap in place**. A **conical spring** on the end bracket lets the carriage be cranked onto the final limit.
- **ILS-01193 (SRE and CRE):** table of spring-hole X/Y and final-limit bracket Z positions, screw length and socket by model: SRE-2010 (with/without folding rail), SRE-2010C, SRE-2010E, **SRE-3000 (¼ in socket)**, CRE-2110 (5/16 in). **Straight rail cut lengths must be 1 in longer** to fit the spring.
- **ILS-01199 (SRE):** template on the end cap marks the spring location.
- **ILS-01195 (CRE):** new mounting bracket and adjustable final-limit bracket within **½ in** of the rocker switch.
- **Test:** run to the spring-loaded end stop until the limit stops it; pull the cap on the front cover and crank the motor shaft (round-handled driver and socket) up until the final limit trips; the rocker must do nothing (or the board loses power); crank back; cap on.
