# ILS - Stairlift Seat Belts and Harnesses

Belt sheets for the Elite seat.
- **ILS-01510 (8-2-2021):** Elite seats **before October 2020** had belt **BLT-00005** (no longer made); replace with **BLT-00035** using kit **SRV-2010-035L/R** (bracket on the side shown) or **-036** for XL seats. Parts don't mix; remove the lap belt tether CRE-00850. Buckle bolt **10 ft-lb**.
- **ILS-01572:** the **new Elite seat from September 27, 2022** uses **BLT-00037**: kits **SRV-2010-040 (indoor)** and **-041 (outdoor)**; receiver and buckle bolts **16.9 Nm (12.5 ft-lb)**.
- **ILS-01566 (11/23/22), 4-point harness SRE-21356XX on the Elite seat:** bracket SRE-21343XX through the backrest cover; straps mirrored side to side, pads on the shoulders not the neck. **The harness doesn't replace the seat belt; the user wears both.**
- **ILS-00524 (2004), SRE-00692 4-point harness retrofit** on older seats (drill four 3/16 in holes in the seat board only).
