# ILS - Stairlift Motor Brake Kits

Three brake-kit sheets. **Brakes are replaceable on all current Bruno carriages**, Elan and Elite.
- **ILS-01281, SRV-3000-016 (SRE-3000):** remove the battery housing and top-mid cover; loosen three screws and lift the brake off. **Check the brake disc drive hub is tight on the motor shaft: if it's loose or came off, the carriage must be replaced.** Brake wiring: **SN 14010330854 and older** both brake wires to the PCB; **14010330855 and newer** one to the PCB, one to the circuit-breaker jumper harness. Blue threadlocker, brake screws **41–42 in-lb (4.7 Nm)**; test loaded and unloaded for drag.
- **ILS-01324 (03/20/25):** **SRV-3000-016 for SRE-3000/SRE-3050** and **SRV-2110-032 for CRE-2110/SRE-2010 carriages (2025 and later)**: same kit except the brake. Replace the roll pin and hex drive nut (groove toward the motor end bell), blue threadlocker on the set screw, then centre the disc by releasing the brake electrically with one motor lead (blue or brown) unplugged (Elite/CRE: with the installation jumper harness fitted).
- **ILS-01595, SRV-2010-045 (Elite SRE-2010/CRE-2110):** lock out the breaker; seat, footrest, covers off; unplug the brake (CRE: from the jumper harness; SRE: from the board, pulled through the black tube); two 7/64 in screws; loosen the square hub's two 1.5 mm set screws; clean the shaft (Scotch-Brite) and deburr the key slot; fit the key and **hex hub at 0.059–0.079 in (1.5–2 mm) height by feeler gauge**; blue threadlocker; brake on with wires matching; run on the shipping jumper; it should **click** clearly; test all safeties.

This settles TechMate's "Elite/CRE brake can't be replaced alone" note: Bruno sells brake kits for both ([[TechMate Stairlift Troubleshooter]]).
