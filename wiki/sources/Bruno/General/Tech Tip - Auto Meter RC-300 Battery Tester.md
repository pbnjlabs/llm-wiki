# Tech Tip - Auto Meter RC-300 Battery Tester

The 20-page **Auto Meter RC-300** operator's manual, posted by Bruno (portal tech-tip 23; image only). The RC-300 is a hand-held **reserve-capacity load tester** for 6 V and 12 V sealed lead-acid batteries of **4–50 Ah**, which covers stairlift, VPL and most mobility batteries.

- 40 A load, 1 x 2.5 in display, 9 V internal battery, stores the last 50 tests; optional infrared printer and PC cable.
- **Use:** clamps on (reversed clamps flash a reversed-connection screen); enter the battery temperature; preliminary check shows voltage, % charge and estimated Ah; **under 11.0 V it asks whether the battery was charged**: charge it before load testing. Enter the rated Ah and run the load test.
- **Results:** **Good**; **Marginal** (consider replacing if it'll be relied on); **Bad: replace**; **Good, needs charge**; **Charge and retest** (not enough charge for a full test).
- Safety: eye protection, no sparks or flames, don't test a frozen battery or one connected to a charger.

PMS house standard: replace every battery in the unit together ([[Mobility-Batteries]]).
