# Tech Tip - Footrest Lift Linkage Replacement

1-page tip (portal tech-tip 62): replacing the **plastic footrest lift link** with the new **spring-steel link** on "SRE-2100" (almost certainly the SRE-2010; there's no SRE-2100) and **CRE-2110** footrests.

1. Remove the old plastic link and its two nylon flange washers; discard them.
2. Keep the lock nut and the M6 button-head screw (pre-applied Loctite).
3. Fit the new link and tighten the screw and nut.
4. Then **back both off one full turn** so the link can float and flex.

Feeds: [[SRE-2010]], [[CRE-2110]]
