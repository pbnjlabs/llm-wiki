# Bruno Lifts Price List (PMS account SC-0307)

Bruno's **vehicle lifts price list**, printed from Bruno's dealer
portal under **PMS's dealer account (SC-0307)**. Two pages, undated.
Columns: **MSRP (plus install and shipping)** and **dealer price**. The
dealer price is **40% of MSRP** for nearly everything (e.g. $3,475 →
$1,390); the backup camera kits are 50%.
The printout **cuts off partway through the PUL-1100 options**.

## Common options and parts (MSRP / dealer)

**ASL-275 / 275L (200 Series)**

- Securement: ASL-K-278 3 belts $445 / $178 (includes underseat hook
  plate or multipoint seat kit when ordered with a lift); ASL-K-285 2
  belts (LW platform) $250 / $100; 1 belt kit $165 / $66; powerchair
  securement arm (HTP Hold-Tite, ASL-16627; needs bumpers ASL-16699,
  $55 / $22) $310 / $124
- Platforms: Large platform (2.5 in wider, 6 in longer; **required for
  1-ton or larger vehicles**; needs 3 belts) $390 / $156; center deck
  standard ASL-16719 or large ASL-16720 (scooters only) $150 / $60;
  wheel chock kit ASL-K-282 $80 / $32; scooter foot kit ASL-16611 $290 /
  $116
- **Swing-away** (angle adjustable, Class II/III): ASL-16603 / ASL-16738 /
  ASL-16603-W1, $855 / $342
- **Hitch adapters**, $185 / $74 each: Class III standard ASL-16604,
  3 in drop/raise ASL-16605, 6 in drop/raise ASL-16606; Class II
  standard ASL-16607, 3 in ASL-16608, 6 in ASL-16609. **The first
  drop/raise adapter is included; extras are charged.**
- Underseat hook plates, $135 / $54: standard; wide DDC-00117 (19–22 in
  seat); extra wide DDC-00118 (23–26 in seat)
- 3rd brake light ASL-16434 $305 / $122; 7-pin trailer connector harness
