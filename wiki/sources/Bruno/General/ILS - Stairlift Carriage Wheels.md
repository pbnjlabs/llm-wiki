# ILS - Stairlift Carriage Wheels

- **ILS-00830 (12/13/23), SRE-2010 nylon wheel kit SRV-2010-003:** angled wheels from outside; **middle wheels** need the batteries out, the carriage traced with a marker, the gear cover and four **M10 carriage bolts** removed (refit to **47 ft-lb**, safety lock washers); **top wheels** through holes after loosening the back plate. **Grease all replaced bearings with Magnalube-G** (or equivalent).
- **ILS-00540 (2008):** stack order for an SRE nylon wheel: nylon washer, flat washer, wheel with bearing, flat washer, nylon washer, precision support washer, heavy-duty retaining ring.
