# ILS - General Install Notices (Hybrid Vehicles, ESD, Dielectric Grease)

Three general Bruno notices that other ILS sheets point to.

**Working on hybrid vehicles (ILS-00642 Rev 1, 11-12-05):**
- **Up to 650 V DC** can be present in hybrid electronics and cabling. High-voltage wiring is marked with **orange or yellow** jacketing, sleeving or tags; HV parts carry "High Voltage" labels. **Never touch them, attach anything to them, or route wires near them.**
- Installing Bruno products doesn't need panels over high-voltage parts removed, but stay aware. Read the vehicle owner's manual first and follow the vehicle maker's accessory rules.
- **Don't power mobility equipment from a hybrid's small 12 V battery**: it isn't sized for the draw. Bruno recommended its self-contained **Pow'r Pack** battery and charger.
- Near HV systems: tested, undamaged, dry **insulated gloves**; no loose metal objects (pencils, scales) that could drop and short; post a **"CAUTION: HIGH VOLTAGE. DO NOT TOUCH"** sign on the vehicle. (Based on Toyota's hybrid bulletin.) The same caution applies to EVs; the Curb-Sider manual also warns about hybrid and EV batteries ([[VSL-6000-6900]]).

**ESD notice (ILS-01340, 02/1/17; ILS-00679 is the vehicle-lift version cited by the trailer-wiring kits):** circuit boards are static-sensitive. **Wear the grounded ESD wrist strap** (Bruno SRV-000-001) and follow its package; ground yourself on bare metal (an exposed bolt, rail or tube screw) before reaching in and again as you work; **handle boards by the edges**, never pressing on the surface.

**Dielectric grease (ILS-00496, 24 May 2004):** a small amount on **motor connections** for good contact. Bruno's later sheets put it on most exposed connectors (VPL connectors in weather, hitch-lift trailer plugs, key switches).
