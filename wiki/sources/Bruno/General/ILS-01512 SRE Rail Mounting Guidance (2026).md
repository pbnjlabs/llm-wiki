# ILS-01512 SRE Rail Mounting Guidance (2026)

ILS-01512 (04/30/26), 7 pages: the current standalone **straight-rail mounting guidance**. Same content as the version bundled in the tech support guides ([[Rail-Mounting-Guidance]]), now naming **SRE-2010/OS, SRE-2010E/EC/EOS, SRE-2010C/CAN, SRE-2010G, SRE-3050/G and SRE-5000/G**.

- Kit hardware (6.5 x 50 mm self-tapping hex screws) is for **5/4 in wood treads**. Alternate fasteners: **50 lb-in torsion per anchor** (wood; max 70 lb-in other materials) and **750 lb pull-out per anchor** (5x safety factor).
- **Hardwood**: 3/16 in pilot holes. **Engineered wood**: check with its maker. **Open treads**: know how the tread is held. **Marble/tile**: depends on the underlay.
- **Steel or steel-pan treads, precast concrete**: through-bolt (¼ in Grade 5 / M6 8.8) with a coated underside bracket at least 3/16 in thick, washers at least the size of the rail mount. **Poured concrete** (3 in+): concrete anchors; check for snow-melt or power lines first.
- Outdoor masonry, pavers, brick, landscape block, **railroad ties**: **not for indoor SRE-2010, SRE-3050 or SRE-5000**; usually concrete pilings beside the stairs; outdoor concrete jobs use the SRE-2010E (or SRE-2010C for steel).
- **New: "Polywood"** (HDPE lumber, polymer or composite decking treads): no standard wood fasteners; custom mounting after expert review. **Glass/brittle**: don't anchor into them.
- Local code (seismic zones etc.) overrides; salt air, wash-down cleaning and vibration may need other hardware; when in doubt, a structural engineer.
