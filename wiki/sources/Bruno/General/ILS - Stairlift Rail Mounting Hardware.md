# ILS - Stairlift Rail Mounting Hardware

Rail-mount sheets.
- **Adjustable stand foot (ILS-01143, 2018):** after setting the height, drill ¼ in through the front extension and pin it.
- **Adjustable spiral stand foot (ILS-01082, 2012):** M12 **50 ft-lb**; **always at least 2 bolts** plate to bracket, three M10 base-to-riser.
- **Tall CRE adjustable foot (ILS-01008, 01/14/25):** CRE-00956XX 14.9–20.9 in and CRE-01036XX 17.9–23.9 in in hole sets A/B/C; M10 **30 ft-lb**, M12 **50 ft-lb**; use all hardware.
- **CRE adjustable clamps (ILS-01213, 2022):** styles K, L, M with hole positions 1–4 (e.g. K2) replace old styles B→K, D/E→L, F→M; hole presets are only a starting point.
- **1 in SRE rail mount spacer SRE-30545 (ILS-01492, 2020):** moves the mounts back from the step nose so all **4 fasteners** bite, and adds a **2° setback toward the wall** to offset lean under load. Short side toward the wall; on wood use the **longer screws in the service kit**; folding rail also uses spacer plate CRE-31349; rail **2 in / 2½ in (carpet) minimum** off the step noses.
- **Fabricated stand feet (ILS-01508, June 2021):** supply-chain substitute for SRE-30501 on the SRE-2010 and SRE-3050; one spacer, 3/16 in wall, same holes, load tested. **Don't loosen the factory spacer hardware.**
- **Rail clamp installation kit SRV-2110-012 (ILS-01484, 2020):** clamp tools over each rail joint top and bottom while fitting the back hardware.
