# Bruno Warranty Summary

One-page Bruno warranty summary by product line (page 1 of
`bruno_warranty.pdf`). Undated; it mentions units made after
**2 Apr 2018**, so it's 2018 or later. Page 2 of the same PDF is a
Harmar page, see [[Harmar Warranty Policy (2018)]].

## Terms

| Product | All parts (mfg defects) | Major parts |
|---|---|---|
| Straight rail **SRE-3000, SRE-2010, SRE-2010C**, made **after 2 Apr 2018** | 2 yr | **Limited lifetime** (rail, gearbox, motor) |
| Same models, made **before 2 Apr 2018** | 2 yr | 5 yr (rail, gearbox, motor) |
| Straight rail **SRE-2010E** | 2 yr | 5 yr (rail, gearbox, motor) |
| Curved rail **CRE-2110, CRE-2110E** | 2 yr | 5 yr (rail, gearbox, motor) |
| All vehicle and outside lifts | 3 yr | — |
| Valet Plus / Turny (HD/Orbit), Valet Limited | 3 yr | — |
| **Vertical platform lift** | **1 yr** | **2 yr** (motor, acme screw, drive nut, brake, pulleys, belt, screw bearings) |

- **Lifetime condition:** the customer must give the lift reasonable
  care and maintenance. Damage from misuse or neglect can void it.
- **Labor:** all products, **30 days from installation**.
- **Demo units sold by a dealer:** vehicle lifts made after March 2003
  get 3 yr from installation. Elevators get 2 yr all parts, 5 yr major
  (motor, gearbox, rail).
- **Refurbished units sold by Bruno:** 1 yr on all parts.
- **Parts warranty:** 90 days from delivery, repair or exchange only.

SRE-3050 isn't listed.
