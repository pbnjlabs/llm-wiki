# Tech Tip - How to Decode a Bruno Serial Number

2-page Bruno article (article 4, modified 3/28/2014; portal tech-tip 170, filed under SRE-3000 but covering all Bruno products). The layout is on [[Bruno-Serial-Numbers]]: **YY MM PP M SSSS**: year, month, product group, model group (from 2012), and the unit's number in that month's production. It also covers component date codes on stairlift seats, footrests and folding rails, and the skin-pack ID.
