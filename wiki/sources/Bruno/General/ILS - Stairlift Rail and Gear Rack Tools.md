# ILS - Stairlift Rail and Gear Rack Tools

- **ILS-01364 (2018), SRE rail cross pin:** keeps the gear rack in the rail if the **bottom end plate comes off** while someone's riding. After tightening the end-plate jack screw, set the drill template after the first rack tooth at the bottom; drill **⅛ in (DRB-13001, kit SRV-3000-023)** through two walls of the extrusion only (it misses the rack); pin flush. About 2 minutes. See also [[SRE-3050]].
- **ILS-01200 (2014), SRE swage tool:** where the gear rack is loose in the rail (after it's torqued between the end caps), clamp the tool over the rack and turn an extra turn to dimple the rail against it. Mark spots with painter's tape.
- **ILS-01447 (2019), setting slope for a simple (CRE) install:** measure slope on **top of the rail** (or the rack flange, not in turns) with a **calibrated digital inclinometer**, **within ±0.5°**, checked **every 2 ft** and each section before the next. Drawing dimensions are only a guide.
