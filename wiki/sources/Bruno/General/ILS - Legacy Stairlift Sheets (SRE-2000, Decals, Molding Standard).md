# ILS - Legacy Stairlift Sheets (SRE-2000, Decals, Molding Standard)

- **ILS-00821 (12/04/2006), SRE-2000 / 2000C Electra-Ride Elite** spec sheet: 400 lb; 2 x 7 Ah; 1.02 hp motor with 20:1 worm gearbox and 4:1 poly-V belt reduction; IR call/send; rail **1½ in above the step nose**; 23–45° (specials 50°); 32 ft/min max (25 loaded); rails 16 and 20 ft; seat swivels at 30° steps to ±60°; 30 A breaker; overspeed optional (commercial package); 8–20 trips per charge; 370 lb unit.
- **ILS-00663 (2006), decal DEC-00321 placement** on straight (SRE-1550, SRE-2000, SRE-2750: under the seat, below the rating decal) and curved (CRE-2100: next to the rating decal on the back of the upper seat cushion) stairlifts.
- **ILS-01096 (2012), cosmetic standard** for SRE-3000 thermo-molded parts (footrest SRE-30036, seat back SRE-30045, lower arms SRE-30041/43, stand foot SRE-30001): A/B/C/D surface grades and allowed defects. Useful only when judging whether cover blemishes are a factory defect.
