# ILS - Stairlift Seat Kits and Adjustments

Seat sheets.
- **Adjustable seat bracket CRE-K-2122XX / CRE-K-2130 (ILS-01047, 2023; ILS-01496):** for tight staircases or an obstruction opposite: the user sits **up to 2 in further back** (¾ in without cutting the seat plate; angle-cut up to 3.25 in / 2.25 in off the corner for more). **Not with a power swivel or power footrest.**
- **2 in offset seat back CRE-K-2140 (ILS-01495, CRE-2110 and SRE-2010):** same goal; **works with a power footrest, not a power swivel**; backrest, armrest and belt bolts **85 in-lb**; two harness routing options.
- **High-degree seat (ILS-01135):** footrest spacer plates; L bracket for 1–2 spacers, Z bracket for 3; socket-head screw with a power swivel.
- **Upholstery:** Elite arms, cushion and back (ILS-01125); Elan **SRE-K-3078** shipped separately, T-20 thread-forming screws, **don't over-tighten** (ILS-01498).
- **Elite swivel stops (ILS-01548):** bolt 1 limits back, bolt 2 forward; **bolt 1 at 45° can let the seat hit the wall**; blue threadlocker.
- **Swivel release handle (ILS-00979, ILS-01553, CRE-2110/SRE-2010):** SRE-20644WG on lever CRE-00815WG with a self-drilling screw (#1 square bit).
- **Elan seat hinge cover and damper (ILS-01554):** damper in, tap the cover with a mallet, pop the tabs until they click.
