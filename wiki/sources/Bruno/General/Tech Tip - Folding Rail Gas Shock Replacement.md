# Tech Tip - Folding Rail Gas Shock Replacement

2-page photo sheet (2022; portal tech-tip 217) for replacing the **gas shock on a Bruno folding rail**. It doesn't name the model.

1. With the rail **completely folded**, remove the shock retaining screw.
2. Slide the shock up so the lower threaded shaft comes up and out.
3. Slide the shock down and out. Reverse to fit the new one.

The hinge carries a **pinch point** warning (DEC-00244).
