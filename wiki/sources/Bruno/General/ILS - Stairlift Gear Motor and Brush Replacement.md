# ILS - Stairlift Gear Motor and Brush Replacement

Four drive sheets.
- **ILS-01365, SRE-3000 motor/gearbox** (2017): carriage off the rail; front cover, IR eyes, key switch, hanger plate; three 6 mm screws (new lock washers); move the ferrite to the new leads; **brown motor lead to the top of the carriage**; with motor bolts just touching, hold the motor toward the board and set the **gearbox adjustment screw to 6 in-lb**; **motor screws 22 ft-lb**, hanger plate screws 15 ft-lb. Noisy going down → adjust the screw ⅛ turn at a time.
- **ILS-01602 (02/03/25), Elite gear motor change, February 2025:** Bruno changed gear-motor supplier for **CRE-2110 and SRE-2010**. Kits: CRE **SRV-2110-030L (MDC-24026) / -030R (MDC-24025)**; SRE **SRV-2010-046L (MDC-24024) / -046R (MDC-24023)**. Also replace the **drive adjustment lever CRE-00909** (and the SRE-2010 **drive lock spacer SRE-20996**). **CRE pinion gear must be heated to 450 °F (232 °C)** to fit. Mount bolts **34 Nm**, flat washers on three holes (not the one nearest the motor). **Leads changed from blue/orange to blue/brown, and the new motor is the same for both hands**, so the connector position now depends on hand: **wrong direction → swap the brown and blue leads on the PCB.**
- **ILS-01610 (10/28/25), SRE-2010 gear motor:** step-by-step for the same swap: seat support plate's six M8 flat-head screws can strip (long handle, tap the bit); spacer SRE-21388 then drive lever CRE-01201; all mount and seat-plate screws **34 Nm**; end plate 22 ft-lb; drive adjustment if it chatters.
- **ILS-01426 (10/23/24), motor brushes (CRE-2110/SRE-2010):** unscrew the brush caps; clear carbon dust with a shop vac on one hole and compressed air in the other (no solvents); fit brushes with the locating tab; run at no load to seat them.
