# ILS - Joey Mounting Kit Fitment by Vehicle

Bruno's **vehicle-specific mounting kit instructions** for the [[VSL-4400]] Joey (inside platform lift) and the older **VSL-4000HW** Joey. 60 sheets, 2005–2026.

- **Kit numbers:** **VSL-K-40xx** = VSL-4000HW kits (2005–2016); **VSL-K-44xx(H)** = VSL-4400 kits (sheets re-issued 10/2022; H = hardware kit). Several 2022 sheets name only the vehicle; the kit number is on the assembly drawing. **VSL-K-4450 (ILS-01482, 2020) is the universal Joey kit**: read the assembly drawing for vehicle notes; not every part gets used.
- **Typical notes:** **Loc-tite / thread-lock on all non-nylock hardware**; the 3rd row usually **folds into the floor or comes out**; plastic sill and scuff plates get **trimmed around the latch bracket**; spare tire and jack may become unreachable. Third-row **power-folding seats must be disabled** ([[VSL-4400]]).
- **2025+ Infiniti QX80 / Nissan Armada (ILS-01670):** the kit is for the **electronic (air) suspension** versions only.
