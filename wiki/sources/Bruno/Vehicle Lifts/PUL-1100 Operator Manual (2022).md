# PUL-1100 Out-Rider Operator Manual (02-04-2022)

Owner's manual for the **PUL-1100 Out-Rider** pickup-bed lift (14
pages). The cover carries a stray "SRE-2010G OPERATOR" footer.

**Capacity by drive:**

| Drive | Capacity |
|---|---|
| **Light-duty actuator** | **100 lb** |
| **Standard** | **200 lb** |
| **Heavy-duty motor** | **350 lb** |

Follow the rating on the unit.

- **Engine off, in park** (parking brake on a manual) to operate.
- **Emergency:** take the **crank** from storage and fit it over the
  motor shaft to bring the device in or down.
- **Dealer inspection once a year.**
- **Troubleshooting:**
  - Not running: is the **breaker ON**?
  - **Grinding:** have the dealer inspect it.
- **Warranty:** 3 years on parts.
