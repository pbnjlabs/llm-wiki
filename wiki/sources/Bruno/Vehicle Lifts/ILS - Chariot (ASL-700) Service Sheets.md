# ILS - Chariot (ASL-700) Service Sheets

Service sheets for the [[ASL-700]] Chariot.

- **Linkage bar (ILS-01244, 5/26/21):** four ASL-70134 bars from the chassis ASL-70127 to the hitch adapter ASL-70139 at the hitch-height setting. **Jam nuts 15 ft-lb, then center lock nuts 30 ft-lb** (hold the jam nut). **Grease all 8 fittings until it seeps** (Magnalube if there's no grease gun). Matches the manual.
- **Actuator-link service kit (ILS-01201, 2014)** and **actuator tether (ILS-01180, 2013):** level ground, park, brake; platform down and unloaded (jockey it to unload the actuator; 2×4 under the rear edge); **key switch OFF** before pulling the mast bolt; rag under the actuator. Actuator wires at the board: **red top, yellow bottom**. The **tether** loops over both link bushings, between the motor and ball screw, ends tied with a heavy 5/16 in cable tie. **Return the old actuator and upper channel to Bruno.**
- **Platform latch SRV-700-002 (ILS-01184, 2014):** train users to **rotate the handle fully to open and lower to latch before driving**; the latch only engages as the platform starts down.
- **Belt keeper (ILS-01243, 2014):** replaces the old belt hook (stop using the hook).
- **Wheel/suspension assembly (ILS-01436, 2018):** lower the platform to lift the wheels slightly, turn the wheel 90°, fender off, loosen the tail light for the four 9/16 bolts. **New assembly 26 ft-lb.**
- **¼ in wheel hub spacer (ILS-01474, 10-31-19):** start the nuts by hand, torque in sequence to **62–65 ft-lb**. ⚠️ The ASL-700 manual says wheel nuts **75–88 ft-lb**, re-checked at 10, 25 and 50 miles. The newer manual wins; ask Bruno whether the spacer setup is meant to differ.
- **Lights stay on with the vehicle off** (aftermarket taillight converter): resistor kit **ASL-70161** (ILS-01480, 2019): brown J5, yellow J4, green J3, white J10 BRK LAMP through the piggybacks; ESD strap. An alternative to the manual's three 2200 Ω resistors.
- **Wireless backup camera ASL-K-712** (ILS-01600, 02/05/25): camera on the license plate bracket, harness through the chassis to **J15 CAMERA**; aim it **under the platform, looking outward**; parking lines start at the platform edge, width at the inner face of the fenders.
