# ASL-250 / ASL-250HTP Out-Sider Operator Manual (01-16-2017)

Owner's manual for the older **ASL-250 and ASL-250HTP** Out-Sider (24
pages). Keep it in the glove box.

- **350 lb (both versions).** Never lift people.
- Drive with care. **Touchless car washes only.**
- **Emergency crank:**
  1. Key OFF.
  2. Crank on the motor shaft, **holding the control arm UP**.
  3. **Clockwise raises, counter-clockwise lowers.** A ⅜ in drill can
     drive it.
- **Dealer service at least every 12 months.**
- **Warranty:** 3 years on parts.
