# Parts Drawings - Vehicle Lifts

Bruno's assembly drawings (exploded views with parts tables), pulled 2026-10-22 from the Brunopartner parts lookup (Service → Parts Lookup by Serial #). That lookup only takes an exact serial number, so the set was rebuilt from each model's top-level drawing, following assemblies three levels down ([[Bruno-Parts-Lookup]]). **These are Bruno's current revisions**: for an older unit, run its serial on the portal for the parts list as built, or check the manual's exploded view. Dealer prices on the portal are manufacturer suggestions and are not recorded here.

Starts from each lift's model drawing (ASL-275, ASL-250, ASL-700, PUL-1100, PUL-1850, VSL-4400, VSL-6000, VSL-6900, VSL-570, AWL-150, AWL-1600).

- **Out-Sider:** ASL-275 main assembly ASL-16600 (ASL-275L ASL-16787), actuator **ADC-12026** (12 V 18 in, Moteck IP66, shared with ASL-250), power harness ASL-16710, swing-away ASL-16603, hitch adapters ASL-16604 etc., brake light ASL-16434, 7-pin kit **INS-K-2002**, lightweight chair kit ASL-K-285.
- **Chariot ASL-700:** actuator **ADC-12018** (12 V 12 in), **PCB ASL-70012**, control arm with key switch ASL-70013, gas spring SPR-00089, swivel bearing BRG-10010, harnesses ASL-70072–70076.
- **Out-Rider PUL-1100:** actuator **ADC-12027** (LA36 300 mm heavy duty), motor/drive assemblies PUL-11068 (standard, gearmotor MDC-12012) and PUL-11069 (heavy duty, **MDC-12019**), lifting belt **BLT-00014**, pendant PUL-11084.
- **Joey VSL-4400:** up/down motor **MDC-12025** (Parvalux), controller **PCB-00155**, control panel VSL-40525, 44 in lift belt **BLT-00034**, carriage wheels SRE-20119, up/out limit switch VSL-40526, pendant VSL-40550.
- **Curb-Sider VSL-6000/6900:** lift head VSL-60416 with motor **MDC-12015** and lifting belt **BLT-00015**. **Big-Lifter VSL-570:** head VSL-60418 with heavy-duty right-angle motor **MDC-12019** (also BLT-00015), pendant VSL-57037, motor harness VSL-57034.
- **AWL-150 Lifter:** 20 A breaker CBK-20001, up/down switch AWL-00142, spring-assist springs SPR-00005/00007. **AWL-1600 Back-Saver:** motor drive AWL-01703 (motor MDC-12015), power harness AWL-01679, platform AWL-01674; it reuses the Out-Sider swing-away ASL-16603 and power harness ASL-16710.
- **Stow-Away PUL-1850:** pendant TAS-20210, door safety switch kit PCL-00319, wiring kit TAS-K-18552.
