# ILS - Out-Rider (PUL-1100) and Stow-Away Service Sheets

- **ILS-00458** (2003), **PUL-1100 limit switch retrofit SRV-1100-010 (standard cab) / SRV-1100-011 (extended cab):** same magnet-and-sealed-switch design as the Curb-Sider kit. The magnet protrudes about ¼ in; **gap 1/16–1/8 in**. An extension harness runs from the cab to the bed with the motor harness. Passenger-side lift: green and white = IN stop; driver-side: OUT.
- **ILS-00703** (2006), **third control station** and **ILS-00774** (2006), **PCL-K-1905 door panel control kit (PCL-1900 + PUL-1100 combo):** pendants mounted in the cab door skin (template, 1/8 in holes, a Dremel for the cut-outs); wires under the sill and through the door boot. F-150 shown.
- **ILS-01406** (3/8/17), **PUL-1850 Stow-Away drawer slides BRG-75012:** ½ in screws in the round holes, 5/8 in with a washer in the carriage-mount holes, keeper nuts flattened; **don't pinch the slide**; blue Loctite 248 on the 3rd limit-switch-mount bolt from the front (left side). The Stow-Away mounting kits are on [[ILS - Pickup Truck Bases and Stow-Away Mounting Kits]].
