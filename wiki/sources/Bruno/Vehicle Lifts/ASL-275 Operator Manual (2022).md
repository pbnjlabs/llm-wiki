# ASL-275 Out-Sider Operator Manual (02-03-2022)

Owner's manual for the **ASL-275 Out-Sider** hitch platform lift, in
English and French (22 pages). **Not for RVs**: motorhomes, buses,
fifth wheels or travel trailers.

- **350 lb maximum** when installed to Bruno's instructions. **Never lift
  people.**
- **Driving:** slow down until used to the handling, and on bumps,
  railroad crossings and gutters. **Touchless car washes only.**
- **Swing-Away option:** lift the latch handle, swing the lift out, then
  open the hatch. **Never swing it with a mobility device on board.**
  Close the hatch before swinging it back and locking the lever.
- **Emergency crank** (needs an able-bodied person):
  1. Lift off the tail-shaft cap and turn the key OFF.
  2. Fit the crank on the motor shaft: **clockwise raises,
     counter-clockwise lowers**.
  3. A **⅜ in drill** can turn it.
- **Dealer service at least every 12 months**, more often with heavy use
  or harsh conditions.
- **Warranty:** vehicle lift Gold, **3 years on parts**.
