# VSL-570 Big-Lifter Operator Manual (02-03-2022)

Owner's manual for the **VSL-570 Big-Lifter** in-vehicle hoist (7 pages):
- 400 lb maximum (per the base decal).
- Docking device to pick-up; **stop before the white stitching reaches
  the belt guide**.
- Swing it in **front end first**.
- Use the pendant, and keep its cord out of the door.
- **Yearly dealer inspection.**
- 3-year warranty.
