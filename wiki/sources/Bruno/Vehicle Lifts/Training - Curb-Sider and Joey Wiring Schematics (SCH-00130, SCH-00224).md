# Training - Curb-Sider and Joey Wiring Schematics (SCH-00130, SCH-00224)

Two wiring drawings from Bruno's dealer **training** library (Installation & Troubleshooting docs). Both are **older revisions** than the drawings in the current install manuals, which win.

- **Curbsider Schematic (VSL-6000).pdf:** **SCH-00130** rev 1 (9/28/06), *Wiring Diagram (VSL-6000/6000E)*. Control board with the **J15 PASS jumper** inset (passenger side vs driver side setting), drive-side out / in cam limit switches, N.C. door safety switch (jumper it together if not used), **30 A fuse** on B+ and a **20 A breaker**, up/down motor (250 lb; separate 400 lb motor shown) and in/out motor, and the exterior pendant (up/down, in/out). The 2023 [[VSL-6000-6900 Installation Manual (2023)]] has the current **SCH-00139** (VSL-6000) and **SCH-00217** (VSL-6900) on pp. 21–22; use those for a current unit and this one for a 2006-era VSL-6000/6000E.
- **Joey Schematic (VSL-4400).pdf:** **SCH-00224** rev 1 (7/19/17), *Wiring Schematic - VSL-4400 Joey*. 2-axis lift control PCB (**PCB-00146** domestic, **PCB-00155** export/CE), out, up and in limit switches, home switch, travelling cable to the pendant (in/up), door safety switch, **30 A fuse** on B+ at the vehicle battery, up/down and in/out motors. The [[VSL-4400 Installation Manual (2026)]] p28 has a later revision of the same SCH-00224; use the manual's.
