# VSL-570 / VSL-570E Big-Lifter Installation Manual (03-13-2023)

Install manual for the **VSL-570 Big-Lifter**, an **in-vehicle hoist**
for wheelchairs, scooters and power chairs **up to 400 lb** (22 pages).
It fits most full-size vans, SUVs and minivans; unusual layouts need a
custom base. Arms: **standard or compact offset, LH or RH**.
- The **maximum load decal on the base** is the rating for that vehicle.
  The vehicle structure, environment and user can lower it.
- **Grade 5 hardware (grade 8 base-to-base-weldment).** PTFE grease
  (**Magnalube**) is supplied.

**Power:**
- Run it from the battery **through the interior, never underneath**,
  with the supplied **30 A** fused twin lead.
- **Disable a third-row power-folding seat.**
- Hybrids and EVs: connect to the 12 V battery.

**Base:**
- **Adjustable** (movable legs; it can move to another vehicle, and legs
  can be trimmed and repainted) or **custom** (fixed legs, separate
  instructions).
- Cut bed liners or mats so the feet sit flat; spacers if needed.
- 3/8 in holes, after **checking underneath**.
- 5/16 in hardware with **4-hole support washers**. **A fender washer in
  place of the support washer lowers the rating.**
- Seal underneath. **The base must be level and the mast vertical.**

**Lift:**
- Positions: **1 = right rear (US standard)**, 2 = left rear, 3 = van
  side door (behind the opening).
- **Motor harness to control harness: black to black, red to red.** Tuck
  it in the junction box and route it over the base notch.
- **6 base bolts, criss-cross, 12 ft-lb (150 in-lb) maximum**, starting
  with the lower right.
- Remove the shipping pin. Raise the arm and capture the link with the
  ½ in bolt.
- **Magnetic pendant holder** (kit VSL-K-4441H): don't let the door
  pinch the cord.

**Adjustments:**
- **Vertical tube:** set the height with the ½ in cross bolt (**don't
  over-tighten**, it distorts the tube) and the 3 set screws, then the
  rubber boot.
- **Arm angle:** turnbuckle and jam nut, as high as possible without
  touching the vehicle.
- **Outer arm:** set the telescoping tube about half-way and test with
  the chair. **Stop before the white sight-line stitching enters the
  belt guide.**
- **Optional stop:** mark the swivel tube, line it up with a base hole,
  drill ⅜ in, and clear the chips with a magnet.

**Docking device:**
- One is included. U-bolt it **as low as possible on the seat post** and
  set it at the device's balance point.
- Adjustable C-arms (inline or offset) span **10–14 in**. At 10 in the
  outer tube needs trimming.

**Maintenance:**
- "Maintenance-free", but inspect it **yearly** (**every 6 months** with
  several uses a day or harsh use).
- Check the belt, the swivel tube (Magnalube), the electrics and the
  docking device.
- **Chain tension:** with no load, remove the motor cover, loosen the 3
  motor-plate nuts, rotate the motor **counter-clockwise** to take up
  slack, then tighten the **pivot (lower) nut first**.

**Troubleshooting:** breaker or fuse, connections, the ground, the
vehicle battery.
