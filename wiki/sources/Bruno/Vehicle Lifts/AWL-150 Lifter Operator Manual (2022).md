# AWL-150 Lifter Operator Manual (02-04-2022)

Owner's manual for the **AWL-150 Lifter** (8 pages).

- **Belt:** **never use the lift if the belt is frayed more than ⅛ in**.
  Check it regularly; the dealer replaces a belt with worn sides.
- **Loading a wheelchair:**
  1. Chair **2 in behind the bumper**. Fold it; the docking device
     passes through the left wheel's spokes, so **don't roll it
     folded**.
  2. Arm up, latched, swung out to 90°. Breaker ON.
  3. Lower to the T-bar and take up the slack.
  4. Lift, steadying the chair; it rotates flat.
  5. **Stop before the white stitching reaches the belt guide.**
  6. Release the swivel lock and swing it in while tapping DOWN.
  7. Lay the arm down beside the chair and strap it with a bungee.
- **Scooters:** a similar sequence (the rear wheel even with the lift
  base).
- **Nothing on the switch box**: a held switch burns the motor and drains
  the battery. Make sure the trunk lid clears the lift.
- **Warranty:** vehicle lift Gold, **3 years**.
