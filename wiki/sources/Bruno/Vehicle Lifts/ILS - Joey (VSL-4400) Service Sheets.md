# ILS - Joey (VSL-4400) Service Sheets

Service sheets for the [[VSL-4400]] Joey and the older **VSL-4000HW** Joey.

**VSL-4400:**
- **ILS-01396** (01/27/25), **lift belt (BLT-00034, 44 in)**:
  - Platform out and on the ground; unbolt the belt end from the back of the platform.
  - **Disconnect the Joey from the battery** (check the vehicle manual first); touch a grounded point before handling the board (ESD).
  - Both mast covers off; unplug the motor leads; lift motor out (four 7/16 screws).
  - The belt guide is tight and **parallel to the motor plate**; the big washer doesn't turn.
  - **Wrap the belt counter-clockwise, catch edge and black thread outside.** Wrapped wrong, the catch edge chews the belt guide; replace the rub block **VSL-40517** (two 3/16 rivets).
- **ILS-01430** (08-20-18), **lift motor MDC-12025**: same teardown; **new hardware** (shoulder screw, lock nut, four screws); check the belt per ILS-01396.
- **ILS-01485** (01-22-20), **in/out motor SRV-4400-008 (MDC-12024 replaces MDC-12022**, better performance):
  - Platform on the ground outside; **pull the lift fuse at the battery**.
  - Note the spade-bolt nut position (**2–3 threads outside the bracket**) to keep chain tension. The chain mustn't drag on its bracket.
- **ILS-01411** (07-05-18), **carriage wheels SRE-20119** (up/down and in/out assemblies, two-person job):
  - Run the lift motor on a 12 V battery to unspool belt.
  - **New retaining rings every time, sharp edge away from the washer.**
  - **Test: lifting current under 25 A, pulling in under 12 A**, two full cycles loaded.
- **ILS-01366** (08/28/22), **cable carrier VSL-40504**: if the last link isn't broken, keep the rivets and clip in the new harness.
- **ILS-01370** (06-10-21), **platform alignment kit**: cap in the square tube, bracket and thinner jam nut, new bolt.
- **ILS-01468** (10-01-19), **barrier offset kit SPOVSL-00007**: offset plates between the barrier and its mounts, using the barrier kit's hardware.

**VSL-4000HW (older Joey):**
- **ILS-00996** (02-07-2011), **manual override**:
  - **Electrical:** check the fuse; shroud off; unplug the red 8-pin pendant connector; buttons A/B/C on the board run the motors **with all limits bypassed**, so tap near the end. **Jumper J8 "XTRA" (D) sets UP/DOWN: OFF = up, ON = down.** "Invalid operation" LED = return it home (all the way up and in) or cycle power.
  - **Mechanical** (if the actuator failed while extending, **unload the mobility device first**): tools under the carriage shroud; unbolt A and B, **zip-tie the mast halves together**, unbolt C to drop the platform off; crank on the motor shaft to bring the carriage in.
- **ILS-00635** (11-1-05): backup tool (SRV-4000-001) on the actuator shaft to raise the tower; **leave a 1 in gap between the links** to get the tool off.
- **ILS-01005** (4-27-11), **carriage lock pin SRV-4000-016**: drill 3/8 in through both rail flanges at the rail end bracket VSL-40320.
- **ILS-01017** (07-15-11), **carriage update**: new safety plate (**spring toward the motor plate; snug the screws without compressing the spring**) and a mast limit switch bumper.
- **ILS-01206** (5-6-14), **drive motor**: one motor hole is the **pivot hole**. Rotate the motor **away from the gear mesh** before tightening; keep the woodruff key.
- **ILS-00748** (08-20-07), **retractable belts VSL-40063**: one belt to the mast, knobs outward.
