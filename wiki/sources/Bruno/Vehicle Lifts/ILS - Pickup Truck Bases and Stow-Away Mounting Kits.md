# ILS - Pickup Truck Bases and Stow-Away Mounting Kits

Bases for **pickup trucks**: Curb-Sider bases for **aluminum truck beds** (**VSL-36894/D, 2020**, and **VSL-36927/D for the 2015+ Ford F-150**, both using kit **VSL-K-6009D/P**), Tundra and Tacoma sub-bases, the **PUL-1100 Out-Rider aluminum bed base PUL-11081D/P (kit PUL-K-1108)** and the **GM pickup 2019+ Out-Rider sub-base PUL-11083D/P (PUL-K-1106)**.

- **Bed liners and rubber mats:** trace around each mounting foot and **cut the liner out so the foot sits flat on the bed**; spacers may be needed to clear a liner.
- **Stow-Away mounting kits** (**TAS-K-18559 / 18560**, Ram 1500 Crew/Quad Cab 2019+, front left / front right; **TAS-K-18561**, GM Crew/Ext Cab 2019+): mount Bruno's **PUL-1850 Stow-Away** under the cab on the rocker studs after the **running boards come off**. The area must be structurally sound. The Stow-Away's drawer slides are on [[ILS - Out-Rider (PUL-1100) and Stow-Away Service Sheets]].
