# PUL-1850 Stow-Away Operator Manual (01-01-2013)

Owner's manual for the **PUL-1850 Stow-Away** under-vehicle transfer
seat (28 pages, most of them emergency backup steps).

**Specs:**
- **300 lb user.**
- In and out by a **12 VDC motor and gear rack**; lifting by a **12 VDC
  linear actuator**.
- Vehicle battery power, **30 A in-line fuse**.
- **Manual backup tools** are included (keep them in the glove box).

**Safety:** level ground, **engine off and in park**, use the handle
while riding, mind the scissor pinch points, never in traffic.

**Getting in:**
1. Door open. Hold UP to raise the seat to the **lower transfer
   position**.
2. Transfer onto it and insert the handle.
3. Hold UP again to the **upper transfer position**, then transfer to
   the vehicle seat.
4. Hold DN to stow it.

**Getting out:** the reverse, using DN to the lower transfer height, then
DN again to stow under the vehicle.

**Seat stuck at the upper transfer height (manual backup):**
1. **Pull the fuse.**
2. Remove 3 screws under the box, slide the carriage out, and pull the
   **gear rack** away from the motor.
3. Put the **safety pin** in a trolley-track hole.
4. Remove the upper actuator bolt and lower the seat pan onto the pin.
5. **Screw the actuator in until about 1 in of chrome shows.**
6. Continue per the manual's photos, then have the dealer repair it.
