# ILS - Out-Sider (ASL-250-275) Install and Service Sheets

Sheets for the hitch-mounted **Out-Sider** lifts, [[ASL-275]] and the older [[ASL-250]].

**Install (ASL-250 quick guides ILS-00848 Rev 6 2014, ILS-00849 2013, ILS-00850 2009; ASL-275 hitch adapter ILS-01466 2019):**
- Check the vehicle's hitch rating, tongue load, GVWR and towing capacity first. **Never run the power wiring under the vehicle.**
- **The lift mount tube must end up 10–12 in off the ground.** Measure the receiver (ground to the inside lip, **round up**) and pick the hitch adapter: **standard ASL-16442 (Class II) / ASL-16446 (Class III)**, **3 in ASL-16488 / ASL-16491**, **6 in ASL-16517 / ASL-16519**, up or down, plus the mast hole set (A–E). There are separate tables with and without Swing-Away. **Magnalube on all sides** of the adapter; tighten the Class III pinch bolt; keep the mount tube **vertical (90°)** with the angle hardware.
- **ASL-275, hitch 6–10 in (most minivans): 6 in adapter in the "up" position, top or 2nd mast hole only** (ILS-01466).
- Swing-Away: platform side **away from the mast touches the ground first** (angle bolt); latch pin binding → adjust the swing-arm screw (clockwise raises). Leave harness slack between the tubes.
- **Platforms (ILS-00849):** 3-piece adjustable with Hold-Tite or retractable belts (dog-bone bracket under the seat front); **HTP** (powerchair) with pivot arm. Run up until the **actuator ratchets**; the pivot arm must **firmly compress the seat** (hard to slide fingers under).
- **ASL-250 → ASL-250HTP conversion (ILS-00773, 2008):** grind off the outer slide tube's centre tab, Teflon grease on the nylon washer. Chock holes by middle-wheel size: default/10 in **7**, 12 in **8**, 14 in **9**. MWD chairs need 3 chocks, FWD/RWD 2. A yellow stop strip goes on the platform.
- Older Meridian (2005): E-Z hitch blue dot up if the hitch is level, down if it droops (5° more tilt); hitch ≤14 in vs 14–18 in adapters; **stainless hardware needs anti-seize** (Crisco in a pinch, never oil or grease). Centre the cams before tightening (ILS-00565). DSP-A-250 is a dealer display stand.

**ASL-275 platform setup (ILS-01455/01456/01457, 2019; ILS-01456 rev 08/05/26):**
- Run the platform **4 in off the ground**; device from the driver's side; scooters **as far forward as possible**.
- Scooter wheels 7.5–10.5 in: **5 holes between chocks**; the front wheel of a **3-wheel scooter gets 2–3 holes** so it can't drop through when turned.
- **MWD power chair:** mast centred on the drive wheel; **9 holes for 12–14 in wheels**; the drive wheel sits about **½ in below the platform**, on the chocks, **never on the ramps**. FWD/RWD: add chock assemblies (call Bruno).
- **ASL-275L with a rigid manual wheelchair:** remove the centre deck; **4 belts** (second belt kit sold separately); the dealer chooses chocks and belt points (through the spokes if needed); **27.5 in max** across the bottom of the wheels.
- **Lightweight folding power chair (ASL-K-285, ILS-01619, 03/17/26):** ASL-275L main assembly (ASL-16787/16788) for chairs **29–75 lb**, or cam kit SRV-275-012; platform ASL-16790 comes with **45° chocks, only for lightweight chairs** (a standard device bends them; swap to ASL-16651). Criss-cross the two belts. Conversion kit SRV-275-011.
- **Swing-Away pivot stop** (large platform, ILS-01470): a rubber stop cuts the swing about 10°.

**Fold tower and cam (ILS-01336 ASL-250/275, ILS-01626 ASL-275L, both 03/10/26):** shim **ASL-16587** between the fold tower bracket and arms. The cam must pivot by hand (remove a thin nylon washer if not). **Spring nut: standard = 1–2 threads showing; ASL-275L = hand-tight to the nyloc, then 10¼ turns** (then back off ½ turn at a time if a loaded platform folds). Too tight → bent fold tower and an unloaded platform that drops suddenly; too loose (275L) → it won't fold.

**Lights and trailer wiring:**
- **7-pin adapters:** ASL-16436 (ASL-250, ILS-01113, 2013); **ASL-16716 (with third brake light) / ASL-16730 (without)** (ASL-275, ILS-01450, 2020). Dielectric grease. With the ignition **off**, the 2-pin twin lead should read **battery voltage**:
  - If not, fit the **30 A trailer power fuse**.
  - If it reads only with the ignition on, **bypass the trailer tow relay** (common on Ford) by wiring to the fuse.
  - On GM, connect the **loose red/black wire** at the fuse block.
- **4-wire adapter ASL-16232** (ASL-250, 2005): brown→orange, white→black, green→blue. Brake light flashing with the turn signal = shared wires (a splitter module fixes it).
- **Third brake light** (ILS-01114, 01/09/25): ASL-250 raise the license plate tube; red to BRK LAMP J9, black J10 (ASL-275: red to the bottom pin J9, black top J10). Test with the brake pedal.
- **Lift LEDs stay on with vehicle lights off** (aftermarket taillight converter): **ASL-16727 resistor kit** (ASL-275, flat-4 cable, ILS-01479, 2019).
- **Hyper-flashing, "trailer connected/disconnected" or bulb-out messages, blind-spot or park-aid alerts** (OEM tow recognition module): **ASL-K-284** (ASL-250/275 with third brake light; others need harness kit SRV-275-010 first) (ILS-01486, 2020). The resistor bracket gets hot. Same idea for the **AWL-1600** lift: **AWL-K-1601** (ILS-01491).
- **Harness replacement SRV-275-010** (ILS-01493, 2020): ASL-16711 mast harness (power + 4-flat trailer, needed for a third brake light) and ASL-16715 Swing-Away harness. Board J1 B+, J2 B-, J3 R turn, J4 L turn, J5 license. **ESD strap (SRV-000-001, ILS-00679).**
- **ASL-250 PCB/license plate retrofit** (ILS-01266, 2015) for units **before S/N 13020100594**: keep the actuator collapsed (or the platform on the ground) while the upper actuator bolt is out, so the inner slide tube can't drop.
- **ASL-250 key switch SRV-250-014** (ILS-01212, 2014): adhesive heat-shrink over the switch against corrosion; dielectric grease.

**Accessories:** **wireless backup camera ASL-K-273** (ILS-01599, 02/05/25) on the license plate bracket (or the third-brake-light bracket), plug **J15 CAMERA**; monitor on the dash or windshield, 12 V socket or the aux harness to the battery. **On with the engine running, off with it off.** Parking lines optional; measure and record the distances on the operator's manual cover and train the user. **Storable exterior cover ASL-16347** (ILS-01050, 2013; also fits HTP and Chariot with brackets). **Add-on rear belt kit** (ILS-01499, 2021).
