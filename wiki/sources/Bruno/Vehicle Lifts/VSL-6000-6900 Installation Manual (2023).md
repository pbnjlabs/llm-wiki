# VSL-6000-6900 Installation Manual (2023)

33-page install manual (P/N 6000/6900-I, 05-08-2023) for the **Curb-Sider VSL-6000 and VSL-6900** boom lifts. Contents: load ratings (6000: 250/400/450 lb; 6900: up to 400 lb), tools and Grade 5/8 hardware, power routing (inside for minivans, under for pickups), custom vs adjustable base, bed liners and aluminum beds, lift-to-base assembly (12 ft-lb max), vertical tube and arm angle, limit switch cams by side, outer arm reach (half device width + 1 in), pendant holder and pickup wheel chock plate, fold-down pin kit, maintenance (belt stitching, chain tension, 6900 actuator/gas spring), emergency crank, customer training, troubleshooting (J11 jumper, encoder), wiring diagrams, exploded views, 3-year warranty. The control board adjustment pages (13–14) are diagrams only.

Raw file is loose in `MANUALS/Bruno/` (not in the Vehicle Lifts subfolder), matching the human's folder.
