# ILS - Curb-Sider Sub-Base Fitment by Vehicle

Bruno's **vehicle-specific base (sub-base) instructions** for the boom lifts: the [[VSL-6000-6900]] Curb-Sider, the older VSL-570/600/670/690 and AWL-150 lines. 144 sheets, 1998–2026. Use this page to find the **base part number and its sheet for a customer's vehicle**, then open the PDF for the drill points and the assembly drawing.

- **Part numbers:** **VSL-36xxx** = the sub-base assembly (a **VSL-26xxx** base weldment plus the **VSL-K-550** sub-base parts kit); a **D** suffix is the driver-side version. Sheets before about 2008 list one base per lift line: **VSL-16xxx / VSL-36xxx** (Curb-Sider), VSL-15xxx and **SBA-00xxx** (other and AWL-150 trunk-lift bases).
- **Typical notes:** pull the rear floor trays (trim them around the base or build a false floor); the lift-side **3rd-row seat stays folded or comes out**; the **jack and spare-tire tools often move**; some bases bolt through the **hitch**; drill through only where shown and check underneath for fuel lines, exhaust and wiring first. **Thick carpet** must be cut out under the feet, or the base sways and creeps under load (Transit Connect).
- **Capacity decal (ILS-00891 Rev 2, 12/20/2025):** if the **lift's capacity is less than the base's rating, replace the base decal** with the kit's decal and punch the lift's capacity. Lift capacity depends on the vehicle (rust, structure, rating), the lift and the base together.
- **Adjustable base (ILS-00704, 2006):** 12 configurations for vehicles without a dedicated base; pick by vehicle and lift side.
- No sheet here for the vehicle? Bruno makes new bases every model year: check the Drawings page or Bruno's vehicle application guide.
