# ILS - Curb-Sider (VSL-6000-6900) Service Sheets

Service and retrofit sheets for the [[VSL-6000-6900]] Curb-Sider boom lift and its predecessors (VSL-570/600/670/690).

- **ILS-01647** (04/08/26), **head assembly and motor cover after shipment**: the head can go on the offset arm before or after the arm is in the drive base. Cut the zip tie holding the swivel tube in (don't remove the tube); head bolt from kit **VSL-K-6011** at **6.75 ft-lb** (9/16); vertical link to the clevis plate snug (two 3/4 wrenches). VSL-6000: join the red and black wires between the motor and head. **VSL-6900: also connect the encoder wires** to the encoder on the side of the head. Tuck the motor wires up behind the motor, then the cover (3 bolts into the U-nuts, hand-tight).
- **ILS-00364** (6/2/22), **lift belt replacement kit** (double-loop belt **BLT-00015**, push-nuts, two-piece belt guide), VSL-6000, VSL-6900 and VSL-570:
  - Docking device off the belt loop (push-nut). **VSL-6900: drop the head and pull the encoder** (two screws) first.
  - Motor shroud off; three motor-bracket bolts; pin out of the spool; fold the arm down; chain off, motor and pulley out; winding drum out; unwind to the belt hold-down.
  - New belt through the arm from the motor end, **black stitching up**, behind the far pulley and through the guide channel.
  - **Grease both sides of the drum and the chain with Teflon grease (Magnalube).**
  - **Chain slack 1/8–1/4 in**, then the motor bracket bolts **70 in-lb**. (A chain that's too tight makes it slow or stall.)
  - VSL-6900: refit the encoder (turn the coupler to line up). New push-nut on the docking device.
- **ILS-00747** (8-15-07), **right-to-left side conversion**: swap the offset arm (pull the head harness out through the arm), then on the board **move the jumper off the two "PASS" pins** to the other two (VSL-6000 board shown). Reset the limit switch cam: rotate the lift fully in to the stop the vehicle needs, loosen the cam set screw, turn the cam until the switch clicks (bend the lever gently if it won't), tighten, test. Top cam = IN stop (black/red).
- **ILS-00732** (04/27/07), **VSL-6900 controller retrofit**: new controller **VSL-60484 (PCB-00093)** replaces **VSL-60452 (PCB-00083)** on VSL-6900s **S/N 07050100342 or earlier**. The new board compensates for belt drop while the head extends; **set jumper J11 OFF** on an older lift.
- **ILS-00917** (11-30-09), **VSL-6900 extend-retract resistor VSL-60492**, fitted in the actuator (EXT/RET, orange and blue) leads at the board; bend it clear of the shroud.
- **ILS-00457** (2003), **limit switch retrofit SRV-670-005 (VSL-600/670) / SRV-690-002 (VSL-690)**: sealed non-contact switches tripped by a magnet in the drive tube's mechanical stop (magnet protrudes about **¼ in**). **Gap 1/16–1/8 in** from switch to magnet. Passenger-side lift: green and white = IN stop; driver-side: OUT stop.
- **ILS-00398**: tensioner kit drawing (SRV-60009 bearing, SRV-60010 weldment) for **VSL-600/670/690 and PUL-1100 bases**; mostly unreadable.
- **ILS-00517** (09-01-04), **keyfob remote**: shielded cables cured RF dropouts from the motor and controller. Range is about **8 ft by design**; hold the fob **4–6 in away from the vehicle** and keep the button pressed. Guide the device by hand.
- **ILS-01467** (9-27-19), **magnetic pendant mount kit VSL-K-4441H** (PUL-1100, VSL-570, VSL-6000, VSL-6900): disc by adhesive pad or self-drilling screw, cable clamp near the connector. **Keep the cord clear of the hatch** (or the cab door on a PUL-1100).
- **ILS-00891 Rev 2** (12/20/2025), **base capacity decal**: if the lift's capacity is less than the base rating, replace the base decal and punch the lift's capacity. The chart shows AWL-150 and VSL-570 (not current), VSL-6000, VSL-6900 and PUL-1100, from 100 to 450 lb, with some ratings needing optional equipment. Capacity = vehicle condition and rating + lift + base and head ratings.
- **ILS-00380** (6-17-05), **docking device for the Invacare Arrow Storm FWD (WLD-00967)**: the chair is over 300 lb with its tail weight. **Remove the tail weight unless the lift is rated 400 lb**; a claw on the crossmember lets the lift pick up the 42 lb weight. See [[Docking-Devices]].
