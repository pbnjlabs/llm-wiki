# AWL-150 Lifter Installation Manual (03-13-2023)

Install manual for the **AWL-150 "Lifter"**, Bruno's small **in-vehicle
hoist for manual wheelchairs** (25 pages).
- It lifts a chair **up to 200 lb** and stores it **upright** (tall column
  sub-base).
- **12 VDC** motor on a 9–14 VDC input, direct switch.
- Grade 5 replacement hardware.

**Power:**
- Check the vehicle owner's manual before disconnecting the battery;
  keep-alive power may be needed.
- Run the twin lead through the **firewall** (existing grommet, or a
  ½ in hole with a grommet and silicone), **inside the cab** and under
  the sill trim.
- **In-line fuse at the battery +, never at the lift.**
- A separate **10 AWG ground** from battery − to bare chassis (ring
  terminal, toothed washer, paint filed off).
- Hybrids and EVs: connect to the **12 V** battery.

**Post-style trunk base:**
- Mount it as close to the rear as possible while still allowing a 90°
  swing. The arm sits at 90° to the bumper for pickup.
- **Check underneath before drilling.**
- Make sure the trunk lid clears the post (bolt backed out 2 turns).
- The base plate is 15 in tall (others on request). Use ⅛ in pilot holes
  and self-tappers, with ¼ in washers as shims.
- Ground wire to a base corner screw with a toothed washer.
- **Two threaded stabilizer rods** from the post top to the front and
  side **drip rails**:
  - **70–80° between them**.
  - 5/16 in holes, with plastic tubing over the threads.
  - Bend the end flanges square to the rod, or the drip rails get
    damaged.
- **The post must be vertical**: the lift must not swing on its own
  when parked level. Seal the drip-rail bolts and floor screws with
  silicone.

**Custom or adjustable sub-base:**
- Left or right rear.
- 5/16-18 × 2 in grade 5 bolts with **4-hole support washers**, or a
  fender washer through a frame member.
- Shim with flat washers. Seal underneath.
- **The lift grounds through its mounting bolts.**

**Adjustment:** height, arm angle (adjuster screw and bolt), arm length,
the T-bar on the chair, and the swivel stops.

**Troubleshooting:**

| Symptom | Causes |
|---|---|
| Dead | Wiring, vehicle battery, corroded cables, or the motor |
| **Switch reversed** | The **belt has wound backwards on the spool** (over-extended); reverse it |
| Weak | Battery, or a twisted belt |
| **Stalls** | Twisted belt, a jammed worm gearbox, or a loose motor-shaft ring clip |
| One direction only | The switch |
