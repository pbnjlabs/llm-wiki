# AWL-1600 Back-Saver Installation Manual (05-29-2020)

Install manual for the **AWL-1600 Back-Saver**, a light **hitch-mounted
lift for a folded manual wheelchair** (20 pages). Have the customer
bring the chair to the install, since the Hold-Tite is set with it on
the lift.

**Hitch first:**
- A **Class I or II (1¼ in) or Class III (2 in) receiver**, fitted by a
  qualified hitch installer after an underbody inspection. **Rusty
  vehicles are unsuitable.**
- The hitch installer can also run the power cable (it ships bagged
  separately).
- **Receiver height 7–25 in** above the ground.
- **Vehicle effects:**
  - Heavier tail and handling changes; tire load ratings matter.
  - **Rear sag may need helper springs or air shocks.**
  - **Re-aim the headlights** with the vehicle loaded.
  - Mind the extra length when reversing.
  - **Touchless car washes only, with the chair off.**
  - The lift and chair must not block the lights.

**Hitch adapter by receiver height (round up):**

| Receiver height | Adapter | Class II (1¼ in) | Class III (2 in) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **6–11 in** | 3 in drop-raise, **up** | ASL-16608 | ASL-16605 |
| **12–15 in** | Standard, **up** | ASL-16607 | ASL-16604 |
| **16–18 in** | Standard, **down** | ASL-16607 | ASL-16604 |
| **19–24 in** | 3 in drop-raise, **down** | ASL-16608 | ASL-16605 |

- **Magnalube on all sides of the adapter.** Anti-seize on the stainless
  hardware, Loctite where shown.
- A ½-13 bolt in the bottom rivet nut lets you adjust the angle.
- Mount it at the **highest practical height**.

**Assembly:** 3 pieces (main lift, platform, Hold-Tite). Platform on a
5/16 × 3.5 in carriage bolt through the **brass belt spacer**, plus a
5/16 × 2 in bolt through the inner slide tube, both with nylocks, snug.

**Swing-Away (optional):**
- Mount it as high as possible.
- **Never swing it with a chair on.**
- Tuck the harness joint inside the upper tube (4 zip ties, leave
  slack).
- If the latch pin binds, adjust the swing arm screw: **clockwise =
  raise**.

**Power:** from the battery **through the interior, never underneath**.
The optional plate holder AWL-01673 plugs into the **4-pin flat** trailer
connector. Put **dielectric grease** on the connections.

**Hold-Tite:**
1. Fold the chair onto the platform, rear wheels centered in the U
   channel.
2. Raise it until about **2 in of travel remains**.
3. Lower the foam U onto the armrests and bolt the clevis.
4. **The foam should deform around the arms.**
   - Armpads deflect: move it up.
   - Chair loose: move it down.

**Operation:**
- Keyed switch under a watertight cover.
- **Holding DOWN after the platform lands unwinds the belt**, which
  delays the next lift.
- **Holding UP after it is fully up stalls the motor.**

**Troubleshooting:**
- Dead or slow: after vehicle service, were the battery wires
  reconnected?
- **Road salt on an unprotected ground**: coat it with dielectric and
  reapply each winter.
- Check the battery and harness connections.
- Burned-out bulbs.

Kit AWL-K-1601 handles hyper-flash and trailer-detect lighting issues
([[Vehicle-Lift-Power-Wiring]]).
