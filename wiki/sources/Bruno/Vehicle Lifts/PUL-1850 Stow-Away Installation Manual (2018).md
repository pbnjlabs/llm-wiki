# PUL-1850 Stow-Away Installation Manual (05-14-2018)

Install manual for the **PUL-1850 Stow-Away**: a **transfer seat that
stows in a box under the vehicle** (pickups) and rises to the door for a
seated transfer (16 pages). The brackets are vehicle-specific; see their
own instructions and [[ILS - Pickup Truck Bases and Stow-Away Mounting Kits]].

**Ground clearance and box:**
- Clearance **12 in (A) and 6 in (B)** minimum.
- The box is about **20 × 14 × 6 in**.
- **Mud flaps recommended** (not supplied).

**FMVSS:** it mounts outside the vehicle and **only runs with the door
open**, so it needs no structural changes and shouldn't affect the OEM's
FMVSS certification.

**Tools:**
- A right-angle drill and a **"Q" (0.332 in) bit**.
- A **rivet nut tool** with a ¼-20 mandrel.
- A floor jack to hold the unit.
- **Blue serviceable threadlocker on all hardware.**

**Electrical:**
- **Control box in the engine bay**, mounted vertically (or with formed
  edges down so water can't get in).
- Main harness routed **on top of or inside the frame**, away from heat
  and moving parts.
- **Battery lead** with an **ATO-style 30 A in-line fuse** at the
  battery +.
- **Keep the polarity right** or the controller is ruined.
- The **pendant extension** runs through the cab (firewall grommet) to
  the control box. A pendant holder goes on the door.

**Magnetic door safety switch:**
- Switch on the door post, magnet on the door, **gap no more than
  ¼ in**.
- Check blue to white with an ohmmeter: **closed with the door open,
  open with the door shut**.
- Blue and white go to the pendant extension.
- **If it runs with the door closed, re-position the switch.**

**Emergency electrical backup:**
1. Unplug the pendant and check the fuse.
2. Pop the cap on the control box: there are LEDs and board buttons.
3. **OUT** (B) until drawer-out LED A lights, then **DOWN** (C) until
   lift-down LED D lights, then **IN** (E) until drawer-in LED F lights.
4. **The board buttons bypass all limit switches.** Have the dealer
   repair it.

Kits: mounting TAS-K-18509, wiring TAS-K-18552. LH shown; RH parts
TAS-20042L/20051L.
