# Tech Tip - PUL-1100 Dimensions

Three image-only Bruno drawings (2008, 2014, 2017; portal tech-tips 220, 219, 221) of the **PUL-1100 Out-Rider**'s clearances, in inches:

| Setup | Top-view reach | Lift height to the head | Arm length (side view) | Offset |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Standard** offset arm + standard outer arm | 42.00 | 35.07 | 40.20 | 12.31 |
| **Crew cab** offset arm + crew cab outer arm | 51.05 | 35.07 | 47.70 | 18.32 |

- **Max height** with the standard arm raised: **67.18 in** from the base to the top of the head. Check it against a truck cap or tonneau.
- **Head lengths:** standard head **PUL-01154 = 40.28 in**, HD head **PUL-11024 = 40.28 in**, extended head **PUL-01151 = 47.78 in**.

Use the drawings in the PDFs for where each dimension is measured.
