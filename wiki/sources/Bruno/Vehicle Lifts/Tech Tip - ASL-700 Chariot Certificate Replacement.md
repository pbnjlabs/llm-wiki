# Tech Tip - ASL-700 Chariot Certificate Replacement

1-page Bruno note (portal tech-tip 26) on the Chariot's **certificate of origin and compliance** (part **MKT-COO**), which the owner may need for title and registration.

- A dealer orders a **replacement** from Bruno engineering with the **serial number and VIN**: **$25**, about **3 days**.
- Once a unit ships Bruno **never prints a new original**. A **reissue** is only for a proven manufacturer error or when a state agency confirms the original was destroyed. Otherwise it's a **replacement**, watermarked as such.
- Resold units can always get a replacement. The certificate says the unit met the FMVSS rules when built; titling and registration depend on the state and don't affect it.
- In states with no title or registration, follow local transfer rules; the certificate normally goes with the bill of sale.
