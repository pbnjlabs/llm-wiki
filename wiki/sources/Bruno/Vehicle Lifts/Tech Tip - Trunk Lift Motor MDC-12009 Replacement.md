# Tech Tip - Trunk Lift Motor MDC-12009 Replacement

1-page Bruno Q&A (portal tech-tip 28) for the **2002–2011 hoist motor MDC-12009** on the old trunk lifts **AWL-100, AWL-125, AWL-150, AWL-160 and VSL-175**.

History: MDC-12009 (tapped shaft, lift belt bolted to it) replaced MDC-12007 in 2002. In early 2008 (ECN3841) the AWL-100, AWL-125 and VSL-175 went obsolete; the new AWL-150's standard (heavy-duty) head **AWL-00182** used drive **BTL-00465** (motor MDC-12012, 8-tooth sprocket, chain and drum). In late 2010 (ECN4653) **BTL-00568** became standard, with easier chain tensioning.

**Replacing an MDC-12009:** either fit a whole new **AWL-00182** head, or replace just the motor with an **MDC-12012** drilled and tapped to match (lift speed may differ slightly).
