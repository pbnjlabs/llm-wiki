# ILS - Docking Device Fitment by Mobility Device

Bruno's **docking device (DD) install sheets**: 60 sheets, 1999–2023, each for a specific scooter or power chair (part prefix **SLD** = scooter, **WLD** = wheelchair). The DD mounts on the mobility device so the [[VSL-6000-6900]] Curb-Sider or [[PUL-1100]] Out-Rider can pick it up. **Most of these devices are discontinued**: the list stops adding new models around 2015 (one 2023 sheet, the Pride Maxima). For a current chair or scooter, ask Bruno which DD fits rather than assuming one here does.

- **Balance point:** every sheet has you find the device's balance point (seat back on, or the seat in the position needed to fit in the vehicle) before fixing the mount, and **adjust after a test lift**.
- **Shrouds:** many need the shroud **notched or trimmed** around the mount; some say **no trimming**. Some need the batteries out to reach the rear brackets.
- **Universal options:** support belts at each frame corner (**WLD-00978**), clamp mounts for 7/8 or 1 in frame tubes (**WLD-00801**), the **Hook 'N Go** belt DD (WLD-01344/01355), and a C-arm for scooters (adjusts 12–14 in without cutting).
- **Heavy chairs:** the Invacare Arrow Storm FWD (WLD-00967, on [[ILS - Curb-Sider (VSL-6000-6900) Service Sheets]]) weighs over 300 lb with its tail weight, which has to come off unless the lift is rated 400 lb. Check the lift and base capacity decal against the device's weight ([[Mobility-Device-Weights]]).
