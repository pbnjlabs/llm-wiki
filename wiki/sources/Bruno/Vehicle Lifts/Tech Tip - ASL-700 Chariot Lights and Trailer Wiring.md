# Tech Tip - ASL-700 Chariot Lights and Trailer Wiring

Two Chariot tips (portal tech-tips 27 and 30).

**Chariot lights stay on with the vehicle off:** usually the vehicle's **taillight converter module**. Modules with a wire to the **vehicle battery** take power from it instead of the taillights and feed a small current to the trailer lights even with the vehicle off.
1. **Preferred:** move that converter wire from the battery to an **ignition-switched** source.
2. **Alternative** (from a converter maker; usually but not always works): put three **2200 Ω, ¼ W or larger resistors** from the **white ground** to the **green, yellow and brown** flat-4 wires, one each. The extra current draw tells the module to shut the lights off.

**Checking the vehicle's wiring:** a flat-4 **trailer wiring tester** (Bruno named the Hopkins 48655, about $5) lights an LED for each circuit when the vehicle-side wiring is right.
