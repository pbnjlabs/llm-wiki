# VSL-4400G Operator Manual (2022)

23-page trilingual (English/Spanish/French) operator manual (P/N 4400G-O, 04-07-2022) for the **Joey VSL-4400G**, the same lift as the VSL-4400. English content: registration, safety (350 lb, level ground, engine may run in park, -25°F), loading with OUT/IN (release OUT when flat on the ground), optional barrier belts, unloading, pinch points, emergency backup, maintenance and troubleshooting, declaration of conformity, warranty.
