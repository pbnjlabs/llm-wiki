# VSL-4400 Joey Operator Manual (02-04-2022, English/French)

The North American **VSL-4400 Joey** owner's manual (32 pages). Its
content matches the trilingual [[VSL-4400G Operator Manual (2022)]]
already on file:

- Pinch points.
- **Emergency backup:** pull the **in-line fuse under the hood** first,
  then use the back-up tool from the manual bag.
- Yearly dealer maintenance.
- Owner troubleshooting.
- **3-year** Gold warranty.

Use either manual.
