# TRANSITIONS Angled Entry Mat Instructions

EZ-ACCESS instruction sheet for the **TRANSITIONS Angled Entry Mat**
(TAEM), the one-piece rubber threshold mat. Doc **20516**, dated
**07-23-2025**. Four pages, scanned. This is a manufacturer manual, so
it **overrides the 2021 web article** ([[Different Types of Threshold
Ramps]]) on this product.

## Covers

- **Heights:** 1/2, 3/4, 1, 1-1/4, 1-1/2, 1-3/4, 2, 2-1/4 and 2-1/2 in
  (every 1/4 in step). The article listed only two sizes (up to 1.5 in
  and up to 2.5 in). The sheet gives **no lengths or widths**.
- **Capacity: 850 lb** (p. 2). The article gives no capacity for the
  mat; the other TRANSITIONS products are rated 700 lb.
- **Warnings** (p. 2): the landing under the mat must be substantial,
  sound, level and unobstructed, and hold at least 850 lb. The mat must
  be **flush against the raised landing**; if it can't be, don't use it.
  Leave enough maneuvering room at both ends. Anchor it before each use.
  Slippery when wet; clear ice, snow, dirt and leaves. Follow the
  mobility device's owner guide for incline, and use a lap belt.
- **Tools** (p. 2): power drill, Phillips bit, 1/4 in drill bit, 1/4 in
  masonry bit and hammer (concrete only), measuring tape, marker, level
  or straight edge, and a tool for trimming or notching.
- **Package** (p. 3): 1 mat, 2 × mushroom-head drive pin anchors
  (1/4 × 1-1/4 in, for concrete), 2 × #10 × 3-1/2 in countersunk
  Phillips ceramic-coated screws (for wood), instructions.
- **Care** (p. 3): sweep, or wash with a soft brush and **non-alkaline**
  detergent, then rinse. Keep it flush against the landing.
- **Placement** (p. 4, Fig. 1): if the raised landing is the same
  height as the mat's top edge, **within 1/4 in**, use the mat as is.
  The top edge must not be more than 1/4 in off the landing height.
- **Trim/notch** (p. 4, Fig. 2): if the landing is **lower** than the
  mat, trim the mat down to that height. It can also be **notched** to
  fit the door jamb. **Trimming only lowers the height; never trim the
  width.** Rubber can bind or pinch hand tools.
- **Anchor** (p. 4, Fig. 3): two anchors, about **1 in in from each
  lower corner edge**.
  - **Wood:** drive the two screws through the mat until the heads sit
    just below the mat surface.
  - **Concrete:** drill a 1/4 in hole through the mat and **2 in deep**
    into the concrete, clear the dust, then hammer in the drive pin
    anchors until the heads sit just below the surface.
- Lifetime warranty; register at ezaccess.com/product-registration.
  Made in the USA.
