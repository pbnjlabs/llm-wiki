# TRANSITIONS Angled Entry Ramp Instructions and Specs

Two one-page EZ-ACCESS sheets for the **TRANSITIONS Angled Entry Ramp**
(TAER): the instructions (doc **14134 REV 08-10-2021**) and the spec
sheet (doc **14288, 06-30-17**). Manufacturer documents. They **settle
the conflict** on [[TRANSITIONS-Threshold-Ramps]]: the height ranges
match the 2021 article, not the narrower ranges on the 2018
[[EZ Access Parts and Pieces]] sheet.

## Covers

Rise range by swivel-foot size (instructions table; the spec sheet
agrees):

| Model | Size (L × W) | Weight | No feet | 1 in feet | 1-1/2 in feet | 2-1/2 in feet |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| TAER 12 | 11.877 × 36.25 in | 5.15 lb | 1-3/8 in | 1-15/16 – 2-3/4 in | — | — |
| TAER 24 | 23.875 × 36.25 in | 10.4 lb | 1-11/16 in | — | 2-1/8 – 3-3/8 in | 3-3/16 – 4-3/8 in |
| TAER 36 | 35.904 × 36.25 in | 15.15 lb | 3-5/16 in | — | — | 3-5/8 – 5-7/8 in |

- **The TAER 24 comes with two sizes of swivel feet** (1-1/2 and 2-1/2
  in). The 12 comes with 1 in feet, the 36 with 2-1/2 in feet.
- **Without feet, each ramp fits one height only** (its own height).
  Between that height and the bottom of the feet range nothing fits:
  e.g. a TAER 12 can't do 1-1/2 to 1-7/8 in (wiki reading of the
  table).
- The spec sheet's decimal range for the TAER 24 with 1-1/2 in feet
  reads 2.154 – **4.361** in, but its fraction and the instructions say
  2-1/8 – **3-3/8** in. Go by 3-3/8; the 4.361 looks copied from the
  next column.
- **700 lb, 350 lb per caster or wheel.** Aluminum, self-supporting,
  indoor or outdoor. Lifetime warranty. Not for commercial use.
- **Feet:** rotate clockwise to thread into the nutsert; don't
  overtighten. Adjust so both feet touch the ground. Foam, rubber or
  other soft material can go under the ramp to protect floors.
- **Fasten before use** through the two pre-drilled holes in the
  bottom: wood, one 1/4 × 1 in self-drilling stainless screw per hole
  (supplied); concrete, 1/4 in masonry bit and 1/4 × 1-1/2 in pin drive
  anchors (**not supplied**).
- Care is the same as the Angled Entry Plate.
