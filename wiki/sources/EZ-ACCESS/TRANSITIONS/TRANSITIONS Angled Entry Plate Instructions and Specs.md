# TRANSITIONS Angled Entry Plate Instructions and Specs

Two one-page EZ-ACCESS sheets for the **TRANSITIONS Angled Entry Plate**
(TAEP): the instructions (doc **13799 REV 08-10-2021**) and the spec
sheet (doc **13761, 06-30-17**). Manufacturer documents, so they
override the 2021 article and the 2018 parts sheet.

## Covers

| Model | Size | Plate height | Max rise | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TAEP 08 | 8 × 32 in | 0.695 in | 1-5/16 in | 3.5 lb |
| TAEP 10 | 10 × 32 in | 0.695 in | 1-5/8 in | 4.25 lb |
| TAEP 12 | 12 × 32 in | 0.695 in | 2 in | 5 lb |

- **700 lb, 350 lb per caster or wheel.** Applied slip-resistant
  surface. Lifetime limited warranty. Ships fully assembled.
- Wheelchairs and scooters only, **not for commercial use**. Use with a
  qualified helper and a lap belt.
- **Fasten it before use**, through the two pre-drilled holes in the
  top:
  - **Wood:** one 1/4 × 1 in self-drilling stainless screw per hole
    (supplied).
  - **Concrete:** mark through the holes, drill with a 1/4 in masonry
    bit to the anchor maker's depth, clear the dust, and set 1/4 × 1-1/2
    in pin drive anchors (**not supplied**).
- **Care:** check for damage and tighten loose fasteners regularly; keep
  it clear of ice, snow and debris; wash with a damp cloth or soft brush
  and non-alkaline detergent.
