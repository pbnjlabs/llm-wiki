# TRANSITIONS Angled Entry Mat Technical Specifications (2025)

EZ-ACCESS spec sheet for the **TRANSITIONS Angled Entry Mat** (TAEM),
doc **20584 REV 09-02-2025**. Five pages: a spec table (p. 1) and a
dimensioned drawing of every size (pp. 2–5, Blank style). Companion to
the install sheet, [[TRANSITIONS Angled Entry Mat Instructions]]
(07-23-2025). Manufacturer document, so it **overrides the 2021 article**
([[Different Types of Threshold Ramps]]) on sizes.

## Covers

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1/2 in | 3 × 36-1/16 in | 3 × 36-7/8 in | 2 lb | Blank |

| 1-3/4 in | 16-3/4 × 36-1/16 | 16-3/4 × 40-5/8 | 21 lb | Blank or Welcome |
| 2 in | 19-1/2 × 36-1/16 | 19-1/2 × 41-3/8 | 29 lb | Blank or Welcome |
| 2-1/4 in | 22-1/4 × 36-1/16 | 22-1/4 × 42-1/8 | 38 lb | Blank or Welcome |
| 2-1/2 in | 25 × 36-1/16 | 25 × 42-7/8 | 49 lb | Blank or Welcome |

- Weight is the same for Blank and Welcome (the Welcome style has a
  "WELCOME" inlay).
- **Black or grey**, recycled rubber, slip-resistant, **850 lb**, made in
  the USA, lifetime limited warranty (register at
  ezaccess.com/product-registration).
- Each 1/4 in step adds 2-3/4 in of length, so the slope eases with
  height: about 1:6 on the 1/2 in mat, 1:10 on the 2-1/2 in (wiki
  arithmetic, usable length ÷ rise). Check the mobility device's owner's
  guide for its allowed incline.
