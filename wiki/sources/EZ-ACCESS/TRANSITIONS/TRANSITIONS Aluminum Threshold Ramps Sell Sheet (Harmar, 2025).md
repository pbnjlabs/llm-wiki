# TRANSITIONS Aluminum Threshold Ramps Sell Sheet (Harmar, 2025)

A two-page sell sheet for EZ-ACCESS's **TRANSITIONS aluminum threshold
ramps**, published by **Harmar** (MK-000206 A, 26 Jun 2025). It carries
**both the Harmar and EZ-ACCESS logos**, so Harmar sells the same
TRANSITIONS products. Filed under EZ-ACCESS because they're EZ-ACCESS
products. Feeds [[TRANSITIONS-Threshold-Ramps]].

## Specs (all 700 lb)

| Product | Width | Sizes | Thresholds | Build |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Modular Entry Ramp** | 34 in | 1, 1½, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 in (heights; the sheet labels them "length") | **¾ – 6¼ in** | Hidden hinge, interchangeable extensions, extruded slip-resistant surface, **pivoting top flap adds ¼ in** |
| **Angled Entry Ramp** | 36 in | 12, 24, 36 in long | **1⅜ – 5⅞ in** | **Swivel feet** for height, angle and surface; won't rust; extruded surface |
| **Angled Entry Plate** | 32 in | 8, 10, 12 in long | **up to 2 in** | One-piece formed aluminum, **applied** slip-resistant surface, no moving parts |

## What it settles

- **Aluminum capacity is 700 lb.** Harmar's 2022 threshold-ramp guide
  ([[Harmar Product Insights (2021-2023)]]) said "up to 600 lb"; this
  newer sheet and every EZ-ACCESS source say 700.
- **Angled Entry Ramp range 1⅜ – 5⅞ in** overall matches the 2021 blog's
  full range, not the narrower 2018 parts-sheet ranges. The sheet doesn't
  give a range per length, so the per-size question on
  [[TRANSITIONS-Threshold-Ramps]] stays open.
- Modular Entry Ramp ¾ – 6¼ in and Angled Entry Plate ≤ 2 in match the
  wiki.
