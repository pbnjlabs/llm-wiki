# TRANSITIONS Modular Entry Mat Instructions and Specs

EZ-ACCESS's two-page instructions for the **TRANSITIONS Modular Entry
Mat** (doc **10270 REV 08-10-2021**) and its one-page spec sheet (TMEM,
dated **11-05-2020**). Manufacturer documents, so they override the
2021 article and the 2018 parts sheet.

## Covers

| Model | Size (H × L × W) | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| TMEM 2.5-1 (mat) | 2.5 × 24 × 48 in | 40 lb |
| TMEMR 2.25 (riser) | 2.25 × 24 × 24 in, sold as a pair | 23.5 lb each |

- **850 lb.** 100% recycled rubber, slip-resistant. Wheelchairs and
  scooters, not commercial use. Lifetime limited warranty.
- **Fitting** (p. 1):
  - Threshold **equal** to 2-1/2 in: use the mat as is.
  - **Lower:** trim the height, depth or width with a reciprocating
    saw, razor knife or hand saw, and notch for the door jamb if needed.
    Unlike the Angled Entry Mat, **this mat can be trimmed narrower**.
  - **Higher, up to 4-3/4 in:** two mats plus one pair of risers. The
    front mat (section A) stays at full height (trim its width only).
    The rear mat (B) sits on the risers (C) and can be trimmed; trim the
    risers to match B's depth and width, and screw B to C with the
    supplied Phillips screws.
- **Fastening** (recommended): wood, 2 supplied Phillips screws each,
  driven until the head is 1/4 in below the surface; concrete, drill
  1/4 in holes **2 in deep, 1 in from the corner edges at the taper**,
  and hammer in the supplied 1/4 in mushroom-head pin anchors.
- **Cargo wedge** (p. 2): trimmed to fill the 1–3 in drop from a
  vehicle's entrance to its cargo floor. Make a cardboard template
  first.
- **Care:** keep it clear of snow, ice and debris; clean with mild soap
  and warm water.
- Saw warning: rubber can bind or pinch a powered saw blade.
