# TRANSITIONS Modular Entry Ramp Instructions and Specs (2022)

EZ-ACCESS's 7-page instructions for the **TRANSITIONS Modular Entry
Ramp** (TMER, doc **21175, 11-14-2022**) and its 4-page spec sheet (doc
**21174, 11-04-2022**; drawings of each size and the anchor pattern).
Manufacturer documents, so they override the 2021 article.

Every size also has a flap (1.79 × 34 in).

- **700 lb**, aluminum, slip-resistant, made in the USA, lifetime
  limited warranty. For people and mobility equipment, not commercial
  use.
- **The sizes leave gaps** above 2.25 in (2.25–2.75, 3.25–3.75,
  4.25–4.75, 5.25–5.75 in), confirmed by this manufacturer table. The
  article wasn't missing sizes.
- **Tools:** tape, drill, Phillips bit, 1/4 in bit, and for concrete a
  1/4 in masonry bit and hammer.
- **Package:** the ramp, the #10-24 × 1/2 in thread-cutting hex washer
  head screws (4 for TMER 1–2, 8 for TMER 3–4, 12 for TMER 5–6), 4 ×
  1/4 × 1 in self-drilling pan head Phillips screws, instructions.
- **Assembly** (§5): start with the 1 in extension (the foot) and add
  extensions in order to the final height. Bring each pair together at
  about **45°**, engage the bulb in the slot, then rotate into place.
  The **flap is optional** (skip it where it would foul a sliding door)
  and goes into the top extension the same way. Drive a #10-24 screw
  into the bulb on **both sides of every joint**, flap included. The
  screws keep the sections aligned but **don't hold them together if the
  ramp is lifted**: carry it by opposite corners, or take it apart.
- **Setup** (§6): the top edge (ramp or flap) must be **within 1/4 in**
  of the landing and flush against it. Foam or rubber under the ramp
  indoors can push it past 1/4 in; the installer must keep the
  variance.
- **Anchor** (§7) the foot and the flap (foot only without a flap):
  - **Wood or aluminum:** drill 1/8 in pilot holes through the holes in
    the foot and flap, then drive the supplied 1/4 × 1 in self-drilling
    screws.
  - **Concrete:** installer supplies **1/2 in anchors** suited to the
    slab, set flush with the ramp surface. Not provided.
- **Care** (§4): check for worn or loose parts and tighten fasteners
  before each use; keep it flush with the landing; clean with a soft
  brush and non-alkaline detergent; store cool and dry.
