# SUITCASE Portable Folding Ramps Brochure

EZ-ACCESS's two-page **SUITCASE** brochure with a "Quick Specs" table,
form **1027_E**, undated. Scanned, no text layer. A manufacturer document,
so it outranks the web clippings used for the SUITCASE page so far.
Feeds [[SUITCASE-Ramps]].

## The three styles (all 800 lb capacity)

| Style | Build | Surface |
|---|---|---|
| **Singlefold** | One piece, folds in half with a **full-length hinge**, carried like a suitcase; built for high traffic | **Extruded** slip-resistant |
| **Singlefold AS** | Use as one unit or **split into two lightweight halves** | **Applied** slip-resistant |
| **Trifold AS** | 3-fold, for longer ramps; one unit or **split into two halves** | **Applied** slip-resistant |

So **"AS" = the ramp separates into two sections and has an applied
grip surface**. EZ-ACCESS's warranty excludes applied non-skid tape
([[EZ-ACCESS Lifetime Warranty (2017)]]).

**The brochure says:** consult the mobility device's owner's guide for
the proper incline and **never exceed its recommendation**.

## Other notes

- **Top Lip Extension (TLE)**, optional: extends the top lip **from 3 in
  to 9 in** so the ramp clears the rear bumper of most SUVs and vans.
- A 2 ft Singlefold is pitched as a car-trunk emergency ramp.
- Made in America; lifetime warranty. EZ-ACCESS: 1-800-451-1903,
  customerservice@ezaccess.com.
