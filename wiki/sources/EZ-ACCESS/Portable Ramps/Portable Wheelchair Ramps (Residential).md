# Portable Wheelchair Ramps (Residential)

EZ-ACCESS blog article (2024-02-06), clipped from the web. Compares the
two portable families.

## Covers

- **SUITCASE** folding ramps, 2–10 ft: Singlefold and Singlefold AS
  (fold in half lengthwise, carry handle); TRIFOLD AS (also folds across
  the width for shorter storage length).
- All SUITCASE ramps accept the **Top Lip Extension** (+6 in transition
  plate, for clearing a vehicle bumper).
- Self-adjusting ground transition plates on portable ramps.
- **GATEWAY 3G** solid one-piece ramps, up to 10 ft, optional handrails;
  doesn't fold — for home use where repositioning is occasional
  (post-surgery, no permanent modification).
- EZ-ACCESS customer service: 1-800-451-1903.
