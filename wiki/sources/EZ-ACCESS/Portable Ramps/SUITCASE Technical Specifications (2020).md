# SUITCASE Technical Specifications (2020)

Three EZ-ACCESS spec sheets, all dated **04-13-2020**: Singlefold (3
pp.), Singlefold AS and TRIFOLD AS (2 pp. each). Each has a spec table
and dimensioned drawings of every length. All models: aluminum,
slip-resistant, built-in carry handle, **800 lb**, wheelchairs and
scooters, lifetime limited warranty.
