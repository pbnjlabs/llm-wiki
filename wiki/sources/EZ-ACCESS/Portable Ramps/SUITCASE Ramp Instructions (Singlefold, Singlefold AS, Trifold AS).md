# SUITCASE Ramp Instructions (Singlefold, Singlefold AS, Trifold AS)

EZ-ACCESS's instruction booklets for the three SUITCASE folding ramps,
all **REV 08-10-2021**: Singlefold (doc **10277**, 5 pp.), Singlefold AS
(**10266**, 6 pp.) and TRIFOLD AS (**10258**, 6 pp.). The three are the
same text except for the unfolding steps and the AS split-ramp section,
so they share this page. Manufacturer documents; they outrank the
brochure and the clippings.

## Covers

- **800 lb**, wheelchairs and scooters only, **not for commercial
  use**. Use with a qualified helper and a lap belt. The landings must
  hold 800 lb. Patented. Lifetime warranty.
- **Package:** the ramp and **2 clevis pins**; the AS ramps also come
  with **2 hinge pins**.
- **Unfolding:**
  - **Singlefold / Singlefold AS:** set the folded ramp with the top lip
    plate (end labeled TOP) fully on the landing, unfold, and center it.
  - **TRIFOLD AS:** stand it about its full length out from the landing,
    unfold the TOP half onto the landing, unfold the second half so both
    halves lie full length (one face down on the other), then open it
    across the width.
- **Both top lip plates must fully overlap a level landing** and rest
  firmly against it. If not, don't use the ramp.
- **Anchor before each use:**
  - **Temporary:** drill 1/4 in holes through the hole in each top lip
    plate, deep enough to seat the pin, and insert the clevis pins.
  - **Semi-permanent:** installer-supplied hardware, set flush with the
    plate.
- **AS ramps split in two** (remove the 2 hinge bolts and nylon
  locknuts) for storage, or to load **unoccupied** equipment with a wide
  wheelbase. Center the load on each half and anchor each half. **With
  someone riding, the halves must be joined** with the hinge pins (spring
  side underneath, pins closed) or the original bolts and locknuts. Keep
  the pins or bolts stored in one side of the hinge. The TRIFOLD AS
  hinge bolt is shown as 1/4-20 × 3-1/4 in.
- **TRIFOLD AS carry:** press the hook-and-loop tabs on the underside
  together so the halves don't swing open.
- **Top Lip Extension (TLE)**, sold separately: extends the top lip
  from **3 to 9 in** to clear an obstruction. **With a TLE the ramp is
  rated 500 lb, not 800.**
- **Care:** check before each use; clear ice, snow and debris; wash with
  a soft brush and non-alkaline detergent; store cool and dry.
- **Incline chart** (last page; same chart in all three): degrees for
  rises of 3–30 in on 2–10 ft ramps, up to about 15°. Wherever the rise
  in inches equals the length in feet the chart reads **4.8°**, which is
  the 1:12 house limit. EZ-ACCESS says to pick the length from the
  incline the chair is rated for. (SUITCASE ramps are exempt from 1:12;
  see [[SUITCASE-Ramps]].)
