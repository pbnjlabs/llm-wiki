# PATHWAY HD Ramp Requirements Worksheet (EZ-ACCESS 4076_A)

EZ-ACCESS's 3-page **Environmental Survey / Ramp Requirements
Worksheet** for a PATHWAY HD Code Compliant system (form **4076_A**; PDF
created 2026-04-22). The commercial version of the
[[PATHWAY 3G Ramp Requirements Worksheet (EZ-ACCESS 4051_D)]]. EZ-ACCESS's
form, not PMS's.

## Covers

- **Order details:** customer ID, contact, delivery needs (appointment,
  residential, expedited, limited access), delivery and install dates,
  **drawing options** (canned, non-stamped footing layout, **stamped
  seismic/wind calculations**), existing building or not.
- **Entry measurements A–J** and **total rise** (threshold + step or
  entry rise ± grade), same as the 3G form. **Bracing at 36 in of rise,
  more bracing at 72 in.**
- **Options:** ramp width 48 / 54 / 60 in or custom; platform sizes
  5 × 5, 5 × 6, 5 × 7, 6 × 6 or custom; rail style picketed guards or
  two-line (**30 in of rise or more requires guards**); handrail 36 in
  standard, lower child rail 25 in; bridge plate 36 / 48 / 60 in; layout
  straight, L or switchback; **slope 1:12 (ADA)** or custom 1:10, 1:8,
  1:6; door style; loops standard, **California** or **Massachusetts
  compliant**; stair height and width (56 in standard); extender
  platform.
- Last page: bird's-eye sketch on a 1 ft grid.
- Steeper custom slopes are offered here, but **PMS builds 1:12 only**
  ([[Ramp-Slope-and-Length]]).
