# PATHWAY HD Multi-Story Modular Stair System (Install Manual and Specs, 2026)

EZ-ACCESS's 31-page installation manual (doc **20122, 07-16-2026**) and
3-page spec sheet (doc **20121 REV 07-17-26**) for the **PATHWAY HD
Multi-Story Modular Stair System**: commercial stairs for 103–144 in
rises, built from a starter riser, an upper riser and, above 112 in, a
35 in middle riser.

## Covers

- **Rating:** 100 psf and 300 lb over 4 sq in. Commercial. **3-year
  limited warranty.**

| Model | Treads | Adjustable rise | ADA/IBC compliant rise | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PHDSSA10310956G | 14 | 103–109 in | 105 in | 512.1 lb |
| PHDSSA11011656G | 15 | 110–116 in | 112 in | 538.0 lb |
| PHDSSA11712356G | 16 | 117–123 in | 119 in | 672.5 lb |
| PHDSSA13814456G | 16 | 138–144 in | 140 in | 788.4 lb |

- Same tread, guard and handrail dimensions as the
  [[PATHWAY HD Adjustable Stair System (Install Manual and Specs, 2026)]]
  (7 in risers, 12-1/4 in treads, 56 in wide, 42-1/4 in guard, 36 in
  handrail); top riser overhang 2 in.
- **Configurations** (manual §1): 70–112 in, starter riser straight to
  upper riser; 119–140 in, with the 35 in middle riser between. Every
  system comes with top and bottom riser closures, legs, feet and
  **bracing** (§6). The bottom closure assumes the starter riser 7 in
  above a level surface.
- **Setting:** platform legs flush with or above the corner pocket top;
  risers level or sloped away **1:50 max**; **riser set screws 26 ft-lb
  minimum** (torque wrench). Same equal-riser rule (3/8 in max variation)
  as the adjustable stair.
- Sections: upper landing (or platform extender), upper / middle /
  starter risers, bracing, riser closures, riser and landing guards,
  closure to the landing, handrails and end loops (standard or extended
  lower loops), handrail termination at the landing, final checks,
  maintenance, deicing.
