# PATHWAY HD Adjustable Stair System (Install Manual and Specs, 2026)

EZ-ACCESS's 36-page installation manual (doc **20155, 04-21-2026**) and
2-page spec sheet (doc **21993, 04-14-2026**) for the **PATHWAY HD
Adjustable Stair System**: commercial aluminum steps, 1 to 8 treads, for
a PATHWAY HD platform or an existing landing.

## Covers

- **Rating:** 100 psf and 300 lb over 4 sq in. Commercial. **3-year
  limited warranty.** Qualified installer; handrails required; regular
  inspection.
- **Sizes** (spec sheet; model PHDSSA + range + 56G):

| Treads | Model | Adjustable rise | ADA/IBC compliant rise | Weight with handrails |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PHDSSA121856G | 12–18 in | 14 in | 69.6 lb |
| 2 | 192556G | 19–25 in | 21 in | 98.0 |
| 3 | 263256G | 26–32 in | 28 in | 123.5 |
| 4 | 333956G | 33–39 in | 35 in | 147.8 |
| 5 | 404656G | 40–46 in | 42 in | 172.1 |
| 6 | 475356G | 47–53 in | 49 in | 197.7 |
| 7 | 546056G | 54–60 in | 56 in | 225.4 |
| 8 | 616756G | 61–67 in | 63 in | 249.7 |

- **Fixed dimensions:** 7 in riser spacing, 12-1/4 in tread, 1-3/8 in
  tread thickness, first step 5–11 in, top riser overhang 3-3/4 in,
  guard 42-1/4 in (3-3/4 in picket spacing, 4 in max to the riser),
  handrail 36 in (1-1/2 in), 64 in overall width, 56 in stair width,
  50-3/16 in usable. Standard polymer or low-profile aluminum feet.
  Standard or **extended lower loops** (extended = 12 in level beyond
  one tread depth, where local code asks).
- **Code note:** for ADA and IBC, every riser and tread in the stairway
  must match; **largest and smallest riser may differ by no more than
  3/8 in**. Only the compliant height in each range gives equal risers.
- **Setting the risers:** level, or sloped away from the landing **no
  more than 1:48 (1/4 in per ft)** for drainage, all feet on the ground;
  never sloped back toward the landing. **Riser leg set screws: 317 in-lb
  minimum** (3/16 in Allen); guard pocket set screws the same.
- **Sections:** upper landing, risers, landing guards or two-line rails
  (two-line closure only up to 30 in elevation), riser guards, guard
  closure, stair handrails and loops, return handrail at the landing,
  connecting the stair handrail to a ramp handrail, lower riser closure,
  return-to-ground and return-to-landing handrails, steps on a platform
  extender, maintenance and deicing.
