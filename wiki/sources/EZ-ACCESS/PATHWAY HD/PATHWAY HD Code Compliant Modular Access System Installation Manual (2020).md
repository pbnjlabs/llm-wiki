# PATHWAY HD Code Compliant Modular Access System Installation Manual (2020)

92-page EZ-ACCESS installation manual for the **PATHWAY HD Code
Compliant Modular Access System** (doc **17753, 03-02-2020**), the
commercial counterpart of the PATHWAY 3G. The first PATHWAY HD manual in
the wiki; until now [[PATHWAY-HD]] relied on one web article.

## How it differs from the PATHWAY 3G

| | PATHWAY 3G (2023) | **PATHWAY HD (2020)** |
|---|---|---|
| Use | Residential | **Commercial, code compliant** |
| Rating | 1,000 lb | **100 psf live load, 300 lb concentrated** |
| Installer | Qualified installer | **EZ-ACCESS approved technician required**; only approved techs may service it |
| Slope | 1:12 ideal, 1:14 – 1:8 allowed | **1:12 required** |
| Ramp widths | 36, 48 in | **48, 54, 60 in** |
| Ramp lengths | 2–8 ft | 2–6 ft plus a **6 ft starter ramp** (tapered, ground only) |
| Rails | Handrail frame 34.5 in | **Frame (two-line rail or picketed guard) plus separate handrail**; optional lower child rail |
| Legs | PSLxxPR 10–100 in | **PHDSLxxPR 10–112 in**, foot through-bolted (5/16 × 3 in) |
| Bracing | Angle brace over 36 in, cross brace 72 in | **Cross brace (TXB36 / TXB736) over 36 in**, sometimes lower |
| Transition | PRUT / PRLT / PRGT | **TTP48/54/60 transition plate + ramp support top**, or the starter ramp |
| Warranty | Lifetime | **3 years** |

## Covers

- **Front matter:** professional install by an **EZ-ACCESS approved
  technician**; inspect visually before each use; **inspect all
  fasteners annually** (more often in harsh environments); consult local
  code for environmental (wind/seismic) securing; don't lay power cords
  on it or hang lights or cords from it. Note freight damage on the bill
  while the driver is there. Leave the manual with the end user.
- **Tools:** 1/2, 9/16, 5/16 in sockets, 5/32 and 3/16 in Allen, level,
  file, mallet, drill, tape, #2 Phillips, 5/16 in bit (braces), box
  knife, hammer, 1/4 in masonry bit, digging tools, hacksaw, pliers,
  clamp.
- **§1 Components:** PHDRHRLU / PHDRHRLL end loops, PHDRHBPR end bracket
  pair, PHDRRC ramp-to-ramp connector, PHDRST ramp support top, PHDRHP
  hanger pair, TTP48/54/60 transition plates, PHDHREP end plug, PHDCPCPR
  / PHDCPCD corner pocket covers, PHDPPC platform connector, PHDSLxxPR
  leg pairs (10–112 in), PHDXB36 (5–6 ft sides and ramps) and PHDXB736
  (7 ft sides) cross braces. Closures by platform side (5, 6, 7 ft):
  PHDCTL two-line, PHDCG picketed guard, PHDTBC turn-back, PHDCTC corner
  turn.
- **§2 Platforms:** legs must be **flush with to 2-3/4 in above** the
  top of the corner pocket (flush or below when the platforms make a
  deck with pocket covers). Upper set screw snug, stand it up, feet
  under the platform, **drill 11/32–3/8 in through leg and foot and fit
  a 5/16-18 × 3 in bolt and locknut**, level, lower set screw. Two
  platforms of the same width join with connectors (6 legs).
  **Decks:** never two leg stubs at one connector; at least two legs
  per platform; alternate connector direction; legs in opposite
  diagonal corners where four corners meet; trim interior legs flush;
  cover unused pockets.
- **§3 Ramps:** same saddle-bracket joint as the 3G (8 bolts); end
  brackets in the insert closest to the end. **Starter ramp:** the
  tapered section must be fully supported by the ground, never
  elevated. With the ramp at the side of a platform, **align the outside
  of the frame post with the inside of the platform angle post** (if
  it's outside, the end loops won't fit). Leg bracket locknuts **20
  ft-lb**, leg set screws **15 ft-lb**, leg-to-foot through-bolt.
  **"Building codes and ADA guidelines call for a maximum slope of 1:12.
  This is also the required slope for the PATHWAY HD system."** Cross
  brace under ramps over 36 in. **To an existing porch:** ramp support
  top plus transition plate, overlapping at least 1 in, anchored (1/4
  × 1 in screws in wood; 1/4 in holes 1-1/2 in deep and mushroom
  anchors in concrete). Plan-view angle up to about **6°** with a
  transition plate and support top.
- **§4 Handrails:** frames (two-line or picketed guard) bolt to the
  bracket studs; handrails mount on adjustable brackets on the frame;
  optional lower **child rail** on the lower holes. Two-line frames can
  be field-converted to picketed guards. **Starter frame lower posts
  must be anchored for a code-compliant system:** 4 concrete anchors,
  1/4 or 3/8 × 2-1/2 in minimum, per lower post support (supplied by
  others).
- **§5 Closures** (pp. 35–73): two-line barrier and mid rail, picketed
  guard closures (5, 6, 7 ft), closure handrails (tube bends sized to
  the platform side), turn-back closures (5 and 6 ft; 7 ft is custom),
  corner turns, a 54 in ramp on a 5 × 5 corner turn, 5 ft wide resting
  platforms, three-platform deck corners. Barrier attachment plates come
  "up sloping" and "down sloping".
- **§6 Final steps:** ramp closure bracket on every free frame post; **2
  ramp end clips at each ramp/platform joint**; end loops wherever the
  handrail doesn't return to a wall, post or the ground (swaged leg up,
  two 1/4 × 1 in screws underneath at 3/8 and 1-1/8 in from the end;
  trim a child rail 3/4 in before fitting a loop); end plugs, corner
  protectors, touch-up, final checks.
- **§7 Options:** **handrail return to ground** (extends at least 1 ft
  level, straight or with a 90° turn, on a 4 × 4 in base anchored to the
  ground or platform); ramp support; **platform extenders** (4-1/2 × 1
  ft for 48 in ramps, 5 × 1 ft for 54 in) so end loops don't intrude on
  a landing, always on four legs; transition plates; bridge plates (6 in
  max gap, flat only, 1 in bearing).
- **§8–9 Maintenance and deicing:** as for the 3G; damaged or loose
  parts must be repaired by an EZ-ACCESS approved technician.
