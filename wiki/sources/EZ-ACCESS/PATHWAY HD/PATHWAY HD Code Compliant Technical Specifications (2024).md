# PATHWAY HD Code Compliant Technical Specifications (2024)

9-page EZ-ACCESS spec and bid sheet for the **PATHWAY HD Code Compliant
Modular Access System** (doc **18555 REV 06-07-2024**): an architect's
specification, dimension tables and drawings.

## Covers

- **Design basis:** 2021 **IBC**; **100 psf** uniform live load and 300
  lb concentrated (steps: 300 lb over 4 sq in). Guards and handrails
  resist 200 lb at any point, or 50 plf horizontal with 100 plf down;
  infill 50 lb over 1 sq ft. Sealed engineering drawings or test results
  on request. Reusable and relocatable. Mill finish, corrosion-resistant
  fasteners.
- **Warranty: 3 years** from delivery (defects in material and
  workmanship).
- **Ramps:** 2–6 ft sections plus a **6 ft starter ramp**, all designed
  for **1:12**; clear widths **48, 54 and 60 in**; **4 in curb** (or a
  barrier a 4 in sphere can't pass); about 1-1/4 × 6 in self-mating
  Gecko Grip deck. Handrail **36 in** (34–38 in allowed), child rail **25
  in**, frame top rail **42 in**, mid rail 21.5 in. Handrail clearance
  1-1/2 in min.

Rail pairs (2 → 6 ft): handrail 2.5 → 6 lb, with child rail 5 → 12 lb,
two-line frame 20.2 → 29.1 lb, guard 23.3 → 41.9 lb.

- **Platforms** (5.5 in minimum height without legs; guard 42 in,
  **picket spacing 3-3/4 in typical, 3-15/16 in max**; two-line mid rail
  22 in): 5 × 5 (65.5 in square, 81.6 lb), 5 × 6 (65.5 × 77.5, 94.3 lb),
  6 × 6 (77.5 square, 107.8 lb), 5 × 7 (65.5 × 89.5, 107 lb), and 1 ×
  4.5 / 1 × 5 ft extenders (28.6 / 30.8 lb). Rail pairs 41.8–49.8 lb
  (two-line), 47–58.2 lb (guards).
- **Legs:** 1.5 in square aluminum, 0.67 lb per inch, vertical
  regardless of slope, through-bolted **7.375 in square polymer feet**.
- **Steps:** 6 in closed risers (4–7 in allowed), 18–60 in heights, 56
  in usable width (60 overall; 50-3/16 in between handrails), 12.5 in
  typical tread (11 in min), 1 in safety tape (2 in optional). Step with
  guards and handrails weighs 83 lb (18 in) to 266 lb (60 in).
- **Transition plates:** 6.73 in long; 47.38 / 53.37 / 59.37 in wide
  (48 / 54 / 60 in ramps); 6.4–8 lb; 1/4 in anchor holes.
