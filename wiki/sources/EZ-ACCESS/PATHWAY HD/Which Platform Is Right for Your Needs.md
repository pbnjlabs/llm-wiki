# Which Platform Is Right for Your Needs?

EZ-ACCESS blog article (2024-06-22), clipped from the web. Commercial
marketing piece on the PATHWAY HD aluminum platform family.

## Covers

- Three PATHWAY HD platform types:
  - **Code Compliant Modular Access System / Platform Walkway** — 48 in
    usable width, two-way traffic, independently adjustable legs, quick
    handrails, designed to ADA and most local codes.
  - **Platform Decking** — custom-size wide surfaces (stages, picnic
    areas, viewing decks), handrails optional.
  - **Work Platforms** — adjustable elevation over cables/pipes, can be
    built multiple stories high, integrates ramps/stairs.
- Example uses: event stages (extra reinforcement available via R&D),
  viewing platforms, elevated walkways, patios, ride loading, graduation
  stages, outdoor ground cover, equipment protection, mezzanine storage.
- Made in the USA.
