# Understanding Ramp Incline: How to Measure and Select the Right Solution

EZ-ACCESS blog article (2025-02-19), clipped from the web. Short guide to
incline ratios and the EZ-ACCESS online Incline Calculator
(https://ezaccess.com/pages/incline-calculator).

## Covers

- Ratio tiers: **1:12** ADA standard; **1:10** "often acceptable" for
  certain residential/light-use; **1:8 or 1:6** for non-mobility uses
  (recreational/industrial loading). Note this conflicts with
  [[How to Measure and Install a Ramp]] — see [[Ramp-Slope-and-Length]].
- Measure rise → pick ratio (start at 1:12, check the equipment owner's
  guide for allowable slope and direction) → enter in calculator, which
  returns length, slope and grade.
- Rise over 30 in may require a resting platform.
- **"At any incline, use the ramp only with a qualified helper."**
