# VA Ramp Returns and Restocking Fee (2025)

**PMS's return and restocking policy for VA ramp orders**, as stated in
a redacted PMS email to the VA on **2 July 2025**. The email is page 3
of 4 of a printed thread; the other pages and the attached FSS return
goods policy aren't in the file.

**Left out on purpose:** the veteran's and VA and PMS staff names, email
addresses, the VA PO number and phone numbers.

## Return and restocking policy

When a VA ramp order can't be installed, under the **FSS contract
36F79721D0058** and its return goods policy:

1. **Custom ramps may not be returnable.** A ramp built to a job's
   custom configuration can be treated as **"custom" and not eligible
   for return**.
2. **Restocking fee: 10% of the order**, **plus shipping**. PMS can
   extend the 10% restocking fee instead of refusing the return.
   **Shipping is extra and set by the manufacturer**, so the final
   price is only known when the VA decides.
3. **Or no return:** **charge the PO as written (full amount) and keep
   the parts for the VA**, to hold until the job can go ahead or **reuse
   on another veteran's ramp**. This saves the VA the restocking fee.

PMS presents both options to the VA and lets the VA choose.

**What usually happens: "VA Stock."** In many cases the VA tells PMS to
**keep the ramp parts as "VA Stock."** PMS holds them and **pulls from
VA Stock to build ramps for other veterans** (per the human).

**VA Stock log:** tracked in an **Excel spreadsheet kept locally on a
co-worker's PC** (not in the wiki). Columns, roughly:

| Column | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| Vet's name | The veteran the parts were originally ordered for |
| Item number / description | The ramp part |
| Used for: | The job the part was later used on |

Check the log before ordering parts for a VA ramp, and record any VA
Stock you use.

**Example:** on this order the 10% restocking fee came to **$658.40**
(an order of about $6,584), before shipping.

## The job behind it

- PMS called on 1 July 2025 to schedule the install.
- The veteran's spouse said they'd just started **months of yard work**
  (irrigation lines, pulling up concrete and grass, laying and watering
  sod). This hadn't been raised at the evaluation.
- PMS already had the ramp in hand, so it offered the VA the two options
  above. The outcome isn't in the file.

**Evaluation lesson:** ask about planned yard or home work and how long
it will take (see [[Ramp-Site-Planning]]).
