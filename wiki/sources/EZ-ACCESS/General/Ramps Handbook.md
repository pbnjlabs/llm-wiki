# Performance Medical Handbook: RAMPS

**Performance Medical Supply's own in-house ramp training handbook.**
The docx author is "PMS"; it was created 2021-02-25 and last modified
2026-06-17. It ends with a training sign-off block (training date,
trainer, employee signature). This is the source of the PMS house
standards in the wiki, and it **outranks every other ramp document**
here for how PMS evaluates and designs ramps. Filed under EZ-ACCESS
because it is written around EZ-ACCESS PATHWAY modular ramps, following
the precedent of PMS's Harmar forms under `sources/Harmar/General/`.

## Covers

- **Home evaluation**:
  - start inside the home and meet the user
  - get the wheelchair type (manual: what size; power: make and model)
    and the user's weight
  - plan the **fire-escape path** out of the house to the ramp:
    narrow interior doorways, sunken floors, obstructions
  - create **5 ft of level space in front of the exit door**, allowing
    for sunken porches, 4 in thresholds, railings
- **Ramp design, "5 basic ADA guidelines"**:
  1. **1:12 on every ramp**: 1 ft of fall per inch of rise. **Slope
     meter must read 4.8 or less.**
  2. **60 in of level, clear space at the top and bottom of every run**,
     at least as wide as the ramp leading to it.
  3. **60 in of level, clear space in front of the door**, at least as
     wide as the ramp.
  4. **No more than 30 ft of continuous ramp without a rest** (a rest is
     60 in of level space, as wide as the ramp).
  5. **At least one handrail must be continuous**: no gaps, spaces or
     stops.
- Components: ramp lengths 2–8 ft; platforms up to 8 × 5 ft.
- **Site documentation**: photograph the whole area, note obstructions
  (bushes, trees), and lay out the ramp with small cones and
  photograph that too.
- Training resource: EZ-ACCESS's 30-minute PATHWAY 3G install video
  (ezaccess.com PATHWAY 3G product page → Videos). It covers platforms,
  ramps, connections, handrails, legs, closure kits, loops, and
  horizontal and vertical pickets.
- Images: an EZ-ACCESS Incline Calculator screenshot, a PATHWAY ramp
  render, and site photos.

## Flag: the worked example rounds the run down

The handbook's example is 4 stairs with a **28.5 in** total rise, giving
a **28 ft** run. The embedded Incline Calculator screenshot shows the
same result (28.5 in, 1:12, 4.76°, run 28 ft). But a 28 ft run over
28.5 in is **4.85°**, which **fails the handbook's own 4.8° slope-meter
rule**. The true 1:12 run is 28.5 ft. Since ramp sections come in whole
feet, **round the run up** (29 ft = 4.68°). This has been raised with
the human; the handbook itself is a raw source and was not edited.
