# EZ-ACCESS Lifetime Warranty (2017)

EZ-ACCESS's one-page **Lifetime Warranty** (Homecare Products, Inc. dba
EZ-ACCESS), form **0001 Rev 06-22-17**. Scanned, no text layer. It
covers "its products" in general, so it's the full warranty text behind
the "lifetime warranty" mentioned on [[PATHWAY-3G]], [[SUITCASE-Ramps]]
and [[TRANSITIONS-Threshold-Ramps]]. Feeds [[EZ-ACCESS]].

## Coverage

- **Lifetime of the product**, defects in material and workmanship under
  normal use.
- **Parts and components only.** Excludes the **powder-coated finish**
  and **applied non-skid tape** (so the grip surface on the "AS" ramps
  isn't covered).
- Remedy: EZ-ACCESS repairs or replaces defective parts **with no
  charge for parts or labor**, or replaces a discontinued product with a
  comparable one, or refunds the purchase price. Its choice.
- **Original purchaser and delivery only; not transferable. Proof of
  purchase required.**

## Making a claim

1. **Contact EZ-ACCESS first**, before asserting any claim.
2. EZ-ACCESS either issues a **Return Authorization (RA)** or schedules
   an **on-site examination**.
3. **Report a defect within 30 days of discovering it.** Damage made
   worse by not acting (failure to mitigate) isn't covered.

## Not covered

- **Installation, alteration, relocation**, faulty maintenance,
  improper loads, negligence, accident, vandalism, misuse or abuse.
- Ordinary wear and tear.
- Shrinkage, warping, fractures, discoloration "within reasonable
  tolerances" from aging, weather, damp, condensation or corrosion.
- **Changes in grading or landscaping**, defects in adjoining
  structures, **soil movement** or changed site conditions.
- Acts of God (fire, flood, wind, hail, lightning, falling trees, snow,
  ice, earthquake, vehicles, etc.).
- No liability for injury or property damage; no incidental or
  consequential damages. **Maximum liability is the product's purchase
  price.**

**For PMS installs:** installation and relocation damage, and ground
settling or regrading under a ramp, are the installer's or owner's
problem, not EZ-ACCESS's. Level, firm footing (see [[Ramp-Site-Planning]])
protects the warranty.
