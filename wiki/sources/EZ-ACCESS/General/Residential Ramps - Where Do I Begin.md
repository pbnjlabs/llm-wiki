# Residential Ramps: Where Do I Begin?

EZ-ACCESS blog article (2023-07-30), clipped from the web. Customer
intake guide — useful as a CSR question script.

## Covers

- **Getting-started questions**: mobility device (drives width/slope);
  will the device change (walker now, wheelchair later → go more
  permanent); which entrance and what obstacles; material (function vs.
  curb appeal); how permanent; DIY vs. professional install; budget and
  insurance/Medicare funding; local permit requirements.
- Length: confirm allowable incline from the device user guide and
  municipality → measure rise → EZ-ACCESS Incline Calculator.
- 1:12 is "just over an 8% grade" and the ratio most device
  manufacturers recommend.
- **Width**: EZ-ACCESS residential ramps come in standard **36 in and
  48 in** widths.
- Platforms: ADA-style 60 × 60 in at top and a rest platform every 30 ft;
  also used for turns.
- Three home ramp families: portable (folding/non-folding),
  semi-permanent (handrails, quick reposition), long-term.
- Dealer installation takes "a matter of hours."
