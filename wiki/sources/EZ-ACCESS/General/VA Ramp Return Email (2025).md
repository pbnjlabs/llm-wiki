# VA Ramp Return Email (2025)

A **real job**: PMS's email to the VA on **2 July 2025** about a VA ramp
that couldn't be installed. Page 3 of 4 of a printed email thread
(printed 30 Sep 2026); the other pages and the attached return goods
policy aren't in the file. Kept because it shows **PMS's options when a
VA ramp order can't go ahead**.

**Left out on purpose:** the veteran's and VA and PMS staff names, email
addresses, the VA PO number and phone numbers. Several are only partly
blacked out on the scan.

## What happened

- PMS called on 1 July 2025 to schedule the install.
- The veteran's spouse said they'd **just started major yard work**
  (irrigation lines, pulling up old concrete and grass, laying and
  watering new sod) that would **take months**. This wasn't mentioned at
  the evaluation; PMS had only heard yard work would happen around the
  first week of July.
- PMS already had the ramp in hand.

## PMS's options to the VA

Under the **FSS contract 36F79721D0058** and its return goods policy:

1. **Charge the purchase order in full and hold the ramp** until the
   veteran's project is done, **or use it for another veteran's ramp**
   to save on future costs.
2. **Return it.** Because of its custom configuration, the ramp **could
   be considered "custom" and not eligible for return**. PMS offered to
   apply a **10% restocking fee** instead ($658.40 on this order),
   **plus shipping**, which the manufacturer sets. The final price is set
   when the decision is made.

PMS said either option was fine and asked the VA to decide. The outcome
isn't in the file.

## Takeaways

- **At the evaluation, ask about planned yard or home work** (sod,
  irrigation, concrete, landscaping) and how long it will take.
- A VA ramp order may count as **custom and non-returnable**. Offer the
  VA either a full charge with the parts held or reused, or a 10%
  restocking fee plus manufacturer shipping.
