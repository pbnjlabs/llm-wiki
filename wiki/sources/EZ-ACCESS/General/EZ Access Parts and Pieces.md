# EZ Access Parts and Pieces

One-page Visio quick-reference sheet (dated 9/12/2018) covering part
numbers and sizes for the whole EZ-ACCESS range PMS installs. No author
is stated. The part nicknames ("Moo-Abs" for the MUAB, "Muck-Bee" for
the MUCB, "Mr. Heeber" for the MRHBPR) suggest an **internal dealer/PMS
cheat sheet** rather than an EZ-ACCESS publication. Its figures come
from field practice, so it is treated as more reliable than the blog
articles for PMS work.

## Covers

- **Rubber thresholds (RT)**: TAEM 1.5-1 and 2.5-1 Angled Entry Mats;
  TMEM 2.5-2 Modular Entry Mat plus TMEMR 2.25 in riser squares; RT
  heights 1–5 in. See [[TRANSITIONS-Threshold-Ramps]].
- **Aluminum thresholds**: TAER12/24/36 Angled Entry Ramps with min/max
  rise. These **differ from the 2021 blog ranges**; see
  [[TRANSITIONS-Threshold-Ramps]].
- **Portables**: Singlefold AS and Trifold AS, 29.5 in wide, weight by
  length; the Top Lip Extension (3 in lip → 9 in). See [[SUITCASE-Ramps]].
- **Bridge plates**: MBP36/48/60 (8 in deep), MDBP (13 × 36 in). Use
  tie straps when there is no gap.
- **PATHWAY**:
  - ramp sizes 2–8 ft × 3 ft, 36 in standard or 48 in bariatric;
    **850 lb capacity**
  - overall width stack-up (36 in usable → 44 in at the feet)
  - standard and low-profile platforms; custom platforms max 5 × 5 ft
  - support tube lengths MSTS10PR–MSTS100PR; low-profile foot
  - transition plate depths
  - braces, connectors, end loops, gate
  - an annotated example layout showing where each connector goes

  All on [[PATHWAY-3G]].
