# Understanding ADA Ramp Requirements

EZ-ACCESS blog article (2026-04-15), clipped from the web. A plain-language
summary of ADA ramp rules — **not the ADA Standards themselves**; for a
real compliance job, check the 2010 ADA Standards and local code.

## Covers

- Slope: 1:12 max (12/24/30 in rise → at least 12/24/30 ft of ramp).
- Width: 36 in minimum clear width between handrails; wider recommended
  for hospitals, schools, public buildings, two-way traffic.
- Landings required at top and bottom of every run, at every change of
  direction, and every 30 ft of run / wherever the rise exceeds 30 in.
- Landings must be level (max 1:48), at least 60 × 60 in clear, and kept
  free of obstructions.
- Switchback ramps: adequate landing/turning space, consistent slope,
  continuous handrails.
