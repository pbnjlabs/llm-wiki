# PMS Ramp Assessment and Approval Forms (Part B)

The two PMS ramp forms that follow the
[[PMS Ramp Site Evaluation Form]] (Part A). Both blank, undated, one
page each. Filed with Part A under EZ-ACCESS/General. PMS's phone and
fax numbers are left out.

## Part B: Modular Ramp Assessment & Customer Agreement (`ramp_eval_B.pdf`)

**This is the drawing sheet Part A calls the "sheet provided."**

- Header: client details (never copied into the wiki), **evaluation
  date**, **evaluator**, **total rise**, **entrance** (front / rear /
  side / other), **threshold 1** and **threshold 2**, **mobility
  device**, **door swing** (in / out), **lower landing surface**.
- **Drawing grid, 1 square = 1 foot.** Draw the proposed ramp in
  relation to the home and landmarks. **Note obstacles such as the
  driveway, sidewalks, trees and shrubs.**
- **Customer agreement:** the customer agrees the proposed ramp system
  will meet their needs, and that as the property owner they agree to
  have it installed as shown.
- **Different owner:** check home property owner, apartment manager or
  other, and that person signs as well.

## Ramp approval sign-off (`ramp_appr.pdf`)

A separate client/veteran sign-off, signed by the client/veteran and
PMS:

- They've examined the **configuration sketch and location of the
  proposed modular ramp** and are satisfied.
- They've received the PMS evaluator's contact information.
- PMS will give the **VA all pertinent project information and their
  preferences**.
- **PMS is the company to call for maintenance or repair.**
- **Important: once the sketch and layout go to the VA and PMS receives
  a purchase order (VA approval), the ramp's layout and location cannot
  be changed.** Ramps are installed exactly as sketched.
