tables, an architect's specification (CSI-style Parts 1–4) and example
drawings. The newest PATHWAY 3G document on file. **Everything is rated
1,000 lb.**

- **Low-profile platforms** (4 × 5, 5 × 5, and an 8 × 5 made of two 4 ×
  5s): 69.2, 82.7 and 140.7 lb bare; **1-9/16 in minimum height**
  (4-9/16 in for the 8 × 5).
- **Expanded-metal 5 × 5:** 80.2 lb bare.
- Platform rails include a 1.5 × 2 in curb (not the vertical picket).

**Transition plates** (usable extension): upper **3.5 in**, lower **6
in**, ground **18 in**; 35-3/8 in wide (36 in ramp) or 47-3/8 in (48
in). **Low-profile ground transitions** (5 ft side, 59-1/8 in wide):
1:6 7.375 in, 1:8 9.75 in, 1:10 11.95 in, 1:12 14.13 in; **bridge**
(4 ft side, level) 7.375 × 47-1/8 in.

**Architectural spec** (Parts 1–3):

- Designed to the **2021/2022 IRC**: 40 psf uniform live load and a 300
  lb concentrated load, for ramps and platforms.
- 6061-T6 or 6005-T5 aluminum; grade 5 zinc-plated structural
  fasteners. Support tubes 0.094 in minimum wall, **7.375 in square
  polymer feet**.
- Surface continuous with **no gap over 1/4 in**; ramp treads 1.125 × 6
  in interlocking, extruded slip-resistant; platform decks knurled.
  **2 in minimum curb.** Top adapter plate at least 3 in, preformed at
  8–12°.
- **Handrails** 1.5 in, both sides, top of grip **30–36 in** above the
  ramp, intermediate rail **18–22 in**; inside rail continuous on turns.
  Mill finish (powder coat optional). Platform curb 1.5–2.5 in above the
  deck.
- **Pickets** (vertical 1 in square or horizontal 1.5 in round): **no
  gap over 4 in**.
- May need state or local approval and inspection. Shop drawings and a
  tread sample on request. Limited lifetime warranty.

## Disagrees with

- **Width stack-up:** the 2018 [[EZ Access Parts and Pieces]] sheet gives
  40-5/8 in outside the handrails and **44 in** outside the feet on a 36
  in ramp; this 2026 sheet gives 40-3/16 and **41-3/16 in**. Go by this
  sheet, and measure the parts on site when clearance is tight.
