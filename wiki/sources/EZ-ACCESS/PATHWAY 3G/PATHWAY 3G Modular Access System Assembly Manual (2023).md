# PATHWAY 3G Modular Access System Assembly Manual (2023)

77-page EZ-ACCESS assembly manual for the **PATHWAY 3G** (doc **16397
REV 08-15-2023**). It **supersedes the 2015 "Pathway" manual**
([[Pathway Modular Access System Assembly Manual]], P/N 10565), which
had a different part-number scheme ("M" codes) and an 850 lb rating.
This one uses the **"P" codes** PMS orders by (PRUT, PRLT, PSLxxPR...,
matching [[VA Ramp Bid Package Example (2026)]]). Where the two
manuals differ, this one wins.

## What changed from the 2015 manual

| | 2015 manual | **2023 manual** |
|---|---|---|
| Capacity | 850 lb | **1,000 lb** (residential only) |
| Part codes | M (MRUT, MSTS...) | **P** (PRUT, PSLxxPR...) |
| Ground transition | MRGT, 12 in, 2 pieces | **PRGT, 18 in, 3 pieces** (also 48 in wide) |
| Plan-view angle, upper transition | ~4° | **~3°** |
| Plan-view angle, lower transition + support top | ~8° | **~6°**, legs on both sides over 3° |
| Ramp slope | not stated | **1:12 ideal; 1:14 to 1:8 allowed** |
| Leg and bracket torques | not given | **20 ft-lb** bracket locknuts, **15 ft-lb** leg set screws |
| New items | — | Low-profile platforms and transitions, 45° angle platform, 8 × 5 turn-back, vertical and horizontal picket rails and closures, expanded-metal surface, ramp-without-handrails starter support |

## Covers

- **Front matter:** 1,000 lb; residential only; **must be installed by
  a qualified installer** and maintained per the manual and local code;
  **handrails are required**; manufacturer parts only; stay off it
  until assembly is complete; don't attach planters, lights or
  decorations; aluminum conducts electricity.
- **Tools** (p. 3): 1/2, 9/16 and 5/16 in sockets or wrenches, drill,
  #2 Phillips, pliers, 1/8 and 5/16 in bits, 5/32 and 3/16 in Allen
  wrenches, box knife, hammer, level, file, rubber mallet, 1/4 in
  masonry bit (concrete), digging tools, hacksaw (handrail kits), 25 ft
  tape, construction adhesive.
- **§1 Components** (pp. 5–11): solid or **expanded metal** surface;
  ramps without handrails or with two-line, vertical-picket or
  horizontal-picket rails. Ramp parts: PRGT/PRGT48 ground transition (18
  × 36 or 48 in), **PSLxxPR support leg pairs (10–100 in in 6 in
  steps)**, PRHBPR end bracket pair, PRRUC universal ramp-to-ramp
  connector, PRSUT universal support top, ramp hanger pair, PRHRLU /
  PRHRLL upper and lower end loops, PHREC end cap, PRUT upper and PRLT
  lower transitions (with corner protectors). Platform parts: straight,
  turn, 8 × 5 turn-back, **45° angle**, low profile; PPPC connector,
  PPTS tie strap, PRLWALP low-profile welded leg pair, PBP36/48/60
  bridge plates, PPLPGT06/08/10/12 and PPLPBT48 low-profile transitions.
  Closures: PPCTL two-line, PPCVP4/5/6 vertical picket, PPCHP
  horizontal picket. Options: PUAB angle brace, PUCB4/5/8 cross brace
  (4 ft, 5–6 ft and 8 ft platform sides), PTDK tie-down kit, PLP landing
  pad, PUG36 gate.
- **§2 Platforms** (pp. 12–24):
  - Outside set screws hold the support tubes; inside ones (one above,
    one below the deck) hold the handrail posts. Tip the platform on its
    side to fit the tubes, snug the **upper** set screw (3/16 in Allen),
    stand it up without loading the tubes, fit the feet pointing under
    the platform, level, then tighten the **lower** set screw.
  - **Tubes may stick up no more than 1/2 in above the corner pocket**
    (the post's lowest hole must stay clear): trim before fitting plugs.
  - Two platforms of the same width join with 2 PPPC connectors and
    tube stubs; a turn-back has 6 tubes.
  - **45° angle platform** for a 45° turn between ramps; use the
    low-profile foot under 4 in.
  - **Low-profile platform:** 1-9/16 in thick (standard is about 3-1/2
    in). Can sit on the ground as a landing pad. Hangers are PRHPLP.
    Both handrail set screws are above the deck. Ground transitions for
    the 5 ft side come in **1:6 (7.32 in), 1:8 (9.70), 1:10 (11.95) and
    1:12 (14.13 in)**, all 59-1/8 in wide; the 4 ft side has only a
    47-1/8 in bridge transition. **Only the 1:6 piece may be used as a
    bridge** (level, at least 1 in onto the landing, gap 6 in max,
    fastened through its two 1/4 in holes).
  - **Bracing:** walking surface **over 36 in** gets PUAB angle braces
    (36–71 in): 4 pairs per platform, 6 for two joined platforms, brace
    bands with 5/16 in hardware, 1/4 × 1 in screws into the side rail.
    **72 in and up** adds PUCB cross braces mid-height on all sides, top
    bands at least 2 ft above the bottom ones, pivot bolt snug, 11/32 or
    3/8 in holes about 1/2 in from each end.
- **§3 Ramps** (pp. 25–34):
  - Assemble face down on cardboard or grass; butt with no gap; 2
    center saddle brackets per joint, 4 × 5/16-18 × 1-1/2 in bolts
    each; **join at most 3 sections at a time**.
  - End brackets go in the **threaded insert closest to the ramp end**.
  - Hangers (PRHP) hook behind the platform side-rail lip; set the ramp
    with hangers close to its side rails.
  - **Support legs:** bracket on the saddle studs (round hole up,
    slot down), level it, **torque the locknuts to 20 ft-lb**; foot
    under the ramp, leg through the bracket, **set screws 15 ft-lb**,
    then thumb screw and plug. Low-profile PRLWALP feet where the end is
    too close to the ground. Concrete pad under feet on soft soil.
  - **Slope:** "ADA guidelines call for a maximum slope of 1:12 ... the
    ideal slope", but the ramp **may be installed from 1:14 to 1:8**
    (about 4–7°); never outside that range. **PMS builds 1:12 only**
    (house standard, [[Ramp-Slope-and-Length]]).
  - Sight down the run and keep sections parallel or the rails won't
    fit.
  - Angle brace under the ramp over 36 in; cross braces at 72 in.
  - **PRGT ground transition:** 3 sections; hook the upper one into the
    end tread, rotate down, slide on the middle and lower sections,
    #8-32 × 1/2 in thread-cutting screws (don't strip). Ground end only.
    Wood and concrete anchors included. On a landing pad use a PRLT.
  - **Single run to a porch:** PRUT at the top, overlapping as far as
    possible; PRGT at the bottom, or a transition plus PRST if the low
    end isn't on the ground.
  - **Anchor the PRUT:** wood, 1/4 × 1 in self-drilling screws; concrete,
    two 1/4 in holes **1-1/2 in deep** and mushroom-head pin anchors.
  - **Plan-view angle** to a platform or porch: PRUT up to **~3°**
    (overlapping at least 1 in); PRLT + PRST and legs up to **~6°**
    (legs both sides over 3°; PRLT always at least 1 in overlap).
- **§4 Handrails** (pp. 35–47): assemble bolts loose, tighten last; one
  rail each side, interchangeable; if the studs don't line up, the end
  bracket is in the wrong insert; PRRC connectors join rails (squeeze
  with pliers, seat with a mallet). Platform rails: pre-assemble angle
  posts (5/16-18 × 1 in bolts), drop into corner pockets (two-line curb
  rests on the pocket; picket post bottoms flush), 2 set screws per
  post, glue the angle caps. 8 × 5 turn-back and 45° platform rails
  (bolt-on stub rails, adjustable elbows, welded "H" extension, support
  bracket on the picket panels).
- **§5 Closures** (pp. 48–59) fill the gap from ramp rail to platform
  post. **With the ramp at the side, put the ramp rail post 1-1/2 to 2
  in from the inside of the platform post.** A centered ramp needs a
  second two-line closure (or extra vertical-picket hardware). Top rail:
  elbow in the ramp rail, measure to the post, **cut the tube 1/16 in
  short**, file, elbow half one end and threaded insert the other, then
  3/32–1/8 in pilot holes and #8-18 × 3/4 in screws about 1/4 in from the
  elbow. Curb and lower rail: closure bracket clamped 4 in above the
  deck, **cut 1/8 in short** for the inserts, 5/16-18 × 1-1/4 in
  self-drilling screw through the bracket slot. Picket closures are
  sized to the platform (4, 5, 6 ft). Turn-back closures: two-line for
  any size, vertical picket only on the 8 × 5.
- **§6 Final steps** (pp. 60–62): **2 ramp end clips at every
  ramp/platform joint** (1/8 in pilot, 1/4 × 1 in screw); end loops
  (O-ring, 1/4 in hex screw, #10 × 1/2 in screw about 1 in from the
  end); end caps on open rail ends; corner protectors at both ends;
  180-grit touch-up on architectural finish only; walk the system,
  clear chips, **re-check level and slope**, cover every rail end.
- **§7 Options** (pp. 63–75): PRST support top (replaces the end bracket;
  longer studs toward the ramp end); PRLT lower transition (either end;
  anchor it unless it's on the ground); **bridge plate flat only, gaps
  up to 6 in, 1 in bearing each side** (wood screws, or 1/4 in holes
  1-1/4 in deep and mushroom anchors in concrete); **landing pad** (PLP:
  contour the ground so it's fully supported, never elevated; bottom
  plate in one of three slots; ramp on the marked side; PRLT onto the
  pad with 2 screws); **tie straps** (gap 1/2 in max for ADA); lightning
  ground rod (within 1-1/2 ft of a tube, 2 in exposed, #8 copper);
  **tie-down kit** (same method as 2015: augers within 10° of vertical,
  brace band 2 in below the pocket, 3/16 in clamps, eyebolts in 5/16 in
  holes about 3/4 in above the side-rail bottom on a bare ramp; no
  concrete anchors in asphalt; not rated for specific soils or seismic
  zones, stamped calcs via EZ-ACCESS sales); 36 in gate (discard the
  latch bag's screws); joining a 45° platform to a standard one (sides
  up to 6 ft, 3–4 in in from one side, bridge plate between).
- **§8–9 Maintenance and deicing** (p. 76): same as the 2015 manual, plus
  the deicer table (see [[GATEWAY 3G Instructions and Technical Specifications]]).
  The 2015 manual's warranty page isn't repeated; see
  [[EZ-ACCESS Lifetime Warranty (2017)]].
