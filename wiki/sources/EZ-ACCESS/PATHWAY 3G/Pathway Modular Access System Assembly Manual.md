# Pathway Modular Access System Assembly Manual

49-page EZ-ACCESS installer's manual (P/N 10565, rev 05-12-15). It is
the first real manual in the EZ-ACCESS set; everything ingested before
it was blog articles. Branded **"Pathway®"** without "3G", but PMS
treats Pathway and PATHWAY 3G as the same product, so this is the
PATHWAY 3G install manual.

Front-page rules: **residential use only**, **850 lb capacity**, two
installers, leave the manual with the end user, and register the
warranty.

## Covers

- §1 **Components and part numbers**: ramp sections (with or without
  handrails), universal platforms (straight, turn, turn-back), transitions
  (MRGT ground, MRUT upper, MRLT lower), connectors, braces, feet and
  support tubes (MSTSxxPR, 10–100 in), bridge plates, gate, tie-down
  kit, landing pad. Tabulated on [[PATHWAY-3G]].
- §2 **Platforms**: support tubes and feet, height leveling with two
  set screws per corner pocket, joining two 5 × 4 ft platforms into a
  5 × 8 ft turn-back, and bracing thresholds (angle braces from 36 in,
  cross braces from 72 in).
- §3 **Ramps**: joining sections with center saddle brackets (at most 3
  joined at a time), hanging ramps on platforms (MRHP hangers), support
  legs and sag removal, the ground transition, a single ramp run to a
  porch, anchoring the upper transition (wood or concrete), and the
  plan-view angle limits (upper transition ~4°, lower transition + MRST
  ~8°; these are **horizontal** angles, not slope, per Fig. 3.19/3.20).
- §4 **Handrails**: one pair per ramp section, joining sections,
  platform rails, end loops (joiner or swaged types), end caps.
- §5 **Final steps**: clip every ramp to its platform (2 clips each),
  touch up the finish, final checks.
- §6 **Optional equipment**: handrail connectors (cut to fit on site),
  ramp support top, lower transition, bridge plate, landing pad,
  platform tie straps, lightning ground rod, **tie-down kit**, 36 in gate.
- §7 **Maintenance and safety** (p. 48), §8 **lifetime warranty** (p. 49).
