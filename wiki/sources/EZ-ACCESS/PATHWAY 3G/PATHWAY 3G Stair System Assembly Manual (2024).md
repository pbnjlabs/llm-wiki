# PATHWAY 3G Stair System Assembly Manual (2024)

19-page EZ-ACCESS manual for the **PATHWAY 3G Stair System** (doc
**14697 REV 10-01-2024**): aluminum steps that hang on a PATHWAY 3G
platform, attach to a deck or porch, or stand alone when anchored.

## Covers

- **850 lb** (lower than the ramps' 1,000 lb), residential only,
  qualified installer, **handrails required**, landings must hold 850 lb.
- **Sizes by rise** (pp. 2–3). Installing outside the code range may
  fail inspection:

| Treads | Code-compliant rise | Non-compliant max |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 8–14 in | 17-3/8 in |
| 2 | 15–21 in | 24-3/8 in |
| 3 | 22–28 in | 31-3/8 in |
| 4 | 29–35 in | 38-3/8 in |
| 5 | 36–42 in | 45-3/8 in |
| 6 | 43–49 in | 52-3/8 in |
| 7 | 50–56 in | 59-3/8 in |

- **Tools:** ratchet with 1/2 in deep socket, 1/8 in bit, 3/16 in Allen,
  #2 Phillips, tape, box knife, level, rubber mallet, file, drill.
- **Options:** PPSCTL platform-to-step closure (two-line; 36 in tubes and
  curb cut on site), PSSxxxx top leg pairs (xxxx = rise; for extra
  support or freestanding use), PUG36 gate.
- **Riser** (§2): treads into the right-hand side rail's channel cleats,
  ridges up, tapped in until the holes line up; 5/16-18 × 2-1/4 in
  carriage bolts; add the left rail; tighten until the cleat just clamps
  the tread (some cleat deformation is normal).
- **Legs and rails** (§3): stair hangers on the top posts (6 holes; the
  bottom posts have 4) at the hole **10 in from the bottom**, mirrored
  left and right. Set the riser so the gap from the bottom tread to the
  ground matches the top tread to the platform, treads level. Drill 1/8
  in through the hanger tabs into the platform side rail and fit 2 × 1/4
  in screws. **Leave at least 6 in between the platform post and stair
  top post if a PPSCTL closure will be used.** Handrail tubes run
  parallel to the side rail between closure elbows (pilot holes and #8-18
  × 3/4 in screws). **Anchor the bottom legs** (anchors not supplied)
  through the front holes.
- **Top leg pairs** (§5): tube through the riser pocket into the foot,
  drill 11/32–3/8 in and **fit the 5/16-18 × 3 in bolt and locknut**
  ("or a dangerous situation may result").
- **Gate** (§6): stair-to-gate adapter on a top post, 1/4-20 bolts, 5 in
  catch bolt, gravity latch on #8-32 screws (discard the bag's screws).
- **Freestanding** (§7): all four feet anchored.
- Maintenance and deicing as in the ramp manual.
