# LiteRider Envy LiNX Owner's Manual (GP162)

38-page owner's manual for the GP162 LiteRider Envy, rear-wheel-drive
power wheelchair, LiNX-controller variant (see
[[LiteRider PTC Service Guide (GP162, PG VR2 Controller)]] for the other
controller variant of the same model number).

Contents:
- Assembly (battery deactivate blocks must be removed before first
  power-on — same "shipping block" pattern as GL111/141's new battery
  packs), disassembly into 4 pieces for transport (23/36/27/37 lb)
- Comfort settings: seat height (2-position center post), arm width,
  armrest angle, footrest angle/height, joystick bracket length/position
  (joystick can be moved to either armrest)
- Operation: joystick, 10-step speed dial, horn, freewheel lever,
  thermal rollback (auto speed reduction on controller overheat, not a
  fault), main circuit breaker with manual reset button (wait ~1 min after
  trip)
- **LiNX diagnostics**: same 7-code flash-code table as
  [[LiteRider Envy LiNX Supplemental Insert (GP162)]] (remote/joystick,
  network/config, left/right motor, left/right park brake, module error).
  OONAPU (Out Of Neutral At Power Up) interlock: 5-second grace window to
  center the joystick before it escalates from a warning to an error
  requiring a power cycle. Drive-inhibit indicated by a right-to-left
  chasing light sequence on the battery gauge, distinct from the charge
  readout and from a fault flash code.
- Battery gauge: same 5-LED (1 red/2 amber/2 green) hardware as
  [[TruCharge-Diagnostics]]/GA541, but this manual's fault states (high
  voltage = all LEDs + flashing green, low voltage = leftmost flashing,
  cut-off = red flashing + status flash code 2 + horn beep every 10s) are
  LiNX-specific, not the TruCharge 10-bar scheme — a third distinct
  diagnostic vocabulary on top of TruCharge and Flash-Beep-Diagnostics,
  confirming diagnostics are controller-specific across Golden's whole
  catalog, not just within one model family.
- Battery charging: 8-hour full charge, 12-hour initial conditioning
  charge for new batteries, charge via joystick port or directly at the
  battery pack
- Technical specs: GP162, 300 lb capacity, 3.5 mph max, 40A controller,
  2×22AH batteries, 15.5 mile range
- **Warranty (Rev. 12.17) — "Consumer Limited Warranty, Power
  Wheelchairs"**: explicitly named as covering **LiteRider Envy GP162,
  Alante GP207, Alante Sport GP205, Compass Sport GP605, and Compass HD
  GP620** — a distinct warranty document from [[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]],
  confirming Golden maintains separate scooter vs. power-wheelchair
  warranty policies. Terms: 5yr frame (steel frame/welds/seat post — GP605
  frame is LIMITED LIFETIME), 13mo caster/anti-tip/armrest/footrest
  structural parts, 13mo drive train (motor/gearbox, brake electrical
  function), 13mo electronics (controller/charger/harness), 13mo
  bearings/bushings/rubber/plastic/seat swivel, 12mo batteries. Excludes
  tires, motor brushes, brake pads, shrouds, bulbs/fuses, upholstery,
  armrest pads, battery cases, and the usual misuse/unauthorized-repair
  carve-outs.
