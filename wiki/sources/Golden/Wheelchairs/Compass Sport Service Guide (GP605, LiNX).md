# Compass Sport Service Guide (GP605, LiNX)

26-page service guide (rev B, Sep 2017) for the Compass Sport GP605
**with the Dynamic LiNX controller**. The older GP605 supplements
(2010–2011) describe a **SHARK** controller, so GP605 has had at least
two controller families. **Check which one is fitted** before choosing a
code table.

- **18–32 VDC** system; **two U1 12V 35 Ah** batteries.
- Covers the LiNX battery gauge, LiNX cabling, remote and power-module
  connections, a connector key, wiring diagrams and battery placement.
- Scenarios: LED array won't light; won't charge (LiNX charging test).
  The status indicator shows flash code 2 or 7 when charging fails.
- **LiNX flash codes 1–7**, the same as [[LiNX-Diagnostics]]. Also covers
  drive inhibit and OONAPU (out of neutral at power-up).
- Replacement steps: drive wheel, front caster, drive train (motor/brake
  and gearbox), power module (mounted on the battery box).
- Voltmeter/ohmmeter appendices.
