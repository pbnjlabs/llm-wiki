# LiteRider PTC Service Guide (GP160)

50-page service guide (rev A) for the **discontinued** LiteRider PTC
GP160 power chair. It predates the GP162 LiteRider Envy. Downloads also
had a byte-identical copy named `GP160 LITERIDER PTC_SG_ REVA_ 082812.pdf`.

- **Controller:** **PG VR2** (joystick control unit, power module, bus
  cable). 18–24 VDC.
- **Batteries:** two 12V **18 Ah** (3052512100). Replace with 18 Ah.
- Motor resistance under 5 Ω, not shorted.
- Covers VR2 controls, locking and charging through the joystick's
  charger socket.
- Scenarios: LED array won't light; won't charge.
- **VR2 flash codes 1–10** (low battery, left/right motor disconnected,
  left/right motor wiring trip, charger connected, joystick trip, control
  system trip, solenoid brake trip, high voltage), plus the
  speed/profile indicator conditions and how to read the TruCharge
  gauge. See [[TruCharge-Diagnostics]] and [[PG-Drives-Trip-Codes]].
- Battery charging guidelines, electrical connections, battery wiring
  diagram.
- Replacement steps: drive wheel, rear caster, motor/brake, power module,
  batteries. Voltmeter/ohmmeter appendices and an IPB.

It is close to the [[LiteRider PTC Service Guide (GP162, PG VR2 Controller)]]:
same controller, same code table.
