# Alante Sport Service Guide (GP205)

50-page service guide (rev A) for the **discontinued** Alanté Sport GP205
power chair.

- **Controller:** **PG VR2, 50 A** power module (3051600070). It has a
  swivel seat and a joystick bracket.
- **Batteries:** two **U1, 12V 35 Ah** (3052512060).
- Motor resistance under 5 Ω, not shorted.
- Scenarios: LED array won't light; won't charge.
- **VR2 flash codes 1–10**, other VR2 status conditions, and the
  TruCharge gauge. See [[TruCharge-Diagnostics]].
- Battery charging guidelines and methods; electrical connections.
- Replacement steps: drive wheel, rear caster, motor/brake, power module,
  batteries. Meter appendices and a long IPB (pp. 26–48).
