# Alanté Series Owner's Manual (GP205, GP206, GP207)

46-page owner's manual covering the **GP205 Alanté Sport** and the
**GP206 / GP207 Alanté**. All three share one spec sheet; only the
Medicare codes differ.

**Specs (p. 38–40), each in CC (captain's) and SS (van/pan seat):**
300 lb, **front-wheel drive**, 3.5 mph, 16.5 mi, 1.75 in clearance,
25 in turning radius, **2-U1**, **50 A remote** controller, 39.5 × 22.25
in. **157 lb (CC) / 154 lb (SS) assembled**: base 67, seat 46 / 43,
batteries 22 lb each. 9 in drive wheels, 6 in casters, **4 in front
anti-tip wheels**. Medicare: GP205 K0823 / K0822; GP206 not coded;
GP207 K0816 / K0815. Warranty 5 yr frame, 13 months drive train and
electronics, 12 months batteries.

Other content:
- **Controller: PG VR2.** 10-LED flash codes 1–10 (the VR2 table on
  [[TruCharge-Diagnostics]]); lock/unlock sequence; VR2 modules not
  user-serviceable.
- Battery access: joystick out, seat off, battery cover off; two
  Velcro-strapped batteries on red/black connectors.
- **Freewheel:** pull the levers **forward** for freewheel, push them
  **back** for drive.
- Warranty list: Envy GP162, Alanté GP207, Alanté Sport GP205, Compass
  Sport GP605, Compass HD GP620 ([[Golden-Wheelchair-Warranty]]).
- Options listed include elevating leg rests and a rear basket.
