# LiteRider Envy Lite Owner's Manual (GP161)

42-page owner's manual for the **GP161 LiteRider Envy Lite**, a lighter
version of the [[GP162-LiteRider-Envy]].

**Specs (p. 35–36):** 250 lb, rear-wheel drive, 3.5 mph, 7.25 mi,
2.5 in clearance, 28.5 in turning radius, **2-18 Ah**, 50 A remote
controller, 33.25 × 22.5 in. **113 lb assembled**: front half 34.5,
rear half 30.5, seat 23, battery pack 25 lb. 8 in flat-free drive
wheels, 6 in casters. Seat 17 × 16 in, two heights. Warranty 5 yr frame,
13 months drive train and electronics, 12 months batteries.

Other content:
- **Controller: PG nVR2** (10-LED gauge). Flash codes 1–10 are the
  dual-motor VR2 table on [[TruCharge-Diagnostics]]; lock/unlock by key
  presses and joystick moves, as on the Alante (p. 27).
- **Disassembly:** seat, battery pack, then lift the frame off the
  drive train with the locking handle. Battery replacement means opening
  the pack: **AGM or gel only**.
- **Freewheel:** push the levers **down** for freewheel, pull **up** for
  drive.
- Thermal rollback; main circuit breaker with reset button **under the
  battery box handle** (wait about 1 min, then reset).
- Warranty list: Envy Lite GP161, Envy GP162, Alante Sport GP208,
  Compass Sport GP605, Compass HD GP620 ([[Golden-Wheelchair-Warranty]]).
