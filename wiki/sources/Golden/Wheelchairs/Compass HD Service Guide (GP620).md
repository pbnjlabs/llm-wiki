# Compass HD Service Guide (GP620)

25-page service guide for the Compass HD GP620. It covers chairs fitted
with the **Dynamic Shark** controller (REMA and REMD remotes) and the
Dynamic **A-Series**. This is the first document on file with a
**Shark fault-code table**. Shark is a Dynamic Controls product, not a
separate brand.

- **Batteries:** two **U1 35 Ah** or two **NF-22 55 Ah**.
- Power harness with the **main circuit breaker**; Shark power module
  and joystick module; swing-away joystick.
- Connector keys for the whole electrical system.
- Scenarios: LED array won't light; won't charge (Shark charging test).
- **Battery load test:** probe the two outside pins of the charger port
  and drive with someone in the chair. After an initial drop the voltage
  should hold at about **24 VDC or higher**. If it keeps falling,
  replace **both** batteries.
- **Shark flash codes 1–11** on the amber wrench LED, plus battery-gauge
  conditions (locked, charge inhibit, OONAPU) and joystick calibration
  with the DX-HHP programmer. See [[Shark-Diagnostics]].
- **Motor test:** 0.3–50 Ω on a cold motor. **Controller motor
  outputs:** about 22 kΩ. **Park brake:** 40–80 Ω.
- Replacement instructions; voltmeter/ohmmeter appendices.
