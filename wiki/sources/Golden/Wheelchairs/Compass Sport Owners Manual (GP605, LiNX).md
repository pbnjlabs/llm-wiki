# Compass Sport Owner's Manual (GP605, LiNX)

36-page owner's manual for the **GP605 Compass Sport** (center-wheel
drive). The first GP605 owner's manual on file.

**Specs (p. 11), CC (captain's) / SS (van/pan):** 300 lb, 4 mph,
**24 mi on 2-U1 or 27.5 mi on 2-40 Ah**, 3.25 in clearance, 24 in
turning radius, **40 A** controller, 39.5 × 24 in, max incline 6°.
**185 lb (CC) / 179 lb (SS) assembled**; base 97, seat 40 (pan) or 46
(captain's), batteries 22 lb (U1) or 29 lb (40 Ah) each. 10 in center
drive wheels, four 6 in casters. Seat height 3 positions, 1 in steps.
Medicare K0823 (CC) / K0822 (SS). Warranty: **lifetime frame**,
13 months drive train and electronics, 12 months batteries.

Other content:
- **Won't power up** until both battery harnesses and the power harness
  to the power module are plugged in and locked (shipping). A
  **battery connection diagram** is inside the rear battery cover.
- Seat: line up the stinger, push down and rotate to lock; ~46 lb with
  arms. Backrest lever folds the back forward on its own; brace it.
- **Controller: Dynamic LiNX (LiNX LE)**: status-light flash codes 1–7,
  OONAPU, drive-inhibit chase, 10-step speed dial, 5-LED gauge with
  high/low-voltage warnings: [[LiNX-Diagnostics]].
- **Freewheel:** pull the levers **forward** for freewheel, push **back**
  for drive.
- Warranty list: Envy GP162, Compass Sport GP605, Compass HD GP620 (GP605
  frame limited lifetime) ([[Golden-Wheelchair-Warranty]]).
