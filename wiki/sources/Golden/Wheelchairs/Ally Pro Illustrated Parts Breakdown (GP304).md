# Ally Pro Illustrated Parts Breakdown (GP304)

7-page IPB (LIT-GP304-IPB, Rev. B). Shares the **joystick (MBE-0055),
battery pack (MBE-0060, 24 V 12 Ah), charger (MBE-0059)**, armrest
headlight/USB unit, seat cushion and drive wheels with the [[GP303-Ally]].
Its own parts: **controller MBE-0068**, main harness MBE-0067, drive
motors MBX-0009 (L) / MBX-0010 (R), red brake handle MBH-0075, **omni
casters MBW-0038**, battery tray MBB-0063, arm-width thumb screws
MBH-0071, rear shrouds with taillights, front shrouds in teal or grey.
