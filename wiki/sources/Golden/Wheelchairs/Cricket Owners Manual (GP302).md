# Cricket Owner's Manual (GP302)

28-page owner's manual (LIT-GP302-OM, 03.12.2024) for the Cricket
rear-wheel-drive folding power chair.

- **Specs:** 300 lb capacity, 3.7 mph, **12 Ah 24 V lithium-ion**
  battery, 2 A off-board charger, **35 A Micon controller**, 39.3 lb
  assembled, 8.5 in drive wheels, 1.6 in anti-tip wheels, freewheel
  levers on both sides.
- **Folding:** the frame and footplate fold.
- **Charging:** plug into the socket on the front of the controller, or
  into the battery pack after removing it. The charger LED is **blue
  while charging and green when full**. The gauge runs left to right
  while charging, and driving is inhibited. Charge 5–8 h after each use.
  If it won't be used for more than a week, charge it fully and remove
  the pack.
- **Max incline 6°.**
- **Beep codes:** two beeps, then the code count. See
  [[GP302-Cricket#Beep codes (Micon controller)]].
- **Warranty:** 5 yr frame, 13 months drive train and electronics,
  12 months batteries.
