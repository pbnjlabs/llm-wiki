# BuzzAbout Illustrated Parts Breakdown (GP164)

8-page IPB; the parts tables are images (electrical page read
visually).

- **Dynamic LiNX:** power module **MBC-605L-CNT-PMOD** (the GP605L's),
  controller cable MBC-605L-CNT-CAB, joystick **MBC-162B-CNT-DLX
  (DLX-REM060-A, the GP162B's)**; program ZZ-PROGRAM-164.
- Batteries 12 V 22 Ah (MBE-BATT1222-BW), 35 A breaker MBE-B35A-BREAK,
  3.5 A charger MBE-CHG35A-STD.
- Lights: light control module MBE-164-LCM and light harness.
- Front and rear frame battery harnesses.
