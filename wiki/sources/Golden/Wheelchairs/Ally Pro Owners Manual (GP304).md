# Ally Pro Owner's Manual (GP304)

36-page owner's manual (LIT-GP304-OM, Rev. C; undated, date from the
PDF). Same platform and electronics as the [[GP303-Ally]], bigger and
heavier.

**Specs (p. 11):** **330 lb**, rear-wheel drive, 5 mph, 13.1 mi,
2.5 in clearance, 37.4 in turning radius, 1 × 24 V 12 Ah lithium
(5.4 lb), 40 A remote controller, 38.9 × 25.2 in. **75 lb assembled**,
heaviest piece 69.6 lb. 10 in pneumatic drive wheels, **10 in
omni-directional casters**. Seat 21.1 × 16.9 in. Warranty 5 yr frame,
13 months drive train and electronics, 12 months batteries.

Differences from the Ally:
- **Adjustable arm width** (two screws under each arm).
- **Reclining backrest:** pull the two pins on the lower back, recline,
  make sure the pins relock.
- Same folding, cruise control, novice mode, app, headlight/USB,
  joystick extension and left-side swap as the GP303.

Fault codes: [[Ally-Fault-Codes]]. The code table's code 90 text says
**265 lb**, which contradicts the 330 lb spec (it's copied from the
Ally Sport); the spec sheet wins.
