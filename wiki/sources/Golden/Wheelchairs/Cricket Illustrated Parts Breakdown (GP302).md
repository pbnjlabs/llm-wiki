# Cricket Illustrated Parts Breakdown (GP302)

5-page IPB (LIT-GP302A-IPB, rev G). Drawings: seat and footrest frame,
seat and back, side components (motors, wheels, anti-tip, fenders),
electrical.

**There are two electrical versions:**

| | GP302**A** | GP302**B** |
|---|---|---|
| Controller | **Micon** (MBE-0034) | **LiNX** (MBE-0051, spelled "Linix") |
| Joystick | Micon MBE-0035 | LiNX MBE-0050 |
| Battery pack | 24V 12 Ah Li-ion MBE-0010 | 24V 12 Ah Li-ion MBE-0054 |

The charger is MBE-0014 (listed as "Li-Ion, 12V2A, GP301A & GP302A").
Left and right motor assemblies are MBD-0003 and MBD-0004. Drive wheel
MBW-0008, anti-tip wheel MBW-0009, caster MBW-0007.

The owner's manual only covers the Micon (A) version. On a GP302B, use
[[LiNX-Diagnostics]] instead of the Micon beep codes.
