# Compass Sport Circuit Breaker Wire Routing Upgrade (GP605)

1-page, under-5-minute field upgrade: reposition the front circuit
breaker so its wires route to the inside rather than the original factory
orientation (17mm socket to unthread/rethread the breaker cap; don't
over-tighten on reassembly). Implies an early-production wire-chafe/routing
issue on GP605 that Golden corrected via field retrofit rather than a
running design change alone.
