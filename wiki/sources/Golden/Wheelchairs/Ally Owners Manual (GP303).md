# Ally Owner's Manual (GP303)

36-page owner's manual (LIT-GP303-OM, Rev. B) for the **GP303 Ally**
folding power chair.

**Specs (p. 12):** 330 lb, rear-wheel drive, 4.4 mph, 9.3 mi, 2.5 in
clearance, 28.5 in turning radius, **1 × 24 V 12 Ah lithium** (5.4 lb),
**40 A remote** controller, 41 × 24.5 in. **56.4 lb assembled**,
heaviest piece 49.5 lb. 10 in pneumatic drive wheels, 6 in solid
casters, 2 in anti-tips. Seat 17 × 16 in. Max incline 6°. Warranty
5 yr aluminum frame, 13 months drive train and electronics, 12 months
batteries.

Other content:
- **Folding:** press the frame release latch (either side works both),
  lift the push handle and push in to the footplate. Unfold until it
  clicks. Power off and empty the basket first.
- **Joystick:** power, speed up/down, lock (press power once when on),
  horn, **SOS** alarm (hold), **Fn = novice mode** (speeds 1–2 only).
  **Cruise control** (hold speed-up at speed 1 until two chimes).
- **Display fault codes and voice prompts:** [[Ally-Fault-Codes]].
- **Phone app** (Bluetooth 4.1, AES-128): drive the chair from the
  phone, set speed, sleep time, cruise control enable, voice volume,
  speed memory, remote power-on. Pairing check: both screens show the
  same code.
- **Freewheel:** two levers at the back, push **down** to unlock. The
  display says "Brake Open" and it won't drive until they're locked.
- Battery: lockable latch on each side; charge via XLR at the joystick or
  the removed pack (charger red/green). Store at 32–122 °F, pack out.
- Left armrest: headlight, USB-A and USB-C. Joystick extends 1.5 in
  (two 0.75 in steps) and moves to the left arm.
