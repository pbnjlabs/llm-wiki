# Stride Owner's Manual (GP301)

28-page owner's manual (LIT-GP301-OM_04.29.2024) for the **GP301
Stride**, a folding rear-wheel-drive power chair.

**Specs (p. 11):** **300 lb** (but see the conflict below), 3.7 mph,
9.3 mi, 1.6 in clearance, 23.6 in turning radius, 24 V 10 Ah Li-ion
pack (3.8 lb), 2 A charger, **50 A** controller, two 24 V 200 W motors.
42.5 × 24.4 × 38.6 in; folded 15.7 × 24.4 × 32.7 in. **58 lb
assembled** (p. 13 says "approximately 55 pounds"). Flat-free tires:
12 in drive, 8 in casters, 2 in anti-tips. Sling seat 17 × 18 in.
Warranty 5 yr frame, 13 months drive train and electronics, 12 months
batteries.

> [!warning] Capacity conflict
> The spec table says **300 lb**; the safety page and the beep-code
> table both say **265 lb**. Use 265 lb until Golden confirms.

Other content:
- **Battery harness behind the pack must be connected** or it won't
  power up. Pack slides onto a rail under the seat and latches.
- **Folding:** power off, freewheel levers in **drive (forward)**, push
  the backrest down, pull the latch, fold. Both **anti-tip brackets
  must be extended and locked** for the folded chair to stand.
- 5-LED battery gauge; charger LED **blue** while charging, green when
  full; driving inhibited while charging; charge at the controller
  socket or the removed pack.
- **Beep codes** (horn beeps twice, then the count) 1–9: identical to
  the Micon table on [[GP302-Cricket]].
- Warranty names "Stride GP301" (metal frame and welds 5 yr):
  [[Golden-Wheelchair-Warranty]].
