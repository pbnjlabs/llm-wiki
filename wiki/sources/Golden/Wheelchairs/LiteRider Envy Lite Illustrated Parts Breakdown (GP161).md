# LiteRider Envy Lite Illustrated Parts Breakdown (GP161)

15-page IPB.

- **Controller: PG nVR2 40 A**: joystick MBC-JSNVR2-40, power module
  MBC-PMNVR2-40 (the owner's manual says 50 A). Program ZZ-PROGRAM-GP161.
- Batteries **12 V 18 Ah** (MBE-BATT1218-BW); 12 V 22 Ah also listed.
- Motors with gearbox and brake: MBD-161-MTR-LGB / -RGB.
- Shared parts: brake handle WE01P121 (GP160 LiteRider PTC), battery
  box and connectors from the GP162B, **35 A breaker MBE-B35A-BREAK**
  (Buzzaround GB106), **3.5 A charger MBE-CHG35A-STD**, joystick
  mounting kit for LiNX and Shark.
