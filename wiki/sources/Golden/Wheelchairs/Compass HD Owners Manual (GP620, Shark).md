# Compass HD Owner's Manual (GP620, Shark, 2017)

38-page owner's manual for the **GP620 Compass HD with the Dynamic
SHARK controller** (the file name says "Conpass"). The 2019 LiNX
edition is [[Compass HD Owners Manual (GP620, LiNX)]].

**Specs (p. 11), SS / CC:** 450 lb, **4 mph, 12 mi**, **75 A**
controller, 2-NF22. Everything else (size, 223 / 226 lb, seats,
footrest, Medicare codes, warranty) matches the 2019 edition. The
controller change brought a lower amp rating (75 → 60 A) but higher
speed and range.

Other content:
- **SHARK flash codes 1–10** (p. 26), read from the SHARK information
  gauge. The numbering differs from the service guide's 11-code table;
  see [[Shark-Diagnostics]].
- OONAPU isn't a fault: let go of the joystick.
- Freewheel: turn the levers as shown (figures 18/19).
- Warranty list: Envy GP162, Alanté GP207, Alanté Sport GP205, Compass
  Sport GP605, Compass HD GP620 ([[Golden-Wheelchair-Warranty]]).
