# BuzzAbout Owner's Manual (GP164, LiNX)

40-page owner's manual for the **GP164 BUZZ™ABOUT** (BuzzAbout), a
rear-wheel-drive take-apart power chair.

**Specs (p. 34–35):** 300 lb, 4 mph, 11 mi, 2.5 in clearance, 28.5 in
turning radius, **2-22 Ah**, **40 A** remote controller, 36 × 23 in.
**155.35 lb assembled**: front half 29.2, rear half 33.85, seat with
arms 34.8, battery pack 28.75 lb. 9 in flat-free drive wheels, 6 in
casters. Captain's seat 17 × 16 in, three heights. Warranty 5 yr frame,
13 months drive train and electronics, 12 months batteries.

Other content:
- **Controller: Dynamic LiNX (LiNX LE)**. Status-light flash codes 1–7,
  OONAPU and drive-inhibit chase: [[LiNX-Diagnostics]].
- Main components include a **headlight/taillight bypass switch** and
  a **circuit breaker reset button**; seat swivel lever.
- **No tools** needed to assemble. Disassembly: joystick cable off,
  seat, battery pack, then lift the frame off the drive train with the
  locking handle. Battery replacement means opening the pack: **AGM or
  gel only**.
- **Freewheel:** push the levers **down** for freewheel, pull **up** for
  drive.
- Warranty list adds **BuzzAbout GP164** to the GP162/GP605/GP208/GP620
  list ([[Golden-Wheelchair-Warranty]]).
