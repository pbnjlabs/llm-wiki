# Carry On Power Chair Owner's Manual (GP130)

28-page owner's manual (LIT-GP130-OM, Rev. B; undated, date taken from
the PDF). The **GP130 is a power chair**, not the GB120 Carry On
scooter, though both are sold as "Carry On".

**Specs (p. 11):** 300 lb, **center-wheel drive**, 3.85 mph, 12.5 mi,
2 in clearance, 21.25 in turning radius, one 12.5 Ah Li-ion pack (6 lb),
2 A off-board charger, 40 A controller, 38 × 24 in. **74 lb assembled**:
base 46, seat 22, battery 6. Drive wheels 7.75 in, casters 5.5 in.
Warranty 5 yr frame, 13 months drive train and electronics, 12 months
batteries.

Other content:
- **Fuse must be installed before first use** (p. 13): pull the battery,
  open the controller cover under the basket, fit the fuse in its slot.
- **Folding seat post** (two spring pins). Seating the seat on the post
  also makes the **seat's electrical connection**, so a seat not fully
  locked down can cause a no-drive.
- **Freewheel:** two red levers in the **center** of the chair, one per
  motor; push **back** to unlock. It won't drive until both are locked.
- Charge at the XLR port on the joystick or on the removed pack; charger
  LED red while charging, green when full. 5–8 h after each use.
- **Fault codes** (p. 21): joystick lights flash the count, some with a
  continuous horn. Table on [[GP130-CarryOn-Power-Chair]].
- Warranty: the power wheelchair text naming "CarryOn GP130"
  ([[Golden-Wheelchair-Warranty]]).
