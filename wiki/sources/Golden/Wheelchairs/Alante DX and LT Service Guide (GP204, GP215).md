# Alante DX and LT Service Guide (GP204, GP215)

38-page service guide (rev A) for the **discontinued** Alante DX (GP204)
and Alante LT (GP215) power chairs.

- **Controller:** **PG VR2**. Parts: VR2 joystick 3023-0021-01, power
  module 3023-0020-07, wiring harnesses, extension cord. 18–24 VDC.
- **Batteries:** Alante DX uses **two 22 Ah** (WP22-12NE, in battery box
  A3064-0021). Alante LT uses **two 18 Ah**.
- **40 A circuit breaker**, part P3026-0033-01. The IPB calls it the
  breaker "for **Compass Jr (Alante DX)**", so the Alante DX was also
  sold as the **Compass Jr**.
- Motor resistance under 5 Ω, not shorted.
- Scenarios: LED array won't light; won't charge (VR2 charging test and
  battery-box charging test).
- **VR2 flash codes 1–10** and the TruCharge gauge. See
  [[TruCharge-Diagnostics]] and [[PG-Drives-Trip-Codes]].
- Replacement steps: drive wheel, rear caster, motor/brake, power module,
  batteries. Meter appendices and an IPB (pp. 27–36).
