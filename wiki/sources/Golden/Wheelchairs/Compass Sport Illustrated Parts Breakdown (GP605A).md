# Compass Sport Illustrated Parts Breakdown (GP605A)

Two editions (Jun 2022, Dec 2023) of the 10-page IPB for the **GP605A**.
The electrical pages are images (read visually).

- **Dynamic LiNX:** controller **DLX-REM050-A** (MBC-605L-CNT-DLX),
  power module MBC-605L-CNT-PMOD, controller cable 1.5 m; program
  ZZ-PROGRAM-605M; **Bluetooth LiNX dongle** MBC-DONGLE-DLR-DLX; Dynamic
  programmer MBX-DXHHP.
- Main harness with **50 A breaker** WE03A603; battery cables SE06A612 /
  SE06A614 (shared with the Companion GC240/340/440).
- Batteries **U1** (MBE-BATTU1-BW) or **12 V 40 Ah** (MBE-BATT1240);
  3.5 A charger.
- Motors with gearbox and brake RA03E102 (L) / RA03E103 (R); brake
  handle MBD-605L-HB; drive wheel MBW-605A-DWA.
