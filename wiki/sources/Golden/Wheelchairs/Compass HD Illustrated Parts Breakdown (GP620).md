# Compass HD Illustrated Parts Breakdown (GP620)

7-page IPB; tables are images (electrical page read visually).

- **Dynamic Shark:** joystick **REMD** (MBC-DYNSJ1) and **75 A Shark
  power module** (MBC-DYNSC75).
- Batteries **22NF** (MBE-BATT22-BW); **8 A charger** MBE-CHG80A-STD;
  main harness with circuit breaker MBE-COCBH (also Compass GP600M/620M
  and Alante GP201); Dynamic programmer MBX-DXHHP.

> [!note] Shark or LiNX?
> This 2024 IPB lists only the Shark, but the 2019 GP620 owner's manual
> is the LiNX edition. Check the joystick before ordering.
