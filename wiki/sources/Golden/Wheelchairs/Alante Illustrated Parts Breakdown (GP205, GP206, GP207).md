# Alante Illustrated Parts Breakdown (GP205, GP206, GP207)

9-page IPB.

- **PG VR2** module MBC-AJPGVR2; joystick MBX-AJVR2JA (also on the
  Alante Jr. GP200 and Alante DX GP204); intermediate joystick cable
  SA77974. Programmer: **PP1 (Penny & Giles)**. Program ZZ-PROGRAM-GP205.
- Motors with gearbox and brake RA04E103 (L) / RA04E104 (R); brakes
  MBD-BRAKE605L/R (Compass Sport brake assemblies).
- Main harness with circuit breaker WE04E601; **U1 batteries**
  (MBE-BATTU1); **3.5 A charger** MBE-CHG35A-STD.
- Joystick mounting hardware for LiNX and Shark is listed too (shared
  with the Compass).
