# Compass HD Owner's Manual (GP620, LiNX, 2019)

39-page owner's manual for the **GP620 Compass HD with the LiNX
controller** (the file name says "LiXN"). See also the 2017 Shark
edition, [[Compass HD Owners Manual (GP620, Shark)]].

**Specs (p. 11), SS / CC:** **450 lb**, **4.5 mph, 21 mi**, 3.5 in
clearance, 20 in turning radius, **2-NF22** (37 lb each), **60 A**
controller, 40 × 25 in, max incline 6°. **223 lb (SS) / 226 lb (CC)
assembled**; base 101, arms 14, seat 34 (pan) or 37 (captain's).
Center drive 10 in, four 6 in casters. 5 seat-height positions in
0.5 in steps; seats 20 × 20 up to 24 × 20 in; footrest height 3–7 in,
angle 0–35°. Medicare K0824 (SS) / K0825 (CC). Warranty 5 yr frame,
13 months drive train and electronics, 12 months batteries.

Other content:
- **LiNX** status-light codes 1–7, OONAPU, drive inhibit:
  [[LiNX-Diagnostics]].
- Freewheel: **turn** the levers as shown in figures 18/19 (no
  direction in the text).
- Thermal rollback and a main circuit breaker.
- Warranty list: Envy GP162, Compass Sport GP605, Alanté Sport GP208,
  Compass HD GP620 ([[Golden-Wheelchair-Warranty]]).
