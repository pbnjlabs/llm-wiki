# Ally Sport Owner's Manual (GP305)

36-page owner's manual (LIT-GP305-OM, Rev. B) for the **GP305 Ally
Sport**, the **power-folding** member of the Ally family.

**Specs (p. 12):** **265 lb**, rear-wheel drive, 3.7 mph, 9.5 mi,
2.5 in clearance, 30 in turning radius, **24 V 10 Ah lithium** (4.6 lb),
40 A remote controller, 42.3 × 24.7 in. **92.1 lb assembled**,
heaviest piece 87.5 lb. 10 in pneumatic drive wheels, 10 in omni
casters. Seat 16.7 × 16.7 in; footrest angle-adjustable. Warranty
5 yr aluminum frame, 13 months drive train and electronics, 12 months
batteries.

Other content:
- **Battery ships disconnected:** remove the dunnage under the pack and
  the cap on the connector, refit the pack and latch it.
- **Power folding:** stand beside it, power on, hold the two fold
  buttons 3 s (voice prompt), tap the joystick. Touching the joystick
  again cancels; restart the sequence. Nobody on it, kids and pets
  clear.
- Novice mode is on the **W** button here (Fn on the GP303/304).
  Joystick extends **2 in in 0.5 in steps**.
- Display shows a **fault-status icon**; codes on [[Ally-Fault-Codes]].
- Freewheel: two levers at the back, push **down** to unlock.
