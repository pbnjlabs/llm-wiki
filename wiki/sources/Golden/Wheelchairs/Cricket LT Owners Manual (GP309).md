# Cricket LT Owner's Manual (GP309)

24-page owner's manual (LIT-GP309-OM, Rev. A; undated, date from the
PDF) for the **GP309 Cricket LT**, a lighter carbon-fiber folding chair
than the [[GP302-Cricket]].

**Specs (p. 12):** 300 lb, rear-wheel drive, 3.7 mph, 9.5 mi,
**1.25 in clearance**, 29.5 in turning radius, 24 V 10 Ah lithium
(**2 lb**), **35 A** controller, 36 × 24 in. **25.8 lb assembled**.
11 in drive wheels, 6.5 in casters, 2 in anti-tips. Seat 18 × 18 in.
Warranty 5 yr frame, 13 months drive train and electronics, 1 yr
battery.

Other content:
- **Unfold:** both hands flat on the seat edges, push down (never put a
  hand between seat and frame). **Fold:** peel back the seat pad's
  Velcro edge and pull up on the two strap handles. The backrest folds
  in half (release levers on the push handles); footrests fold.
- Joystick fits either arm (retaining knob); arms flip up with the red
  button.
- **Battery ships disconnected:** hook the two lower tabs inside the
  frame and rotate up until it clicks. Charge at the joystick's or the
  pack's XLR port; charger LED **blue** charging, green full. Press the
  pack gauge's button twice to read charge off the chair.
- **Freewheel:** two **red** levers at the back, push **backward** to
  unlock.
- **Beep codes** (horn beeps twice, then the count): table on
  [[GP309-Cricket-LT]]. Close to the Micon table, but 1 and 8 differ.
- The seat belt **must** be used. Seat and back fabric inspection is a
  required maintenance item.
- No warranty section in this manual; terms are on the spec page only.
