# Alanté Sport Owner's Manual (GP208)

42-page owner's manual for the **GP208 Alanté Sport** (the file name
misspells it "Aante"). Different from the GP205 Alanté Sport: lighter,
faster and with a 40 A controller.

**Specs (p. 36):** 300 lb, **front-wheel drive**, 4.3 mph, 15 mi,
2.25 in clearance under the foot support, 28 in turning radius,
**2-U1**, **40 A** controller, 41.5 × 25 in. **146.5 lb assembled**:
base 66, seat 37, batteries 21 lb each. Front tires 230 × 75, 6 in
casters, 2.5 in front anti-tip wheels. Captain's seat 18 × 18 in.
Medicare K0823. Warranty 5 yr frame, 13 months drive train and
electronics, 12 months batteries.

Other content:
- **Controller: PG VR2** (10-LED flash codes 1–10, lock/unlock):
  [[TruCharge-Diagnostics]].
- **Freewheel is the reverse of the GP205–207:** push the levers
  **back** for freewheel, pull them **forward** for drive.
- Warranty list: Envy GP162, Compass Sport GP605, Alanté Sport GP208,
  Compass HD GP620 ([[Golden-Wheelchair-Warranty]]).
