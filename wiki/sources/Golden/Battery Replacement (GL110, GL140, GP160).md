# Battery Replacement (GL110, GL140, GP160)

1-page procedure (rev B, LR_BATT_REPLACEMENT_REVB_031412). Explicitly
covers LiteRider (GL110/GL140) **and** LiteRider PTC (GP160) — same
battery pack design. GP160 isn't ingested yet; cross-reference for that
future ingest.

Steps: remove battery pack → loosen circuit breaker button cover with
adjustable wrench → remove 8 screws → open pack, noting battery harness
position exactly → disconnect/replace top and bottom 12V batteries in
series (bottom terminals down, top terminals up) → reconnect harness/
breaker → reassemble. Warns: replace inline fuse only with exact
type/rating; connect per wiring diagram to prevent shock.
