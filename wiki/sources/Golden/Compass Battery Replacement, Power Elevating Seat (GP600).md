# Compass Battery Replacement — Power Elevating Seat (GP600)

3-page, two-person procedure specific to GP600 units with the **power
elevating seat** option (the elevating seat adds a 4-post detent-pin
lift/receiver system that must be raised, decoupled, and lifted clear
before the batteries underneath are reachable — a plain non-elevating
Compass wouldn't need this). Key steps: raise seat, power down, disconnect
controller cable and arm assemblies, expose battery cover via side locking
tabs, unplug motor M+/M- from the controller only (leave the 2-pin
connector), pull all 4 seat-rail detent pins, lift the entire seating
system off (2-person lift), cut the factory battery shipping tie-wrap,
swap batteries observing polarity, reverse the process — seat-post
realignment (loosening 2 bolts per post under the power-seat cover) is
called out as the most likely sticking point on reassembly.
