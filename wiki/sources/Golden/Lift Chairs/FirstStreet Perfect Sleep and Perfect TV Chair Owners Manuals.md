# FirstStreet Perfect Sleep Chair and Perfect TV Chair Owner's Manuals

Two 16-page manuals for chairs Golden built "exclusively for
**firstSTREET**": **The Perfect Sleep Chair** (Jul 2019, 6-button
control) and **The Perfect TV Chair** (May 2017, AutoDrive control).
Both are MaxiComfort chairs: two 9 V batteries, **26 in** wall
clearance, **375 lb**, Golden's standard lifetime / 3-year / years 4–7
warranty.

**Perfect TV Chair sound system:** built-in speakers fed by a **TV audio
transmitter** (plugs into the TV's red/white audio-out jacks; 5 V 1 A
adapter). Turn the system on at the panel **hidden in the left arm**,
press Mode until the light is **blue**, listen for pairing beeps, and set
the TV to external speakers. Keep the chair within **32 ft** of the
transmitter, unobstructed.
