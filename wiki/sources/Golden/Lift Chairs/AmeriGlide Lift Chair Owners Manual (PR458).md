# AmeriGlide Lift Chair Owner's Manual (PR458)

12-page owner's manual (MSWARB-AG 06/19) for the **PR458** sold by
**AmeriGlide**. 2-button control, two 9 V backup batteries, **18 in**
wall clearance. **Capacities:** PR458-SMA 300 lb; MED, LAR, S23, M23
375 lb. Warranty in AmeriGlide's name: lifetime mechanism and frame
(labor 3 yr), 3 yr electrical parts (labor 1 yr), no years 4–7; claims
approved by AmeriGlide.
