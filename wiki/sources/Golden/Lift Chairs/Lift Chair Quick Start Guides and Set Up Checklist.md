# Lift Chair Quick Start Guides and Set Up Checklist

Three sheets:

- **Set Up Checklist** (2015), signed by the dealer and customer:
  inspect with the customer and note damage; confirm model, size and
  color; set the wall distance, power, leg levelers and a full recline;
  run every function and option; explain SmartTek; review the backup
  batteries (yearly, and after any use); teach the hand control, options,
  getting in and out, seated when not in use, the weight limit; show the
  manual and warranty; single-person use.
- **PR511 quick start** (LIT-511-566-SKN-QS, Rev. A): a chair with **air
  hoses** (air massage) and heat pads. Join the back (red/black locks,
  a twist-lock circular connector, color-matched hoses, two heat-pad
  wires), route wires between the back bar and the seat (behind the back
  bar on the Small). **Needs two outlets:** the right cord to the
  transformer with lithium backup, the left "Accessory" cord to a
  second transformer.
- **PR721 Perfect TV Chair** quick start (2015): 26 in from the wall,
  9 V batteries supplied, the TV audio transmitter (blue mode light,
  within 32 ft) and a **convenience table** that folds out of the right
  arm.
