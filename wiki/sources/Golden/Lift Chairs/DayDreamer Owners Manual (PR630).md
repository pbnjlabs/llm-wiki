# DayDreamer Owner's Manual (PR630)

16-page owner's manual (MSWARB630-051117) for the **DayDreamer PR-630**
with **Power Pillow**. A MaxiComfort chair with the AutoDrive Head Rest
control (S5/S6 headrest, memories S8/S10). **375 lb**, **25.5 in** wall
clearance, two 9 V backup batteries in the transformer. UL 73 and CSA
C22.2 No. 68 listed. Standard lifetime / 3-year / years 4–7 warranty.
