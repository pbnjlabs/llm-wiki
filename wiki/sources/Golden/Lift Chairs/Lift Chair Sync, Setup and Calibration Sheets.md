# Lift Chair Sync, Setup and Calibration Sheets

Five one- and two-page sheets. **After replacing any motor, control
box, hand control or cable, the sync must be done.**

- **3-Way Sync** (Jan 2019, 2-motor 3-way chairs): hold **S2** until the
  motor stops, then **S3** until it stops, then **S2 + S3** together
  until the backlight goes out; release, the lights come back on and the
  hand control is reset. Both motors must be fully retracted.
- **Maxi Sync** (Jan 2019): AutoDrive: hold **S4**, then **S2**, each
  until the motor stops. AutoDrive with cradle: **S4**, **S2**, then **S5**.
- **Troubleshooting for 4- and 5-Zone Recliners**
  (INF-AutoDrive-GT-11.11.2021): the **November 2021 revision** of
  [[AutoDrive Hand Control Resets and Calibrations]]. Check power (lit
  hand control, green transformer and cord LEDs, control box
  connections); **no buttons work** → hold M 4 s, press RISE
  (disable off); **only RISE and RECLINE work** → M 4 s, then A, E, C, ZG
  (function lock off); factory presets → M 4 s, A, B; still short →
  calibrate (5-zone: hold **A + C + D**, 4-zone: **A + C**, until it
  stops, then **5 more seconds**). Verify with SIT.
- **Initial Hand Control Setup** (LIT-GT-3100-3200, Aug 2020): ZK3100-C5M
  (5 motors: hold three buttons) and ZK3200-HC (hold two), then 3 more
  seconds.
- **ZKAD-4 / ZKAD-HR insert** (Aug 2016): the S7 and SP buttons were
  renamed; **old-style** disable is SP then S7; **new-style** is hold SP
  4 s until it flashes, then S7.
