# Relax The Back Lift Chairs Owner's Manuals (PR126/631, PR128, PR130, PR805)

Golden-built chairs sold by **Relax The Back (RTB)**, five files, four
documents. The **PR126/PR631** manual is on file twice, byte for byte
(under Private Labels as "RelaxBack" and under Archived as
"DreamMaker"); one page covers both.

| Manual | Chair | Clearance | Capacity | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PR126 / PR631 (Jul 2019) | "The Zero Gravity Lift Chair" (MaxiComfort) | 25.5 in | 375 lb (PR-126M, PR-126L, PR-631) | AutoDrive HR (headrest) control |
| PR128 (Jun 2018) | Zero Gravity Lift Chair with **Twilight** | 27 in | 375 lb (MED, LAR) | **Hand-pump air lumbar**: squeeze the black bulb to firm, press the button between bulb and hose to deflate |
| PR130 (Apr 2021) | Zero Gravity Lift Chair with **Twilight** | 27 in | 375 lb (MED, LAR) | **ZK3100-C5M**: setup = hold S1, S3 and S11 (twilight in) + 3 s; headrest; lumbar S15/S16 |
| PR805 (Jul 2019) | **Bordeaux** | 19.5 in | 300 lb | 4-button: back up/down, footrest up/down |

**Warranty:** the zero-gravity chairs carry Golden's standard lifetime /
3-year / years 4–7 terms. The **Bordeaux** has its own: lifetime on the
metal frame, scissor mechanism and springs; 3 years on the motor,
electrical parts and seat cushion; 1 year on the wood frame; labor for 1
year "with a labor service contract".

**RTB Limited Warranty Protection Plan** (all four, 12 months from
purchase): Golden provides **in-home service**, parts, labor and freight;
if it can't be fixed to the RTB franchisee's satisfaction, a replacement
chair. Repair at home first; if a returned chair turns out not to be a
factory fault, the franchisee pays a **15% restocking fee** and freight.
Service must be authorized by Golden (800-624-6374).
