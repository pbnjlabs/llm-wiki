# PL340 Lift Chair Owner's Manual

16-page owner's manual (PL340-100419) for the **PL340**, an unbranded
private-label chair ("the manufacturer"), with heat and massage.

- **PWR button must be on** for anything to work: press once (green), and
  a **20-minute timer** starts; press again to restart it. Hold PWR 4 s
  to **lock** (red) or unlock (blue, then press PWR again).
- Up/down arrows; **MSG** (vibration) and **Heat** buttons, each with a
  20-minute shut-off; heat and vibration pause while the chair moves.
- Two 9 V backup batteries, **17 in** wall clearance, **375 lb**.
- **Warranty:** lifetime on the lift mechanism, frame and recline
  mechanism; **1 year** electrical parts; labor subsidized 1 year; **no
  in-home service** on this model.
