# MaxiComfort Lift Chairs Owner's Manual (2021)

28-page owner's manual (MSWARBMC-060821) for all **MaxiComfort**
(zero-gravity, 4-position) chairs: **PR510, PR512, PR535, PR632, PR713,
PR735, PR756 and PR766**. Filed by Golden under "Archived".

- **Models and capacities:** PR535-PSA and PR766-SMA **300 lb**; PR535
  MED/LAR/TAL, PR510 SME/MLA, PR512-MLA, PR632, PR713, PR735-MLA, PR756MC,
  PR756L, PR766 MED/LAR **375 lb**; **PR535-M26 500 lb, PR535-M28 600 lb**.
- **Wall clearance about 25 in** for all MaxiComfort models.
- **Power:** transformer only, 9 V backup, or lithium pack, by model.
  "**No battery backup for PR632**."
- **PR535-M28** has a **knock-down (KD) back**; **PR535-M26** uses the
  6-button 3-motor control (same as the PR502).
- Hand controls: **AutoDrive** (memory buttons), **AutoDrive Head Rest**
  (PR512: S5/S6 headrest, memories S8/S10) and **AutoDrive Head Rest &
  Lumbar** with the **ZK3200-HC** setup/reset (hold **S1 backrest down +
  S3 footrest up**, then 3 more seconds). PR535, PR632, PR735 and PR766
  route the hand control through the inside arm (J-clips).
- **Warranty:** Golden's standard lift chair terms
  ([[Golden-Lift-Chairs]]): lifetime lift/recline mechanism and frame
  (labor subsidized 3 yr), 3 yr electrical parts (labor 1 yr),
  years 4–7 pro-rated.
