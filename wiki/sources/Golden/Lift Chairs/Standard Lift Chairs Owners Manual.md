# Standard Lift Chairs Owner's Manual (2020)

20-page owner's manual (MSWARB-GT-CHR-OWN-110420) for Golden's
**standard 2-button lift chairs**: PR200, PR355M/L, PR401SME/MLA,
PR501 (JP, S, S-23, M, L, T, M26, L26, T28), PR531 (PSA, MED, LAR, TAL,
M26, T28, S23) and **PR931-MED**.

- **Wall clearance** (wall and corner): PR200 11 in; PR355M 17, L 18;
  PR401SME 18, MLA 19; PR501JP/S/S-23/M 17, L 18, T 19, M-26D 17,
  L-26D 18, T-28D 19; PR531-PSA and S23 16, MED 17, LAR 19, TAL 21,
  M26 18, T28 21; **PR931-MED 5 in** (wall hugger).
- **Wide dual-motor models** (PR501M26/L26/T28, PR531-M26/T28) use an
  **inline 9 V battery holder** zip-tied to the rear of the frame, and a
  bolt-on back (7/16 in flange nuts).
- 2-button control (S1 up, S2 down), 1 A USB.
- **Capacities:** Jr. Petite and Small 300 lb; Medium, Large, Tall
  375 lb; S23 wide 375 lb; **dual-motor 26 in and 28 in wide 500 lb**;
  Capri 325 lb; Cambridge 375 lb; PR931-MED 350 lb.
- **Warranty:** standard lifetime / 3-year / years 4–7, except the
  **Capri and Monarch** (6 months labor; Monarch 2 yr and Capri 1 yr
  electrical; no years 4–7).
