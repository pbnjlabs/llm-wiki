# Massage and Heat System Manual

2-page operating sheet (undated; date from the PDF) for the lift chair
**massage and heat** hand control.

- **PWR** once: lumbar massage starts in SELECT mode, medium intensity
  and speed. Zones: **1 lumbar, 2 seat**. Massage stops on its own after
  **15 minutes**.
- Modes: **SELECT** (pick zones), **WAVE** (motors run up and down),
  **PULSE** (on/off). INTENSITY and SPEED +/− (hold for big changes;
  speed works in WAVE and PULSE).
- **Heat:** LO (amber) or HI (red), with or without massage, **30-minute
  timer**; press LO or HI again to continue.
- Use only the supplied power supply; no outdoor use; check the skin
  under the heated area.

If massage or heat is dead, the Universal manual says check the wiring
from main power to the **junction box** and from the junction box on
([[Golden-Lift-Chairs]]).
