# Member's Mark Lift Chair Assembly Manual (PL830)

16-page assembly and owner's manual (REV_121819) for the **PL830**,
sold under **Member's Mark** (support 866-335-0886). The text
calls it an **UltraComfort** chair, not Golden, even though it is in
Golden's library.

- Components: base, back, AC plug, transformer, hand control. The back
  slides onto the steel posts and locks; a flat screwdriver on the black
  tab in each sleeve releases it.
- Connect the **power pillow** black connector and lock it.
- 4-button control: up/down, **HEAD** up/down. Two 9 V backup batteries.
  Allow **20 in** to recline. Inside-arm hand control routing.
- No warranty text or capacity in the manual.
