# PR502 Owner's Manual (2019)

20-page owner's manual (MSWARB5 071119) for the **Model 502**, Golden's
**700 lb** bariatric lift chair.

- **Two transformers** with **four 9 V batteries**; both must be in a
  working outlet. The back installs from a separate sheet in the arm
  cover bag.
- **20 in** wall clearance.
- **3 motors**, 6-button control: S1/S2 back, S3/S4 footrest down and
  lift, S5 lower then footrest up, S6 close and lift from recline (all
  three motors stop when the back is closed and the chair is at full
  height).
- Includes a parts diagram (recline mechanism RM502, lift mechanism
  LM502 and others).
- **Warranty is short:** **2 years** on the lift and recline mechanisms
  and frame (labor subsidized 2 yr), **1 year** on motors, control box,
  power cord, transformers and hand control (labor 1 yr).
