# Lift Chair Footrest and Lift Options

Four sheets:

- **Footrest extension** (2017 and Nov 2021 editions): extension board
  on left/right brackets bolted to the footboard and scissor mechanism.
  Properly installed, the extension sits **slightly lower** than the
  footboard when open.
- **5 lb footplate weight** (BB5632, 2012): screwed to the footrest to
  help it **close for very light users**.
- **Straight lift option** (May 2021, from serial **GT2001RAP01783**):
  makes the chair lift straight up. Per side: pull hairpin A and detent
  pin B, move stick C up, refit the pin. All 2-position, 3-position and
  MaxiComfort chairs; **not Twilight models or the PR502**.
