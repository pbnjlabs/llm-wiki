# Discontinued Lift Chair Service Guides (115/165, PR116, PR117/119)

Three short service guides (14–20 pages) from Golden's "Discontinued
Models" folder, in the same format as the [[Lift Chair Service Guide]]:
components (transformer and motor about **27 V DC**; extension
open-circuit test), general troubleshooting, tools, replacement steps,
wiring, installation, operation and boxing.
