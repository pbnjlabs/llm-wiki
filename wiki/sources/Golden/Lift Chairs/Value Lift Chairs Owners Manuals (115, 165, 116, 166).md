# Value Lift Chairs Owner's Manuals (115/165, 116/166)

Two 12-page manuals from Golden's **private label** folder, written as
"the manufacturer" with no brand: **Models 115 and 165** (Dec 2015) and
**Models 116 and 166** (Nov 2017). Simple 2-button chairs, two 9 V
batteries in the transformer, **300 lb**.

| | 115 / 165 | 116 / 166 |
|---|---|---|
| Wall clearance | 17 in | 18 in |
| Back | KD brackets | Bolt-on (7/16 in nuts) |
| Warranty | **1 year** on lift, frame, recline and electrical; **labor not covered** | **Lifetime** lift and recline mechanisms; **1 year** wood frame and electrical; **labor not covered** |
