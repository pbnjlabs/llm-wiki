# AutoDrive Hand Control Resets and Calibrations

A one-page sheet (P/N MSWSADRR-GT, file INF-AutoDrive-10.06.2021) for
Golden's **AutoDrive** power lift chairs. These are 4- and 5-zone
recliners. Revised Nov 2021 ([[Lift Chair Sync, Setup and Calibration Sheets]]). This was the **first lift chair procedure document** in the
wiki.

It covers three procedures:
1. **Power Disruption Calibration.** Use it when the backrest won't
   return fully upright or the footrest won't fully extend after a power
   disruption. Also do it after replacing electrical parts, or when a
   motor falls out of sync with the control box. Chairs built **before
   2021** are more sensitive to power fluctuations. Where outages are
   common, Golden suggests a separate battery backup (UPS).
2. **Position Accuracy Calibration.** Use it when the preset buttons
   (REJUV, SIT, SLEEP, TV, ZG) drift from where they were set. The chair
   goes through its full range of motion to Trendelenburg and relearns
   its positions.
3. **Factory Default Reset.** Use it when presets were lost in a power
   disruption. The other choice is to reprogram each button, per the
   owner's manual.

The button sequences differ by hand control. There are three:
ZK3100-C5M (PR515, PR761), ZK3200-HC (PR632, PR446, PR448) and ZKAD-5
(PR510, PR766, PR535). The **ZKAD-5 can't be power-disruption
calibrated in the field.** See [[Golden-Lift-Chairs]].
