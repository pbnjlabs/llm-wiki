# Lift Chair Control Box and Parts Replacement Sheets

Nine replacement sheets (2017–2021).

- **Control boxes GM3100-CB (5 motor) and GM3200-CB (4 motor, cradle
  port sealed: don't open it)** and **GMC3M-CB** (PR514, PR128): hook and
  loop into the metal protection plate, against the frame, cables and
  24 in ty-wraps routed **exactly as shown** (wrong routing affects
  operation and can damage parts), then the setup/sync procedure and a
  full function test watching for pulled cables.
- **PR130** (5-motor Twilight): parts list (lift GM3100-LM, back
  GM3100-RM, cradle GM3100-CM with Hall sensors; control box GM3100-CB;
  hand control ZK3100-C5M; extension ZKAD-EXT-1; power supply
  GM2100-TR). **Control box motor plugs are color coded: grey = lift,
  green = back, yellow = cradle, red** (fourth). Calibration after any
  electrical part. Back (headrest and lumbar cords on red/black locks),
  motors, LM5124 frame (it can spring open: pinch hazard), seat and arms.
- **PR514** back (clevis pin on the back motor tip, three flange nuts a
  side), seat and arm replacement. **PR931** (wall hugger) KD back, seat
  and arms.
- **PR805** Bordeaux: footrest and back recline motors (unplug the two
  cables, pull two detent pins); touch pad (cable tab faces down);
  optional hand control ZK805-2 plugs into the control box phone jack;
  legs screw in (rear legs curved, front straight).
- **Right-to-left hand control conversion**: re-route the extension
  along the left side of the lift frame (mark the old ty-wrap spots with
  tape), restaple, move the wire clamp and the black hook.
