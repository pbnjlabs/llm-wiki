# MaxiComfort with Twilight Owner's Manual (PR514, PR405, PR761, 2020)

24-page owner's manual (MSWARB514+405+761-0120), the earlier edition of
[[MaxiTwilight Owners Manual (PR515, PR405, PR761)]] with the **PR514**
in place of the PR515. Same Twilight cradle, AutoDrive with
Twilight-Control (headrest and lumbar), 24 in wall clearance and
**375 lb** capacities (PR514-MLA, PR405-MLA, PR761-MLA). **Two 9 V
batteries** go in the transformer. Standard lifetime / 3-year / years
4–7 warranty. The newer PR515 edition adds the ZK3100-C5M setup step.
