# PR446 and PR448 Owner's Manual (2021)

20-page owner's manual (MSWARB-PR446-PR448-041621) for the **PR446-MED,
PR446-LAR and PR448-MLA**, which use the **ZK3200-HC** AutoDrive Head
Rest & Lumbar control.

- **Install:** KD back onto its brackets, plug the **red and black**
  connectors (red to red) and lock them, zip the back to the seat. No
  battery backup is mentioned.
- **Wall clearance:** PR446 21 in, PR448 24 in.
- **Hand control routing:** unclip from the J-clip, unlock and separate
  the extension (this disables the chair), route through the other
  J-clip, reconnect and lock.
- **Setup/reset:** hold **S1 backrest down + S3 footrest up** until it
  stops, then 3 more seconds. If it still misbehaves: wrong buttons,
  let go too early, or power lost during setup; repeat.
- **Buttons:** S7 RISE, S5 SIT/HOME, presets S6/S8/S9/S10 (ZG, TV, SLEEP
  and others), micro adjust S1–S4, headrest S11/S12, lumbar S13/S14,
  SP = M (program).
- **Program a preset:** hold SP about 3 s until the backlight flashes,
  then within 5 s press S5, S6, S8 or S10. **Factory reset:** hold SP
  about 4 s, then S1, then S4 (each within 5 s). **Disable:** SP 4 s,
  then S7 (repeat to enable). **Function lock** (only S7 and S9 work):
  SP 4 s, then S1, S2, S3, S6. **Lights out:** SP, then S1, S2, S5.
- **PR448 extras:** wireless charger, storage compartment, USB, **power
  headrest and lumbar**, large device mount.
- **375 lb** (all three). Standard lifetime / 3-year / years 4–7
  warranty.
