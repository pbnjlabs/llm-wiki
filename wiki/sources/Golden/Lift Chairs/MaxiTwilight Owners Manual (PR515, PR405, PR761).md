# MaxiComfort with Twilight Owner's Manual (PR515, PR405, PR761, 2021)

24-page owner's manual (MSWARB515+405+761-041621) for the **Twilight**
chairs: a **cradle** that tilts the whole seat back ("Twilight"),
giving TV, lounge, zero gravity, rejuvenate and Trendelenburg positions
in or out of twilight.

- **Capacities 375 lb** (PR515-SME, PR515-MLA, PR405-MLA, PR761-MLA).
- **Wall clearance** PR515-SME 23 in, PR515-MLA 24, PR405-MLA 21,
  PR761-MLA 24. No battery backup is mentioned in the install steps.
- **PR405:** simple 4-button control (S1 up/lift, S2 down/recline, S3/S4
  cradle in/out) and a 1 A USB in the pocket.
- **PR515 / PR761:** **AutoDrive with Twilight-Control**, headrest and
  lumbar (S15/S16), **ZK3100-C5M**. **Setup/reset: hold S1 backrest down,
  S3 footrest up and S11 TWILIGHT in** until it stops, then 3 more
  seconds. Hand control routes through the inside arm.
- Warranty: Golden's standard lifetime / 3-year / years 4–7 terms.
