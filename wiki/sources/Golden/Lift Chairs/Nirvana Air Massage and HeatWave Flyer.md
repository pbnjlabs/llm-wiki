# Nirvana Air Massage and HeatWave Flyer

2-page product flyer (LIT-SHT-NIRVANA-GT, Rev. A) for the **Nirvana
Swedish Air Massage** with **HeatWave far-infrared heat**, an option on
Golden lift chairs. Marketing, not a manual, so it carries less weight.

- **10 air chambers** over the lumbar, glutes and hamstrings; zones Back,
  Seat or Both; **Mode 1** gentle, **Mode 2** longer stretching massage;
  massage runs in **15-minute cycles**.
- **HeatWave:** far-infrared from shoulders to feet; starts low, can go
  high; timer defaults to **30 minutes**, up to an hour.
- Separate massage and heat on/off buttons on the remote.
