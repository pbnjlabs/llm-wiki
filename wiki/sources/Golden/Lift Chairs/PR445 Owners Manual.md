# PR445 Owner's Manual (2019)

20-page owner's manual (PR445-WARB-071119) for the **PR445-MED**: a
2-motor chair with a 10-button control: up/down, backrest (S3/S4),
footrest (S5/S6), headrest (S7/S8), lumbar (S9/S10); 1 A USB.

- Install: two 9 V batteries in the transformer; the KD back drops onto
  its brackets, then plug the **red and yellow** connectors (red to red,
  yellow to yellow) and lock them; zip the back to the seat fabric.
- **375 lb**, **21 in** wall clearance. Hand control cord clips into the
  quick-release clamp above the magazine pocket.
- Standard lifetime / 3-year / years 4–7 warranty.
