# Lift Chair Service Guide (2021)

98-page service guide (GT_SG_08.05.2021) for Golden's 2-position,
3-position, independent and MaxiComfort lift chairs. **The first Golden
lift chair service guide on file.** Parts orders: Golden tech support,
lift chair/bed tech **x502**, parts@goldentech.com; have the model and
serial number ready. Several pages are images (Smart Tek troubleshooting
p. 46, motor comparisons p. 24–25, grease points p. 92–93); they were
read visually.

Contents, all distilled onto [[Golden-Lift-Chair-Service]]:
- **Components with test values:** transformer about **27 V DC** at the
  chair; control box (500 and 700 lb chairs) **27–32 V DC**; heat pad
  **about 40 Ω**; massage unit **about 13.8 V DC and 25 Ω**; extension
  wire tested for continuity. Everything is replace-only.
- **General troubleshooting** (motor heard or not heard) and the **Smart
  Tek** LED table by model group (p. 46).
- **Models quick reference** (p. 8–9): lift frame, recline bar, motors,
  transformer, hand control and scissor mechanism for about 60 models.
- **Replacement procedures:** hand control, extension, back, control
  box, lift motor, lift frame, arms, scissor mechanisms, recline bar,
  seat, recline straps, **fork head** (left-hand thread), spacer bar,
  headrest motor and pillow (PR630/632), PR502 scissors, LM5124 cradle
  frame (Twilight).
- **Electrical diagrams with part numbers:** single, dual and triple
  motor; PR505/535-M26; MaxiComfort (with headrest, with Twilight);
  3-way Twilight (PR405); wall hugger (PR931); 5-motor (PR515/761).
- **Heat and massage generations** HV3000, HV3100, HV3200, HV4000,
  HV4100, HV4200 with cut-in dates; AutoFlex lumbar; Shiatsu.
- Okin old vs new lift motors; Richmat GM1400-LM vs GM1450-LM.
- Power connections and battery backup wire; back installation; grease
  points and recline-bar lubrication; boxing instructions.
