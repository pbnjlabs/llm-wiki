# Lift Chair Back Assembly Sheets

Six one-page back sheets (2012–2019):

- **2-button chairs:** footrest about level to expose the brackets, slide
  the back's studs into them, 7/16 in flange nuts, back up, Velcro.
- **MaxiComfort** (2012, 6-button 2015, AutoDrive 2016): same, plus pin
  the **back recline motor tip** to the back recline bar (clevis pin and
  hair pin).
- **PR502:** semi-recline, slip the back into the bracket slots, bolts
  with washers (BB5591, BB5592, BB5617) through the wooden back frame
  into the weld nuts, tighten the slot bolts.
- **PR931 and 544:** KD brackets: lift the tabs to remove; push down
  until it clicks to install.
