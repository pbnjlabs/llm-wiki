# PR404 Owner's Manual (2019)

20-page owner's manual (PR404-WARB-071119) for the **PR404-MED-SPC**:
a lift chair with a **6-button control**: up/down (S1/S2), headrest
(S3/S4) and lumbar (S5/S6). **375 lb**, **17 in** wall clearance,
two 9 V backup batteries. Standard lifetime / 3-year / years 4–7
warranty.
