# PR118 and PR202 Owner's Manuals (2021, 2023)

Two editions: the **Jan 2021** manual for the PR118 and PR202
(MSWARB-GT-PR118+PR202-122320, 17 pages) and the **Jan 2023** manual
for the PR118 alone (MSWARB-GT-PR118_01.18.2023, 20 pages).

- 2-button chairs with a 1 A USB; two 9 V batteries in the transformer;
  the hand control plugs into a lock at the back of the seat.
- **Wall clearance:** PR-118 17 in, PR-202 20 in.
- Capacity table is the standard-chair one (Jr. Petite/Small 300 lb,
  S23 375, dual-motor 26/28 in 500, PR931 350, Cambridge 375).
- **Warranty:** lifetime frame and mechanisms, but **only 6 months
  subsidized labor** on the PR118 (and PR202 in 2021), and labor on
  electrical parts limited to 6 months.

> [!warning] PR118 electrical warranty contradicts itself
> The 2023 text says "The Monarch and PR118 chair has electrical parts
> warranty of 2 years. The PR118 is limited to 1 year electrical parts
> warranty." Check with Golden before promising either.
