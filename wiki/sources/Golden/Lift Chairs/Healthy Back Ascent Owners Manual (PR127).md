# Healthy Back Ascent Owner's Manual (PR127)

16-page owner's manual (MSWARB-HB 06/19) for the **Ascent**, a
MaxiComfort chair sold by **Healthy Back** (model PR127). AutoDrive
control, two 9 V backup batteries, **25 in** wall clearance, **375 lb**.
Warranty is issued in Healthy Back's name: lifetime lift/recline
mechanism and frame (labor 3 yr), 3 yr electrical parts (labor 1 yr),
**no years 4–7**. Claims must be approved by Healthy Back.
