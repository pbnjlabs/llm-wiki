# PR117 and PR119 Owner's Manual (2020)

16-page owner's manual (117+119-060519) for **Models 117 and 119**.
Written as "the manufacturer", not Golden.

- 2-button control; two 9 V batteries in the transformer.
- **PR117:** bolt-on back (7/16 in flange nuts), **18 in** wall clearance.
- **PR119:** back with a **back motor**: rotate the back recline bar,
  bolt it on, then fix the motor tip to the recline bar with the clevis
  pin and hairpin clip. **22 in** wall clearance.
- **375 lb** each.
- **Warranty: one year** on the lift, frame, recline mechanism and
  electrical system (repair or replace).
