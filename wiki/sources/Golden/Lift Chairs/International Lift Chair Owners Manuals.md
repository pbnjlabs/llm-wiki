# International Lift Chair Owner's Manuals (2004, 2015)

Two older manuals from Golden's "International" folder.

**English/French (Mar 2004, bilingual, for US/Canada):** the old
single-switch chairs.
- **Thermal overload:** 5–6 continuous cycles can trip the motor's
  internal thermal protector; wait **10–15 minutes** and it resets.
  "Chair will not operate" therefore includes a hot motor.
- **Motor keeps running and won't stop:** defective switch or motor.
- 12 V UL/CSA transformer, two 9 V backups, about **14 in** to recline.
- Optional **Tranquil-Ease HC-3703H** massage and heat.
- 2004 warranty: lifetime / 3-year / years 4–7, with **Capri and
  Monarch** limits; capacities: small 300 lb, medium–tall 375 lb,
  **dual-motor 26 and 28 in wide 450 lb** (500 lb in the 2020 manuals),
  Capri 325, Imperial and Relaxer 375.

**French (Nov 2015):** a French-market manual ("Fauteuils releveurs")
with French model names and wall distances in **cm**, e.g. PR501 Confort
Plus 43 cm, PR907 dos au mur 15 cm, PR502 W33 XXXL 51 cm. 2-button
single-motor and multi-button dual-motor controls with ZG and TV
presets. Not for US chairs.
