# Universal Lift Chair Owner's Manual (2024)

32-page owner's manual (MSWARB-GT-UNV_03.11.2024) that covers **every
current Golden lift chair**, whatever the model. It has **no specs,
weight capacities or warranty**: the weight limit is on a **label on the
rear of the lift frame**, next to the serial number. The hand-control
pages are photos; the button sequences below were read from them.

**Intended use:** an electric positioning chair for medical purposes,
in homes, hospitals and nursing homes.

**Power** (three set-ups, by model): transformer only (a UPS can be
added); transformer with **two 9 V batteries** (backup, not included);
or transformer with a **rechargeable lithium pack** that slides onto it
and charges while the chair is plugged in. Both backups only bring the
chair back to seated once. **Replace 9 V batteries after one use and
every year.** The transformer supplies **29 V DC**. Plug into a
115–120 V outlet; no extension cords.

**Wall clearance:** about **18 in** for 1-zone chairs and **25 in** for
2-, 4- and 5-zone chairs; recline it fully once to check.

**SmartTek power lights:** green LEDs on the transformer and on the
motor-to-transformer cord, plus a backlit hand control. Lights out =
check connections and the outlet; lights on but a fault = call the
retailer and say which light is out.

**Back installation:** three kinds: bolt-on (7/16 in flange nuts),
bolt-on with a back motor (clevis pin and hair pin on the motor tip),
or **KD brackets** (push down until it clicks; lift the tabs to remove).
**Frame extensions** (rotate out, refit the detent clevis pins) for
users uneasy when reclined.

**Hand controls:** 2-button, 6-button, and the backlit RISE / RECLINE /
ZG or ZG+ / TV / HOME / SLEEP or REJUV control (ZG+ and REJUV on chairs
with a **cradle**, which also tilts). Optional HEAD and LUMBAR buttons.
Details and every M-button sequence: [[Golden-Lift-Chairs]].

**Calibration** (required before first use, and after outages, surges or
an abrupt unplug): hold **backrest down + footrest up** (+ the cradle
button on cradle chairs) until it stops, then **3 more seconds**. Chairs
without a control box can't be calibrated.

**Features:** storage compartments, **2 A USB**, cup holders, wireless
charger, device mount, power grommet.

**Basic troubleshooting** (p. 27–28): dead chair (transformer
unplugged, switched outlet, breaker, transformer LED out → try another
outlet → replace; **hand control disable** left on); partial function
(damaged hand control, unplugged motor or control-box cable, out of
calibration, **function lock** on); presets wrong (factory reset); no
backlight (**lights-out** feature on); stops mid-lift (outage, out of
calibration, wire pulled loose, backup battery flat); heat/massage dead
(junction box wiring); rocking (leg levelers, obstruction).
