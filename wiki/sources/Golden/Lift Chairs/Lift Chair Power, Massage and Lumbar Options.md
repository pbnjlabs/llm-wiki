# Lift Chair Power, Massage and Lumbar Options

Five sheets:

- **YH combo pack** (Dec 2021): transformer **YH-D290020-A** (100–240 V
  in, **29 V DC 2 A** out) and **lithium backup YH-B1600B** (11.1 V,
  2200 mAh, 24.42 Wh) slide together. LED **red charging, green full**.
  Sealed, not repairable. **Recharge the backup every 3 months and
  charge/discharge it every 6 months**; store charged and separated.
  Charge at 32–113 °F.
- **Heat/vibrate (DHV) power supply:** the heat/vibrate wire is behind
  the back rail on the **right** side; it plugs into its own supply
  and wall outlet.
- **AutoFlex** automatic lumbar (2013): Autostart 1, 2 or 3 presses
  for three cycling programs (about 12 min), Autostop, manual +/−.
- **Shiatsu** massage (2014): on/off, timer 5–30 min (default 15),
  rolling and target modes; replacing the unit (cable on the left), its
  control box and extension (details on the service guide).
