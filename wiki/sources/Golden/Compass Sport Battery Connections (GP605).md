# Compass Sport Battery Connections (GP605)

1-page notice: all GP605 units ship with batteries **intentionally
disconnected** to preserve shelf life — the chair will not power on until
both battery cables and the controller power cable are connected. Remove
front/rear battery covers, verify connections, push connectors firmly
until locked, replace covers.
