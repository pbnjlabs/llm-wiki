# Companion Tiller Replacement Bolt Instructions (GC240, GC340, GC440)

1-page instructions for replacing the two M8 shoulder bolts that pivot the
tiller (under the tiller boot) — tap out the old bolt, tap in the new one,
secure with washer and lock nut, repeat for the opposite side.
