# LiteRider & Buzzaround Transaxle Change

1-page procedure explicitly naming [[GL110-140-LiteRider]] and
[[GB1xx-Buzzaround-XL]] (GB106/GB116/GB146): swapping an old-style
transaxle for the new style. Critically requires a matched controller
swap at the same time — old brake/motor-brake isn't interchangeable, and
skipping the controller causes erratic behavior (fast stops, lurching).
