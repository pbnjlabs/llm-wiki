# PG Drives S-Drive Brochure

PG Drives Technology's 2-page brochure for the **S-Drive scooter
control system**, created 2020-12-01. Marketing, not a service manual.
Filed under Golden/General because Golden uses the S-Drive on the
[[GA541-Avenger]]. Trip codes are on [[PG-Drives-Trip-Codes]].

## Key facts

- **Current ratings:** **45, 70, 90, 120, 140 and 200 A**, from small
  transportable scooters to large, fast ones.
- **Molex connectors** for signal wiring across the range; battery and
  motor connectors vary by rating. They sit **recessed on top** of the
  unit.
- **Sealing:** electronics **IPX5**; optional cover and slotted gasket
  seals the connectors to **IPX4**, removable without disconnecting any
  wiring.
- **On-board diagnostics:** stores the **count of the last eight system
  problems** (S-Drive and other scooter components) and a **run timer**
  of total motor drive hours. Read with a **programming tool**; useful
  for intermittent faults.
- **Drive control:** smooth on all surfaces and gradients; **roll-back
  when starting on an incline is almost eliminated**.
- **TruCharge display output:** 10 LEDs (**3 red, 4 yellow, 3 green**)
  showing charge remaining, which also **flash to show faults** (see
  [[TruCharge-Diagnostics]]).
