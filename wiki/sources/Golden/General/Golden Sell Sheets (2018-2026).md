# Golden Sell Sheets (2018–2026)

Fourteen two-page Golden **sell sheets** (spec sheets), one per model or
pair. Official Golden specs: capacity, speed, range, dimensions,
**assembled weight**, piece weights, batteries, warranty. Grouped on one
page like other one-sheet sets. Sheet codes give the dates (e.g.
`SHT-GC440-022624` = 26 Feb 2024). Text read from the text layer; the
Buzzaround EX, Cricket, Compass Sport and Companion spec pages were
checked against the page images.

Most of these are on **Golden's 2026 VA lineup** ([[Golden]]). The
weight comparison for vehicle lifts is on [[Mobility-Device-Weights]].

## Other details worth knowing

- **GC440 (2024 model)**: new tiller with digital dash and USB port,
  high-back stadium seat, **full front and rear suspension**, U1
  batteries standard (50 Ah optional), 3.5 A charger, 90 A Dynamic
  R-Series controller. Power elevating seat (6 in, with lockout) option.
- **GC540**: 2-NF22, 8 A charger, 120 A controller, 5-year frame.
- **GR595 Eagle**: off-road design, 13 in pneumatic tires, front and rear
  suspension, **140 A controller**, 8 mph, 5-year frame.
- **GB150**: "next generation" of the Buzzaround XL 4-wheel; MicroTurn
  (39 in turning radius), 3.5 in ground clearance.
- **GP302 Cricket**: forward-folding carbon fiber chair, folded 30.2 ×
  22.25 × 14 in, flip-up arms, memory-foam cushion, airline friendly;
  K0899.
- **Warranties:** scooters lifetime frame (GC540 and GR595: 5 years),
  2 yr drive train and electronics, 1 yr batteries (GB120: 2 yr).
  Power chairs: GP605 lifetime frame, others 5 yr; 13 months drive
  train and electronics.
