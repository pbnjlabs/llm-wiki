# Two-Piece Brake Replacement

1-page procedure covering brake replacement on three model lines that all
use the same electromagnetic brake body but different handle shapes:
LiteRider (GL110/GL140), Buzzaround XL (GB116/GB146), Buzzaround Lite
(GB106). 5-step swap: unbolt 4 screws from motor, mount new brake in
freewheel position at 12 o'clock, attach model-specific handle, re-bolt.

Confirms the brake mechanism itself is shared across Buzzaround and
LiteRider — only the handle geometry differs per model. Feeds:
[[GB1xx-Buzzaround-XL]] (GL110/GL140 LiteRider side not yet ingested —
flag for that future cluster).
