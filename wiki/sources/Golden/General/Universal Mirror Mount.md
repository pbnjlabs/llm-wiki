# Universal Mirror Mount Installation

1-page install sheet: clamp bracket goes around the tiller grip on either
side, mirror threads into the bracket to secure it, jam nut locks the
angle. Warns that far-left/right mirror placement can make the mirror the
scooter's widest point, causing doorway clearance issues.
