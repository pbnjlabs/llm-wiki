# Adjustable Depth Seat Instructions

1-page procedure: adjusts seat depth to 18" or 20" by relocating the seat
base on its hinge bolts and the arm bar to a matching hole set. 18" depth
requires an additional arm bar strap/bolt/nut per side that 20" doesn't
need. Tools: 5mm/4mm Allen, 13mm wrench.
