# Battery Charging Insert

1-page general battery care guidance: 12-hour initial conditioning charge
for new batteries, daily 6-8hr charging, full-charge-then-disconnect for
storage over a week, monthly check for stored batteries, off-board charger
only (never automotive/wet-type).
