# GB148 (Buzzaround Extreme, 4-wheel) Rental Bumper Kit

Part MBB-148R-KIT — same general bumper set as the GB118 kit (front/left/
right/rear + right-angle brackets) but the 4-wheel platform requires
removing a front wheel and the plastic nose piece to fit the front
bumper, which the 3-wheel GB118 kit does not need. Confirms GB118 and
GB148 are 3-wheel vs. 4-wheel variants of the same "Buzzaround Extreme"
platform, not unrelated models.
