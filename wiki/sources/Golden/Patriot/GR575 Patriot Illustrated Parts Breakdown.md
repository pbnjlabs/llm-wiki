# GR575 Patriot Illustrated Parts Breakdown

17-page IPB (2024), a standalone version of the parts section in the
2014 service guide.

- **PG S-Drive** controller MBC-575D-CNT on left/right mounts;
  programmer **SP1 with S-Drive adaptor** (MBC-AVPROG).
- **Hand brake** assembly MBB-575D-HBA and cable; brake drum and disc
  (MBD-575D-BRAKE-DR / -DS); brake harness.
- Transaxle complete MBX-575D-TRANS-BA; motor mounting hardware kit.
- Main harness; rear harness with circuit breaker; battery cables;
  charger port; key switch.
- Batteries **22NF** (MBE-BATT22) or **Group 24 75 Ah** (MBE-BATT24-BW).
