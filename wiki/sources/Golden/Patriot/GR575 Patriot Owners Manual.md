# GR575 Patriot Owner's Manual

40-page owner's manual for the **GR575 Patriot** (2020). The first
Patriot owner's manual on file; only the 2014 service guide was here
before.

**Specs (p. 14–15):** 400 lb, **10 mph**, 16 mi, 6.75 in clearance to
the deck, 3.5 in to the transaxle, **76.5 in turning radius**, **2-NF22
or 2-75 Ah**, **140 A** controller, 57 × 28 in, 40 in wheelbase.
**302 lb assembled**: base 152, seat 44, batteries 37 (NF22) or 52
(75 Ah) each. 13 in pneumatic tires, no anti-tips. **Mechanical hand
brake.** Captain's pillow-top high-back seat 20 × 18 in, slide seat.
Warranty **5 yr frame**, 2 yr drive train and electronics, 1 yr batteries.

Other content:
- **S-Drive beep codes** 2/3/5/6/7/8/9: fast beeps for 2 s, then the
  count once. 3 may only show going downhill (regeneration); 8 says
  check the freewheel switch; 9 says check tire inflation.
  [[S-Drive-Beep-Codes]]. Codes and serial number under the floor mat.
- **Freewheel:** pull the lever **back toward N**; push forward toward
  **D** to drive.
- Warranty list: the standard scooter list including Patriot GR575
  ([[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]]).
