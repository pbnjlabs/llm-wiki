# GR575 Patriot Service Guide

52-page service guide (rev A) for the Patriot GR575 heavy-duty scooter.

- **Controller:** **PG (P+G) S-Drive, 140 A** (3051600200). 18–24 VDC.
- **Batteries:** two 12V **75 Ah** (3052512110). Requires 75 Ah.
- **Fuses:** a **125 A** battery fuse, plus 15 A and 3 A fuses; a main
  circuit breaker.
- Motor resistance under 50 Ω (can be as low as 0.2 Ω).
- S-Drive wiring diagram and tiller interface socket (CN7).
- Scenarios: key on, no power; won't charge.
- **Beep codes 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9.** See [[S-Drive-Beep-Codes]].
- Replacement steps: controller, drive wheel, dash, key switch, high/low
  switch, motor brushes, motor/brake and transaxle, batteries, shrouds.
- Appendices: meters, wiring diagrams, a full IPB (pp. 29–48),
  dashboard and hand brake (p. 49).
