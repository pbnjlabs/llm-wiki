# Compass Caster Wheel Mounting (GP600M/GP605M/GP615M/GP620M)

1-page procedure for upgrading to the newer spoke-style caster wheel across
**all four** Compass model numbers at once (GP600M, GP605M, GP615M,
GP620M) — direct textual confirmation these four share the same caster
fork/wheel mounting hardware. Old style used 3 washers (one under the lock
nut, two between fork and wheel); the new wheel replaces the two
fork-to-wheel washers with supplied spacers and discards all three old
washers.
