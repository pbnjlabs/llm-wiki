# Buzzaround Extreme Service Guide (GB118, GB148)

30-page service guide for the Buzzaround Extreme (EX): GB118 (3-wheel)
and GB148 (4-wheel). Same template as the Buzzaround XL guide.

- **Controller:** Dynamics R-Series.
- **Two battery packs** (left and right). **40 A** circuit breaker
  (replace with the exact rating); a **100 A** fuse in the left-side
  pack; a **10 A** charger fuse; a 1 A throttle fuse.
- **Motor** 0.8–1.5 Ω; **brake** 45–52 Ω.
- Beep/flash codes 1–9: the same as [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]].
- Replacement steps: drive wheel, drive train, throttle pot, battery,
  circuit breaker, battery-harness fuse, controller, main harness,
  control panel, motor/brake, intermediate harnesses, power harness.
- Voltmeter/ohmmeter appendices.
