# Buzzaround Luxury Owner's Manual (GB119, GB149)

44-page owner's manual for the **Buzzaround Luxury (LX)**: GB119
3-wheel, GB149 4-wheel. The EX platform with a heavier-duty rating and
a high-back seat.

**Specs (p. 14–15), GB119 / GB149:** **375 lb**, 5 mph, 18 mi, 4 in
clearance, 40.5 / 54 in turning radius, 2-U1 (two 24 lb packs), **90 A**
controller, 45 × 22 / 44 × 22 in, wheelbase 34.7 / 33.8 in. **168 / 174
lb assembled**: front half 50 / 57, rear half 36, **seat 32**. 9 in solid
tires; pneumatic optional on the GB149. **High-back seat** 17 × 16 in
with headrest; optional 20 × 18 in stadium seat. Warranty lifetime
frame, 2 yr drive train and electronics, 1 yr batteries.

Other content:
- **Flash/beep codes 1–9:** [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]].
- **Tiller fuse panel** (center of the tiller, blade fuses): **1 A** top
  = dash, lights and power (dead dash, no drive); **2 A** middle = USB
  port; **10 A** bottom = XLR charging port (won't charge).
- **Freewheel:** push the lever **forward toward N**; pull back toward
  **D**.
- Warranty list adds BUZZaround Luxury GB119/GB149 ([[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]]).
