# Frame Lock Adjustment (GB116/GB146)

1-page fix for two symptoms: the fold-in-half frame latch won't stay down,
or the front/rear sections have play after locking. Root cause is a
threaded adjustment bolt seated too far in/out of the receiver cup.
Adjust in 1/4" (then 1/8") increments with a 6mm Allen while holding the
17mm lock nut.

Confirms GB116/GB146 are a front/rear-splitting foldable design (unlike
GB120 Carry On's non-folding deck). Feeds: [[GB1xx-Buzzaround-XL]].
