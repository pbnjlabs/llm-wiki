# Buzzaround XL Owner's Manual (GB117, GB147)

44-page owner's manual for the Buzzaround XL. It covers the standard
models and the **HD** models (GB117HD/GB117SHD, GB147HD/GB147SHD).

**Specs (p. 15–16)**

| | GB117 | GB147 | GB117 HD | GB147 HD |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Capacity | 300 lb | 300 lb | 325 lb | 325 lb |
| Batteries | 2-12 Ah | 2-12 Ah | 2-22 Ah | 2-22 Ah |
| Range | 8 mi | 8 mi | 14 mi | 14 mi |
| Turning radius | 37 in | 47 in | 37 in | 47 in |
| Length × width | 42.5 × 21.5 in | 41.5 × 21.5 in | 42.5 × 21.5 in | 41.5 × 21.5 in |
| Axle to axle | 33.5 in | 32 in | 33.5 in | 32 in |
| Assembled weight | 102 lb | 110 lb | 112 / 115 lb | 120 / 123 lb |
| Front half | 35 lb | 43 lb | 35 lb | 43 lb |
| Rear half | 25 lb | 25 lb | 25 / 28 lb | 25 / 28 lb |
| Battery pack | 19 lb | 19 lb | 29 lb | 29 lb |

All models: 4 mph, **50 A controller**, 3.0 in ground clearance, 8 in solid
tires, 23 lb seat, Medicare K0800 (HD: K0801). Where two HD weights are
given, the second is the S (suspension) version. Warranty: lifetime
frame, 2 yr drive train and electronics, 1 yr batteries.

Other content:
- **Delta tiller:** one-hand throttle from either side.
- **Disassembly:** seat, battery pack and basket come off, the tiller
  folds down and locks, then a **drivetrain release lever** separates
  the front frame from the rear (drivetrain) section.
- **Flash/beep codes 1–9:** the same as [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]].
  Codes and serial number are printed **under the battery box**.
- **Warranty:** the shared scooter warranty text. Its covered-models list
  includes GB117D/H/Z and GB147D/H/Z, GL109, and the BUZZaround LX
  (GB119/GB149). See [[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]].
