# Buzzaround Carry On Service Guide (GB120)

26-page service guide for the GB120 Carry On.

- **Controller:** Dynamics R-Series (under a cover).
- **Battery:** one 24 V lithium-ion pack, **15 Ah or 6.5 Ah**. It
  supplies 24–29 V and sits in the centre of the upper deck on two
  spring-loaded connectors. A fully charged pack reads about 29 V.
- **Fuses:** 35 A battery fuse and a 3 A fuse.
- **Motor** 0.8–1.5 Ω (brake resistance is also listed).
- Beep/flash codes 1–9: the same as [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]], worded
  "battery pack" instead of "batteries".
- Replacement steps: drive wheel, anti-tip, throttle pot, battery pack,
  fuses, controller, dash and dash components, upper and lower main
  harness, transaxle assembly, steering assembly, tiller shock.
