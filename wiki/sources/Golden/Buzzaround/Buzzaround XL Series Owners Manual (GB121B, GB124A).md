# Buzzaround XL Series Owner's Manual (GB121B, GB124A)

44-page owner's manual (LIT-XLSERIES-OM_08.05.2022) for the 2022
**Buzzaround XL** (-STD) and **Buzzaround XLS HD** (-SHZ), models
GB121B and GB124A. The spec tables (p. 14–15) are images.

| | GB121B-STD | GB124A-STD | GB121B-SHZ (XLS HD) | GB124A-SHZ (XLS HD) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Capacity | 300 lb | 300 lb | **325 lb** | **325 lb** |
| Range | 10.33 mi | 10.29 mi | 8.9 mi | 9.82 mi |
| Turning radius | 37.5 in | 45.5 in | 37.5 in | 44 in |
| Length / wheelbase | 41 / 31.5 in | 40 / 30.5 in | 41 / 31.5 in | 40 / 30.5 in |
| Clearance under deck | 3.5 in | 3.5 in | 4 in | 4 in |
| Assembled | 93 lb | 98 lb | 100 lb | 105 lb |
| Front / rear half | 32 / 24 lb | 37 / 24 lb | 35 / 28 lb | 40 / 28 lb |
| Medicare | K0800 | K0800 | not coded | not coded |

All: 4 mph, **2-12 Ah** (16 lb pack), **50 A** controller, 20 in wide,
seat 21 lb (17 × 14.5 in), 7.5 in front and 8 in rear solid tires.
Warranty lifetime frame, 2 yr drive train and electronics, 1 yr
batteries. The turning radii suggest GB121B is the 3-wheel and GB124A
the 4-wheel (not stated).

Other content:
- Delta tiller with dual throttle levers and a phone holder; seat
  swivel; battery pack with a **wireless (contact) connector**.
- **Tiller lock knob** (push and turn 90°) locks the front wheel so the
  folded scooter can be carried by the tiller tube.
- **Disassembly:** seat, battery pack, basket; lock and fold the tiller;
  pull the **drivetrain release lever** and lift the front frame off.
- **Beep codes 1–9:** [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]]. Same 3 A / 5 A fuses
  as the GB121.
- **Freewheel:** push the lever **forward toward N**; back toward **D**.
- Warranty list adds **GB121A** under BUZZaround LT and **GB121B,
  GB124A** under the XL series ([[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]]).
