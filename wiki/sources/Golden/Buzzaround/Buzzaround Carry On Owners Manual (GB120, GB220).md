# Buzzaround Carry On Owner's Manual (GB120, GB220)

28-page owner's manual (LIT-GB120-GB220-OM, Rev. D; undated, date from
the PDF). Covers the GB120 and the new **GB220 Carry On HD**. The first
owner's manual on file for either.

**Specs (p. 12), GB120 / GB220:**

| | GB120 | GB220 |
|---|---|---|
| Capacity | 300 lb | **400 lb** |
| Speed | 3.6 mph | 4.29 mph |
| Range (12.5 Ah + 6.5 Ah) | 14.4 + 7.5 = 21.9 mi | 14.2 + 7.4 = 21.6 mi |
| Controller | 50 A | **32 A** |
| L × W | 38 × 19.5 in | 40 × 23.5 in |
| Clearance under deck / transaxle | 4 / 1.5 in | 4.63 / 2.3 in |
| Assembled | **65.5 lb** | **77.5 lb** |
| Main body / seat / battery | 47.5 / 12 / 5 lb | 52.5 / 20 / 5 lb |
| Tires front / rear | 6 / 7 in | 7.25 / 8.25 in |
| Folded height, no seat | 12 in | 12.63 in |

Both: two lithium packs (12.5 Ah and 6.5 Ah) in the box, 28.7 in
wheelbase, 48.75 in turning radius. Warranty **lifetime frame, 2 yr
drive train, electronics and batteries**.

Other content:
- **Fuse must be installed at setup:** pull the battery, remove the
  controller cover under the seat pedestal, fit the fuse.
- Arms: GB120 pull up to lock; GB220 arms slot into the seat and rotate
  down.
- **Freewheel switch**, usable **only with the battery installed**.
- Tiller lock hooks hold the folded tiller; bottle and cane holders
  mount either side.
- **Fault codes 1–9** (battery meter flashes, horn beeps): the
  R-Series layout in plain words; see [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]].
- Charge at the pack or the console port; it won't move while
  charging. Lithium packs are maintenance free and travel-safe.
- No warranty text section.
