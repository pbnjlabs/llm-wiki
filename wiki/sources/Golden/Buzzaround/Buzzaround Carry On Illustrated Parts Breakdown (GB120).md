# Buzzaround Carry On Illustrated Parts Breakdown (GB120)

18-page IPB, the first GB120 parts list on file.

- **Controller: Dynamics DR50-A01, 50 A** (3051600180) under a cover
  with thumb screws.
- **Brake switch** with wiring MBE-120A-BRKS (the freewheel switch);
  key switch, speed pot, paddle with pot.
- Fuses: **3 A** MBE-FUSE3A1 (same as the Companion) and **35 A blade**.
- Batteries: lithium 15 Ah MBE-BATT-15LITH and **6.5 Ah airline-approved**
  MBE-BATT-65LITH; battery harness; latch handle.
- Charger: Li-ion 24 V 2 A **MBE-0028**, also used on the GP130 power
  chair.
- Upper and lower main harnesses, charger port with wiring, transaxle
  mounting kit; Dynamic programmer.
