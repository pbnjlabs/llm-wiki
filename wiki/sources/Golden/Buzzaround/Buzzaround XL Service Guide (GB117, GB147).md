# Buzzaround XL Service Guide (GB117, GB147)

30-page field service guide (rev A) for the **current-generation
Buzzaround XL**, GB117 (3-wheel) and GB147 (4-wheel). Same template as
the [[Companion Service Guide (GC240, GC340, GC440)]].

- **Controller:** Dynamics R-Series. 18–24 VDC system.
- **Batteries:** two 12 Ah **or** two 22 Ah in a removable pack.
- **Protection:** 30 A circuit breaker on the battery pack; 40 A
  battery fuse and 5 A charger fuse inline on the battery harness;
  1 A inline fuse on the throttle pot.
- **Motor** 0.8–1.5 Ω; **brake** 45–52 Ω.
- Front and rear frame halves join through a front intermediate harness
  and a rear intermediate connector, so the scooter **separates in two**.
- Charger port on the battery pack and on the tiller. Charger test:
  about 27 V at rest, rising to about 29.5 V when the charger is plugged in.

Contents: component reference (location, function, failure signs, test,
expected reading), wiring diagram; Scenario 1 (key on, no power) and
Scenario 2 (won't charge) voltage trees; beep codes 1–9 (same as
[[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]]); replacement steps for drive wheel, drive
train, throttle pot, battery, breaker, battery-harness fuse, controller,
main harness, control panel, motor/brake, intermediate harnesses and
power harness; voltmeter/ohmmeter appendices.
