# BuzzAround CarryOn Transportation Guide (Battery)

Airline-travel guidance for the GB120's two lithium-ion battery pack
options: a 15AH/360WH pack (PN MBE-BATT-15LITH, **not** air-safe) and a
6.5AH/156WH pack (PN MBE-BATT-65LITH, air-safe subject to airline
policy). Both rated 24-29V, 10A working current, 80% capacity retained
after 300 cycles. Procedure: notify airline, power off, press release
button to remove pack(s), stow in protective carrying cover.

Confirms GB120 uses a **quick-release removable battery pack** design
(button-release), distinct from Buzzaround EX's charging-dock-based dual
packs. Feeds: [[GB120-Buzzaround-CarryOn]].
