# Buzzaround Lite Service Guide (GB106)

54-page service guide (rev A) for the **discontinued** Buzzaround Lite
GB106. Downloads also had copies named `GB106 Buzzaround Lite_SG_ REVA_
040711.pdf` (byte-identical, by md5).

- **Controller:** **PG (P+G) S-Drive**, not the Dynamics R-Series used on
  the other Buzzarounds. Its tiller interface socket is CN7. So
  [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]] does **not** apply. Use
  [[S-Drive-Beep-Codes]].
- 18–24 VDC; requires **two 12V 12 Ah** batteries. Load them in the pack
  with the terminals facing **rearward**.
- Motor resistance under 50 Ω (can be as low as 0.2 Ω).
- The charger won't work if battery voltage is too low; boost-charge
  first.
- Scenarios: no power; power to the console but won't run; charger tests.
- Beep codes 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 (S-Drive set). Code 3 (high voltage)
  may appear only going downhill, when regeneration pushes power back
  into the batteries.
- Replacement steps: controller, drive wheel, control panel, throttle
  pot, drivetrain (motor/brake and transaxle), batteries.
- Appendices: meters, wiring diagrams (rear, console, S-Drive),
  troubleshooting flow charts, and a full IPB (pp. 35–51).
