# Buzzaround XL Service Guide (GB116, GB146)

96-page service guide (rev A) for the **discontinued** first-generation
Buzzaround XL, GB116 (3-wheel) and GB146 (4-wheel). Downloads had a
second copy, `GB116+GB146 Buzzaround XL_SG_REVA_100711.pdf`, with
identical text, so only this one was filed.

- **Controller:** Dynamics R-Series. The IPB lists a **DR50-A01 (50 A)**.
  18–24 VDC system with two 12 Ah **or** two 18 Ah batteries (the IPB
  lists the 12 Ah pack).
- **Motor** under 50 Ω (can be as low as 0.2 Ω). **Brake** under 80 Ω,
  not shorted. These are looser limits than the GB117/147 guide gives.
- Warns not to mix up the red/black motor wires and the red/black battery
  wires on the controller.
- Beep codes 1–9: the same as [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]].
- Replacement steps: drive wheel, drive train, throttle pot, battery,
  circuit breaker, battery-harness fuse, controller, main harness,
  control panel, brake, front and rear intermediate harnesses.
- Appendices: voltmeter, ohmmeter, wiring diagrams (pp. 28–31),
  **troubleshooting flow charts** (pp. 32–43).
- **Full IPB for both models:** GB116 pp. 44–68, GB146 pp. 69–93.
