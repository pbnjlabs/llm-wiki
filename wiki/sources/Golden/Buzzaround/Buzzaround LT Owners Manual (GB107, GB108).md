# Buzzaround LT Owner's Manual (GB107, GB108)

44-page owner's manual (LIT-107-108-OM_11.15.2022) for the **Buzzaround
LT** (the manual's headings call it "Buzzaround Lite"): GB107 3-wheel,
GB108 4-wheel. The first manual on file for the GB108.

**Specs (p. 13–14), GB107 / GB108:** 300 lb, 3.61 / 3.13 mph,
5.22 / 5.42 mi, 2.5 in clearance, 39 / 48.5 in turning radius,
**2-12 Ah**, **50 A** controller, **180 W motor**, 41.75 × 22.5 /
44 × 23 in, 33.5 in wheelbase. **87 / 91.5 lb assembled**: front half
28 / 34, rear half 20.5, seat 13.5, battery pack 17.5 lb. 8 in solid
tires. Medicare K0800. Warranty lifetime frame, 2 yr drive train and
electronics, 1 yr batteries.

> [!note] Weights disagree with the sell sheet
> The Jul 2025 sell sheet ([[Golden Sell Sheets (2018-2026)]]) gives
> 85.5 / 95 lb. Use the higher figure for lift checks.

Other content:
- **Flash/beep codes 1–9** (R-Series table, code 3 just "consult your
  provider"): [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]]. Codes are printed on the
  scooter as well.
- **Freewheel:** push the lever **forward toward N**; pull back toward
  **D** to drive.
- Motor braking limits pushing speed with the key off; no-power slope
  procedure.
- Warranty list names BUZZaround LT GB107, GB108 ([[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]]).
