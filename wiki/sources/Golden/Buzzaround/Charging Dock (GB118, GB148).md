# Buzzaround EX Charging Dock

Indoor-only dual-footplate dock that charges **two removable battery
packs** simultaneously (contrast with GB120 Carry On's single swappable
pack). Fuse replacement: remove 9 bottom screws, open fuse holder, swap
the 10A blade-style fuse (must match exact type/rating) — a good/bad fuse
visual comparison is included.

Confirms Buzzaround EX (GB118/GB148) uses dual removable battery packs,
not a fixed onboard battery. Feeds: [[GB118-148-Buzzaround-EX]].
