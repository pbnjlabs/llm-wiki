# Buzzaround Extreme Owner's Manual (GB118, GB148)

44-page owner's manual for the **Buzzaround Extreme** (GB118EX 3-wheel,
GB148EX 4-wheel). The first owner's manual on file for the EX.

**Specs (p. 14–15), GB118 / GB148:** 350 lb, 5 mph, 18 mi, 4 in
clearance, 40.5 / 54 in turning radius, **2-U1** in two 24 lb packs,
**90 A** controller, 45 × 22 / 44 × 22 in, wheelbase 34.7 / 33.8 in.
**154 / 161 lb assembled**: front half 46 / 53, rear half 36, seat 23.
9 in solid tires; **pneumatic optional on the GB148 only**. Standard
seat 18 × 16 in, optional 20 × 18 in. Warranty lifetime frame, 2 yr
drive train and electronics, 1 yr batteries. Weights match the sell
sheet.

Other content:
- **Flash/beep codes 1–9:** [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]].
- **Freewheel:** push the lever **forward toward N**; pull back toward
  **D** to drive.
- Warranty list: BUZZaround LT GB107, XL series GB117/GB147 (D, S, H, Z),
  Extreme GB118/GB148, LiteRider GL109/GL111/GL141, all Companions,
  Avenger, Patriot ([[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]]).
