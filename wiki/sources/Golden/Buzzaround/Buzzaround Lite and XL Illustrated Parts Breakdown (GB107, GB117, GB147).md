# Buzzaround Lite and XL Illustrated Parts Breakdown (GB107, GB117, GB147)

25-page IPB (rev B, Jan 2025) covering the **GB107** Buzzaround LT
(called "Lite" in the filename) and the GB117/GB147 Buzzaround XL. It has
20 drawings: tiller, chassis moldings, front chassis, steering, rear
chassis, seat and electrical for each model, plus the 12 Ah and 22 Ah
battery packs and accessories.

Key points:
- **Suffix variants:** GB117 and GB147 come in **D, H, S and Z**
  versions. D and H share one steering system and rear chassis, the same
  as GB107. S and Z use a different steering system (front fork with
  shock absorbers) and a different rear chassis. The owner's manual
  calls the HD models "HD" and "SHD", so **S most likely means
  suspension**. That is an inference; the IPB doesn't say.
- **Controller:** Dynamic **DR90-A01** (MBC-BZ7-CNT-MOD), the same on all
  three models. The 2019 owner's manual lists a 50 A controller and the
  2011 GB116 IPB lists a DR50-A01, so the controller part has changed
  over time.
- **Transaxle:** MBX-BZ7-TRANS-BA, the same for all variants.
- **Batteries:** 12V/22 Ah MBE-BATT1222-BW. Chargers: 1.5 A MBE-BZ7-CHG15
  and 3.5 A XLR MBE-CHG35A-STD.
- Motor intermediate connector halves (MBE-BZ-MICMALE / MICFEMALE) join
  the front and rear halves.
