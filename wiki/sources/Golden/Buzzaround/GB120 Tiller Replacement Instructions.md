# LIT-MB-0015 — GB120 Tiller Replacement Instructions (MBB-120A-TA-COMPLETE)

7-page full tiller-stem swap: power down, remove seat, stand unit upright
(kickstand) or flip it, pull the 8mm tiller bolt and 13mm tie-rod bolt,
disconnect the main wiring harness (white connector under a cap in the
battery compartment), pull the lower tiller stem/bearings, remove tiller.
Reassembly is orientation-critical: lubricate both bearings (exposed balls
down on top bearing, up on bottom bearing), and the lower steering stem's
4 cross-notches must align with the tiller while the steering tab points
directly rearward and handlebar is straight — getting this wrong misaligns
the steering. Tiller screw gets medium-strength thread locker.
