# Buzzaround Lite Owner's Manual (GB121)

42-page owner's manual for the **GB121 Buzzaround Lite** (2022).

**Specs (p. 14):** 300 lb, 4 mph, 7.5 mi, 3.5 in clearance, 38 in
turning radius, **2-12 Ah**, **50 A** controller, 41 × 20 in, 31.5 in
wheelbase. **100 lb assembled**: front half 32, rear half 24, seat 23,
battery pack 18 lb. 7 in solid tires. Seat height 3 positions, 0.75 in
steps. Medicare K0800. Warranty lifetime frame, 2 yr drive train and
electronics, 1 yr batteries.

Other content:
- **Beep codes 1–9** (R-Series, code 3 "consult provider"):
  [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]].
- **Fuses** for non-code faults: **3 A under the battery pack** (dead
  dash, no drive) and **5 A under the charger port cover** (XLR port
  won't charge).
- **Freewheel:** push the lever **forward toward N**; back toward **D**.
- Warranty list (rev 11.09.2020) names BUZZaround LT **GB121**
  ([[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]]).
