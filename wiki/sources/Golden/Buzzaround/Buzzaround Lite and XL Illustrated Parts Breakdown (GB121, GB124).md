# Buzzaround Lite and XL Illustrated Parts Breakdown (GB121, GB124)

10-page IPB (LIT-GB121-GB124-IPB, Rev. B); tables are images. Covers
the GB121 (Lite) and GB121A/B/C and GB124A/B (XL), with separate
**3-wheel** (GB121) and **4-wheel** (GB124) steering pages, which
confirms GB121 = 3-wheel and GB124 = 4-wheel.

- **Controller: Dynamics DR50-A01, 50 A** (3051600180); programs
  ZZ-PROGRAM-GB121 (GB121A) and ZZ-PROGRAM-GB121B-GB124A.
- Batteries **12 V 12 Ah with F2 slip-on terminals** (MBE-BATT1212-F2-BW).
- Transaxle complete MBX-121A-TRANS-BA; main harness MBE-121A-MWH
  (GB121) or MBE-0026 (GB124); headlight, dash and tiller charger port
  harnesses.
