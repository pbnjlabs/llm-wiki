# Buzzaround LT Illustrated Parts Breakdown (GB107, GB108)

7-page IPB (LIT-107-108-IPB, Rev. D) for the GB107A and GB108A; tables
are images (electrical page read visually).

- **Controller: Dynamics DR50-A01, 50 A** (3051600180, also DR50-B01);
  program ZZ-PROGRAM-GB107A-GB108A. The 2025 Lite/XL IPB lists the GB107
  with the **DR90-A01** instead; check the label.
- Battery box 12 V 12 Ah (MBE-0003, batteries not included); **1.5 A
  charger** MBE-BZ7-CHG15; flash-code beeper MBE-0024; key switch,
  horn, throttle pot, charger port, main, motor and brake harnesses
  (MBE-0004 to MBE-0024).
