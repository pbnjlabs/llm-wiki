# GA541 Avenger Operator Manual

41-page owner's manual for the GA541 Avenger 4-wheel scooter. Contents:

- Introduction, safety guidelines/symbols, EMI/RFI warnings (unintended
  movement/brake release risk from nearby transmitters)
- Technical specifications (see [[GA541-Avenger]] for the full table)
- Operation: hand controls (TruCharge meter, speed dial, thumb-lever
  "wig-wag" forward/reverse control, tiller adjustment), seat controls
  (armrest height/width, seat height/rotation)
- Riding technique: mounting/dismounting, basic driving, steering,
  control through tight spots (doors, ramps, curbs, grass/gravel),
  driving on inclines (max recommended 8.0°, procedures for backing down
  a slope under power vs. in freewheel/emergency mode)
- Disassembly/assembly: splits into front half + rear half via a ball
  detent pin, for transport (won't fit in a compact car trunk)
- Diagnostics: TruCharge Battery Display 10-code fault table (1=low
  voltage ... 10=high battery voltage) — see [[TruCharge-Diagnostics]]
- Care/maintenance: 50A main circuit breaker reset procedure, tire
  inflation, battery charging (12hr initial charge, 6-8hr routine,
  AGM/GEL-cell SLA only), cleaning
- **Consumer Limited Warranty (Scooters)** — this section's warranty text
  explicitly covers many Golden scooter models under one shared policy,
  not just the Avenger: BUZZaround LT (GB107), BUZZaround XL Series
  (GB117D/H/Z, GB147D/H/Z), BUZZaround Extreme (GB118, GB148), BUZZaround
  Luxury (GB119, GB149), LiteRider Series (GL111, GL141), LiteRider HD
  (GL112), all Companion models (GC240, GC340, GC440), Avenger (GA541),
  and Patriot (GR575) — see [[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]]
