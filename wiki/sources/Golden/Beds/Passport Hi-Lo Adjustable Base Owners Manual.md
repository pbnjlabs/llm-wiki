# Passport Hi-Lo Adjustable Base Owner's Manual

24-page owner's manual (LIT-BD100A-OM, Rev. B; undated, date from the
PDF) for Golden's **Hi Lo Tilt HS adjustable base**, sold as the
**Passport** bed. Specs, parts list and wiring overview (p. 5–8) are
images.

**Specs (p. 5):** **880 lb (400 kg)** including mattress and occupants,
spread evenly (Twin, Twin XL, Full, Queen); height **14.5–23.5 in**;
backrest 0–65°, legrest 0–35°; power supply (SMPS) **100–240 V AC in,
29 V DC 1.8 A out**; 2.4 GHz remote (the manual prints "2.4 MHz");
massage, Bluetooth control, floor light, remote flashlight, wall
hugger.

**Not a hospital bed:** for **in-home use only**, "not approved for
hospital use", not for tent oxygen. No side rails as standard (fall
risk; side rails are an accessory).

Other content:
- **Duty cycle 10%:** 2 min of motor run needs 18 min rest. Massage no
  more than 2 h in any 6 h.
- **Electronics:** HE150 control box (BDE-0002), head motor (blue dot,
  BDE-0004), foot motor (yellow dot, BDE-0006), two tilt motors
  (BDE-0007), two massage motors (BDE-0008), SMPS GM2102-TR-PK, remote
  BDE-0001 (3 × AAA). Purple-tab and yellow-tab connectors pair the
  head and foot motor cables.
- **Assembly** (two people): head and foot sections join with KD
  connectors, U-bars and rods; center cross braces with clevis and
  cotter pins; wing bolts; motors; legs in 5, 2.5 and 2 in sections.
  Flip it right side up without leaning on the legs. Use a surge
  protector, no extension cord.
- **Remote:** head, legs, hi-lo, tilt; flat, zero gravity, sleep and TV
  presets; two memories; floor light; head/foot/wave massage with a
  10/20/30 min timer.
- **Pairing:** press the control box's pairing button twice (LED on,
  60 s), then hold the two remote buttons shown until it beeps; within
  2 m. Interference: unplug and replug to change channel.
- **Two bases (King XL):** sync cable BDE-0005 between control boxes;
  one remote at a time.
- **Power or remote failure:** hold the **emergency lowering button**
  on the control box to flatten it. Two 9 V batteries in the SMPS give
  backup power to flatten an unloaded base only.
- **Troubleshooting:** unplug 30 s to reset; check the outlet/breaker;
  re-pair; remote batteries; clear obstructions and wall space; duty
  cycle.
- **Warranty:** year 1 parts and labor; years 2–3 parts only; lifetime
  on the steel frame and mechanical parts (not electronics or motors).
  Residential use only; claims go through the retailer and need Golden
  approval. The **Gateway mattress** has its own 5-year warranty.
