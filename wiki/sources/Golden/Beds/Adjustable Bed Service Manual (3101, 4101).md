# Adjustable Bed Service Manual (3101, 4101)

12-page service manual (2012) for Golden's **discontinued 3100 and 4100
series adjustable beds** (filed under lift recliners, discontinued). Not
the current Passport base.

- Frame parts by size (Twin vs Full/Queen main frame, head and foot
  U's, drive U's and links).
- **Motor:** one combined motor that pushes onto the drive bars (marked
  head and foot): **BPBMO-A (3101)**, **BPBMO-IR (4101, with vibrators)**;
  motor caps BPBMOSL. Hand controls BPHC-A / BPWHCV. 5-pin (hand
  control) and 7-pin (vibrator Y-connector, 4101) sockets on the motor.
  Unplug the bed in the flat position first.
- Vibrators on rubber mounts (replace cracked mounts); King bed lock
  BL100; bed risers (2–6 in; 4100 risers outside the wood trim);
  half rails; headboard/footboard brackets.
