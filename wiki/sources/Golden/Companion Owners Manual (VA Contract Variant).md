# Companion Owner's Manual — Department of Veterans Affairs Contract Variant (GC240, GC340, GC440)

40-page operator manual. "VA" in the filename is **Department of Veterans
Affairs**, not a state regulatory variant — confirmed by the packing list
(includes a "Military Branch Patch") and a VA-specific warranty section,
not a generic scooter difference. Otherwise this is a standard Companion
owner's manual (specs, controls, riding, disassembly/assembly,
transporting, care and maintenance) — see [[GC240-340-440-540-Companion]]
for consolidated specs and procedures.

Key content:
- Specs table for GC240 (350 lb, 3-wheel)/GC340 (400 lb, 3-wheel, spring
  suspension)/GC440 (400 lb, 4-wheel, spring suspension) — 5-5.4 mph, 15-18
  mile range, 90A controller, lifetime frame warranty
- Flash/Beep Codes 1-9 (same numbering as the Service Guide's Flash Codes)
  — see [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]]
- **Warranty**: "Department of Veterans Affairs Warranty" — same coverage
  tiers/wording as [[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]] (lifetime frame / 2yr
  drivetrain+electronics / 1yr batteries+wear parts), packaged specifically
  for VA Medical Center purchasers, PLUS an added "Two-Year Warranty
  Service Plan" section unique to the VA contract (24mo parts, 12mo labor,
  administered by Golden ext. 505 "VA Tech")
