# Buzzaround EX Tie Down Kit

Part MBX-BZ48-TDB. Adds transport tie-down points by disassembling each
anti-tip wheel assembly, sandwiching a tie-down bracket between the
anti-tip bar and a longer replacement bolt, and reassembling. **Warning**:
tie-down must angle inward toward the scooter or it can be damaged.

Feeds: [[GB118-148-Buzzaround-EX]].
