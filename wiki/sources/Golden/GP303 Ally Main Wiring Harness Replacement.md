# GP303 "Ally" Main Wiring Harness Replacement

10-page procedure (title has a typo: "Hanress" for "Harness") for
replacing the main wiring harness on the **GP303 "Ally"** — a foldable
power wheelchair, distinct from and unrelated to the GP600-series Compass
line despite the shared "GP" prefix (this was misfiled as a Compass-family
document when clustering by number; corrected here). The Ally folds
compactly (frame collapses into a bow shape for transport) and routes its
main harness through the armrests and a hinged frame spacer bar.

Procedure: remove battery and shrouds, flip the unit onto its footplate,
unplug the harness from the control module (three connectors: positive,
negative, EXPN), fold each armrest up 90° and strip its cover to access
the harness routing through the arm tube, remove seat cushion and armrest
rubber deco covers, fold the frame into its transport "bow" shape to
access a hinged spacer bar, cut zip ties, pull the harness through the
frame, then fish the new harness through using a bent steel wire/fish
wire, re-tie-wrap, and reverse all steps. No motor-harness disconnection
required — only the main harness is replaced.
