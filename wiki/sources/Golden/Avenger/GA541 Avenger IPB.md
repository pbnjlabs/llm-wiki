# GA541 Avenger IPB (Illustrated Parts Breakdown)

17-page parts catalog for the GA541 Avenger scooter, organized by 7
drawing groups (DWG 1-7) plus miscellaneous items and accessories:

- DWG 1 — Tiller Assembly (dashboard, tiller bar, switches, horn, key
  switch, basket, grips)
- DWG 2 — Chassis Moldings (deck/rear shrouds, turn signal light/harness,
  scuff guards, carpet, floor mat)
- DWG 3 — Front Chassis Assembly (frame, front axle, seat post, connecting
  pin, bumper, battery strap)
- DWG 4 — Steering System (headlight assembly, steering shaft, connecting
  rods, tiller knuckle, wheels/tires/tubes, hub)
- DWG 5 — Rear Chassis Assembly-Drive System (750W motor with brake,
  transaxle, wheels, brake, rim, hub)
- DWG 6 — Seat Assembly (captain's seat, arms, swivel base)
- DWG 7 — Console and Wiring System (dash circuit board, taillight,
  battery/main/controller harnesses, S-Drive controller module, 70A
  circuit breaker, 8.0A charger, keys)
- Miscellaneous: 22NF battery, base/top box, foam wheels, SP1 programmer
  with S-Drive adaptor
- Accessories: cover, crutch/cane holders, flag, rear basket, cup holder,
  oxygen/walker holders, quad cane holder, deluxe bag, mirror, seat belts
  (60"/78")

Notable: uses a PG (Penny & Giles) "S-Drive" electronic control system —
S-Drive controller module (MBC-541D-SDMODULE), dashboard circuit board
(MBE-AVDCB), and a dedicated SP1 programmer/adaptor for it.
