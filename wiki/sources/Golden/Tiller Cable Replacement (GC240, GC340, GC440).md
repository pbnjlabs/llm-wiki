# Companion Tiller Cable (GC240, GC340, GC440)

Part number SE06E207. 1-page removal/installation procedure for the tiller
adjustment cable: remove front/rear tiller shrouds, release the cable lock
and jam nuts, fish the old cable out through the adjustment handle, fish
the new cable back in and re-secure the lock/adjuster/jam nuts.
