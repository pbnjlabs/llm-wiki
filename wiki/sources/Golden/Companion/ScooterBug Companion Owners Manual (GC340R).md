# ScooterBug Companion Owner's Manual (GC340R)

40-page owner's manual for the **GC340R**, a 3-wheel Companion variant
marked "ScooterBug" on the cover. The manual doesn't explain the name.

**Specs (p. 14):** 400 lb, **3.1 mph** (the GC340 is 5.1), 20.9 mi
on U1 or 25.4 mi on 40 Ah, 4.5 in clearance under the deck, 41 in
turning radius, **2-U1 (40 Ah optional)**, **90 A** controller,
45.5 × 23.5 in, 34.5 in wheelbase. **176 lb (U1) / 192 lb (40 Ah)
assembled**: base with batteries 104, seat 31.5, batteries 22 (U1) or
29 (40 Ah) each. 9 in front, 10.75 in rear solid tires.

What's different from a GC340:
- **Front anti-tippers (3 in) as well as rear**, and a **wrap-around
  bumper** as standard.
- Its warranty section covers **the GC340R only**: lifetime frame, 2 yr
  drive train and electronics, 1 yr batteries ([[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]]).

Same R-Series flash/beep codes 1–9 ([[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]]); serial
number under the mat, right rear; brake-handle freewheel lever
("engage" / "disengage" on the label).
