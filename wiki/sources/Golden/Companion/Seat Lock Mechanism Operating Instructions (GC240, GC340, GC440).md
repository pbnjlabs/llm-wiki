# Seat Lock Mechanism Operating Instructions (GC240, GC340, GC440)

1-page instructions: push the seat down onto the seat post until it locks
over the seat swivel daisy; pull and hold the seat lock handle outward to
release. Same mechanism on left-hand, right-hand, and power-elevating seat
variants — only the handle location differs.
