# Replacement Potentiometer with Mounting Bracket (GC221, GC321, GC421)

1-page instructions for replacing the throttle potentiometer assembly (Tocos
or Clarostat, plastic or metal paddle) on the older Companion generation:
remove rear tiller cover, swap the potentiometer/paddle assembly, reinstall.
