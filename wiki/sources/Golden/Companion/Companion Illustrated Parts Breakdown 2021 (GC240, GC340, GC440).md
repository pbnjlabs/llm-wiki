# Companion Illustrated Parts Breakdown 2021 (GC240, GC340, GC440)

31-page IPB (rev 04.27.2021) for GC240/GC340/GC440 **before the 2024
GC440 change**, with no GC540. The newer
[[Companion and Companion HD Illustrated Parts Breakdown]] (Nov 2024)
supersedes it for current units. Use this one for **pre-2024 units**.

Drawings: tiller, chassis moldings, front chassis, steering, rear
chassis and drive system, seat, electrical, miscellaneous, accessories.

Useful parts:
- Controller **3051600170, Dynamic DR90-A01 (90 A)**. This is the same
  part the newer IPB and the GL111/141 IPB list.
- Transaxle **MBX-CT3-TRANS** (CT3).
- Batteries: **U1 (MBE-BATTU1-BW)**, or **12V/40 Ah (MBE-BATT1240)**.
  There is a power-seat battery cable set with an extra lead.
- Chargers: 3.5 A XLR (MBE-CHG35A-STD) and 5.0 A (MBE-CHG50A-STD).
- Circuit breaker assembly SE06E602; 3 A blade fuse (MBE-FUSE3A-BLADE)
  in the dash.
- A separate controller cover is listed for **VA/DME** units
  (MBB-C40-CC-A).
- Several parts are shared with other lines: the mirror set fits the
  GB106 after January 2010, the mirror bracket fits GB106/116/146 and
  GL110/140, and the basket plate fits the GL110.
