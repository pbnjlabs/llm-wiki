# Companion Owner's Manual, DME Edition (GC240, GC340, GC440)

40-page owner's manual (LIT-GC240-GC340-GC440-OM-DME, Rev. A). The file
name says 2025; the PDF is dated Nov 2023. This is the **retail (DME)
edition** of the same book as [[Companion Owners Manual (VA Contract Variant)]].
A word-level comparison shows the only differences:

- Suspension is described as **optional** here; the VA edition says the
  GC340 and GC440 have it and the GC240 doesn't.
- The standard **consumer scooter warranty** (models list, GR575 5-year
  frame note) instead of the VA warranty and its 2-year service plan.
- No military branch patch or USB disclaimer.

**Specs (p. 13–14, images), GC240 / GC340 / GC440:** 350 / 400 / 400 lb,
Medicare K0807, 5.1 mph. Range 15.1 mi (GC240); GC340 19.2 mi on 35 Ah or
29.13 mi on 50 Ah; GC440 18.7 / 28.34 mi. Batteries **12V 35 Ah U1 × 2**
(GC340/440 may take **50 Ah × 2**). Charger **3.5 A** (GC240, 148 in
cord) or **8 A** (GC340/440, 10.5 ft). **90 A** controller. **Sleep
mode after 15 min.** Length 42.25 / 46 / 48 in; wheelbase 30 / 34.5 /
35.5 in. **Assembled 170.8 / 207 / 222 lb; heaviest piece 50 / 57 /
67.5 lb.** Front tires 9 / 9 / 10.75 in, rear 10.75 in. Seat 18 × 16 in
(20 × 18 optional); **power elevating seat option, 6 in rise**. Warranty
lifetime frame, 2 yr drive train and electronics, 1 yr batteries.

Codes 1–9 on [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]]; serial number under the
carpet, right rear.
