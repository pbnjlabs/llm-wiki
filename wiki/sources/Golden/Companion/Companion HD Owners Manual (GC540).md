# Companion HD Owner's Manual (GC540)

40-page owner's manual (LIT-GC540-OM-DME, Rev. A; undated, date from
the PDF). The first GC540 owner's manual on file; before this only the
IPB covered it.

**Specs (p. 14–15):** **500 lb**, 5.7 mph, 26.8 mi, 6.25 in clearance to
the deck, 3.75 in to the transaxle, 51 in turning radius, **2-NF22**
(37 lb each), **8 A** off-board charger, **120 A** controller, 48 × 26
in, 36 in wheelbase. **225.5 lb assembled**: base 101.5, seat 50,
batteries 37 each. 10.75 in solid tires. Captain's chair 22 × 20 in,
4 seat heights. No mechanical hand brake. Medicare K0808. Warranty
**5 yr frame**, 2 yr drive train and electronics, 1 yr batteries.

Other content:
- LCD display, power LED, speed dial, light switch, dual throttles.
- **Flash/beep codes 1–9**, the same R-Series table:
  [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]].
- **Freewheel:** pull the lever **back toward N**; push forward toward
  **D** to drive.
- Warranty: the scooter text with no model list; it says "*The **GR540**
  is warranted for FIVE YEARS*", a typo for GC540 ([[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]]).

> [!note] Controller rating
> This manual says **120 A**; the Companion/Companion HD IPB lists a
> Dynamics Rhino 2 DS120 rated 140 A. Go by the label on the unit.
