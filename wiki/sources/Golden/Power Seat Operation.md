# Power Seat Adjustment

1-page operator guidance: power-elevating seat switch on the tiller below
the throttle. Speed interlocks to seat height — half speed at ~50% seat
height, no movement at all above ~75% seat height. Explicitly framed as
"Problem Solving" — a scooter that won't run may just need the seat
lowered.
