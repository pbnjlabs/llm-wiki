# GR596 Eagle All Terrain Owner's Manual

28-page owner's manual (LIT-GR596-OM, Rev. B) for the **GR596 Eagle All
Terrain**, the successor to the [[GR595-Eagle]].

**Specs (p. 12):** **500 lb**, **11.2 mph**, 34 mi on 75 Ah, 4 in
clearance under the deck, 3.5 in to the transaxle, 76 in turning
radius, **75–100 Ah** batteries, **200 A** controller, **63 × 31.7 in**,
43.5 in wheelbase. **377 lb (75 Ah) / 407 lb (100 Ah) assembled**: main
body 208, seat 54, batteries 52 (75 Ah) or 67 (100 Ah) each. Folded
without the seat it's 20.5 in high. 13 × 6.5 in pneumatic tires
(keep at **35 psi**), 3 in anti-tips. Captain's high-back seat 20 × 18
in. Max incline 6°. Warranty **5 yr frame, 13 months drive train and
electronics, 1 yr batteries** (spec page; no warranty section).

Other content:
- Assembly: tiller up, rear shroud off (unplug its wiring), seat post
  bolted in facing the right way, batteries strapped in and plugged,
  seat drops in at 45° and rotates forward to lock.
- Dash: speed dial, dual throttles, lights, horn, **turn signals,
  hazards**, digital display with three themes, odometer. USB and a
  **Bluetooth speaker** ("Golden") by the key switch.
- **Power on with the key or an NFC fob** held to the reader above the
  display; 2 keys and 2 fobs in the box.
- **Freewheel switch** at the rear near the right drive wheel; works
  only with the batteries in.
- **Display E-codes with beeps:** E1 sleep mode (15 min idle, key off
  and on), E2–E9 match the S-Drive beep codes ([[S-Drive-Beep-Codes]]).
- Charger port on the **left side of the tiller**; it won't move while
  charging.
