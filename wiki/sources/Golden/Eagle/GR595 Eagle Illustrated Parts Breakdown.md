# GR595 Eagle Illustrated Parts Breakdown

19-page IPB (Jan 2025). Drawings: tiller, chassis moldings, front
chassis and steering, rear chassis and drive system (two drawings),
seat, electrical (two drawings), accessories.

Useful parts:
- Controller **MBC-595-CNT** (S-Drive). Circuit breaker with harness
  MBE-595-BREAKER.
- Batteries: **22NF (MBE-BATT22-BW)** or **Group 24, 75 Ah
  (MBE-BATT24-BW)**. The 75 Ah option is a **Group 24** battery.
- Charger: **8 A high-power** MBE-8ACHG-HP; XLR charger port MBE-595-CP.
- Transaxle MBX-595-TRANS-BA. Front and rear main harnesses; a
  **Bluetooth harness**; turn signals; headlight.
- Body panels come in red, stone grey and desert tan.
