# GR595 Eagle Owner's Manual

40-page owner's manual for the Golden Eagle off-road heavy-duty
scooter.

- **Specs:** 500 lb, 8 mph, 32 mi range, **two NF22 or two 75 Ah**
  batteries, **140 A** controller, 69 in turning radius, 56.5 × 27 in,
  40 in axle to axle, 5 in deck clearance (3.5 in under the
  transaxle/motor), 13 in pneumatic tires (**35 psi**), 3 in anti-tip
  wheels, captain's high-back seat with slide and swivel.
- **Weights:** **300 lb assembled**, base 239 lb, seat 49.5 lb,
  batteries 37 lb each (NF22) or 52 lb each (75 Ah). The Jun 2024 sell
  sheet says **302.5 lb assembled with a 179 lb base**. The base figures
  don't agree, so weigh the unit if it matters for a lift.
- **Controller:** PG **S-Drive**. Fault codes flash and also show as an
  E-code on the LCD: 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 (see [[S-Drive-Beep-Codes]]).
  The manual adds checking tire inflation under code 9. Codes and serial
  number are printed **under the floor mat**.
- Also covers disassembly and assembly, transporting, care, batteries
  and charging, "if it doesn't operate" and warranty.
