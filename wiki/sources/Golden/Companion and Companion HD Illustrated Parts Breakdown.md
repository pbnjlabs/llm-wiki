# Companion & Companion HD Illustrated Parts Breakdown (GC240, GC340, GC440, GC540)

18-page parts catalog covering the current Companion lineup, LIT-COMPB-IPB.
Establishes that **GC540 ("Companion HD") is a distinct heavier-duty
variant**, not just a color option of GC240/340/440:

- Shares the tiller/handle assembly design with GC340/GC440 (same drawing
  page), but has its own frame components throughout: front chassis, rear
  chassis, chassis moldings, and electrical system are all separate
  drawing pages for GC540 vs. the shared GC240/GC340/GC440 pages.
- Different battery: GC540 uses one 22NF battery pair (MBE-BATT22-BW) vs.
  GC240/340/440's U1 batteries (or 50AH on 340/440).
- Different controller: GC540 uses a Dynamics Rhino 2 (DS120, 140A) vs.
  GC240/340/440's Dynamics 90A DR90-A01.
- Different seat: GC540 uses an SN2 swivel-with-lock seat (distinct part
  numbers) vs. the standard slide mechanism (MBH-MMSSM) shared by
  GC221/321/421/240/340/440.
- Front/rear wheel sizes and anti-tip wheel size (2" GC540 vs. 3" GC240)
  also differ.
