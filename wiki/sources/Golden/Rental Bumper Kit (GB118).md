# GB118 (Buzzaround Extreme, 3-wheel) Rental Bumper Kit

Part MBB-118R-KIT: front/left/right/rear bumpers + 4 right-angle brackets.
Install: remove rear anti-tip wheels and (optionally) rear wheels, bolt
rear bumper to frame, bolt right-angle brackets to frame sides, mount side
bumpers to brackets, slide front bumper on and secure with 4 set screws,
final-tighten all 16 bolts. No front-wheel removal needed (contrast with
the 4-wheel GB148 kit).

Feeds: [[GB118-148-Buzzaround-EX]].
