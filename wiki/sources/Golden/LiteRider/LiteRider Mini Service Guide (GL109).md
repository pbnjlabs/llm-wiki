# LiteRider Mini Service Guide (GL109)

28-page service guide (rev A) for the **discontinued** LiteRider Mini
GL109.

- **Controller:** Dynamics R-Series. 18–24 VDC; **two 12 Ah** batteries
  in a removable pack.
- Main parts: rear frame, a drivetrain (transaxle, motor and brake),
  plastic decks, a deck basket and **anti-tip wheels**.
- Beep codes 1–9: the same as [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]].
- Replacement steps: drive wheel, drive train, throttle pot, battery,
  circuit breaker, battery-harness fuse, controller, front-to-rear
  harness, control panel, motor/brake.
- Voltmeter/ohmmeter appendices.
