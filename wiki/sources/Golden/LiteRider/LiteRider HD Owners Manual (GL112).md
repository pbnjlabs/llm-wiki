# LiteRider HD Owner's Manual (GL112)

42-page owner's manual for the **GL112 LiteRider HD**.

**Specs (p. 14–15):** **400 lb**, rear-wheel drive, 4 mph, 20.7 mi
(U1) or 25 mi (40 Ah), 6 in clearance to the deck and 2 in to the
transaxle, 40 in turning radius, **2-U1 standard or 2-40 Ah**, **90 A**
controller, 44 × 24 in, 32 in wheelbase. **171 lb assembled** with a
**one-piece 102 lb frame** (it doesn't split like the other
LiteRiders); seat 27 lb, batteries 21 lb each. 10 in solid tires,
**3 in front and 2 in rear anti-tip wheels**. 20 × 18 in stadium seat,
slide seat standard. Warranty lifetime frame, 2 yr drive train and
electronics.

Other content:
- **Beep codes 1–9** (one long beep, then the count): R-Series table,
  [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]].
- **Freewheel:** push the lever **up** for freewheel, **down** for
  drive.
- Warranty list adds **LiteRider HD: GL112** ([[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]]).
