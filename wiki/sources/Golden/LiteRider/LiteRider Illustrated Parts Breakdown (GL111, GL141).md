# LiteRider Illustrated Parts Breakdown (GL111/GL141)

13-page parts catalog covering the newer LiteRider generation: tiller
assembly, chassis moldings, front chassis (GL111 3-wheel vs. GL141
4-wheel frame variants), 3-wheel and 4-wheel steering systems, rear
chassis & drive system, battery pack, two seat-assembly revisions (split
by serial number — models built before vs. on/after specific serials get
different seats), electrical system, miscellaneous items, and accessories.

Confirms the **controller is shared with the Companion line**: table entry
"Controller, 90 Amp for Companion GC240/GC340/GC440 and LiteRider
GL111/GL141" (part 3051600170) — direct textual evidence, not inferred,
that GL111/141 uses the same Dynamics R-Series 90A controller as
[[GC240-340-440-540-Companion]]. This is why [[Flash-Beep-Diagnostics]]
applies to both lines.

Transaxle part (5B, RA11E103) is labeled "for LiteRider GL110/GL140" even
in the GL111/141 IPB — the drivetrain component didn't change between
generations, only electronics/cosmetics did.
