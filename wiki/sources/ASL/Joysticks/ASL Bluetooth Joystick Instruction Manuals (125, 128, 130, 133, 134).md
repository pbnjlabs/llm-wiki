# ASL Bluetooth Joystick Instruction Manuals (125, 128, 130, 133, 134)

Five two-page sheets for ASL's **Bluetooth specialty joysticks**. All use
the same **Bluetooth interface box** (4.38 × 2.38 × 1.25 in, 60 in leads,
multiple connector options) and connect to a **LiNX connector or Input
Module, Omni or Omni 2, or Enhanced Display / SCIM** (the MEC also
**MK6i**). Rev 8-2024 except the 130 (Rev 5-2020). Feeds
[[Alternative-Drive-Controls]].

| Model | Type | Force to move / throw | Mounts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **125 Orbit** | Mini proportional (cup/foam/cork tips) | **50 g, 4 mm** | 660 Nucleus midline, 603OM platform, 674 palm tray, 613 flex arm, 687/688 dot switch systems, 628 swing-away headrest, 625 collar |
| **128 Molecule** | Micro proportional | **8 g, 3 mm** | as Orbit (673 palm tray) |
| **130 MEC** (Micro Extremity Control) | Micro joystick, hand or chin | **18 g, 9 mm** | hand: 603M, 660, 613 (+644 ring); chin: 625, 625A small collar, 628 |
| **133 Compact, single switch** | Compact proportional, one mono plug | **240 g, 23 mm** | 603 platform (L or R), 660 |
| **134 Compact, dual switch** | As 133, two mono plugs | **240 g, 23 mm** | 603, 660 |

## Shared interface box

- **Slide switches (DIP):** both **down = regular control**; **switch 1
  up = 3-directional proportional**.
- **User port:** any switch press puts the joystick into **Bluetooth
  mode** (long beep).
- **L/R mouse click port:** stereo plug = left and right click, mono =
  left click. **It must be plugged in before the chair powers up or it
  isn't recognized.**
- **Bluetooth status light:** flashing = not paired, solid = paired.
  Pairing switch starts pairing. Pairs to an ASL accessory or straight to
  an iOS or Android device.
- **Forward indicator:** Orbit has a mark on the side, Molecule and MEC an
  arrow underneath, and on the Compact the **cable comes out of the
  right side**.

## Model notes

- **MEC:** **pushing straight down from neutral = reset**, or set the
  interface box so **reverse becomes reset**. The **dust cover must stay
  on at all times** or it throws errors.
- **133:** its one mono plug goes to the user port (drive ↔ Bluetooth) or
  the left-click port; leave it unplugged if the client hits it by
  accident. **134:** two mono plugs for left/right click, the user port,
  or a seating interface (seat functions on demand).
- All: liquid or debris in the top causes **error codes or unwanted
  movement**; not in rain or snow. Reset switch to a port programmed to
  stop the chair. Two-year warranty.
- Accessories listed: 558 mouse emulator, **557-4** 4-output switch
  receiver (USB or ASL 540 power; with an ASL 555 transmitter it lets a
  non-ASL drive control act as switches), 540 battery.
