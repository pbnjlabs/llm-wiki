# Bulletin: Lifting Strap Replacement Procedure, Boom Lifts

CSV-000047 Rev A, effective 29Jan16, worked example on an AL-425. Applies
to Harmar's "Boom Lifts" — the inside/hoist vehicle-lift family
documented in the [[Hoist-Series-Inside-Vehicle-Lifts]] page.

## Covers
Step-by-step strap swap: unplug power/lifting motor, remove the arm from
the post, remove the motor cover (manual-override lowering if no 12V
power is available), unwind the strap, replace the strap tab/hook
hardware, feed the new strap over both rollers and the top of the spool
(critical — strap must wind flat with no twist), and reassemble. Notes
excessive strap wear is usually caused by lifting at an angle (chair/
scooter not centered under the strap).
