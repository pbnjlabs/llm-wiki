# Video: Changing the Hand Control Side and Wire Harness (Pinnacle SL300)

Harmar training video, 7:50, newer captioned style with voiceover. The
file name carries "090425v04" (probably v4 of 4 Sep 2025). It moves the
**armrest control switch** (paddle/hand control) from one armrest to
the other on a [[Pinnacle-SL300]] seat, re-routing its harness through
the armrest tube. Questions to training@harmar.com.

**Tools (0:08):** **3/16 in hex wrench**, **5/8 in socket**, wire cutters,
crimpers, Phillips drill bit, **four spade connectors**, a **pull wire**
(fish tape) and tape.

## Steps

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:28 | Pop the cap off the seat-belt bolt; remove the **seat belt and bolt** (3/16 in hex plus 5/8 in socket). Both sides. |
| 0:45 | Remove all the screws under **both armrests** (Phillips) and take off the bottom cover. |
| 1:08 | Fold the armrest down and take out the **control switch**. **Cut the single orange, green and black wires.** Leave the **short orange wire** in place, cut the **long orange** from its connector, and take the last orange off the switch terminal. |
| 1:33 | Remove the remaining covers. Fold the armrest up and pull the harness out of the back of the armrest. |
| 1:56 | Lay the seat face down. Remove the **rubber grommet** and pull the harness out of the bottom of the armrest tube. |
| 2:11 | Take the **rubber plug** out of the other armrest. Push a **pull wire** down the tube from the top, tape the harness on, and pull it through and out by the armrest. |
| 3:04 | Grommet onto the harness. Route the harness **between the armrest bolt and the metal frame**. |
| 3:23 | Strip the green, black and orange ends and both ends of the small orange jumper. Crimp a spade on the **small orange**; **twist the two oranges together** under one **blue spade**; spades on the **black** and the **green**. |
| 4:48 | Connect each wire to its terminal on the switch (matching the original). |
| 5:10 | Covers and screws back on. Fit the switch into its opening and fix it from underneath with its screw. |
| 6:28 | **Seat belt on the switch side:** push the harness aside so the **bolt doesn't pinch it**. Refit the **nylock nut** (3/16 in hex, 5/8 in socket) and the cap. Both sides. |
| 7:14 | **Rubber plug into the now-empty harness hole**; push the grommet home with a screwdriver. |

The video doesn't show which terminal each colour goes to; note or
photograph the switch before cutting.

## Checked against the manuals

Matches "Procedure to Switch Armrest Control from Right to Left Hand" in
the [[Pinnacle SL300 Install Manual (2026, Rev L)]]: power off, remove the
paddle switch, cut the connectors, remove the seat-belt bolts, reroute the
harness, re-terminate, reassemble. The manual's first step is **turn the
unit OFF**, which the video doesn't say. With **power footrest or power
swivel**, the extra control wiring must be moved too; see
[[Pinnacle Power Swivel and Power Footrest Install Supplement]].
