# Bulletin: Pinnacle Rail Length Change (2018)

CSVF-000051 Rev A, effective 8/6/2018. Affects SL300/SL600/SL600HD (the
"SL600HD" name is not seen anywhere else in this wiki's Harmar coverage).

## Covers
Standardized the single rail section length to 7'9" (a standard unit now
ships with 15'6" total, two 7'9" sections) and changed the recommended
overrun from 13" down to 7" — matching what the current SL300/SL600
install manuals already document, confirming this 2018 change is now
just the baseline. Mid-section options: 3', 5', or 7'9". Formula: measure
bottom landing to top nose (A to B), add 7" of overrun to determine total
rail length to cut.
