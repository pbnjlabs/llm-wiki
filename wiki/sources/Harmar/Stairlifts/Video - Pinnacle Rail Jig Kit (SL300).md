# Video: Pinnacle Rail Jig Kit (SL300)

Harmar training video, 3:33, newer captioned style with voiceover. The
title card says **Pinnacle SL300**. The file name carries "090425v04"
(probably v4 of 4 Sep 2025). It shows how to **drill and pin a cut rail
end** so it can be spliced to the next section, using Harmar's **rail
jig**. Questions to training@harmar.com.

**Kit and tools (0:10):** C-clamp, **rail jig**, **splice pins**, **¼ in
drill bit**, **countersink bit**, and two **pin depth gauges** (small
and large).

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:19 | 1. Slide the jig over the end of the rail. |
| 0:30 | 2. C-clamp the jig to the rail. |
| 0:34 | 3. Drill through the jig's holes (¼ in). |
| 0:43 | 4. Turn the rail over and repeat on the other side. |
| 1:06 | 5. Remove the clamp and jig. |
| 1:09 | 6. **Countersink the holes top and bottom**, deep enough that the **screw heads sit flush or just below the rail surface**. |
| 1:33 | 7. Drive the pins in **about ½ in** with a hammer. |
| 2:03 | 8. **Small gauge sets the depth of the inner pins.** |
| 2:36 | 9. **Large gauge sets the depth of the outer pins.** |

No pin depths are given in numbers; the gauges set them.

## Checked against the manuals

The [[Pinnacle SL300 Install Manual (2026, Rev L)]] says only that
"Harmar offers a rail jig that allows holes to be drilled in the end of
a rail; contact techservices@harmar.com", and tells you to take the
charge strips and wire harness out before cutting. It also says **don't
cut the end of the rail that has the joint** (splice pins). This video
is the only procedure on file for a jig-drilled end. The jig's part
number isn't given.
