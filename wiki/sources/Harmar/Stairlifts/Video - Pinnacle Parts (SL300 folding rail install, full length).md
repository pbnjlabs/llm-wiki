# Video: Pinnacle Parts (SL300 folding rail install, full length)

Harmar training video, 15:46, titled **"Pinnacle Parts – Pinnacle
SL300"**. The file name carries "050626v07" (probably v7 of 6 May 2026).
Despite the title, it's the **whole folding-rail install in one video**:
the 13 short clips in
[[Video - Pinnacle Install Series (Parts 01-13, folding rail)]], in the
same order with the same captions and voiceover, plus an intro ("a
typical two-story home") and parts layout at the start and a few steps the short clips don't have. **The title card
confirms the series is an SL300**, so its 25 ft-lb footplate torque
conflicts with the current SL300 manual (46 ft-lb); see the series
page. Times below are for this long version.

## What's in the box (0:10)

Labelled on screen: chassis, seat, footrest, **charge strips**, **3 ft
mid rail**, **7'9" top rail (cut to length)**, **folding rail**, **end
cap**, **splice bars**, **six mounting brackets**, hardware box.

## Steps the short clips don't have

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:38 | **Mount the brackets with their bolts towards the wall.** |
| 4:43 | **Before joining sections, pull the charge wire tight from the top** so it can't get pinched between them. |
| 4:51 | **Join the folding rail and the mid rail**: fit the flange-head screws into the splice-bar threads with a 3/16 in hex. The heads must sit low enough for the white gear rack to slide over. **Same on the underside.** |
| 5:44 | Join the **top rail and mid rail** the same way, top and underside. |
| 6:41 | **Slide the white gear-rack pieces down to close the gaps at both splice joints**, but **don't fill the whole gap: leave at least two rack pieces out**. In part 06 the last piece is cut to fit and slid in after the chassis is loaded. |

Everything else (bracket spacing, splice bars, fish-taping the charge
wire, chassis plate and hockey-stick bracket, loading, end cap,
clearance, anchoring, footplate, swivel post, seat, final adjustment)
matches the short clips. The same conflicts with the manuals apply:
**3 in minimum rail clearance** and **46 ft-lb SL300 footplate bolts**.
