# Video: Pinnacle Folding Rail Hand Change

Harmar training video, 9:19, newer captioned style with voiceover. The
file name carries "100825v02" (probably v2 of 8 Oct 2025). It converts a
Pinnacle **folding rail from one hand to the other** on the bench,
with the chassis off ([[Pinnacle-SL300]]; the kit is DWG 130-00551 in
[[Harmar Parts Diagrams]]). Questions to training@harmar.com.

**Tools (0:08):** **½ in (13 mm) wrench**, **3/16 in Allen**, **¼ in
Allen**, **9/16 in socket**, **½ in socket**, marker.

## Strip down

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:24 | Lay the folding rail on the bench. |
| 0:29 | Take the **wire retaining clips** off both ends of the **gas strut** and pull the strut off its ball mounts. |
| 0:57 | Remove the **ball mounts** from the pivot link and the sliding lift arm (13 mm). |
| 1:07 | **3/16 in Allen:** the bolt holding the fork link to the link-arm bracket, then the bolt holding the link arm to the pivot bracket. Set the link aside. |
| 1:45 | **¼ in Allen:** the **shoulder bolt holding the fork plate**, then the shoulder bolt from the sliding lift arm. |
| 2:49 | ¼ in Allen on the shoulder bolt holding the folding linkage; 13 mm on its nut. Put the shoulder bolt and **jam nut** back on the **top side of the folding link** and set it aside. |

## Swap the brackets

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 3:30 | **Mark the rail** where the slider assembly sits. |
| 3:45 | Loosen (don't remove) the **two nylock nuts** on the slider (½ in socket). Slide it off, **turn it 180°**, slide it back on to the mark. |
| 4:21 | **Flip the whole folding rail over.** |
| 4:44 | **Mark the pivot bracket position**, loosen its two bolts (**9/16 in**), take the **FR pivot bracket** off, **flip it over**, and refit it to the mark. |

## Rebuild

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 5:48 | Shoulder bolt into the slider with its jam nut; screw it into the folding-link bracket and refit the bracket bolt. Tighten with the ¼ in Allen; **jam nut tight with 13 mm**. |
| 6:42 | **Fork bracket** on, shoulder bolt, tighten (¼ in Allen). |
| 7:13 | **Link arm under the fork and folding brackets**; a small shoulder bolt at each end; tighten (3/16 in). |
| 7:51 | Ball mounts back on the link arm and the fork bracket; tighten (13 mm). |
| 8:13 | Gas strut onto one ball, **compress it slightly** to get it onto the other, and refit **both retaining clips**. |
| 8:54 | With the rail flipped back, **tighten the slider's nylock nuts** (½ in socket). |

## Checked against the manuals (the manual wins)

Same job as "Reversing the Folding Rail Handing" in the
[[Pinnacle SL300 Install Manual (2026, Rev L)]], which **only a trained
dealer, installer or service tech** may do (about 15+ minutes). The
manual adds what the video leaves out:
- **The gas strut is under compression.** Keep control of it.
- **Pinch points** at the folding joint when the rail is folded.
- **Torques:** lift-arm pivot shoulder bolt about **15 ft-lb**; lower
  slide-bracket nuts about **10 ft-lb** (½ in socket).
- **Final slider position:** unfold the rail and set the lower slide
  bracket with about a **¼ in gap between the shoulder bolt and the
  front end of the slot**, then tighten. The video just goes back to the
  reference mark.
- Protect the finish with carpet or cardboard on the bench.
