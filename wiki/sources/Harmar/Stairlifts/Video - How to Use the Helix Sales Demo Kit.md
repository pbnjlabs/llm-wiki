# Video: How to Use the Helix Sales Demo Kit

Harmar training video #11, 6:06, presented by Harmar's VP of
Marketing. How to use the **Helix demo rail kit** in an in-home sales
visit for the [[Helix]] curved stairlift. **It's a sales pitch**: the
figures are Harmar's marketing claims, not specs from a manual. No date.

**Kit (0:20):** a **2 ft section of rail**, **two mounting brackets**, an
**upholstery sample**. Bring the **Helix Lookbook** and a tape measure.

## Talking points

| Time | Claim |
|---|---|
| 0:36 | **Dual-beam (twin-tube) rail**: open design, so it looks lighter and anything dropped behind it is easy to retrieve. |
| 0:55 | The rail takes **about 7 in from the wall**, so a **36 in stair leaves about 29 in** to pass. |
| 1:32 | **Gear rack mounted underneath**: it stays out of dust and debris, and its **lubricant stays off clothes, pets and kids**. Some competitors put the rack on top. |
| 2:17 | **About 8 lb per foot** of rail, against 12–16 lb for competitors. So fewer brackets and **2–3 bolts per foot** instead of 6–8. |
| 2:58 | **Square bracket at the top** of the stairs for extra hold; **round brackets** along the run, which stick out less. |
| 3:21 | Rail in **black or beige** as standard; custom colours cost extra. Upholstery **beige or black** standard; samples of either or both are available; custom colours optional. |
| 3:53 | The **Lookbook** shows styled homes, outdoor installs, colour schemes and sweeping staircases. |
| 4:37 | **100% made in the USA**, rails fabricated in **Sarasota, Florida**. Every rail is custom, so it stays close to the wall and at a **consistent height above the steps** the whole way. Prefab-section competitors can't match that. |
| 5:32 | Called "the fastest-growing stairlift in the United States". |

## Use

Useful sales points for a Helix quote. Note the **gear rack needs
lubrication** (unlike the Pinnacle's nylon rack). Measuring and clearances
come from [[Helix Photo Measuring Manual (2023, Rev C)]] and the
[[Helix Install Manual]], not from this video.
