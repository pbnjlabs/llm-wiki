# Video: Changing the Pinnacle Board

Harmar training video #5, 9:27, presented by a member of Harmar's
technical support group. Replacing the **circuit board** in a Pinnacle
chassis ([[Pinnacle-SL300]], [[Pinnacle-SL600]]). The seat and footrest
removal is the same as [[Video - Changing the Batteries in the Pinnacle]].
No date or board part number.

## Getting to the board

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:11 | Power off, unplug the seat and footrest cables, take the footrest covers off (lift the cover, don't pry the Christmas-tree clips), wiggle the seat off, loosen the footrest-frame bolts and lift the footrest off. |
| 1:39 | Chassis top cover off (screws), then the metal inner cover: **Allen screws on either side** (the size is misheard as "30-second"; probably 3/32 or 5/32 in, so check). |
| 5:10 | An **anti-static wrist strap** is worn (shown, not stated). |

## Wires on the board (all labelled on the board)

| Label | What |
|---|---|
| **C+ / C−** | Charge wires from the charge shoes at the bottom of the chassis |
| **IR** (two plugs) | Upper and lower infrared sensors. **Either sensor can go in either port.** |
| **M1 / M2** | Motor |
| **B− / B+** | Battery. **Keep these apart from the motor leads**: they look alike. |
| **Brake** | Electromagnetic brake (small pink or brown wires) |
| **30-pin** | At the back of the board: all the sensors |

**Label the wires before unplugging if in any doubt** (2:52).

## Swap

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 3:36 | Remove the **4 corner screws**; the board comes free. |
| 4:36 | The new board comes in an **anti-static bag**. **Screw it down first, then reconnect.** |
| 5:53 | Reconnect from the **back of the board forward**: 30-pin, brake, B−, B+, motor, IR, C+ / C−. |
| 7:23 | **Power on briefly**: a start-up beep and a **blinking diagnostic light** mean it's good. |
| 7:49 | Reassemble: hang the footrest frame on its keyhole slots, **level or slightly uphill**, tighten top and bottom bolts; covers and clips; seat onto the post with the lever held down; plug in the seat and footrest cables. |

## Checked against the manuals

No board-replacement procedure is on file. Footrest bolt torque on
reassembly: **46 ft-lb SL300** (2026 Rev L), **25 ft-lb SL600**; see
[[Video - Pinnacle Install Series (Parts 01-13, folding rail)]]. The SL300
and SL600 use the same control software
(`800-00004 SOFTWARE, SL300/SL600`, [[Harmar Parts Diagrams]]).
