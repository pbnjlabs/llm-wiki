# Video: How to Change the Helix Batteries

Harmar training video #33, 1:26, by a Harmar Technical Services tech.
Battery swap on the [[Helix]] curved stairlift. No date.

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:10 | Remove the **front shroud (4 Phillips screws)** (already off in the video). |
| 0:22 | Disconnect the **negative and positive leads** and feed them back through the chassis so the old batteries can come out. |
| 0:34 | Remove the **jumper wire**, then lift out the old pair. |
| 0:39 | Fit the new batteries **one at a time** and refit the jumper: **negative of one battery to positive of the other** (series). |
| 0:58 | Run the leads back through the chassis: **black (negative) to the negative of one battery, the positive lead to the positive of the other.** |

## Checked against the manuals

Matches the [[Helix Install Manual]] battery section: black wire to the
black terminal of one battery, red wire to the red terminal of the
other, the **white jumper** from black of one to red of the other. The
manual adds the warning the video leaves out: **wired wrong, it can
damage the universal access board.** No battery part number or size in
the video.
