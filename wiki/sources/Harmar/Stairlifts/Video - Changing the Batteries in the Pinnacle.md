# Video: Changing the Batteries in the Pinnacle

Harmar training video #6, 4:26, presented by a member of Harmar's
technical support group. Battery swap on a Pinnacle
([[Pinnacle-SL300]], [[Pinnacle-SL600]]). No date or battery part number.

## Removal

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:12 | **Power off** and **unplug the seat and footrest cables** from the chassis. |
| 0:27 | Take the **covers off the footrest frame**. Put the screwdriver **between the frame and the cover and lift the cover**, not on the clips themselves, so the **Christmas-tree clips** don't break. |
| 0:48 | **Seat off:** grip the seat supports and wiggle it back and forth until it pops off. Set it on the upper landing. |
| 1:06 | **Loosen the bolts on the front of the footrest frame**, lift the whole footrest assembly off, and set it aside. |
| 1:40 | **Chassis cover: 2 Phillips screws on top**, then the cover comes off either side. The batteries are at the **back** of the unit. |
| 1:59 | **Remove the bracket** to get at the battery leads, disconnect, and lift the batteries out. Drop in the new ones and reconnect. |

## Reassembly

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 2:52 | **Hang the footrest frame** on its bolts with the **keyhole slots** on the back. **Set it level, or slightly uphill if not level**, then tighten the top and bottom bolts. |
| 3:35 | Covers back on with the Christmas-tree clips. |
| 3:51 | **Seat:** line its two holes up, hold the swivel (seat) lever down, and it locks onto the post. |
| 4:08 | Plug the footrest and seat cables back in. |

## Checked against the manuals

No step-by-step battery procedure is on file; the owner's manuals only
say replace batteries **about every 1–2 years**, use compliant
replacements from the dealer, and inspect yearly. **When re-hanging the
footrest, use the install manual's level and torque**: footplate bolts
**46 ft-lb on the SL300** (2026 Rev L) or **25 ft-lb on the SL600**. See
[[Video - Pinnacle Install Series (Parts 01-13, folding rail)]] for the
torque disagreement.
