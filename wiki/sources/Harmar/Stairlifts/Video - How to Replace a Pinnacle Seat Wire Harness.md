# Video: How to Replace a Pinnacle Seat Wire Harness

Harmar training video #7, 10:17, by a Harmar Technical Services tech.
Taking out and refitting the **seat wiring harness** on a Pinnacle
seat ([[Pinnacle-SL300]], [[Pinnacle-SL600]]); the demo re-uses the old
harness. No date or harness part number.

**Tools:** Phillips screwdriver, wrenches for the seat-bottom bolts,
needle-nose pliers or **Heyco pliers** (for the Heyco strain-relief
bushing).

## Removal

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:11 | Remove the cover (**2 Christmas-tree clips** each side). Loosen the two bolts and set the **seat bottom** aside with its hardware. |
| 0:48 | Turn the seat round and remove the **black Heyco clip** from the back (squeeze with needle-nose or Heyco pliers). |
| 1:06 | Remove the **seat swivel sensor** (a nut each side) and unplug it. |
| 1:20 | **Control arm:** one Phillips screw, tap it off, and unplug the **6-pin connector** (pinch the locking tab). |
| 1:49 | Feed the harness back out. If it's tight, take off the other arm too. **Keep the two nylon washers** with each arm. |

## Refit

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 3:33 | Feed the harness through the back of the seat, then the second 6-pin and the **seat cut-off switch** connector. |
| 4:01 | **Run one 6-pin up each armrest tube.** Then the control arm can be moved to either side later. |
| 4:25 | Seat cut-off switch lead down through the bottom of the seat; plug in, wrap the wire, refit the sensor and its two nuts. |
| 4:52 | Refit the arms: **nylon washer on the inside**, angle and rotate the arm in, **lock nut on the control-arm bolt**. Plug the control arm in (6-pin). |
| 7:00 | Refit the bracket and the seat bottom: a nylon nut each side, then on each bolt a **standard nut followed by a lock nut**. They set the **tension on the nylon washer**. |
| 8:29 | Bracket and Christmas-tree clips back on. |

## Checked against the manuals

No harness procedure is on file. The newer SL300 seat in
[[Video - Changing the Hand Control Side and Wire Harness (Pinnacle SL300)]]
routes only one harness to the control-switch armrest and plugs the
other tube, so the two designs differ; check which seat you have.
