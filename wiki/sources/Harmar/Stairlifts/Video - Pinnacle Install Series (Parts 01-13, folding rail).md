# Video: Pinnacle Install Series (Parts 01–13, folding rail)

Thirteen short Harmar clips (31 s to 1:35 each, about 13 min in all),
newer 1080p style with on-screen captions read by a voiceover. They
install a **Pinnacle with a folding rail**, the hinged bottom section,
on a training staircase, set up **right-hand**. The clips themselves
don't name the model, but the same footage joined into one video is
titled **Pinnacle SL300**
([[Video - Pinnacle Parts (SL300 folding rail install, full length)]]),
which also adds the rail-joining steps. Most steps apply to the
[[Pinnacle-SL600]] too; the torque doesn't (see below). The files are dated June 2026; the videos show no
date. Questions go to **training@harmar.com** (end card).

Ingested from Whisper transcripts, OCR of the captions, and frames every
10 s. Times are per clip. **Where a clip and the manual differ, the
manual wins** (see the bottom of the page).

## 01 Prepping the folding rail (0:48)

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:05 | Turn the folding rail **upside down**. |
| 0:10 | Loosen the top and bottom bracket bolts. |
| 0:13 | Hook the bracket inside the rail extrusion and push to spring it into place. |
| 0:20 | Slide the other bracket on **about 6–8 in from the end**. |
| 0:25 | **Snug only** with a **½ in deep-well socket**; you'll adjust later. |

## 02 Splice bars (1:04)

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:05 | Each splice bar has a **square end and a round end**. Slide the **square end** into the channel until the holes line up; the round end sticks out to guide the next rail. |
| 0:15 | Fix each with **two ¼ in flange-head screws**, **3/16 in Allen**. |
| 0:54 | The screw heads must sit **low enough on the top of the rail for the white gear rack to slide over them**. |

## 03 Mid rail section (0:49)

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:05 | Same again on the mid rail: splice bars on the **upward-facing end**. |
| 0:13 | Brackets **about 4–6 in from each end**, lightly snugged (½ in deep-well). Final adjustment comes when the sections are joined on the stairs. |
| 0:36 | Same for the upper rail: its bracket goes on the **last step before the landing**. |

## 04 Rail assembly and charge wire (1:35)

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:05 | Lay the sections on the stairs with a little gap between them. |
| 0:22 | Push a **fish tape** up through the mid rail, into the top rail, until it comes out the top. |
| 0:34 | Tape it to the **top charge-strip connector** and start feeding the wire in. |
| 0:51 | **Slide the charge strips into place before pulling the wire.** |
| 0:58 | Pull the fish tape until the charge wire comes out the end of the rail. |
| 1:07 | Remove the tape, **plug the charge connectors together**, and **tape over the joint** so it can't pull apart during the rest of the install. |

## 05 Prepping the chassis for the folding rail (1:07)

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:04 | Position the **folding rail plate** on the chassis; start the top bolt, then the bottom bolt, a few turns each. |
| 0:17 | The **small Phillips screw in the small hole aligns the plate**; tighten it. |
| 0:28 | Snug both bolts with a **5/16 in hex**. Clip says torque them to **25 ft-lb** after the footplate is fitted and levelled (see the conflicts below). |
| 0:40 | The **"hockey stick" obstruction bracket goes on the downhill obstruction flap**: two small screws, Phillips. |

## 06 Loading the chassis (0:52)

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:04 | Slide the chassis on. **Keep fingers clear between the chassis bottom and the rail.** |
| 0:11 | Guide it down until it meets the **worm gear**. |
| 0:18 | Power switch on, wait for the beep, then push the **black switch** to drive it down **about 6 in**. |
| 0:32 | **Cut the last piece of gear rack** and slide it in. |
| 0:38 | Slide in the **top cam** and lock it with a **5/64 in hex**. |

## 07 End cap (0:58)

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:05 | Tuck the extra charge wire into the rail end; it passes through the **notch on the back of the end plate**. |
| 0:18 | End cap on with the **4 self-threading screws** from the hardware pack, **T30 Torx** bit. |
| 0:40 | One **rack compression screw** in the threaded hole in the end cap, **as tight as you can by hand with a 5/32 in hex**. |
| 0:50 | Plug the charger into the charge plug at **either the top or the bottom**; this install charges at the top. |

## 08 Running clearance (0:45)

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:05 | Clip says rail-to-stair-nose clearance is **2–3 in for a folding rail**, "closer to 2 in" for a standard rail. **The manuals say at least 3 in**; see the conflicts below. |
| 0:19 | Check with a tape and shift brackets as needed. Also check clearance from the wall or obstacle to the rail. |

## 09 Anchoring the rail (0:31)

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:05 | Harmar supplies **2 in wood screws**. For anything other than wood, **source anchors locally**. |
| 0:14 | Drill, extension and **3/8 in nut driver**; screws go in the **front and back of every bracket**. |

## 10 Footplate (1:12)

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:03 | **Keyhole slot** on the back of the footplate: loosen the chassis bolts and hang it on them. |
| 0:14 | Fold it down and remove the **cover (4 Phillips screws)**. |
| 0:37 | Put a level on the footrest and tip it **within 0.5° of level**. |
| 0:46 | Tighten front and back bolts with a **9/16 in socket**. Clip says **25 ft-lb**; the current SL300 manual says 46 (see the conflicts below). |
| 0:56 | Cover back on. |

## 11 Swivel post (0:58)

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:03 | For a **right-hand install the swivel post has to be moved** to the other side. |
| 0:09 | Remove its **2 screws (5/32 in hex)** and lift it out. |
| 0:26 | **Move the plastic plug** to the hole you just emptied. |
| 0:31 | Refit the post on the other side with the same 2 screws. |

## 12 Seat (1:05)

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:05 | **Hold the swivel handle while lowering the seat** so the stop tab clears the swivel post. |
| 0:19 | Plug in the **8-pin and 6-pin connectors**. |
| 0:29 | Power on, wait for the beep, drive to the top. |
| 0:42 | **Re-tighten every rail bracket** with a ½ in deep-well and a hand ratchet, then run it to the bottom. |

## 13 Final adjustment (1:08)

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:05 | Drive down until the folding rail is **fully deployed**: there must be **no gap at the hinge**. |
| 0:18 | If there is a gap, adjust the **threaded supports (feet)**. |
| 0:25 | The rail brackets have a **3° lean built in**, so the **outer foot is always longer than the inner one**. |
| 0:35 | Clip says use **two 9/16 in wrenches** on the feet. |

## Conflicts with the manuals (the manual wins)

- **Footplate bolt torque.** The clips say **25 ft-lb**. That's what the
  SL600 manual and the 2024 SL300 Rev K say. The current
  [[Pinnacle SL300 Install Manual (2026, Rev L)]] and the
  [[Pinnacle Power Swivel and Power Footrest Install Supplement]] say
  **46 ft-lb**. The video is an SL300, so this is a real conflict.
  **SL300: use 46 ft-lb. SL600: 25 ft-lb** per
  [[Pinnacle SL600 Install Manual]] until Harmar says otherwise.
- **Running clearance.** The clips say **2–3 in** to the stair nose
  ("closer to 2 in" on a standard rail). Both install manuals say the
  underside of the rail must be **at least 3 in above the stair nose**
  for footrest clearance, measured at the second-step bracket and the
  upper bracket on a folding rail. **Use 3 in minimum.** The manual's
  wall clearance for a folding rail is also **3 in minimum** (about ½ in
  left at the gas-spring ball).
- **Foot wrench size.** The clip says 9/16 in; the SL300 Rev L says a
  **½ in open-end wrench**, and the SL600 manual says 9/16 in. Both
  manuals agree the outer foot is set taller because of the built-in
  lean.

## Agrees with the manuals

Splice-bar orientation and 3/16 in Allen, 2 in #14 wood screws on a
3/8 in nut driver, T30 Torx end-cap screws (don't over-torque), the
compression screw hand-tight with a 5/32 in Allen, 5/64 in for the cams,
charging from either end.
