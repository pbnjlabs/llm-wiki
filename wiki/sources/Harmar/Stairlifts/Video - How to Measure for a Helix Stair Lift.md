# Video: How to Measure for a Helix Stair Lift

Harmar training video, 13:00, narrated by a Harmar staff
member. How to shoot a curved staircase with Harmar's **camera
measuring kit** (PhotoMap) so Harmar can draw the [[Helix]] rail in CAD.
No date. The current written guide is
[[Helix Photo Measuring Manual (2023, Rev C)]]; **where they differ,
the guide wins.** Background: [[Staircase-Measuring-System]].

## Laying out markers

| Time | Point |
|---|---|
| 0:17 | **Scaling marker first:** at the front of a step, a second marker exactly **24 in** out. Everything is scaled from it. |
| 0:47 | Markers round the landings to show the walls and floor area. |
| 1:17 | **A marker on the leading edge of every step**, bottom to top. **Carpet: put it at the edge of the carpet**, not the wood underneath, so the chair clears the overhang. |
| 1:56 | **Handrail bolsters or newel posts** that stick out: tell Harmar or photograph them, so they can offset the rail. Line a marker up with the front of the post. |
| 2:12 | On landings and round turns: **a marker every 16–18 in** at the edge. Slide markers out to show any lip or overhang. |
| 3:31 | **Mark both sides of top and bottom landings**, so the customer can change the park position after seeing the drawing without a second visit. |

## Taking the pictures

| Time | Point |
|---|---|
| 4:26 | **Carry the camera up as you lay markers**, then shoot from the top down. **Once you start shooting, nothing moves.** Markers are numbered in sequence. |
| 5:15 | At each landing, shoot from **all four corners** and the centre: it's a 3D model, so it needs many angles. |
| 5:54 | Going down: hold the camera **as high as possible** with the **tips of your shoes in the frame**. Down two steps, shoot down, turn round, shoot up, repeat. |
| 6:14 | The camera comes set to **"C" (custom)**. **Don't change it.** If you hit zoom or a setting by mistake, turn it off and on to restore the presets. |
| 6:41 | No blur, and nothing moving (pets). **Overlap each picture by about a quarter** with the last. |
| 9:42 | **Two straight-down shots of the scaling markers with a tape measure** on the centre marks between them. |

## Paperwork and hand-off

- Fill in the **order form**: rise, tread and stair width; the
  **narrowest point**; any **odd step**; and on page 2 the **user's
  size**. A tall user may not fit a 30 in stair (10:46).
- **Check Harmar's drawing against the house** before signing off;
  build starts from it.
- **Download the pictures straight from the camera** with the supplied
  cable. Don't let Windows or a photo app import them: they alter the
  files and the software won't work (12:22).

## Most common mistakes (11:44)

**Blurry pictures** and **moved markers**, especially a marker moved
from one step to another. It shifts that step in the drawing and throws
the whole rail off.

## Checked against the manuals

Matches the 2023 Rev C guide in outline (leading-edge markers, carpet
runners, overlap, the common-errors page). The guide gives landing marker
spacing as **16–24 in** along the walls; the video says 16–18 in, which is
inside that range.
