# Video: How to Use the Pinnacle Sales Demo Kit

Harmar training video #23, 7:14, presented by Harmar's VP of
Marketing. How to use the **Pinnacle demo kit** in an in-home sales
visit. **It's a sales pitch**: the figures are Harmar's marketing
claims, and two of them disagree with the manuals (see below). No date.

**Kit (0:20):** a **2 ft rail section with brackets**, a spare piece of
**nylon-polymer gear rack**, and the Pinnacle's **worm gear**. Bring
literature and a tape measure.

## Talking points

| Time | Claim |
|---|---|
| 0:54 | Aluminium rail with a **nylon-polymer gear rack, steel-reinforced** (competitors use steel racks). |
| 1:23 | The worm gear meets the rack at **six points of contact**: smooth, stable, low friction. |
| 1:44 | Rated **350 lb**; the same rack and worm with a different gear ratio is used on Harmar's **600 lb** model. |
| 2:05 | Low friction means a smaller motor and less battery draw: **40–60 trips on one charge** in a power cut (competitors' steel rack and pinion: 9–12). Lower power bills. |
| 3:03 | **Most compact on the market**: **10½ in** into the stairway with the chair folded. The rail is **6 in tall** above the step (competitors 9–17 in), mounts **½ in from the wall** and is **5¼ in wide**, so under 6 in on an open stair. **36 in stair leaves 30 in past the rail.** |
| 4:10 | Narrow stairs: a 34 in stair with 10½ in taken still leaves the **code minimum clearance** (the video says "20 in"; 34 − 10½ = 23½ in). |
| 4:47 | **Folding rail**: the bottom section folds up when the chair is sent 2–3 steps up, clearing a hallway or doorway. |
| 5:33 | **Top of the stairs**: a **taller bracket** brings the rail end level with the top step, and the swivel seat lets the rider get off facing away from the stairs. |
| 6:20 | Harmar's straight-lift brochure: **Vantage** (value), **Summit Series** (outdoor), **Pinnacle** (premium). |

## Checked against the manuals (the manual wins)

- **Capacity:** the video says **350 lb**. That's the
  [[Pinnacle-SL600]]. The [[Pinnacle-SL300]] is **300 lb** per its
  manuals and warranty. The "600 lb model" isn't on file (no manual).
- **40–60 trips on battery** and the steel-reinforced nylon rack with no
  lubrication match [[Harmar Product Insights (2021-2023)]].
- **Code clearance:** the stairway clearance rule is in
  [[Stairlift-Evaluation]]; don't take the spoken "20 in" from the
  video.
- Zero-overrun with a tall bracket (SL600TB1) and the folding rail are
  covered on [[Pinnacle-SL300]] and in the install manuals.
- The Vantage and Summit have no manuals on file.
