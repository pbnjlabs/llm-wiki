# Technical Service Alert: Pinnacle Seat/Footrest/Motor/PCB Supply Chain Changes

CSVF-000026 Rev B, effective 16Feb2021. A "may require different tools"
alert for techs, covering several concurrent supplier transitions on
Pinnacle stairlifts.

## Covers
- SL600 gets a spring-assist flip-up seat bottom (more like SL300's); new
  SL300 gets an upgraded seat belt buckle (retractable belts still
  optional)
- New footrest/seat hardware switches from 7/16" to 10mm hex (metric) for
  height-adjustment bolts; seat wire harness wrap is temporarily black
  (reverting to white later); new SL300 footrest tread has no cut-away
  strip
- Two interchangeable motor suppliers now in circulation: grey/SEI-marked
  (7mm hex manual lowering) and black/MDI-marked (6mm hex) — explains why
  both SL300 and SL600 manuals hedge on "1/4" or 7mm socket depending on
  your model" for manual override
- New Pinnacle PC board supplier, backwards compatible to units built
  after July 2017 (older units need a power supply/charger swap too);
  brighter LED
