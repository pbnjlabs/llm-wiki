# Video: Stair Lift Beep Tones

Harmar training video #4, 4:26, presented by a member of Harmar's
technical support group. How to use the beep tones on Harmar's
**Universal Accessibility Board (UAB)** products. It's demonstrated on a
Pinnacle. No date.

## Which products

The UAB is in the **Pinnacle, Pinnacle HD, Vantage and Sierra incline
platform lift** (0:11). "Pinnacle HD" is probably the SL600; the Vantage
and Sierra have no manuals on file. The [[Helix]] board is also called a
universal access board in its manual.

## What the video says

| Time | Point |
|---|---|
| 0:38 | **Most common tone: about 30 seconds long, meaning the unit isn't charging** (not on the charge strip). |
| 0:52 | **Ordinary tones** (minor): an obstruction at the upper or lower end of the chassis, or the seat open. On the Pinnacle they come with an **amber light on the armrest**. The **Vantage has the tone only**. |
| 1:27 | **Error tones** (major) stop travel up or down. Example: obstructions at the **top and bottom at the same time**, which can't happen in normal use. The demo gives **five error tones** and needs a **reset**. |
| 2:27 | **Diagnostic trick:** if the unit won't go down, deliberately trip a switch at the top end. If a downstairs-side switch is stuck, the conflict throws the error code and shows you which side the fault is on. |
| 3:08 | **Low battery:** while travelling up, **one chirp per second**, but it keeps going to the top. Let it charge before the next use. |
| 3:53 | The full tables are in each unit's install and owner's manuals. |

## Checked against the manuals (the manual wins)

- **Top and bottom obstruction together:** the video counts **5**
  tones. The SL300, SL600 and Helix tables give **4** for conflicting
  OBSTRUCTION UP/DOWN; 5 is FOOTREST UP/OBSTRUCTION DOWN. Possibly the
  demo tripped a footrest switch, or the count included the power-on
  beep. **Go by the table in [[Harmar-Stairlift-Beep-Codes]]**, counting
  beeps after the first power-on beep.
- **Off the charge strip:** the manuals describe an **intermittent beep
  repeating every 5 minutes** until the lift is parked on a charge
  point; the video describes it as a 30-second tone. Both mean the same
  thing: **park it at a charge point.**
- The once-a-second low-battery chirp while still moving isn't in the
  manuals' tables; low battery voltage is listed as a minor fault.
