# Video: Correct Position of the Lower Roller Assembly (Helix)

Harmar training video #28, 0:55, by a Harmar Technical Services tech.
One install check on the [[Helix]] curved stairlift. No date.

| Time | Point |
|---|---|
| 0:13 | The **plunger switch on the bottom roller must sit above the bottom track**. |
| 0:20 | Common mistake: the roller assembly is **top-heavy and tends to flip over** while you're installing the chassis. |
| 0:27 | Symptom: run the unit to the bottom; when it hits the cam it **acts as if it's on its final limit**, with the **red LED on the side of the chassis**. |
| 0:44 | Always check the plunger is above the bottom track when installing. |

Frames 0:10–0:40 show the lower roller pair and plunger on the
twin-tube rail.

## Checked against the manuals

The [[Helix Install Manual]] covers rollers only as "tilt the upper
rollers to match the rail angle" when loading the chassis, and lists
"final limit switch detected" as **9 beeps** with a red status LED
([[Harmar-Stairlift-Beep-Codes]]). The flipped-roller cause isn't in
the manual. So: **a false final-limit fault at the bottom of a new
install = check the lower roller plunger first.**
