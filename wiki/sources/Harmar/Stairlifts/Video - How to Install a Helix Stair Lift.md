# Video: How to Install a Helix Stair Lift

Harmar training video, 29:18, presented by a Harmar staff member with
a helper. A full [[Helix]] install in a house with a 180° turn and a
drop-nose bottom (tile and hardwood floors), then studio segments on the
chassis, seat, charge stations and diagnostics. 1080p; no date. The
written procedure is the [[Helix Install Manual]]; **the manual wins any
conflict** (none found).

## 1. Rail on the stairs (0:09–5:30)

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:24 | Find the **install manual and the drawing**. The drawing shows which numbered leg goes on which step. |
| 0:42 | Legs are **numbered on the bottom**. Put a **felt pad on each foot** (tile or wood; not needed on carpet) and set each on its step, bottom first. |
| 1:11 | Start the **set screw in the back of each leg** only a little, so the rail can still drop in. |
| 1:27 | Rail sections are **tagged in order from 1 at the bottom**. Lay each into its legs. |
| 1:54 | **Run the charge wire through each section as you go**, not with a fish tape at the end; a 30–40 ft rail with turns binds the tape. Keep it straight, not twisted or pinched. **Tie it off at the bottom leg** so enough is left for the bottom charge station. |
| 3:03 | **Mid-station** (park part-way up): drill a hole in the lower tube for the wire, **only on a straight section, never on a turn or bend**. |
| 3:46 | Snug a leg set screw just enough to stop the rail tipping. |
| 4:25 | **Three screws per joint**: front and back of the large tube, one in the small tube. **Snug, not tight.** A T-handle ball-end Allen speeds it up. |
| 5:02 | **Close every joint gap** or the wheels bump over it. Tap with a rubber mallet if needed. |

## 2. Angles and clearance (5:33–9:00)

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 5:36 | **All legs loose** except the top and bottom (on the floors) and the one leg at the turn. |
| 6:04 | **Angle meter** on each section against the drawing (here 28° and 34°; 33.1° read was close enough). The top section has a **level** and must be level. |
| 6:16 | **Raise or lower the turn leg** to set the pitch; top and bottom are fixed by the floors. It usually has to come **up**, because the long rail sags. |
| 8:25 | Get it wrong and the chair won't ride level through the turn and the straight: the rider feels they're falling sideways. Everything is set from a level top. |

## 3. Loading the chair and setting the line (9:01–15:15)

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 9:05 | With a **drop nose** the chair can't go on at the bottom, so **load it at the top**. Put **foam or a pad on wood floors** for the chassis. |
| 9:48 | Slide the rollers onto the **mounting rail post** (a loading stub kept in the toolbox), up to where the sprocket meets the gear rack, then **power on and drive it onto the rail**. A helper holds the rail top so it doesn't tip. |
| 11:24 | Run it round the turn and **shift the whole rail to get the tightest line round the post/corner**, for the most knee room. Check both sides. |
| 12:16 | **Bolt the turn leg to the floor first** as the pivot. Then square the rail to the handrail at bottom and top with a tape. The rail is set slightly in at the top so it can be pulled out to line. |
| 13:14 | **Screw every foot to the floor first, then tighten the leg set screws.** Tightening set screws first twists the rail as the feet sink into carpet. |
| 14:46 | Recheck spacing top and bottom: even with the handrail. |

## 4. Studio: chassis, seat and charge stations (15:43–23:10)

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 16:06 | **Cover off** (4 screws). Keep the **footrest partly up** while sliding the cover off, or it binds. |
| 16:31 | Inside: 2 batteries and a bag with wire, charge-station connectors, **2 grommets** for the rail wire exit, and a small **Allen set screw for the seat**. |
| 16:48 | Batteries side by side: **red to + on one, black to − on the other, jumper between them**. |
| 17:10 | The board on that side is the **wireless remote receiver**; its pairing button is covered in the manual. |
| 17:35 | **Seat:** feed its wires down through the post hole. **Put white lithium grease on the seat shaft.** Drop the seat until it locks. Two harness sets: **both arms are pre-wired**, so the switch can move to the other arm. |
| 18:28 | **Seat set screw** into the groove in the post so the seat can't lift off. **Snug only**; too tight stops the swivel. |
| 19:13 | **Charge station:** spring-loaded contacts under a cover; clamps to the rail with **one Allen screw**; adjusts up, down, left, right and rotation. |
| 19:46 | Crimp the rail wire to the power-supply tail (connectors from the bag); screw the connectors to the spring contacts. |
| 20:28 | **Mid-station**: smaller cover, same contacts, **no stop**, so the chair drives past it. End stations have a **stop pin**. |
| 21:32 | **Align**: bring the chassis in, line the charge strips up dead centre so the springs compress, tighten the two bolts, then drive in and check it stops on the pin. |
| 22:43 | **Power supply**: 6 ft cord plus a lead with a connector to the gray tail (so it can be unplugged without rewiring). **Green light = ready.** |
| 23:14 | **Fit the safety (hard final limit) on the rail** at each end, snug. If the sprocket hits it, it jams the rail. |

## 5. Controls, safeties and diagnostics (23:42–28:34)

- **On the chassis:** E-stop, power switch, **diagnostic (PC) port**,
  three status lights. Seat belt; armrests, seat and footrest fold.
- **Safeties:** on the rail, at the bottom near the steps, and on the
  footrest (each side); **seat swivel sensor**: swivelled, it tones and
  won't move.
- **Lights:** power on; **green** status; **yellow blinks on the charge
  station**, faster when low, slower as it charges. **Not blinking = not
  on the charge station properly.**
- **Off the charge station too long:** six to eight beeps, a pause of
  about 30 s, repeat until parked.
- **Diagnostic software** (25:24): shows which safety is tripped or has
  failed, power-supply and battery voltage, motor speed. See
  [[Video - Helix Curved Stairlift Diagnostic]].
- **Seat tilt** (26:40): loosen the top nut on the **leveling joint**,
  take out its set screw, turn the joint to tilt the chair left or
  right (some riders like a slight uphill tilt), then refit the set
  screw and lock the nut.
- **Remotes** (27:44): two supplied; **radio frequency**, so the chair
  comes when called even from another room. **Warn the customer it
  will move on its own.** More can be paired.
- **Hand-over:** go through the owner's manual; have them **fill in the
  warranty card** (last page) and send it to Harmar.

## 6. Finish (28:44)

**End caps on every rail end and every leg**, then demo everything to the
customer.

## Checked against the manuals

Matches the [[Helix Install Manual]]: angle meter and drawing, leg set
screws only snug until the rail is set, **feet secured to the floor
first, then set screws**, white lithium grease on the seat post, seat
set screw, charge-station alignment, mid-charging station, **hard final
limit at both rail ends with end and support caps**, leveling joint for
uphill tilt. Battery polarity: wired wrong, it can damage the universal
access board ([[Video - How to Change the Helix Batteries]]). Lower
roller orientation:
[[Video - Correct Position of the Lower Roller Assembly (Helix)]].
