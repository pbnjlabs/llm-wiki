# Pinnacle SL300 Owner's Manual

P/N 610-00058 Rev K, 05SEP2025. Owner's manual for Harmar's **Pinnacle
SL300** straight-rail residential stairlift. On-disk filename is just
`SL300.pdf` — not obviously a Harmar Pinnacle doc from the filename alone.

## Covers
- 300 lb max payload, residential-only (outdoor and commercial
  installations explicitly **not covered** by warranty / not approved)
- Optional Folding Rail (SL300FR — chassis/footrest obstruction sensors),
  optional Power Swivel, optional Power Footrest
- Manual seat swivel (85° max, 45° intermediate latch), remote call/send
- Major/minor fault beep-code tables — see [[Harmar-Stairlift-Beep-Codes]]
- Manual override (1/4" or 7mm socket at motor shaft, model-dependent)
- 2-year limited warranty + 10-year gear rack warranty — see
  [[Harmar-Warranty]]
