# Outside Vehicle Lifts Installation & Service Manual

P/N 630-00106 Rev B, 04APR2022. Like the owner's manual, this is a
**generic install/service manual spanning Harmar's entire AL-series**
Outside Vehicle Lift line (hitch classes 1/2/3/5) despite the on-disk
`AL100-...` filename — it includes a hitch-weight table and hitch-adapter
sizing chart covering roughly 25 models (AL003 through AL580-HDX).

**Byte-identical (md5-confirmed) to**
`AL300-OutsideLifts-Installation-and-Service-Manual.pdf` and
`AL301-OutsideLifts-Installation-and-Service-Manual.pdf` — three
filenames, one document.

## Covers
- Hitch class 1/2/3/5 compatibility, per-model tongue-weight table,
  detailed hitch-adapter selection chart (HV/D1/D2 dimensions, ~40 adapter
  part numbers)
- Vehicle wiring: 23' harness (SAE J1128), black wire to battery negative
  first / red to positive last, 20A self-resetting circuit breaker; a
  stand-alone battery pack (BAT141386) is offered for hybrid/EV vehicles
  without a usable 12V accessory battery — see
  [[AL-Series-Outside-Vehicle-Lifts]] for comparison with Bruno's
  [[Vehicle-Lift-Power-Wiring]]
- Swing-away/hitch-post installation, AL030 Power Tote ball-mount install,
  AL300-FULL full lift-system install (scooter/power-chair hold-down
  hardware)
- Wheel-cradle and hold-down-foot height adjustment procedures (model-
  specific: AL100/160/300 cradle geometry, AL560/560XL platform, AL580-HDX
  mid-wheel power-chair gap adjustment for Quantum Edge 2.0/3.0 bases)
- Troubleshooting table + manual override (hex-shaft crank)
