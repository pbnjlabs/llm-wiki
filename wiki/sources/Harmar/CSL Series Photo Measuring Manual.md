# CSL Series Photo Measuring Manual (Harmar)

24-page Harmar "Helix PhotoMap" staircase measuring manual for Harmar's
CSL Series curved stairlift (P/N 610-00016-01 Rev B). **Different
manufacturer from Bruno** — physically sits in `MANUALS/Bruno/Bruno Curved
SL/` but documents Harmar's own competing measuring kit, not a Bruno
product. See [[Harmar]] and [[Staircase-Measuring-System]] for how it
compares to Bruno's Picture Perfect system.

Contents: kit contents (paper markers, calibration markers, camera, sync
cable, order form), marker placement (calibration points 2ft apart,
landing markers 16-24" apart, two markers per tread), photographing
markers (camera "C" setting, no zoom, ≥14 targets/7 markers per photo,
~50+ photos), photo sequence (top-down, 3-steps-down-and-shoot pattern),
submitting photos (Dropbox preferred, email, or physical camera return),
sample photo sequence, and common errors to avoid.
