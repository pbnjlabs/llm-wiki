## Settings

The side plates bolt on in different positions, so one adapter works as
a **standard (no drop)** adapter or a **2 in, 3 in or 5 in drop**
adapter. As a standard, 2 in or 3 in adapter, the excess material **may
be cut off**; paint the cut to protect it from damage and corrosion.

## Choosing the setting

1. **Measure the hitch-to-ground height (X)** from the **bottom surface
   of the hitch receiver** to the ground.
2. **Round to the nearest inch:** ½ in or more rounds up, less than ½
   in rounds down (e.g. 18½ in → 19 in).
3. Read the setting off the chart. The range shifts slightly depending
   on the lift's mount:

| X (hitch to ground) | Standard mount, AL107 hitch post (no swing-away) | Swing-away, AL105 / AL105L (tailgate or back hatch access) |
|---|---|---|
| 6–16 in | AL-123/HD standard adapter, or AL1235U no drop | AL-123/HD standard adapter, or AL1235U no drop |
| 17–18 in | AL-123/HD standard adapter, or AL1235U no drop | **2 in** drop |
| 19 in | **2 in** drop | **3 in** drop |
| 20 in | **2 in** drop | **5 in** drop |
| 21 in | **3 in** drop | **5 in** drop |
| 22–23 in | **5 in** drop | Contact Harmar Technical Service |
| 24–26 in | Contact Harmar Technical Service | Contact Harmar Technical Service |

"Contact Harmar Technical Service" means asking about availability of
larger drop adapters. The chart's middle columns are slightly offset from
the row labels in the scan, so these row boundaries were read from the
cell borders; check against the PDF (p. 2) if a reading lands on an edge.

Refer to the lift's Installation and Owner's manuals for the rest of the
install.
