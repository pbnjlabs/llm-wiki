# Inside Lifts Installation and Owner's Manual (2017, Rev F)

Harmar's combined install and owner's manual for the **inside (hoist)
lifts**: AL215, AL225, AL425, AL425HD, AL435 and the AL435T truck lift.
P/N 999-3B01-AB Rev F (TEC0091), dated 18 Dec 2017, 52 pages: owner
section (operation, safety, maintenance), installer section (wiring,
trial fit, three base types, lift install, adjustments, docking
devices), troubleshooting, exploded views and wiring diagrams per model,
warranty. Feeds [[Hoist-Series-Inside-Vehicle-Lifts]].

## Models

| Model | Type | Capacity | Rotation |
|---|---|---|---|
| AL215 | Single axis | 250 lb | **Manual** |
| AL225 | 2-axis | 250 lb | Power |
| AL425 | 2-axis | 400 lb | Power |
| AL425HD | 2-axis | 450 lb | Power |
| AL435 | 3-axis (power extension) | 400 lb | Power |
| AL435T | Truck lift, 3-axis | 400 lb | Power |

Base adapter in the box: **BA00** standard (any flat floor), **BA01R1**
Stow 'n Go (folding 3rd-row seat wells), **BA22** heavy duty (flat
floor; AL435/AL435T). Docking device is separate.

## Operating (owner)

1. Park the chair or scooter alongside and parallel to the vehicle; set
   the parking brake. Fold the seat back.
2. **OUT** rotates the arm out (AL215: by hand). AL435/AL435T: **EXTEND**
   pushes the arm farther out for wide chairs.
3. Lower the strap, attach the docking device, and check the **strap is
   taut and points straight down** before lifting (otherwise the chair
   swings).
4. **UP** to raise; AL435 series: **RETRACT** shortens the radius to
   swing in through narrow openings. **IN** once it clears the bumper.
5. **Lower until the chair sits firmly on the floor**, leaving some
   strap tension. Never transport it hanging.
6. When not in use, run the strap up so the hook doesn't swing. **AL215
   has no rotation lock:** hook the strap to the gusset nearest the side
   wall and snug it, not over-tight.
- Outside exposure: put **dielectric grease or thermal paste** on both
  hand-control connector halves.
- Stop lifting before the hook touches the strap roller or frame.
- New car or new chair: have the dealer recheck the setup.

**Maintenance:** check the strap every use (worn or damaged: don't use
it); **dealer inspection yearly** (motors, frame, harness, moving
parts); touch up chips with gloss black enamel; keep the hand control
inside the vehicle (truck installs: unplug it and keep it in the cab).

## Installing

**Order:** trial fit → vehicle harness → base adapter → base → arm.
Don't lift the whole box alone; components come out separately.

**Wiring** (the #1 cause of lift problems):
- ~23 ft harness to SAE J1128, with a **20 A self-resetting breaker**
  near the battery end. The end connector is loose so the wire fits
  through small openings.
- **Black to battery negative first. Red to positive last**, at the very
  end. **Never to a secondary power source**, always straight to the
  battery.
- Route through the firewall or under the vehicle away from exhaust,
  brake and fuel lines, gas tank, pinch points and road debris; grommet
  any floor hole (3/8). **Don't cut or shorten the harness**: coil and
  tie the excess.
- Check the pins' retaining flanges (they bend when pulled through), seat
  the pins (A = red, B = black), make sure the rubber seals are in, close
  the retainer.

**Trial fit:** every model needs at least **three holes** in the floor.
Put the base in the far back corner, mount the lift on it, and run it
through its full range with the chair's folded length, width and height
simulated before marking holes.

**Base types:**
- **Flat floor (BA00):** drill one pilot hole, check underneath, then
  the rest; 3/8 in holes. Spare tire under the floor: bolt through steel
  floor or frame. Storage wells: All Thread Kit.
- **3rd-row seat well (BA01R1):** vertical joiners on the well's back
  wall with mounting holes above the threshold; 2 holes in the well
  floor **plus** 3 through the back wall (mandatory for heavier loads);
  telescoping and articulating legs, J-hook to a seat anchor.
- **Truck bed:** base in the corner nearest the tailgate, **at least 1
  in clear on all sides**; long leg parallel to the bumper, short leg
  front-to-back, **at least 4 in of each leg extension inside the base
  legs**. Measure from an outside body panel to find frame and
  cross-members underneath. **Never bolt to the bed skin alone**: go
  through frame, cross-member or thickened structure; if impossible,
  large plates (not supplied). Pilot 1/8 in, then 3/8 in; **28–30
  ft-lb**.
- **Heavier chairs: bolting through frame rails or cross-members is
  required.**

**Lift install:** power-rotation lifts get a mechanical stop bolt in the
motor housing: **3/8-16 × 2.50 for passenger side (flat side), × 3.00
for driver side (curved side). Never both.** Feed the lifting wire
through the base adapter **before** mounting the base or motor housing.
Motor housing to base **28–30 ft-lb**. Cut the full-thread stud short
after tightening. Attach the arm, plug in the lifting motor.

## Adjustments

- **Height:** AL425/425HD/435/435T: remove bolt, loosen 4 set screws,
  slide the inner post, refit. AL215/225: set screws only.
- **Pitch:** AL425/425HD: turnbuckle. AL435/435T: pin and bolt. AL215/225
  have a fixed **5° preload**, no adjustment.
- **Rotation:** limit rings set the travel. **Driver side or wrong
  direction: swap the wires at the rotation motor** and flip the
  rotation limit switches if needed. Set the limit ring for "IN", then
  run the mechanical stop bolt in to touch, **back off ¼ turn**, lock the
  nut at **25 ft-lb**; the bolt must not touch when the limit switch
  trips. Don't set limits that over-rotate into the vehicle.
- **AL215:** manual rotation, two base set screws act as stops; snug,
  not so tight the arm won't turn.
- **Docking devices:** DD19 C-arm and DD21 spreader bar are the most
  common; see [[Harmar Product Insights (2021-2023)]] for the full list.

## Troubleshooting (dealer only)

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Won't run, slow or intermittent | Bad connection or breaker | Clean and tighten connections; inspect the whole harness for chafe; verify the breaker (~6 in from the battery) with a meter or swap it; then the flowchart: known-good 12 V shop battery straight to the motor, then to the lift plug, then through the vehicle harness |
| Strap winds backwards | Button held too long, strap unwound "like a yo-yo" | Hold the button that extends the strap until it rewinds the right way; **strap always wraps over the top of the spool** |
| Chair tips while lifting | Lifted off center | Adjust the docking device for center of gravity; check it's the right DD |
| Arm hard to rotate | Dry or dirty base bushings | Clean, grease with white lithium |

**A test light or meter can lie:** one strand of a chafed wire shows 12
V but can't run the motor. Test current too, or use a known-good battery.

## Warranty (p. 46)

**3 years, parts, transferable**, excluding labor, batteries, paint,
rust and covers. Starts at installation (**no later than 180 days from
manufacture**) if the certificate goes back **within 10 days**. Installed
by an authorized Harmar distributor or certified installer. **Applications
not approved by Harmar's Compatibility Calculator void the warranty**,
and transfer requires an approved application. Claims go through the
selling dealer to Harmar Technical Services with the serial number. See
[[Harmar-Warranty]].
