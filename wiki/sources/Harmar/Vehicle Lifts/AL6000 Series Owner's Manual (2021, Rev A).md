# AL6000 Series Owner's Manual (2021, Rev A)

P/N 630-00152 Rev A, 27AUG2021. "AL6000 Series Universal Hybrid Lift"
owner's manual, covering **AL6000** and **AL6000HD** — an **older,
distinct hardware generation** from the 2025 AL600/AL690 line documented
in [[Hybrid Vehicle Lifts Owner's Manual (AL600-AL690, 2025)]]. Same
indications of use (lift/secure/transport an unoccupied mobility device)
and the same wired hand control / optional wireless key-fob as the
AL600/AL690 generation, but a materially richer accessory set.

## Securement options (more elaborate than AL600/AL690)
- **Cambuckle retention straps** (same corner-hook style as AL600/AL690)
- **Optional Q'Straint retractors** — auto-retracting straps, usable in a
  2-retractor (opposite corners) or 4-retractor configuration
- **Optional hold-down arm** — rotates via a T-handle to press down on
  the seat cushion directly instead of/in addition to strap securement;
  can rotate out of the way or over the seat center
- **Optional puddle lights** — illuminate automatically whenever the
  platform is out (up or down), shut off once it retracts horizontally
  inward; if left extended a long time, a power switch atop the control
  box must be manually turned off to kill the lights

## AL617 Extension Plate
Same scooter front-wheel accessory plate referenced in the 2025
AL600/AL690 manuals — confirms this accessory is shared across both
Hybrid-lift hardware generations.
