# Hybrid Vehicle Lifts Installation and Service Manual (AL600/AL690, 2025)

P/N 630-00177-A Rev A ("V5"), 01JAN2025. Dealer/installer manual for the
current-generation **AL600** and **AL690** Hybrid Vehicle Lifts.

## Installation
- Two-person job (a single-person disassembly workaround is documented —
  detach the tower from its base, place the base first, then re-attach)
- Wiring harness first (~19', SAE J1128, black-to-negative first/
  red-to-positive last, 20A self-resetting breaker), same convention as
  [[AL-Series-Outside-Vehicle-Lifts]] and [[Vehicle-Lift-Power-Wiring]]
- **AL600** (3rd-row/rear-cargo mount): leg mounted 32"-42" from the
  closed back door's internal surface; two install paths depending on
  whether the vehicle has removable or folding 3rd-row seating — folding
  seats need a separate **BA-02 kit** (well-height-adjustable leg
  assembly, not detailed further here since it ships with its own
  instructions)
- **AL690** (side-door mount): front leg 42"-52" from the closed side
  door's internal surface; rear leg installed 24"-36" behind the front
  leg, both legs verified in-line/square by corner-to-corner measurement
- Seat attachment points ("dog bones") preferred over drilling; if
  drilling is required, holes must avoid gas tank/fuel/brake/electrical
  lines and attachment points must sit flush or lower than the floor
- Chair/scooter prep: Anchor Plate (center-seat-post devices) or
  Anchor U-Bolts, kit ALA580 (tubular-frame devices, no center post)

## Manual override
Both motors (Lifting: up/down: Rotational: in/out) use a 1/4" hex at the
center of the armature cover — CW/CCW mapping differs by motor (Lifting:
CW=up, CCW=down; Rotational: CW=out, CCW=in). Power must be disconnected
at both connector plugs before use; power-drill users must use slow
speed only, never impact/hammer drills.

## Troubleshooting
Single flowchart: bad electrical connections/circuit breaker is the
stated #1 cause of slow/intermittent/non-operation, diagnosed by applying
known-good power progressively closer to the motor (shop battery → lift
plug → vehicle harness) until the fault isolates. Platform tipping
front/back during lift = uneven mobility-device positioning, not a lift
fault. Rattling while driving = platform not fully docked (press "IN"
until it locks).

## Maintenance
6-month dealer inspection; annual grease/lubrication of the rails with
Jet Lube CB-2 Multipurpose Grease (or equivalent) — a single blanket
instruction, notably less detailed than the AL6000 series' rail-by-rail
grease diagrams (see [[AL6000 Series Installation and Service Manual (2022, Rev C)]]).
