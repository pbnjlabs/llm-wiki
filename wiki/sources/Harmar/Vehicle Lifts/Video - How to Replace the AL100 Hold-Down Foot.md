# Video: How to Replace the AL100 Hold-Down Foot

Harmar training video #13, 2:40, presented by a member of Harmar
product management. Replacing the scooter **hold-down foot** assembly on
an AL100 ([[AL-Series-Outside-Vehicle-Lifts]]). No date or kit part
number.

## Why it fails

The foot **drives down onto the scooter deck automatically** as the
lift rises. The assembly is a **spring, two small brackets and the arm**.
They get **bent** (often in an accident) or the **spring loses its
tension**.

## Steps

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:32 | Take out the bolts (one at the back) and the **whole assembly comes off**. Push up and the spring pops out. |
| 0:52 | With the arm and brackets off, slide the spring assembly up and off. **Check the rod is straight.** |
| 1:03 | The **replacement kit** has the arm and the two brackets. |
| 1:14 | Slide the rod back down with the spring on it. Fit one arm through its hole and drive the spring back up. **It must go through the plastic hole at the top**; it can take some fiddling. |
| 1:32 | Bolt through to hold it, fit the other arm and its nut, line up, bolt through, and tighten everything. |
| 2:16 | **Reset the foot for the scooter**: with the scooter on, run the bolt down, slide up, and set the tension again, because the new parts change it. |

## Checked against the manuals

The video gives no values for the re-set. Use **Hold-Down Foot Height
Setting** in the [[Outside Vehicle Lifts Install Manual (2025, Rev D)]]:
nuts run down, scooter on, raise to the motor clutch, raise both nuts so
the foot rests on the deck, run the bottom nut up hand-tight, lift the
arm to take out the slack. There should be **pressure on the deck and the
bottom lock washer compressed**. Too loose or too tight can damage the
lift or the scooter. The manual also has a Hold-Down Foot Kit install
page.
