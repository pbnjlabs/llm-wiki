# Bulletin: AL1232 Hitch Adapter Cold Weld Inspection

CSV-000014. Affects AL1232 hitch adapters from orders received in
December 2015 (invoices M66000-M66999) — a safety recall-style bulletin,
not a routine service note.

## Covers
A **cold weld** (weld that sits on top of the joint in a convex bead
rather than penetrating and bonding, concave) can break free under load,
risking the hitch adapter separating from the vehicle — property damage
and injury risk called out explicitly. Dealers must inspect affected-range
adapters, replace any showing cold welds, photograph the suspect part,
and **destroy it** — Harmar reimburses 1 hour of labor ($65) plus mileage
($0.575/mi) for replacement, submitted to warranty@harmar.com referencing
CSV-000014.
