# Video: How to Install the AL425HD Hoist Lift (Permobil)

Harmar training video, 12:46, by a Harmar Technical Services tech with
an assistant. Installing an **AL425HD** hoist lift in a **pickup bed**
(legged truck base) and lifting a **Permobil M3** power chair with its
model-specific docking device ([[Hoist-Series-Inside-Vehicle-Lifts]]).
1080p; no date.

**In the box (0:11):** base, legs (long leg and the long leg with the
articulating leg), base assembly (motor and rotation unit), offset
post, arm (boom), hardware pack, owner's manual (it lists the tools).
**Two people**: it's not a one-person job (2:25).

## Base and legs

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:48 | Set the base in the corner of the bed and line it up with the holes. **Keep clear of brake lines, fuel lines and the tank.** |
| 1:17 | Slide in the legs and line up their holes. |
| 1:46 | Bolt through into **solid structure**: next to the frame and cross members, so the bed doesn't flex and the bolts don't tear out of the sheet metal. |
| 2:32 | The helper underneath fits **lock washers and nuts** on the front and back legs. |
| 2:56 | Sometimes a **larger steel plate** is needed under the bed instead of just a washer. **S-brackets** go on the base's main bars for a solid mount. |

## Base assembly, post and arm

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 3:51 | Two cables: one goes up the post; the other runs **under the base cover**, out through a small hole in the side of the base, and joins the first. |
| 4:31 | Set the base assembly on, **flush with no gaps**, with the cover lifted clear so it doesn't crack. Check the wires are free, then take the cover back off. |
| 5:24 | **Three Allen bolts, two short and one long.** The **long one goes in the far corner nearest the middle of the bed**. Nuts on the back, tighten, refit the cover. |
| 6:39 | **Post:** take the end cap off, fish the wire through, slide the post on, fit the bolt and nut through the base hole, and tighten front and back. Then the **four set screws** to hold the post tight. Cap back on. |
| 8:12 | **Arm:** fish the wire through, line it up, run the bolt through the back of the post and tighten. Plug in; push the spare wire back into the post. |

## Power, test and limits

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 9:25 | Lift the motor cover and connect the **power wire**. Plug in the hand control, turn the battery pack on, turn the lift on. |
| 10:03 | Test **up, down, out and in**. |
| 10:20 | Under the cover: **two limit switches and two limit rings**. Set them so the arm **doesn't swing too far in or out** and hit the bed sides. Do this **before** lifting a chair (same method as [[Video - How to Set the Limit Switch on AL800 Series Truck Lifts]]). |

## Lifting the chair

| Time | Point |
|---|---|
| 10:48 | The docking device is made for the **Permobil M3** only. |
| 11:05 | **Keep the strap straight under the pulley.** Lifting from the side frays the strap; a worn strap **snaps and drops the chair**. |
| 11:26 | One hand on the chair, one on the remote, so it doesn't hit the vehicle or bed. Raise until it **clears the tailgate**, swing in until it stops on the limit ring, then lower. |
| 12:32 | **Leave a little tension on the strap** so the chair can't move in transit. |

## Checked against the manuals (the manual wins)

The [[Inside Lifts Installation and Owner's Manual (2017, Rev F)]] (which
covers the AL425HD) agrees: avoid fuel, brake and electrical lines; drill
**3/8 in** holes through the bed and, where possible, the frame; if the
frame can't be used, **large plates (not supplied)** spread the load
(call tech service); leave strap tension in transit. It gives the
**torque the video leaves out: base, legs and anchors 28–30 ft-lb.**
Strap wear and replacement: [[Bulletin - Boom Lift Strap Replacement]].
Docking-device fitment: [[Docking-Devices]]. Power harness rules (20 A
breaker, never cut it): [[Vehicle-Lift-Power-Wiring]].
