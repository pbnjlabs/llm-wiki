# Outside Vehicle Lifts Owner's Manual

P/N 630-00105 Rev B, 18MAR2022. Despite the on-disk filename
(`AL100-OutsideLifts_Owners-Manual.pdf`), this is a **generic owner's
manual for Harmar's entire hitch-mounted Outside Vehicle Lift line**, not
an AL100-specific document — it names AL010, AL015, AL050, AL100,
AL100HD, AL100-DE, AL105, AL105L, AL160, AL300-FULL, AL560, AL560XL,
AL580, AL580XL, and AL580-HDX by model throughout.

**Byte-identical (md5-confirmed) to** `AL300-OutsideLifts_Owners-Manual.pdf`
and `AL301-OutsideLifts_Owners-Manual.pdf` — three filenames, one document.
One source page covers all three.

## Covers
- Unoccupied-mobility-device-only use (NOT for human transport — will void
  warranty)
- Lowering/loading/securing/raising/unloading/stowing sequence; swing-away
  open/close procedure (AL105/AL105L)
- Basic maintenance (pivot-point oiling, vertical tube greasing, paint
  touch-up)
- Manual override (hex-shaft crank at motor cover) and basic
  troubleshooting
- 3-year transferable warranty — see [[Harmar-Warranty]]
