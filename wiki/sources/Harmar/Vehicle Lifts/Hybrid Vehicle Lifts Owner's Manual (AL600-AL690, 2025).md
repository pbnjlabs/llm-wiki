# Hybrid Vehicle Lifts Owner's Manual (AL600/AL690, 2025)

P/N 610-00107-A Rev A ("V3"), 17MAR25. End-user owner's manual for
Harmar's **AL600** (rear-cargo mount) and **AL690** (side-door mount)
Hybrid Vehicle Lifts — the current (2025) generation of a lift family
previously only known to this wiki via a bare parts-diagram mention (see
[[Harmar Parts Diagrams]]). Indications of use: lift, secure, and
transport an **unoccupied** mobility device.

## Key content
- Wired 2-button hand control (UP/DOWN) or optional wireless key-fob;
  integrated LEDs double as a flashlight; auto-off after ~2 min idle;
  magnetic back for stowage on a metal surface once retracted
- AL600: drive on/off from either side, heaviest portion of the
  chair/scooter closest to the lifting tower; optional **AL617 Extension
  Plate** lets a scooter's lighter front wheels rest ahead of the main
  platform so the heavy end still lands nearest the tower
- AL690: loading is usually reverse-in/forward-out given the side-door
  orientation
- Securement: 4 corner strap hooks into either an Anchor Plate slot
  (center-seat-post chairs) or U-bolts (tubular-frame chairs/scooters,
  not included with the lift itself — see the install manual)
- 3-year limited parts warranty, same term as the rest of the AL-series —
  see [[Harmar-Warranty]]
