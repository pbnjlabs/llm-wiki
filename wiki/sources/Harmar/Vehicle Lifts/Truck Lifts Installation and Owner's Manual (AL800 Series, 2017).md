# Truck Lifts Installation and Owner's Manual (AL800 Series, 2017)

Harmar's install and owner's manual for the **pickup truck-bed lifts**
AL815CC, AL825, AL825CC and AL835. Inside pages carry P/N 630-00053 Rev A
(TEC00037, 20 Feb 2016); the back cover is dated 29 Jun 2017. 44 pages.
(The page footers wrongly say "Hybrid Installation & Owner's Manual".)
Feeds [[Hoist-Series-Inside-Vehicle-Lifts]].

## Models

| Model | Cab | Type | Capacity |
|---|---|---|---|
| AL815CC | Crew cab | Performance | **135 lb** |
| AL825 | Standard cab | Performance | **210 lb** |
| AL825CC | Crew cab | Heavy duty | 225 lb |
| AL835 | Standard cab | Heavy duty | 350 lb |

Driver-side or passenger-side install (the passenger-side base is sold
as an option). **Capacity conflict:** Harmar's 2022 FSS price list says
AL-815CC 150 lb and AL-825 225 lb. This manual and Harmar's 2026
[[Harmar Mobility Solutions Chart (2026, Rev F)]] both say **135 and
210**; go by those.

## Operating (owner)

1. Chair or scooter parallel to the truck; lock its wheels and turn it
   off. Set the parking brake.
2. **UP** to raise the arm above the bed wall, then **OUT**. Make sure
   it has rotated all the way before lowering.
3. Lower, fold the seat back if needed, attach the docking device (any
   Harmar DD fits the double strap hook; the quick pin holds it; the DD
   can stay on the arm).
4. **UP until the wheels clear the bed wall**, **IN**, then lower.
5. **Stop lowering when about ¾ of the weight is on the bed floor**,
   straps still taut. That's the transport position.
- **Keep the remote out of rain and snow.** If it plugs straight into
  the lift, unplug it after each use and keep it in the cab.
- Rotation motor has a **manual override** for power failure:
  disconnect power before removing the cap.
- Yearly dealer inspection; touch up chips with gloss black enamel.

## Installing

- Two people to lift it in; otherwise use the short disassembly
  procedure.
- **Wiring:** same harness and rules as the inside lifts: ~23 ft, SAE
  J1128, 20 A self-resetting breaker; black first, **red last**; never a
  secondary power source; don't cut the harness. The motor can draw **up
  to 30 A**. See [[Vehicle-Lift-Power-Wiring]].
- **Base:** in the bed corner, **at least 1 in clear all round**; leg
  extensions in as far as they go. **Three 3/8 in holes**, through the
  frame where possible; **two more** if the frame isn't captured, the
  bed is thin, or the load is heavy. **Heavier chairs: bolting through
  the frame is mandatory.** Measure from an outside body panel, check
  underneath (gas tank, spare, suspension), pilot, recheck, then drill.
  Tighten to about **30 ft-lb**. Last resort if the frame can't be used:
  the two anchor brackets, to keep the bed from tearing.
- **Power base** onto the base, 3 socket-head screws, ~30 ft-lb.
- **Post:** most installs need about **180° of rotation**. Set height in
  2 in steps (1 in fine-tune) so the post's horizontal tube **just clears
  the bed wall**; level with the set screws. Cams set the rotation;
  **passenger side or wrong direction: swap the rotation motor wires**
  and flip the cut-off switches if needed.
- **Arm:** 5/8-11 × 3 in bolts (Loctite patch), **at least 100 ft-lb**.
  Plug in the actuator. Strap arm hole position sets the reach; reverse
  the straps if the arm is flipped. **Bolt always goes in from the top**
  (a bottom nut hits the actuator). Never wrap the strap over the strap
  keeper's edge.
- **Control box in the cab:** 10 ft coiled wire with a small power
  switch, so the hand control lives in the cab. Find a cab-to-bed pass
  through or drill ¼ in; route away from debris and exhaust; wire the
  colors into the labeled terminals; mount with the self-drilling screws
  (or Velcro or ties if the customer doesn't want holes). Test every
  button.

## Troubleshooting (dealer only)

| Problem | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Won't run, slow or intermittent | Bad connection or breaker (#1 cause) | Clean connections, inspect the harness, verify or swap the breaker (~6 in from the battery), test with a known-good shop battery |
| Wrong direction, too little or no rotation | Rotation limit switches | Switches should only be pressed at end of travel; check cams and switches |
| Chair tips while lifting | Off center | Adjust the DD for center of gravity; right DD? |
| Arm hard to rotate | Dry or dirty bushings | Clean, white lithium grease |
