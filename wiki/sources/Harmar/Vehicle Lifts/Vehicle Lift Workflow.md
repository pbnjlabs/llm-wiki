# Vehicle Lift Workflow (PMS)

**Performance Medical Supply's own workflow** for quoting a Harmar
vehicle lift. Two scanned pages, undated. It's a PMS document, so it's
house practice for how PMS estimates vehicle lifts, not a Harmar spec.

**Left out on purpose:** the form's Harmar contact phone number and the
Etowing.com login. Look those up on the raw PDF, not in the wiki.

## Page 1: Phone interview for car lift

The same intake sheet as [[Vehicle Lift Phone Interview Form]]:

- Customer name and phone (never copied into the wiki)
- Vehicle year, make and model
- Mobility device type
- Does the vehicle have a receiver hitch? Yes / no
- If not, order one from **etrailer.com or etowing.com**: hitch # and
  order #
- Check **harmar.com** for which lift is compatible
- Product ordered, a Harmar contact line, and date ordered

## Page 2: Vehicle lift estimates

1. **Complete the Harmar phone interview sheet** (page 1).
2. **Use the harmar.com vehicle compatibility calculator** to pick the
   lift:
   - **AL100 series:** scooters
   - **AL301XL series:** power wheelchairs
   - **"HD" models:** larger mobility devices
3. **Swing-away:** check that the lift's "options box" (below the lift
   on harmar.com) offers a swing-away, if one is needed. A swing-away is
   **needed for any vehicle that needs rear access**: SUV, truck,
   hatchback, minivan, Jeep.
4. **Every lift install has a hitch adapter. Include it on the
   estimate.**
5. **Find the hitch to order** (if the vehicle has none) on
   etrailer.com or etowing.com.
