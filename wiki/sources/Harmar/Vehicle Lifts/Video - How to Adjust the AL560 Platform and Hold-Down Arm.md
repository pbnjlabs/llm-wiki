# Video: How to Adjust the AL560 Platform and Hold-Down Arm

Harmar training video #14, 2:44, presented by a member of Harmar
product management. Setting up an **AL560** outside power-chair lift
([[AL-Series-Outside-Vehicle-Lifts]]) for a mid-wheel-drive chair. No
date.

## Platform cradles

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:16 | As shipped: **two small brackets in the middle, two outer cradles**. |
| 0:22 | Space the middle brackets so the chair's **centre drive wheels drop between them**. They work as front and back wheel chocks. |
| 0:40 | Here the front casters sit on the flat section and the rear casters sit between the back cradle and the flat. Centred, the chair barely moves as it lifts. |

## Hold-down arm

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:52 | Raise the chair about halfway, **swing the arm round over the seat**, centred, set as high as it goes. |
| 1:01 | Raise to the top until the **motor clutches** (ratcheting sound). There's about 1 in of space between the arm and the seat. |
| 1:14 | Drop the arm until it **just touches the cushion**. |
| 1:20 | **Drive the lift down about 1 in** for a thin cushion, about **2 in for a thick one**, then slide the arm down until it touches again. |
| 1:51 | Snug the **two bolts at the back** with two wrenches. |
| 2:04 | Raise again: the arm **compresses the cushion** and locks the chair in place. |
| 2:21 | To unload: lower, fold the arm aside, lower the platform, drive off, fold the platform up. |

**Never use the arm on a chair with no cushion**: it relies on the
cushion to hold the chair (1:35).

## Checked against the manuals

The [[Outside Vehicle Lifts Install Manual (2025, Rev D)]] ("AL300-FULL,
AL560 & AL580 – Hold-Down Arm Adjustment") is the same procedure, with
two differences. **The manual wins on both:**
- It lowers the platform **2–3 in** before tightening (the video uses
  1–2 in, depending on the cushion).
- It torques the two bolts and nuts to **8 ft-lb**.

The **AL560/AL560XL platform** screws and nuts ship loose for adjustment
and **must be tightened to 8 ft-lb** before use, with thread showing
past the nylon in every nut.
