# Vehicle Lift Phone Interview (internal PMS intake form)

Not a Harmar-authored technical manual — a short (1-page) **Performance
Medical Supply internal intake script** for a phone sales call about a
vehicle lift. Captures: customer name/phone, vehicle year/make/model,
mobility device type, whether the vehicle already has a receiver hitch
(directs to etrailer/etowing.com to order one if not), which lift is
compatible (checked at harmar.com), and a contact line for "Harmar/Naidine"
(941-308-7309) — likely a specific Harmar sales/dealer-support contact PMS
works with directly, not a general Harmar support line.

Kept in the wiki for completeness (it's on disk in `MANUALS/Harmar/`) but
not linked into [[AL-Series-Outside-Vehicle-Lifts]] as a technical
reference — it documents PMS's own sales workflow, not the product.
