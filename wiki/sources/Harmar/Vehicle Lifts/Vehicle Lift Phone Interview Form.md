# Vehicle Lift Phone Interview (internal PMS intake form)

Not a Harmar-authored technical manual — a short (1-page) **Performance
Medical Supply internal intake script** for a phone sales call about a
vehicle lift. Captures: customer name/phone, vehicle year/make/model,
mobility device type, whether the vehicle already has a receiver hitch
(directs to etrailer/etowing.com to order one if not), which lift is
compatible (checked at harmar.com), and a contact line for a specific
Harmar sales/dealer-support contact PMS works with directly (phone number
omitted from the wiki; see the raw form).

The same sheet is page 1 of [[Vehicle Lift Workflow]], which adds PMS's
estimate steps (lift choice by device, swing-away, hitch adapter on
every estimate). That workflow is linked from
[[AL-Series-Outside-Vehicle-Lifts]].
