# Video: Installing and Programming the Remotes on a Hybrid Lift

Harmar training video #3, 3:39, presented by a Harmar staff member.
Fitting the optional **wireless (RF) receiver** to a **Hybrid AL600**
([[Hybrid-Vehicle-Lifts]]) and pairing the key-fob remote. No date or kit
part number.

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:12 | Tower cover off to reach the control board. The RF kit ships pre-assembled with instructions. |
| 0:27 | **Wear a ground strap**, grounded to the lift (which is grounded to the car). |
| 0:35 | **With power disconnected**, plug the RF board's lead into the **small 8/10-pin connector just above the hand-control input** on the main board, **black and red wires on top**. It's keyed; **don't force it**. |
| 1:03 | Power back on and **turn the power switch back on**. A **green LED** on the RF board should light (on some it only lights once you start pairing). |
| 1:31 | **Pairing:** hold the small **black pairing button** next to the LED, **press any button on the remote, release the remote button, then release the pairing button**. The order matters. |
| 1:58 | Test the remote. |
| 2:04 | To mount it: power off, unclip the main board from its standoffs, fit the RF board's two round holes over the same standoffs, snap the main board back on top, tuck the wires underneath (it's tight), cover on, power on and test. |

## Checked against the manuals

The [[Hybrid Vehicle Lifts Owner's Manual (AL600-AL690, 2025)]] only
mentions the "optional wireless" key-fob remote and changing its
battery. The install manual has no RF kit procedure, so this video is
the only one on file. Ask Harmar for the kit's own instruction sheet
when ordering. **Kit identified 2026-10-27:** **AL412** (remote 390-00056, receiver 360-00015), for serials 170805-00006 to 210323-00003 only; BOM on [[Hybrid Electrical Kit Drawings (AL412, 140-00023, 140-00030, 380-00099)]]. Its instruction sheet is **CSV-000038** (still not on file).
