# Hybrid Electrical Kit Drawings (AL412, 140-00023, 140-00030, 380-00099)

Four Harmar engineering drawings (five sheets) for the electrical kits of
the **AL600 / AL690 Hybrid** lifts ([[Hybrid-Vehicle-Lifts]]). They're
the gallery images on Mobility Scooters Direct's product pages
(watermarked), pulled 2026-10-27 with the
[[Parts Catalog - Mobility Scooters Direct (Harmar)]]. They're Harmar's
own drawings, so they rank with Harmar's documents, but they're the
revision the retailer happened to post; check the current revision with
Harmar (800-833-0478) before relying on a part number.

## AL412: AL600 remote upgrade, with circuit breakers (Rev C, 27APR21)

The **RF wireless remote kit**. **Only for AL600/690 lifts with serial
numbers 170805-00006 to 210323-00003** ("contain circuit breakers"). Other
serial ranges need a different kit (not on file).

| Item | Qty | Part no. | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 360-00015 | PCB, RF receiver, 6-channel |
| 2 | 1 | **390-00056** | Remote, RF, 2-button (Helix call/send transmitter) |
| 3 | 4 | 410-00008 | Double-stick tape, ½ in |
| 4 | 1 | 530-00454C | Lid, RF Hybrid enclosure |
| 5 | 1 | 530-00583C | RF bracket |
| 6 | 1 | 530-00615C | Enclosure back, Hybrid |
| 7 | 1 | 620-00063 | Sticker, circuit breaker, AL600-690 |
| 8 | 1 | CSV-000038 | **Instructions, AL412, RF remote for AL600-690 w/ CB** |
| 9 | 2 | PHTS-8-32×0.31-BZ | Pan head thread-forming screw |

Notes: **static mat and wrist strap** while handling; clean the bracket
back with alcohol and fit four ½ in double-stick pads behind the folded
tabs; slip the PCB between the bracket onto the **4 PEMs** with the
connections through the window and the wires out the bottom; keep it in its
static bag until fitted. History: Rev A 18MAY15; Rev B 22AUG17 (new
bracket, instructions CSV-00038, larger lid, sticker, tape); Rev C 27APR21
(serial range, new enclosure back and screws). The retailer also sells the
remote alone as **390-00056** and the kit as **AL412**. Fitting and pairing
are shown in [[Video - Installing and Programming the Remotes on a Hybrid Lift]]
(ground strap, plug into the small connector above the hand-control input,
hold the pairing button). **The written instruction sheet CSV-000038 is
still not on file.**

## 140-00030: Hybrid wiring upgrade kit for raised limit switches (Rev A, 10DEC15)

Older kit: on/off switch 312-ZC04-AA, **2 × limit switch 318-ZC01-AA
(Ormon V-15G31-C24-K)**, universal mobility board 360-00010, modified
harness kit 390-00032-MOD, enclosure 530-00331C / 530-00332C, instructions
630-00069, hardware. Wire per **650-00002-MOD**; set the PCB jumpers for the
600.

## 380-00099: Hybrid control harness (Rev B, 08FEB21)

From the **5-pin DIN panel socket** (Neutrik NYS325, the hand control
input) to an **8-circuit Molex Mini-Fit Jr housing** (39-01-2080, terminals
44485-1212) on the board, plus two red ¼ in QCs and a blue ¼ in QC, in ¼ in
braided loom (12.2 in overall). All **20 AWG GXL**: black, white, and
four red. Sheet 2 is the wiring schematic (pin positions and colours as
drawn). Rev B changed the terminals from Micro-Fit 3.0 to **Mini-Fit Jr**.
