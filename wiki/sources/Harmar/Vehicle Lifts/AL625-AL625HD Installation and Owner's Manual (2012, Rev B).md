# AL625 / AL625HD Installation and Owner's Manual (2012, Rev B)

Install and owner's manual for the **AL625 and AL625HD** inside-platform
("hybrid") van lift. P/N 999-3C13-AA Rev B (TEC2009), © 2012, 24 pages.
The text calls the lift the **"Freedom Lift Tracker"**, so it's an older,
acquired design. Feeds [[Hybrid-Vehicle-Lifts]].

**Status:** on Harmar's 2022 FSS price list (AL-625HD-12, "HD hybrid van
lift"), but **not on Harmar's 2026 Mobility Solutions chart**, which
lists only the AL600 and AL690 as hybrids. Likely discontinued; check
with Harmar before quoting one. The manual's own phone number
(866-378-6648) is old; use Harmar tech support, 800-833-0478.

The manual never says how the AL625 and AL625HD differ; both are covered
by the same text.

## Key facts

- **Max 350 lb**, power chairs and scooters only.
- Parts: trolley assembly (on tracks, with a lead screw and actuator),
  platform assembly, interlock kit, wired pendant, **RF remote**, battery
  charger, mounting bars. A "Mobile Power" battery pack is referenced in
  troubleshooting.
- **Control cut-off switch: "RED IS ON."** Turn it off whenever the lift
  isn't in use, so the remote or pendant can't run it.
- Chair brake on and **strapped down** for transport.

## Installing (12 steps)

1. **Wiring:** black to battery negative, **red last**; run the harness
   back and secure the plug about even with the end of the hitch
   receiver; coil excess, don't cut it; never a secondary power source.
2. **Mounting bars** on the vehicle's **rear seat attachment points**
   with 4 U-bolts (½ in tube spacers if needed); center them. Or bolt
   the trolley's side rails **straight to the floor** (four 3/8 in
   holes; check underneath for tank, lines, rust or collision repair;
   seal the underside with silicone). Dodge Grand Caravan: position it
   so the spare tire release bolt stays reachable.
3. Trolley on the bars; 4. **leveling feet** (jam nut under to raise;
   trim the threads if the high-threshold feature is removed);
5. trolley **as far rearward as possible** so the platform clears the
   bumper, centered, then tighten.
6. Prop the front boom off the lead screw (or have a helper), **IN** to
   extend the actuator, watch the roof, pin the upper boom. Move out ~2
   in and **remove the two shipping blocks** from the tracks. Decide
   whether to keep the high-threshold kit.
7. **OUT**, then slide the platform's vertical tube into the boom head
   (two lower holes).
8. Run it empty; check bumper, threshold and side clearances.
9. **Level the platform with the customer's chair on it** (¼ in set
   screws).
10. **Vertical-to-horizontal transition:** a limit switch ends lifting
    and starts travel in. Near 350 lb, adjust the contact screw (in =
    more lift, out = less).
11. **Home position:** the stop rod trips a limit switch; the
    **alignment pins must seat in the front plate holes**, or adjust the
    platform angle under load. The pins and straps together secure the
    chair.
12. **Straps:** center positions for power chairs, side loop positions
    for scooters; buckle on the side nearest the vehicle.

## Maintenance

- **Keep the tracks clear**: even small stones can stop it.
- Check straps and clearances every use; hardware **monthly**.
- **Twice a year:** wheel-bearing grease on the lead screw and all
  bearing points.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Check |
|---|---|
| Won't work | Cut-off switch on (red); power cord plugged into the harness; vehicle battery; Mobile Power pack LED (red = charge); pendant plugged in |
| Remote dead, pendant works | RF remote batteries; **RF fuse** |
| Runs slowly | Weak vehicle battery or battery pack; load over 350 lb (remove items stored on the chair) |
| Chair loose or tight | Re-adjust the strap |
| Loud clicking after lifting | Actuator clutching out: adjust the boom limit switch contact screw |
| Platform hits bumper or vehicle | Alignment hardware loose or misaligned |
