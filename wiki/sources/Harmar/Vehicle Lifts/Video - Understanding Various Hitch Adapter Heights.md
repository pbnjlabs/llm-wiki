# Video: Understanding Various Hitch Adapter Heights

Harmar training video #12, 5:35, by a Harmar Technical Services tech.
A tour of Harmar's hitch adapters, then three vehicles showing how hitch
height picks the drop. No date or part numbers except the AL1235U.

## The range (0:10–2:02)

| Type | Lengths | Drops |
|---|---|---|
| **Class 2** | short, standard, long, extra long | 2 in, 4 in |
| **Class 3** | short, standard, extra long | 2, 4, 5, 6 in |
| **HD** (for HD lifts, e.g. the 500 HD and AL301XL HD) | | 2, 4, 6, 8 in |
| **AL1235U universal drop** (class 3) | | **standard or 2, 3, 4, 5 in**; "the most common one we send out" |
| **RV** adapter | | |

Length depends on how far the lift has to stand off the bumper (a spare
tire, for example). The lift fits to a **hitch post**, which fits the
adapter. Swing-aways have the post pre-welded.

## On the vehicles

| Time | Vehicle | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 2:10 | Cadillac, **class 2**, about **10 in** off the ground | **Class 2 standard adapter**. It sits close to the ground, so **flip the standard adapter upside down** for about **1½–2 in more ground clearance**. That's Harmar's usual advice. |
| 2:49 | Car, **class 3** | AL1235U at standard height looks right; 2 in drop would also work; 3 and 5 in are too low. |
| 4:02 | Mid-size truck, class 3 | 5 in drop is too close to the ground; probably 3 in. |
| 4:41 | Lifted truck, **21 in** | Standard can't reach. **6 in drop.** |

Measure with a tape. Tires, wheels and lift kits change what you need.

## Checked against the manuals (the chart wins)

The video picks by eye. **Use the chart:** HV (hitch height, top of the
receiver to the ground) against the lift and post. In
[[Bulletin - AL1235U Universal Drop Hitch Adapter]] the AL1235U settings
are **standard, 2, 3 and 5 in** (no 4 in; a sales sheet's 4 in is
overruled by the bulletin), and **21 in HV on a standard AL107 post is a
3 in drop**; the 6 in AL1236 is for 20–24 in. The video's "21 in" may
not have been HV, so don't take a 6 in drop from it. Inverting is allowed
only on the standard adapters and the class 2 2 in, class 2 5 in and
class 3 2 in drops. Full rules: [[Hitch-Adapters]].
