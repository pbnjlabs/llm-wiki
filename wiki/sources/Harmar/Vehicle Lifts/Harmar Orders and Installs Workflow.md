# Harmar Orders and Installs Workflow (PMS)

PMS's own steps for **ordering Harmar vehicle lifts** and **invoicing
Harmar installs**. Built from two scans:

- `harmar_orders_workflow.pdf`: 2 handwritten pages (undated)
- `vl_workflow.pdf`: a real order email and VA invoice from 14 Sep 2021,
  marked **"as reference"**

**Left out on purpose:** customer and staff names, addresses, phone
numbers, email addresses and the VA PO number. People are described by
role.

## 1. Ordering a lift from Harmar

Email PMS's **Harmar order contact** (the same contact on the
[[Vehicle Lift Phone Interview Form]]).

- **Subject:** `New order for <veteran's last name>`
- **Body:**

  > Good morning/afternoon,
  >
  > I need to place a new order for:
  >
  > Vehicle:
  > Mobility device:
  > Lift:
  > SA: *(swing-away)*
  > Class: — adapter *(if a drop hitch is needed)*
  >
  > Thanks!

**Reference example (2021):** Vehicle: 2003 Ford F-150 std cab short
bed. Mobility device: Golden GC440 4-wheel scooter. Lift: **AL100**.
SA: **AL105**. **Class III hitch adapter with universal drop.**

## 2. Invoicing a VA vehicle lift (reference invoice, 2021)

Header fields: Veteran ID, P.O. number, technician, PA, serial number,
equipment ("VEH LIFT").

| Item code | Description | Price (2021) |
|---|---|---|
| MISC | "Install a vehicle lift on veteran's <year make model>", with the PMD (e.g. GT GC440 4-wheel scooter) | $0.00 |
| AL 100 | Universal scooter lift, installation included | $1,891.46 |
| AL 105 | Swing-away option (400 lb capacity), installation included | $427.14 |
| HITCH CLASS III | Class 3 **receiver hitch for the vehicle**, installation included | $241.21 |
| Parts | Universal drop (the Harmar drop hitch adapter) | $200.00 |
| GSA FSS-65 II F | Contract number **36F79721D0058**, patient mobility devices, vehicle lifts | $0.00 |
| ZONE 6 | **VA-specific** travel zone charge, by county (zone chart not yet on file) | $190.00 |

The **zone charge applies only to VA jobs**; non-VA invoices don't carry
it.

Total on the sample: $2,949.81. Prices are from 2021 and are only a
reference for the invoice layout.

## 3. Harmar Lift Squad installs (Harmar pays PMS)

For installs PMS does **for Harmar**, through Harmar's **Lift Squad**:

1. **Print the paperwork** from the email.
2. **Create a project invoice.**
   - Customer/job: **Harmar Mobility**
   - Misc line: `Harmar Lift Squad install – <year make model>` (e.g.
     2003 GMC Sierra)
   - PMD line: the mobility device (e.g. GC440 scooter)
   - Lines for the **lift**, the **swing-away** and the **hitch
     adapter**
3. **When the install is complete:** scan the Harmar paperwork and PMS's
   invoice, attach the **install pictures**, and **email everything to
   PMS's billing contact**.
