# AL6000 Series Installation and Service Manual (2022, Rev C)

P/N 630-00151-C, 11NOV22. Dealer/installer manual for **AL6000/AL6000HD**
— the older Hybrid Lift generation (see
[[AL6000 Series Owner's Manual (2021, Rev A)]]). Substantially more
detailed than the 2025 AL600/AL690 install manual in three areas:

## Adjustable L-shaped mounting brackets
All four base mounting brackets slide along a channel (loosen, don't
remove, 4 screws per bracket; tee-nuts can be pushed back into alignment
if they escape) — lets the same lift accommodate different seat
attachment-point spacings without custom drilling. The 2025 AL600/AL690
manual has no equivalent adjustable-bracket mechanism described.

## BA25 kit (3rd-row folding-seat installation)
A separate foot+top-leg assembly kit for vehicles where the 3rd row folds
into a floor well rather than being removable — adjustable to the well's
measured depth in 1/4" increments, mounted around the folded seat rather
than through it. (The 2025 manual's equivalent is the similarly-purposed
but differently-executed **BA-02 kit**, referenced but not detailed in
[[Hybrid Vehicle Lifts Installation and Service Manual (AL600-AL690, 2025)]].)

## Hold-down arm adjustment
Vertical (slide the bracket on tee-nuts, 12 screws) and horizontal
(remove/reposition the whole arm assembly, including a full 180° flip)
adjustment procedures, keyed to measuring the mobility device's
seat-to-ground height and desired hold-down position — supports the
optional hold-down arm accessory documented in the owner's manual.

## Platform tilt adjustment
Set screws on the platform's back flange adjust tilt independent of the
lift's mounting angle — not present in the 2025 AL600/AL690 manual.

## Maintenance (rail lubrication)
Far more granular than the 2025 manual's single blanket "grease the
rails" instruction: names Loctite LB 8700 (or Loctite Moly-50, or
"similar Metal-Free Molybdenum Disulfide Petrolatum grease") specifically,
and walks driver-side/passenger-side inner/middle rails and the base
outer rail individually with diagrams — explicitly **never** grease the
base's middle or inner rails, only its outer rail underside plus all
tower-section rails.

## Manual override
Same 1/4" hex mechanism and CW/CCW-to-motor-direction mapping as
AL600/AL690 (Lifting: CW=up; Rotational: CW=out) — confirms the override
system carried forward unchanged between hardware generations despite the
accessory differences above.
