# Harmar Mobility Solutions Chart (2026, Rev F)

Harmar's two-page **vehicle-lift comparison chart**, MK-000173 Rev F,
dated **11 May 2026**. Columns per model: vehicle types, devices,
capacity, install weight, powered functions, securement, hitch class,
arm height / reach, included accessories, options, warranty. The newest
Harmar lineup document on file. Read from the page images.

**Current lineup per the chart:** hoist lifts AL215, AL225, AL425,
AL425HD, AL435, AL055, AL065; truck lifts AL435T, AL815CC, AL825,
AL825CC, AL835; inside-platform hybrids **AL600, AL690**; hitch-mounted
AL001, AL003, AL010/AL050, AL015, AL030, AL100/AL100HD, AL100-DE,
AL160, AL300/AL300HD, AL300-FULL, **AL300RV**, AL301XL/AL301XLHD,
AL500/AL500HD, AL560/AL560XL, AL580/AL580XL, AL580-HDX. **Not on it:**
AL625/AL625HD (on the 2022 FSS list; likely discontinued).

(AL300RV 30 in). Most include the **AL107 hitch post**, an **AL123 class

AL010/AL015) and a license plate mount.
