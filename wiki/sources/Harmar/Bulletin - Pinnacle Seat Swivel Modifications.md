# Bulletin: Pinnacle Seats May Require Additional Installation Steps

CSV-000064 Rev A. Affects SL300/SL600 Pinnacle seats shipped between
Nov 18 and Dec 8 (serials ASSPIN201118-00016 through
ASSPIN201207-00013) — a batch-specific manufacturing alert, not a
general design issue.

## Covers
Symptoms: seat swivel bars don't fully release from the seat post, swivel
springs lose function right after install, seat won't lock at the 80°
swiveled position, LED/beep codes show an activated interlock even when
the seat is in the locked travel position. Root cause: the magnetic screw
on the swivel bar isn't inserted far enough, or a stop tab on the chassis
underside leaves too much gap. Fixes: adjust the magnetic screw in small
increments (Fig. 4-5), replace a damaged swivel spring (two interchangeable
replacement part numbers, 460-00033 or 15199), or bend the chassis stop
tab to eliminate riding-position play. Escalation: Proximity Sensor
Replacement Kit 140-00159 if adjustments don't resolve it.
