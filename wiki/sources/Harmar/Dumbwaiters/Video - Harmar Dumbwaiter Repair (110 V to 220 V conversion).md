- the **drive board**, which the narrator calls the "Regent" board or

## Steps

| Clip | Time | Step |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:24 | Plan: **take the white wire off and replace it with an orange wire**, then **set the drive from 110 to 220**. |
| 2 | 0:00 | **Cut the white wire from the transformer.** Cut both ends off the orange and white wires, and **put a new terminal on the orange wire** to connect it to the white wire going to the drive board. |
| 3 | 0:00 | **Take the cover off the drive**: push a screwdriver into the tab above it and lift it off. |
| 3 | 0:09 | **Move the two orange jumpers to the 220 slots.** |
| 4 | 0:00 | With the jumpers in the 220 slots there should be **two bare terminals on each side** (the empty 110 positions). The lift now takes **220 V, 60 Hz**. |
