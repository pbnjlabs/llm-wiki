# Video: How to Wire an Automatic Door Operator on a Highlander CPL

Harmar training video #16, 2:26, presented by a member of Harmar's
Regulatory Affairs and Quality team. Wiring the platform-gate **door
operator** (called a "DMT" operator) on a **Highlander CPL**, Harmar's
commercial platform lift from before the [[HighlanderII]]. **No CPL
manual is on file.** No date.

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:18 | The operator is already mounted on the **rear guard panel and the platform gate**; it can also go on the front guard panel. Lifts ship with **4 holes pre-drilled** in the panel for it. |
| 0:40 | Take the wire from the tower. For the **lower door operator it's the red and white pair**. |
| 0:52 | Operator cover off; feed the wire up through a hole in the bottom of the operator. |
| 1:01 | **Twist the wire ends tightly.** Loose strands can short and cause electrical faults. |
| 1:16 | Connect to **terminals 1 and 2** on the operator's PC board. **Polarity doesn't matter** (white to 2, red to 1 in the video). Tighten with a small screwdriver. |
| 2:02 | **Tug each wire** to check it's held. |
| 2:14 | Switch and cam adjustments are in **the manual packed with the door operator**. |

The operator's own manual isn't on file either. For the current lift,
the [[HighlanderII]] install manual covers automatic door openers (the
Harmar order form lists VPL-078-D, "automatic door opener for full
door"); check its wiring section rather than this video.
