# Bulletin: Highlander II PCB Moisture Protection Plate Retrofit

CSV-000067 Rev A, effective 17Dec20 (bulletin issued 16Jul21). Adds a
plexiglass "Moisture Protection Plate" over the Highlander II control
PCB.

## Covers
Anti-static precautions, removing/documenting-with-photo the existing
board's wiring before swap, dielectric grease application points (plug
pins, terminal blocks, status indicator cable — with emphasis on fully
coating pin 1's back side), mounting the plexiglass cover with asymmetric
nylon standoffs (2 white on the left side, 1 white + 1 black on the
right), and a post-install functional cycle test (6 full trips minimum,
3 on AC and 3 on battery backup if equipped).
