# Video: How to Wire a Flush or Surface Mount Call Station for a VPL

Harmar training video #27, 3:14, presented by Harmar's regulatory and
quality assurance lead. Wiring a landing **call/send station** for a
Harmar vertical platform lift. No date or lift model given.

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:10 | The station comes on a **4 × 4 box**. Remove the cover screws (flat or Phillips). |
| 0:31 | Two ways to mount it: **surface** (the box on the wall), or **flush** (just the cover into a 4 × 4 box already recessed in a stud wall). |
| 0:49 | Tools: **wire strippers and crimpers**. |
| 1:06 | Run the wiring, normally **in conduit**. **Check the national and local electrical codes** for what's required. |
| 1:21 | The station has **red, white and blue** wires: **match colour to colour** with **crimped butt connectors** (blue, then white, then red in the video). |
| 2:22 | Orient the faceplate so the **push button and up/down arrows point the right way**, fold the wires in, and refit the cover (4 screws). |

## Checked against the manuals

The [[HighlanderII]] install manual (Lower Landing Call Stations) says
the same in outline: surface or flush mount, but on a **2-gang outlet
box**, connections **crimped**, and mounting heights per **state and
local codes (ASME A18.1-2020)**. For a Highlander II, follow its manual.
