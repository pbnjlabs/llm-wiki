# Highlander II with Enclosure Owner's Manual

P/N 630-00154 Rev A, 22DEC2021. Covers the **Highlander II with Enclosure**
vertical platform lift (VPL) product family: VPL400, VPL600, VPL800,
VPL1000, VPL1200, VPL1400 series (per warranty section).

## Covers
- 750 lb max payload, 115V AC / 15A / single-phase, 2/3 hp motor + ACME
  screw drive, optional battery backup
- Call/Send operation, entering/exiting platform, emergency lowering
  procedure (manual override via 1/4" socket at the top cap)
- 4-LED top-cap status code table (green/amber/red combinations) — see
  [[HighlanderII]]
- Residential vs. commercial maintenance/inspection requirements, 6-month
  dealer maintenance schedule
- 3-year limited warranty — see [[Harmar-Warranty]]
