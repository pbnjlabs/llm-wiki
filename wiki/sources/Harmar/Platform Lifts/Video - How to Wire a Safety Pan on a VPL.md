# Video: How to Wire a Safety Pan on a VPL

Harmar training video #31, 1:19, by a Harmar Technical Services tech.
No date. The lift appears to be an older Harmar residential VPL
(black tower, controller box at the top of the tower), probably the
same generation as [[Video - Electronic Components of the Residential VPL (RPL)]].
Probably not a [[HighlanderII]].

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:12 | Pull the **wiring harness out of the bottom of the platform**. It's tucked in for shipping. |
| 0:20 | It ends in a **2-pin Molex connector**. Run it up along the **trolley** and through a **clip** held by **2 Phillips screws** (removed beforehand in the video); refit the clip with the harness through it. |
| 0:37 | Plug the **red and black 2-pin Molex** into the safety pan harness. |
| 0:52 | At the **controller at the top of the tower**, find the plug labelled **SAFETY PAN**. **Remove the jumper; nothing else goes in its place.** It's tagged "remove jumper": a **3-pin connector with one red wire looped** back into the same plug. |

## Checked against the manuals

The same idea is in the current manuals: [[VPL400-X Install Manual]]
says "remove the safety pan jumper and plug in the platform safety pan
harness", and lists a **Safety Pan Jumper** plug at the controller. No
manual for this older RPL generation is on file.
