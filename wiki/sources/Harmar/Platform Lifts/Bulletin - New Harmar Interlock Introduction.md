# Bulletin: New Interlock Introduction (Highlander II, All Gates)

CSV-000077 Rev A, effective for units built 7/18/22 onward. Harmar
introduced its own gate interlock (replacing Honeywell) to improve lead
times/quality/supply — gate mounting holes and key openings changed to
accept either brand going forward; Honeywell stock is kept for spares/
preference.

## Covers
Harmar interlock: IP65 moisture rating, hand-switchable in the field,
commercial-rated on all versions, round barrel key. **Not backwards
compatible** with pre-7/18/22 gates. Internal jumper wiring differs
(connects B-C instead of Honeywell's B-D); wire colors differ too
(white/red/green+yellow-stripe vs. Honeywell's white/orange/green, both
with an added black wire on landing gates). A double-jog-up-then-normal
behavior on platform gates is intentional (solenoid-enable feature tied to
pulling on the door) — two extra spacer washers between keeper and keeper
plate make this work correctly.
