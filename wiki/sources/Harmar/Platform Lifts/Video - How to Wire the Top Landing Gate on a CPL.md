# Video: How to Wire the Top Landing Gate on a CPL

Harmar training video #20, 4:47, presented by a member of Harmar's
Regulatory Affairs and Quality Assurance team. Wiring the **top landing
gate** on a Harmar **CPL** (commercial platform lift): the gate's
**call/send** station and its **EMI interlock**. **No CPL manual is on
file.** No date.

The colours below are for this gate and harness. **They don't all
match colour for colour, and they may differ on other lifts**, so check
the wiring diagram for the lift you're on.

## Call/send (on the upper gate frame)

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 0:24 | Cover off. There are two black harnesses. The **gate** one has **blue, yellow, red**; the **machine** harness coming up has **red, black, red, blue, white**. |
| 0:48 | Twist-on wire nuts: **red to red, blue to blue, white to yellow**. |

## Interlock

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 1:38 | **Blue and brown** together (pre-twisted) under one nut. |
| 1:57 | A **black** from the gate harness to a **black** from the interlock. |
| 2:21 | Then **black to green**, **orange to yellow**, **yellow to white**. |
| 3:32 | **One red wire is left over and isn't used.** |
| 3:02 | Keep spare wire nuts: some don't grip. **A bad connection means a gate failure.** |

## Test and finish

| Time | Step |
|---|---|
| 3:40 | Test the **call station**: up and down. |
| 3:52 | Interlock test with the door off: lift the **small silver bar on the back of the open interlock** and insert the interlock piece. |
| 4:02 | At the main control, **remove the jumper from the top-landing interlock plug** and plug in the harness. |
| 4:18 | Hold the door switch and run the lift down and up from the unit control. |
| 4:28 | Secure all the wiring inside the gate frame and fit the cap on the back. |
