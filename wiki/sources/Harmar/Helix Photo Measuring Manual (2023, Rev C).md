# Helix Photo Measuring Manual (2023, Rev C)

P/N 610-00016-01 Rev C, 3NOV23 — the **Helix PhotoMap** staircase-measuring
kit manual (calibration/landing/step marker placement, camera settings,
photo sequence, Dropbox/email submission). Functionally the same document
as [[CSL Series Photo Measuring Manual]] (Rev B, 2019-07-08), which is
physically misfiled under `MANUALS/Bruno/Bruno Curved SL/` — this is a
**newer revision of the same manual**, now correctly filed under the
dedicated `MANUALS/Harmar/` folder the human created. See
[[Staircase-Measuring-System]] for the cross-manufacturer comparison with
Bruno's Picture Perfect system.

## Notable content (not diffed line-by-line against Rev B)
- Explicitly requires a **minimum of 14 targets / 7 markers per photo**,
  and includes a "Common Errors" section with labeled sample-error photos
  (blurry, too much angle/low shots, target cut off, target washed out,
  target blocked by rail, too many markers)
- Kit contents: markers, calibration markers, camera, data/sync cable,
  charger, thumb drive (videos/forms/manuals), tape measure
- Submission: harmar.com/project-image-upload (Dropbox, preferred),
  Helix@harmar.com (email), or physical camera return (last resort)
- Contact: Harmar Technical Support, 800-833-0478
