# Hoist Vehicle Lift Brochure

P/N MK-000200 Rev B, 04AUG26. Consumer-facing brochure revealing a
**second, entirely distinct Harmar vehicle-lift category** alongside the
already-documented [[AL-Series-Outside-Vehicle-Lifts]]: **"Hoist" lifts**,
mounted inside the vehicle's cargo area, that swing outside to load/lift
a mobility device and then stow it inside (preserving use of the backup
camera and protecting the device from weather). See
[[Hoist-Series-Inside-Vehicle-Lifts]] for the model-by-model writeup.

## Covers
- Product positioning: Harmar frames its whole vehicle-lift catalog as
  three architectures — hitch-mounted **outside** (AL-series), **hoist**
  (inside, cargo area), and **hybrid** (inside platform) — this brochure
  covers only the hoist category
- Per-model specs for AL055, AL065, AL215, AL225, AL425, AL425HD, AL435,
  AL435T, AL815CC, AL825, AL825CC, AL835 (lifting capacity, install
  weight, adjustable length/height, powered functions, securement,
  included/optional accessories)
- Truck-bed hoist lifts (AL435T, AL815CC, AL825/AL825CC, AL835) vs.
  van/SUV/minivan hoist lifts (AL055 through AL435)
- Same 3-year transferable warranty (parts only) as the AL-series outside
  lifts — see [[Harmar-Warranty]]
- Founded 1998, Sarasota FL; company-level marketing claims (load-testing
  practice, LiftSquad dealer/service network)
