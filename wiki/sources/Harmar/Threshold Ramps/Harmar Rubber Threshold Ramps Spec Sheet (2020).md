# Harmar Rubber Threshold Ramps Spec Sheet (2020)

Harmar's two-page sell sheet for its **rubber threshold ramps**,
MKT-000171 Rev A, 17 Jul 2020. These are the **RAMPRBKIT 1R–4R** items
on Harmar's FSS list ([[Harmar VA FSS Price List (2018-2023)]]). Read
from the page images (the spec table's text layer is scrambled). Feeds
[[TRANSITIONS-Threshold-Ramps]] (comparison section).

**PMS mostly uses EZ-ACCESS TRANSITIONS for thresholds** (per the
human); this is on file for comparison and VA requests.

## How it works

- **Modular rubber sections that lock together front to back** with a
  hidden **dovetail** joint. Each section adds **½ in of rise**, from **1
  in up to 4 in**. You build the height you need.
- High-impact **recycled rubber**; indoor or outdoor; can be **trimmed,
  cut or notched** for a custom fit; maintenance-free.
- **850 lb** capacity, **36 in usable width**.
- **Max incline 4° (7%)**, which Harmar says meets ADA. That's gentler
  than 1:12 (4.8°), so it's within the PMS house standard.

## Sections

| Rise | Section depth | Usable width | Overall width | Weight per piece |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 in | 10.4 in | 36 in | 39 in | 10.2 lb |
| 1.5 in | 6.9 in | 36 in | 41 in | 11.4 lb |
| 2 in | 6.9 in | 36 in | 42.5 in | 13.2 lb |
| 2.5 in | 7 in | 36 in | 44.2 in | 17.2 lb |
| 3 in | 7.5 in | 36 in | 46 in | 22.6 lb |
| 3.5 in | 7 in | 36 in | 46 in | 27.2 lb |
| 4 in | 7 in | 36 in | 46 in | 28.6 lb |

The 1 in piece is the leading edge. **Wiki arithmetic, not on the
sheet:** if each rise is built from all the pieces up to it, a 4 in
ramp is about **53 in deep** (10.4 + 6.9 + 6.9 + 7 + 7.5 + 7 + 7) and
weighs about **130 lb** in total, a slope of about 1:13. Check the
actual kit contents before quoting depth.
