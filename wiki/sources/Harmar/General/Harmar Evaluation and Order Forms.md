# Harmar Evaluation & Order Forms

Six dealer-facing "Evaluation/Order Form" PDFs — sales/configuration
paperwork, not technical documentation, but each confirms real SKU/option
naming useful for cross-checking model pages. Grouped into one source
page since they're all the same genre and none warrants a full technical
writeup on its own.

- `Harmar-MKF-000021-S-SL600-OF-V2.pdf` (23JUN26, MKF-000021 S) —
  Pinnacle **SSL-SL600** order form. Confirms SKU prefix "SSL" (not
  "SL"), SL600FR-LH/RH folding rail options, SSL-KEY/SSL-MLT/SSL-WA/
  SSL-EXT/SSL-AB1 accessory codes.
- `Harmar-MKF-000050-L-SL300-OF-V2.pdf` (23JUN26, MKF-000050 L) — Pinnacle
  **SSL-SL300** order form, same accessory-code family, plus a
  SL300COS ("chassis obstruction sensors") configuration and
  SSL-RET (dealer-installed retractable seat belt).
- `Harmar-MKF-000060-G-HighlanderII-OF-V2.pdf` (26JUL24, MKF-000060 G) —
  Highlander II VPL order form. Confirms VPL-R (residential) vs. VPL-C
  (commercial, bundles lighted E-stop/alarm + handrail) as the top-level
  split; VPL-030/VPL-033 "READYUPS" battery-backup naming; full
  platform/gate/door/fascia SKU catalog. Notes VPL units ship F.O.B. from
  a Missouri factory (distinct from the Sarasota, FL headquarters).
- `Harmar-MKTF-000002-L-Helix-OF.pdf` (4NOV2022, MKTF-000002 L) — Helix
  order form. **Confirms the Over-Speed Governor (OSG) is standard
  equipment on Helix for both commercial and residential applications**,
  ASME 18.1-2017 compliant — see [[Overspeed-Safety-Brake]]. Also a
  detailed pre-sale checklist (staircase eval, photos via
  harmar.com/project-image-upload) feeding into
  [[Staircase-Measuring-System]].
- `Harmar-MKTF-000064-A-VA-HelixPhone-OF.pdf` (12AUG2022, MKTF-000064-A) —
  a VA-specific **phone evaluation** script for Helix (parallel to the
  already-ingested [[Vehicle Lift Phone Interview Form]] for AL vehicle
  lifts) — not linked as technical content.
- `Harmar-MKTF-000065-B-VPL400-X-OF.pdf` (14APR23, MKTF-000065-B) —
  VPL400-X order form. Confirms VPL400-X is **residential-only** (no
  VPL-C/commercial checkbox at all, unlike the general Highlander II
  form) and has its own e-stop SKU, VPL-012-X.
