# Harmar VA FSS Price List (2018–2023)

Harmar's own **Authorized Federal Supply Schedule Price List** for the
VA: Patient Mobility Devices, **FSC Group 65, Part II, Section F
(65 II F)**, FSC Class 6530. **Contract 36F79718D0537**, modification
P00008 dated **04/15/22**, contract period **Aug 9, 2018 – Aug 8,
2023**. Harmar Mobility, Sarasota, FL; business size: large. Pages
29–35 of PMS's [[PMS FSS Contracts Binder]].

Awarded SINs: **F-7** transport (vehicle) lifts, price list pp. 5–6 of
Harmar's numbering; **F-8a** threshold ramps, p. 7. Harmar's 65 II A
list (stairlifts, VPLs) isn't in the binder.

**How PMS uses it (per the human):** a manufacturer's FSS price is the
**ceiling for what PMS can charge the VA** for that item, and the
reference for **building VA bids**. Not needed for daily work; it's on
file for when it's asked for. **The FSS price lists are already loaded
into PMS's QuickBooks**, so VA estimates use the QuickBooks items. These are Harmar's prices, not PMS rates.
PMS sells Harmar vehicle lifts to the VA under **Supply's own 65 II F
contract, 36F79721D0058** (see [[VA-FSS-Contracts]]).

> [!warning] Older contract period
> The contract has been **renewed** (5-year terms, per the human). This
> copy shows the 2018–2023 period at the April 2022 modification, so
> prices may have changed. Check the current list (GSA eLibrary or the
> VA MedSurg catalog search) before quoting a number.

Harmar VA contact: **va@harmar.com, 800-833-0478** (8 a.m.–7 p.m. ET,
Mon–Fri), also used for return authorizations.

## FSS warranty (pp. 30–31)

- **3 years** from the date of retail purchase on material, mechanical
  and electrical components (parts), **batteries, paint and covers**,
  if the product was installed, maintained and used properly.
- **1 year of labor**, covering all labor costs, **for purchases through
  Harmar's FSS contract**.
- Doesn't cover the vehicle, or lift defects caused by the vehicle;
  maintenance or adjustments; consequential damage or loss of use.
  Void if lost, damaged by accident, over-stressed, misused, neglected
  or modified.
- Defective parts go back to Harmar prepaid for inspection before
  credit, repair or replacement (Harmar's choice).

This is **better than Harmar's retail vehicle-lift warranty**, which
excludes labor, batteries and paint. See [[Harmar-Warranty]].

## Returns (pp. 31–32)

- **Stock items:** no returns after **30 days**. **Custom parts** can't
  be returned.
- **Every return needs an RMA** from Harmar first (va@harmar.com or
  800-833-0478, with the order number, account number, item numbers and
  contact info). Harmar credits once the RMA is issued, before the item
  arrives. RMAs can't be extended or reissued.
- Serial numbers are assigned to the end user and recorded on the
  invoice paperwork. **Mark the RMA number on all return documents and
  on each package's address label.**
- Return everything: all original parts, packing, blank warranty cards,
  accessories and documents, in as-new condition.
- **Ordering entity's error: 25% restocking fee plus freight.** Harmar's
  error: no fee, no freight.
- **End user expiration** (the veteran died): accepted automatically,
  **no restocking fee**. Custom-built product: credit only for common
  parts.
- **End user refusal: 25% restocking fee.** Harmar will try to talk the
  user into going ahead with the install first.
- **PO expiration:** if the install can't happen within **70 days of
  the PO date**, Harmar sends the purchasing agent a disposition letter
  and helps decide by **day 180**. No decision by day 180: Harmar closes
  it out and issues RMAs to get the product back.

Compare PMS's own VA return rule (10% restocking plus manufacturer
shipping, or "VA Stock") on [[VA Ramp Returns and Restocking Fee (2025)]].
