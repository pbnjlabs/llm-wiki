# Harmar Product Insights (2021–2023)

Eleven one-page **"Product Insights — From Harmar's Product Experts"**
sheets, dated May 2021 to Feb 2023. Short dealer-education sheets, part
marketing and part tech notes, grouped on one page like
[[Harmar Product Spec Sheets]]. **They rank below the install and
owner's manuals.** Where they disagree, the manual wins (two conflicts
are flagged below).

## Vehicle lifts

**Selection of Vehicle Lift Type** (`Harmar-Lift-Selection.pdf`, May
17, 2021). Pick hoist, hitch-mount or hybrid by vehicle, user and use:

| Factor | Points to |
|---|---|
| Vehicle: van/SUV | Hoist, hitch mount, hybrid |
| Vehicle: truck | Hoist (truck lift), hitch mount |
| Vehicle: sedan | Hitch mount |
| User highly capable (walking/standing, hand strength, dexterity) | Hoist, hitch mount |
| User medium | Hybrid, hitch mount |
| User low | Hybrid or hitch mount **with hold-down** |
| Device protected from weather | Hoist (SUV/van), hybrid. Not: hitch mount, truck hoist |
| Securement | Docking device: hoist/truck, hybrid. Hold-down bar/arm: hitch mount. Q-Straint retractors: hitch mount, hybrid |
| Ease of use | Easy: hitch mount. Moderate: hybrid. Moderate+: hoist |
| Fits in a garage | Hoist, hybrid. **Hitch mount sticks out past the vehicle** |

Then check fit with **Harmar's Compatibility Calculator** (hitch class,
max tongue weight, cargo area, mobility device).

**Docking Devices for Insider, Truck and Hoist Lifts**
(`Harmar-Docking-Devices.pdf`, Aug 2, 2021). Inside lifts **need a
docking device**, the link between lift and chair or scooter, usually
on the center seat post or the four-post seat frame.

| Type | Use | Models (P = power chair, S = scooter, M = manual chair) |
|---|---|---|
| **C-Arms** (13): square receiver, square or round seat post, power seat lift, 4-point seat mount | Scooters, power chairs, with a device-specific adapter | DD13 (S), DD19 (P/S), DD24 (P/S), DD28 (P/S), DD29 (S), DD30R1 (P/S), DD33 (P), DD38 (P), DD40 (P/S), DD41R1 (P), DD44 (P), DD46 (S), DD49 (S) |
| **Spreader bars** (9), 19, 24 or 30 in; attach to the frame or tie-down loops | Power chairs | DD20 (P/S), DD21 (P/S), DD27 (P), DD32 (P), DD36 (P), DD37 (P), DD43 (P), DD45 (P), DD48 (M) |
| **Straight bar** | Seat-off lifting of scooters and power chairs | DD17 (P/S) |
| **Cable harness** | Small folding scooters | DD39 (S) |
| **Manual WC lifting bars** (3) | Rigid and folding manual chairs; folding chairs upright or on their side | DD22, DD23, DD34 |
DD45, the **4-point docking system**, is a spreader-bar type whose
Black Diamond carabiners clip to all four tie-down loops. **The
Compatibility Calculator names the exact DD for each lift and chair.** Docking device prices are on
[[Harmar VA FSS Price List (2018-2023)]].

**Hitch Receiver Adapters** (`Harmar-Hitch-Adapters-Product-Insight.pdf`,
Dec 2, 2021). Required on every hitch-mount lift. Two rules: **the
platform must reach the ground and sit level**, and **once it touches
the ground it must not keep running and start lifting the car** (that
damages the lift). Measure bumper distance and hitch height, then use
the Compatibility Calculator or the size chart in the lift's install
manual. **AL1235U** is the universal (most common) adapter.

> [!warning] AL1235U drop settings disagree
> This sheet says the AL1235U sets as standard or a **2, 3, 4 or 5 in
> drop**. Harmar's AL1235U install bulletin
> ([[Bulletin - AL1235U Universal Drop Hitch Adapter]]) lists standard,
> **2, 3 or 5 in**, with no 4 in. Go by the bulletin and its chart.

**Travel scooters** (`Harmar-Travel-Scooters-and-Lifts.pdf`, June 2,
2021). Sales sheet: travel scooter users often find they need a lift a
few months in (heaviest scooter pieces 28+ lb). Features the **AL015
Profile Micro**:

| AL015 Profile Micro | |
|---|---|
| Use | 3- and 4-wheel compact scooters |
| Lift capacity | 150 lb |

| Securement | Automatic hold-down foot, front and rear wheel cradles |
| Options | Swing-away, battery pack, scooter cover, 4-wheel cradle |
| **Min. vehicle tongue weight** | **200 lb** |

## Highlander II VPL

**The Guardian System** (`Harmar-Guardian-System.pdf`, Jan 26, 2021).
The top cap's **4 LEDs** show status by color and solid/flashing (the
sheet says "over 50 statuses"):

| Lights | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Green solid | OK to operate |
| Green flashing | OK to operate; on battery backup or in **Service Mode** |
| Amber solid | OK to operate; service recommended |
| Amber flashing | **Limited** (up or down only); minor fault |
| Red solid | No function; check E-stop, interlocks, etc. |
| Red flashing | **Do not use.** Major fault, service required |

- **Cycle count:** service warning at **7,500 cycles**, **shuts down at
  8,000** until a dealer does a full motor and drive-system evaluation
  and resets it per the install manual.
- Other required-service flags follow certain faults (flood switch,
  OSG, slowing travel speed). They're meant to make the dealer inspect
  the unit in person.
- **Service Mode:** a PC-board button that bypasses ancillary safeties
  for a set time, then restores them automatically. Press again to
  extend. Lights flash green.

**New Highlander II VPL — what you need to know**
(`Harmar-Product Insights - New VPL Install Preparation Rev 2.pdf`, Aug
10, 2022). Install differs from Harmar's older RPL/CPL platform lifts
and from other VPLs:

- **Get trained:** 45-minute online LiftSquad Academy course (training@harmar.com,
  800-833-0478); Harmar says it saves 1–2 hours and cuts return trips.
- **Power:** ships with a **temporary power cord**. **NEC requires it be
  permanently wired within 60–90 days.** No disconnect is supplied; the
  optional battery backup needs a switch (the sheet says "single throw,
  double pull").
- **Protect the PC board** in the top cap: static-sensitive. Watch wire
  location and color on the gate interlocks, and **wear a static
  strap**.
- **Jumpers:** circuits come pre-jumped per the order form. Don't jump
  anything else; use **Service Mode** instead.
- **Safety nut:** inspect the safety nut and retaining bolts for
  shipping looseness before first run.

> [!warning] Service Mode time disagrees
> The Guardian sheet (2021) says **15 minutes**; the install prep sheet
> (2022) says **30 minutes**. The manuals: **30 minutes, and up only,
> not down** in the Highlander II install manual (no enclosure, Rev G);
> **10 minutes** in the with-enclosure Installation and Service Manual.
> Go by the manual for the unit in front of you. See [[HighlanderII]].

**Highlander II Q4 2022 product changes**
(`Harmar-Product Insights - Highlander II 2023.pdf`, Feb 21, 2023):

- **PC board:** wider trace spacing, sturdier circuitry, better
  conformal coating (see also [[Bulletin - Highlander II PCB Moisture Protection Plate]]).
- **Software:** new battery-warning voltage levels; longer fault
  debounce to avoid false trips.
- **Interlocks:** Harmar's own interlock replaces Honeywell; more
  weather resistant; **not backwards compatible** (details on
  [[Bulletin - New Harmar Interlock Introduction]]).
- Third-party environmental testing passed (heat, humidity, salt fog,
  cold, wind, rain).
- **Non-slip tape replaced by textured non-slip paint** on platforms and
  ramps.

## Pinnacle stairlifts

**Pinnacle Drive System** (`Harmar-Pinnacle-Drive-System.pdf`, Aug 16,
2021):

- **Nylon polymer worm and gear rack**, steel-reinforced. 5–6 points of
  thread contact (vs 2–3 for steel rack and pinion). Same system on the
  300 lb and 600 lb models.
- **No lubrication needed**: no grease on clothes, steps or the track.
- Low friction: quiet, efficient; **40–60 trips on battery** after a
  power outage.
- **Integrated overspeed brake, mechanical, standard**: works without
  power, **deploys above 70 ft/min descent**, a pawl springs open at
  high rpm and catches a housing bolt, **stopping within ½ revolution**
  (within inches). See [[Overspeed-Safety-Brake]].
- Chassis rides on the track's center line, close to the wall; the
  track installs on **either side** of the stairs.
- The sheet says "Pinnacles are commercially rated". The SL300's own
  manual and warranty say **residential only**; the manual wins
  ([[Pinnacle-SL300]]). The SL600 is commercial-rated.

**Reduced intrusion / zero overrun** (`Harmar-Reduced-Intrusion.pdf`,
Aug 16, 2021). A Pinnacle can be set so the track doesn't stick out
onto the upper landing (door swing, walkway, basement door at the top):

1. Gradually reduce the distance from the step front for the standard
   (short) brackets, raising the rail above the step nose.
2. Use **one tall bracket (SL600TB1)** at the upper landing.
3. **Cut the track 6–9 in shorter** to line up with the front edge of
   the top step.
4. Set the limit cam so the **top of the footrest is level with the
   upper landing**. The seat swivel gets the front half of the seat over
   the landing edge.
5. Result: zero intrusion into the landing.

With a door or frame at the top, **load the chassis onto the rail before
fastening the brackets** to the steps. Harmar typical drawings are
available on request.

## Helix

**New remotes for Helix** (`Harmar-Helix-Remote-Product-Insight.pdf`,
Nov 4, 2021). New-look remote, shipped from **October 2021**:

- Same functions, **25% more range**, better encoding (less stray-signal
  trouble). RF, no repeaters needed, through walls and floors, indoors
  and out.
- Works on older Helix lifts, but **the chassis circuit board and the
  remotes must be replaced together**.
- Same housing as the Pinnacle remotes, but **not interchangeable** with
  them.
- Helix team: 800-833-0478, helix@harmar.com.

## Threshold ramps

**Choosing the Right Threshold Ramp** (`Harmar-Choosing the Right
Threshold Ramp.pdf`, June 28, 2022). Consumer-level guide:

- Questions: indoor or outdoor; **rise** (walking surface to top of the
  threshold); width (door opening, or the device); room on the walkway
  for the ramp's length; **total weight** (device + user + anyone
  pushing).
- **Rubber:** rises **1–4 in in ½ in steps**, up to **850 lb**, 36 in
  usable width, slip-resistant, conforms to uneven ground, can be
  trimmed, no drilling. These match Harmar's RAMPRBKIT 1R–4R FSS items.
- **Aluminum:** up to **6 in rise**, up to **600 lb**, light, portable,
  rust-resistant, fixed or adjustable.
- The sheet cites ADA's **12 in of run per 1 in of rise**, the same as
  the PMS 1:12 house standard ([[Ramp-Slope-and-Length]]).
