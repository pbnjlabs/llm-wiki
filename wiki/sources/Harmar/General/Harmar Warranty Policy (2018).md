# Harmar Warranty Policy (2018)

Harmar's general **dealer** warranty policy, page 6 of a Harmar
document, **updated 22 Feb 2018**. It's filed as page 2 of
`bruno_warranty.pdf`, after the [[Bruno Warranty Summary]]. It says it
supersedes earlier policies and is retroactive. It covers process only;
each product's term is on its own certificate (see [[Harmar-Warranty]]).

## Terms

- **Registration:** a Warranty Registration Form must be filed with
  Harmar **within 10 days of installation** (installation date, serial
  number and ID, customer and dealer/installer info). It ships with
  every product, or register online at harmar.com ("Register Product").
- **Start date:** the installation date on the registration form. **No
  form filed: the warranty starts on the ship date from Harmar.** Stock
  sitting in dealer inventory starts **180 days after shipment** if
  stored properly.
- **Claims:** go through an authorized Harmar dealer. The dealer files
  with Harmar Technical Support by phone (**1-800-833-0478**) or email
  (techservices@harmar.com).
- **Reimbursement:** Harmar issues an **RMA** and ships the replacement
  part, billing the dealer for the part and outbound freight. When the
  returned part is confirmed defective, the dealer is credited for part
  and freight. If it doesn't qualify, Harmar may charge the RMA freight.
  If Harmar doesn't need the part back, it invoices and issues an
  offsetting credit.
- **Labor:** labor and travel **aren't part of the standard warranty**.
  Harmar may pay reasonable labor if it was **authorized in writing in
  advance** by Harmar Technical Services, at **$65/hr mobility** and
  **$75/hr accessibility**. Labor claims must be in writing **within 45
  days** of the work. These are Harmar's rates, not PMS rates.
- **Accuracy:** dealers must file accurate registration forms and use
  Harmar-approved aftermarket parts.
