# Pinnacle Power Swivel and Power Footrest Install Supplement

P/N 630-00166-01 Rev C, 14OCT25. A supplement to the SL300/SL600 install
manuals covering the Power Swivel and Power Footrest options, sold as
SKUs SL300-PS, SL300-PF, SL300-PSPF, SL600-PS, SL600-PF, SL600-PSPF.
**Confirms both options exist for both SL300 and SL600** — resolves the
open question raised when [[Pinnacle-SL600]] was first ingested (its one
owner's manual never mentioned these options).

## Covers
- Full left-hand/right-hand power-swivel motor/gear/limit-switch
  pre-assembly, then mounting onto the chassis and seat
- Power footrest: **SL600 uses its native adjustable footrest structure
  for both models** — SL300 gets the SL600-style footrest when the power
  option is fitted, not its own standard manual footrest
- Left/right control relocation: **SL600 ships pre-wired to both
  armrests** (just move the switch); **SL300 requires re-routing wiring**
  between armrests — a real hardware difference between the two chassis,
  not just a cosmetic one
- Power swivel: ~5s full-load swivel time, self-resetting overload fuse
  (2-min cooldown), same stuck-off-home-position recovery procedure as
  described in the [[Pinnacle-SL300]] owner's manual
- Power footrest: ~3s deploy/retract, self-resetting fuse, **must never
  be moved manually** (will damage the mechanism)
- Annual maintenance addition: inspect power-swivel shoulder bolt torque
