# Bulletin: Repairing a Clicking Worm Gear (Installation Damage)

P/N 630-00088 Rev A, effective 18Mar16. Affected products: **all
Pinnacle models, Sierra IL500** — the first mention of a "Sierra" model
line in this wiki; not otherwise documented.

## Covers
Worm gear damage from dropping the chassis during installation (always
lower the chassis gently by hand until the worm gear touches the rack).
Diagnosis: power off, 7mm socket on the manual override, visually inspect
the worm gear. Repair: if the damaged area is ≤0.25"×0.25"×0.25" and
matches the reference photos, trim it with a razor knife and chamfer the
cut edges; anything larger requires replacement, not repair.
