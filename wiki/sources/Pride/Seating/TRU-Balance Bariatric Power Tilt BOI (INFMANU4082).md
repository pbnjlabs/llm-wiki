# TRU-Balance Bariatric Power Tilt BOI (INFMANU4082)

20-page end-user manual for the **TRU-Balance Bariatric Power Tilt**
(Rev D, Jan 2017). Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

- Weight capacity: the chair's or **600 lb**, whichever is less.
- **Full speed while tilted up to 20°**; a full drive inhibit at a
  factory-set tilt angle beyond that.
- Armrest angle dial: 0° at the white dot, −30° to +30° in 5° steps.
