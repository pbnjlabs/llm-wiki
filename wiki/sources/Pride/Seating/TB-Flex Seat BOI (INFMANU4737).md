# TB-Flex Seat BOI (INFMANU4737)

20-page end-user manual for the **TB-Flex** seat (Rev F, Feb 2020).
Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

- Up to **30° tilt**, **10 in lift**, recline **85–120°**; **300 lb**
  or the chair's limit.
- Inhibit matrix: elevated = ¼ speed; tilt or recline past 20° = full
  drive lockout; **tilt/recline past 110° = full lockout**.
- **Manual recline** option 85–120°: past 110° the chair is fully
  inhibited and **won't tilt**. Angle-adjustable back 85–135°.
- Seat height 5 in range in 1-in steps; armrest 9–14 in; two-post
  armrest 9–13 in.
