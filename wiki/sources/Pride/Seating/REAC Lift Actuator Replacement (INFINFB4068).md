# REAC Lift Actuator Replacement (INFINFB4068)

14-page instruction, Rev C, March 2019, for kits KIT1900773, 1900783,
1901189 and 1901190: replacing the **REAC lift actuator** on a TRU-Balance
lift seat.

- **Lift Lockout Kit first.** Raise the seat fully, power off, fit the
  lockout block in the **rear right** slide channel of the lift frame
  with the clevis and cotter pins, then lower the lift **until the
  slide arm rests on the block** and stop. The block holds the seat once
  the front pin is out; never run the lift against it.
- Remove: rear wire cover (enclosed type is snipped off and discarded;
  gloves and glasses), unplug the actuator, free the harness from the
  wire chain and sleeve, lift the inhibit switch off its block (**don't
  move the switch block**), front pin (screw), rear pin (E-clips, tap
  out).
- Install: motor can to the right; rear pin with plastic washers and
  E-clips; run the actuator (through the joystick or an **actuator test
  harness** on the joystick XLR) to line up the front pin; inhibit
  switch back on; route the harness exactly as before.
- **Electronics:** Q-Logic 3 with AM1/AM2/LAM2 → actuator straight to
  the module, then a **firmware update** (programmer and internet).
  Q-Logic 3 with AAM, Q-Logic 2, or NE+ → fit the **REAC lift actuator
  intermediate board** between actuator and module; no firmware update.
- Test: raise fully, confirm it holds, **remove the lockout kit**, cycle
  the lift, check iLevel engages with no warnings, check drive speed
  raised and lowered.
- The seat module must be **mounted on the back of the chair** if
  there's tilt or recline (internal angle sensors).
