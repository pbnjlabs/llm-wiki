# TRU-Balance 4 Parts Replacement Guide (INFMANU5359)

42-page Quantum **Parts Replacement Guide** for the **TRU-Balance 4**
power positioning system, Rev B, November 2022. Illustrated on the
**Q6 Edge 3, Q6 Edge 3 Stretto and 4Front 2**, with and without
iLevel.

Sections: chest bar and receiver, knee block and receiver, back pan,
seat pan, side rails, width and depth brackets, armrests, rear
electronics cover, **AAM** (actuator module) and its port map, bus and
multiplier harnesses, back shroud, seat back, recline actuator, AFP
(articulating foot platform) lowers, calf pads, upper, elevate actuator
and harness, rear shroud and wire guides, side shroud covers, iLevel
roller switch, iLevel seat assembly, **anterior tilt limit switch**,
seat base, seat interface rails. Appendices: width, depth and armrest
adjustment, base electronics, and three **Q-Logic 3 programmer**
procedures.

Key points (details on [[Tru-Balance-4-Power-Positioning]]):
- **20° and 30° anterior tilt need the chest support and the knee
  block.**
- **Legrest length** may be no more than **3.5 in less than the
  seat-to-floor height** (19 in STF → 15.5 in max).
- After an AAM, actuator or seat base swap: **Actuator Calibration**;
  after a seat base or different-angle tilt switch: **Teach Anterior
  Tilt and Transfer Position**; after a legrest length or STF change:
  **Teach Elevate vs AFP Articulation** (AFP near the floor locks out
  driving). All under Seat → Memory Seating Setup.
- Don't retract the recline actuator past min-stroke; the back falls
  back when the top bolt comes out.
- Stretto iLevel seat assembly uses a **spacer** between frame and
  interface rail.

This is a parts guide, **not** the TRU-Balance 4 technical
troubleshooting guide that Q-Logic 3 codes 150–177 refer to; that one
is still missing.
