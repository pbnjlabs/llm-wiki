# TRU-Balance 3 Basic Operation Instructions (INFMANU4800, Rev G 2025)

24-page end-user manual for the **TRU-Balance 3** seat, **Rev G, Mar
2025**. Newer revision of [[Tru-Balance 3 Power Positioning Systems BOI]]
(Rev D, 2019, which stays on file). Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

Confirms the 2019 numbers: **50° tilt** (less on some bases), **12 in
lift**, recline **85–175°**, tilt/recline to 175°, **450 lb** max.
Same inhibit matrix (elevated = ¼ speed and iLevel; tilt or recline
past 20° or combined past 125° = full drive lockout). iLevel boots
limit climbing to 1–1.5 in and allow 20° tilt/recline while engaged.
Armrest and headrest adjustments match the TB4 BOI.
