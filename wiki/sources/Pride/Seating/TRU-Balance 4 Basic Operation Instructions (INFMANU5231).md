# TRU-Balance 4 Basic Operation Instructions (INFMANU5231)

24-page end-user manual for the **TRU-Balance 4** power positioning
system (Rev D, Apr 2025). Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

- **Range:** up to **50° tilt** (less on some bases), **12 in lift**,
  recline **85–165°**, combined tilt/recline to 165°. **300 lb** max
  (or the chair's limit if lower). TB3 is 450 lb and 175°.
- **Anterior tilt** (optional, 10°, 20° or 30°): **knee blocks
  required past 10°**; **knee blocks and chest support** at 20° or
  30°.
- **Inhibit matrix:** elevated = recline/tilt/elevate lockouts and ¼
  speed; tilted or reclined past 20° or combined past 125° = full drive
  lockout; iLevel active when elevated.
- **Anterior tilt speed matrix:**

  | Lift | Anterior 0–10° | 11–20° | 21–30° |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 0–2 in | 2 mph | 0.5 mph | lockout |
  | 2–6 in | 2 mph | 0.5 mph | lockout |
  | 6–12 in | 2 mph | lockout | lockout |

- **iLevel not engaged during power anterior tilt = 0% (full drive
  lockout)** when elevated. Q-Logic 3 indicators 1a–1e (no
  restrictions, safe elevation, power anterior tilt, reduced speed,
  full lockout).
- Memory seating (Q-Logic 3 / 3e): user-adjustable memory positions
  if the provider enables them.
- Armrests: flip-up (8–10, 11–13, 14–16 in posts; flip to 95° to
  adjust), two-post 9–13 in; headrest no more than 7 in above the
  bracket. Cover wash at or below 140 °F, no tumble dry.
