# TRU-Balance 3 Seating Kits: Down Post, Reinforcement Plate, AFP

**INFINFB3542, TB3 back-to-tilt-base reinforcement plate (KIT169366,
Rev A, Sep 2016). This is the recall fix.** PQ-365's Product Recall
page: on TB3 seats built **13 Jun 2013 – 10 Nov 2014**, the joint
between the TB3 back and the tilt base can separate and fail
structurally, made worse by altering the chair's angle inhibits. Pride
issues a reinforcement kit (2 plates and hardware); owners were sent
letters; unsure units → Pride **Upgrade department 866-222-6212**.
Install per side: remove the mounting screws and block from one side
(loosen the other), fit the plate in the **most rearward slot** with
its radius toward the outer edge of the channel (17 N·m), block under
the plate, then set the back position and torque the 4 screws to
**30 N·m (22 ft·lb)**. **The plate costs about 25 mm of back depth
adjustment.** The sheet repeats the TB3 warnings and gives TB3 as up to
50° tilt, **10 in of lift, 95–175° recline**, with **full drive inhibit
at 25° tilt**; the 2019 TB3 BOI says 12 in, 85–175° and lockout past
20°.

**INFINFB4283, down post for TB3 Version 3 flip-back armrests** (Rev C,
June 2021; filename says TB4): down posts 8–10, 11–13 or 14–16 in;
receiver block reversed for 10 in armpads, forward for 14 or 17 in;
post at about **70°** to the armrest top and 2–5 in from it; top and
bottom adjustment rods within one setting of each other; adjustment
screws 8–12 N·m, mounting hardware 12.5–17.5 N·m, hinge screws
10–15 N·m; receiver block locked; run the recline to check clearance.

**INFINFB3077, Articulating Foot Platform** (Rev A, June 2012): remove
any front riggings; set the adjustment brackets to width; fit the
lowers and calf pads; slide the AFP onto the side rails flush with the
rail ends, t-nuts and cap screws (**15 N·m**); plug the AFP mono plug
into the seating harness. TRU-Balance 4 AFP service is on
[[Tru-Balance-4-Power-Positioning]].
