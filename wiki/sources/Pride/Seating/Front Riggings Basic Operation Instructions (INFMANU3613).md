# Front Riggings Basic Operation Instructions (INFMANU3613)

20-page manual for Quantum **front riggings** (Rev N, Jul 2023).
Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

| Rigging | Range | Max user weight |
|---|---|---|
| Swing-away 60–90° leg rests, ELRs | infinite leg angle | — |
| High-pivot power ELRs | 80°–10° | 300 lb |
| Power articulating leg rests | 70° or 80° | 300 lb |
| Center-mount AFP | elevates to 10° | 450 lb (by seat) |
| Single-actuator AFP | elevates to 13° | 300 lb |

AFPs operate at 40–105 °F. Foot platform angle 80–95° by bracket or
lever; tools 4, 5, 6 mm Allen and 8, 13 mm sockets.
