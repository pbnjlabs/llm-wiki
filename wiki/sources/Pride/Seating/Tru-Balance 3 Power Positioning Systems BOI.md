# TRU-Balance 3 Power Positioning System — Basic Operation Instructions (INFMANU4800)

28-page manual for the **TRU-Balance 3 Power Positioning System**, a
tilt/recline/elevate seating system mounted on Quantum power bases,
Rev D/October 2019. Max seating weight 450 lbs (204 kg) or the power
chair's own capacity, whichever is less.

Covers: range of motion (up to 50° tilt, 12 in of lift, 85–175° recline,
tilt/recline combined up to 175°, with a factory-programmed drive-inhibit
angle and a full inhibit matrix by elevated/tilted/reclined state — see
[[Tru-Balance-3-Power-Positioning]] for the reproduced table), the optional
**iLevel** elevating-seat feature (speed-limited to ~25% when not fully
engaged, restricts obstacle climbing to 1–1.5 in while active, indoor-only/
limited-outdoor-hardscape use), flip-forward back, four armrest styles
(flip-up, two-post, heavy-duty drop-in, pediatric quick-height drop-in)
each with their own height/angle/width adjustment procedure, controller
position/extension/height adjustment, seatback height/width adjustment,
TRU-Comfort cushion system (lumbar/lateral/thoracic supports, removable
cover, hand/machine-wash ≤140°F, no bleach/dryer), lateral support
install/adjust, headrest adjustment, and an **Optional Toggle Operation**
table mapping tilt/recline/elevate/leg-rest functions to 4 different toggle
switch styles.

Warranty: 3-year structural frame, 2-year electronics/TRU-Comfort/Sport
Back cushion, 18-month (24-month Canada) actuator.
