# Pride Charger Indicator Codes Quick Reference (INFINFB2586)

1-page Pride quick reference, Rev A, December 2010: the LED meanings
for **ten Pride off-board and onboard chargers** (2 A to 8 A), each
identified by its Pride part number. Older chargers; today's models may
ship with different ones, so match the part number on the charger
label.

Reproduced in full on [[Battery-Charger-LED-Diagnostics]] (Pride
section). The one pattern that isn't obvious: on the **Guest onboard**
chargers (4 A and 5 A), "extended green / no green LED" **after more
than 24 hours** is a **fault**. And the colors don't mean the same thing across chargers:
red is "power on" on the Merits and Power Cube but "fault" or "error"
on others, and "charging" on the 5 A Guest.
