# Pride Technical Troubleshooting Guide — Quantum Rehab Diagnostics (2004)

158-page chapter of Pride's 2004 Technical Troubleshooting Guide for
**Quantum rehab chairs of that era**: primary controllers (Dynamic
Europa G80/G80A/G80T, Dolphin; PG Remote Plus 3-key, VSI 4- and 6-key),
programmers (Dynamic DX HHP, PG PP1), **secondary/specialty controls**
(chin, RJM, finger steering, Switch-It touch pad and head control, Tash
switches, MagiTek, HMC mini joystick, PG Omni Plus, attendant control,
multi-input switch box, ASL scanners), Motion Concepts TRx (Helix
4.1/5.1, M12 interfaces), and **seating** (Versa Tilt and power
tilt/recline actuators through TAM, ALM or toggle).

Useful checks:
- **Tilt actuator motor:** 0.5–15 Ω (3–15 Ω on toggle setups); under
  0.5 Ω suggests a shorted winding.
- **Versa Tilt inhibit:** two limit switches in series give half speed
  and full lockout; the parallel resistor is **10 kΩ (Dynamic)** or
  **22 kΩ (PG)**.
- A flashing status light on a DX module (TAM etc.) = replace that
  module.
