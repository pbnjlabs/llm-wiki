# Quantum End-to-End Chart (INFINFB4546, 2025)

2-page Quantum Rehab side-by-side chart of the whole US Quantum line,
INFINFB4546/Rev G/October 2025. Grouped by drive type:

- **Mid-wheel drive:** Q6 Edge 3 Stretto, Edge 4, Edge 3, Edge 2.0,
  Edge HD, J4E, J4EHD, J4, J6.
- **Front-wheel drive:** 4Front 2, 4Front 2 HD, Q1450.
- **Rear-wheel drive:** R-Trak.
- **Outdoor series:** Outback (4-motor independent drive, 15 in
  pneumatic, Group 27, K0899).

Per model it gives: HCPCS group/codes, **VA FSS contract
(V797D-70001)**, weight capacity, max speed (and speed with iLevel or
elevation), ground clearance, turning radius, length, width, drive and
caster wheels, base and battery weight, motor package (2- or 4-pole),
battery group, **available electronics** (QLNES, Q-Logic 3/3e,
Q-Logic NE/NE+, VR2), seating-system size ranges (TRU-Balance 4,
Synergy, TRU-Balance 3 static/power tilt/lift & tilt, HD versions,
TRU-Balance Flex, Q4, Q Captain) and seat-to-floor heights.

Footnotes worth knowing:
- **6.25 mph only with Q-Logic 3.** **7 mph needs the optional 4-pole
  motors plus Q-Logic 3** (Edge 3); without 4-pole motors the max is
  6.25 mph.
- Edge HD with iLevel: minimum seat 20 × 20 in, maximum 24 × 24 in;
  capacity drops from 450 to **400 lb with iLevel**.
- J6: **250 lb with TRU-Balance** systems.
- Seat-to-floor heights are measured unloaded with an 18 × 18 in seat;
  the 4Front sits about 0.25 in lower loaded. Add 3/8 in for pneumatic
  tires.
- Battery weights may vary ±3 lb by maker.
- HCPCS codes are not billing advice; the provider decides the code.

Where this chart disagrees with a model's own spec sheet, the model
pages say so (e.g. Edge 4 casters, 4Front 2 HD turning radius).
