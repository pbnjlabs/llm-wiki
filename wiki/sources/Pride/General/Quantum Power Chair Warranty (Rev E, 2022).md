# Quantum Power Chair Warranty (Rev E, Oct 2022)

2-page Quantum power chair warranty text from the PQ-365 Warranty
section (labelled "Warranty Policy").

| Term | Covers |
|---|---|
| **Lifetime** | Structural frame: main frame, fixed-position seat post |
| **1 year** | Electronics (charger, controller, joystick), metal seat framing, harnesses, **motor/gearbox**, power seat actuator, accessories, anti-tip forks, front riggings and brackets, caster forks, caster beam |
| **6 months** | Bearings and bushings; **reconditioned units** |
| **Batteries** | 12 months from the battery maker |

Not covered: wear items (shrouds, brake pads, motor brushes, tires and
tubes, upholstery and seating, fuses and bulbs), labor/service
calls/shipping unless authorized **in advance**, unauthorized repairs,
damage from contamination, abuse, battery leakage, commercial use,
improper use or storage. Warranty service through a Quantum provider;
no part returns without written authorization. Implied warranties
limited to 1 year.

Individual Quantum models print their own terms in their owner's
manuals and charts (e.g. the TRU-Balance 3 3-year frame/2-year
electronics on [[Tru-Balance-3-Power-Positioning]]); check the model.
