# Pride and Quantum Warranty and Return Policy (updated 16 Jul 2026)

4-page provider policy from the PQ-365 Warranty section. The newest
Pride document on file; it governs warranty and returns for every
Pride/Quantum product, with each product's term in its owner's manual.

**Warranty**
- **Capital product warranty starts on the date of sale to the
  consumer** if sold within **12 months** of the ship date; otherwise
  it starts one year after ship date. Original purchaser only.
- **Purchased parts: 90 days** (stocked parts start no later than 90
  days from invoice).
- **Rental units:** start at first rental if rented within 2 months of
  ship date, else 2 months after ship; **no claims after 15 months**
  from ship date.
- A replacement doesn't reset the clock (part replaced at 8 months of a
  1-year warranty has 4 months left). The removed part becomes Pride's.
  Replacements may be refurbished.
- **Warranty service is the provider's job; parts are Pride's.**
  Written authorization first. Removing ID labels voids it.
- **Excluded:** batteries (battery maker's warranty), wear items (brake
  pads, motor brushes, upholstery and seating), labor, service calls
  and shipping unless authorized in writing, unauthorized repairs or
  modifications, damage from contamination, abuse, accident, battery
  leakage, commercial use, improper operation/maintenance/storage,
  setup and adjustments, fit or preference.

**Returns** (all need an **RA** from Technical Service 1-877-800-1248)
- Warranty parts: Pride sends a prepaid **call tag** with the
  replacement; box the old part, RA on the outside. Sometimes they'll
  just say to dispose of it.
- Non-warranty returns: request within **60 days of invoice**; at least
  **35% restocking** plus freight. Parts are final after 60 days;
  hardware isn't returnable; parts not in new condition only on a
  warranty claim. **Lithium batteries can't be returned** (hazmat).
- Credit timing: returned within **6 months of RA issue = full credit**,
  after 6 months 50%, after 9 months none; RAs expire after 1 year.
- Use Pride's pre-selected carrier and approved packaging.
- Special orders / custom items (Quantum Custom dept 877-465-2999):
  case by case.

**Receiving shipments:** inspect cartons with the driver present; note
damage on the carrier's paperwork ("Carton damage, subject to
inspection" if you can't open it then); inspect within **48 hours**;
refuse cartons with exposed damaged product ("Carton damaged and
refused"); mark shortages "SHORT" with the count. Refusing an order
without approval = freight plus 35% restock.
