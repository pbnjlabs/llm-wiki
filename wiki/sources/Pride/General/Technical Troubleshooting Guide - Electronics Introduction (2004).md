# Pride Technical Troubleshooting Guide — Electronics Introduction (2004)

10 pages (pp. 6–14) from Pride's general **Technical Troubleshooting
Guide**, Rev B, January 2004: the basic-electricity chapter for new
technicians.

Covers voltage, current and resistance; Ohm's law (24 V across 60 Ω =
0.4 A); AC vs DC; the parts of a circuit; scooter and power chair block
diagrams (input device → controller → motors); the brushed DC motor
(armature, commutator, brushes, permanent magnets; reverse polarity to
reverse direction); and multimeter use: set the range above the
expected value, black lead on the most negative point for DC volts,
**measure resistance only with the circuit unpowered** and the part
isolated from other circuits.

Battery points repeat the [[Technical Troubleshooting Guide - Battery Guide (2004)|Battery Guide]]:
sealed lead-acid only, test by full charge then a 10 s load test.
