# Pride Education Sheets: Battery Load Test and Using a Multimeter

Two undated sheets from the Education Instructions section of the PQ-365
library.

**Battery Load Test** (1 page). When: the chair or scooter is slower
than usual or the motors quit early, no power, or the battery gauge
blinks one red bar or slow red bars. A voltmeter can show **12–14 V**
on a battery that still can't carry a load; the **controller needs at
least 18 V** under load. Shroud off, battery cables off, tester + to +
and − to −, hold the load **10 seconds**, read under load. Good
batteries → keep troubleshooting connections and harnesses.

**Using a Volt Ohmmeter** (5 pages). Analog vs digital meters; AC test
at the outlet (range above the expected value; keep probes apart); DC
battery test (disconnect the battery, prefer a load tester: a
"partial open" can read fine on a meter and drop under load); resistance
(power off, discharge capacitors, zero the meter). A long harness can
read up to about 1 Ω (18 AWG: up to 0.6 Ω per 100 ft); 0 Ω between two
different wires of one harness = a short.
