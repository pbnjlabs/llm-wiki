# Pride Technical Troubleshooting Guide — Battery Guide (2004)

29 pages (pp. 288–315) from Pride's general **Technical Troubleshooting
Guide**, Rev B, January 2004 (prideservice.com). Three parts: a consumer
guide to choosing batteries, a technician guide to checking them, and
"It All Starts With the Batteries!", a technical reference with quizzes.
Lead-acid only; it predates Pride's lithium products.

Technician content:

- **Check the batteries first** on every service call and every
  maintenance visit. Bad batteries mimic many other faults.
- **Open-circuit voltage vs state of charge** (rested 3 h or after a
  10 s load, 77 °F):

| State of charge | Per 12 V battery | Pair (24 V) |
|---|---|---|
| 100% | 12.8 V | 25.6 V |
| 75% | 12.5 V | 25.0 V |
| 50% | 12.2 V | 24.4 V |
| 25% | 12.0 V | 24.0 V |
| 0% | 11.8 V | 23.6 V |

  Charge before 25% (12.0 V); **never below 10.5 V per battery.**
- **Load test:** a standard automotive (CCA) load tester, **at least
  50 A for 10 seconds**, on fully charged batteries. In the field, take
  an OCV reading first; a pass is a pass, a fail on undercharged
  batteries means recharge and retest before condemning them. A deep
  cycle capacity tester is more accurate but rare.
- **Charger check (most Pride products):** charger in the wall, unplug
  the **3-pin DC harness**; the **two outside pins** on the charger side
  should read **25–30 VDC**; the other side should read battery voltage.
  For a pre-2002 Victory, Rally or Shuttle: watch battery voltage rise
  (even 0.1 V) when the charger is plugged in.
- **Pride chargers:** bulk (up to 80–90%), absorption (14.2–15 V per
  battery, current tapers), float (12.8–13.2 V, under 0.5 A). They
  handle gel and AGM.
- Most batteries that fail in the first year fail from **poor charging
  habits**. Dirt across the terminals self-discharges a battery and
  traps heat; life halves for every **15 °F over 77 °F**.
- **Storage:** charge fully, disconnect, check monthly, recharge below
  80% (sulfation), never below 32 °F; self-discharge 1–15% a month.
- Sealed (gel or AGM) only in Pride products; gel and AGM are air-travel
  approved when labeled.

Its FAQ says replacing both batteries in a series pair "may not be
necessary but is recommended". **PMS house standard overrides this:
always replace every battery** ([[Mobility-Batteries]]).
