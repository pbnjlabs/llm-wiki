# Power Chair Consumer Safety Guide (INFMANU3992)

32-page consumer safety guide shipped with every Pride/Quantum power
chair (Rev L, May 2026). The owner's manuals point to it for the full
warnings. Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

Worth teaching at delivery ([[Joint-Commission-DMEPOS]]):
- **Inclines:** straight up, keep moving; down at the slowest speed,
  forward only, never backward; no freewheel on any slope; don't use
  tilt, recline, iLevel or elevating leg rests on a slope. Stay within
  the chair's rated slope. Releasing the joystick on a hill can **roll
  back about 1 ft** before the brakes hold.
- Pride's table says the ADA "requires 5° (8.7%)". **PMS builds ramps
  at 1:12 (4.8° or less) regardless** (house standard, [[Ramps Handbook]]).
- **Curbs:** square on; don't attempt over **2 in** (4 in with a curb
  climber); never back down.
- **Motor vehicles:** the chair is a seat only with a **WTORS** tie-down
  and occupant restraint; the positioning belt is not a seat belt.
- **Positioning belt** anchors so the belt sits **45–75°** to the
  horizontal across the pelvis.
- Batteries: same make, chemistry, Ah and age. Smoke or smell from the
  battery box: move it to a ventilated area.
- EMI/RFI: tested to 20 V/m; radios and phones can cause unintended
  movement.
