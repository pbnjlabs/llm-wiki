# Pride Provider Forms: Field Service Request and Quote/Order Checklists

- **Request for Field Service** (FDA/1300-2 Rev B, 1 page): Pride's
  form for sending a Pride field technician (FSTJOBS@pridemobility.com,
  570-655-5574). Asks for a labor contract yes/no, account details, a
  **bill-back authorization with signature and PO#** (required for
  anything billed to the account), serial number, model, purchase date,
  original order number, customer contact and the service requested.
  Leave customer details out of the wiki.
- **Parts and Product Quote & Order Checklists** (2 pages): what to
  have ready when calling Pride. Parts quote: account #, contact, email,
  phone/fax, part(s) with part numbers if known, **unit serial
  number**, ship-to. Parts order adds quote #, PO#, ship method.
  Product quote/order: model #, accessories, PO#, shipping instructions.
  US 800-800-8586, Canada 888-570-1113.
