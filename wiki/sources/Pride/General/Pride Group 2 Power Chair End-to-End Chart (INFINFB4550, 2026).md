# Pride Group 2 Power Chair End-to-End Chart (INFINFB4550, 2026)

1-page Pride Mobility chart of its **Group 2 (FDA Class II)** power
chairs, INFINFB4550/Rev D/January 2026: Jazzy Air Med, Go Chair Med,
Jazzy EVO 613 series (incl. EVO 613 Li), EVO 614, EVO 614HD, Jazzy
Elite ES, Jazzy Select, Jazzy Elite 14, Jazzy 600 ES, Jazzy Elite HD and
Jazzy 1450.

Per model: model number, capacity, speed, ground clearance, turning
radius, length, width, wheels, suspension, range, heaviest piece,
drivetrain, brakes, **batteries and battery weight, charger amps**, seat
weight and sizes, seat-to-ground height, Synergy seating, **standard
electronics** (PG VR2, PG GC3, Dynamic LiNX), specialty controls,
**HCPCS codes** and **warranty**.

Also prints the **battery HCPCS codes**: U-1 E2365, NF-22 E2361,
Group 24 E2363, lithium E2397, 18 Ah K0733.

Warranty (most models): 5-year limited frame; 13-month limited
electronics, drive motors and batteries. EVO 613: 3-year limited on its
lithium battery. Jazzy 1450 K0826/K0827: lifetime limited frame; K0839:
lifetime frame, 2-year electronics, 18-month motors.
