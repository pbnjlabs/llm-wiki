# Scooter Consumer Safety Guide (INFMANU3989)

28-page consumer safety guide for Pride scooters (Rev K, Jan 2025).
Same structure as the [[Power Chair Consumer Safety Guide (INFMANU3992)]].
Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

Scooter-specific: lean forward going up an incline; releasing the
throttle on a hill can roll back **about 1 ft** (about **3 ft** in
reverse); stopping distances on slopes are much longer than on level
ground; curbs over 2 in not recommended; the positioning belt is not a
restraint in a vehicle. Same ADA 5° table (PMS: 1:12, [[Ramps Handbook]]).
Lithium battery fire: dry chemical, foam, CO2 or water fog.
