# Pride EMI/RFI Information

2-page undated consumer FAQ (written for scooters; the same applies to
power chairs): radio-frequency interference from cell phones, two-way
radios, CB, TV/radio stations and similar can cause **unintended
motion or brake release**, or damage the controls.

- FDA asks for at least **20 V/m** immunity; Pride's product passed
  20 V/m. Adding accessories or modifying the unit can change that.
- At 20 V/m: stay **3 ft from a hand-held** two-way radio and **10 ft
  from a mobile** one; don't use hand-helds with the unit powered on.
- Key out when getting on or off; never leave the key in an unattended
  scooter.
- Unintended motion: switch off (pull the key) as soon as it's safe,
  and **report it to Pride at 1-800-800-8586** (the FDA wants all
  incidents reported, with any nearby radio source noted).

For PMS, an unintended-motion report is also a Joint Commission
incident ([[Joint-Commission-DMEPOS]]). Feeds into: [[Power-Chair-Troubleshooting]].
