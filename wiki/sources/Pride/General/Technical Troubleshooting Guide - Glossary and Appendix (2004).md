# Pride Technical Troubleshooting Guide — Glossary and Appendix (2004)

The back matter of Pride's 2004 Technical Troubleshooting Guide.

- **Glossary** (12 pages): terms from Active-Trac to the controller
  module names (ACU attendant control, ALM actuator/lighting module, ARC
  actuator remote control, CLAM, TAM…). Handy for decoding older
  manuals.
- **Appendix A–V** (39 pages): fault code and solution tables for
  **PG Drives**, **Dynamic**, **DL-50**, GS80 joystick, DA80 dual
  attendant, Omni Plus, PM80 power module, **VSI/Pilot/Remote Plus**,
  digital **Curtis**, **Egis** and **Rhino** scooter controllers, Sport
  scooter beep codes; PP1a keypad and Dynamic HHP use; Dynamic and Remote
  Plus power module pinouts (M1/M2); IPC and Legend/Celebrity console
  setup; **power chair and scooter fuse locations by model and serial
  range**.
