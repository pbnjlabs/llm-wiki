# Lithium-Ion Battery Product Insert (INFINFB4079)

21-page insert (Rev C, Dec 2023; English, French, Spanish) that
**replaces the Batteries and Charging section** of the owner's manual
on Pride lithium products. The library also had Rev B (Dec 2022), not
kept. Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

- **Go-Go Endurance Li: first charge, or after 25 days of storage, the
  battery is in shutdown mode and must be charged OFF the scooter.**
- Charge order: product off and in drive → XLR lithium charger into
  the product → charger into the wall → **wait 2 min**: two red LEDs =
  charging; green = full → battery disconnect switch on (if fitted).
  Wrong order: unplug both ends, wait 1 min, start again.
- Only the supplied charger. Charger inhibit: the product won't run
  while charging on board.
- Won't charge: XLR into the product first, then the wall; check the
  **battery fuse**; check both cord ends.
- Battery meter: green full, yellow draining, red recharge now; read it
  at full speed on level ground. Scooter meters dim automatically.
