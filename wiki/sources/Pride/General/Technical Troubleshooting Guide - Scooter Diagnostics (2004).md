# Pride Technical Troubleshooting Guide — Scooter Diagnostics (2004)

40-page chapter of Pride's 2004 Technical Troubleshooting Guide for
**older scooters**: the 70 A **IPC** controller (pre-2000), **analog
Curtis**, 24 V/70 A **digital Curtis** (Legend, Celebrity, Sundancer;
fault flashes on the console status LED or beeps) and PG **Egis**.
Component-by-component walk from batteries through breaker, charger,
interface harness, key switch, throttle pot, controller and
motor/brake.

Values worth keeping for any older Pride scooter:
- Breaker under 1 Ω; open or over 3 Ω = replace. Charger 25–30 V DC.
- **Throttle pot** about **2.5 kΩ** at center (IPC: changes about
  300 Ω with throttle; Curtis: ±2.5 kΩ, 5 kΩ end to end).
- **Motor** 0.5–3 Ω. **Brake** 30–100 Ω by age: older about 45 Ω,
  newer 30, 65 or 100 Ω.
- Brake microswitch: closed in drive.
- IPC controller setup (center the throttle, set forward/reverse
  speeds) is in the guide.
