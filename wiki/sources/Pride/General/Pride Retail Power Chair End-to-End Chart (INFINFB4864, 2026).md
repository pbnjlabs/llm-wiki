# Pride Retail Power Chair End-to-End Chart (INFINFB4864, 2026)

1-page Pride Mobility chart of its **retail** (K0899, not coded by DME
PDAC) power chairs, INFINFB4864/Rev A/January 2026: Jazzy Air 2, Go
Chair, Jazzy Ultra Light, Jazzy Carbon, Carbon 27, Carbon 27X and Carbon
HD.

Same columns as the Group 2 chart. Notable: the Ultra Light and the
Carbon family run on **lithium** (24–25.2 V, 10–20 Ah) with 2–3.5 A
chargers; the Carbons have **35 A brushless** electronics and **don't
disassemble** (heaviest piece = the whole chair, 27.8–39.6 lb). Jazzy
Air 2 uses a 40 A PG VR2, the Go Chair a 40 A PG GC3, the Ultra Light a
40 A **PG nVR2**.

Warranty varies: Jazzy Carbon lifetime limited frame plus 1 year on
electrics, motor/drive, bearing sleeve and battery; Carbon 27 and Carbon
HD 5-year frame, 1-year electronics, motors and batteries; the others
5-year frame, 13-month electronics, motors and batteries.
