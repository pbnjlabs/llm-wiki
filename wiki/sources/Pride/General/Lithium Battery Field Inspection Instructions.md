# Lithium Battery Field Inspection Instructions

1-page Pride "Technical Instructions" sheet for checking a Pride
**lithium** battery in the field. Undated.

1. Fully charge with the proper charger, then **measure the voltage**.
2. Below the minimum in the table → battery is defective, replace it.
3. **Near 0 V may be "sleep mode"**, not a dead battery, on the Go-Go
   Endurance, Jazzy EVO 613, Pursuit 2 and Bandit: wake it per the
   device's own instructions first.

**Do not load test lithium batteries.**

| Product | Battery part | Minimum |
|---|---|---|
| Jazzy Passport Travel | BAT1802153 | 22 VDC |
| Jazzy Passport Standard | BAT1708123 | 22 VDC |
| iRIDE | MEC1901028 | 30 VDC |
| i-Go folding scooter (Samsung) | MEC2000135 | 22 VDC |
| i-Go folding scooter (Panasonic) | MEC2200931 | 22 VDC |
| Go-Go Endurance | BAT2005749 | 22 VDC (sleep mode) |
| Go-Go Endurance MAXX | BAT2005745 | 22 VDC (sleep mode) |
| Jazzy EVO 613 | BAT2004389 | 22 VDC (sleep mode) |
| Pursuit 2 | BAT2204837 | 22 VDC (sleep mode) |
| Bandit 20 Ah | BAT2204152 | 30 VDC (sleep mode) |
| Bandit 40 Ah | BAT2204150 | 30 VDC (sleep mode) |
| Jazzy Carbon | BAT2200009 | 19.6 VDC |
