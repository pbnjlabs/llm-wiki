# Quantum R-Trak Specifications (INFINFB4461)

2-page Quantum spec sheet for the **R-Trak** rear-wheel-drive chair,
US/UK/AU/EU, INFINFB4461/Rev B/August 2023.

Headline: Class B (Group 3), 300 lb, up to **7 mph** (3.5 mph with the
10 in lift), range 16.34 mi on Group 24 (C20 75 Ah / C5 62 Ah), 3.97 in
ground clearance at the battery tray, 8 A off-board charger, max slope
7.5°, climbs 2.36 in. Full figures on the model page.
