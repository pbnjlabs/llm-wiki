# R-Trak Parts Replacement Guide (INFMANU5383)

38-page Quantum PRG, Rev A, July 2023. The first service document for
the [[R-Trak]] (rear-wheel drive).

Sections: chair prep (power off, drive mode, **unplug the bus**, jack),
seating and towers, front and rear doors, drive wheel (14 × 3 or
14 × 4 in), tire and tube, standard and lighted fenders and caster
caps, caster arm shroud, **axle brace**, front caster arm, caster
wheel, suspension assemblies, motor and motor mounts, anti-tip, door
and top shrouds, circuit breaker, power harness, batteries and
harnesses, power module, bus cable, PTO backup camera, and Q-Logic 3
and NE+ wiring appendices.

| Fastener | Torque |
|---|---|
| Seating / towers | 32–38 N·m; some nuts 15–20; 30–35 N·m |
| Lug nuts | 22–27 N·m |
| Split-rim screws | 4–5 N·m, Loctite 242 |
| Axle brace to frame | 18–22 N·m |
| Caster arm pin cover screw | 2–3 N·m |
| Caster wheel nut | 20–25 N·m |
| Suspension pin cover screws | 3–4 N·m |
| Outside motor mount: nuts / screws | 10–14 / 18–22 N·m |
| Anti-tip wheel nut | 8–12 N·m |
| Breaker bracket screws / breaker to bracket | 10–14 / 4–6 N·m |
| Breaker lead nuts | 11.3 N·m ±10% |

Motor: suspension pin out and the suspension held up; remove the outside
motor mount (4 nuts, 2 screws); slide the motor off the inside mount.
Don't adjust spring tension. New nylon nuts or patched screws.
