# J4E and J4E HD Owners Manual (INFMANU5454)

28-page owner's manual for the **J4E and J4E HD** ([[J4]]), Rev B,
Nov 2024. Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

Standard Quantum owner's manual layout: safety, the chair's parts and
freewheel levers, assembly, comfort adjustments, batteries and charging,
care and maintenance (daily/weekly/monthly/yearly checks, cleaning and
disinfection, wheel change, battery replacement). The shared Pride
content is distilled once on [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]]. **No
specification table or warranty section**: specs are on the separate
spec sheet in the owner's package, warranty on the warranty card
([[Pride-Warranty]]). Seating systems (TB-Flex, Synergy, TRU-Balance
3/4, Q4) and the controller have their own manuals.

J4E specifics:
- Optional **power elevating seat** run from the controller (see the
  [[Q-Logic 3e Basic Operation Instructions (INFMANU5154)]]); never
  raise it in freewheel. Q4 seat has its own manual.
- **Articulating foot platform: elevates to 13°**; single actuator
  **300 lb** max user weight, dual actuator **450 lb**; operating
  temperature **40–105 °F**.
- Infrequent use: charge at least once a week for 24 h.

Feeds: [[J4]], [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]].
