# J4 Specifications

2-page Quantum spec sheet for the **J4**, Rev A/May 2019 (no INFINFB
number printed).

Headline: 300 lb, up to 5 mph, range up to 17.6 mi, two NF-22 batteries
(**AGM or Gel only**; this older sheet doesn't list Hybrid Gel), 8 A
off-board charger, ATX suspension, 14 in flat-free drive wheels, max
slope 6°, climbs 2 in. Full figures on the model page.
