# J4E Specifications (INFINFB4645)

2-page Quantum spec sheet for the **J4E** (J4 with power elevate), US,
INFINFB4645. The filename says Rev B/May 2024; the sheet itself prints
**Rev B/June 2024**.

Headline: Group 2, 300 lb, up to 5 mph, **1.25 mph with elevate**, range
up to 16.1 mi, two NF-22 batteries, 8 A off-board charger, total mass
under 377.2 lb with TRU-Balance 3, max slope 6°, climbs 2 in. Full
figures on the model page.
