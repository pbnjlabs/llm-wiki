# Q6 Edge 3 Stretto Specifications (infinfb4124)

2-page spec sheet for the **Q6 Edge 3 Stretto**, US/UK/AU markets,
INFINFB4124/Rev E/May 2025.

- Class of use: B (Group 3); weight capacity 300 lbs (136 kg)
- Max speed: up to 6.25 mph (10 km/h); 4 mph UK-only; 3.5 mph while iLevel
  is engaged
- Range: up to 15.9 mi (40Ah batteries) / 20.3 mi (55Ah batteries)
- Turning radius: 21 in (533 mm); ground clearance 2.9 in (74 mm)
- Length 36 in (without front riggings); width 21.5 in (14-in drive wheel)
  or 20.75 in (12.5-in drive wheel)
- Drivetrain: two motor, Mid-Wheel 6 drive; "Intelligent Braking" electronic
  regenerative + disc park brake; suspension: SRS (Smooth Ride Suspension)
- Drive wheels: 14 in or 12.5 in, pneumatic or flat-free
- Batteries: two 12V sealed lead-acid deep-cycle, 40Ah or 55Ah (AGM,
  Gel-Cell, or Hybrid Gel); 8-amp off-board charger
- Max rated slope 7.5° (13.2%); max obstacle climb 3.5 in (90 mm); sound
  level <65 dB

Notably faster and longer-range than the [[J6]] (6.25 mph/20.3 mi vs. 4.7
mph/10 mi), with a larger 8-amp charger vs. J6's 5-amp — consistent with
the Q6 Edge 3 Stretto being a newer, higher-spec platform.
