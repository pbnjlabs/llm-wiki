# Q6 Edge 3 Stretto Parts Replacement Guide (INFMANU5103)

27-page Quantum **Parts Replacement Guide** for the **Q6 Edge 3
Stretto**, Rev B, 15 Feb 2023.

Sections: chair prep (NE/NE+ or Q-Logic 3 joystick off, drive mode,
unplug joystick, jack up), seating systems (TB3 static/recline, TB3.5
tilt, TB3 REAC lift and tilt, TRU-Balance 4, TB Flex static and
pediatric tilt), seat towers, front and rear door covers, drive wheels
(5- and 3-spoke), tires and tube, caster wheel and fork, front and rear
caster arms, suspension and damper shock, shrouds and fenders (incl.
lighted fender), motors and mounts, **battery stops (55 Ah vs 40 Ah)**,
batteries, power module (NE/NE+ and Q-Logic 3 port maps), power
harness and circuit breaker, iLevel base for TB3 and for TB4.

Torque highlights (full table on [[Pride-Parts-Replacement]]):
lug nuts 22–27 N·m; rim screws 4–5 N·m with Loctite 242; caster fork
nut 25–30 N·m (new nut); caster axle nut 12–16 N·m; caster arm 15–20;
suspension pivot pins and damper ball studs 10–15; motor and motor
mount 15–20; seat interface 32–38 (TB Flex 15–20); towers 22–28.

Gotchas:
- **Motor mount holes depend on the hub:** 5-spoke hubs use the top
  holes, 3-spoke the bottom.
- **55 Ah battery stops** sit in slotted holes for side-to-side
  adjustment; **40 Ah** batteries need their stop removed to get the
  batteries out.
- iLevel caster-arm stop: **sloped edges of the backing plate face up.**
- Battery wiring diagram (LAB1908221) is inside the front door cover.
