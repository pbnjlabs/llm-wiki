# Q6 Edge Basic Troubleshooting Instructions (INFINFB2594)

2-page basic troubleshooting supplement for the **Q6 Edge family**
(covers chairs equipped with **Q-Logic, NE+, or NE electronics** — not
Q6-Edge-3-Stretto-specific), Rev B/July 2011.

Symptom-based checklist, not a diagnostic-code table:

- **"Dead" system** (no joystick light/display): check battery
  install/wiring, terminal tightness, quick-disconnect harness, main
  circuit breaker reset button, joystick-to-power-module connection.
- **Reduced range**: battery age (>1 yr degrades), charge frequency/duration
  (8–14 hrs, at least weekly even if unused), wall-switch-controlled
  outlets, prohibits extension cords for charging, battery cleanliness,
  terminal/breaker connection tightness, worn motor brushes.
- **Slower operation**: speed/profile setting, low battery, thermal
  protection (chair self-limits/stops when internal temp is too high — let
  it cool).
- **Pulling to one side**: joystick not centered, uneven tire pressure
  (includes psi→bar/kPa conversion formulas), caster wheels not pivoting
  freely.
- **Clicking noise**: normal brake-release click on joystick input;
  otherwise check drive-wheel lug nuts.
- **Fault indicated**: Q-Logic-electronics chairs show numeric error codes
  (see [[Joystick-Controllers]]); NE+/NE-electronics chairs show icons
  instead (not covered in this wiki — no NE+/NE manuals on file).

Explicitly a supplement to the owner's manual, not a replacement — see
[[Q6-Edge-3-Stretto]] for the model page and its operator manual source.
