# Jazzy Ultra Light Owners Manual (INFMANU5449)

28-page owner's manual for the [[Jazzy-Ultra-Light]] (Rev B, Dec 2024).
Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

- One **lithium-ion** battery in a removable box; the **battery power
  switch is also the main circuit breaker**, on the back of the box.
- Charge through the controller socket or the battery-box port. Pride
  charger: amber LED while charging, green when done; done in 24 h or
  call the provider.
- Freewheel lever, anti-tip wheel; otherwise the standard Pride
  safety/care content ([[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]]).
