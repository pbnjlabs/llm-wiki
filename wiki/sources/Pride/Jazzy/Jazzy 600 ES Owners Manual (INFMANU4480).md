# Jazzy 600 ES Owners Manual (INFMANU4480)

36-page owner's manual for the [[Jazzy-600-ES]] (Rev D, May 2020).
Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

Standard Pride owner's manual layout (safety, your power chair,
freewheel, assembly, comfort adjustments, batteries and charging, care
and maintenance). Shared content is on [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]]. No
spec table or warranty in the manual: specs come on a separate insert
([[Pride-Power-Chair-Lineup]] has the 2026 chart values).

Specifics:
- Freewheel: **push both levers down** for freewheel.
- Seat on trapeze bars and front/rear extrusions; Synergy and
  TRU-Balance seats have their own manuals; transit option has a
  supplement.
- Adjustable seatback: **90°, 102°, 105° or 107°**.

Feeds: [[Jazzy-600-ES]], [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]].
