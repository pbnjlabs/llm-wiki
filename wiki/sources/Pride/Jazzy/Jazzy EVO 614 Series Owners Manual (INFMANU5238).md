# Jazzy EVO 614 Series Owners Manual (INFMANU5238)

32-page owner's manual for the **Jazzy EVO 614 series** (614 and 614HD),
Rev B, Sep 2026. Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

Standard Pride owner's manual layout (safety, your power chair,
freewheel, assembly, comfort adjustments, batteries and charging, care
and maintenance). Shared content is on [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]]. No
spec table or warranty in the manual: specs come on a separate insert
([[Pride-Power-Chair-Lineup]] has the 2026 chart values).

Specifics:
- **Two freewheel levers on the motors: pull up** for freewheel. Stand
  to one side on level ground when switching.
- Manual recline lever; TRU-Balance seats have their own manuals.
- **Charge reminder:** driven 8 h or more, switched off and not charged
  → the reminder shows at the next power-off.
- Raise the base at least 1 in for wheel service.
