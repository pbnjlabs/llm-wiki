# Jazzy Elite ES Owners Manual (INFMANU4355)

36-page owner's manual for the **Jazzy Elite ES** (Elite Gen 2), Rev
D, Jan 2017 ([[Jazzy-Elite]]). Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

Standard Pride owner's manual layout (safety, your power chair,
freewheel, assembly, comfort adjustments, batteries and charging, care
and maintenance). Shared content is on [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]]. No
spec table or warranty in the manual: specs come on a separate insert
([[Pride-Power-Chair-Lineup]] has the 2026 chart values).

Specifics: freewheel = **pull both levers rearward toward the seat
post**; ELRs give 12–19 in footrest range; charge 8–14 h; charger LED
not done in 24 h = call the provider.

Feeds: [[Jazzy-Elite]], [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]].
