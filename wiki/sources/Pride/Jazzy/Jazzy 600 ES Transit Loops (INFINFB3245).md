# Jazzy 600 ES Unoccupied Transit Securement Loops (INFINFB3245)

1-page instruction, Rev A, June 2013. Four forged mounting brackets
with tie-down loops (front L/R, rear L/R) replace the trapeze-bar
screws: discard the old screws and washers; **front: M10 × 30 mm**,
**rear: M10 × 55 mm** (grade 12.9, nylon patch) through the loop, frame
and trapeze L-bracket, **35 N·m**. Fit the tie-down hook decals and the
"**not rated for occupied transport**" decals. The loops are for
securing the chair **unoccupied** only.
