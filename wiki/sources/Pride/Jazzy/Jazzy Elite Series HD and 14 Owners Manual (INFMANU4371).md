# Jazzy Elite Series HD and 14 Owners Manual (INFMANU4371)

32-page owner's manual for the **Jazzy Elite HD and Elite 14** (Rev
D, May 2022) ([[Jazzy-Elite]]). Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

Standard Pride owner's manual layout (safety, your power chair,
freewheel, assembly, comfort adjustments, batteries and charging, care
and maintenance). Shared content is on [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]]. No
spec table or warranty in the manual: specs come on a separate insert
([[Pride-Power-Chair-Lineup]] has the 2026 chart values).

Specifics:
- Freewheel: **rotate both levers outward**, away from the seat.
  **The chair gives no warning or code if only one lever is in
  drive**; it can still drive, unpredictably. Check both by eye. Flip
  the foot plates down to reach them.
- **HD seat depth by user weight:** 270–400 lb any depth 18–24 in;
  401–450 lb **not on an 18 in deep seat** (capacity drops to 400 lb
  on 18 in).
- Heavy-duty drop-in and elevating leg rests (12–19 in).
