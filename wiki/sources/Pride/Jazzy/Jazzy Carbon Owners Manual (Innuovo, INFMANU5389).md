# Jazzy Carbon Owners Manual (Innuovo, INFMANU5389)

14-page (scanned, two pages per sheet) owner's manual for the folding
[[Jazzy-Carbon]], written by the maker **Zhejiang Innuovo
Rehabilitation Devices** (China). The file name says Rev A Oct 2022
(5389); the inside page says INFMANUI-GOUK Rev A, July 2020. Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

- Specs: 940 × 610 × 960 mm open, **720 × 310 × 610 mm folded**; seat
  height 500 mm, width 380 mm; **18 kg without battery**, battery
  1.8 kg; **6 km/h**; ≥15 km range; climbs ≥6° (<10°); **2 × 24 V
  250 W** motors; **24 V 12 Ah lithium**; **35 A** controller; charger
  **2 A** (AC 100–240 V, 24 V); solid tires, 178 mm front, 216 mm
  rear; **300 lb (136 kg)**.
- Unfold: pull the footrest forward, pull seat and back apart until
  the **latch clicks onto the locking pin**; check the latch before
  sitting. Fold: pull up the rear latch, lift the seat cushion.
- **Brake lever on each motor**: position A = drive (electric), B =
  push (manual). Never manual on a slope.
- Anti-tippers: extend to the outer notch for slopes.
- Joystick: power, battery (all bars OK, 3–4 OK, under 2 charge),
  5-step speed (4–6 km/h), horn. **Auto power-off after 20 min**.
- Charging: battery under the seat, charges on or off the chair (3-pin
  socket). Red light = charging, green = nearly full, then 1–2 h more;
  6–8 h total.
- Inspection table (each use: brake, battery, cleanliness; weekly:
  driving, wiring, devices; monthly: each part, tires; semi-annual:
  motor). Have the provider check controller settings every **6–12
  months**.
- Beep codes 2+1 to 2+9 (see [[Jazzy Carbon Diagnostic Code Sheets]]).
- Warranty: **lifetime frame; 1 year** on controller, motor/drive,
  bearings and **battery**; not the shell, tires or cushion.
