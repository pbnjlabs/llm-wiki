# Jazzy 614 Series Anti-Spin Kit (INFINFB2308)

3-page instruction, Rev A, Feb 2009, for **KITASMB1672**: front and
rear spring mounting brackets with an extension spring per side, added
to the Jazzy 614 series suspension (the kit name says anti-spin; the
sheet doesn't say what symptom it fixes).

Per side: power off, drive mode, block the base up slightly; remove the
shock's upper and lower bolts (keep them; **discard the lower bushing**,
keep the upper); remove the two bottom motor-mount screws; hook the
spring to the rear bracket and fit it under the upper motor-mount screw
(finger tight); hook the spring to the front bracket; refit the shock
bottom through the front bracket into the caster arm and the top to the
suspension arm; tighten the motor-mount screws (6 mm hex). A 6 mm
T-handle is recommended.
