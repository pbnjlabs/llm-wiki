# Jazzy EVO Series Owners Manual (INFMANU5239)

40-page owner's manual for the **Jazzy EVO 613 and 613 Li** (Rev A,
Apr 2021) ([[Jazzy-EVO-613]]). Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

Standard Pride owner's manual layout (safety, your power chair,
freewheel, assembly, comfort adjustments, batteries and charging, care
and maintenance). Shared content is on [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]]. No
spec table or warranty in the manual: specs come on a separate insert
([[Pride-Power-Chair-Lineup]] has the 2026 chart values).

Specifics:
- Two battery sections: **sealed lead acid** (two 12 V deep-cycle; the
  charger won't run **below 18 V**) and **lithium** (one sealed battery
  box; don't open it or replace cells inside).
- Lithium temperatures: operate −4 to 122 °F (best 59–77 °F); store 23
  to 77 °F; **charge 32 to 104 °F** (best 68 °F). Short/interval
  charging and partial discharge suit lithium. Not every lithium
  battery is rated for air travel.
- Charge reminder after 8 h driving without a charge (same as EVO 614).
- Rear caster wheels; ELRs 12–19 in.
