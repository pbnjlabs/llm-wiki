# Jazzy Motor Replacement Instructions (Jazzy 614/614HD, Jazzy Elite ES)

Two short bilingual sheets (2019).

**INFINFB4069, Jazzy 614 and 614HD** (Rev B, Feb 2019): power off,
drive mode; batteries and seat out; chair on blocks; front shroud off;
take the **O-ring off both freewheel levers**; unplug the motor at the
power module; wheel off; remove the bolt holding the motor mounting
bracket to the frame and the bolt at the top of the suspension strut;
remove the 2 bolts holding the spring mounting plate (swing the caster
arm up to relieve spring tension); pull bracket and motor off together
without hitting the freewheel lever; swap the bracket to the new motor;
reverse. **Reinstall the freewheel O-rings.** Metric tools; an impact
tool helps with seized bolts. New nylon nuts.

**INFINFB4089, Jazzy Elite ES series** (Rev A, Mar 2019): seat, top
shroud and batteries out, on blocks, front shroud off, unplug the motor;
wheel off; unbolt the motor; **move the freewheel lever to the new
motor**; new hardware; reverse.
