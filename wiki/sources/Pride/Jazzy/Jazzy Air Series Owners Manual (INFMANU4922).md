# Jazzy Air Series Owners Manual (INFMANU4922)

36-page owner's manual for the **Jazzy Air** series, including the
**Jazzy Air 2** ([[Jazzy-Air]]), Rev D, Feb 2024. Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

Standard Pride owner's manual layout (safety, your power chair,
freewheel, assembly, comfort adjustments, batteries and charging, care
and maintenance). Shared content is on [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]]. No
spec table or warranty in the manual: specs come on a separate insert
([[Pride-Power-Chair-Lineup]] has the 2026 chart values).

Specifics:
- **Power elevating seat** by **REAC** (reac-group.com): actuator duty
  cycle **6 min on / 54 min off**. Speed is reduced while elevated (see
  the spec insert).
- Under-seat storage on the Air 2 only.
- Charge 4 h more after the charger says full; infrequent use, weekly
  for 24 h. Keep between **−13 °F and 122 °F**.
- Lift the drive wheel at least 1 in off the ground for service.

Feeds: [[Jazzy-Air]], [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]].
