# Jazzy Fuse Locations

2-page table for **legacy Jazzy and Jet chairs** (about 2000–2002):
model, electronics (Remote, Remote Plus, Dynamic, 9-pin connector),
**fuse location** and manufacture-date/serial ranges, with the IPB
generation and page for each.

Covers Jazzy 1100, 1101, 1103, 1104, 1105, 1113, 1113 ATS, 1115, 1120,
1120-2000, 1121, 1122, 1143, 1170, 1400, 1420, 1470; Jet 1, 1 HD, 2, 3,
7, 10, 12; Pride LX, LX 10; Blast 650/850/HD; Dynamo, Dynamo ATS.
Typical locations: **under the utility tray**, on the side of the
utility tray (1101/1121), on the sliding tray (1120-2000, 1122), under
the swivel cover on the tray (1400/1420/1470), on the battery box
(1105, 1115, Jet 12), on the off-board charger (Pride LX). No IPB on
the website for the 1101 and 1121.

Only for older chairs that still turn up; not used for current models.
