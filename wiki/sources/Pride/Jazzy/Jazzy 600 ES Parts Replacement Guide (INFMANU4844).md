# Jazzy 600 ES Parts Replacement Guide (INFMANU4844)

18-page **Parts Replacement Guide** for the **Jazzy 600 ES**, Rev A,
16 Jan 2017 (Quantum-branded, published the same day as the
[[Q6 Edge Parts Replacement Guide (INFMANU4845)]]). The chair is built
on the same base layout as the original Q6 Edge: front battery door,
rear electronics door, center panel, top shroud, articulating beam,
motors with a hub, axle key and freewheel linkage. Procedures and
torque values match the Q6 Edge guide step for step.

Sections: chair prep, seating (Contour seat, TB3 seat frame, Synergy
seat), front battery door and rear electronics door, center panel and
top shroud, **run-flat drive wheel**, front/rear caster, articulating
beam, drive motor, foot platform, batteries, joystick, power module,
battery harness and circuit breaker.

| Fastener | Torque |
|---|---|
| Drive wheel lug nuts | 25 N·m (18.4 ft·lb) |
| Hub nut on motor shaft | 51 N·m (37.6 ft·lb) |
| Motor to motor bracket | 7 N·m (5 ft·lb) |
| Caster nut | 27–34 N·m (20–25 ft·lb) |
| Articulating beam nut | 40 N·m (30 ft·lb) |
| Foot platform bracket | 25 N·m (18.4 ft·lb) |
| Seat (Contour, TB3, Synergy) | 27.1 N·m (20 ft·lb) and 35.3 N·m (26 ft·lb) |

**Do not disassemble the run-flat wheel** (replace the wheel assembly).
Re-seat the axle key; new nylon-insert nuts; replace both batteries
together.
