# Go Chair Owners Manual (INFMANU4750)

36-page owner's manual for the [[Go-Chair]] travel chair (Rev E, Nov
2022). The library files it under an EU path, but the manual is
US/AU/EU/UK. Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

Standard Pride owner's manual layout (safety, your power chair,
freewheel, assembly, comfort adjustments, batteries and charging, care
and maintenance). Shared content is on [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]]. No
spec table or warranty in the manual: specs come on a separate insert
([[Pride-Power-Chair-Lineup]] has the 2026 chart values).

Go Chair specifics:
- Comes apart for transport; freewheel lever on each motor, **pull
  both toward the rear** for freewheel. Don't charge in freewheel.
- **Main circuit breaker on the front of the battery box**; the battery
  box has its own charger port, so it charges on or off the chair.
- Seat height: two positions 1 in apart.
- Infrequent use: charge once a week for **12–14 h**. Keep between
  18 °F and 122 °F.

Feeds: [[Go-Chair]], [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]].
