# Jazzy and Quantum 1450 Specifications (INFINFB3028)

2-page spec sheet covering both the **Jazzy 1450** (2VHD, 2SPVHD) and the
**Quantum 1450** (3SVHD, 3SPVHD), US, INFINFB3028/Rev H/April 2023.

Headline: Class B (Group 3), **600 lb**, up to 4.9 mph (4 mph UK), range
up to 16.5 mi, two Group 24 70 Ah batteries (52 lb each), base 195 lb,
8 A off-board charger, swing-arm suspension, front-wheel drive. The two
brands differ only in **max slope (Jazzy 6°, Quantum 7.5°)** and
**climb (Jazzy 2 in, Quantum 2.25 in)**. Full figures on the model page.
