# Pride Lift Chair Measuring, Out-of-Box Setup and Leg Levelers

**Measuring a lift chair** (INFINFB2333 Rev B, Mar 2013): adjustable
T-square and tape, inches, **don't compress the foam**. A seat center
to floor; B seat depth along the center; C seat width **4 in from the
seatback**; D top of seatback to seat (T-square against the lumbar);
E overall width across the armrests, 4 in from the back; F top of arm
to seat, 4 in from the back.

**Knock-down (KD) out-of-box setup** (INFINFB1670, Nov 2004): chairs
ship in the KD position. Check the **locking clip is vertical**; slide
the back's rear bracket sleeves onto the male KD brackets (one side at
a time on wide chairs, keep it level); push down until **each side
clicks**; **pull up to confirm it's locked**; fasten the back fabric
to the hook-and-loop flaps. Heat and massage chairs: plug the quick
releases together first (heat is labelled, massage isn't; models 450
and C30 have two heat pads). Removal: unhook the fabric, unplug heat
and massage, lift each clip with a large flat screwdriver while lifting
that side.

**Leg levelers** (KIT2107022 / KIT2107023, INFINFB4494 Rev A, Aug
2022): 4 or 8 levelers; pre-installed on all models except the
**PLR3955LT**. If the chair rocks, adjust: tighten to lower, loosen to
raise.
