# Pride Lift Chair Motor, Transformer and Battery Backup

**Single motor/transformer** (DRVASMB1546, INFINFB1766 Rev B, Dec
2006): kit = 24 V DC motor DRVMOTR1171, **external 115 V low-voltage
transformer** CTLDC1301, 7 ft Y cable, clevis and cotter pins. Chair
**seated**, turned over; pull the cotter and clevis pins; new motor;
Y cable clamped just before it splits, routed to the side pocket;
transformer → Y cable → motor and hand control; **run one full cycle
to check no wire is pulled tight or pinched**.

**Motor replacement** (INFINFB2149 Rev A, Oct 2008; motors DRVMOTR1171
standard, DRVMOTR1139 Megamat Plus, DRVMOTR1308 Okin Delta Drive):
chair **fully upright**, unplugged; KD chairs: back off first; upside
down; unplug the motor; keep the cotter and clevis pins; bottom clevis
first; reconnect; **put the chair in full recline to fit the top
clevis** to the lift/recline bracket (DRVMOTR1308: to the T-bar/side
bracket); infinite-position chairs: one motor top to the **kidney
bracket**, the other to the lift/recline bracket; test fully.

**Lithium-ion battery pack** (INFINFB4282 Rev A, July 2020): a backup
pack that **clips onto the external transformer** (grooves toward the
transformer, slide until it clicks). Remove: unplug the transformer,
tap the pack end to free it, slide and lift. **Take the pack off if the
chair won't be used for a long time**: the chair draws on it even
idle. Covered 1 year (lift chair warranty).

**Radiance (PLR3955) and Atlas Plus phone charger tray:** flip the
chair forward (black denim on the right), disconnect the tray's wire
(the only one of its kind; the other is hard-wired to the control
box), remove the 2 tray screws (#2 Phillips) and fish the wire out;
for arm access flip the chair on its back and remove 2 bolts (10 mm).
The Atlas Plus has no hole to feed the wire through.
