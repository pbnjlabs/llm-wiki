# VivaLift Product Inserts (Premier PLR5900, Heat, Tilt)

Three VivaLift! option inserts. Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

- **VivaLift! Premier PLR5900 (INFINFB4715, Rev B, Feb 2025):**
  armrest control with power, heat and **air massage**; heat/massage
  **turns off after 20 min** with a beep; **tilt** (tilt rearward /
  forward buttons; forward returns to upright).
- **VivaLift! with Heat (INFINFB4294, Rev A, Mar 2021):** heat button
  cycles LOW, HIGH, OFF; wireless charger warnings.
- **VivaLift! with Tilt (INFINFB4474, Rev B, Dec 2023):** tilt
  feature, armrest heat/massage control (20 min auto-off); hold tilt
  forward + tilt rearward until all LEDs flash to enter set mode.
