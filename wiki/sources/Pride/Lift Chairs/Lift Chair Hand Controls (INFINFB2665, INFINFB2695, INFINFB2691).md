# Pride Lift Chair Hand Controls (INFINFB2665, INFINFB2695, INFINFB2691)

**Hand control quick reference** (INFINFB2665 Rev K, June 2021;
INFINFB2695 Rev A, April 2011 is the older two-language version):
- Buttons: up (to seated or standing), down (to seated or reclined; on
  **infinite-position** models it only extends the footrest, the back
  stays up), recline, incline, footrest up/down, sit, stand, **USB port**
  (iPhone: MFi-certified cables only), up/down toggle with **inhibit
  switch** (left = lift and footrest, right = seatback recline), heat
  and massage switches (auto-off 20 min), heat button (1× low green,
  2× high red, 3× off), massage mode and intensity, **memory buttons**
  (hold 1 and 2 for 3 s to enter set mode, then hold the button to
  store), **lock/unlock** (hold about **5 s to lock**, button lit; hold
  about **10 s to unlock**, all LEDs flash). Trendelenburg and
  "zero gravity" positions explained.
- **Power LED** lights only while a button is pressed; present but dark
  in use → provider.
- **Flash codes, Table 1:** buttons blink **every second** = fault in
  the hand control or the USB device/cable (unplug the USB device:
  stops → device; still blinks → replace the hand control). Buttons
  blink **every 5–7 seconds** = the chair **lost power, low-power
  mode**, USB charging off → check the outlet. (Memory-button controls
  don't show these.)
- **Table 2 (5-LED controls):** LED 3 = **actuator fault** (replace
  actuator); 3 + 4 = **zone 1A/1B**; 3 + 5 = **zone 2A/2B**; 3 + 1 =
  **back heat**; 3 + 2 = **base heat** (replace that part).

**Two-button hand control replacement** (KITASMB1858 Limoss, INFINFB2691
Rev B, July 2011): **the motor control box must be removed along with
the hand control.** Raise the chair fully (borrow a working hand
control if needed), unplug, tip forward; cut the ties, unplug at the
extension's quick disconnect, pull the old control out the front, keep
the cable mount and screw; discard the old extension; new extension to
the actuator cable, through the **cleat on the left of the actuator**,
over the lift frame crossbar, cable mount; thread the new hand control
through the cushion area from the front; connect, take up slack, tie.
