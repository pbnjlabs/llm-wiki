# Pride Lift Chair Frame and Mechanism Replacement (12 sheets)

Pride's technical instructions (2007–2017) for the lift mechanism.
Common to all: chair **seated** (or as stated), **unplugged**, KD
chairs have the **back removed first**, then **turned upside down on a
table or other stable support**; keep cotter and clevis pins; test
after.

| Sheet | Part | Key points |
|---|---|---|
| INFINFB2074 | Single motor lift frame FRMASMB2118 | Frame bolts to the arm rails with a machine bolt (T-nut) and a wood screw at each corner; side brackets 4 wood screws per side |
| INFINFB2067 | Dual motor heavyweight frame FRMASMB7143 | Motors rear first |
| INFINFB2073 | Infinite motor (expansion) frame FRMASMB2591 | Scissor **T-linkage** out of the T-bracket; rear motor stroke tube off the **kidney bracket**; back at about **90°** when reconnecting the rear motor |
| INFINFB2076 | Infinite motor scissor pair FRMASMB1377 | Change one side at a time; footrest has upper and lower holes on the scissor front |
| INFINFB3036 | 3-position (FBS/SS) scissors | Seat base off **one side at a time**; scissors pushed fully forward in the slots |
| INFINFB2075 | T-bracket (single FRMASMB2122, dual FRMASMB1671, infinite FRMASMB1673) | Pull the footrest out for access; dual motor chairs have two sets of lift/recline brackets |
| INFINFB2077 | Lift/recline brackets BRK0102D014 (pair) | Same position as the originals; lift the frame or push the footrest slightly to line up pins |
| INFINFB2072 | Side bracket BRK0102D020 | #25 Torx; run the motor up and down to align; bracket edge even with the seat rail |
| INFINFB2071 | Heavyweight pivot FRMASMB7321 (545/555) | Ribbon switch adhesive lined up with the frame weldment; motors **out of time** → TechnoBulletin LC-029 (not on file) |
| INFINFB2138 | KD sleeves and clips (Super Sagless frame) KITASMB1545 | Sleeve screws **6.8 N·m (5 ft·lb)**; metal tabs fully into the lower KD bracket |
| INFINFB3738 | KD clip securement mech kit | Picture-only, multilingual (2017) |
| INFINFB2068 | Buttons | Tufting needle INDPART2000, clip and twine CLISTL1014; knot about **1.5 in**; through the stuffing, not the outside back; specify fabric color |

**Fitting any lift frame:** fit the adhesive **frame spacers** (stop
mechanical noise); front of the frame about **1 in from the front
corners**; run the motor to lift the back of the frame about 2 in, then
lower until it touches the arm wood; keep **3/16–1/4 in between the
lift/recline bracket and the frame** so the footrest closes; keep the
T-bracket **level**; footrest won't close → move the footrest slightly
up or down.
