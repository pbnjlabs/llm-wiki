# Lift Chair Product Inserts (Transformer, Wireless Charger, Heat)

Three short lift chair inserts. Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

- **External transformer wall mount (INFINFB2689, Rev D, Jan 2020):**
  hold it on the wall, mark or drill the holes; transformers vary by
  supplier, so trace yours as a template.
- **Smartphone wireless charger (INFINFB4095, Rev C, Dec 2022):**
  Qi phones only, face up, flat (case may need to come off); no
  watches. **Take the phone off before raising the chair**; don't
  close the retractable arm on anything.
- **Chair with heat, cup holder and wireless charging (INFINFB4400,
  Rev A, Dec 2022):** hand control with USB, heat (LOW/HIGH/OFF),
  power headrest and lumbar, back recline/incline, leg rest, and memory
  (hold M1 + M2 until the LEDs flash, then hold the button to save).
  UP returns every power option to neutral while lifting. Empty the cup
  holders before raising.
