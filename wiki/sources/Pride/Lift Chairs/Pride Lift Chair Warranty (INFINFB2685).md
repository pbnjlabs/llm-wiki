# Pride Power Lift Recliner Warranty (INFINFB2685)

6-page product insert, Rev O, May 2021 (English and Canadian French;
the library lists it under Canada).

- **Lifetime:** lift and recline mechanisms (metal fatigue, welds,
  bushings) and chair frame, for the original owner. Not on models
  **102, 105, 106, 107, 250, 358XXL**. Pride may replace only the
  defective part. **Over the weight capacity voids the warranty.** Use
  only the numeric part of the model number (ignore LC, NM, SR prefixes
  and S, M, L, PW suffixes).
- **7-year pro-rated (Heritage, Infinity, Oasis; Elegance and 108
  Canada only):** 4 years full on motor, hand control,
  transformer/control boxes, heat pad and massage (not VivaLIFT!),
  recline mechanism and frame (358XXL); then **25% off** list in year 5,
  **15%** year 6, **5%** year 7.
- **2 years (model 250):** motor, frame, transformer/control boxes,
  hand control, recline mechanisms.
- **1 year (105; 102, 106, 107 Canada):** same parts.
- **RF wireless pendants:** 90 days against breakage, not loss.
- **Lithium-ion backup pack:** 12 months.
- **Not covered:** fabric; labor, service calls and shipping (the
  owner pays; provider sets the visit cost); circumstances beyond
  Pride's control; unauthorized repairs; contamination, abuse, battery
  leakage.
