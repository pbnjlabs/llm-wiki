# Lift Chair Series Owners Manual (INFMANU3446)

24-page owner's manual for **Pride power lift chairs** (Rev Y, Jun
2025; the back half repeats Rev X, May 2025). Fills the lift chair
owner's manual gap. Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

- **Setup:** KD back: locking clip vertical, slide on, **click on each
  side**, pull up to check; plug in hand control and heat/massage
  harnesses (heat and massage to the **junction box**, never straight
  to the motor; single-motor chairs can take the hand control at the
  motor). Back of the chair **30 in from the wall** when seated; level
  with the 4 leg levelers.
- **Power:** low-voltage DC (24/39 V) from the external transformer.
  **No extension cords**; a UL single-outlet plug-in surge protector
  (1000 J minimum) is allowed. Transformer on the floor in the open or
  wall-mounted, **nameplate facing down**, never under the chair or
  covered.
- **Battery backup** (returns a reclined chair upright **once** in an
  outage): **two 9 V alkaline** in the transformer, replaced after
  every use **or every 6 months**; remove them if the chair is unplugged
  over an hour. Or a **lithium-ion pack** clipped on the transformer:
  charge to green first; runs the chair **up to 48 h** unplugged;
  remove it for long storage.
- Duty cycle **2 min on / 18 min off**; operate at 50–104 °F; indoor
  only.
- **Dual-motor timing reset:** no movement either way, one motor
  extended farther and the pressure safety switch pinched = out of
  sync. **Hold UP to the full lift position** to reset. Use full lift
  when getting in and out to keep them in time.
- **Stops mid-lift:** thermal shutoff in the transformer; wait **10
  min**. Or a power failure with no backup.
- One direction only: sticking hand control button.
- Heat/massage dead: junction box to transformer, green LED on the
  transformer, rear connections, hand control LEDs.
- Rocking: adjust leg levelers. Serial number on the steel lift frame
  near the motor and on the wood frame under the scissors.
- Heat pad 106–118 °F; **20 min at a time**; never while sleeping or
  with heat rubs; not with nerve damage or rheumatoid arthritis.
- Fabric: Crypton cleaning kit, or mild soap and water, blot dry.
