# Pride Lift Chair Basic Troubleshooting Instructions (INFINFB2061)

2-page Pride sheet for **lift chairs, excluding models with Okin
motors**, Rev A, May 2008. The first Pride lift chair document on file.
A supplement to the lift chair technical troubleshooting guide,
installation instructions and owner's manual (none of which are on
file).

**Before troubleshooting:**
1. Transformer in a live, **non-switched** outlet; test the outlet.
2. All connections plugged in and locked, per the out-of-box setup.
3. Cables free, not pinched by the chair; power cable from control box
   to transformer not crimped, cut or cracked (replace cable and/or
   transformer).
4. Reconnect everything, power included, while testing.
5. Fit a new motor bottom/base first, with the chair fully reclined if
   possible.
6. **Dip switch** in the standard control box set correctly, or the
   chair won't follow the hand control.

Symptom → likely causes (from the sheet's table):

| Symptom | Check |
|---|---|
| Stopped working | Transformer, motor, hand control, motor relay box, control box |
| Clicks and doesn't work | Motor, control box, relay box, hand control |
| Squeaks | Scissors, loose wood frame, bolts too tight/loose, lift frame rubbing wood frame |
| Pops | Scissors, wood frame, T-bracket catching |
| Drops / drops suddenly and quits | Bent or broken scissors or lift frame, T-bracket catching, cracked lift/recline bracket, broken side bracket pin, **broken motor clevis or stroke tube** |
| Footrest won't open / close fully | Side brackets, frame adjustment, motor or stroke tube, loose bolts, scissors; footrest mounted too high/low; fabric catching |
| One side touches floor first / sits crooked | Twisted wood frame, bent scissor, loose bolts, bent lift frame, uneven floor, leveling leg, worn seat foam, **dual motors out of timing** |
| Won't recline or lift all the way | **Dual motors out of timing**, bent scissors, broken lift frame or side brackets |
| Functions crossed (infinite-position motors move together, heat/massage runs a motor) | **Control box dip switch** |
| Wrinkles, bunching, popping springs or buttons | Foam, fabric, chaise pad position; spring clips not crimped; button string or clip; getting in and out without the hand control |
