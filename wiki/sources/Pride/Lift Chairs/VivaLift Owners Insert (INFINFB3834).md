# VivaLift Owners Insert (INFINFB3834)

12-page **VivaLift!** power recliner insert (Rev J, Dec 2022): hand
control guide, fabric care, assembly, warranty and weight capacity.
The library lists it as the "VivaLift! Owner's Manual". Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

- Hand controls A–D: USB, power headrest and lumbar, UP (all to
  neutral and lift), DOWN, back recline (to horizontal) and incline,
  leg rest; control C adds memory and heat.
- **Assembly:** clip vertical, slide the back on until **both sides
  click**; plug in lumbar/headrest; wings by the A-A/B-B labels; chair
  upside down: plug the three small cables at the junction box first,
  route along the frame (not through the mechanism), clip them; control
  box cables **A to A, B to B**; leg levelers (PLR3955LT ships without
  them). Lithium-ion backup on the transformer, charged to green.
- **Warranty:** lifetime lift/recline mechanism and frame; **4 years**
  hand control, transformer/control box, motor, power headrest and
  lumbar, then **25/15/5%** off retail in years 5–7; **lithium pack 1
  year**; no fabric, labor or shipping.
- **Weight capacity 400 lb**; exceeding it voids the warranty.
