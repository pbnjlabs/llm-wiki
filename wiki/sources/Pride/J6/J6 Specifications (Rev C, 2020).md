# J6 Specifications (Rev C, 2020)

2-page Quantum spec sheet for the **J6**, Rev C/March 2020 (no INFINFB
number printed). **Newer than** the 2015 [[J6 Specifications]]
(INFINFB3447 Rev A) and [[J6 VA Specifications]] already on file.

What changed against the 2015 sheets:
- **Range: up to 13.4 mi** (2015 standard sheet: 15 mi; VA sheet: 10 mi).
- **Class of use A**; weight capacity **300 lb, but 250 lb with
  TRU-Balance 3**.
- Ground clearance now given at three points: 2 in at foot plate, 2 in
  at caster arm, 2.5 in at frame.
- Casters: front 5 in solid, rear 6 in solid.
- **Component weights:** base 100.2 lb (without seat and batteries), seat
  55.4 lb, batteries 23.4 lb each.
- Batteries: two 12 V **U-1**, AGM, Gel-Cell **or Hybrid Gel** (nano
  silica gel with glass-mat separators).
- Unchanged: up to 4 mph, 22 in turning radius, 40.87/34.74 in length,
  23.19 in width, 10 in drive wheels (pneumatic or solid), ATX
  suspension, 5 A off-board charger, 6° max slope, 2.4 in climb.
