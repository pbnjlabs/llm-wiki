# J6 Operator Manual (INFMANU3964)

28-page operator manual for the **J6** power wheelchair, Rev G/August 2018.

Covers:
- **Assembly**: UMS (Universal Mounting System) seat attachment, power seat
  option, transit tie-down securement points (front/rear anchor points,
  never on adjustable/removable parts like armrests or wheels).
- **Comfort Adjustments**: seat height (4 trapeze-bar positions), seat
  position, manual recline, seatback angle, armrest width/angle, controller
  position, foot platform height/depth/angle; optional power elevating seat
  (speed-reduced while elevated); positioning belt install/adjust — not a
  vehicle seat belt.
- **Batteries and Charging**: two 12V deep-cycle sealed (AGM/Gel-Cell)
  batteries, 5-amp off-board charger, battery break-in procedure, FAQs on
  range/storage/shipping/public-transportation (FAA-compliant, no
  spill/leak risk).
- **Care and Maintenance**: daily/weekly/monthly/yearly check schedule,
  general cleaning guidance (lighter-weight than the newer
  [[Q6 Edge 3 Stretto Operator Manual|Q6 Edge 3 Stretto OM]]'s disinfectant
  list — this is an older manual revision), wheel replacement (pneumatic
  tube vs. solid-tire-insert assembly, 5-lug or 3-lug hub variants), battery
  replacement procedure with wiring diagram, corrective-maintenance
  checklist, storage/disposal guidance.

No Q-Logic-specific programming content — hand control/controller operation
is covered by the separate controller manuals (see
[[Joystick-Controllers]]).
