# J6 Seat Tower Installation (INFINFB3185)

4-page instruction, Rev A, February 2013 (kit MEC131564): swap the
J6's seat towers between **short, medium and tall**.

**Transit rule:** a J6 built with the **short** towers meets ANSI/RESNA
WC Vol. 4 §19 / ISO 7176-19 for **occupied** transit. Fitting medium
or tall towers (or doing this modification) makes it **unoccupied
transit only**; replace decal DWR1235L169 with **DEC8120018** ("not
rated for occupied transport").

Steps: power off, drive mode, joystick unplugged, seat back flipped
forward; remove the seat-interface-plate screws (keep, discard the
nylock nuts) and the transit-plate screws; unclip the safety snap pins
and swap the towers; transit plate on with the new screws, **X pattern,
30 N·m (21.7 ft·lb)**; seat and interface plate on with new nylock
nuts, X pattern, **30 N·m**.
