# J6 VA Specifications (infinfb3097)

2-page spec sheet for the **J6 VA** (VA-contract variant of the J6),
INFINFB3097/Rev C/October 2015.

- Weight capacity: 300 lbs (136 kg)
- Max speed: up to 4.7 mph (7.6 km/h)
- Range: up to 10 miles (16 km)
- Turning radius: 22 in (55.9 cm); ground clearance 1.99 in at footplate
- Length: 40.87 in with front riggings / 34.74 in without; width 23.19 in
- Drivetrain: two motor, mid-wheel 6-drive; "Intelligent Braking" electronic
  regenerative + disc park brake
- Drive wheels: 10 in, pneumatic or solid; suspension: Active-Trac Extreme
  (ATX)
- Batteries: two 12V U-1 (AGM or Gel-Cell), 5-amp off-board charger
- Max incline 6° (10.5%), max obstacle climb 2.4 in

**Quirk vs. the standard-contract J6** (see [[J6 Specifications]]): same
base hardware and component weights, but the VA variant is governed
*faster* (4.7 vs 4 mph) and *shorter-range* (10 vs 15 mi) than the standard
US J6 — a performance/governing difference between contract variants, not
a hardware difference.
