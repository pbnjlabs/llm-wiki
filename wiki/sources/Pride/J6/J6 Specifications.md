# J6 Specifications (infinfb3447)

2-page spec sheet for the standard-contract **J6**, INFINFB3447/Rev
A/October 2015.

- Weight capacity: 300 lbs (136 kg)
- Max speed: up to 4 mph (6.44 km/h)
- Range: up to 15 miles (24.14 km)
- Turning radius: 22 in (55.9 cm); ground clearance 1.99 in at footplate
- Length: 40.87 in with front riggings / 34.74 in without; width 23.19 in
- Drivetrain: two motor, mid-wheel 6-drive; "Intelligent Braking" electronic
  regenerative + disc park brake
- Drive wheels: 10 in, pneumatic or solid; suspension: Active-Trac Extreme
  (ATX)
- Batteries: two 12V U-1 (AGM or Gel-Cell), 5-amp off-board charger
- Max incline 6° (10.5%), max obstacle climb 2.4 in

Identical base hardware/component weights to the [[J6 VA Specifications|J6 VA]] variant, but governed for longer range at lower top speed — see that
page for the comparison.
