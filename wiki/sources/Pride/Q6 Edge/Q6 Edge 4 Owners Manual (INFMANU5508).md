# Q6 Edge 4 Owners Manual (INFMANU5508)

28-page owner's manual for the [[Q6-Edge-4]] (Rev B, Feb 2026; the
library also had Rev A, Jun 2025, not kept). Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

Standard Quantum owner's manual layout: safety, the chair's parts and
freewheel levers, assembly, comfort adjustments, batteries and charging,
care and maintenance (daily/weekly/monthly/yearly checks, cleaning and
disinfection, wheel change, battery replacement). The shared Pride
content is distilled once on [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]]. **No
specification table or warranty section**: specs are on the separate
spec sheet in the owner's package, warranty on the warranty card
([[Pride-Warranty]]). Seating systems (TB-Flex, Synergy, TRU-Balance
3/4, Q4) and the controller have their own manuals.

Edge 4 specifics:
- **Two battery builds**: **22NF** (front and rear battery behind the
  front cover and rear door, quick-release connectors) or **Group 24**
  on a **slide-out tray** behind the rear door. Group 24 swap: rear
  shroud off (two yellow latches), unplug the **left motor harness** so
  the door swings and the **main controller power harness** so the tray
  comes out, pull the tray to its locks, press the **yellow button** to
  open the breaker on the rear battery, front battery in first, then
  check the decal on the rear negative harness for the **ceramic fuse**
  stack-up and torque, and re-engage the breaker (yellow lever to the
  left).
- iLevel: "iLevel not engaged" = **25% speed** when elevated
  ([[iLevel]]).
- Drive wheel: five lug nuts; deflate fully before splitting the rim.
- Q-Logic 3 error **#75** "chair is not charging" when the charger is in
  the controller but not the wall.
