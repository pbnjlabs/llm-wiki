# Q6 Edge 4 Specifications (INFINFB4695)

2-page Quantum spec sheet for the **Q6 Edge 4**, US/UK/AU,
INFINFB4695/Rev B/July 2025. The Downloads folder held two byte-identical
copies (one with a "(1)" suffix); only one was filed.

Headline: Class B (Group 3), 300 lb, up to **7 mph** (4 mph UK; **4.5 mph
with iLevel**), range up to 18.1 mi, **Group 24** batteries (62 Ah C/5,
75 Ah C/20), 8 A off-board charger, **4 Nm brakes**, SRS suspension,
7 in flat-free casters front and rear, max slope 7.5°, climbs 3.5 in.
Full figures on the model page.
