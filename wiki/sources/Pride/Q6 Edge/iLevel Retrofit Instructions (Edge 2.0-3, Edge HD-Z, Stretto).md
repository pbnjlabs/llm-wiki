# iLevel Retrofit Instructions (Q6 Edge 2.0/3, Edge HD/Z, Stretto)

Three Quantum instructions for adding **iLevel** to an existing power
base. All need **Q-Logic 3** electronics and an AAM (NE+ with AM1, AM2
or LAM2 also allowed on the Stretto), and the chair must be
**reprogrammed** with the latest power base and iLevel seat programs
afterwards (prideprovider.com / pq-365.com). Technician-only, two
people, chair unoccupied, new kit hardware.

**INFINFB3460, Q6 Edge 2.0 and Edge 3** (Rev F, May 2023): switch
anchor brackets on the crossbar bolts (bracket parallel to, not
touching, the upper suspension weldment), crossbar bolts **35 N·m**;
route the iLevel harness switches over and behind the motor spring;
**suspension enhancers** on the upper suspension pivots **8–12 N·m**
(snug, free to move); upper springs to the anchor brackets; switch
cover plates; seat towers and cable guides **35 N·m**; lug nuts
**27 N·m**; lift-and-tilt on the slip plate (hex bolts 22 N·m, flat
heads 17.6 N·m); iLevel cable ball end into the suspension enhancer
with its retainer; back 22–26 N·m; iLevel base harness ELE145674 →
intermediate harness ELE145770 → **AAM port IN-C**; lift harness
"L-Inhibit" into ELE145770. **Adjust the switch paddle so the switch
lever makes full contact when the seat is up.**

**INFMANU4509, Q6 Edge HD and Q6 Edge Z** (Rev B, June 2023): batteries
out; switch brackets with microswitches on both sides; suspension lock
harness routed with the motor harnesses; caster brackets
(rounded protrusion up, left/right specific) 8–12 N·m; **iLevel lockout
assemblies** at the front suspension 15 N·m; towers, tie-downs and
cable brackets 35 N·m; interface plates 21 N·m; lug nuts 27 N·m; seat
21.7 N·m; back 22–26 N·m; **cross the cables at the front**; connect
the lift inhibit switch and suspension lock harness to the
intermediate harness. **Adjust the engagement pad (jam nut) so the
switch lever makes full contact when the seat is up.**

**INFINFB4312, Q6 Edge 3 Stretto** (Rev A, Dec 2020): kit has a REAC
lift intermediate board, seat interface, iLevel harnesses and base
components, lift-and-tilt seat base, caster arm and cable block, and
a redesigned back mount. iLevel switch onto the **suspension limiter**
(1.5–2.0 N·m); limiter slid between suspension and frame (4.5–6 N·m;
mount 18–25 N·m); caster arm stop with backing plate **sloped edges
up** (15–20 N·m); seat 27.1 N·m; back 20 N·m; REAC lift intermediate
board between the lift actuator and the seat module; wheels 25 N·m.
**Don't power on until all harnesses and bus cables are connected.**

**Final check (all three):** no errors on the joystick; full speed in
every profile with the seat down; run the seat up and down several
times and confirm iLevel engages; correct reduced speed with the seat
fully up.
