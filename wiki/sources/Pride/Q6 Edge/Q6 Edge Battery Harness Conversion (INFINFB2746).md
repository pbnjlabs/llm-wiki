# Q6 Edge Battery Harness Conversion (INFINFB2746)

4-page picture instruction, Rev A, June 2011, for converting the
original [[Q6-Edge]] to the new battery harness (the same change as
TechnoBulletin JAZZY-171, "improved battery harness routing", June
2011; see [[Pride TechnoBulletins (2006-2024)]]).

Seat off (two people), joystick and seat harnesses unplugged, front
battery door off (slide the latch inward), batteries and their
harnesses off, top shrouds and rear door off; remove the old harness
and wire loom (**leave the stabilizer bridge**); attach the new harness
to the power module and breaker; route and tie it; reassemble.

Warnings: **don't cut or shorten the battery harness**; coil any
excess and tie it. Misrouted battery harness can disconnect the
batteries in a sudden stop. Technician-only.

Feeds into: [[Q6-Edge]].
