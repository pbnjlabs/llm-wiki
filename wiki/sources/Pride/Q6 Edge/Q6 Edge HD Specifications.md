# Q6 Edge HD Specifications

2-page Quantum spec sheet for the **Q6 Edge HD**, Rev C/February 2020
(no INFINFB number printed).

Headline: Class B (Group 3), **450 lb** (400 lb with the TRU-Balance
10 in power-adjustable heavy-duty lift; 500 lb with TRU-Balance
Bariatric Tilt/Synergy, UK only), up to 4.7 mph, range up to 17.5 mi,
two **Group 24** batteries (52 lb each), 8 A off-board charger, max
slope 7.5° (US). Max seat 28 × 28 in TRU-Balance Bariatric Tilt. Full
figures on the model page.
