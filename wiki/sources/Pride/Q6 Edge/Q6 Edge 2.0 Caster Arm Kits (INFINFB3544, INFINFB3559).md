# Q6 Edge 2.0 Caster Arm Kits (6 in ↔ 8 in)

Two near-identical instructions (2016): **KIT168287** (INFINFB3544,
6 in → 8 in caster arms) and **KIT168288** (INFINFB3559, 8 in → 6 in).
Each kit has front and rear caster arm assemblies, casters, mounting
hardware and caster arm bumpers.

Steps: block the frame up; wheels off; front battery shroud off;
unbolt the lower strut and damper; remove the caster arm bumpers and
press in new ones with **Loctite 411** on top; swap the front caster
arms; reattach damper and strut; rear: harnesses out through the rear
electronics cover, rear strut off, swap the rear arms; reassemble.

| Fastener | Torque |
|---|---|
| Front caster arm to frame | 20 N·m (14.75 ft·lb); may need a rubber mallet, sandpaper inside the barrel |
| Lower strut to caster arm | 8–12 N·m |
| Rear caster arms to frame | 55 N·m (40.5 ft·lb) |
| Rear strut | 8–10 N·m |
| Lug nuts | 25 N·m (18.4 ft·lb) |

**Critical last step, set the front spring length with adjustable
pliers:** **73.8 mm ± 1.5 mm with 8 in arms** (KIT168287),
**80 mm ± 1.5 mm with 6 in arms** (KIT168288). Both sheets say "attach
the 8 in caster arms" in the rear step; the 8 → 6 kit means its own
6 in arms.
