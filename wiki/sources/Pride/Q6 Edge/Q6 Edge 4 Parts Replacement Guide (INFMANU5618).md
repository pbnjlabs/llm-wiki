# Q6 Edge 4 Parts Replacement Guide (INFMANU5618)

50-page Quantum **Parts Replacement Guide** for the **Q6 Edge 4**, Rev A,
April 2026. The first service document for the Edge 4 on file (before
this, only the spec sheet).

Sections: chair prep (power off, both motors in drive, **unplug the bus
cable from the base**, jack up), seating and towers (TRU-Balance 3 REAC
lift, TB3 tilt, TRU-Balance 4 tilt, TB4 lift and tilt, TB Flex), front
and rear door shrouds, drive wheel (14 × 3 in or 14 × 4 in), tire and
tube, standard and **lighted** fenders, front caster caps and shrouds
(standard and lighted), rear caster caps, side shroud inserts, center
shroud, front and rear suspension, caster fork and wheel, front and rear
caster arms, caster wheel, drive motors (incl. **encoder motors**),
power module (Q-Logic 3 and QLNES port maps), full-base and accent
light modules, rear door, light harness, **AM3 / AM3L** for the
Q-Captain seat, batteries and harnesses (**slide-out battery tray**),
circuit breaker and tray, iLevel base parts (caster-arm stop,
suspension limiter, suspension lock harness), the **Quantum PTO Backup
Camera** module, and backup camera wiring by configuration.

Torque highlights (full table on [[Pride-Parts-Replacement]]):
lug nuts 22–27 N·m; rim screws 4–5 N·m with Loctite 242; motor to mount
15–20 N·m; caster arm to frame 30–40 N·m; front suspension 10–15, rear
6–12 N·m; caster wheel bolt 12–16 N·m; caster stem nut set by **turning
torque (0.20–0.45 N·m to start rotating)**, not a fixed value; seat
interfaces 30–40 N·m by seat.

Gotchas:
- **Accent-light and backup-camera modules sit in the drive-inhibit
  path.** A connector not fully seated and latched gives a **drive
  inhibit** error. The backup camera can't be used with a remote stop
  switch.
- **Re-route harnesses exactly as found** (motor, light and iLevel
  harnesses are bundled); cable-tie head faces in so it doesn't hit the
  fender.
- **Battery harness at the breaker:** short wire to the left at about
  120°, long wire up from the right and over the top.
- **Terminal fuse torque is printed twice, differently:** 10–15 N·m in
  the rear door section, **3.7–4.1 N·m** in the battery section. Confirm
  with Quantum before torquing; the lower value is the safer start.
- Replace any nylon-insert nut **or patched screw** removed.
