# Q6 Edge 2.0, 2.0X, 3, 2.0HD and Q4 2 Owners Manual (INFMANU4620)

32-page owner's manual covering the **Q6 Edge 2.0, 2.0X, 3, 2.0HD and
Q4 2** (Rev N, Jan 2025). The one owner's manual for both
[[Q6-Edge-2.0]] and the standard [[Q6-Edge-3]]. Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

Standard Quantum owner's manual layout: safety, the chair's parts and
freewheel levers, assembly, comfort adjustments, batteries and charging,
care and maintenance (daily/weekly/monthly/yearly checks, cleaning and
disinfection, wheel change, battery replacement). The shared Pride
content is distilled once on [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]]. **No
specification table or warranty section**: specs are on the separate
spec sheet in the owner's package, warranty on the warranty card
([[Pride-Warranty]]). Seating systems (TB-Flex, Synergy, TRU-Balance
3/4, Q4) and the controller have their own manuals.

Specifics:
- **iLevel boots** on the base (two); don't force the freewheel levers.
- Seat mounts on **trapeze bars** and a seat interface weldment.
- Optional **power elevating seat toggle switch**; never raise the seat
  in freewheel.
- Synergy and TRU-Balance 3 seats have separate manuals.

Feeds: [[Q6-Edge-2.0]], [[Q6-Edge-3]], [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]].
