# Q6 Series, Q6 Edge HD and Q6 Ultra Owners Manual (INFMANU4237)

32-page owner's manual for the **Q6 series: Q6 Edge HD and Q6 Ultra**
(Rev F, Jan 2024). Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

Standard Quantum owner's manual layout: safety, the chair's parts and
freewheel levers, assembly, comfort adjustments, batteries and charging,
care and maintenance (daily/weekly/monthly/yearly checks, cleaning and
disinfection, wheel change, battery replacement). The shared Pride
content is distilled once on [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]]. **No
specification table or warranty section**: specs are on the separate
spec sheet in the owner's package, warranty on the warranty card
([[Pride-Warranty]]). Seating systems (TB-Flex, Synergy, TRU-Balance
3/4, Q4) and the controller have their own manuals.

Specifics: quick-release battery connectors and motor connectors on the
base; seat on trapeze bars. Synergy and TRU-Balance 3 seats have their
own manuals.

Feeds: [[Q6-Edge-HD]], [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]].
