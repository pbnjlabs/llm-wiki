# Q6 Edge Parts Replacement Guide (INFMANU4845)

22-page Quantum **Parts Replacement Guide** for the **Q6 Edge**, Rev A,
16 Jan 2017. Titled "Q6 Edge", not 2.0. Its NE / NE+ / Q-Logic joysticks,
TRU-Balance / TRU-Balance 3 / Contour seats and "semi-independent
articulating beam" fit the original Edge ([[Q6-Edge]]); check the IPB
before using it on an Edge 2.0.

Sections: chair prep (power off, drive mode, unplug joystick, block up),
seating removal, front battery door and rear electronics door, center
panel and top shroud, drive wheel (4- and 5-spoke hubs), tire and tube,
caster, articulating beam, drive motor, foot platform, batteries,
joystick, power module, battery harness and circuit breaker.

Torque values:

| Fastener | Torque |
|---|---|
| Drive wheel lug nuts | 25 N·m (18.4 ft·lb) |
| Hub nut on motor shaft | 51 N·m (37.6 ft·lb) |
| Motor to motor bracket | 7 N·m (5 ft·lb) |
| Caster nut | 27–34 N·m (20–25 ft·lb) |
| Articulating beam nuts | 40 N·m (30 ft·lb) |
| Foot platform bracket | 25 N·m (18.4 ft·lb) |
| TRU-Balance / TB3 interface screws | 27.1 N·m (20 ft·lb) and 35.3 N·m (26 ft·lb) |

Warnings worth knowing: **re-seat the axle key** when the hub comes off
(brake failure otherwise); deflate tires fully before splitting a rim;
**never reuse a nylon-insert lock nut**; don't adjust spring tension;
replace both batteries together; battery harness misrouting can
disconnect the batteries in a sudden stop. Battery wiring diagram is
inside the front battery door.
