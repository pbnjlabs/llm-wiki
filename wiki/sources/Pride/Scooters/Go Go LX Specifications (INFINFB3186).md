# Go Go LX Specifications (INFINFB3186)

Pride "Universal" product specifications sheet, INFINFB3186/Rev F/November 2019. Multilingual: the
English column is on the right of page 1 (Dutch, Spanish, French,
German and Italian fill the rest).

Headline: models **50LX (3-wheel) and 54LX (4-wheel)**, 300 lb, 4.4 mph, 12 or 18 Ah batteries (standard or extended-range packs), range 5.5–12.6 mi, 2 A off-board charger, max slope 6°, climbs 2 in. With 12 Ah batteries it doesn't meet AS/NZS 3695.2 or EN12184.

Full figures on the model page.
