# Travel Scooter Series Owners Manual (INFMANU4392)

128-page "universal" travel scooter owner's manual (English, French,
Spanish; Rev R, Jan 2026) for models AAP4, 29, 36, 336, 39, 40/44, 40X/44X, 40E/402E/44E/442E/443E/
443EMS, 40LX/44LX, 49, 493, 50LX/54LX, 50LXLIT/54LXLIT, 53/53HD, 54,
54ALX, 54HD, 66/67, 73/74, 83/84 and the Jazzy ZTS. That covers the
[[Elite-Traveller]] (40E/44E), [[Go-Go-LX]] (50LX/54LX),
[[Go-Go-Sport]] (73/74), [[Revo]] (66/67) and [[Ultra-X]]. A features
table lists console, battery pack style, on- or off-unit charging,
frame lock-up and options by model. Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

Beep code table (scooter OMs): 1 battery too low to run; 2 battery
low; 3 voltage too high or charger still connected; 4 current limit
time-out; 5 freewheel lever in freewheel; 6 throttle not centered at
start-up; 7 speed pot error; 8 motor volts error; 9 other internal
error. Turn off and on after fixing ([[S-Drive-Beep-Codes]]).

Won't move: the freewheel lever **forward = freewheel**, brakes off and
motor power cut; pull it back and cycle the key.

[[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]].
