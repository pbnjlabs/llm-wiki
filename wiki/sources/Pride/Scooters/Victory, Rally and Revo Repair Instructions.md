# Victory, Rally and Revo Repair Instructions (4 sheets)

- **Victory series fuse replacement** (KITASMB1803, INFINFB2562 Rev B,
  June 2011): the fuse compartment is on the **back of the tiller**.
  ATO fuses: **10 A (red), 5 A (tan), four 1 A (black)**. Replacements
  must match the rating exactly.
- **Rally, Shuttle and Victory motor/transaxle** (INFINFB1310, 2003):
  seat and rear shroud off, both rear wheels off, transaxle brackets
  off, unplug the motor; when separating them, if the planetary gear
  stays on the transaxle put it back in the motor: its **lower rim must
  sit flush with or below the motor face plate** or the motor breaks
  on reassembly; O-ring around the planetary gear.
- **Revo throttle pot** (ELEASMB2663, INFINFB1425, 2003): key out,
  battery box off; throttle lever off (2 setscrews, 2 mm hex); console
  top off; unplug the 3-pin; swap the pot; refit the lever so its
  **throw is equal forward and reverse**.
- **SC60 Revo battery box** (INFINFB2314, June 2009): fits an
  off-board 2.5 A Battery Maximizer charger with an **XLR receptacle**
  on the battery box in place of the onboard charger.

Feeds into: [[Victory]], [[Revo]].
