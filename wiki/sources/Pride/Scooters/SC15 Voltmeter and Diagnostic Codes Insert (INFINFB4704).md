# SC15 Voltmeter and Diagnostic Codes Insert (INFINFB4704)

12-page multilingual insert for the SC15 (Rev A, Sep 2024). Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

**Lithium battery meter:** 3 green = 90–100%; 2 green 80–90; 1 green
60–80; 4 yellow 50–60; 3 yellow 40–50; 2 yellow under 30; 1 yellow
under 20; all red = dead. Read it at full speed on level ground.

**Beep codes (repeat 4 times, then stop; key off/on to repeat):** 1
steady tone, too low to run; 2 low; 3 voltage too high or charger
connected; 4 **Hall effect sensor/motor**; 5 freewheel lever forward;
6 throttle not centered; 8 motor volts error; 9 other internal errors
(off a few minutes, then call the provider). No code 7 is listed. This
is the S-Drive-style list; the SC15 owner's manual gives the Jiuniu
list (2, 4–7, 9) instead. Count the beeps and check both.
