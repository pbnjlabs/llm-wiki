# Revo Specifications (INFINFB3884)

Pride "Universal" product specifications sheet, INFINFB3884/Rev D/April 2020. Multilingual: the
English column is on the right of page 1 (Dutch, Spanish, French,
German and Italian fill the rest).

Headline: models **66 (3-wheel) and 67 (4-wheel)**, **400 lb**, 5.2 mph, range 10.3–17.8 mi depending on wheel count, tires and load, two **U1** batteries (25 lb each), 3.5 A off-board charger, max slope 6°, climbs 2.5 in.

Full figures on the model page.
