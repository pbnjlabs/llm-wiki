# Go Go Carbon and Super Portable Code Sheets

Two one-page sheets from the Go Go section of the basic operations
library (undated, uploaded 2026). Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

**Go Go Carbon (LCD icons 1–10):** 1 battery low; 2 bad motor
connection; 3 motor fault; 4 controller overheating (off a few
minutes); 5 LCD screen/wiring; 6 charge inhibit (is it charging?); 7
throttle (restart); 8 controller/wiring; 9 brake (brake lever in
electric/drive position?); 10 high battery voltage (stop charging).

**Go Go Super Portable, Jiuniu controller (light flashes):** 2 battery
low; 4 Hall sensor; 5 magnetic brake disconnected; 6 accelerator wire
off or not at rest at power-on; 7 accelerator signal; 9 controller.

No Go Go Carbon model page or owner's manual on file.
