# Go-Go Elite Traveller Repair Instructions (6 sheets)

Part-replacement sheets for the Go-Go Elite Traveller **SC40E/44E**
(2005) and the **Elite Traveller Plus SC53/54** (2011). Prep for all:
key out, scooter in **drive mode**.

- **Throttle pot** (ELEASMB4829, with ISO resistor; INFINFB1923):
  split the console shroud, unplug from the front-to-rear harness, swap
  the pot in the bottom half, re-tie the harness to the tiller.
- **Speed pot** (DWR9975H001, 25 kΩ; INFINFB1924): unplug, swap in the
  top shroud half, then **turn the pot to full rabbit and fit the knob
  with its mark on the rabbit**.
- **Motor** (DRVMOTR1269; INFINFB1922): separate front and rear, rear
  shroud off, unbolt the power connector and the motor from the
  transaxle; move the brake to the new motor **with the brake handle
  pointing up (freewheel position)** so the holes line up; motor and
  brake leads into the connector in the exact positions; **keep the
  connector spring**; new hardware, coupler facing the right way; tighten
  the connector screws after rejoining the halves.
- **Drive wheel** (WHLASMB1705, 2.5 × 8 in flat-free): shaft key in
  place, nut **28 N·m (20 ft·lb)**.
- **Controller** (ELEASMB4893, **S-Drive 45 A**; INFINFB1921): main
  shroud and controller cover off, unplug motor/brake,
  controller/battery and inhibit harnesses.
- **Elite Traveller Plus throttle pot / lower console** (INFINFB3022):
  key out, **not in freewheel**, front wheel locked; basket and console
  off, tiller off its mount, front-to-rear harness pulled out through the
  tiller; swap pot and lower console; pull the harness back with **a
  string taped to the sheath (not the connectors)**; route it **under**
  the handlebar.
