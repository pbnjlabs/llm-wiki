# ET Series Specifications (INFINFB4539)

Pride "Universal" product specifications sheet, INFINFB4539/Rev D/April 2026. Multilingual: the
English column is on the right of page 1 (Dutch, Spanish, French,
German and Italian fill the rest).

Headline: models **SC402E (3-wheel), SC442E, SC442EMS, SC443E, SC443EMS (4-wheel)**, 300 lb, 4.23 mph, 12 or 18 Ah, range 5.5–14.1 mi, 2 A charger (**3.5 A on the EMS models**), max slope 6°. The filename spells two models "ESC442EMS" and "SC443"; the sheet prints SC442EMS and SC443E.

Full figures on the model page.
