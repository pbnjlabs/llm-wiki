# Victory 9/10 Technical Troubleshooting Guide (INFMANU3986)

21-page dealer guide, Rev A, January 2010, for the **Victory 9 and
Victory 10** scooters (PG S-Drive, ELEASMB5404; tiller fuse block).

- **No power:** key fully in and turned fully clockwise; **check the
  key fuse in the tiller fuse block** (about 1 Ω). Breaker; charger
  port connector 12a pins 1–2 (over 18 V: go to the console; under:
  charge); each battery over 9 V; left/right battery harnesses; breaker
  (via the battery connector module); controller power harness;
  controller pins 7–13; interface harness pins 4–10 at 7d (under the
  front shroud near the front wheel); console 10g pins 1–4; tiller fuse
  block.
- **Power on, won't drive, no beeps:** charger not in the port; port
  pins 2–3 continuity (inhibit) through programming harness/run plug,
  controller, interface harness, tiller harness, charger harness; key
  switch circuit through console, tiller harness and interface harness.
- **Beep codes** (rapid beeps then the count, once): 3 high battery;
  5 brake (**pull the freewheel lever back for drive**; brake
  **30–65 Ω**); 6 throttle off-center or pot spring (pot about
  **2.5 kΩ**, more than 0.5 kΩ off → pot); 7 throttle/speed pot (4.7 kΩ
  and 2.5 kΩ at the controller plug; **speed pot 100 kΩ**); 8 motor
  (0.5–1.5 Ω; brushes under 0.25 in → brushes); 9 controller trip
  (freewheel by **pushing the lever forward**, push test: brake, then
  motor, then transaxle).
