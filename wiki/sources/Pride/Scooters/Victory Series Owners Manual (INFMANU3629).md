2012) with a specification appendix. Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

All: rear-wheel drive, sealed transaxle, 24 V motor; regenerative and
electromechanical brakes; **3 A off-board charger** with U1 batteries;
seat 28 lb. Hi/lo switch: **HI 5.25 mph, LO 2.625 mph**. Tiller fuses
(automotive type): off-board charger **10 A**, key switch **5 A**,
lights and turn signals **1 A**. Baskets 15 lb max. Charge 8–14 h.
Freewheel forward with the key on = no drive until lever back and key
cycled.
