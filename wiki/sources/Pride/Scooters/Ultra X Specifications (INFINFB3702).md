# Ultra X Specifications (INFINFB3702)

Pride "Universal" product specifications sheet, INFINFB3702/Rev J/August 2026. Multilingual: the
English column is on the right of page 1 (Dutch, Spanish, French,
German and Italian fill the rest).

Headline: models **S39 (3-wheel), S49 and SC493 (4-wheel)**, 300 lb, 4.3 mph, **single motor**, 12 Ah standard in the US (18 Ah standard internationally), range 6.2–14.2 mi, 2 A off-board charger, max slope 6°. Class A is only reached with 18 Ah batteries.

Full figures on the model page.
