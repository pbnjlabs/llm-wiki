# Go Go Sport Specifications (INFINFB3254)

Pride "Universal" product specifications sheet, INFINFB3254/Rev E/April 2025. Multilingual: the
English column is on the right of page 1 (Dutch, Spanish, French,
German and Italian fill the rest).

Headline: models **73 (3-wheel) and 74 (4-wheel)**, **325 lb**, 4.7 mph US/Canada (4 mph international), 18 Ah batteries, range 12–12.75 mi, 2 A off-board charger, max slope 6°, climbs 2 in. The 4-wheel turns in 41.5 in with the EZ Turn option.

Full figures on the model page.
