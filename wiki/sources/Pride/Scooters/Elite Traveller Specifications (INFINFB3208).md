# Elite Traveller Specifications (INFINFB3208)

Pride "Universal" product specifications sheet, INFINFB3208/Rev C/November 2019. Multilingual: the
English column is on the right of page 1 (Dutch, Spanish, French,
German and Italian fill the rest).

Headline: models **40E (3-wheel) and 44E (4-wheel)**, 300 lb, 4 mph, 12 Ah standard (12 Ah extended, 18 Ah and 18 Ah extended optional), range 6–12.4 mi, 2 A off-board charger, max slope 6°, climbs 2 in.

Full figures on the model page.
