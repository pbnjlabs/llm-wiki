# Elite Traveller Basic Troubleshooting Instructions (INFINFB2039)

2-page supplement for the **Elite Traveller, Elite Traveller Plus and
Elite Traveller Plus HD** travel scooters, Rev B, 31 Jul 2008. The older
Elite Traveller series; the current 40E/44E is on a 2019 spec sheet
(check the scooter's beeps against this table before relying on it).

- **Won't power up:** key fully in; battery pack seated tight; quick-
  disconnect joined; red to +, black to −; push in the main breaker
  reset.
- **Range drops:** batteries over a year old; charge daily users 8–14 h,
  idle scooters weekly for 12–14 h; watch the meter rise while charging
  (no rise → charger fuse, outlet); wall-switched outlet; no extension
  cords; don't run the meter into the red.
- **Powers up but won't move:** **power-down timer** after long idle
  (remove and reinsert the key); **charger plugged into the scooter**
  inhibits driving even if it isn't in the wall; beep code.
- **Meter dips and the motor surges under throttle:** charge fully.

| Beeps | Condition | Do |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Battery voltage too low | Charge fully |
| 3 | Battery voltage too high | Provider |
| 5 | Solenoid brake trip; freewheel lever may be in freewheel | Remove key, lever to drive, restart |
| 6 | Throttle trip: throttle pressed while inserting the key | Release throttle, reinsert key |
| 7 | Throttle or speed pot fault | Provider |
| 8 | Motor disconnected | Provider |
| 9 | Possible controller trip: battery pack installed with the key in, or overheated | Wait several minutes; reinsert key |
