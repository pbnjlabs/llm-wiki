# Go-Go Elite Traveller Technical Troubleshooting Guide (INFMANU3206)

18-page dealer guide, Rev C, December 2006, for the **Go-Go Elite
Traveller with the PG S-Drive controller** (45 A, ELEASMB4893).

**Beep codes** start with **ten fast beeps**, then the count; it plays
once (reinsert the key to hear it again).

- **No power:** breaker (reset; under 1 Ω); battery box connector 1h
  pins 1–3 over 18 V; under 18 V → charge; in the box: harness, battery
  pair (under 1 Ω across the pair = bad batteries), **fuse 5c**,
  console (connector 4c over 18 V → console), front-to-rear harness,
  interface harness (S-Drive pins 7–13), power interface harness.
- **Power on, won't move, no beeps:** key switch circuit (connector 3a
  pins 2–3 under 1 Ω with key on), front-to-rear and interface
  harnesses, controller.
- **Beep 3**, over **32 V**: drive it down (not into the red); charger
  output over **30 V** → replace the charger.
- **Beep 5**, brake: drive mode; brake **30–65 Ω** at the motor plug,
  the brake itself, then the motor interface harness.
- **Beep 6**, throttle off-center at key-on.
- **Beep 7**, throttle/speed pot: **throttle pot 5 kΩ** end to end,
  **12.5 kΩ** each side of the wiper; **speed pot 0 Ω** at hare,
  **25 kΩ** at tortoise; then harnesses, then controller.
- **Beep 8**, motor: **0.5–1.5 Ω**.
- **Beep 9**, controller trip: key in while fitting the battery box, or
  overheated (rest 10–15 min); if it drives then trips, freewheel and
  push: drags → remove the brake, then the motor, to find whether
  brake, motor or transaxle is binding.
