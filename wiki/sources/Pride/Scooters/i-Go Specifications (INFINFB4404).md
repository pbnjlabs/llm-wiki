# i-Go Specifications (INFINFB4404)

Pride "Universal" product specifications sheet, INFINFB4404/Rev B/January 2023. Multilingual: the
English column is on the right of page 1 (Dutch, Spanish, French,
German and Italian fill the rest).

Headline: model **S20**, folding, **275 lb**, 4 mph, **25.2 V 9.6 Ah lithium-ion** (4.8 lb), front-wheel-drive brushless hub motor, 60.6 lb without battery, range 7–9.3 mi, 3.5 A off-board charger, max slope 6°, climbs 1.57 in.
