# Go Go Super Portable SC15 Owners Manual (INFMANU5479)

88-page non-medical owner's manual (English, French, Spanish; Rev C,
May 2025) for the **Go Go Super Portable, SC15** ([[Super-Portable-SC15]]).
Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

- One sealed **lithium-ion battery box** (may or may not be removable),
  **battery disconnect switch**, circuit breaker (push the reset toward
  the rear), fuse under a rubber cover.
- **Magnetic charger connector**: keep it **6 in from implanted
  medical devices**. Charge order: charger into the scooter first, then
  the wall; two red LEDs = charging, green = full; a scrolling battery
  symbol shows when powered on while charging.
- **Fault beeps (Jiuniu controller):** 2 battery low; 4 Hall sensor; 5
  magnetic brake disconnected (freewheel lever back to drive); 6
  throttle not at rest or a wire off at power-up; 7 throttle signal; 9
  controller. Same as the [[Go Go Carbon and Super Portable Code Sheets]].
- Dead: breaker, battery charged and seated, disconnect switch **on**.
