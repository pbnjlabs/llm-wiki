# Pride Scooter Brake Replacement (INFINFB1742)

2-page instruction, Rev B, March 2005, for Pride's universal scooter
brake kit (Anderson Powerpole and AMP/Molex connectors, heat shrink,
several brake nuts and dowel pins).

1. Cut the ties, unplug the brake from the controller harness, unbolt
   the brake.
2. **Two-wire vs four-wire:** if the new brake has 4 wires and the old
   one 2, shorten one brake-coil wire and one microswitch wire by half,
   solder them together, and terminate the other two with the kit
   connectors.
3. **Brake nut** on the motor shaft: keep it if it matches the new pad
   and isn't rounded; otherwise replace. Setscrew-type and dowel-pin
   types **aren't interchangeable**: use the same type.
4. Fit the brake **in the freewheel position** (easier alignment),
   nut into the pad; screws **a quarter turn past snug**, no more.
5. Route as original, connect, **lever to drive**, test.
