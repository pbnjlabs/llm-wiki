# Super Portable SC15 Specifications (INFINFB4573)

Pride "Universal" product specifications sheet, INFINFB4573/Rev A/July 2024. Multilingual: the
English column is on the right of page 1 (Dutch, Spanish, French,
German and Italian fill the rest).

Headline: model **SC15**, folding, 300 lb, 4.5 mph, **lithium-ion 11.7 or 16 Ah**, 200 W brushless transaxle, 41.6 lb without battery, range 8.8–10.1 mi (11.7 Ah), off-board charger, max slope 6°, climbs 1.57 in, folds to 11.81 in tall. The sheet's **reversing width (18.43 in) looks like a misprint**: it's smaller than the 21.54 in overall width.

Full figures on the model page.
