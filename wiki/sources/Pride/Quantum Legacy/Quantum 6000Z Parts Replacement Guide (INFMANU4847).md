# Quantum 6000Z Parts Replacement Guide (INFMANU4847)

26-page PRG, Rev A, January 2017. Sections: battery and electronics
doors, center panel and side shrouds, drive wheel (5- and 4-spoke
hubs), caster assembly, front caster arm assembly, foot platform,
**Hammer motor** and **H2 motor** (H2 with Accu-Trac: unplug the
Accu-Trac lead too), articulating beam, **rear swing-arm**, batteries,
joystick, power module, battery/power harness and breaker.

| Fastener | Torque |
|---|---|
| Lug nuts (4- and 5-spoke) | 25 N·m (18.4 ft·lb) |
| 4/5-spoke main hub nut | 51 N·m (37.6 ft·lb) |
| Caster nut | 30 N·m (21.7 ft·lb) |
| Strut to caster arm (shoulder bolt + nut) | 10 N·m (7.4 ft·lb) |
| Caster arm to frame | 27.6 N·m (20.4 ft·lb) |
| Motor to bracket (Hammer and H2) | **12 N·m (8.9 ft·lb)** |
| Articulating beam screw | 59 N·m (43.5 ft·lb) |
| Strut to frame / to swing-arm | 10 N·m |
| Swing-arm to frame | 21.5 N·m (15.9 ft·lb) |
