# Quantum 614 with NE+ Technical Troubleshooting Guide (INFMANU3988)

18-page dealer guide, Rev A, January 2010. Same format and tests as the
[[Q6 Edge Technical Troubleshooting Guide (INFMANU4109 Rev B, 2012)|Q6 Edge]]
and [[J6 Technical Troubleshooting Guide (INFMANU4132)|J6]] guides, for
the **Quantum 614 with NE+**.

- **No power:** charger socket about 25 V → NE+ joystick; under 18 V →
  charge; then front and rear battery harnesses, battery cable, breaker
  (under 1 Ω), power module bus port (pins 1–4), bus extension.
- **Inhibit:** PTO 4-pin pin 3–4 (never 1–2); power module socket
  **pin 1–9**; inhibit must be **High Inhibit**.
- **System Lock:** key symbol right of the battery gauge. Lock: power
  on, **press the Mode switch until it turns off** (the Q6 Edge guide
  says hold both profile keys). Unlock: on, forward to beep, back to
  beep.
- **Open motor:** 0.5–1.5 Ω per motor (right motor plug 7a, left 8a);
  both good → power module.
- **Motor/brake:** freewheel levers in drive; brake **about 35 Ω**
  (pins 3–4); both good → power module.
- **Communications/software:** bus and multiplier/splitter continuity
  under 1 Ω, then Quantum.
- **LCD messages** (NE+): the Q6 Edge/J6 list plus **left/right
  indicator, hazard and running light errors** (press that key; call
  Quantum if it stays).
