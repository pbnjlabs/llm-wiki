# Quantum 600 Series Parts Replacement Guide (INFMANU4842)

22-page PRG, Rev A, December 2016, for the **Quantum 600 series**
(same family as the Jazzy 600 / 600 XL). Electronics shown: **VSI,
Q-Logic, Remote Plus, VR2**. The VSI has **no separate power module**
(it's built into the joystick).

Sections: foot platform/front battery door and rear electronics door
(the rear door stays tethered to the top shroud by a lanyard), shroud
assemblies, drive wheel (**star** drive wheel and **silver/black rim**
wheel), caster assembly and wheel, drive motor, foot platform,
articulating beam, front caster arm, batteries, joystick, power module.

| Fastener | Torque |
|---|---|
| Star drive wheel main nut | **51 N·m (37.6 ft·lb)**; re-seat the axle key |
| Silver/black rim lug nuts | 25 N·m (18.4 ft·lb) |
| Caster nut | 30 N·m (21.7 ft·lb) |
| Motor to bracket | 7 N·m (5 ft·lb) |
| Articulating beam | 40 N·m (30 ft·lb) |

One step prints "51 N·m (21.7 ft·lb)"; 51 N·m is 37.6 ft·lb as
elsewhere in the guide.
