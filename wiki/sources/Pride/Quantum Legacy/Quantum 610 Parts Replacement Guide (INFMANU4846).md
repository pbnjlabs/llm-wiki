# Quantum 610 Parts Replacement Guide (INFMANU4846)

18-page PRG, Rev A, January 2017. Same layout as the Q6 Edge and
Quantum 600 guides: battery and electronics doors (rear door on a
lanyard), shrouds, drive wheel, tire and tube, caster assembly and
wheel, front caster arm, drive motor (with the freewheel lever), foot
platform, articulating beam, batteries, joystick, power module.

| Fastener | Torque |
|---|---|
| Drive wheel main nut | 51 N·m (37.6 ft·lb); re-seat the axle key |
| Caster nut | 30 N·m (21.7 ft·lb) |
| Foot platform hardware | 25 N·m (18.4 ft·lb) |
| Articulating beam screw | **58 N·m (43 ft·lb)** |

Freewheel on the Q610: push the levers **inward** for drive, pull
**outward** for freewheel ([[Quantum-Jazzy Freewheel Operation (INFINFB2121)]]).
