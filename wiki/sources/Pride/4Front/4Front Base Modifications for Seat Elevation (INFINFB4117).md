# Quantum 4Front Base Modifications for Safe Seat Elevation (INFINFB4117)

8-page instruction, Rev A, August 2019: prepare a **Quantum 4Front**
power base for an **elevating lift** seat (a non-iLevel upgrade; the
seat kit has its own instructions).

- **KIT1902966** (users **over 200 lb**): new seat interfaces (1- or
  3-position towers) **and** new suspension (rear shock 350 lb, front
  shock 450 lb, pivot pins, caps, cover).
- **KIT1902968** (users **under 200 lb**): seat interfaces only.

All old hardware is discarded. Suspension swap per side: wheel and
fender off (watch the light harness), outer caster arm cover and pivot
pin caps off, pull the pivot pins (**the caster arm and motor drop**:
keep hands clear), fit the rear 350 lb shock in the caster arm's top
hole and the front 450 lb shock to frame and motor mount, new pins and
caps. Torque values are printed in the figures (not legible in the
text copy; read the PDF).
