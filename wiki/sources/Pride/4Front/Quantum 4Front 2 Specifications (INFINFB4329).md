# Quantum 4Front 2 Specifications (INFINFB4329)

2-page Quantum spec sheet for the **4Front 2** front-wheel-drive chair,
US/UK/AU/EU, INFINFB4329/Rev A/April 2021. Downloaded twice
(byte-identical); one copy filed.

Headline: Class B (Group 3), 300 lb, up to 6.25 mph (3.5 mph with the
10 in lift), range 16.34 mi (Group 24) or 17.4 mi (Group 34), SRS
suspension, 14 in flat-free drive wheels, 9 in casters, 4 in anti-tips,
8 A off-board charger, max slope 7.5°, climbs 3.34 in. Full figures on
the model page.
