# Replacing a VR2 Controller Cable (INFINFB2005)

2-page technical instruction, Rev B, May 2007, for kit **DWR1111H077**
(VR2 main cable), shown on a Jazzy 600.

Steps: power off, drive mode; **ESD precautions** (ground yourself);
cut the cable ties; unplug from the base (rear shroud may come off);
take the VR2 off its mount; remove 2 Phillips screws from the charger
socket/cable assembly and **5 Torx (T-10)** screws to split the
controller; unplug the 3-pin harness from the top half; fit the new
cable, reverse, re-tie as original.

Feeds into: [[Pride-Controller-Flash-Codes]], [[Jazzy-600-ES]].
