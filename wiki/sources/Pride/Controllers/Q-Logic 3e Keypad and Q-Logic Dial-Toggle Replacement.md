# Q-Logic 3e Keypad and Q-Logic Dial/Toggle Replacement

Two picture-only instruction sheets (no text layer; read from the
images).

**INFINFB4286, Q-Logic 3e keypad replacement** (Rev A, Sep 2020, 3
pages):
1. Gently pry the old keypad off the joystick; discard it.
2. Clean leftover adhesive from the controller and keypad recess with
   a little **isopropyl alcohol** on a dry cloth.
3. Peel the backing off the new keypad.
4. Press it into the recess; it must sit **fully seated and flush**
   (the sheet shows a raised edge as wrong).
ESD-sensitive device: handle with ESD precautions.

**INFINFB3395, Q-Logic dial and toggle replacement** (Rev A, Jun 2015,
4 pages; Q-Logic display joystick with speed dial and on/off–mode
toggle):
- **Dial:** pry the old dial off with a small screwdriver; line up the
  flat surfaces and press the new one on firmly.
- **Toggle:** pry off the old toggle; check the **spring inside the new
  toggle is in place**; line up the flats and press on.
