# Tilt Threshold Definitions for Seating Modules

3-page sheet defining what each **tilt threshold** parameter does, in
three versions. Undated.

- **AAM, all units except the R4000** (unless the R4000 has a lift and
  tilt seat).
- **AAM on R4000 units** without lift and tilt: Threshold 1 becomes
  plain "tilt will no longer go back"; Driving 2 is unused.
- **AM1, AM2 and LAM2:** the same four functions, but numbered
  differently (Driving 1–4).

Every version also defines **Tilt Threshold Head Control**: with a 3-,
4- or 5-switch head array, between this angle and the drive-lockout
angle the head array drives as a **2-switch** (right pad forward/
reverse, left pad left/right, rear pad inactive), shown by two arrows on
the drive screen.
