# GC3 Controller Basic Operation Instructions (INFMANU3937)

16-page Pride BOI for the **GC3** controller (a PG Drives product; Pride's charts call it "PG GC3"), INFMANU3937/Rev F/July 2021. IPX4. Used on the Go Chair, Go Chair Med, Jazzy EVO 613, Jazzy Elite ES and Jazzy Select.

Covers: on/off and horn keys, **speed dial**, 5-LED battery meter, lock/unlock, sleep, **thermal rollback to 50%**, the **flash codes** (same meanings as VR2), and the **charge reminder**: after **8 hours or more of driving** without a charge, the left red LED flashes 4 times at every power-down. To reset it, **power the controller on before plugging in the charger and leave it on for one full charge**.
