# VR2 Controller Basic Operation Instructions (INFMANU3354)

20-page Pride/Quantum BOI for the **PG Drives VR2**, INFMANU3354/Rev I/February 2022. Pride uses it on the Jazzy Air Med, Jazzy Air 2, and (90 A, 6-key) the Jazzy 1450 and Quantum Q1450.

Covers: keypad (on/off, horn, speed/profile up and down, up to five drive profiles), **10-LED battery meter** (left red flashing = 10% or less; step-up = programming, inhibit or charging; blink every 2.5 s = sleep; ripple = joystick off center at power-up), actuator buttons on the 6-key and 9-key versions (two actuators), drive/function inhibit shown by flashing speed lights, lock/unlock, sleep (power cycle to restart), **thermal rollback to 50%**, the **10 flash codes**, storage 18–122 °F. Off-board charger through the 3-pin socket: **8 A max, Neutrik NC3MX plugs only**.
