# Q-Logic Seat Configuration Settings (AM1, AM2, LAM2)

1-page chart of configuration numbers for the smaller actuator modules.
Undated.

- **AM1** (one actuator, A1): 3 articulating foot platform, 4 elevating
  seat, 8 recline, 20 tilt.
- **AM2 and LAM2** (two actuators, A1 + A2): 1 independent legs, 2
  combined legs, 3 foot platform, 4 elevating seat, 7 elevate + foot
  platform, 8 recline, 11 recline + foot platform, 14 recline + elevate,
  20 tilt, 23 tilt + foot platform, 24 tilt + elevate, 28 tilt +
  recline.

The numbers match the AAM chart's numbering for the same functions.
