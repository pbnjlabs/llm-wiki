# NE Controller Basic Operation Instructions (INFMANU3757)

12-page end-user manual for the **NE** controller (Rev D, Jan 2016).
Fills half of the NE/NE+ gap on the Missing manuals list. Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

- Keypad: on/off, horn, profile up/down; LCD with charging indicator,
  7-segment battery meter, actuator error, motor brake fault, profile/
  speed, fault, warning and temperature indicators. Up to **5
  profiles**.
- Battery meter: bottom segment flashing = 10% or less; segments
  scrolling up = charging/inhibit; all or none flashing fast = fault.
- **Off-board charger/programming socket** (front of the joystick):
  charger current **8 A max**, **Neutrik NC3MX** plugs only.
- **Lock:** with the power on, **hold on/off until it shuts off**.
  Unlock: power on, full forward to a beep, full reverse to a beep.
- Sleep 0–60 min. Thermal rollback to 20%.
- Icon table: power section error, joystick out of center,
  communication, motor/brake, low/high battery voltage, drive thermal
  warning, open motor, low battery, charging inhibit.
