# NE+ Controller Basic Operation Instructions (INFMANU3765)

16-page end-user manual for the **NE+** controller (Rev E, May 2023):
standard joystick, the lights version (turn, hazard, light keys) and
the **attendant control**. Fills the other half of the NE/NE+ gap.
Pulled from the PQ-365 owner's manuals / basic operations library on 2026-10-20.

- Up to **two actuators**, picked by the actuator keys, profile keys
  plus joystick (forward = **lower**, reverse = **raise**), a mono-jack
  switch, or the attendant control. You can drive and run actuators
  until the actuator's drive lockout is reached.
- **Attendant control overrides the hand control.**
- **Lock: hold both profile keys** until it shuts off. Unlock: power
  on, full forward to a beep, full reverse to a beep. This settles the
  guides' disagreement (the Quantum 614 guide says the Mode switch).
- Charger socket: **8 A max**, Neutrik NC3MX. Sleep 0–240 min. Thermal
  rollback 20%. Storage −40 to 149 °F short-term, −13 to 122 °F
  long-term.
- Icon table adds left/right indicator, hazard and running-light
  faults, **speed limit warning (return the seat to upright)**, idle
  mode, attendant control fault and "locked out". Low battery: charging
  takes **12–14 h**.
