# newVSI Controller Basic Operation Instructions (INFMANU4935)

16-page Pride BOI for the **newVSI** integral controller (PG Drives), INFMANU4935/Rev C/May 2022. No Pride model on the 2025–2026 end-to-end charts lists it, so it's likely on older chairs.

Covers: keypad (on/off, horn, speed/profile keys), 5-segment battery meter, 2-pin battery and 4-pin motor/brake connectors, lock/unlock, sleep (meter blinks every 2.5 s; **press on/off twice** to restore), **thermal rollback that cuts current progressively and stops the chair at the limit**, flash codes. Off-board charger through the 3-pin socket: **12 A max, Neutrik NC3MX only**; self-tests run 100 times a second.
