# Controller Locking Instructions (INFINFB2311)

1-page quick reference, Rev B, May 2009: how each Pride controller
shows it's locked, and how to lock and unlock it.

| Controller | Locked looks like | Unlock | Lock |
|---|---|---|---|
| **VSI** | Profile LEDs scroll left to right | Joystick forward to a beep, back to a beep | Power on, hold on/off to a beep, release; forward to beep, back to beep (long beep) |
| **GC / GC2** | Battery LEDs scroll left to right | Forward to beep, back to beep | Same as VSI |
| **VR2** | Profile LEDs scroll right to left | Forward to beep, back to beep (long beep) | Same as VSI |
| **Dynamic** | Battery LEDs flash in sequence | **Horn twice** within about 10 s while they scroll | Power on, hold on/off **4 s** |
| **Remote Plus** | Lock symbol flashes | Insert and remove the **3-pin lock key (KEYJAZ1001)**, or unplug the joystick cable to reset | Insert the 3-pin key |
| **NE+** | Lock symbol on LCD | Forward to beep, release to center, back to beep | Hold the **profile keys together** until it turns off |
| **Q-Logic** | "System Lock" | Forward to beep, center, back to beep | Push the **mode lever forward** until it turns off, or AUX menu (must be programmed for lock) |
| **Flight** | Battery LEDs scroll right to left | **Horn twice** before the ripple ends (about 10 s) | Hold on/off **2 s** |
