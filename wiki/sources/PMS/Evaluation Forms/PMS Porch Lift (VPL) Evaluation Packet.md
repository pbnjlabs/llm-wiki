# PMS Porch Lift (VPL) Evaluation Packet

PMS's **porch lift (vertical platform lift) evaluation** packet. Four
scanned pages, blank, undated. House practice. Summarized on
[[Porch-Lift-Evaluation]]. PMS's phone and fax numbers are left out.

## Page 1: Porch Lift Evaluation

- **Payer:** VA / **DSN/OIDD** / private pay/other
- Date of evaluation; veteran/client name, phone, address (customer
  details; never copied into the wiki)
- **User:** age, weight, height
- **Mobility device:** manual / power wheelchair / scooter, with
  **width, height, make, model**
- **Owned or rented?** If rented, landlord address, phone and fax, to
  get **written approval** (owner approval form attached).
- **HOA or mobile home park office approval needed?** (Permissions form
  attached.)
- Signatures: evaluator (print and sign) and date;
  client/veteran/caregiver, with the relationship if not the client.

## Page 2: site measurements

**Evaluation location:** front / rear / side / garage.

Entry diagram measurements (same A–J letters as the ramp form):

| | Measurement |
|---|---|
| A | Width of steps (**between handrails**, if any) |
| B | Depth of steps |
| C | Rise of steps |
| D | Width of porch (if it differs from the steps) |
| E | Rise of porch (if it differs from the steps) |
| F | Depth of porch |
| G | Width of door (**usable space**) |
| H | Rise of exterior threshold (**threshold ramp needed?**) |
| I | Rise of interior threshold (threshold ramp needed?) |
| J | Depth of threshold |

Site checks, with PMS's limits:

- **Surface type** of the porch/deck.
- **Does the lift height clear garage doors?** Record the garage door to
  ceiling height. **Watch all headroom heights from the entry door to the
  VPL.**
- **Door direction** (in/out, left/right), **including any screen or
  glass door**.
- **Concrete pad:** L × W × depth, or N/A if one must be poured.
  **Minimum 5 ft × 5 ft × 4 in.**
- **Nearest GFI outlet:** distance from the door, or N/A if one must be
  installed. **Max 10 ft from the VPL.** **Test the outlet.**
- **Bottom call box:** mounting location, **max 10 ft from the VPL**.
- **Interlock at the door:** only for **VPLs installed directly at the
  door**.
- **Head clearance: 100 in**, or **120 in for a 72 in VPL** (53 in rise
  platform / 77 in tower).
- **Gate hinge side**, while standing in the lift: left or right.

## Page 3: Evaluation Site Sketch

- Job name, date; blank sketch area; veteran/client and evaluator sign.
- **Note obstacles:** driveways, sidewalks, trees, shrubs, electric,
  water, fences, door widths, distance to doors, HVAC vents, electrical
  outlets and other problem areas.
- **Total rise: ground to the bottom of the door threshold.**
- **Pictures: the VA requires pictures of all entry doors into the
  house**, plus a full view of the front of the home and site,
  sidewalks, parking/driveway and possible problem areas.

## Page 4: HISA customer agreement

Client/veteran and PMS sign. By signing, the client agrees that:

- They've examined the configuration sketch and location of the
  proposed VPL or other lifting equipment.
- **PMS is their chosen contractor** for the work in the **HISA** price
  presented to the VA. The work can include pressure-treated
  wood/deck/rails/steps, iron gates, a concrete pad or sidewalk and the
  VPL.
- PMS gives the VA all project information and their preferences.
- **The VA pays PMS up to the veteran's maximum HISA benefit. The
  veteran pays PMS anything above it**; no payments over the benefit are
  made by the veteran without prior approval.
- The VA has the final say on what's spent; the VA takes no
  responsibility for the work, materials or equipment.
- **PMS is the company to call for maintenance or repair.**

See [[VA Form 10-0103 HISA Application]].
