# PMS Stair Lift Evaluation Form (Rev Mar 2024)

PMS's own **stair lift evaluation form**, "Rev MAR 2024". Six scanned
pages, blank. House practice. Summarized with the PMS photo guidance on
[[Stairlift-Evaluation]].

## Page 1: job details

- **Evaluation requested by:** private pay / VA medical center / other
- User name, property address, email ("please write clearly"), phone
  (customer details; never copied into the wiki)
- **User height and weight**
- Eval date

## Page 2: stair type and user measurements

- Stair type: **straight**, **curves and spirals**, or **straight with a
  flat or sloped landing**
- **Indoor / outdoor**
- **Required user measurements** (seated):
  1. **Seat to head**
  2. **Back to knee**
  3. **Seat width, hip to hip**

  **Feasibility rule (PMS):** add **back to knee + hip to hip**. That
  total must **fit within the stair width while the user is seated on the
  lift**. If it doesn't fit, it's a **"no bid."** The same check decides
  **whether the handrail has to be removed** (per the human).
  
  The **29 in** figure is the limit for a **new-construction standard
  stairway, 34 in wide**: a total over 29 in won't fit on a standard 34 in
  stair. On a wider or narrower stair, compare against that stair's
  width.

## Page 3: straight stair lift measurements

- **Rail position: decided by management** when the eval is reviewed.
- Stair width, stair height (rise), stair run (front to back of step)
- **Electric outlet: must be within 4 ft of the stairs** (top or
  bottom). If not, **an electrician is required**.
- **Handrail to be removed?** If not, how many inches from the wall.
- Ceiling bulkhead height (if any)
- Bottom landing L × W; **distance to the nearest obstruction at the
  bottom** (e.g. a door opening or frame)
- Top landing L × W; distance to the nearest obstruction at the top
- **Track length: stand at the top and let the tape measure feed to the
  floor.** Write it down.
- Number of stairs
- **Folding track:** only if the track at the bottom would cause an
  obstruction or there isn't enough space. **Straight rail stair lifts
  only.**

## Page 4: seat options and multiple flights

- **Seat** (required): standard or **power swivel**. **The power swivel
  won't work with the larger seat.**
- **Seat size:** standard **18–19 in wide** or larger **24 in wide**.
- **Adjustable seat bracket** (yes/no, how many): for narrow staircases
  and taller users, to gain clearance.
- **Multiple straight flights:** width of each flight and number of
  stairs per flight (up to 4), plus the total.

## Page 5: curved stair lift measurements

- **Rail position: decided by management.**
- Stair height, stair run
- **Width of each flight** (required, up to 3)
- Handrail to be removed? Inches from the wall
- Electric outlet within 4 ft of the stairs, or electrician required
- Number of stairs per flight
- Ceiling bulkhead height
- Top landing L × **W across the top of the stairs**; distance to
  obstruction at the top
- Bottom landing L × **W across the bottom of the stairs**; distance to
  obstruction at the bottom
- **Camera pics taken?** (see [[Staircase-Measuring-System]])

## Page 6: rail options (curved)

- **Top overrun: 24 in minimum**, or none
- **Bottom overrun: 36 in minimum**, or none
- **90° park** or **180° park**, at the top and/or bottom, or none
- Landing measurements: bottom, **mid**, top (L × W)
- Additional comments
