# PMS FSS Price List 65 II A (2021-2026)

**Supply's own** (Performance Medical Supply's) Authorized Federal Supply
Schedule price list for the VA: Medical Equipment and Supplies, **FSC
Group 65, Part II, Section A (65 II A)**, FSC Classes 6510, 6515, 6530,
6532, 6540, 6545. **Contract 36F79721D0084.** The file name says
"UPDATED ... Eff 11.01.2025", but the list itself gives the contract
period as **April 15, 2021 to April 14, 2026**, so it may predate the
renewal. **Open:** whether the renewed 65 II A contract kept the same
number. Handed over by the human 2026-10-28.

**Prices are left out of the wiki on purpose (per the human)**: they're
in PMS's QuickBooks and the file itself. So are personal email
addresses, staff names and the DUNS number.

Awarded SIN: **A-76**, lifts, patient equipment for hospital and home
use. All Harmar.

## Terms (non-price)

- Maximum order $50,000 (SIN A-76); no minimum. All 50 states and DC.
  Point of production: Sarasota, FL.
- **Quantity discount:** an extra 3% on invoiced orders of 3 or more
  items to the same location on the same order.
- Payment **2% 20, net 30** (invoiced orders only). Purchase cards
  accepted at all dollar amounts.
- Delivery **14 days ARO**; expedited **8 days ARO**, government pays
  the difference for overnight or 2-day, waived for presidentially
  declared emergencies. FOB destination in the 48 states and DC; point
  of exportation for AK, HI and PR.
- Installation terms: "Not Applicable" on the form, although every
  item line includes installation.
- **Warranty (effective 01/01/2021):** the same wording as the 65 II F
  list: equipment and installation free of defects for **12 months from
  acceptance**, FAR 52.212-4 (o) and (p), replace-and-ship as the only
  remedy.
- **Returns (effective 01/01/2021):** full refund within **60 days**
  of inspection and acceptance, original packaging where possible,
  **RMA first**, **10% restocking fee**. Not accepted: no serial or
  UPC, **special configuration orders such as curved stairlifts**, more
  than 60 days after acceptance, or no notice. PMS's shipping error: PMS
  pays freight both ways, no restocking fee. Customer's error: 10% plus
  return freight, customer bears the risk of loss. Returns ship to
  PMS's North Augusta office.
