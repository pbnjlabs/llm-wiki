# Delivery Ticket and Invoice Examples (PMS)

PMS's **annotated examples** for filling in delivery tickets and
invoices, with handwritten instructions. Two PDFs, 4 pages. The invoices
are real past jobs (2022) with customer details blacked out.

**Left out on purpose:** customer names and addresses, veteran ID, PA
name, PO and invoice numbers, equipment serial numbers, and all phone
numbers (including PMS's office list and the 24/7 service line printed
on the ticket).

Blank copies of both tickets, plus the Rights & Responsibilities notice
and the communication log, are on [[PMS Delivery Paperwork (Blank Forms)]].

## Delivery tickets (`delivery_ticket_ex.pdf`)

Two kinds. **Use the right one for the payer.**

### VA Delivery Ticket / Plan of Care (VA jobs)

- **Top corner:** tech's name, date of the job, **morning or
  afternoon**.
- **Patient:** veteran's **LAST NAME, FIRST NAME**; date of the job.
- **Address: must be a physical address.**
- **Equipment delivered:** what the job was, with the **serial number
  (S/N)**:
  - Eval SL, VL, CL or VPL for repairs
  - Repair SL, VL, CL or VPL
  - Install: whatever item is being installed

  (SL = stairlift, VL = vehicle lift, CL = ceiling lift, VPL = vertical
  platform lift.)
- Patient status; yes/no checks (patient/caregiver can use the
  equipment, needs more education, PMS phone number visible,
  environment suitable, home safety checklist done); hazards; problems;
  plan.
- **Patient/Family Input: only the patient or family writes here.**
- The ticket certifies the safe-use instructions were explained and
  gives PMS's 24/7 equipment-failure number.
- Technician and patient/caregiver sign, both dated **the date of the
  job**.

### Home Medical Equipment ticket (private pay or DSN only)

- **This ticket is for private pay or DSN only.**
- Top corner: tech's name, date of the job, morning or afternoon.
- Name: LAST NAME, FIRST NAME. Date of the job.
- **Description:** eval/repair of the equipment, with its **S/N**. **If
  delivering a loaner, include the loaner's S/N too.**
- **Assessment fee: $85.00 for private pay**, **more if the job is
  farther from the shop**.
- **Private pay parts: taxed**, and priced at **part cost ÷ 0.65 = what
  PMS charges**.
- Co-insurance and insurance-allowable lines: N/A.
- Patient or guardian signs; **the tech signs as witness**.

## Invoices (`invoice_ex.pdf`)

### Private pay sale (2022 example: Pride lift chair)

- Note: **Private Pay**. PO: "PRIVATE PAY". Terms: **due on receipt**.
- Line: MISC "Private pay sale of Pride lift chair", then the item with
  model, fabric/color, serial and SKU (e.g. LC-358 medium).
- **"All sales of lift chairs are final - no refunds or exchanges of
  lift chairs."**
- **SC sales tax 6.0%** charged.

### VA repair: converting an estimate to an invoice (2022 example: Pride J6)

Marked "for reference".

- **Bill to: Dorn VA Hospital** (Columbia SC). A partly legible
  handwritten note next to it is left out, as agreed.
- Header: Veteran ID, VA P.O. number, technician initials, PA, serial,
  equipment (e.g. PWC).
- **Serial number:** include the letter prefix (e.g. **JE** on a Pride
  serial). **Harmar serials: no letter prefix** (confirmed by the
  human).
- **When converting the estimate: remove the "evaluate" part** of the
  description, so the invoice says what was repaired.
- Example lines: MISC repair description ("Repair Pride J6 PWC … batteries
  were bad, replaced batteries …"); **MU-1** U1 sealed gel 12 V batteries
  × 2 at $149.99; Labor 0.5 hr at $50.00; **Zone 12** (Richland County SC)
  $247.00.
- No sales tax line on the VA example.
