# PMS FSS Price List 65 II F (2025-2030)

**Supply's own** (Performance Medical Supply's) Authorized Federal Supply
Schedule price list for the VA: Patient Mobility Devices, **FSC Group 65,
Part II, Section F (65 II F)**, FSC Class 6530. **Contract
36F79721D0058**, contract period **11/15/2025 – 11/30/2030**: the renewed
5-year term. Undated Word file; the warranty and returns text is
effective **July 15, 2025**. Handed over by the human 2026-10-28.

This is PMS's list, not a manufacturer's. It replaces Harmar's own list
([[Harmar VA FSS Price List (2018-2023)]]) as the check for what Supply
actually has on contract. **Prices are left out of the wiki on purpose
(per the human)**: they're in PMS's QuickBooks and the file itself. So
are personal email addresses, staff names and the DUNS number.

Awarded SINs: **F-7** transport (vehicle) lifts and **F-8C** modular
ramps.

## Terms (non-price)

- Maximum order $100,000 (SIN F-7); no minimum. All 50 states and DC.
- Points of production: Sarasota, FL and Morganfield, KY.
- No quantity discounts; **net 30**. Purchase cards accepted at and
  above the micro-purchase threshold.
- Delivery **14 days ARO** (UPS or other carrier); expedited **8 days
  ARO**, installation not included in that time frame, no extra freight
  charge. FOB destination in the 48 states and DC; point of exportation
  for AK, HI and PR.
- **Installation included.** No repair-parts, maintenance or service
  terms.
- **Warranty (PMS to the purchasing agency, FAR 52.212-4 (o) and
  (p)):** equipment **and installation** free of defects for **12
  months from acceptance**. Void for use against instructions,
  unauthorized repairs or alterations, abuse, misuse, poor maintenance,
  accident or someone else's negligence. PMS's only obligation on a
  breach: replace the defective item and pay its shipping. No liability
  for consequential damages. Warranty matters go to PMS's main office
  (phone and email on the list).
- **Returns:** full refund within 60 days of inspection and acceptance,
  original packaging with serial number or UPC, **RMA first**. **10%
  restocking fee.** Not accepted: no serial or UPC, special orders,
  returned without notice, or "more than 30 days after delivery"
  (**conflicts with the 60-day window** a few lines up; the 65 II A
  list says 60 days in both places). PMS's shipping error: PMS pays
  freight both ways, no restocking fee. Ordering facility's error: it
  pays return freight plus 10%. Shipping damage: PMS files the carrier
  claim.

These are **PMS's terms to the VA**, separate from the manufacturer's
own warranty on the product. Harmar's own FSS list gives 3 years
including batteries, paint and covers plus 1 year labor, but that's on
Harmar's contract (see [[Harmar-Warranty]]).
