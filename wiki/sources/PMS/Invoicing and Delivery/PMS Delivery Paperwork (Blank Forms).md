# PMS Delivery Paperwork (Blank Forms)

Four blank one-page PMS forms from `Admin/Paperwork/Misc. Tickets`,
undated. How to fill in the two tickets is on
[[Delivery Ticket and Invoice Examples]]. Phone and fax numbers are left
out, including the 24/7 service line and the Joint Commission number.

## VA Delivery Ticket / Plan of Care (`va_ticket.pdf`)

The blank VA ticket. Patient, date, address, telephone; equipment
delivered and serial numbers; patient status (disoriented, oppositional,
non-compliant, awake and alert); comments; five yes/no checks (proper
use shown, needs more education, PMS phone number visible, environment
suitable, home safety checklist done); hazards; patient/family input;
problems; plan. The patient certifies they were shown safe use, given
PMS's 24/7 equipment-failure number, and offered PMS's Notice of Privacy
Policies. Technician and patient/caregiver sign and date.

## Home Medical Equipment ticket (`other_ticket.pdf`)

The blank private pay/DSN ticket. Checkboxes at the top: **home
environment safe**, **equipment suits patient's needs**,
**rights/responsibilities** (see below), **safety information**. Date,
name, address, phone; sales/rental lines (code, description, rental or
purchase, price); sale and monthly rental totals with the amounts billed
by PMS, expected co-insurance and insurance allowable. The patient
acknowledges the costs and their share, and that they'll be billed if
insurance doesn't pay on time. Patient or guardian and a witness sign.

## Communication Log (`comm_form.pdf`)

A lined log for one customer (name, phone, address at the top), with a
narrow right-hand column. The form doesn't label the columns.

## Rights & Responsibilities (`rights_resp.pdf`)

The client notice behind the ticket's "rights/responsibilities" box. The
client signs and dates it.

**Client rights:**

- Personal service with concern, respect and dignity; included in their
  plan of service.
- No denial of equipment or supplies because of race, religion, color,
  national origin, sex, age, handicap, marital status or **source of
  payment**.
- Can communicate in their native language.
- **Knows the services, equipment and charges before service.**
- **Records are confidential**; released only with written permission or
  a court order; available to the client on written request.
- Questions answered; informed of rights and obligations before service
  starts; manages their own finances; helped to exercise their rights.
- Can voice grievances and suggest changes **without reprisal**.
- Can request an **itemized statement** of items and services.
- The public can report concerns to the **Joint Commission's Office of
  Quality Monitoring**.
- **Advance directives** (living wills, durable power of attorney) are
  respected and followed once PMS is told about them.

**Client responsibilities:**

- Tell PMS about any **change in physician or insurance**.
- Follow their physician's orders.
- Tell PMS about any **hospital or primary care facility admission**
  while on PMS's services.
