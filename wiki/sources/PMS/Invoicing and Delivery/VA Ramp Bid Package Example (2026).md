# VA Ramp Bid Package Example (2026)

A **real, complete VA ramp bid package**, 3 pages, dated 29–30 Sep 2026.
It shows three of the four pieces PMS sends for a VA modular ramp bid.
The fourth, **site pictures taken during the eval**, is part of every
bid package but isn't in this file (per the human). Customer details are partly blacked out on the scan.

**Left out on purpose:** the customer's and evaluator's names, address,
phone numbers, the estimate number and the project reference number.

## 1. Part B drawing (filled in on site)

[[PMS Ramp Assessment and Approval Forms (Part B)]], filled in by hand:

- Evaluation date 29 Sep 2026; **total rise 31 in**; **rear**
  entrance; **threshold 1: ¾ in**; mobility device **MWC** (manual
  wheelchair); door swing in; lower landing surface **concrete**.
- Sketch on the 1 ft grid: the porch and steps, an **A/C unit** next to
  the porch, **"PARK"** marking the parking side, a porch dimension of
  **14 ft × 12½ ft**, the ramp route (**25 ft** run along the house),
  and **two 5 × 5 platforms** at the turn.
- Signed by the homeowner (the veteran's spouse) the same day.

## 2. EZ-ACCESS layout printout

The layout drawn in EZ-ACCESS's design tool (company EZ-ACCESS, "Project
Name / Ref No." = customer's last name):

- **Project information:** surface **SOLID**; handrail **TWO_LINE**;
  ramp width **36 in**; **linear feet 41 ft**.
- Layout: from the porch, **upper transition (PRUT)** → **3×6** ramp →
  **5×5 platform (PPS55TL)** → **5×5 platform (PPS55TL)** (180° turn) →
  **3×4** → **3×5** → **3×8** → **3×8** → **lower transition (PRLT)**
  toward parking.
- Ramp sections add up to **31 ft for 31 in of rise**, the 1:12 house
  standard.
- Comments box, handwritten: **"Mod time goes here."**

Patterns worth copying:

- A **MISC line** describing the job, then the **GSA FSS contract line**
  at $0, then parts, then an **FSS discount line**.
- **No labor line.** Labor is **included in PMS's markup**. PMS adds a
  labor line **only** for extra work such as **porch modification** or
  **debris clean-up** (per the human).
- **No zone charge** appears on this estimate (compare the VA repair and
  lift invoices on [[Delivery Ticket and Invoice Examples]] and
  [[Harmar Orders and Installs Workflow]]).
- The **PATHWAY 3G "P" part codes** used for ordering (PPS55, PRSTL8,
  PRUT, PRLT, PSL…PR and so on) are listed on [[PATHWAY-3G]].
