# Asheville FSS Sales Guidelines (2025)

"**Guidelines for Asheville FSS sales on Performance Medical Supply
schedule**." Two scanned pages, handwritten "March 2025" and "per" a VA
contact. How the **Asheville office** sells to the **Asheville VA
hospital** under PMS's **FSS contract**, using PMS's Columbia office for
the fax, payments and books.

Two companies are involved: **Performance Medical Supply ("Supply")**,
which holds the FSS schedule, and its **sister company, Performance
Medical Solutions ("Solutions")**, which runs the Asheville office
(confirmed by the human).

**Left out on purpose:** staff and VA contact names and the fax number.
Staff are described by role.

## Flow

**1. RFQs.** The Asheville VA emails RFQs to
**harmarlifts@performancemedicalsupply.com** or faxes the Columbia
office. The Columbia fax isn't dependable, but the Asheville VA prefers
to fax; it's OK to tell Asheville purchasing agents to fax the
**Asheville office** instead.

**2. Estimates.** Asheville staff log into **Supply QuickBooks**,
estimate the lift, save it there, and **email all estimates through
harmarlifts@performancemedicalsupply.com**.

**3. Receiving orders.** The Asheville VA faxes POs to Columbia (PMS
will try to tell them email is encrypted and secure). **Columbia scans
and emails them to harmarlifts@**.

**4. Invoicing.** Asheville staff **convert the estimate into an invoice
in Supply QuickBooks**.

**5. Ordering.** Asheville staff **order the lift on the Solutions Harmar
account**, ship it to the Asheville location, receive it and install
it.

**6. Charging the PO.** The Asheville VA emails the completed paperwork
to **PMS@performancemedicalsupply.com**. Columbia takes payment **by
credit card**, posts it in Supply QuickBooks, and emails the receipt and
invoice back to Asheville (asheville@performancemedsolutions.com and/or
harmarlifts@). Asheville forwards receipts and completed paperwork to
the Asheville purchasing agent.

**7. Monthly reconciliation.** On the last day of the month, Supply runs
a **"sales by item detail" report, class Asheville**. That total counts
toward Asheville's commission and monthly sales.

- **Total Asheville sales × 0.96** = what Supply owes Solutions. The
  **0.96** covers the **Visa charges** Supply pays and the **FSS IFF
  fee** Supply pays quarterly. (Handwritten: "IFF fee 1%, Visa".)
- Example: $22,452.00 × 0.96 = **$21,553.92**.
- Solutions invoices Supply for that amount; Supply pays Solutions by
  check, deposited to the Solutions account.
