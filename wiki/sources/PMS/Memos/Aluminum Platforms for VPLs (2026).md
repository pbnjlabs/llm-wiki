# Aluminum Platforms for VPLs (2026)

A PMS email to a VA contact, **7 Oct 2026**, subject "Aluminum
Platforms for VPLs", answering the VA's question about using **aluminum
(modular) platforms** as the landing for a porch lift (VPL). Two
scanned pages. Sent from PMS Supply's North Augusta office, copied to
PMS Columbia. **PMS house practice.**

**Left out on purpose:** the sender's and VA contact's names and email
addresses.

## PMS's position

**Aluminum platforms can work with a VPL in some cases, but not always**:
some setups won't meet building code or ADA. PMS is happy to use them
"on some of the VPLs".

## When it doesn't work

1. **60 × 60 in is sometimes too small** for the upper platform to get
   the chair through the front door:
   - there's a **storm door**,
   - the **power chair is large**,
   - the **access is adjacent** (the lift exits to the side).
2. **Platform 30 in or higher:** the **standard aluminum platform rails
   don't meet building code**. It needs **vertical pickets spaced 3.5
   in**, which means a **commercial aluminum railing system**. The memo
   shows a horizontal two-rail platform ("can't be used at 30 inches or
   higher") and a vertical-picket platform ("can be used 30 inches or
   higher").
3. **Aluminum steps on a 5 × 5 aluminum platform** are often hard to add,
   because they **leave a gap where the chair could roll off the
   platform**.
