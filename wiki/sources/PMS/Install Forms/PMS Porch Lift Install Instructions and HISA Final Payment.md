# PMS Porch Lift Install Instructions and HISA Final Payment

Two scanned pages, blank, undated, used at the end of a porch lift
(VPL) install. House practice. PMS's phone and fax numbers are left out.

## Page 1: Instructions for Bruno and Harmar Porch Lifts

The customer initials each line to confirm they were shown it. Header:
date and **lift serial #**.

- **Safety platform:** it shuts the lift off when it hits an
  obstruction.
- **One passenger and one wheelchair** only. **Passenger plus wheelchair
  must not exceed 600 lb.** (The Bruno VPL-3100B/3200B and Harmar
  Highlander II are rated 750 lb; see [[VPL-3100B]], [[VPL-3200B]],
  [[HighlanderII]]. The form gives the customer a lower 600 lb figure.)
- **Key switch** is near the up/down paddles; shown how to use it.
- Shown how to use the **emergency down crank** (hand crank tool).
- **Check the GFI monthly and after a storm.**
- **Battery (DC) unit:** told how the lift charges. **Electric (AC)
  unit:** told it **only works when the power is on**.
- Given the **manufacturer's owner's manual**.
- **PMS annual preventive maintenance program** for Bruno and Harmar
  lifts: does the customer want prices and program info in a year? Y/N.
- If they chose AC: "I understand that battery operated unit is an
  option I refused."
- **Liability:** PMS isn't liable for damages from misuse, neglect or
  faulty operation; the customer was trained, read the owner's manual,
  and operates the lift at their own risk.
- Customer and staff signatures and dates.

## Page 2: HISA Statement of Satisfaction and Request for Final Payment

For **VA HISA jobs** ([[VA Form 10-0103 HISA Application]]).

- **Veteran's statement:** the approved alterations were made under
  Public Law 104-205, the work is complete, and they're satisfied. The
  VA takes no responsibility for maintenance, repair or replacement and
  gives no warranty.
- **Request for final payment** for the approved amount. **Attach:**
  1. the **final invoice** with actual costs for materials, labor,
     permits and inspections
  2. **color photographs of the completed work**
- Veteran (or designee) signs and dates. The form also asks for the
  veteran's SSN (never copied into the wiki).
- **Vendor statement of compliance:** the PMS installer states the work
  was provided and installed per the **Uniform Federal Accessibility
  Standards (UFAS), July 1991**, applicable UFAS supplements, and the
  **current Southern Building Code**. Contractor signs and dates.
