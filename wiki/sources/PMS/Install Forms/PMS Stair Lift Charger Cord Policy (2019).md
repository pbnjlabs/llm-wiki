# PMS Stair Lift Charger Cord Policy (2019)

"Stair Lift Installation Policy: Charger Cords", **effective 13 Sep
2019**. 3-page scan.

## The rule

- **Stair lift battery charger wires/cords must be covered by a cord
  cover.**
- The cover is the **Legrand 9-Piece 3.75-in White Cord/Cable
  Organization Kit** (CordMate **C110**), **Lowe's item # 65449**.
- **Every office that does stair lift installs must keep it in stock.**

## What the pages show

- **Page 1:** before/after/painted photos. The cord runs from the
  outlet along the wall in the channel, and the channel can be painted
  to match the wall.
- **Page 2:** two install examples. The cord drops straight down from
  the outlet in channel, then runs along the top of the baseboard.
- **Page 3:** CordMate install steps and parts list.
  - Tools: tape measure and a PVC cutter or fine-tooth hacksaw.
  - Steps: cut the channel to size, lay in the wire and snap it closed,
    peel off the tape backing and stick it to the wall, add fittings as
    needed.
  - Kit C110: 1 outside elbow, 1 inside elbow, 2 couplings, 2 flat
    elbows, 3 channels (3 ft each, 9/16 in wide × 7/16 in deep).
