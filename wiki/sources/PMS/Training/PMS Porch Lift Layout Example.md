# PMS Porch Lift Layout Example

A real porch lift (VPL) job layout, kept as a training example. 2-page
scan, undated. Lift model and manufacturer aren't given. **Customer
details left out on purpose.**

## Page 1: hand-drawn site plan

The kind of sketch [[Porch-Lift-Evaluation]] asks for. It shows:

- Street parking, the driveway, and a curved sidewalk to the porch
  steps.
- The **front of the home**, porch, screen room, and a **threshold
  ramp** at the entry door.
- A new **5 × 12 ft concrete pad** off the sidewalk, beside the porch.
- The **VPL** on the pad, with its **tower** on the side next to the
  house.
- A **gate** at the porch edge, with its swing drawn.
- A **post-mount call/send** station at the edge of the pad.
- **"Install GFI"** marked on the house wall next to the tower.

## Page 2: marked-up photo

A photo of the front of the home (it already has an aluminum ramp to the
porch). The lift and pad location is drawn on in marker, with an arrow
to the pad and lines for the path from the yard.

## Takeaways

- A layout should show the **pad size**, **lift and tower position**,
  **gate**, **call/send station**, **GFI location**, and how the user
  gets from parking to the lift.
- Pair the sketch with a marked-up photo of the site.
