## Fitting points by part

- **Knee walker:** back straight, good handlebar height, knee resting
  comfortably on the pad.
- **Cane:** measure from the floor to the **lowest point on top of the
  handle**. The diagram shows the handle level with the wrist, arm
  hanging relaxed, with "too tall" and "too short" examples.
- **Crutches:** underarm supports about **5 cm below the armpit**;
  handgrip adjusted for a **slight bend at the elbow**; length set with
  the adjusting bolts and wing nuts. The handgrip height is measured from
  the floor to the **wrist joint**, with the user standing in their best
  posture.
- **Walker:** fit like a cane. Place the walker **10–12 in in front of
  the patient**. Standing, set the height **level with the greater
  trochanter** (hip bone). That gives **20–30° of elbow flexion** with the
  shoulders relaxed. Weight goes on the heels of the hands with the
  elbows partly bent. Avoid leaning the trunk forward.
- **Rollator:** handle height **level with the wrists**.
