# Ordering and Returning Parts (PMS)

Three scanned pages of PMS office procedure, undated: how to order
parts, the repair evaluation form, and how to return parts. Not tied to
one manufacturer, so filed under a `PMS` folder.

## Ordering parts (p. 1, handwritten)

1. **Call the vendor.**
2. Ask for the **quote to be converted into an order**.
3. Give the **quote #** and **account #** (if asked).
4. Ask for an **email confirmation**.
5. **The PO is the veteran's last name.**
6. **Write the confirmation # on the quote paper.**

## Repair Eval Information form (p. 2)

The form techs fill in on a repair evaluation, for the office:

- Nature of repair
- Veteran/patient name, address, phone, email (customer details; never
  copied into the wiki)
- Equipment info, **photos taken** (checkbox), **serial number**,
  **model**
- **Evaluation notes** ("please add additional info admin needs to
  know"):
  - Eval time, repair time
  - **Repairs done in the shop?**
  - **Two techs needed for repair?**
  - Notes
- **Replacement recommended?**
- **Newer version** (`MANUALS/Admin/Paperwork/Evals/tech_eval.pdf`, same
  title) adds a **"Repair completed @ eval?"** checkbox and an email
  checkbox, and asks "Repairs need to be done in the shop?" and "Are two
  techs needed for repair?". Otherwise the same.
- Manufacturer tech/rep, quote #
- **Reminder: the PO is the user's last name, and all quotes from the
  manufacturer are emailed to PMS's quotes inbox**
  (quotes@performancemedicalsupply.com).

## Returning parts (p. 3)

**Our fault** (PMS ordered it wrong):

1. Box the item.
2. **Write the RA number on the outside of the box in at least two
   places.**
3. Take it to the freight company and **get the tracking number
   (receipt)**.
4. **Put the tracking number and RA number in the patient's file.**

**Their fault** (the manufacturer sent the wrong item):

1. Ask the manufacturer to **send a call tag**.
2. Box the item.
3. Call the freight company to pick it up.
4. **Write the RA number on the outside of the box in at least two
   places.**
5. **Copy the call tag into the patient file**; it has the tracking
   number.
6. **Copy the RA number into the patient file.**

**Why it matters:** items don't get mailed back, or PMS can't produce
the tracking number to prove they were. **No tracking number, no return
credit.**
