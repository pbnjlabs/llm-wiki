# PMS Electrical Work Orders and Preferred Electricians

How PMS orders outlet work from an outside electrician for porch lift
and stair lift installs, from two scans:

- `electrical_work_orders.pdf`: 3 example work orders, marked by hand
  "VPL Example", "VPL Electrical Work Ord Example" (15 Aug 2024) and
  "Stair Glide Elect. Req Example"
- `elecricians_preffered.pdf`: "Electricians 2024", PMS's list of
  electricians, with handwritten additions

**Left out on purpose:** customer names, addresses and phones, and PMS
staff names and phones. **Electrician names and phone numbers are
included** (per the human).

## How a work order is set up

- **Date**, **to** the electrician (company name and email).
- **Homeowner information** (name, address, phone) with a note:
  **"Electrician, please contact the homeowner for scheduling."**
- **Approval limit:** if the job goes over the limit, the electrician
  must **call PMS for approval before** doing the work.
- **Payment in full is made by PMS, not the homeowner.**
- **Work request** (below), then "Thank you" and the PMS sender.

## Porch lift (VPL) outlet

- **Approval limit: $450.00.**
- **Install a 110 V GFI outlet for the porch lift.** On the 2024 example,
  the lift was already installed and ready for the electrician.
- The outlet goes **where the homeowner can reach the reset button**.
- **Check the outlet for proper operation.**
- **Don't hide it under crawl spaces or decks.**

This matches the porch lift eval, which wants the **GFI within 10 ft of
the VPL** and tested ([[Porch-Lift-Evaluation]]).

## Stair glide (stair lift) outlet

- **Approval limit: $350.00.**
- **Install one 120 V outlet** for the stair lift. It charges the lift's
  **two 12 V batteries**.
- Outlet at **staircase #1**, with a **sketch included**.
- Say which side the lift goes on: **left or right, standing at the
  bottom of the stairs looking up**.
- **Doesn't need a dedicated circuit.**
- **Within 3–5 ft of the top or bottom of the stairs**, on the **same
  side as the lift**.
- **Accessible for the technicians** doing the install.
- **Don't hide it under crawl spaces or inside closets.**
- **Check the outlet for proper operation.**

**Techs go by the eval form: within 4 ft of the stairs**
([[Stairlift-Evaluation]]), per the human. The example work order's
"3–5 ft" is older wording; write 4 ft on new work orders.
