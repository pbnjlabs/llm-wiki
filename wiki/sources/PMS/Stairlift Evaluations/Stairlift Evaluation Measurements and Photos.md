# Stairlift Evaluation Measurements and Photos (PMS)

PMS's guidance for **stairlift evaluations**: what to measure and which
photos to take. Built from two undated PMS documents:

- `Important Stairlift Eval Items.pdf`: 1 page, a photo of a staircase
  marked with the five measurements
- `Evaluation Do's & Don'ts.pdf`: 3 pages of example photos, good and
  bad

House practice. For Harmar's camera-based measuring, see
[[Staircase-Measuring-System]].

## Measurements (Important Stairlift Eval Items)

1. **Landing width**, side to side.
2. **Landing depth**, top to bottom (front to back).
3. **Every stair's width.** If they differ, note each step: number them
   or make a drawing.
4. **Doorway width**, plus the **door facing (trim) width** and the
   **door width** itself.
5. **From the bottom of the last step to any doorway, obstruction,
   etc.**

## Photos

**Acceptable eval photos** (take all five):

1. Top landing
2. Entire top landing area
3. Bottom landing area
4. Bottom landing **with the stairs**
5. Entire staircase

**Not acceptable:** photos that don't show the **entire** bottom
landing, the **entire** staircase, or the top landing. The examples
are cropped, cluttered, or shot from a spot that cuts off part of the
area.

**Also shoot the areas away from the stairs**, at the top and bottom:
hallways, living rooms, kitchens and so on. These show the **space for
rails, the lift's park position, etc.**
