# PMS Grab Bar Waiver of Liability

One page, blank, undated. The customer signs it before PMS installs
grab bars.

The customer confirms they understand that **PMS isn't responsible for
cracked or damaged tile, or damage to fiberglass surfaces**, now or in
the future, from installing grab bars. PMS staff do their best to
prevent damage, but **some damage may occur because of the age and type
of surfaces in some bathrooms**.

Customer signs and dates; the technician signs and dates.

The waiver mentions fiberglass, but PMS's current practice is **not to
install in fiberglass surrounds** ([[Grab-Bar-Installation]]). The
fiberglass wording may be older than that rule.
