# PMS Grab Bar Quote Response (VA, 2026)

**PMS's reply to the VA grab bar request for quote**
([[RFQ - Bathroom Grab Bars (VA, 2026)]]), dated **3 June 2026**. Two
scanned pages. It's PMS's own statement of how it installs grab bars,
so it's house practice. Install methods are collected on
[[Grab-Bar-Installation]].

**Left out on purpose:** PMS's phone and fax numbers, and the staff
member's name and email.

## What PMS offers

| Item | PMS answer |
|---|---|
| Locations | Shower and toilet |
| Grab bars in tile | **Yes** |
| Grab bars in wood/drywall | **Yes** |
| Grab bars in fiberglass surrounds | **No** |
| Labor warranty | 2 years |
| Load | 350 lb |

The RFQ asked for **ceramic tile and fiberglass**. PMS does tile and
drywall but **not fiberglass**.

## Deliverables and answers to the RFQ

- **COI** attached.
- **References:** VA medical centers in Fayetteville, Salisbury NC and
  Richmond VA.
- **Ceramic tile:** tape on the wall, glass drill bits, seal the holes
  before installing.
- **Drywall:** blocking behind the wall when necessary.
- **Fiberglass surrounds:** no.
- **Timeline:** 1 hour per grab bar; delivery within 5 business days
  unless the patient has scheduling issues.
- **Payment:** government credit card accepted.
- **Material warranty:** the grab bar manufacturer's warranty, if the
  bar was bought from PMS.
- **Service area:** within **90 miles one way of the Columbia SC VA
  hospital**.
- **Mounting in ceramic tile:** locate studs or use wall anchors; tape
  the wall; tile/glass drill bit, wet if required; **avoid the hammer
  drill**; seal the holes before installing.
- **Blocking and reinforcement:** don't install in fiberglass with a
  void behind it; install directly into studs; use heavy-duty anchors if
  studs can't be found; **a bar over 24 in with no studs needs blocking
  behind the drywall**.
- **Lead time:** 5 business days.
- **Warranty contact:** PMS leaves a receipt and delivery ticket with
  its 800 number, address and office location.
