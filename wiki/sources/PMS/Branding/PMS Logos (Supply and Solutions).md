# PMS Logos (Supply and Solutions)

The logos of PMS's two companies (see [[VA-FSS-Contracts]] for which one
does what). Handed over by the human 2026-10-28 as a `PMS Branding`
folder and kept under `MANUALS/Admin/PMS Branding/`. Both are black on
white, and both turn one letter "o" into a wheelchair user.

| Company | File | Look | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Supply** (Performance Medical Supply Inc.) | `Performance Medical-logo.jpg` | Bold italic sans "**Performance**", the "o" of "for" drawn as a wheelchair user; script "*Medical Supply Inc.*" below | 400 × 132 px, grayscale, tight crop. Saved from Photoshop CS3, 2016-05-12 |
| **Solutions** (Performance Medical Solutions) | `Performance-Medical-Solutions LOGO.jpg` | Serif "Performance Medical" over a large italic serif "***Solutions***", the first "o" drawn as a wheelchair user | 476 × 437 px, RGB but black and gray only. The logo is only about 312 × 104 px of it; the rest is white margin |

**Use with care:** both files are small. They're fine on screen, in
email signatures and on forms at about 1–1.5 in wide, but they'll look
soft any larger or in print. There's no vector (SVG, EPS, AI) or
transparent-background version on file; ask the human if one is needed.
The Solutions file needs cropping before use.

No colors, fonts or usage rules came with them. The 2016 date on the
Supply file is when it was last saved, not when the logo was designed.
