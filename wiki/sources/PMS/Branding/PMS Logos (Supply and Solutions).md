# PMS Logos (Supply and Solutions)

The logos of PMS's two companies (see [[VA-FSS-Contracts]] for which one
does what). Handed over by the human 2026-10-28 as a `PMS Branding`
folder and kept under `MANUALS/Admin/PMS Branding/`. Both are black on
white, and both turn one letter "o" into a wheelchair user.

| Company | File | Look | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Supply** (Performance Medical Supply Inc.) | `Performance Medical-logo.jpg` | Bold italic sans "**Performance**", the "o" of "for" drawn as a wheelchair user; script "*Medical Supply Inc.*" below | 400 × 132 px, grayscale, tight crop. Saved from Photoshop CS3, 2016-05-12 |
| **Solutions** (Performance Medical Solutions) | `Performance-Medical-Solutions LOGO.jpg` | Serif "Performance Medical" over a large italic serif "***Solutions***", the first "o" drawn as a wheelchair user | 476 × 437 px, RGB but black and gray only. The logo is only about 312 × 104 px of it; the rest is white margin |

**Use with care:** both files are small. They're fine on screen, in
email signatures and on forms at about 1–1.5 in wide, but they'll look
soft any larger or in print. There's no vector (SVG, EPS, AI) or
transparent-background version on file; ask the human if one is needed.
The Solutions file needs cropping before use.

No colors, fonts or usage rules came with them. The 2016 date on the
Supply file is when it was last saved, not when the logo was designed.

## Website colors and fonts (performancemedicalsupply.com)

Checked 2026-10-28 at the human's request. The site is WordPress on a
Bluehost-built theme (YITH Wonder child theme, "Performance Medical
Supply"). Nothing on the site states brand rules, so this is **what the
site uses**, not an official style guide.

**Logo on the site:** `logo-new.png` (uploaded 2023-12, 271 × 81 px,
transparent PNG; copied into `MANUALS/Admin/PMS Branding/`). It's the
**Supply logo in color**: "Performance" and the wheelchair figure in
black, "Medical Supply Inc." in **navy, about #0C1561** (sampled from
the image). It's the only color version on file, but it's even smaller
than the JPEG, so it's still screen-only.

| Role on the site | Color | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | **#21447B** (dark blue) | Headings, links, header text |
| Secondary | **#3764B4** (medium blue) | Accents |
| Tertiary / header background | **#C7DBFF** (pale blue) | Header band, light panels |
| Base | #FFFFFF | Page background |
| Contrast | #000000 | Body text |

- **Fonts:** headings in **Oswald** (condensed sans); body text in the
  visitor's system font (no web font).
- The logo navy (#0C1561) is darker and more violet than the site's
  primary blue (#21447B); they're close but not the same color.
- The yellow (#E8D833) and the other bright colors in the page code are
  WordPress and Bluehost plugin defaults, not PMS colors.
- **Solutions** isn't on this site; its logo is black only, with no
  colors on file.
