# 2026-10-14 — Harmar outside lift: stuck lifting a load (J-cam flat spots)

**Status: open (known issue; fix not reported yet).** PMS field
experience on Harmar vehicle lifts. The manuals don't cover it. Not tied
to one job.

> **Key takeaways (per the human):**
> 1. **The J-cam can develop flat spots over time.**
> 2. **Symptom: the lift gets stuck lifting a load. Without a load
>    there's no issue.**
> 3. **Don't confuse it with a bad actuator, or a battery that doesn't
>    have enough power to lift the load.**

## Unit

- Harmar hitch-mounted outside vehicle lift
  ([[AL-Series-Outside-Vehicle-Lifts]]). The human said "Harmar vehicle
  lifts". In the manuals on file, the J-cam appears only on the outside
  lifts: [[Outside Vehicle Lifts Owner's Manual]] Figure 4-1 (p. 13)
  shows it next to the **platform roller, where the platform meets the
  mast**, and the AL100 and AL301XL assembly drawings list it
  ([[Harmar Parts Diagrams]]). Which models the human has seen it on:
  not recorded.
- Serial number: not recorded (general note, not one job).

## Symptom

- **Gets stuck lifting a load** (mobility device on the platform).
- **No issue without a load.**

## Checked

Rule out the two look-alikes before blaming the J-cam (per the human):
- **Actuator going bad.**
- **Battery without enough power to lift the load.**

How to tell them apart from J-cam wear: not recorded yet.

## Root cause

**Flat spots worn into the J-cam over time** (per the human).

## Fix

Not reported yet.

## Parts used

None recorded. For reference only: the current AL100 and AL301XL
drawings list the J-cam as **530-00716, "J-CAM, CASTED"**, which
replaced **H11010** (AL100 in 2019; AL301XL in 2021, after switching
back and forth) ([[Harmar Parts Diagrams]]). Confirm the part for the
lift's model and serial with Harmar before ordering.

## Takeaways

1. **J-cam flat spots develop over time** (per the human).
2. **Stuck lifting a load, fine without one: think J-cam** (per the
   human).
3. **Not the same as a bad actuator or a weak battery** (per the human).

From the manuals (not field experience):
- Harmar's outside lift troubleshooting covers only power, not the
  J-cam. The motor draws up to 20 A, so a meter or test light can show
  12 V through a bad connection. Test current as well as voltage, or run
  the lift from a known-good, fully charged battery
  ([[Outside Vehicle Lifts Install Manual (2025, Rev D)]], Tech Note:
  Motor Testing and Section 7).
- The owner's manual says to oil pivot points on all moving parts every
  6 months; Figure 4-1 shows the J-cam and platform roller among them.
