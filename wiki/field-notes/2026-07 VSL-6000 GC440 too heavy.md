# 2026-07 — VSL-6000 Curb-Sider couldn't lift a 2024+ GC440 (too heavy)

**Status: resolved. PMS paid to replace the actuator with a heavier-duty
one.** PMS field experience on a Bruno [[VSL-6000-6900]] Curb-Sider with a
Golden [[GC240-340-440-540-Companion]] GC440. **Recalled from memory**
(2026-10-08): the job was **around July 2026**; the exact day isn't known,
so the filename uses the month only.

> **Key takeaways (per the human):**
> 1. **Be aware of the GC440 upgrade.** The 2024-and-newer GC440 is
>    heavier, and **PMS and the VA both missed the weight change**. Check
>    the scooter's year before matching it to a lift.

## Unit

- Bruno **VSL-6000** Curb-Sider, **probably the lowest-rated
  configuration (250 lb)**, per the human's recollection. Serial number
  not recorded.
- Mobility device: Golden **GC440, new model (2024+)**.

## Symptom

The Curb-Sider **couldn't lift the scooter**. It was too close to the
lift's upper weight limit.

## Checked

Not recorded.

## Root cause

**The 2024+ GC440 is heavier than the earlier model, and the lift was
chosen without accounting for it.** By the wiki's figures the new GC440
is 222 lb assembled with Golden's U1s, or about **242 lb with the MK
M50-12 SLD M batteries PMS installs** ([[Mobility-Device-Weights]]). On a
250 lb VSL-6000, that leaves about 8 lb for the docking device and pickup
hardware. (These figures are wiki arithmetic, not measured on this job.)

## Fix

**The actuator was replaced with a heavier-duty model, at PMS's
expense** (per the human). Which part was fitted isn't recorded. For
reference, Bruno's price list offers a **400 lb upcharge (VSL-60418, HD
head)** and a **450 lb upcharge (VSL-60524: HD drive base, 30 A board, HD
arm, exterior pendant)** for the VSL-6000
([[Bruno Lifts Price List (PMS SC-0307)]]); whether either applied here
isn't known.

## Parts used

Heavier-duty actuator (part number not recorded). PMS covered the cost.

## Takeaways

1. **Key takeaway (per the human): be aware of the GC440 upgrade.** PMS
   and the VA both missed that the 2024+ model is heavier.
2. **Get the mobility device's year, not just the model**, on every
   vehicle lift order. The GC440's serial number is under the carpet,
   right rear.
3. **Check the lift's capacity decal against the device's real weight,
   with batteries and docking device**, and leave margin. Curb-Sider
   ratings: VSL-6000 250 / 400 / 450 lb; VSL-6900 up to 400 lb.
4. Weights and lift pairings: [[Mobility-Device-Weights]].
