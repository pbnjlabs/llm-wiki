# Pride / Quantum Lineup: batteries, chargers and controllers

One table per Pride product family, built from Pride's three
end-to-end charts ([[Quantum End-to-End Chart (INFINFB4546, 2025)]],
[[Pride Group 2 Power Chair End-to-End Chart (INFINFB4550, 2026)]],
[[Pride Retail Power Chair End-to-End Chart (INFINFB4864, 2026)]]) and
the scooter spec sheets. The column that matters most on a service call
is **electronics**: it decides which fault table applies.

| Electronics | Fault table |
|---|---|
| Q-Logic 3 / 3e, QLNES | [[Q-Logic-Fault-Codes]] (and [[Joystick-Controllers]]) |
| Q-Logic NE / NE+ | LCD icons and messages; table from the Q6 Edge and J6 TTGs on [[Power-Chair-Troubleshooting]] §7 ([[Joystick-Controllers]]) |
| PG VR2, nVR2, GC3, newVSI | [[Pride-Controller-Flash-Codes]] |
| Dynamic ("Dynamic LiNX" on Pride's charts) | [[Pride-Controller-Flash-Codes]] |
| 35 A brushless (Jazzy Carbon family) | Not on file |

A chair can be ordered with either option in its electronics cell, so
**check the hand control on the chair**. Batteries: replace every
battery in the chair as a matched set (house standard,
[[Mobility-Batteries]]).

## Lithium batteries

Pride's [[Lithium Battery Field Inspection Instructions]]: fully charge,
measure, replace if under the minimum (19.6–30 V by model). **Never
load-test lithium.** On the Go-Go Endurance, Jazzy EVO 613, Pursuit 2
and Bandit a reading near 0 V may be **sleep mode**, not a dead battery.
Details: [[Mobility-Batteries]].

## Warranty (Pride's chart terms, 2026)

- **Most Jazzy and Go Chair models:** 5-year limited frame; 13-month
  limited electronics, drive motors and batteries. EVO 613 Li: **3-year
  limited lithium battery**.
- **Jazzy 1450:** K0826/K0827 builds lifetime limited frame, 13 months
  electronics/motors/batteries; K0839 build lifetime frame, **2-year
  electronics, 18-month motors**, 13-month batteries.
- **Jazzy Carbon:** lifetime limited frame, 1 year on electrics, motor,
  bearing sleeve and battery. **Carbon 27 and Carbon HD:** 5-year frame,
  1-year electronics, motors, batteries. **Carbon 27X, Ultra Light, Air
  2, Go Chair:** 5-year frame, 13 months on the rest.
- Quantum warranties aren't on the Quantum chart; see each owner's
  manual (only the [[J6 Operator Manual]] and
  [[Q6 Edge 3 Stretto Operator Manual]] are on file).

**Battery HCPCS** (Group 2 chart): U-1 E2365, NF-22 E2361, Group 24
E2363, lithium E2397, 18 Ah K0733. Pride says HCPCS codes aren't billing
advice.
