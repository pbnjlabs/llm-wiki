# Grab Bar Installation

How PMS installs bathroom grab bars. From PMS's reply to the 2026 VA
request for quote ([[PMS Grab Bar Quote Response (2026)]]), so this is
**house practice**. The VA's requirements are on
[[RFQ - Bathroom Grab Bars (VA, 2026)]]. The wiki has no grab bar
manufacturer manual or ADA grab bar guide yet.

## What PMS installs

- Shower and toilet locations.
- **Tile: yes. Wood/drywall: yes. Fiberglass surrounds: no.**
- Rated for a **350 lb** load. **2-year labor warranty**; material
  warranty is the grab bar maker's (if bought from PMS).
- About **1 hour per grab bar**.

## Mounting

**Ceramic tile**

1. Locate the studs, or use wall anchors.
2. **Tape the wall** where you'll drill.
3. Drill with a **tile/glass bit**, wet if required. **Don't use the
   hammer drill.**
4. **Seal the holes** before installing the bar.

**Drywall and wood**

- Install **directly into studs**.
- No studs: use **heavy-duty anchors**.
- **Bar over 24 in and no studs: add blocking behind the drywall.**

**Fiberglass**

- **PMS doesn't install in fiberglass surrounds.** Never install into
  fiberglass with a **void behind it**.

## VA jobs

- Bars (12, 18, 24 and 32 in) are supplied by VA Prosthetics (PSAS);
  PMS installs.
- Service area: within 90 miles one way of the Columbia SC VA hospital.
  Trip charge by distance ($175 / $225 / $275 for 0–30 / 31–60 / 61–90
  mi).
- Leave a receipt and delivery ticket with PMS's 800 number, address
  and office location.
- Before and after photos recommended (per the RFQ).
