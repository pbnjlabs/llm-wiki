# Pride / Quantum Parts Replacement (torque and procedures)

Pride's **Parts Replacement Guides** (PRGs) side by side: how to get at
each part and what to torque it to. Sources:
[[Q6 Edge Parts Replacement Guide (INFMANU4845)]] (2017),
[[Jazzy 600 ES Parts Replacement Guide (INFMANU4844)]] (2017),
[[Q6 Edge 3 Stretto Parts Replacement Guide (INFMANU5103)]] (2023),
[[Q6 Edge 4 Parts Replacement Guide (INFMANU5618)]] (2026) and, for the
seat, [[TRU-Balance 4 Parts Replacement Guide (INFMANU5359)]] (2022,
on [[Tru-Balance-4-Power-Positioning]]). Part numbers are in each
chair's IPB (none on file).

**Use the guide for the chair in front of you.** Values changed between
generations because the parts did (the 2017 chairs bolt the motor to a
bracket at 7 N·m; the Stretto and Edge 4 hang it on a mount at
15–20 N·m). Don't carry a number across models. Where a guide gives no
value, tighten until the parts are clamped without movement and without
deforming them. A **calibrated torque wrench** is on every guide's
tool list.

## Before any repair (all guides)

1. Joystick **off**.
2. Both motors in **drive** (not freewheel).
3. Unplug the joystick or **bus cable** from the base (Edge 4: at the
   rear door).
4. Raise the chair on blocks or a wheelchair jack. Chair **unoccupied**
   (Stretto, Edge 4).
5. Photograph harness routing and cable-tie points before cutting ties
   (camera icon in the newer guides). Re-route exactly as found.

## Procedures worth knowing

- **Drive motor (2017 Q6 Edge, Jazzy 600 ES):** wheel off; if the new
  motor has no hub, remove the hub nut, loosen the setscrew and slide
  the hub off. **Keep the axle key**: a missing or misplaced key can
  cause **electronic brake failure**. Loosen the 2 setscrews on the
  freewheel linkage, unplug the motor at the power module, cut the ties,
  unbolt.
- **Drive motor (Stretto, Edge 4):** wheel and fender off, unplug at the
  power module (Edge 4 **encoder motors** also unplug from the PTO
  harness), support the motor and remove 3 screws. Stretto: **5-spoke
  hubs use the top mount holes, 3-spoke the bottom.** Edge 4: cable-tie
  heads face in, toward the frame, so they clear the fender.
- **Power module:** behind the rear door. Swap harnesses one at a time
  (motors, power, bus, PTO/run plug; on the Edge 4 QLNES also actuator
  and iLevel inhibit). On the Edge 4 the light module, AM3/AM3L or backup
  camera bracket comes off first.
- **Batteries:** wiring diagram inside the front door (Edge 4: slide-out
  tray behind the rear door). Replace **both** together, same type
  (Pride's own warning, and the PMS house standard,
  [[Mobility-Batteries]]). Edge 4 breaker: short wire to the left at
  about 120°, long wire up from the right and over the top. Battery
  harness misrouting can disconnect the batteries in a sudden stop.
- **Tires:** deflate completely before splitting a rim; no high-pressure
  hose. **Never take apart a run-flat wheel.**
- **Edge 4 drive inhibit after service:** accent-light and backup-camera
  modules carry the run plug path. A connector not fully seated and
  latched gives a **drive inhibit**.
- **Hardware:** never reuse a **nylon-insert lock nut** (Edge 4: or a
  **patched screw**). **Don't adjust spring tension** anywhere on the
  chair. Get a second person to lift the seat off.

## Joint Commission (house standard)

Repairs follow the manufacturer's instructions (EQ.01.03.01): these
values are the spec. Record the repair ([[Joint-Commission-DMEPOS]]).
