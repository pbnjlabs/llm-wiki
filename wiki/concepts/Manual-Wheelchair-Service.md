# Manual Wheelchair Service

Cross-brand reference for **manual wheelchairs**, from the Sunrise Medical
Quickie 2 family ([[Quickie-2-Series]]) and Ki Mobility's line
([[Ki-Mobility]]). PMS sees these chairs mainly on Scootaround VA repairs
([[Scootaround-VA-Repairs]]). **Ki and Quickie are the only manual
wheelchair makes the human has worked on** (2026-10-26). Permobil's **TiLite**
and **PDG Mobility** manuals were added the same day ([[Permobil]]):
[[TiLite-Rigid-Series]], [[TiLite-Folding-Series]], [[TiLite-CR1]],
[[TiLite-Pilot]], [[PDG-Fuze]], [[PDG-Stellar]], [[PDG-Eclipse]]. Check the chair's own manual for any value
below before relying on it.

## Default torques

| | Sunrise Quickie 2 family | Ki Mobility |
|---|---|---|
| Unspecified fasteners | M5 44, M6 62, M8 88 in-lb; others 60 in-lb | **40 in-lb** |
| Wheel locks | 115 in-lb (144 max) | 50 in-lb lock bars (Catalyst) |
| Casters | 75 in-lb | per tech manual figure |
| Axle | axle plate bolts 144 in-lb; sleeve nuts 30 ft-lb | Catalyst outside nut 35–41 ft-lb; bottom nut 55 ft-lb |
| Tilt chairs | — | pivot joint screws **240 in-lb** (Focus, Arc, Flip) |
| Rigid towers | — | Rogue 2/XP tower clamps **70 in-lb in sequence 1-2-3-4** |

Never reuse Sunrise fasteners; don't overtighten Ki frame hardware.
**TiLite** (metric): axle sleeve 26 Nm (X/2GX 22 Nm), footrest clamps and
backrest 10 Nm, caster wedge 25 Nm; thread-patch fasteners reused **at most
twice**, then Loctite 242. **PDG**: Stellar seat depth insert 18 ft-lb, sub-frame
25 ft-lb, Fuze M50 rear caster arm 18 ft-lb.

## Setup checks (both brands)

- **Wheel locks** sink at least 1/8 in into a properly inflated tire; set tire
  pressure first, and re-set locks after moving the axle.
- **Anti-tips** 1-1/2 to 2 in off the ground (Sunrise), level and working
  (Ki).
- **Casters square** to the floor; re-check after any axle height, wheel
  size, camber or center-of-gravity change.
- **Rear axle position** is the main stability adjustment: forward makes the
  chair tippier backward. Small steps, anti-tips on, spotter.
- **Toe to zero** on rigid chairs with camber (Ki Rogue, Ethos, Clik).
- **Caster flutter, pulling to one side, squeaks:** equal tire pressure,
  tighten hardware and spokes, square casters, Teflon-based lube in frame
  joints, clear hair and debris from caster housings.

## Lubrication and cleaning

Teflon-based lube only (Tri-Flow). **No WD-40, 3-in-One or petroleum
solvents** on either brand. Ki: sanitize with 70% alcohol, 15 minutes.
TiLite: **no water-based lubricants** near caster forks or axles. **PDG is the
exception:** its manuals say to oil quick-release axles monthly with **3-in-1
oil**. Follow the chair's own manual.

## Inspection schedule

Users check tire pressure and wheel locks weekly, the rest monthly; **a
dealer inspects at least once a year**. That matches the Joint Commission
rule to maintain per the manufacturer ([[Joint-Commission-DMEPOS]]).

## Slopes

Sunrise: under **6°**. Ki: under **9°**. Both: straight up and down, never
turn on a slope. PMS still builds access ramps at 1:12 (4.8°)
([[Ramp-Slope-and-Length]]).

## Transit

Only chairs with the factory transit option (WC-19 / WC-4 §19 / ISO 7176-19)
can carry a rider in a vehicle: forward facing, pelvic and shoulder belts,
back reclined no more than 30°, accessories removed. A chair that's been in
a collision should be replaced. Some Ki chairs are **not** transit rated
(Catalyst 4), and some have a lower transit capacity (Rogue ALX 125 lb,
Little Wave 125 lb). TiLite: only the **Z, ZRA, X, Twist and Pilot** with the
factory Transit Tie-Down Option (ZR, Aero T, TRA, TR and 2GX never); the
**Transport** option is for an empty chair. PDG: only with its Transit Tiedown
System (4 white hook symbols). See [[Transport-and-Tie-Downs]].

## Warranty

Both brands: frame (and cross brace) for the original purchaser's life,
expected frame life 5 years, other parts 1 year, wear items excluded, void if
the serial tag is removed. Dealer gets an RA first.
