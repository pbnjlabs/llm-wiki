# Automatic Door Operators

How to choose and survey for an automatic door. The products are
[[AutoSlide]] (sliding doors and the AutoSwing) and [[Open Sesame]]
(swing doors). These are home accessibility products, not mobility
equipment.

## Which product

| Door | Product | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Sliding patio, screen, barn or pocket door | [[AutoSlide-Drive-System]] | Standard or Elite motor, chosen by the friction test. iLock versions lock |
| Swing door, residential | [[Open-Sesame-Model-133]] | Door swings freely by hand. Residential bundles |
| Swing door, public, storm/screen door or gate | [[Open-Sesame-Model-233]] | Clutch always engaged, so it works as a closer |
| Swing door, app or smart-lock control | [[AutoSwing]] | AutoSlide app, Yale/August locks, pet mode. Max 47 in, 198 lb |

## Comparison

| | Open Sesame 133 / 233 | AutoSwing |
|---|---|---|
| Mounts | On the door (inward doors), lintel (outward), or parallel | Transom header, inside; pull arm for in-swing, push arm for out-swing |
| Power | Plug-in 24 V AC 20 VA transformer, low-voltage cable run to an RJ-11 jack | 100–240 V AC adapter, 25 V DC. Socket by the operator's left end; cord not run through or hidden in walls |
| Backup | 12 V 1.2 Ah sealed lead-acid, about 2 days | Lithium 21.6 V (Pro version) |

| Locks | 12 V DC strike output, 1 A | Relay, maglock, strike, smart lock |
| Warranty | 3 yr / 10 yr limited ($450 cap) | See [[AutoSlide]] |

## Site survey (from the Open Sesame form)

Use [[Open Sesame Field Survey Forms]]. Most of it applies to any swing
operator:

1. **Hinge side and swing direction, looking from the inside.** This
   decides the mount and arm.
2. **Door material.** A **light hollow door needs through-bolts or
   stiffening** for a door-mounted unit.
3. Frame material, door height and width.
4. **Clearance above the door:** a lintel mount needs **at least 4 in**.
   A door mount needs at least 1¼ in between the door and the ceiling.
5. **Door-to-wall distance with the door open 90°:** a door mount needs
   **at least 2⅝ in**.
6. **Reveal:** ½–4½ in for a standard lintel mount, 4½–9½ in for the
   deep-reveal unit.
7. Hinge type (spring, butt, swing-clear, center or offset pivot), and
   whether the existing closer can be moved to the other side.
8. **Electric strike?** Needed for a spring latch, deadbolt or mortise
   lock. Strikes: Adams Rite 7440, HES 1006, Trine 3234.
9. **115 V AC outlet within 30 ft.**
10. French doors: open one door or both?
11. **Pinky test:** open the door and push it closed with your little
    finger. Check for weather stripping or dragging. **If the door drags,
    has thick weather stripping, or doesn't close smoothly, fix that before
    installing.** The AutoSwing manual says the same: the door must work
    properly and be balanced first.

## Activation options

- **Remotes:** RF fobs and visor transmitters, which can go on a
  wheelchair armrest or joystick housing. Open Sesame remotes reach up to
  100 ft through walls.
- **Push pads and touch pads**, wired or wireless, a keyless entry pad
  (useful for home health staff), and wheelchair ECU input.
- **Voice:** Open Sesame's Voice Interface (Alexa, no hub needed), or the
  AutoSlide app.
- **Proximity or pet tags:** AutoSlide's RFID reader with K9 tags. Open
  Sesame operators use the same reader through a 6-wire cable (see
  [[Open Sesame Proximity Tag Reader Wiring]]).

## Battery

Open Sesame operators use a **12 V 1.2 Ah sealed lead-acid** battery (MK
ES1.2-12 or equivalent) that lasts 3–5 years. A weak battery shows up as
a short opening, slow movement, or a strike that won't release. Test it
with the operator off: it should read 13–14 V DC. See
[[Mobility-Batteries]].
