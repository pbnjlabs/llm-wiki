# Ambulatory Aids Fitting

Quick fitting guide for canes, crutches, walkers, rollators and knee
walkers, from PMS's staff training ([[Ambulatory Aids Training]]).
Training videos for each aid are listed on that page.

| Aid | Height set to | Other checks |
|---|---|---|
| **Cane** | Top of the handle (lowest point) **level with the wrist**, arm hanging relaxed | Compare against "too tall" / "too short" |
| **Crutches** | Handgrip at the **wrist joint**, user standing in best posture | Underarm pad **about 5 cm (2 in) below the armpit**; **slight elbow bend** at the handgrip; set with the bolts and wing nuts |
| **Walker** | **Greater trochanter** (hip bone), standing | Walker **10–12 in in front**; **20–30° elbow flexion**, shoulders relaxed; weight on the heels of the hands; no forward trunk lean |
| **Rollator** | Handles **level with the wrists** | — |
| **Knee walker** | Handlebar at a comfortable height with the back straight | Knee resting comfortably on the pad |
