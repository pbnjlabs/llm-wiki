# Golden Consumer Limited Warranty (Scooters)

Golden uses ONE warranty policy text across many scooter models, not a
per-model warranty — confirmed by the [[GA541 Avenger Operator Manual]]'s
warranty section (rev. 11.17), which explicitly lists the models it
covers:

- BUZZaround LT (GB107)
- BUZZaround XL Series (GB117D/H/Z, GB147D/H/Z)
- BUZZaround Extreme (GB118, GB148)
- BUZZaround Luxury (GB119, GB149)
- LiteRider Series (GL111, GL141)
- LiteRider HD (GL112)
- All Companion models (GC240, GC340, GC440)
- Avenger (GA541)
- Patriot (GR575): see [[GR575-Patriot]]

Note the part numbers named here (GB117/GB119/GB149/GR575/GL112) don't
exactly match every model number seen in the raw `MANUALS/Golden/`
filenames (which skew toward GB106/116/118/146/147/148 and GL110/140) —
likely because this warranty text lists Golden's *current* product line
as of the warranty's revision date, while the manual collection includes
older/superseded model variants too. Don't assume every filename-derived
model here is still covered verbatim; check part-number overlap when in
doubt.

The [[Buzzaround XL Owners Manual (GB117, GB147)]] (rev 10.23.19) carries
the same text. Its list names **LiteRider GL109, GL111, GL141** and calls
GB119/GB149 the **BUZZaround LX**. The suffixes on GB117/GB147 are D
(standard), **H (HD)** and Z (also S on the IPB). See [[GB1xx-Buzzaround-XL]].
The same OM gives the GB117/147 a **lifetime frame**, 2 yr drive train
and electronics, and 1 yr batteries.

## Lists in the 2020–2025 owner's manuals

The same REV.11.17 text, with the list growing as models arrive:

- **2020** (Extreme, LX, LiteRider HD, Patriot manuals): adds
  **BUZZaround Luxury GB119/GB149** and **LiteRider HD GL112**; XL
  suffixes D, S, H, Z.
- **2022** (Buzzaround LT, Lite and XL series manuals, rev 11.09.2020):
  LT = **GB107, GB108, GB121, GB121A**; XL series adds **GB121B,
  GB124A**.
- **Companion DME edition** (2023): the standard list plus "*The GR575
  is warranted for FIVE YEARS*".
- **GC340R ScooterBug** (2019): its own section naming **only the
  GC340R**, same tiers.
- **GC540 Companion HD** (2023): no model list; "*The **GR540** is
  warranted for FIVE YEARS*" (a typo for GC540).

Manuals with **no warranty section**, terms on the spec page only:

| Model | Frame | Drive train / electronics | Batteries |
|---|---|---|---|
| GB120 / GB220 Carry On (Rev. D 2025) | Lifetime | 2 yr | **2 yr** |
| **GR596 Eagle All Terrain** (2025) | **5 yr** | **13 months** | 1 yr |

The GR596 is the first Golden scooter on file with **13-month** drive
train and electronics coverage, like the power chairs.

## Coverage tiers

- **Lifetime**: structural frame components — steel frame, frame welds,
  tiller frame, front fork, seat post. (GR575 Patriot: 5 years instead of
  lifetime. Per Golden's sell sheets, the **GC540 Companion HD and GR595
  Eagle** also carry a **5-year** frame warranty, and the **GB120 Carry
  On's batteries are 2 years**: [[Golden Sell Sheets (2018-2026)]].)
- **2 years**: drive train (transaxle, motor/brake assembly — electrical
  function only; increased transaxle noise alone is NOT a defect) and
  electronic assemblies (controller, dash assembly, wire harnesses,
  battery charger, potentiometer assembly — charger heating during use is
  NOT a defect).
- **1 year**: batteries supplied by Golden; also bearings, bushings,
  rubber components (excl. tires), plastic components (excl. body),
  seat swivel mechanism, armrests. Accessories are covered 1 year from
  purchase.

## Not covered

Tires/tubes, motor brushes, brake pads, ABS plastic shrouds, bulbs/fuses,
armrest pads, upholstery, lost/stolen parts, battery fluid spillage,
misuse/abuse/negligence, improper operation/storage, commercial use,
unauthorized repairs/modifications, exceeding weight capacity,
non-Golden accessories, acts of nature. Warranty runs from the *initial*
consumer's purchase date and requires a filed registration card.

## VA contract packaging

The [[Companion Owners Manual (VA Contract Variant)]] — a GC240/340/440
operator manual packaged for Department of Veterans Affairs Medical
Center purchases (packing list includes a "Military Branch Patch") —
carries this **same warranty text verbatim**, just retitled "Department
of Veterans Affairs Warranty" rather than the generic consumer heading.
It adds one VA-specific extra: a "Two-Year Warranty Service Plan" (24
months parts, 12 months labor, administered by Golden's VA-contract
service line, ext. 505) that isn't part of the standard consumer
warranty. Confirms this is one underlying policy reused across sales
channels, not a materially different warranty for VA buyers.
