# Rail Mounting Guidance (ILS-01512)

Bruno's single cross-model reference document for anchoring a stairlift
rail into different staircase construction types. Titled "SRE-2010 Model
Rail Mounting Guidance," but the identical document (same revision P2,
same date 2022-09-26, same figures) is bundled verbatim into both the
[[SRE-2010 Tech Support Guide]] and the [[SRE-3050 Tech Support Guide]] —
confirmed by direct comparison of both PDFs. Treat it as applicable to any
Bruno straight-rail model, not just the SRE-2010.

## Construction types covered, and whether Bruno-provided hardware works

| Construction | Bruno hardware OK? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Standard solid wood (5/4" fir/pine) | Yes | No predrilling normally needed; 6.5mm x 50mm self-tapping screws |
| Hardwood (maple, hickory, Brazilian cherry) | Yes | Pilot hole required (3/16" bit) to prevent splitting |
| Engineered wood (composite, post-2010 construction) | Evaluate case by case | Varies by manufacturer; must meet 750 lbf clamp tensile / 50 lb-in torsional minimums |
| Open wooden treads | Evaluate case by case | Pilot hole (3/16"); must understand tread-to-stringer/wall fastening |
| Marble/tile | Evaluate case by case | Underlay must be identified first; special fasteners likely needed |
| Steel treads/landings or steel pans w/ poured cement | **No** | Requires thru-drilling + underside bracket (≥3/16" thick, coated), ¼" Grade 5 / M6 Grade 8.8 through-bolting |
| Poured cement (≥3" thick) | **No** | Special anchors per local code; hammer drill; caution for embedded snow-melt cable/power lines |
| Cement precast | **No** | Thru-drilling + underside bracket, same as steel |
| Cinder block/paving stone/stone/brick/landscape block | **No** (generally not recommended for mounting at all) | Concrete pilings adjacent to stairs are the usual workaround |
| Railroad ties / landscape timbers | Evaluate case by case | Check for internal decay; shim to level |
| Glass/brittle materials | **No** | Requires custom mounting + expert inspection before quoting |

## Universal minimums (from the doc)

- Torsional load rating: ≥50 lb-in/anchor for typical wood treads (don't
  exceed 70 lb-in for other materials)
- Clamp (tensile pull-out) load: ≥750 lbf/anchor
- These were derived via worst-case testing with a 5x safety factor

## When in doubt

Bruno recommends bringing in a structural/construction engineer to inspect
unusual staircase construction and confirm both structural adequacy and
local code compliance — especially in seismic zones, coastal/salt-air
environments, or areas with frequent vibration (nearby roads/rail).
