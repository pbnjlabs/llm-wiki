# VA FSS Contracts

How PMS sells to the VA under the **Federal Supply Schedule (FSS)**:
which contract goes with which product and company, how pricing is
capped, and how Bruno's and Harmar's own FSS terms compare. Built from
the [[PMS FSS Contracts Binder]] plus the invoice, bid and process pages
linked below.

Most of this isn't needed for daily work. Pull it up when the VA asks
for a contract number, a price list or a terms question, or when
building a VA bid.

## Which contract to use

PMS works through two companies. **Supply** (Performance Medical Supply)
holds the Harmar contracts; **Solutions** (Performance Medical
Solutions) is the Bruno dealer.

| Product | Schedule / SIN | Company | FSS contract |
|---|---|---|---|
| Harmar stair lifts, porch lifts (VPL) | 65 II A / A-76 | Supply | **36F79721D0084** |
| Harmar vehicle lifts | 65 II F / F-7 | Supply | **36F79721D0058** |
| Modular ramps (EZ-ACCESS PATHWAY) | 65 II F / F-8C | Supply | **36F79721D0058** |
| Bruno vehicle lifts | 65 II F / F-7 | Solutions | **36F79719D0128** |
| Bruno stair lifts, porch lifts | 65 II A | — | **None. Solutions doesn't handle any 65 II A work** |

- **Renewed:** the contracts have been renewed, on **5-year terms** (per
  the human).
- **On the paperwork:** a VA estimate or invoice carries the contract
  as a **$0 line** right after the MISC job line:
  - Ramps: `GSA FSS-65 II F 8C`, "Contract number 36F79721D0058, modular
    ramps", then an FSS discount line at the end
    ([[VA Ramp Bid Package Example (2026)]]).
  - Harmar vehicle lifts: `GSA FSS-65 II F`, "Contract number
    36F79721D0058, patient mobility devices, vehicle lifts"
    ([[Harmar Orders and Installs Workflow]]).
- **Asheville office:** Asheville sells to the Asheville VA on Supply's
  schedule through Supply QuickBooks. Supply pays the **FSS IFF fee
  (1%)** quarterly, which is part of the × 0.96 monthly reconciliation
  ([[Asheville FSS Sales Guidelines (2025)]]).

## Pricing: the manufacturer's FSS price is the ceiling

Per the human:

- **A manufacturer's FSS price for an item is the ceiling for what PMS
  can charge the VA** for it.
- The same price lists are the **reference for building VA bids**.
- They are still the **manufacturer's** prices, not PMS rates.
- **All of the FSS price lists are already loaded into PMS's
  QuickBooks.** Build VA estimates from the QuickBooks items. The wiki
  copies are for when someone asks for a list itself.

Price lists on file (older contract periods, so check the current list
first):

- [[Bruno VA FSS Price List (2021-2026)]]: 65 II A stair lifts and VPLs
  (e.g. SRE-3050 $2,192.40, SRE-2010 $3,035.85, VPL-3153B $5,265.46).
- [[Harmar VA FSS Price List (2018-2023)]]: 65 II F vehicle lifts,
  installation included (e.g. AL-100-12 $1,935.01, AL-300-12
  $1,991.31), plus rubber threshold ramps.

Not in the binder: Harmar's 65 II A list (stair lifts, VPLs), Bruno's
65 II F list (vehicle lifts), and PMS's own FSS price lists.

**Checking current prices:** the VA's **MedSurg Catalog Search** (VA
National Acquisition Center) shows every contractor's FSS price for an
item side by side. This is how VA purchasing compares PMS with other
dealers. GSA eLibrary has each contractor's terms and price list. See
the [[PMS FSS Contracts Binder]] for a sample search. A search can
match lifts with similar descriptions, so check the catalog number: the
AL160 is a different lift from the AL-100.

## Bruno vs Harmar FSS terms

From each manufacturer's own price list. These are the manufacturers'
terms, not PMS's.

| | Bruno (65 II A: stair lifts, VPL) | Harmar (65 II F: vehicle lifts) |
|---|---|---|
| Max / min order | $50,000 / $50 or 1 unit | $100,000 / none |
| Delivery | 15 days ARO straight rail; 42 days curved rail and VPL | 14 days ARO |
| Expedited | 10 / 21 days, no extra charge | 10 days; government pays for overnight/2-day |
| Payment | Net 30 | 2% 20, net 30 |
| Installation | "Negotiated at the ordering level" | Included in lift prices |
| Parts warranty | Indoor stairlift: lifetime major / 2 yr parts. Outdoor or custom rail: 5 yr / 2 yr. VPL: 2 yr major / 1 yr parts | 3 yr, **including batteries, paint and covers** |
| Labor warranty | 30 days | **1 year** on FSS purchases |
| Warranty starts | Install date; indoor stairlift no later than 14 months after shipping | Retail purchase date |
| Return window | Within 30 days of the RA; nothing older than 60 days | Stock items within 30 days |
| Restocking | 15% minimum; installed product refused or 25%+ | 25% for ordering error or the veteran refusing; **none if the veteran died** |
| Custom items | Not returnable. **Curved rail:** dealer site eval before ordering; $350 cancellation after drawings, 50% once in production | Custom parts not returnable; custom-built units credited for common parts only |
| Stalled PO | — | Install not possible within 70 days of the PO: disposition letter; no decision by day 180: Harmar pulls the order |

ARO = after receipt of order.

**PMS's own VA return rule** is different and is what PMS offers the VA:
10% restocking plus the manufacturer's shipping, or charge the PO in
full and keep the parts as **VA Stock**
([[VA Ramp Returns and Restocking Fee (2025)]]).

**Damaged deliveries (Bruno):** note all visible damage with the driver
before signing, report concealed damage to the carrier right away,
request an inspector, and keep the product and packing until the
inspector's report is done. Details are on the Bruno price list page.
