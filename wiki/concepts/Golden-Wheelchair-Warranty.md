# Golden Consumer Limited Warranty (Power Wheelchairs)

A **separate** warranty policy from [[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]] — Golden
maintains distinct warranty documents for scooters vs. power wheelchairs,
confirmed by the [[LiteRider Envy LiNX Owners Manual (GP162)]] (Rev.
12.17), which explicitly names the models it covers:

- LiteRider Envy GP162
- Alante GP207
- Alante Sport GP205
- Compass Sport GP605
- Compass HD GP620

Note GP204 "Alante DX" (ingested in this wiki) is not in this list by that
exact number — likely the same Alante family under a different/newer part
number (GP207 vs GP204), consistent with the same kind of part-number
drift already seen on [[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]]. Don't assume GP204 is
definitely covered without checking a GP204-specific document.

## Coverage tiers

- **5 years**: structural frame components (steel frame, frame welds,
  seat post). **GP605's frame is warranted for LIMITED LIFETIME**,
  exceeding the standard 5-year term.
- **13 months**: other structural parts (caster beam/forks, anti-tip
  forks, armrests, footrest); drive train (motor/gearbox assembly, brake
  assembly — electrical function only, increased gearbox noise alone is
  NOT a defect); electronic assemblies (controller, battery charger, wire
  harnesses excluding boot covers — charger heat during use is NOT a
  defect); other parts (bearings, bushings, rubber components excl. tires,
  plastic components excl. body, seat swivel mechanism).
- **12 months**: batteries supplied by Golden.
- Accessories: 1 year from purchase, standard or optional.

## Not covered

Tires/tubes, motor brushes, brake pads, ABS plastic shrouds, bulbs/fuses,
armrest pads, upholstery, battery cases — plus the standard exclusions
(loss/theft, battery fluid spillage, abuse/misuse/negligence, improper
operation/storage, commercial use, unauthorized repairs/modifications,
exceeding weight capacity, non-Golden accessories, acts of nature).
Warranty runs from the initial consumer's purchase date and requires a
filed registration card. No other express warranty; implied warranties
excluded where allowed by state law.
