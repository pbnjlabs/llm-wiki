# Vehicle lift emergency operation

How to get a Bruno vehicle lift (and the Stow-Away seat) home when the
power or controls fail, so the user can be unloaded or the vehicle
driven. On most models the crank **only brings the lift home**: have the
dealer repair it before it's used again.

**Before any electrical backup**, pull the **in-line fuse at the battery**
(Joey, Stow-Away) or turn the key OFF (Out-Sider, Chariot). That makes
sure the controls can't fight you. The **board-button backups bypass
every limit switch**: tap the buttons near the end of travel.

| Lift | Method | Direction and notes | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ASL-275 Out-Sider** | Crank on the **motor tail shaft** (lift the cap), key OFF. A ⅜ in drill can drive it. | **Clockwise raises, counter-clockwise lowers.** Needs an able-bodied person. | [[ASL-275 Operator Manual (2022)]] |
| **ASL-250 / 250HTP Out-Sider** | Crank on the motor shaft, key OFF, **holding the control arm UP**. ⅜ in drill OK. | Clockwise raises | [[ASL-250 Operator Manual (2017)]] |
| **ASL-700 Chariot** | Emergency tool (in the glove box) driven by a drill, key OFF | Clockwise raises, counter-clockwise lowers. Also backs the motor off if the folded platform was pulled in too far. | [[ASL-700]] |
| **PUL-1100 Out-Rider** | Crank on the motor shaft (engine off, in park) | **To return the lift into the vehicle only**, not a full load or unload | [[PUL-1100 Operator Manual (2022)]] |
| **VSL-4400 Joey** | **Pull the under-hood fuse.** Backup crank from the manual bag; remove the black caps. | **Motor #1 clockwise = up, then motor #2 clockwise = in.** | [[VSL-4400]] |
| VSL-4400 board backup | Shroud off, buttons on the board | **A in, C out, B actuator** (direction set by the jumper). Bypasses all limits. | [[VSL-4400]] |
| **VSL-4000HW Joey** (older) | Fuse, shroud off, **unplug the red 8-pin pendant** connector | Buttons **A out, B in, C up or down**; **J8 XTRA jumper OFF = up, ON = down**. After an INVALID OPERATION LED, return it home or cycle power. | [[VSL-4000HW Joey Installation Manual (2017)]] |
| VSL-4000HW manual backup | Tools under the shroud | **If the actuator failed as it started to extend, take the mobility device off first.** | same |
| **VSL-6000 / 6900 Curb-Sider** | **Hand crank under the base cover** (4 thumb screws; the 450 lb version has a rubber boot) | Brings the lift **home only** so the vehicle can be driven. Store the crank in the glove box. | [[VSL-6000-6900]] |
| **PUL-1850 Stow-Away** (transfer seat) | Electrical: unplug the pendant, check the fuse, pop the control-box cap | **OUT** until drawer-out LED A, **DOWN** until lift-down LED D, **IN** until drawer-in LED F. Bypasses limits. | [[PUL-1850 Stow-Away Installation Manual (2018)]] |
| PUL-1850 manual (seat stuck high) | **Pull the fuse**, tools in the glove box | Drop the gear rack out of the box and fit the safety pin. Unbolt the actuator and lower the pan onto the pin, then shorten the actuator to about 1 in of chrome. | [[PUL-1850 Stow-Away Operator Manual (2013)]] |
| **AWL-1600 Back-Saver** | No crank in the manual | Holding UP after full height stalls the motor; holding DOWN after landing unwinds the belt | [[AWL-1600]] |

**Gaps:** no emergency method is documented on file for the **VSL-570
Big-Lifter** or the **AWL-150 Lifter**. Ask Bruno Technical Service
(1-800-656-0552). The Harmar and other lifts have their own methods on
their model pages.

**After any emergency use:** find the cause before it runs again. The
usual suspects are the vehicle battery and ground (corrosion, road salt;
see [[Vehicle-Lift-Power-Wiring]]), the in-line fuse, the breaker, the
pendant or key switch, and the motor or actuator. Record the job as a
field note.
