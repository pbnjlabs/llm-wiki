# Harmar Warranty

Unlike Bruno (one stairlift warranty + one vehicle-lift warranty — see
[[Warranty]]) or Golden (split scooter/wheelchair — see
[[Golden-Scooter-Warranty]], [[Golden-Wheelchair-Warranty]]), **every
Harmar product line documented so far carries its own distinct warranty
term**, confirmed from each product's own owner's manual:

| Product | Coverage | Term | Batteries | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[Helix]] (CSL series) | Motor, gearbox, rail | Limited lifetime | 1 yr | Other parts/electrical: 2 yr |
| [[HighlanderII]] (VPL400-1400) | Material/mechanical/electrical parts, excl. labor/paint | 3 yr from install date | 1 yr | Paint adhesion/deep rust: 1 yr; coastal (within 1 mi) rust excluded after 90 days |
| [[AL-Series-Outside-Vehicle-Lifts]] | Parts, excl. labor/batteries/paint | 3 yr, **transferable**, from retail purchase | — | Does not cover vehicle defects, or lift defects caused by a vehicle defect |
| [[Pinnacle-SL300]] | Material/mechanical/electrical parts | 2 yr from retail purchase | 1 yr | **Gear rack: 10 yr** separately; residential-only, outdoor/commercial excluded |
| [[Pinnacle-SL600]] | Motor, gearbox, worm gear, gear rack | **Limited lifetime** | 1 yr | Other parts/electrical: 3 yr; outdoor excluded, but commercial IS covered (unlike SL300) |

## Shared boilerplate across all five
- Warranty registration: a separate form in the back of the manual, must
  be returned within 10 days of installation/purchase; a QR-code
  submission option appears starting with the SL600 manual (2026) but not
  in the older documents
- Standard exclusions: loss, accident damage, over-stress, misuse,
  neglect, unauthorized modification
- Defective parts must be returned prepaid to Harmar for inspection;
  Harmar's sole remedy is credit/repair/replacement, never consequential
  damages or loss-of-use
- Registered mailing address for all warranty forms: 1500 Independence
  Blvd., Suite 220, Sarasota, FL 34234; fax 1-866-234-5680
