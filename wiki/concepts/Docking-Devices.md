# Docking Devices (vehicle lifts)

A **docking device (DD)** is the fitting mounted on the **mobility
device**, usually on the center seat post or the four-post seat frame.
A boom-style lift's strap hook, C-arm or pickup claw grabs it to raise
the chair or scooter. It's the link between the lift and the device,
and it's chosen for the specific chair or scooter.

## Which lifts need one

| Needs a docking device | Lift |
|---|---|
| **Yes** | Harmar **inside (hoist) lifts** AL215, AL225, AL425, AL425HD, AL435, AL435T ([[Hoist-Series-Inside-Vehicle-Lifts]]) |
| **Yes** | Harmar **truck lifts** AL815CC, AL825, AL825CC, AL835 ([[Hoist-Series-Inside-Vehicle-Lifts]]) |
| **Yes** | Bruno **VSL-6000 / VSL-6900 Curb-Sider** ([[VSL-6000-6900]]) |
| **Yes** | Bruno **PUL-1100 Out-Rider** pickup lift ([[PUL-1100]]) |
| No: strap hooks to an anchor | Harmar inside-platform **hybrids** AL600, AL690, AL6000 ([[Hybrid-Vehicle-Lifts]]). Strap hooks attach to an **anchor plate** (center-post devices; AL590 24 in, AL591 27 in) or a **U-bolt kit ALA580** (tubular frames). Harmar's lift-selection sheet still lists hybrids under "docking device" securement |
| No: belts or hold-down | Hitch-mount platform lifts: Harmar AL-Series ([[AL-Series-Outside-Vehicle-Lifts]]), Bruno [[ASL-250]], [[ASL-275]], [[ASL-700]]; inside platform lift Bruno [[VSL-4400]] (barrier belts) |

**Harmar ships the DD separately** ("docking device not included" on
every model in the [[Harmar Mobility Solutions Chart (2026, Rev F)]]).
**Bruno ships one in the box**: the Curb-Sider comes in 3 cartons and
the Out-Rider in 4, and one carton is the docking device. Bruno's DD part
numbers aren't on file.

## Harmar DD types

From [[Harmar Product Insights (2021-2023)]] (`Harmar-Docking-Devices.pdf`,
2021). P = power chair, S = scooter, M = manual chair.

| Type | Use | Models |
|---|---|---|
| **C-arms** (13): square receiver, square or round seat post, power seat lift, 4-point seat mount | Scooters and power chairs, with a device-specific adapter | DD13 (S), **DD19** (P/S), DD24 (P/S), DD28 (P/S), DD29 (S), DD30R1 (P/S), DD33 (P), DD38 (P), DD40 (P/S), DD41R1 (P), DD44 (P), DD46 (S), DD49 (S) |
| **Spreader bars** (9): 19, 24 or 30 in; clip to the frame or tie-down loops | Power chairs | DD20 (P/S), **DD21** (P/S), DD27 (P), DD32 (P), DD36 (P), DD37 (P), DD43 (P), DD45 (P), DD48 (M) |
| **Straight bar** | Seat-off lifting | DD17 (P/S) |
| **Cable harness** | Small folding scooters | DD39 (S) |
| **Manual chair lifting bars** (3) | Rigid and folding manual chairs | DD22, DD23, DD34 |

- **DD19 (C-arm) and DD21 (spreader bar) are the most common**
  ([[Inside Lifts Installation and Owner's Manual (2017, Rev F)]]).
- **DD45** is the 4-point docking system: a spreader bar whose
  carabiners clip to all four tie-down loops.
- **Harmar's Compatibility Calculator names the exact DD** for each lift
  and device. Use it rather than guessing from the table.
- Truck lifts: **any Harmar DD fits the double strap hook**. A quick pin
  holds it, and the DD can stay on the arm
  ([[Truck Lifts Installation and Owner's Manual (AL800 Series, 2017)]]).
- VA FSS prices, which are the ceiling for VA work ([[VA-FSS-Contracts]]):
  DD14, 17–18, 22–23, 31 **$128.13**; DD19–20 and the AL256 series
  **$169.13**; DD21, 24–30, 32–44 **$215.25**
  ([[Harmar VA FSS Price List (2018-2023)]]).

## Bruno pickup devices

The Curb-Sider and Out-Rider pick up the DD with a **receiver-style
C-arm**, a **C-arm** or a **pickup claw**
([[VSL-6000-6900 Operator Manual (2022)]], section 4). The **dealer
installs the docking device** on the mobility device, following the
instructions that come with it.

## Fitting the lift to the device

- **Curb-Sider:** set the outer arm so the belt hangs at **half the
  device's width plus 1 in** (e.g. 24 in wide → 13 in). The arm adjusts
  up to 9 in. Park the device so the DD is **directly under** the pickup
  and the scooter swings in **front end first**, then power it in slowly
  and check clearance ([[VSL-6000-6900 Installation Manual (2023)]]).
- **Out-Rider:** lift until the C-arm or claw is about **2 in below the
  belt guide** (white stitched sight line), swing in over the truck box,
  and set the vertical tube so the device clears the box wall by about
  **2 in** (the ideal). Recheck after every height change
  ([[PUL-1100 Installation Manual]]).
- **Harmar hoists:** the strap must be **taut and straight down** before
  lifting, or the chair swings.

## Operating

- Bruno: **hold the C-arm or claw** while the arm swings out so it
  doesn't hit the vehicle. **Stop lifting before the white stitching on
  the belt reaches the belt guide.** In transport the device sits level
  with the pickup still hooked on and the belt taut.
- Harmar truck lifts: lower until about **¾ of the weight is on the bed
  floor** with the straps still taut. That is the transport position.

## Troubleshooting and maintenance

| Problem | Check |
|---|---|
| **Chair tips while lifting** | DD off center: adjust it for the center of gravity. **Check it's the right DD** for the device |
| Device hits the vehicle or truck box | Arm offset (Curb-Sider) or vertical-tube height (Out-Rider) |

- **"Check and adjust the docking device"** is on Bruno's yearly
  dealer inspection for both the Curb-Sider and the Out-Rider (every 6
  months with heavy use).
- When choosing a lift, **count the DD in the load**. A 2024+ GC440
  (about 242 lb with PMS's batteries) left a 250 lb Curb-Sider about
  8 lb for the docking device and pickup hardware
  ([[2026-07 VSL-6000 GC440 too heavy]], [[Mobility-Device-Weights]]).

## Not the same thing

- **Bruno underseat hook plates** (DDC-00115, 15–18 in seat;
  DDC-00117, 19–22 in; DDC-00118, 23–26 in) are **belt anchors** for the
  ASL-275 platform lift's tie-down kit, not hoist docking devices
  ([[Bruno Lifts Price List (PMS SC-0307)]]).
- **Wheelchair transit "docking" systems** secure an occupied chair for
  riding in a vehicle. The [[Q6-Edge-3-Stretto]] manual allows either
  tie-downs or a docking-type system. They have nothing to do with
  lifts.
- **Ceiling-lift docking stations** are charging points at the end of a
  track ([[Vancare]], [[Ceiling-Lift-Evaluation]]).
