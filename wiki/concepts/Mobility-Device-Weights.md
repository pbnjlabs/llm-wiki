# Mobility Device Weights (for vehicle lifts)

Assembled weights and sizes of the scooters and power chairs PMS works
with, for **choosing a vehicle lift** and checking the **hitch**.
Golden figures are from Golden's own sell sheets ([[Golden Sell Sheets (2018-2026)]]);
assembled weights **include batteries**.

## The hitch check (outside lifts)

For a hitch-mounted lift, Harmar's rule (Outside Lifts install manual,
see [[AL-Series-Outside-Vehicle-Lifts]]) is:

> **lift weight + swing-away + mobility device ≤ the hitch
> manufacturer's max tongue weight**

Also check the device fits the lift's capacity and platform, and use the
lift maker's compatibility tool (Harmar's Compatibility Calculator).
Swing-aways: AL105 adds 40 lb, AL105L 25 lb. Bruno's lifts have their own
ratings (see [[ASL-275]], [[ASL-700]]).

**GC440 2024 change:** the 2024-and-newer GC440 (222 lb) is heavier than
the earlier model and needs an appropriate Harmar lift (per the human).
With an AL100 and an AL105 swing-away it comes to **345 lb on the
hitch**, which leaves almost no margin on a hitch rated around 350 lb.
The pre-2024 GC440's weight isn't on file.

The **Avenger and Eagle** are long (57.5 and 56.5 in) and heavy; check
platform length and capacity before assuming a scooter lift fits. The
Eagle with 75 Ah batteries is heavier than 302.5 lb (each 75 Ah battery
is 52 lb vs 37 lb for NF22).
