# Pride / Quantum Care, Charging and Maintenance (owner's manuals)

What every Pride and Quantum owner's manual says about routine care,
charging and storage, in one place. Every chair manual pulled
2026-10-20 repeats the same text with small model differences (noted
below). Main sources: [[Q6 Edge 4 Owners Manual (INFMANU5508)]] (the
newest), the Jazzy and scooter owner's manuals, the
[[Lithium-Ion Battery Product Insert (INFINFB4079)]] and the
[[Power Chair Consumer Safety Guide (INFMANU3992)]].

**Joint Commission (house standard):** these are the manufacturer's
user instructions, so they're what PMS teaches at delivery and what
the user's maintenance follows. Leave the owner's manual, the safety
guide and the controller BOI with the user ([[Joint-Commission-DMEPOS]]).

## Charging (sealed lead acid: AGM or gel)

- Use **only the supplied charger**, never an automotive one, and
  **no extension cord**. Chair off and **in drive (not freewheel)**.
  Charger into the controller or charger port first, then the wall.
  Unplug from the wall first when done.
- **Daily use:** charge after every day's use, **8–14 h**, and leave it
  about **4 h more** after the charger shows full.
- **Infrequent use** (once a week or less): charge **at least once a
  week for 24 h** (the Go Chair manual says 12–14 h).
- **Once a month, charge 48 h straight** (Quantum and newer Jazzy
  manuals).
- Charger LED not "complete" within **24 h**: unplug it and call the
  provider.
- The charger **won't start on a nearly flat battery** (EVO 613: below
  **18 V**). That's a provider job ([[Mobility-Batteries]]).
- **New batteries:** full charge first (about 90%), drive gently, full
  charge again; 100% after 4–5 cycles.
- While charging the drive is inhibited and the gauge scrolls. Gauge
  scrolling with **no charger plugged in** = a seat function or
  accessory is holding the inhibit; check the seat position. Q-Logic 3:
  charger in the chair but not the wall = **#75** "chair is not
  charging"; unplug it from the chair and power-cycle.
- EVO 613/614: drive 8 h or more without charging and a **charge
  reminder** appears at the next power-off.

## Charging (lithium)

From the lithium insert and the lithium chair/scooter manuals:

- Supplied **XLR lithium charger** only; into the product first, then
  the wall; **wait 2 min**: two red LEDs = charging, **green = full**.
  Out of order? Unplug both ends, wait 1 min, redo.
- **Go-Go Endurance Li:** new, or stored **25 days**, the battery is in
  **shutdown mode** and must be charged **off the scooter**.
- Won't charge: XLR order, the **battery fuse**, the disconnect
  switch, both cord ends.
- EVO 613 Li temperatures: run −4 to 122 °F, store 23–77 °F, **charge
  32–104 °F**. Never charge a frozen battery.
- SC15: **magnetic** charger connector; keep it **6 in from implanted
  medical devices**.
- Don't open a lithium battery box or replace cells in it.

## Routine checks (the user's schedule)

| When | Check |
|---|---|
| Daily | Joystick returns to neutral, boot not torn (don't repair it); controller harness not frayed; solid tires for flat spots; seat, armrests and riggings for loose hardware |
| Weekly | Disconnect the controller from the base and look for corrosion; everything fastened (don't overtighten); **tire pressure** to the psi on the tire; **brake test**: level ground, 3 ft clear, low speed, push the joystick slowly until the brakes click, release, repeat back, left, right |
| Monthly | Drive tire and caster wear; caster fork flutter (bearings); loose hardware; clean off hair and dirt; shocks and dampers for bent rods |
| Yearly | Provider service, especially with daily use |

Positioning belt: inspect **before every use**. Wheel bearings are sealed;
no lubrication.

## Cleaning and disinfection

- Damp cloth and mild non-abrasive cleaner; **never hose it down**.
  Wet chair: towel dry, 12 h somewhere warm, then check joystick and
  brakes.
- Hard/soft surfaces: Lysol/Clorox spray or wipes; vinyl-safe wipes on
  vinyl. Pride's suggested list: Birex SE, Bleach-Rite, Citrace,
  Dispatch, **diluted bleach 10% or less (pH 7–9; pH 10.5+ damages
  vinyl over time)**, Clorox, Green Works, PDI Sani-Cloth HB, Lysol,
  Oxivir, Virox, CaviWipes.
- Electronics: power off, **disconnect the batteries**, microfiber cloth
  damp with **70% isopropyl / 30% water**; never spray into the
  product.
- **Nothing on brake pads, discs, transaxle or motor/gearbox.** No
  chemicals on a vinyl seat (soapy water, dry it).
- Matte shrouds: matte-paint products only, no wax or ArmorAll. Gloss
  shrouds: light car wax is fine.
- Chairs exposed to incontinence: inspect often for corrosion.

The Joint Commission's between-patient cleaning applies on top of this
([[Joint-Commission-DMEPOS]]).

## Storage and transport

- Short-term storage −40 to 149 °F; long-term **−13 to 122 °F**, ideally
  68–70 °F. Older manuals: keep it between **18 °F and 122 °F**.
- Long storage: full charge, **disconnect the battery harnesses**,
  charge periodically, boards under the frame to avoid tire flat spots.
- Shipping: original carton, batteries boxed separately. AGM/gel
  batteries meet FAA rules; not every lithium battery is air-rated.
- In a vehicle, the chair is a seat only with a **WTORS** tie-down and
  occupant restraint; the positioning belt is not a seat belt.

## Battery replacement (provider job)

Provider or qualified tech only, seat empty, goggles and gloves (lead).
**Replace both batteries together, same make, chemistry, Ah and age**
(Pride's rule and the PMS house standard: replace every battery in the
device, [[Mobility-Batteries]]). Wiring diagram on a decal (front cover
on most Quantum bases). Edge 4 procedures: [[Q6 Edge 4 Owners Manual (INFMANU5508)]].

## Wheel change

Power off, **not in freewheel**, chair on blocks. Pneumatic: **deflate
completely** before taking the wheel apart; remove only the lug nuts to
take the wheel off; split-rim screws separate the halves; reinflate to
the psi on the tire. Solid-insert wheels are replaced as an assembly.
Torque values: [[Pride-Parts-Replacement]].

## When to call the provider

Motor noise, frayed harnesses, cracked connectors, uneven tire wear,
jerky motion, pulling to one side, bent wheels, won't power up, powers
up but won't move, loose seat. Have the **model, serial number, the
problem and the error code** ready. Battery gauge dark at power-up:
harness connections, **circuit breaker**, battery connections.

## Freewheel lever direction by model

| Chair | Freewheel |
|---|---|
| J4, J4E, Edge, R-Trak, 4Front, 1450 | lever on each motor (see the decal) |
| Jazzy 600 ES | push both levers **down** |
| Jazzy Elite ES | pull both **rearward** toward the seat post |
| Jazzy Elite HD / 14 | rotate both **outward**; **no warning if one is left in freewheel** |
| Jazzy EVO 614 | pull both **up** |
| Go Chair | pull both toward the **rear** |
| Jazzy Carbon | brake lever on each motor, **B = push**, A = drive |
| Pride scooters | lever **forward = freewheel**, back = drive; cycle the key after |

Never freewheel on a slope. More: [[Freewheel-Mode]].
