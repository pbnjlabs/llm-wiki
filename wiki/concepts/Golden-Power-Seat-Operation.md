# Golden Power Seat Operation

General operator guidance for scooters with a power-elevating seat
(switch mounted on the tiller below the throttle lever) — cross-links to
[[GP6xx-Compass]] (GP600 has a documented power elevating seat, see its
battery-replacement source page).

Source: [[Power Seat Operation]].

Speed is interlocked to seat height, as a tip-prevention measure:
- Seat at ~50% max height → scooter speed limited to half the speed-dial
  setting.
- Seat at ~75% max height → scooter **will not move at all** on throttle
  input.
- Must lower the seat below ~75% to drive again at all, and below ~50% to
  regain full speed.

**Troubleshooting relevance**: a scooter that "won't move" or "seems
speed-limited" after someone raised the power seat is very likely this
interlock working as designed, not a fault — lower the seat first before
assuming a controller/motor problem.
