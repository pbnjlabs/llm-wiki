# ADA Ramp Requirements

A summary of ADA ramp rules as EZ-ACCESS presents them
([[Understanding ADA Ramp Requirements]], with supporting details from
[[Residential Ramps - Where Do I Begin]] and
[[Understanding Ramp Incline]]). **This is a vendor's plain-language
summary, not the 2010 ADA Standards.** For any real compliance job,
verify against the Standards and local code.

| Item | Requirement (per source) |
|---|---|
| Max slope | **1:12**: at least 12 in of run per inch of rise |
| Min width | **36 in clear between handrails** (wider recommended for hospitals, schools, and two-way traffic) |
| Landings required | Top and bottom of every run; every change of direction; every **30 ft of run**, or where rise exceeds **30 in** |
| Landing size | At least **60 × 60 in** clear, kept free of obstructions |
| Landing slope | Level, max **1:48** |
| Switchbacks | Adequate landing and turning space, consistent slope, **continuous handrails** |

- "Every 30 ft of run" and "30 in of rise" are the same limit at 1:12.
  In the Standards, the operative rule is a **30 in max rise per run**.
- The handrail and surface specs are **not captured**. The clipped
  article ends before those sections.

## Does it apply to homes?

**Slope: yes, always.** The wiki standard is 1:12 (4.8°) for every
access ramp, home or commercial. The only exception is temporary-use
[[SUITCASE-Ramps]]. The paragraph below applies only to the
other ADA items.

Usually not. Every EZ-ACCESS article says ADA applies to **public and
commercial** access routes. For homes it is a planning benchmark, and
the real constraints are local code and permits, the device's owner
manual, and the available space. The residential guidance in
[[Configuration Factors for Residential Ramp Systems]] (≤ 30 ft
between landings) and in [[Residential Ramps - Where Do I Begin]]
(60 × 60 in top platform) mirrors ADA anyway, and so should a CSR's
default recommendation.

Commercial product: [[PATHWAY-HD]] (Code Compliant Modular Access
System, 48 in usable width).
