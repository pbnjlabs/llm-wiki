# Mobility Batteries

Cross-reference of replacement batteries by size, from the MK Battery
and Interstate price sheets ([[Battery Price Sheets (MK, Interstate)]]).
Prices and full specs are on that page.

**Check before swapping.** These are matched by **size group and
capacity only**. Confirm the dimensions, terminal type and chemistry
(gel vs. AGM) against the device's manual; some manufacturers specify
one chemistry. For charging, see [[Golden-Battery-Charging]] and
[[Battery-Charger-LED-Diagnostics]].

> **House standard (per the human): always replace every battery in the
> device.** This applies to every device. Replace the whole set as a
> matched set, even if only one battery has failed. Don't just swap the
> bad one.

## Testing lead-acid batteries (Pride)

From Pride's [[Technical Troubleshooting Guide - Battery Guide (2004)]]
(Rev B, 2004; lead-acid only). **Check the batteries first on every
call and every maintenance visit**: weak batteries mimic other faults.

| State of charge | Per 12 V battery | Pair |
|---|---|---|
| 100% | 12.8 V | 25.6 V |
| 75% | 12.5 V | 25.0 V |
| 50% | 12.2 V | 24.4 V |
| 25% | 12.0 V | 24.0 V |
| 0% | 11.8 V | 23.6 V |

Open-circuit voltage, batteries disconnected, rested 3 h (or after a
10 s load), 77 °F. Charge before 25%; **never below 10.5 V per
battery**.

- **Load test:** fully charge, then a standard (CCA) load tester, **at
  least 50 A for 10 seconds**. A meter can read 12–14 V on a battery
  that fails under load; the **controller needs at least 18 V** under
  load ([[Battery Load Test and Multimeter Education Sheets]]). In the field without time to charge: OCV
  first, then load-test. A pass is a pass; a fail on undercharged
  batteries means charge and retest before condemning them (shop
  charge, or a second visit).
- **Charger:** Pride chargers run bulk, absorption (14.2–15 V per
  battery) and float (12.8–13.2 V, under 0.5 A), and handle gel and
  AGM. Output test and LED meanings: [[Battery-Charger-LED-Diagnostics]].
- **Early failures** (under a year) are usually **charging habits**.
  Dirt across the terminals self-discharges a battery and holds heat;
  life halves for every 15 °F over 77 °F.
- **Storage:** charge fully, disconnect, check monthly, recharge below
  80% (sulfation starts), never store below 32 °F.
- Pride's FAQ says replacing both batteries "may not be necessary but is
  recommended". **The house standard overrides it: replace every
  battery** (top of this page).

**Lift chair battery backup:** some Pride lift chairs take a lithium-ion
pack that clips onto the external transformer; remove it for long
storage (the chair drains it); 1-year warranty
([[Lift Chair Motor, Transformer and Battery Backup]]).

**Scooter voltmeters (GENERAL-009CA):** the lithium voltmeter
ELE2008025 reads 3 green 90–100%, 4 yellow 50–60%, all red = dead;
charge before fewer than **4 yellow** remain. SLA TruCharge voltmeters
need a charge above **26 V** to reset ([[Pride TechnoBulletins (2006-2024)]]).

## Lithium batteries (Pride)

From Pride's [[Lithium Battery Field Inspection Instructions]]:
**fully charge with the correct charger, then measure. Below the
minimum = replace. Never load-test lithium.** Near 0 V on the Go-Go
Endurance (and MAXX), Jazzy EVO 613, Pursuit 2 or Bandit may be **sleep
mode**: wake it per the device's instructions first.

| Pride product | Battery part | Minimum after full charge |
|---|---|---|
| Jazzy Passport Travel / Standard | BAT1802153 / BAT1708123 | 22 VDC |
| iRIDE | MEC1901028 | 30 VDC |
| i-Go (Samsung / Panasonic cells) | MEC2000135 / MEC2200931 | 22 VDC |
| Go-Go Endurance / Endurance MAXX | BAT2005749 / BAT2005745 | 22 VDC |
| Jazzy EVO 613 | BAT2004389 | 22 VDC |
| Pursuit 2 | BAT2204837 | 22 VDC |
| Bandit 20 Ah / 40 Ah | BAT2204152 / BAT2204150 | 30 VDC |
| Jazzy Carbon | BAT2200009 | 19.6 VDC |

Lithium packs are Pride-specific parts, not MK/Interstate size groups.
Pride Technical Service: 1-877-800-1248.

**Charging Pride lithium** ([[Lithium-Ion Battery Product Insert (INFINFB4079)]]):
supplied XLR charger into the product first, then the wall; wait 2 min
for two red LEDs; green = full. Wrong order: unplug both, wait 1 min,
redo. **Go-Go Endurance Li: new or stored 25 days = shutdown mode;
charge it off the scooter.** Won't charge: plug order, **battery fuse**,
disconnect switch. EVO 613 Li: charge only at 32–104 °F.
Owner's-manual charging and storage rules for lead-acid and lithium:
[[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]].

## In the field

- **Scootaround VA repairs:** batteries are the one non-OEM part allowed, from **MK, Interstate or Electro Battery** only ([[Scootaround-VA-Repairs]]).

- Pride Q6 Edge 3 (standard): 2 × Interstate **DCM0055** (22NF, 55 Ah),
  matching the NF-22 size on its spec sheet ([[Q6-Edge-3]]). See
  [[2026-09-25 Q6-Edge-3 iLevel no power]].

- Prism P440 ceiling lift: 2 × 12 V 2.3 Ah sealed lead-acid, both
  replaced. On ceiling lifts ([[P440]], [[C-450-C-625]]), **tape the
  batteries together** so they keep a good connection. See
  [[2026-10-01 P440 batteries no lift]].

- Golden GP162 LiteRider Envy: 2 × MK **ES17-12** (18 Ah). Per the
  human: when the call is "batteries not charging," **check the charger**
  first. See [[2026-10-05 GP162 bad charger stays green]].

- Open Sesame door operators: 12 V 1.2 Ah sealed lead-acid, **MK
  ES1.2-12** or equivalent, 3–5 year life ([[Automatic-Door-Operators]]).

- Bruno stairlifts: 2 × 12 V sealed lead-acid. **SRE-3000** Elan:
  BTR-12008 = **MK ES3-12** (3 Ah); SRE-3050 also 3 Ah; SRE-2010 and
  CRE-2110 7 Ah. About 3–5 years, 10–15 trips per charge on an Elan. See
  [[Stairlift-Maintenance]].

## Joint Commission (house standard)

PMS adheres to the Joint Commission DMEPOS standards ([[Joint-Commission-DMEPOS]]). Store
equipment and spares with their **battery charge requirements** in mind
(EQ.01.05.01 EP 2), and maintain them per the manufacturer (EQ.01.03.01).
