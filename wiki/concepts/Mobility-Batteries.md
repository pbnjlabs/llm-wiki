# Mobility Batteries

Cross-reference of replacement batteries by size, from the MK Battery
and Interstate price sheets ([[Battery Price Sheets (MK, Interstate)]]).
Prices and full specs are on that page.

**Check before swapping.** These are matched by **size group and
capacity only**. Confirm the dimensions, terminal type and chemistry
(gel vs. AGM) against the device's manual; some manufacturers specify
one chemistry. For charging, see [[Golden-Battery-Charging]] and
[[Battery-Charger-LED-Diagnostics]].

## Cross-reference (12 V)

MK size groups are read from the part names (MU-1 = U1, M22NF = 22NF,
M24 = group 24, and so on); Interstate lists its groups.
