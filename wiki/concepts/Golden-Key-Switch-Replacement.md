# Golden Key & Key Switch Replacement

Universal replacement procedure using part **MBH-C40-KSWITCH** (key switch
with removable jumper — a universal part) plus **MBH-C40-KSET** (the key
itself, identified by 3 lines cut into it; must be paired with the
KSWITCH part).

Two console variants determine the procedure:
- **With a connector**: unplug the defective switch, remove the jumper
  from the new KSWITCH (not needed for this variant), plug in, zip-tie to
  secure.
- **Without a connector**: desolder/remove push-on connectors from pins 1
  and 3 of the old switch, install the new switch, then plug the new
  switch's own loose jumper wires into those same push-on connectors
  (jumper *is* needed for this variant), zip-tie to secure.
