# Golden Dealer Tips

Two short general-guidance documents, not tied to install/repair of a
specific part.

Sources: [[Golden Tips]], [[Touch Up Paint Tips]].

## Golden Reminders / Service Tips

- **Mobility devices**: keep batteries charged; never carry passengers;
  never exceed max weight; remove the key when unattended.
- **Lift chairs**: leave in seated position when not in use; never allow a
  second person on the chair; use high-end alkaline/lithium for the
  battery backup, changed on the same schedule as smoke-detector
  batteries; don't test new backup batteries by taking the chair out of
  recline (shortens battery life).

Note: this is the only document in the entire Golden collection that
mentions **lift chairs** — no lift chair model manuals are on file. Golden
apparently sells lift chairs as a product category not otherwise
represented in this manual set.

## Touch-up paint

- Red: a specific consumer nail polish (Fergie "Wet-n-Wild" A020 Velvet
  Lounge), available at drug stores.
- Blue: Plasti Kote 1016 GM Dark Blue Met spray paint, from auto parts
  stores — apply thin coats via Q-Tip (small scratches) or masked spray
  (larger areas).
- Warn: perform in a well-ventilated area (paint fumes).
