# PMS Office Procedures

Hub for Performance Medical Supply's own office and billing workflows.
These come from PMS documents, so they're **house practice**, not
manufacturer specs. Customer and staff contact details are never copied
into the wiki; look them up on the raw files.

## Common rules

- **PO = the veteran's (user's) last name**, for parts orders and
  manufacturer quotes ([[Ordering and Returning Parts]]).
- **Manufacturer quotes go to PMS's quotes inbox**
  (quotes@performancemedicalsupply.com).
- **Returns need a tracking number.** No tracking number, no return
  credit. RA number on the box in two places, and tracking and RA
  numbers in the patient file.

## By paperwork

**Delivery tickets and invoices**
- VA Delivery Ticket / Plan of Care vs. private pay/DSN ticket; private pay and VA invoice examples: [[Delivery Ticket and Invoice Examples]]
- Private pay: assessment fee $85 (more if far from the shop); parts = cost ÷ 0.65, taxed; SC sales tax 6%; lift chair sales are final.
- VA: bill the VA hospital; serial with letter prefix (none for Harmar); drop the "evaluate" wording when converting an estimate; VA-only zone charge.
