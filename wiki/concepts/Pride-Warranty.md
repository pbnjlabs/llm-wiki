# Pride / Quantum Warranty and Returns

How Pride warranty works for a provider, and the terms by product.
Sources: [[Pride and Quantum Warranty and Return Policy (2026)]] (the
governing policy, updated 16 Jul 2026),
[[Quantum Power Chair Warranty (Rev E, 2022)]],
[[Pride Warranty Update Inserts (2010)]],
[[Pride Lift Chair Warranty (INFINFB2685)]] and the 2025–2026 lineup
charts ([[Pride-Power-Chair-Lineup]]). **The product's own owner's
manual or insert sets its term**; the policy sets how claims and returns
work.

## How it works (2026 policy)

- **Start date = date of sale to the consumer**, if sold within 12
  months of Pride's ship date; otherwise one year after ship date.
  Original purchaser only.
- **Rental units:** start at first rental if within 2 months of ship
  date, else 2 months after; **nothing after 15 months** from ship.
- **Parts bought from Pride: 90 days.**
- **Warranty labor is the provider's.** Pride supplies parts; labor,
  service calls and shipping aren't covered unless Pride authorizes them
  **in writing, in advance**.
- **Call Pride Technical Service 1-877-800-1248 for an RA first.** A
  prepaid call tag comes with the replacement part; the old part goes
  back with the RA on the box. Sometimes they say to scrap it.
- A replacement doesn't restart the warranty clock.
- Not covered: wear items (brake pads, motor brushes, tires/tubes,
  shrouds, upholstery and seating, fuses and bulbs), **batteries** (the
  battery maker's warranty), damage from abuse, accident, contamination,
  battery leakage, commercial use or poor maintenance/storage,
  unauthorized repairs or modifications, setup and adjustments.
  Removing ID labels voids it.

Calling Pride for parts or a quote: have the account #, contact,
**unit serial number**, part numbers and PO# ready; a Pride field
technician can be requested on Pride's form (bill-back needs a
signature and PO#) ([[Pride Provider Forms (Field Service Request, Quote and Order Checklists)]]).

## Returns (non-warranty)

- Request within **60 days of invoice**; at least **35% restocking**
  plus freight; purchased parts final after 60 days; hardware never
  returnable; **lithium batteries can't be returned**.
- Credit: back within **6 months of RA = full**, 6–9 months **50%**,
  after 9 months none; RAs expire after a year.
- Receiving: inspect with the driver present, note damage on the
  carrier's paperwork, inspect within **48 hours**, refuse cartons with
  exposed damaged product, mark shortages.

## Terms by product

| Product | Frame | Electronics / drive | Other |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Quantum power chairs** (2022 warranty text) | Lifetime (frame, fixed seat post) | **1 year**: charger, controller, joystick, harnesses, motor/gearbox, power seat actuator, accessories, forks, riggings | Bearings and bushings 6 months; batteries 12 months (maker) |
| **J6, Jazzy 600** (2010 inserts) | Lifetime | **2 years** electronics and harnesses; motor/gearbox and seat actuator **18 months** on K0835 / K0848–K0857 builds, else 13 months | Accessories, brakes 1 year; bearings, plastics, batteries 6 months |
| **Jazzy Select family, Jazzy 614/614HD, Z-Chair** (2010 insert) | **5 years** | **13 months** (incl. motor/gearbox, brakes' electronic function) | Batteries 6 months (maker) |
| **Current Jazzy and Go Chair** (2026 chart) | 5 years | 13 months incl. batteries | EVO 613 Li battery 3 years; Carbon family and Jazzy 1450 differ ([[Pride-Power-Chair-Lineup]]) |
| **Lift chairs** (2021 insert) | Lifetime lift/recline mechanism and frame (not 102/105/106/107/250/358XXL) | Heritage, Infinity, Oasis: motor, hand control, control box, heat, massage 4 years then 25/15/5% pro-rated | RF pendant 90 days; lithium backup 1 year; fabric not covered; **over the weight limit voids it** |
| **Reconditioned units** | 6 months on everything | | |
| **TRU-Balance 3 seating** | 3 years structural | 2 years electronics and cushions | Actuator 18 months ([[Tru-Balance-3-Power-Positioning]]) |

Newer models may carry different terms: check the product's manual.
Warranty doesn't extend to third-party payers or renters under a rental
agreement (2010 inserts); the 2026 policy's rental terms above apply to
units rented out.
