# Transport and Tie-Downs

How scooters, power chairs and other equipment are secured when they
travel: with someone riding in the chair, empty in a vehicle or on a
vehicle lift, on an airplane, and on PMS's own delivery runs. Pulled
together from the pages and manuals linked below. Choosing a vehicle
lift by weight is on [[Mobility-Device-Weights]]; the hoist and truck
lift fitting is on [[Docking-Devices]].

## 1. Riding in the chair in a vehicle (occupied transit)

**Default: nobody rides in a scooter or power chair in a vehicle.** Every
Golden owner's manual says never sit on the unit while it is being
transported and fasten it down with an approved tie-down system (e.g.
[[Ally Owners Manual (GP303)]], [[Buzzaround XL Series Owners Manual (GB121B, GB124A)]]).
No Golden scooter or chair on file is rated as a vehicle seat.

Only a chair **rated for occupied transit** can carry its user, and then
only with the right securement:

- **Pride's rule** (consumer safety guides, [[Pride-Power-Chair-Care]]):
  in a motor vehicle the chair is a seat **only with a WTORS** (wheelchair
  tie-down and occupant restraint system). **The chair's positioning belt
  is not a seat belt.**
- **Q6 Edge 3 Stretto:** either a **4-point WTORS strap tie-down**
  (ANSI/RESNA WC Vol. 4 §18 / ISO 10542) **or** a **docking-type** transit
  system (§19). The manual treats them as two separate approved methods,
  not to be mixed ([[Q6-Edge-3-Stretto]]).
- **J6:** rated for occupied transit (ANSI/RESNA WC Vol. 4 §19 / ISO
  7176-19) **only with short seat towers**. Medium or tall towers make it
  **unoccupied only** (decal DEC8120018) ([[J6]]).
- **Jazzy 600 ES transit loops** (INFINFB3245): bolt-on tie-down loops,
  front and rear, **unoccupied only**; fit the "not rated for occupied
  transport" decals ([[Jazzy-600-ES]]).
- Check the chair's own manual and decals every time. Seating changes
  (taller towers, added accessories) can take away an occupied rating.

## 2. Empty chair or scooter in a vehicle

### Where to strap it

- Use the unit's **tie-down points**, not handles or levers. **Never use
  freewheel lever handles as tie-down points** (Pride, [[Freewheel-Mode]]).
- **Golden Buzzaround EX GB118/148:** tie-down kit **MBX-BZ48-TDB** adds
  brackets at the anti-tip wheels; straps must **angle inward** toward
  the scooter or the brackets can be damaged ([[GB118-148-Buzzaround-EX]],
  [[Tie Down Kit (GB118, GB148)]]).
- **Pride chairs** with transit loops or securement points: front left,
  front right, rear left, rear right ([[Jazzy 600 ES Transit Loops (INFINFB3245)]],
  [[J6 Operator Manual]]).

### Handling it (Golden owner's manuals)

- **Don't lift it by plastic body parts or the dash**; Golden won't cover
  the breakage. **Don't use the tires or wheels to lift a rear section**:
  it can rotate and hurt someone.
- Pad it with moving blankets; stow every piece so nothing can slide or
  become airborne.
- Never lift by moving parts (arms, tiller, footplate).

### Taking it apart to load it

| Unit | Pieces (heaviest) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Buzzaround XL / Lite / LT | Front half, rear half, seat, battery pack (front half 28–43 lb) | Drivetrain release lever; tiller lock knob on the GB121B/124A lets you carry it by the tiller |
| Companion GC240/340/440 | Seat, rear shroud, batteries, front/rear frame (heaviest piece 50 / 57 / **67.5 lb**) | T-handle beside the seat post ([[GC240-340-440-540-Companion]]) |

## 3. On a vehicle lift

All of these lifts carry an **unoccupied** device only. Riding on one is
"not for human transport" and **voids the warranty** (Harmar).

| Lift type | How the device is held | Transport position |
|---|---|---|
| **Outside (hitch) platform**, Harmar AL-series, Bruno ASL-250/275, Chariot | Q'Straint self-tensioning or retractable straps, or a **hold-down arm** (AL560, AL580-HDX), wheel chocks; Bruno platform tie-down kit | Chair **brake on** and strapped down (AL625). **Remove the seat or tie it down** with the lift maker's straps: a loose seat can blow off into traffic (Golden) ([[AL-Series-Outside-Vehicle-Lifts]]) |
| **Inside hoists and truck lifts** (Harmar AL4xx/AL8xx, Bruno Curb-Sider, Out-Rider) | A **docking device** on the chair or scooter ([[Docking-Devices]]) | Inside: **set it firmly on the floor** with a little strap tension; **never transport it hanging**. Truck: lower until **about ¾ of the weight is on the bed**, straps taut. Bruno: device level, pickup hooked, belt taut. **Engine off** to load (Curb-Sider) |
| **Inside platform hybrids** (Harmar AL600, AL690, AL6000) | Four cam-buckle straps; Q'Straint retractors optional (AL600, AL6000) | Strapped on the platform ([[Hybrid-Vehicle-Lifts]]) |

- The lift's rated capacity must cover the device **plus** the docking
  device; leave real margin (a 2024+ GC440 overloaded a Curb-Sider:
  [[VSL-6000-6900]], [[2026-07 VSL-6000 GC440 too heavy]]).
- Hitch lifts: lift + swing-away + device must be within the hitch's
  tongue-weight rating ([[Hitch-Adapters]], [[Mobility-Device-Weights]]).

## 4. Air travel

- **Sealed AGM and gel batteries** meet FAA rules (Pride; Golden's
  manuals say the same of their sealed batteries). **Not every lithium
  battery is air-rated** (Pride).
- **Golden Buzzaround Carry On GB120:** the **6.5 Ah / 156 Wh** pack is
  airline-approved; the **15 Ah (12.5 Ah) / 360 Wh** pack is **not** for
  carry-on. Notify the airline ahead, power off, remove the pack(s), use
  the protective covers; **at most 2 packs carried on, each 160 Wh or
  less**; tell the crew where the batteries are
  ([[GB120-Buzzaround-CarryOn]], [[BuzzAround CarryOn Transportation Guide]]).
- **Golden Cricket GP302:** sold as "airline friendly" (3.8 lb 12 Ah pack)
  ([[GP302-Cricket]]).
- Shipping a chair: original carton, batteries boxed separately (Pride).

## 5. PMS deliveries (house standard)

The Joint Commission DMEPOS standards require PMS to **secure equipment
during transport** to prevent damage and staff injury, and to deliver it
clean and undamaged ([[Joint-Commission-DMEPOS]]).

- **Hospital beds (MedMizer AC GOV):** **two technicians** load and unload
  every piece; split the bed for transport (unplug control box ports 3
  and 4, remove the black bolt and the top bolts and spacers into the
  hardware bag); **strap it in the van**; a transport blanket protects
  floors ([[MedMizer-AC-GOV-Bed]]).
- **Porch lifts:** travel easiest on a **trailer** to the job
  ([[VPL-Installation]]).
- **Ceiling lifts (Prism P440):** turn the hooks upward for transport and
  storage ([[P440]]).
- **Scooters and chairs:** follow sections 2 and 3: tie-down points,
  pieces padded and secured, nothing loose in the van.

## Not this kind of tie-down

**Ramp storm tie-downs** (EZ-ACCESS PATHWAY 3G **MTDK** kit: auger
anchors and wire rope at each platform corner) are about anchoring a
modular ramp against wind, not transport: see [[PATHWAY-3G]] and
[[Ramp-Site-Planning]].
