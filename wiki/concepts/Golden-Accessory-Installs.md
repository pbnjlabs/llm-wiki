# Golden Accessory Installs

Universal accessory-install procedures that apply across Golden's scooter
and power-wheelchair lines generically (not model-specific) — each is a
short (1-page) install sheet.

Sources: [[Accessory Receiver Installation]],
[[Adjustable Depth Seat Instructions]],
[[Cane and Crutch Holder Installation]], [[Cup Holder Installation]],
[[Oxygen Holder and Walker Holder Installation]],
[[Universal Mirror Mount]], [[Mobility All Models Flag Installation]],
[[Seat Belt Instructions]].

- **Accessory receiver**: bolts to the arm receiver tube at the rear of
  the seat frame (2 bolts, 5mm Allen + 13mm lock nuts). This is the
  mounting point several other accessories below attach to.
- **Adjustable depth seat**: 18"/20" seat depth, adjusted by relocating
  the seat base on its hinges and the arm bar to matching hole sets
  (5mm/4mm Allen, 13mm wrench). 18" depth requires an additional arm bar
  strap/bolt/nut per side.
- **Cane & crutch holder**: straps to the seatback via an adjustable
  horizontal harness strap — no tools.
- **Cup holder**: three armrest styles (molded channel, metal channel,
  flush-mounted), each with its own mounting method for the "L"-shaped cup
  holder mount; the cup basket itself is a separate push-button-release
  piece.
- **Oxygen holder / walker holder**: both mount into the same accessory
  bracket (above) via a locking knob — interchangeable, not both at once.
- **Universal mirror mount**: clamps around the tiller grip on either
  side; note it can become the scooter's widest point if positioned too
  far out, affecting doorway clearance.
- **Flag installation**: two-piece pole (upper/lower sections) slides into
  a sewn pocket on the left side of the seatback — literally "all mobility
  products," the only accessory doc explicitly scoped that broadly.
- **Seat belt installation**: anchors mount using the seat's existing arm
  adjustment knobs (through the arm receiver tube), not separate hardware —
  same generic seat-figure caveat as the flag doc ("may not match your
  seat").
