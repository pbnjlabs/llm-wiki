# Scootaround VA Repairs

How PMS does VA wheelchair and scooter repairs that come through
**Scootaround**, the VA's wheeled-mobility repair contractor (contract
started around spring 2024). These are **house practice and Scootaround
rules**, gathered from [[Scootaround VA Repair Workflow]],
[[Scootaround VA Repair Vendor Guide and Contract Update (2025)]] and
[[Scootaround Work Order and Invoice Examples (2025)]].

## What's covered

VA-issued **power chairs, manual chairs and scooters** only. **Not
covered:** stair or chair lifts, ramps, rollators, equipment the VA
didn't issue, new add-on accessories, cosmetic damage that doesn't
affect safe use, and smart drives or power assists.

Before assigning a job, Scootaround **calls** to confirm PMS can take it
on time, **has a parts account with the manufacturer**, services that
kind of device, and that the veteran is **within 2 hours**.

## Step by step

1. **Call** from Scootaround. If PMS accepts, the **work order email**
   ("Initial Work Approved – Proceed with Assessment") arrives within
   about 30 minutes. **Print it twice and pin the email.** **Check it has
   a serial number.** If not, reply asking for it.
2. **Contact the veteran within 24 hours** to book the assessment.
3. **Assess in person.** Check mechanical, electrical and seating, and
   watch the veteran use the device. No phone or photo-only
   assessments. **Don't question the VA's prescribed device or
   settings.** Check the serial number with the manufacturer for
   **warranty**.
4. **Quote by day 7** by **replying to the work order email** (see the
   checklist below).
5. **PO email** ("Purchase Order Approved – Proceed with Repair"),
   usually within 30 minutes of approval. Order the parts and **book the
   repair**. Do only what's on the PO. **Extra work needs an updated
   quote and an amended PO.**
6. **Finish by day 20.** Report any delay (back-ordered parts, can't
   reach the veteran) right away, **or the claim can be given to another
   vendor**.
7. **Reply to the PO email** with **the tech's name, the completion
   date, and the WO #**.
8. Scootaround pays **within 30 days**. Invoice per
   [[Scootaround Work Order and Invoice Examples (2025)]].

**Deadlines differ between Scootaround's documents:** assessment within
3 business days (vendor guide) or 5–7 business days (April 2025 email).
Aim for 3.

## Quote checklist

- Work order number and the **veteran's name**.
- Make, model and **serial number**, as assessed.
- **One line per one-way trip.** Round trips aren't billable.
- Assessment time, then repair labor time, on **separate lines**.
- Each part with its **OEM part number and name**.
- **No tax** (tax exempt; use the form that came with the contract).
- Warranty parts at **$0.00**, marked as warranty. Labor and travel are
  still billed.

**Never bill:** round-trip travel, shipping, hazmat fees, shop supplies,
flat service-call rates, or unapproved aftermarket or generic parts.

## PMS rates

| Line | Rate |
|---|---|
| Assessment labor | $80/hr, **15-min steps** ($20) |
| Travel (one way) | $80/hr, **30-min steps** ($40) |
| Repair labor | $80/hr, **15-min steps** |
| Parts | **MSRP × 0.75** |
| Golden Technologies parts | No MSRP published: **MSRP = 2 × PMS's dealer price**, then × 0.75 |
| Batteries | Handwritten note: **"30% off MSRP"** (MSRP × 0.70). **Unconfirmed.** See below |

## Parts rules

- **OEM parts only**, bought from the manufacturer, never from a
  third-party wholesaler.
- **Batteries are the exception:** MK, Interstate or Electro Battery
  (see [[Mobility-Batteries]]).

## When a job doesn't go ahead

- **Quote not approved** (the VA may replace the device): send a revised
  quote for **assessment and one-way travel only**.
- **Cancelled after parts were bought:** the VA pays the **restocking
  fee**. Quote the assessment, one-way travel and restocking fee, and
  attach the manufacturer's invoice.

## Open questions

- **Battery pricing:** is it MSRP × 0.70 ("30% off MSRP") for batteries,
